Three scripts, one text

Read in the script you grew up with.

The same verse, the same meaning, in Telugu, Devanāgarī or English letters. The whole app follows your choice.

Telugu

శుక్లాంబరధరం విష్ణుం శశివర్ణం చతుర్భుజం ।
ప్రసన్నవదనం ధ్యాయేత్ సర్వవిఘ్నోపశాంతయే ॥

తెల్లని వస్త్రాలు ధరించిన, చంద్రుని వంటి కాంతి గల, నాలుగు చేతుల, ప్రసన్న వదనుడైన విష్ణువును సర్వ విఘ్నాల శాంతికై ధ్యానించాలి.

Devanāgarī

शुक्लाम्बरधरं विष्णुं शशिवर्णं चतुर्भुजम् ।
प्रसन्नवदनं ध्यायेत् सर्वविघ्नोपशान्तये ॥

श्वेत वस्त्र धारण किए, चन्द्र के समान कान्ति वाले, चार भुजाओं वाले, प्रसन्न मुख विष्णु का सभी विघ्नों की शान्ति के लिए ध्यान करें।

English

śuklāmbaradharaṁ viṣṇuṁ śaśivarṇaṁ caturbhujam ।
prasannavadanaṁ dhyāyet sarvavighnopaśāntaye ॥

Meditate on Viṣṇu, robed in white, bright as the moon, four-armed, with a serene face, for the quieting of all obstacles.

The reader

Recite with it, line by line.

Press play and the verse lights up as it is read, at the pace you set. The meaning sits under each line. Repeat one verse, or the whole hymn.

  • Mūla text, checked against the printed sources
  • Sahasranāmas as verses or as a list of names
  • Type as large as you need it
Nitya

Japa, pārāyaṇa and vows, kept for you.

Touch the beads to count. Set a pārāyaṇa and the app gives you today's portion. Take a vow and the app knows which day it falls on.

  • Japa māla to 108, with rounds
  • Pārāyaṇa in daily portions, with a tracker
  • Nōmulu, vratas and dīkṣās with their rules
Calendar

The pañcāṅga for your town, not someone else's.

Sunrise, tithi, nakṣatra, rāhu kāla and the sandhyā times are computed for the place you choose. Festivals fall on the day they fall for you.

  • Any town in India or abroad
  • Pitṛ days and the tarpaṇa register
  • A reminder before each sandhyā, if you want one
