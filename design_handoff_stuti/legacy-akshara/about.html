What this is

A companion for the reciter

It does one thing: it helps you recite — correctly, with understanding, and eventually by heart.

Three scripts, one source

Devanāgarī is the source of truth; Telugu and Roman are derived at render time.

Meaning, line by line

The sense of the line you are on — not a summary of the hymn.

Unhurried, and elder-first

Large type, generous targets, a day and a night face. Nothing blinks or counts down.

Traditional texts, openly

Public domain, and given whole — no verse withheld, no paywall on scripture.

On accuracy. A text that presents itself as complete when it is not is the one thing this project will not ship.
