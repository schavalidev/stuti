Daily plan · in plain words

One month. One main job a day.

Monday 7 September to Sunday 4 October. Each day has one job that matters most — do that first. Below it are smaller jobs for the same day. Do them if there is time; skip them if there is not. Ticks here and on the 4-week guide are the same ticks — that page explains the "why" behind each day.

Every day, no matter what

Do the main job first. Before mail, before small fixes. If only one thing gets done today, it is that one.
From 14 September, no new features. Only fixes and words. Write "frozen" on a note and keep it where you can see it.
Reply to every message the same day. One warm line is enough. From 20 September this is the most important habit you have.
Test on a phone, not the laptop. Every change you ship, open it once on your own phone before you close for the day.
If a day says "ask" or "book", do it that day. The reply will be slow. That is why it is early.
Sundays are light. One small job, then stop. Tired people make expensive mistakes in week four.
What this month is not: it is not the full product with accounts, sync and notifications. That needs a server and takes six to ten weeks — it begins on 5 October. This month puts what already works on the web, in front of real people, honestly.

By Sunday: the app opens from your own web address on your phone and two other people's. The accountant is booked. Every fake screen says it is not ready yet.

Things you will be waiting on

These take weeks and you cannot speed them up by working harder. Check on each one every Friday.

AccountantBooked day 2, call day 15. Then papers for the entity — weeks. Giving cannot take money until the entity and its bank account exist.
TrademarkSearched day 2. Filing, if you file, is a separate decision after launch.
Payment gatewayApplied for on day 19 at the earliest. Needs the entity's papers. Days to approve. If not ready by launch, the app says "giving opens soon".
ReviewersAsked on day 17. Replies take weeks. Launch with sources shown; add names as they come.
TestersListed day 7, invited day 14. Some will never install. Twenty active households is a good beta.

Monday 5 October — what comes next

The real build begins: a server, real sign-in, practice that syncs between phones, reminders that ring with the app closed, giving that actually settles. Six to ten weeks. Its plan is the page of beta findings you wrote on day 21, plus the tracker's "must become real" list. Keep replying to messages within the time you promised. Keep Sundays.
