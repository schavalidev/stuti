To live testing · 24 Aug – 6 Sep 2026

Launch plan

Fourteen days from a finished prototype to twenty–fifty invited households using Stuti on their own phones. One builder, so the plan protects the critical path: the hosted app is the beta; the port to the production codebase runs alongside and never steals a day from it.

What "live testing" means

On Sunday 6 September, 20–50 invited testers receive a link and a passcode. They install Stuti to their home screen, use it as their daily companion for at least a week, and report through the in-app feedback door. Scope of the beta build: the phone app plus the tablet/desktop layout and the website, with audio cut, the full corpus shipped with unproofed texts flagged, and full usage analytics that count actions but never people.

Track A · critical path
The beta: this prototype, hardened and hosted
The HTML app deployed to a static host behind a passcode. Every day until 6 Sep serves this track first.
Track B · parallel
The port: the production codebase begins
Repo setup, engine smoke test, token port — from the handoff bundle. Moves only on days Track A is green; the real port sprint starts 7 Sep, informed by beta findings.
Out of scope
Audio, real accounts, the kids app, a paid tier
Recorded recitation is a v2 promise; auth stays a stub behind the passcode latch; the kids app waits; dakṣiṇā stays the only ask.

Risks, named

R1 · the big one
One pair of hands
Everything below exists to protect this: freeze on 31 Aug, the port defers, week two is fixing and testing only — no new features, however tempting.
R2
Safari can evict local data
An uninstalled web app that goes unused for a week can lose its storage — and all progress lives on-device. The welcome note insists on installing to the home screen; an export comes with v2.
R3
mailto with nowhere to go
iPads without a mail app strand the feedback send. The copy-to-clipboard fallback already exists; device pass 2 proves it.
R4
Corpus trust
A wrong akṣara in a sacred text costs more than a crash. Unproofed texts are flagged, the "text is wrong" feedback kind is one tap away, and corrections get same-week deploys.
R5
Analytics against the app's character
Counting is fine; watching is not. No identifiers, no verse content in events — and the welcome note says exactly what is counted, in plain words.
R6
The passcode is a latch, not a lock
Right-sized for fifty friendly households. If the URL leaks, rotate the code and redeploy — five minutes, not a crisis.

Beta week · 6–13 September

  • Triage twice daily, thirty minutes each: morning and evening. Severity ladder from the test plan decides what moves.
  • Hotfix rule: blockers the same day, majors within forty-eight hours, minors batch to the week after.
  • Day 1: watch the first sessions; be reachable. Day 3: a gentle nudge to testers who have not opened it. Day 7: the findings review.
  • All reports through the in-app door — one inbox, with the build stamp attached to every mail. Calls happen, but the caller is asked to also send it in.
  • The findings review on 13 Sep decides two things: what the prototype fixes next, and what the port must do differently.
After the beta, Track B becomes the main line: the handoff bundle in design_handoff_stuti_app/ is the port's brief — engine as-is, stylesheets verbatim, the hymn shape as the data contract. Beta findings fold into it as amendments, not rewrites.
