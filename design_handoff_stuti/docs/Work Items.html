Work items

Everything standing between the prototype and the beta on 6 September, grouped by what it is for. Group A blocks the beta; nothing else does. Checkmarks persist on this device. Effort: S is an hour or two, M a half-day, L a full day or more.

A · Beta blockers

The gate on 5 September checks exactly this group. Dates follow the launch plan.

Pick the host and make the first deploy
Netlify or Cloudflare Pages — free static hosting, HTTPS out of the box. Deploy the project folder as-is, confirm fonts, emblems and the service worker load from the hosted origin, keep the URL private.
SMon 24
Passcode gate
A small screen before first run: one shared passcode, stored locally once entered (a new stuti-beta-key), warm refusal copy for the wrong code. A latch for invited testers, not security.
MTue 25
Web manifest, icons, install path
manifest.json with name, colours and 192/512 icons drawn from the brand drop; apple-touch-icon and status-bar meta for iOS. Verify Add to Home Screen and standalone launch on both platforms.
MTue 25
The feedback inbox goes real
The sheet mails feedback@stuti.app — that address must receive mail, or FB_TO in stuti-feedback.jsx changes to one that does. Test the mailto on all four devices and the copy fallback where mail is absent.
SWed 26
Usage analytics, wired and restrained
Pick the tool (see open decisions), load it from the entry pages, and emit events from the router and key actions: screen views, onboarding completed, text opened, reading depth, script switched, japa session, guide opened, search used, install. No identifiers, no verse content.
MWed 26
Audio affordances cut cleanly
Audio is out of the beta: remove or disable anything that implies a recording exists — no dead play buttons. Text-only recitation cues may stay if they stand on their own.
SThu 27
Unproofed texts flagged
A provenance mark per text — proofed against its printed source, or awaiting proof. The unproofed ones carry a quiet line in the reader that links to the "text is wrong" feedback kind. Ship everything; hide nothing.
MThu 27
Device passes, fixing as found
Android Chrome and desktop on Friday; iPhone and iPad Safari on Saturday — install, standalone, safe areas, offline launch, fonts, tap targets.
LFri 28 – Sat 29
Almanac spot-check against a published pañcāṅga
Tithi at sunrise, nakṣatra, māsa label, sunrise/sunset and sandhyā windows for several dates and at least two places; include an Ekādaśī viddha case and the adhika-māsa month naming.
MSun 30
Feature freeze
After breakfast on Monday 31, only fixes land.
Mon 31
Go / no-go walked, final deploy made
The gate lives in the launch plan. Green means deploy, verify once from a phone, stop.
