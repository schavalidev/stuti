The app, driven end to end

Stuti.html, probed live in the browser as a reciter would use it: every tab, every card, every sheet, every reader control, in Telugu, Devanāgarī and Roman. Three passes — a static one that checked every navigation call against the router and every cross-file reference against its definition, a live one that clicked through the flows and read the console after each screen, and a third that went through the doors the first two had stopped short of: the japa beads, a plan started and finished and dropped, a saṅkalpa taken and let go, a sign-in and a sign-out, the reset armed and confirmed, and the padlocked share and print carried all the way to a file on disk. Practice data was photographed before each destructive test and put back after. Four verdicts: works (does what it looks like it does), rough (works, but with a caveat worth knowing), broken (did not do what it says — all five found faults are fixed), and by design (a door that is deliberately closed, and says so).

What the audit found and changed

Five faults across three passes. The first two were copy; the three the locked doors gave up are a missing translation, a door that opened on a malformed address, and a promise that came undone on one tap. Every fix landed the same day; the rows above read as the app behaves now.

The reminder card spoke in a key

The collapsed daily-reminder card on Nitya read 7:00 AM · plusTithiDays — the string plusTithiDays was asked of the translation table and never given to it, in any script. The key now exists in all three: the card reads 7:00 AM · పంచాంగ విశేష దినాల్లోనూ, and its Devanāgarī and Roman forms match the voice of the sheet it opens.

Search STOTRAS, shouted

The Roman placeholder in the search field read Search STOTRAS — a caps-lock slip in the translation table, not a style. It reads Search stotras now; the Telugu and Devanāgarī placeholders were already right.

What the static pass proved

Every go("…") in the 34 app modules names a view the router renders — the one stray, go("all") in the reset panel, is a local function that shares the name, not a route. Every window.* component and store referenced across files is defined by the file that should define it. An unknown hash or route falls through to the home rather than to a blank screen, and the onboarding's skip, back, next and begin all call real handlers.

What the live pass touched, honestly

Driving a real app writes real state, and the third pass wrote a great deal of it. Every destructive test was bracketed: storage photographed before, the deletion confirmed, the photograph put back, and the test's own writes — 542 beads on today, ten mālās on yesterday, a Hanumān plan, a Pradoṣa vow, an account for test@example.com, a spent month of share and print — unwound afterwards to the values the shelf held before. Reading positions were the one thing the audit could not test without moving: the Mahāgaṇapati Sahasranāma's saved place was walked and then set back to the beginning, and the home's continue card now names that hymn as last read. Nothing else moved — script, theme, the Nitya lens, reminder settings, japa counts, plans and favourites were either untouched or restored to the values they held before the audit began.

A spoken label still in English

The flower that adds a hymn to the daily recitation carried its whole meaning in an aria-label — and that label was hardcoded Add to daily recitation, so a Telugu reader on a screen reader met one English sentence in a Telugu app. It now reads the stored script choice and speaks నిత్య పారాయణంలో చేర్చండి, with Devanāgarī and Roman beside it.

An account for not-an-email

The sign-in door checked only that the handle was not empty, so not-an-email created an account named after it — a handle that would come back wrong on the day sync arrives. The continue button now waits for an address with a domain, or ten digits of a phone number, and stays visibly shut until it has one.

A vow undone by one tap

Removing a plan arms and asks again. Removing a saṅkalpa did not: the × called remove on the first tap, and a promise with its whole kept-record went with it — while its own spoken label already said Remove?, as though it were asking. It now arms like a plan, names what the next tap will do, and disarms itself after four seconds.

What is deliberately still closed

Two things, now that the rest has been driven. Giving is the real padlock: a tier can be chosen and confirmed, and what comes back is an honest not-yet with a preview lamp — no payment path exists to test. Share and print are not padlocked at all; they are metered at one of each a month, and the version-2 sheet is what a spent allowance looks like. Restoring from a file ends at the system picker, which no probe can fill. And the calendar still names a day's observances without linking them — the observance data lives in the library's lenses, and the day sheet stays a ledger.
