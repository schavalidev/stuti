Stuti — Library page, five directionsThe root of the Library tab: one pool of 111 texts across eight deities, re-sliced by lens — the same pool in every frame so the structures compare honestly. A is the shipping screen. B and C re-dress the deity grid (presence versus density), D flips the root to a printed index of texts, E opens with search. Each frame also wears a palette from the app's own world: A the shipping day theme, B a sandstone dusk, C the night theme as built, D turmeric paper with sindūra rubrics, E a dawn wash for the hour it opens. Mix and match freely — B pairs with the deity page's garbhagṛha, D with its granthasūcī. A second band below re-selects the deity pigments themselves.

The deity pigments, re-selectedThe current hues were chosen by iconography — each is right alone, but fed through one formula and spread evenly round the wheel they render as eight candy pastels: a sweet box, not a shrine shelf. Three re-selections on the shipping layout, so only the color changes; emblems in plain ink so the swatches alone speak — production would re-tint the emblem set. F grinds a color per deity from one mineral paint-box, G removes deity color from the root, H moves it from fill to ink. The board at right compares every swatch.
F · KhanijaSame slots, hand-ground pigments: sindūra, vibhūti, kuṅkuma, nīla, mayūra, haridrā, drākṣā, gairika. Chroma evened, warmth shared — identity without candy.
