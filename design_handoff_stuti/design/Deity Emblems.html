Proposal · deity marks

From bīja to emblem

Each deity’s medallion carries an emblem — its āyudha or attribute drawn in the same line register as the app’s icons — instead of the seed-syllable. Same ring, same per-deity hue; only the mark inside changes.

ॐ Two deities — Śiva and Viṣṇu — currently share the same bīja ॐ, so their medallions are identical today. Distinct emblems tell them apart at a glance.
