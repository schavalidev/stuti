Śiva · corrected colour

White, not blue

Śiva is karpūra-gauram — camphor-white, ash-smeared. Only the throat is blue: Nīlakaṇṭha. So the medallion reads white / ash, and I keep blue as an optional accent on the ring rather than the body. Three ways to sit it in the seal:
