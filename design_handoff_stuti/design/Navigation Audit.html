What changed

The audit was taken and then spent. Everything above that was a fault is fixed; three rows stay honest about what a website cannot answer for.

The library has a history

A drill-in is a pushState now, and a popstate listener reads ?lens=, ?k= and ?q= back out — the reader was already written, only the writer was wrong. Probed: grid → Śiva → Back → grid → Forward → Śiva, and a vratam two levels deep unwinds one level at a time. Every lens row carries the URL it produces, so cmd-click and middle-click work, and the search query now lives in ?q=.

The catalogued titles stop leaving the site

readHref() sends the 105 titles without verses to get.html — the page whose whole job is to explain why they wait in the app — instead of into the app build's library tab at no particular title. The “in the app” tag and the href now say the same thing.

The script switch reaches the whole page

The home page is drawn by one draw() registered on both SITE_ONLANG and SITE_ONLOC; the almanac registers the same for its sheet; the reader re-renders its head as well as its verse. Featured titles, today's tile, the tithi headline and the prev/next text names all follow the pill.

Settings reports what it does

“Where you left off” reads the record the reader writes, which now carries the title as well as the id and verse, and offers a link back to it. Day/Night goes through one SITE_SETTHEME in the masthead so the icon cannot disagree. The place chooser relabels itself on the way out and redraws on SITE_ONLOC.

Two pictures became controls

The chip over the sky is the app's own place chooser, wearing the translucent styling it always had; the sandhyā plate opens the almanac it names. The reader's progress bar seeks, its lines take a pointer cursor, its speed chip labels itself from a table, and Space is left to whatever button has focus.

The way out, and the way past

The changelog carries a line back to the website. A skip link sits ahead of the masthead, the current nav link carries aria-current, and the script and face buttons carry aria-pressed. The calendar marks the day whose sheet is open and keeps the month you were browsing through a script or place change.

What is deliberately still open

The sandhyā plate's three times are fixed text — a computation, tracked in the parity audit, not a navigation fault. The calendar still has no route from a date to the vratam kept on it, for the same reason: the observance data is not loaded there. And Stuti.html and Kids.html remain one-way doors on purpose — they are the app, not a page of the site, and an app does not wear a back-to-the-website line.
