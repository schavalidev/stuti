A · Slab

A thick block with a real edge. The painting is the top face; the name is set into the front lip in the deity's own colour. Tilts toward the finger.

B · Lacquer

Full-bleed painting under glass: a hairline gold inner frame, parallax, and a light that sweeps across when touched. Closest to what is live, minus the stone frame.

C · Frame & mat

A museum mount: thin coloured frame, square window, the name printed on the mat below. Calm and flat-ish; the depth is in the frame and the recessed window.

D · Prints on the table

Each tile is a photo print tossed down at a slight angle. Touch one and it straightens and lifts. The most playful of the five.

E · Flip

Square, painting on the front. Hover or press and it turns over to a solid colour back with the name and epithet.

B · Lacquer, at night

The same tile on the dark theme — the painting stays lit, the frame goes to brass.
