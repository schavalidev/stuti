# Festival calendar — sourced from Aarsha Vani

Extracted from the "Major Festivals in this Month" section of each issue of *Aarsha Vani*
(Rushipeetham Charitable Trust's monthly Sanatana Dharma e-magazine), covering the source PDFs
in `~/Downloads/Aarshavaani/`.

**Status:** staged reference data, not yet wired into the Stuti app. Stuti (this repo) is one
part of a larger planned project ("Akshara" — a broader Sanatana Dharma library); this festival
data, along with the rest of the Aarsha Vani material (stotra commentary, guru's discourses,
dharma articles — not yet extracted), is being kept ready for whichever of the two products
ends up with a festival-calendar feature.

**Coverage:** Every issue supplied so far has been processed below, added incrementally as new
batches of PDFs arrive. See the running gap note at the end of the file for exactly which months
are still missing.

**Format per entry:** `<Festival Name> — <Gregorian date> — <tithi/paksha/māsa as given> —
<brief note if any>`. Dates and tithis are as printed in each issue (each issue is authored
against the Telugu/Āndhra pañcāṅgam for that lunar cycle — panchangam conventions can differ
slightly by region/tradition, so treat these as this magazine's stated dates, not a
universally-agreed calendar). Weekday given in the magazine is kept for cross-checking.

**Known raw-extraction quirks to clean up before using this data downstream:**
- Some entries carry two neighboring tithis/festivals with slightly conflicting weekdays as
  printed in the source (e.g. Aug 2022 "Śrāvaṇa Paurṇima" listed right after "Jandhyala
  Purnima" with a different weekday) — kept verbatim, flagged inline where an agent caught it.
- "River Sindhu Puṣkarams" (Nov 2021) is a multi-day event, not a single-day festival — spans
  Nov 21–Dec 2, 2021.
- A few festival lines have no note field where the source page didn't editorialize on that
  entry (e.g. Skanda Pañcamī, Jul 2022) — left blank rather than invented.

## AarshaVani October 2021

Aindra Ēkādaśī (Dharmaprabha Ēkādaśī) — October 2, 2021 (Saturday) — Bhādrapada Bahuḷa Ēkādaśī — Observing this ēkādaśī vrata and giving oil seeds in charity grants best merits; Satya Harishchandra regained his wife, son, and kingdom through this vrata.

Pōlāla Amāvāsya, Mahālaya Amāvāsya — October 6, 2021 (Wednesday) — Bhādrapada Bahuḷa Amāvāsya — Some families worship 'Pōlēramma' (unified Mahākāḷi-Mahālakṣmi-Mahāsarasvati); last day of Mahālaya Pitṛupakṣa, tarpaṇa offered to pitṛus.
Śarannavarātri begins; Tirumala Śrīvāri Navarātra Brahmōtsavālu Prārambhaṁ — October 7, 2021 (Thursday) — Āśvayuja Śukla Pratipada — 'Śaradārādhya' per Śrī Lalitā Sahasranāma; Divine Mother worshipped in one of Her infinite forms for nine days.
Upāṅga Lalitā Gauri Vratam, Nāga Puja — October 10, 2021 (Sunday) — Āśvayuja Śukla Pañcamī — Śrī Lalitādēvi worshipped as 'Pañcamī'/'Pañcabhūtēśī'; serpent worship relieves serpent-related evils.
Sarasvatī Puja (worship of books) — October 11, 2021 (Monday) — Āśvayuja Śukla Ṣaṣṭhī, Mūla nakṣatra — Students performing Sarasvatī Puja attain great heights in education.
Dēvi Tri-rātra Vrata begins — October 12, 2021 (Tuesday) — Āśvayuja Śukla Saptamī — Alternative for those who cannot do all nine days: 7 days from Tadiya, 5 from Pañcamī, or at least 3 from Saptamī.
Durgāṣṭamī — October 13, 2021 (Wednesday) — Āśvayuja Śukla Aṣṭamī — Worship of Durgādēvi removes all troubles.
Mahānavamī, Svārōciṣa Manvādi — October 14, 2021 (Thursday) — Āśvayuja Śukla Navamī — Principal day for Dasara Puja.
Vijaya Daśamī (Āyudha Puja, Śamī Puja, Kalaśōdvāsana, Aparājita Puja) — October 15, 2021 (Friday) — Āśvayuja Śukla Daśamī — Worship of Jagadamba done a second time; kalaśa revocation; kings worshipped their weapons during 'Vijayā' muhūrta; śamī tree worshipped at dusk.
Pāśāṅkuśa Ēkādaśī (Parāṅkuśa Ēkādaśī, Ramyaikādaśī) — October 16, 2021 (Saturday) — Āśvayuja Śukla Ēkādaśī — Performing this ēkādaśī vratam with prescribed rules protects from hell and uplifts departed souls.
Gōvatsa Dvādaśī (Padmanābha Dvādaśī, Tulā Saṅkramaṇaṁ) — October 17, 2021 (Sunday) — Āśvayuja Śukla Dvādaśī — Anantha Padmanābha vratam obtains grace of Lord Nārāyaṇa; Cow worshipped along with Calf.
Kaumudī Pūrṇimā (Akṣa Krīḍa, Kōjāgarī Vratam, Kaumudī Vratam) — October 19, 2021 (Tuesday) — Āśvayuja Śukla Pūrṇimā — Kōjāgarī vratam (offering rice pudding with milk to Śrī Lakṣmīdēvi, staying awake, playing dice) as taught by Vālakhilya Maharṣi.
Śaratpūrṇimā (Mahāśvinī, Vālmīki Jayanti) — October 20, 2021 (Wednesday) — Āśvayuja Pūrṇimā ('Mukhya Rāka') — Highly auspicious worship of Divine Mother; Vālmīki Jayanti marked by reading Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa.
Candrōdaya Umā Vratam (Aṭla Tadiya) — October 23, 2021 (Saturday) — Āśvayuja Kṛṣṇa Tadiya (Tṛtīyā) — Day-long fast, Gauri vratam in evening, arghya offered to Moon; girls' festival with sesame, lentils, gongura leaves, curd.
Saṅkaṣṭahara Caturthī — October 24, 2021 (Sunday) — Āśvayuja Kṛṣṇa Caturthī — Vratam performed with prescribed procedures bestows Gaṇapati's grace, removing obstacles.

Nāgula Chaviti — November 8, 2021 (Monday) — Kārtika māsa — Offer milk in anthill, rice flour mixed with sugar and sesame sweets to serpents; also worship Gaṇapati and Subrahmaṇya; no cutting of vegetables or using knives/tools
Nāga Pañcami — November 9, 2021 (Tuesday) — Kārtika māsa — Worship of Garuḍa removes toxins; Garuḍa Pancami vrata keeps poisons away; milk offered at serpent hills; farmers worship serpents for bumper harvest
Agnipūja / Mahā Ṣaṣṭhi (Subrahmaṇyārādhana) — November 10, 2021 (Wednesday) — Śuddha Ṣaṣṭhi, Kārtika māsa — Worship of Fire/lamp; worship of Subrahmaṇya (Agnigarbha) augurs wellbeing
Gōṣṭhāṣṭami Vratam / Kārtavīryōtpatti — November 11, 2021 (Thursday) — Kārtika māsa — Worship of cow, decorating cowshed, circumambulating cows grants long life and prosperity; worship of Kārtavīrya (Dattātrēya devotee) on his birthday helps recover lost items
Akṣaya Navamī Vratam / Viṣṇu Trirātra Vratam / Kṛtayugādi — November 12, 2021 (Friday) — Kārtika māsa — Popularly Akshaya Navami; bathing in rivers, worship and charity give inexhaustible results
Yājñavalkya Jayanti — November 13, 2021 (Saturday) — Kārtika māsa — Worship of sage Yājñavalkya, who had vision of Sun God and Goddess Saraswati
Utthānaikādaśi / Ananta Ēkādaśi (Alabhya Yōgam) / Bhīṣma Pañcaka Vratam — November 15, 2021 (Monday) — Kārtika Śukla Ēkādaśī — Viṣṇu rises from yōganidra on this day; fasting and Viṣṇu worship yield immense merits; Bhīṣma Pañcaka Vrata begins (continues through Pourṇima)

Saṅkaṣṭahara Caturthī / Bhauma Caturthī — November 23, 2021 (Tuesday) — Kārtika māsa — Observing saṅkaṣṭahara caturthī vrata in Kārtika is special; falling on a Tuesday this year makes it Alabhyamahāyōga
Uttpanna Ēkādaśī — November 30, 2021 (Tuesday) — Kārtika Kṛṣṇa Ēkādaśī — Marks the day the presiding deity of ēkādaśī manifested; observing this vrata with fasting and worship is dear to Viṣṇu

## AarshaVani December 2021

Subrahmaṇya Ṣaṣṭhi — December 9, 2021 (Thursday) — Mārgaśīra Māsa, Ṣaṣṭhī — Birthday of Kumāraswāmi; worship provides relief from evils related to serpents (Kujadōṣa).

Bhīṣma Aṣṭami — February 8, 2022 (Tuesday) — Māgha Śuddha Aṣṭamī — Day Bhīṣma merged his soul with Lord Krishna; Tarpaṇa performed for Bhīṣma.
