Privacy policy

Stuti (स्तुति) · Effective [DATE]

Stuti is an app for reading and reciting stotras, keeping a daily practice, and following the pañcāṅga. It is provided by [OPERATOR NAME], [ADDRESS] ("we"). This policy says what information the app uses, where it goes, and what you can do about it. It is written to meet India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

In short: the app works fully without an account, and most of what you do stays on your phone. If you create an account, your practice records are kept on our server so they are safe and the same on each phone. We do not show advertising. We do not sell or share your information for anyone's marketing.

1. What stays on your phone

The name, gotra and other saṅkalpa details you enter are kept only on your phone. They are never sent to us, with or without an account, and they are not included in backups to your account.

Your favourites, reading positions, japa counts, vows, plans, reminders and settings are stored on your phone. Without an account, they stay there.

2. If you create an account

  • Sign-in details. Your email address or mobile number, or, if you sign in with Google, the email address and name Google shares with us. You may also give a display name.
  • Your practice records. Favourites, reading positions, place, script and language, japa counts, plans, vows, the thread of practice days, keeps, the ledger, the lamp, and reading settings. These are kept so they are safe and the same on each phone you sign in on.
  • Reminder settings. Your chosen place and time zone, sandhyā reminders, quiet hours, digest time, almanac settings and vows, kept for each phone. They are used to time reminders.

Only you can read these records. When two phones hold different records, the app shows both and asks which to keep; it does not combine or overwrite them without asking.

3. Information sent without an account

  • Usage counts. A small set of events, such as a screen opened, a text opened, or japa counted, with the day, the platform (Android or web) and the app version. No account, device identifier, phone number, name or time of day is attached. They tell us which parts of the app are used. No counts are sent when your browser's Do Not Track setting is on.
  • Feedback. What you write in the feedback form, the kind of message, and, if you gave one, a reply address. A report of a problem also carries technical details about the app and your phone (version, screen, settings, recent errors), which the form shows before you send. Feedback is emailed to us and kept so we can act on it. If you are signed in, it is linked to your account.
  • Error reports and practice logs. While the "Send logs" setting is on, the app sends us reports when something goes wrong, and a log after each Follow session (the lines followed, and timings). In the Android app, the audio recorded during a Follow session is also sent, when it is shorter than about six minutes. These carry a random identifier for the phone, not your name or number. They are used only to find and fix faults. You can turn this off at any time in Settings.

4. Your location

Pañcāṅga and sandhyā times depend on where you are. You can choose a city from the list, or allow the app to use your phone's location. If you allow it, the coordinates are sent once to BigDataCloud to find the name of the nearest town, and then only the place is kept. If you have an account, that place is part of your practice records.

5. Microphone

  • Voice search uses your phone's or browser's own speech service (on most phones, Google's). What you say is handled by that service under its own privacy terms.
  • Follow listens on the phone itself to keep your place in the text. The audio is not sent anywhere, except as described for practice logs in section 3.

6. Giving

If you choose to give, the payment is made on Razorpay's page. Your card, bank or UPI details go to Razorpay and not to us. We do not keep payment information in the app.

8. How long it is kept

  • Account and practice records: until you delete the account.
  • Feedback: until it has been dealt with, and at most [2 years].
  • Error reports and practice logs, including audio: at most [12 months].
  • Usage counts: kept, since they cannot be linked to you.

9. Your rights and choices

  • Delete your account in the app: Settings → Account → Delete my account. This removes the account and every record kept under it on our server. What is on your phone stays on your phone.
  • Delete everything on your phone from Settings, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Stop logs and audio by turning off "Send logs" in Settings.
  • Take your data with you using export in Settings, which works with or without an account.
  • Ask us to show, correct or erase information we hold about you, or to delete feedback or logs from your phone, by writing to the address below. We will reply within [30 days].

10. Children

Stuti is meant for users aged 18 and over, or younger users with a parent's or guardian's consent. We do not knowingly create accounts for children without that consent. If you think one exists, write to us and we will delete it.

11. Security

Information travels over encrypted connections. On our server, each account's records can be read only by that account. No system is perfectly secure, and if a breach affects you we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

12. Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app will say so before the change takes effect. The date above shows the latest version.
