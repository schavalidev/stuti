Stuti (स्तुति) · Effective 27 October 2026

Stuti stands on work that is not ours: the texts themselves, the editions that preserved them, and a good deal of software and data that other people gave away. This page names all of it. Several of the licences below ask for exactly that, and the asking is fair.

1. The texts

The stotras, sūktas, sahasranāmas and purāṇic chapters in Stuti belong to the tradition that has carried them. They are ancient and nobody owns them.

A particular printed edition is a different matter. Stuti's texts are prepared by collating published editions — Gītā Press above all, along with scholarly critical editions and, for the Vedic texts, prints that follow the reciter's own śākhā. Where editions disagree, the reading we follow and the variants we know of are recorded with the text.

The English and Hindi meanings, the Telugu glosses, the word-by-word notes, the arrangement of the texts and every piece of writing in the app's own voice are our work, and are ours.

4. Pañcāṅga and astronomy

The pañcāṅga is calculated on the device. The solar position follows chapter 25 of Jean Meeus's “Astronomical Algorithms”, and the lunar position the abridged ELP-2000/82 of its chapter 47. The implementation is ours; the algorithms are published science and are credited here because the work deserves naming, not because a licence demands it.
