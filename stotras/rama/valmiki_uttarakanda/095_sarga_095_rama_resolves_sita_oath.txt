Title: Śrīmad Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa — Uttarakāṇḍa, Sarga 97: Rāma resolves to have Sītā take an oath to prove her purity
Devanāgarī: श्रीमद्वाल्मीकीयरामायणे उत्तरकाण्डे पञ्चनवतितमः सर्गः

Author: Vālmīki

Recension note: Text-only file, written 3 Oct 2026: the English, Telugu and Hindi meanings are still to be added, and the witnesses' differences listed here were found by machine and have not been adjudicated or bracketed into the line. The print numbers 17 verses, as the Gītā Press ebook does. Of the 36 half-lines, 8 were confirmed by three scans agreeing exactly, 17 were read off the page and confirmed by a scan, and 8 were settled against the page by reading it again with every witness in view. 3 half-lines that the scans' alignment missed were read off the page and added. 1 line that the machine had taken for verse was left out after checking the page. As a check on the first group, 6 of its half-lines were also read blind off the page; 6 agreed. The critical edition carries 33 of these half-lines in recognisable form and differs from the print at verses 1, 3, 5–7, 10–11, 13, 15–17; its absence elsewhere is not evidence against the print. The packet opened this sarga with दूतान् शुद्धसमाचारानाहूयात्ममनीषया and had placed the sarga's true first half-line, रामो बहून्यहान्येव तद् गीतं परमं शुभम्, in the previous sarga's tail, where it is printed just after this sarga's heading. That half-line was read off the page and added as the first pāda of verse 1, and verses 1 to 3 were regrouped to follow the print's order. Verse 17 is a four-pāda triṣṭubh. Its first and third pādas (इति सम्प्रविचार्य राजसिंहः and विससर्ज मुनीन् नृपांश्च सर्वान्) were missing from the packet and were read off the page and added. The packet had taken the closing colophon for a verse and numbered it 95; it is the sarga colophon and was removed from the verse sequence. The Gītā Press colophon numbers this sarga 95 (पञ्चनवतितमः); the corpus files it as sarga 97.

Verse count: 17, plus the closing colophon printed unnumbered.

--- verse 1 ---
deva:
रामो बहून्यहान्येव तद् गीतं परमं शुभम् ।
शुश्राव मुनिभिः सार्धं पार्थिवैः सह वानरैः ॥
iast:
rāmo bahūnyahānyeva tad gītaṁ paramaṁ śubham |
śuśrāva munibhiḥ sārdhaṁ pārthivaiḥ saha vānaraiḥ ||

--- verse 2 ---
deva:
तस्मिन् गीते तु विज्ञाय सीतापुत्रौ कुशीलवौ ।
तस्याः परिषदो मध्ये रामो वचनमब्रवीत् ॥
iast:
tasmin gīte tu vijñāya sītāputrau kuśīlavau |
tasyāḥ pariṣado madhye rāmo vacanamabravīt ||

--- verse 3 ---
deva:
दूतान् शुद्धसमाचारानाहूयात्ममनीषया ।
मद् वचो ब्रूत गच्छध्वमितो भगवतोऽन्तिके ॥
iast:
dūtān śuddhasamācārānāhūyātmamanīṣayā |
mad vaco brūta gacchadhvamito bhagavato'ntike ||

--- verse 4 ---
deva:
यदि शुद्धसमाचारा यदि वा वीतकल्मषा ।
करोत्विहात्मनः शुद्धिमनुमान्य महामुनिम् ॥
iast:
yadi śuddhasamācārā yadi vā vītakalmaṣā |
karotvihātmanaḥ śuddhimanumānya mahāmunim ||

--- verse 5 ---
deva:
छन्दं मुनेश्च विज्ञाय सीतायाश्च मनोगतम् ।
प्रत्ययं दातुकामायास्ततः शंसत मे लघु ॥
iast:
chandaṁ muneśca vijñāya sītāyāśca manogatam |
pratyayaṁ dātukāmāyāstataḥ śaṁsata me laghu ||

--- verse 6 ---
deva:
श्वः प्रभाते तु शपथं मैथिली जनकात्मजा ।
करोतु परिषन्मध्ये शोधनार्थं ममैव च ॥
iast:
śvaḥ prabhāte tu śapathaṁ maithilī janakātmajā |
karotu pariṣanmadhye śodhanārthaṁ mamaiva ca ||

--- verse 7 ---
deva:
श्रुत्वा तु राघवस्यैतद् वचः परममद्भुतम् ।
दूताः सम्प्रययुर्बाढं यत्र वै मुनिपुङ्गवः ॥
iast:
śrutvā tu rāghavasyaitad vacaḥ paramamadbhutam |
dūtāḥ samprayayurbāḍhaṁ yatra vai munipuṅgavaḥ ||

--- verse 8 ---
deva:
ते प्रणम्य महात्मानं ज्वलन्तममितप्रभम् ।
ऊचुस्ते रामवाक्यानि मृदूनि मधुराणि च ॥
iast:
te praṇamya mahātmānaṁ jvalantamamitaprabham |
ūcuste rāmavākyāni mṛdūni madhurāṇi ca ||

--- verse 9 ---
deva:
तेषां तद् भाषितं श्रुत्वा रामस्य च मनोगतम् ।
विज्ञाय सुमहातेजा मुनिर्वाक्यमथाब्रवीत् ॥
iast:
teṣāṁ tad bhāṣitaṁ śrutvā rāmasya ca manogatam |
vijñāya sumahātejā munirvākyamathābravīt ||

--- verse 10 ---
deva:
एवं भवतु भद्रं वो यथा वदति राघवः ।
तथा करिष्यते सीता दैवतं हि पतिः स्त्रियाः ॥
iast:
evaṁ bhavatu bhadraṁ vo yathā vadati rāghavaḥ |
tathā kariṣyate sītā daivataṁ hi patiḥ striyāḥ ||

--- verse 11 ---
deva:
तथोक्ता मुनिना सर्वे राजदूता महौजसम् ।
प्रत्येत्य राघवं सर्वं मुनिवाक्यं बभाषिरे ॥
iast:
tathoktā muninā sarve rājadūtā mahaujasam |
pratyetya rāghavaṁ sarvaṁ munivākyaṁ babhāṣire ||

--- verse 12 ---
deva:
ततः प्रहृष्टः काकुत्स्थः श्रुत्वा वाक्यं महात्मनः ।
ऋषींस्तत्र समेतांश्च राज्ञश्चैवाभ्यभाषत ॥
iast:
tataḥ prahṛṣṭaḥ kākutsthaḥ śrutvā vākyaṁ mahātmanaḥ |
ṛṣīṁstatra sametāṁśca rājñaścaivābhyabhāṣata ||

--- verse 13 ---
deva:
भगवन्तः सशिष्या वै सानुगाश्च नराधिपाः ।
पश्यन्तु सीताशपथं यश्चैवान्योऽपि कांक्षते ॥
iast:
bhagavantaḥ saśiṣyā vai sānugāśca narādhipāḥ |
paśyantu sītāśapathaṁ yaścaivānyo'pi kāṁkṣate ||

--- verse 14 ---
deva:
तस्य तद् वचनं श्रुत्वा राघवस्य महात्मनः ।
सर्वेषामृषिमुख्यानां साधुवादो महानभूत् ॥
iast:
tasya tad vacanaṁ śrutvā rāghavasya mahātmanaḥ |
sarveṣāmṛṣimukhyānāṁ sādhuvādo mahānabhūt ||

--- verse 15 ---
deva:
राजानश्च महात्मानं प्रशंसन्ति स्म राघवम् ।
उपपन्नं नरश्रेष्ठ त्वय्येव भुवि नान्यतः ॥
iast:
rājānaśca mahātmānaṁ praśaṁsanti sma rāghavam |
upapannaṁ naraśreṣṭha tvayyeva bhuvi nānyataḥ ||

--- verse 16 ---
deva:
एवं विनिश्चयं कृत्वा श्वोभूत इति राघवः ।
विसर्जयामास तदा सर्वांस्ताञ्छत्रुसूदनः ॥
iast:
evaṁ viniścayaṁ kṛtvā śvobhūta iti rāghavaḥ |
visarjayāmāsa tadā sarvāṁstāñchatrusūdanaḥ ||

--- verse 17 ---
deva:
इति सम्प्रविचार्य राजसिंहः ।
श्वोभूते शपथस्य निश्चयम् ।
विससर्ज मुनीन् नृपांश्च सर्वान् ।
स महात्मा महतो महानुभावः ॥
iast:
iti sampravicārya rājasiṁhaḥ |
śvobhūte śapathasya niścayam |
visasarja munīn nṛpāṁśca sarvān |
sa mahātmā mahato mahānubhāvaḥ ||
