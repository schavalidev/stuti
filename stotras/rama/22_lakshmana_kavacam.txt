Blurb: This is the companion armour to the Rāma and Sītā kavacas already in this folder, from the same dialogue between Sutīkṣṇa and Agastya in the Ānanda Rāmāyaṇa. Its portrait is the most consistent of the three. Every epithet in the dhyāna, and most in the kavaca, define Lakṣmaṇa by his relation to someone else. He stands behind Rāma. His eyes are fixed on Rāma. He holds the parasol above Rāma's head. He carries Rāma's bow and quiver. He does what Sītā tells him, which is sītāvākyakaraḥ. He is sleepless, vinidraḥ, so that the others may sleep. Set against these are the epithets of his other identity: Śeṣa, the thousand-mouthed, the king of serpents, the bearer of the terrestrial globe, the father of Citraketu. The same list therefore holds both the attendant who never sits down and the cosmic snake upon whom the world rests. The phalaśruti presses the point about companionship with an unusually blunt comparison: reciting Rāma's armour without Lakṣmaṇa's is like offering food with the ghee left out, and Raghunandana will not be pleased by the one recited alone.

--- verse 1 | section: Mangalacaranam ---
deva:
अगस्त्य उवाच ।
सौमित्रिं रघुनायकस्य चरणद्वन्द्वेक्षणं श्यामलं
बिभ्रन्तं स्वकरेण रामशिरसि च्छत्रं विचित्राम्बरम् ।
बिभ्रन्तं रघुनायकस्य सुमहत्कोदण्डबाणासने
तं वन्दे कमलेक्षणं जनकजावाक्ये सदा तत्परम् ॥
iast:
agastya uvāca |
saumitriṁ raghunāyakasya caraṇadvandvekṣaṇaṁ śyāmalaṁ
bibhrantaṁ svakareṇa rāmaśirasi cchatraṁ vicitrāmbaram |
bibhrantaṁ raghunāyakasya sumahatkodaṇḍabāṇāsane
taṁ vande kamalekṣaṇaṁ janakajāvākye sadā tatparam ||
en: Agastya said: Saumitri, dark of hue, his gaze resting on the two feet of the lord of the Raghus; holding in his own hand a many-hued parasol above Rāma's head; bearing the lord of the Raghus' own great Kodaṇḍa bow and quiver — him I salute, the lotus-eyed, ever attentive to the word of Janaka's daughter.
tel: అగస్త్యుడు పలికెను: రఘునాయకుని పాదద్వయమునందే దృష్టి నిలిపినవాడు, శ్యామవర్ణుడు, తన చేతితో రాముని శిరముపై విచిత్రమైన ఛత్రమును ధరించినవాడు, రఘునాయకుని మహాకోదండమును బాణాసనమును మోయువాడు, కమలనేత్రుడు, జానకి మాటయందు ఎల్లప్పుడూ తత్పరుడు అయిన సౌమిత్రికి నేను నమస్కరించుచున్నాను.
hi: अगस्त्य ने कहा: जिनकी दृष्टि रघुनायक के चरणयुगल पर टिकी है, जो श्यामल हैं, जो अपने हाथ से राम के शिर पर विचित्र छत्र धारण किए हुए हैं, जो रघुनायक का महान् कोदण्ड और बाणासन उठाए हुए हैं, जो कमलनयन हैं और जनकपुत्री के वचन में सदा तत्पर हैं — उन सौमित्रि को मैं प्रणाम करता हूँ।

रामस्य मस्तके दिव्यं बिभ्रन्तं छत्रमुत्तमम् ।

rāmasya mastake divyaṁ bibhrantaṁ chatramuttamam |

--- verse 5 | section: Dhyanam ---
deva:
एवं ध्यात्वा लक्ष्मणं च राघवन्यस्तलोचनम् ।
कवचं जपनीयं हि ततो भक्त्यात्र मानवैः ॥
iast:
evaṁ dhyātvā lakṣmaṇaṁ ca rāghavanyastalocanam |
kavacaṁ japanīyaṁ hi tato bhaktyātra mānavaiḥ ||
en: — having meditated in this way on Lakṣmaṇa, whose eyes are fixed upon Rāghava, people should then recite this armour here with devotion.
tel: ఇట్లు రాఘవునిపై దృష్టి నిలిపిన లక్ష్మణుని ధ్యానించి, తరువాత మనుష్యులు భక్తితో ఈ కవచమును జపించవలెను.
hi: इस प्रकार राघव पर दृष्टि टिकाए हुए लक्ष्मण का ध्यान करके, फिर मनुष्यों को भक्तिपूर्वक इस कवच का जप करना चाहिए।

--- verse 6 | section: Kavacam ---
deva:
लक्ष्मणः पातु मे पूर्वे दक्षिणे राघवानुजः ।
प्रतीच्यां पातु सौमित्रिः पातूदीच्यां रघूत्तमः ॥
iast:
lakṣmaṇaḥ pātu me pūrve dakṣiṇe rāghavānujaḥ |
pratīcyāṁ pātu saumitriḥ pātūdīcyāṁ raghūttamaḥ ||
en: May Lakṣmaṇa protect me in the east, and Rāghava's younger brother in the south; may Saumitri protect me in the west, and the best of the Raghus in the north.
tel: తూర్పున లక్ష్మణుడు నన్ను రక్షించుగాక, దక్షిణమున రాఘవానుజుడు రక్షించుగాక, పశ్చిమమున సౌమిత్రి రక్షించుగాక, ఉత్తరమున రఘూత్తముడు రక్షించుగాక.
hi: पूर्व में लक्ष्मण मेरी रक्षा करें, दक्षिण में राघवानुज रक्षा करें, पश्चिम में सौमित्रि रक्षा करें और उत्तर में रघूत्तम रक्षा करें।

hi: नीचे महावीर रक्षा करें, ऊपर नृपात्मज रक्षा करें, मध्य में रामदास रक्षा करें और सब ओर सत्यपालक रक्षा करें।

--- verse 13 | section: Kavacam ---

--- verse 14 | section: Kavacam ---

Recension note: The two stotranidhi editions (IAST and Devanāgarī) are identical throughout, including the viniyoga and nyāsa prose, which is omitted here per the convention already used for files 02 and 21. The source prints no verse numbers, so the numbering above is this file's own; v. 19 is a hinge verse whose first half completes the kavaca (protection at all times) and whose second half opens the phalaśruti (Agastya's address to Sutīkṣṇa), and it is placed in the Kavacam section on that account. One printing artifact was removed: both editions carry a stray numeral — "८" in the Devanāgarī, "8" in the IAST — at the end of the first half-line of v. 17 (mama pādāṅgulīḥ sadā |8), evidently a leftover verse number from a numbered exemplar; since this corpus does not carry verse numbers inside the text, it has simply been dropped. Lakṣmaṇa's Śeṣa identity supplies several epithets that make no sense of the human brother — sahasrāsyaḥ (v. 15), nāgendraḥ and citraketupitā (vv. 16–17, Citraketu being Śeṣa's son in purāṇic genealogy), and bhūgoladhārī (v. 18) — and these are translated in that register. Note also aṅgadatātaḥ in v. 16: the Aṅgada meant is Lakṣmaṇa's own son by Ūrmilā, not Vālin's son of the same name who appears in file 15.
