Verse count: 11

Blurb: A syncretic hymn addressed jointly to Śiva and Rāma — and, within individual verses, to Hari, Kṛṣṇa and Mādhava as well. Its refrain, "śiva hare vijayaṁ kuru me varam" ("O Śiva, O Hari, grant me the boon of victory"), and its epithets move fluidly between Śaiva imagery (Hara, bearer of the celestial Gaṅgā, Śaṅkara) and Vaiṣṇava imagery (lotus-eyed Rāma, Kṛṣṇa, Rāghava beloved of Sītā, protector of Gajendra), presenting the two as a single object of devotion. The closing verses promise victory and, ultimately, the vision of Viṣṇu to whoever recites it daily at dawn.

--- verse 1 | section: Shivaramashtakam ---

शिव हरे विजयं कुरु मे वरम् ॥

ajajaneśvarayādava pāhi māṁ
śiva hare vijayaṁ kuru me varam ||
en: O Śiva, O Hari, O friend of Śiva and Rāma, O Lord; O remover of the three kinds of affliction, O all-pervading one; O lord of unborn Brahmā and of the Yādavas, protect me — O Śiva, O Hari, grant me the boon of victory.
tel: ఓ శివా, హరీ, శివరాముల మిత్రుడా, ప్రభూ; త్రివిధతాపములను పోగొట్టు ఓ విభూ; అజుడు (బ్రహ్మ), యాదవుల ప్రభువా, నన్ను రక్షింపుము — ఓ శివా, హరీ, నాకు విజయమును వరముగా ప్రసాదింపుము.
hi: हे शिव, हरि, शिव-राम के मित्र, प्रभो; त्रिविध तापों को दूर करने वाले हे विभो; अज (ब्रह्मा) और यादवों के स्वामी, मेरी रक्षा करो — हे शिव, हरि, मुझे विजय का वरदान दो।

--- verse 4 | section: Shivaramashtakam ---

hi: जय हो, युधिष्ठिर के प्रिय राजन्; जय जय, अर्जित पुण्य के सागर; जय हो, कृपामय कृष्ण, तुम्हें नमस्कार — हे शिव, हरि, मुझे विजय का वरदान दो।
