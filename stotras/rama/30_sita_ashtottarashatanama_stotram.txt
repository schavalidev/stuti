Recension note: **Gītā Press followed throughout; nothing is adopted from elsewhere and no reading is emended.** **Name count 111, not 108.** The nāmāvalī counts every word the stotra offers as a name. **The Ānanda Rāmāyaṇa's own commentary numbers the same names 1–108**, and the three splits that make the whole difference can now be named. It does not count `वरा` in v. 16 as a name, where Gītā Press does (no. 106). And it counts v. 17 `दिव्यचन्दनसंस्था श्रीर्मूलकासुरमर्दिनी` as one name, its no. 108, where Gītā Press makes three (nos. 109–111). Those three splits are the difference: 111 − 3 = 108. The stotra says `एवमष्टोत्तरशतं` in v. 17, so the 108 is the text's own count and the 111 is the nāmāvalī's way of laying it out. From v. 3 `मा श्रीः` it takes two names, no. 19 `मायै` and no. 20 `श्रियै`. **Duplicates:** `गौर्यै` (nos. 66 and 70, from vv. 9 and 10) and `श्रियै` (nos. 20 and 110, from vv. 3 and 17). **Stotra and nāmāvalī agree throughout**, with these forms: v. 1 `रमावनिसुता` → nos. 6–7 `रमायै` + `अवनिसुतायै`; v. 13 `रामचन्द्रैकरञ्जनी` → no. 88 `रामचन्द्रैकरञ्जिन्यै`; v. 17 `श्रीर्मूलकासुरमर्दिनी` → nos. 110–111 `श्रियै` + `मूलकासुरमर्दिन्यै`. No. 108 `मातुलुङ्गकरे धृतायै` keeps the phrase of v. 16 whole, locative included; in the index it is written with its internal space as the print has it. **Mūlakāsura.** The demon Mūlaka, whom Sītā kills, belongs to the Ānanda Rāmāyaṇa and not to Vālmīki; the viniyoga makes `मूलकासुरमर्दिनी` the supreme mantra of the text. **The citron.** `मातुलुङ्गी` (no. 11) and `मातुलुङ्गकरे धृता` (no. 108) refer to the citron she holds, and the viniyoga makes it her śakti. **One possible variant, not adopted.** In v. 13 Gītā Press prints `रामवामाङ्कसंस्था`, "seated on Rāma's lap". The Ānanda Rāmāyaṇa text and its Hindi gloss appear to read `रामवामाङ्गसंस्था`, "abiding at Rāma's left side". The difference is one letter, `क` against `ग`, and the witness is an OCR of that edition. An OCR is good enough to locate a disputed reading and not good enough to settle one, so Gītā Press is followed and nothing here is changed; a page image of that edition would settle it. **Cross-reference:** `rama/29_sita_sahasranama_stotram.txt` is Sītā's thousand names, a different work; the Ānanda Rāmāyaṇa is not the source of that file.

Blurb: Sītā named through her whole story: the citron in her hand, the golden deer her mind ran after, the aśoka grove, the fire she lived in, Vālmīki's hermitage and the Sarayū. She is also Annapūrṇā, Mahālakṣmī and Sarasvatī, weighed against gold, her feet revered by Hanumān, and the slayer of the demon Mūlaka, an episode this Rāmāyaṇa alone tells.

--- verse 1 | section: Viniyogaḥ ---
deva:
अस्य श्रीसीतानामाष्टोत्तरमन्त्रस्य अगस्तिर्ऋषिः । अनुष्टुप्छन्दः । रमेति बीजम् । मातुलुङ्गीति शक्तिः । पद्माक्षजेति कीलकम् । अवनिजेत्यस्त्रम् । जनकजेति कवचम् । मूलकासुरमर्दिनीति परमो मन्त्रः । श्रीसीतारामचन्द्रप्रीत्यर्थं सकलकामनासिद्ध्यर्थं जपे विनियोगः ।
iast:
asya śrīsītānāmāṣṭottaramantrasya agastirṛṣiḥ | anuṣṭupchandaḥ | rameti bījam | mātuluṅgīti śaktiḥ | padmākṣajeti kīlakam | avanijetyastram | janakajeti kavacam | mūlakāsuramardinīti paramo mantraḥ | śrīsītārāmacandraprītyarthaṁ sakalakāmanāsiddhyarthaṁ jape viniyogaḥ |
en: Of this mantra of the hundred and eight names of Śrī Sītā, the seer is Agasti, the metre anuṣṭubh; 'Ramā' is the seed, 'Mātuluṅgī' the power, 'Padmākṣajā' the pin, 'Avanijā' the weapon, 'Janakajā' the armour, and 'Mūlakāsuramardinī' the supreme mantra. It is applied in japa to please Śrī Sītā and Rāmacandra and to accomplish every desire.
tel: ఈ శ్రీసీతానామాష్టోత్తరమంత్రమునకు అగస్త్యుడు ఋషి, అనుష్టుప్ ఛందస్సు; రమ అని బీజము, మాతులుంగి అని శక్తి, పద్మాక్షజ అని కీలకము, అవనిజ అని అస్త్రము, జనకజ అని కవచము, మూలకాసురమర్దిని అని పరమమంత్రము; శ్రీసీతారామచంద్రప్రీతికై, సకలకామనాసిద్ధికై జపమునందు వినియోగము.
hi: इस श्रीसीतानामाष्टोत्तर मन्त्रके अगस्ति ऋषि, अनुष्टुप् छन्द, 'रमा' बीज, 'मातुलुङ्गी' शक्ति, 'पद्माक्षजा' कीलक, 'अवनिजा' अस्त्र, 'जनकजा' कवच और 'मूलकासुरमर्दिनी' परम मन्त्र है; श्रीसीता-रामचन्द्रकी प्रीतिके लिये तथा समस्त कामनाओंकी सिद्धिके लिये जपमें इसका विनियोग है।

deva:

ॐ सीतायै हृदयाय नमः । ॐ रमायै शिरसे स्वाहा । ॐ मातुलुङ्ग्यै शिखायै वषट् । ॐ पद्माक्षजायै नेत्रत्रयाय वौषट् । ॐ जनकात्मजायै अस्त्राय फट् । ॐ मूलकासुरमर्दिन्यै इति दिग्बन्धः ।

en: Placement on the heart and the rest: 'to Sītā' on the heart (namaḥ), 'to Ramā' on the head (svāhā), 'to Mātuluṅgī' on the topknot (vaṣaṭ), 'to Padmākṣajā' on the three eyes (vauṣaṭ), 'to Janakātmajā' on the weapon (phaṭ); with 'to Mūlakāsuramardinī' the quarters are bound.
