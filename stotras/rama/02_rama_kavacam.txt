Title: Śrī Rāma Kavacam
Devanāgarī: श्रीरामकवचम्
Telugu: శ్రీ రామ కవచం

Author: Sage Agastya (traditional attribution; narrated by Agastya to Sutīkṣṇa)

Source / recension: https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_raama/raamakavachaAnanda.html (Śrīmad Ānanda Rāmāyaṇe Manoharakāṇḍāntargataṁ Śrīrāmakavacam, from the Manoharakāṇḍa, sarga 13)

Verse count: 34

Sections:
రామనామ మాహాత్మ్యం — Rāmanāma Māhātmyam, the glory of the name Rāma (vv. 1–6)
ధ్యానం — Dhyānam, meditation on Rāma in his martial, battlefield form (vv. 7–15)
కవచం — Kavacam proper, assigning each limb and faculty to a protecting epithet (vv. 16–31)
ఫలశ్రుతి — Phalaśruti, the fruits of recitation (vv. 32–34)

Blurb: The sage Agastya narrates this kavaca to Sutīkṣṇa in the Manoharakāṇḍa of the Ānanda Rāmāyaṇa. It opens philosophically, identifying the very word 'Rāma' with the non-dual, formless Brahman. It then turns to a martial dhyāna of Rāma upon Indra's chariot, amid the roar of the vānara army fighting Rāvaṇa's host. The kavaca proper works down the body, and then through the subtler faculties of speech, of mind and of karma, assigning each to a different epithet of Rāma. The colophon notes that it is one of six companion kavacas, for Hanumān, Lakṣmaṇa, Sītā, Rāma, Bharata and Śatrughna, which are traditionally recited together. This one alone is prescribed when the full set cannot be recited.
