--- verse 45 ---

--- verse 46 ---

--- verse 47 ---

--- verse 48 ---

iast:
yāvad gacchāmi saumitre mṛgamānayituṁ drutam |

--- verse 49 ---

iast:

--- verse 50 ---

iast:
yāvat pṛṣatamekena sāyakena nihanmyaham |

--- verse 51 ---

प्रदक्षिणेनातिबलेन पक्षिणा जटायुषा बुद्धिमता च लक्ष्मण ।

iast:
