Blurb: This is almost certainly the most widely sung Rāma stuti in North India, and its whole effect comes from its diction. Tulasīdāsa writes Awadhi so saturated with Sanskrit vocabulary that a listener who knows only Sanskrit can follow most of it, while the grammar and the rhyme are vernacular throughout. The first verse spends itself entirely on a single word, kañja, meaning lotus, repeated four times in one line for the eyes, the face, the hands and the feet, so that the image accumulates rather than argues. The second reaches for two comparisons at once: a beauty like countless Kāmadevas, and a yellow garment like lightning upon a dark monsoon cloud. The third and fourth run through lineage and ornament, and the one battle-feat the poem admits. The fifth breaks the frame. The poet names himself, and then asks not for heaven or release but for Rāma to come and live in the lotus of his heart, which is the same lotus that the first verse had used for Rāma's own body.

--- verse 1 | section: Rama Stuti (Tulasidasa Krtam) ---
deva:
श्री रामचन्द्र कृपालु भजु मन हरण भव भय दारुणं ।
नवकञ्ज लोचन कञ्ज मुख कर कञ्ज पद कञ्जारुणम् ॥
iast:
śrī rāmacandra kṛpālu bhaju mana haraṇa bhava bhaya dāruṇaṁ |
navakañja locana kañja mukha kara kañja pada kañjāruṇam ||
en: O mind, worship the compassionate Śrī Rāmacandra, who takes away the dreadful fear of birth and death — whose eyes are fresh lotuses, whose face is a lotus, whose hands are lotuses, whose feet are lotuses red as the dawn.
tel: ఓ మనసా! భవభయమనే భయంకరమైన దానిని హరించు కృపాళువైన శ్రీరామచంద్రుని భజించుము — నూతన కమలముల వంటి కన్నులు, కమలము వంటి ముఖము, కమలముల వంటి చేతులు, అరుణకమలముల వంటి పాదములు కలవాడు ఆయన.
hi: हे मन! कृपालु श्रीरामचन्द्र का भजन कर, जो संसार के भयंकर भय को हर लेते हैं — जिनके नेत्र नवीन कमल जैसे हैं, मुख कमल जैसा है, हाथ कमल जैसे हैं और चरण अरुण कमल जैसे हैं।

--- verse 2 | section: Rama Stuti (Tulasidasa Krtam) ---
deva:
कन्दर्प अगणित अमित छवि नव नील नीरज सुन्दरं ।
वटपीत मानहु तडित रुचि शुचि नौमि जनक सुतावरम् ॥
iast:
kandarpa agaṇita amita chavi nava nīla nīraja sundaraṁ |
vaṭapīta mānahu taḍita ruci śuci naumi janaka sutāvaram ||
en: His radiance beyond measure, as though of countless Kāmadevas; beautiful as a fresh blue lotus; his yellow garment as if it were the flash of lightning — pure and shining. I bow to the noble husband of Janaka's daughter.
tel: లెక్కింపరాని కందర్పుల వంటి అమితమైన కాంతి కలవాడు, నూతన నీలకమలము వలె సుందరుడు, పీతాంబరము మెరుపు కాంతివలె ప్రకాశించువాడు, పవిత్రుడు — జనకపుత్రిక పతియైన ఆ శ్రేష్ఠునికి నేను నమస్కరించుచున్నాను.
hi: जिनकी छवि असंख्य कामदेवों से भी अधिक अपार है, जो नवीन नील कमल के समान सुन्दर हैं, जिनका पीत वस्त्र मानो बिजली की चमक हो — ऐसे पवित्र, जनकनन्दिनी के वर को मैं नमन करता हूँ।

--- verse 3 | section: Rama Stuti (Tulasidasa Krtam) ---
deva:
भजु दीन बन्धु दिनेश दानव दैत्यवम्शनिकन्दनं ।
रघुनन्द आनन्दकन्द कौशल चन्द दशरथ नन्दनम् ॥
iast:
bhaju dīna bandhu dineśa dānava daityavamśanikandanaṁ |
raghunanda ānandakanda kauśala canda daśaratha nandanam ||
en: Worship him — friend of the destitute, lord of the day, uprooter of the whole race of dānavas and daityas; the joy of Raghu's line, the very root of bliss, the moon of Kosala, the delight of Daśaratha.
tel: దీనబంధువు, దినేశుడు, దానవ దైత్యవంశములను నిర్మూలించువాడు, రఘునందనుడు, ఆనందకందుడు, కోసలదేశపు చంద్రుడు, దశరథనందనుడు — ఆయనను భజించుము.
hi: भजन कर उनका — जो दीनों के बन्धु हैं, दिनेश (सूर्यवंशी) हैं, दानव और दैत्यवंश का समूल नाश करने वाले हैं; रघुकुल के आनन्द, आनन्द के कन्द, कोसल के चन्द्रमा, दशरथनन्दन हैं।

--- verse 4 | section: Rama Stuti (Tulasidasa Krtam) ---
deva:
शिर मुकुट कुण्डल तिलक चारु उदार अङ्ग विभूषणं ।
आजानुभुज शरचापधर सङ्ग्राम जित करदूषणम् ॥
iast:
śira mukuṭa kuṇḍala tilaka cāru udāra aṅga vibhūṣaṇaṁ |

en: A crown upon his head, earrings, a lovely tilaka, his generous limbs decked with ornaments; arms reaching to his knees, bearing arrow and bow, victor in battle over Khara and Dūṣaṇa.
tel: శిరమున కిరీటము, కుండలములు, మనోహరమైన తిలకము, ఉదారమైన అంగములపై ఆభరణములు; ఆజానుబాహువులు, శరచాపములు ధరించినవాడు, సంగ్రామమున ఖరదూషణులను జయించినవాడు.
hi: सिर पर मुकुट, कानों में कुण्डल, सुन्दर तिलक, उदार अंगों पर आभूषण; घुटनों तक लम्बी भुजाएँ, बाण और धनुष धारण किए हुए, संग्राम में खर-दूषण को जीतने वाले।

Recension note: LANGUAGE FLAG — this text is Awadhi (Sanskritized Awadhi), not Sanskrit; see the Source / recension paragraph above for how the deva:, iast: and hi: fields are handled in consequence. Two readings in the stotranidhi text differ from the reading most commonly printed in Vinaya Patrikā editions and are reproduced here as the source has them, in both its editions (so they are recension readings of this site's text, not one-off typos): v. 2 वटपीत / vaṭapīta where the usual printed reading is पट पीत / paṭa pīta, "yellow cloth" (the translation follows the sense "yellow garment", which both readings support); and v. 4 करदूषणम् / karadūṣaṇam where the usual reading is खरदूषणं / kharadūṣaṇaṁ, the demons Khara and Dūṣaṇa, which is how the line is rendered above since no other sense fits saṅgrāma jita, "conquered in battle". A third orthographic point: v. 3 prints दैत्यवम्श- (m + śa) where standard spelling has दैत्यवंश- (anusvāra); this is a spelling variant only, and the transliteration follows the printed form.
