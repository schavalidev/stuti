Blurb: Composed entirely in the flowing bhujaṅgaprayāta ("serpent-gliding") meter traditionally attributed to Ādi Śaṅkarācārya, this stotra presents Rāma simultaneously as the personal, saguṇa Rāmacandra of the Rāmāyaṇa — seated with Sītā and Lakṣmaṇa, attended by Hanumān — and as the impersonal, nirguṇa Brahman, "beyond form, beyond a base for anything." Its most distinctive stretch (vv. 23–25) is a triple negative-particle (a-) construction listing what Rāma's true nature is not, alongside repeated cries of prasīda ("be gracious") and a direct plea for fearlessness in the face of death (v. 22).

--- verse 1 ---
deva:
विशुद्धं परं सच्चिदानन्दरूपं
गुणाधारमाधारहीनं वरेण्यम् ।
महान्तं विभान्तं गुहान्तं गुणान्तं
सुखान्तं स्वयं धाम रामं प्रपद्ये ॥
iast:
viśuddhaṁ paraṁ saccidānandarūpaṁ
guṇādhāramādhārahīnaṁ vareṇyam |
mahāntaṁ vibhāntaṁ guhāntaṁ guṇāntaṁ
sukhāntaṁ svayaṁ dhāma rāmaṁ prapadye ||
en: I take refuge in Rāma — pure, supreme, whose very form is being-consciousness-bliss; the basis of all qualities yet himself without any base, the most excellent; great, resplendent, dwelling in the cave of the heart, the end of all qualities, the end of all happiness, himself the self-luminous abode.
tel: శుద్ధుడు, పరుడు, సచ్చిదానందస్వరూపుడు; గుణములకు ఆధారమైనవాడు, తనకు ఆధారము లేనివాడు, శ్రేష్ఠుడు; గొప్పవాడు, ప్రకాశించువాడు, హృదయగుహలో నివసించువాడు, గుణములకు అంతమైనవాడు, సుఖమునకు అంతమైనవాడు, స్వయంప్రకాశమైన ధామమైన రామునికి శరణు వేడుచున్నాను.
hi: शुद्ध, परम, सच्चिदानंदस्वरूप; गुणों के आधार किन्तु स्वयं निराधार, श्रेष्ठ; महान, प्रकाशमान, हृदयगुहा में निवास करने वाले, गुणों के अंत, सुख के अंत, स्वयं-प्रकाश धाम राम की मैं शरण लेता हूँ।
