Blurb: The vānaras go with Rāma to the Prasravaṇa mountain and begin to tell what happened. Rāma asks the questions that matter to him: where is Sītā, and how is she disposed towards him. The vānaras urge Hanumān to speak, and he bows his head in the direction where Sītā is before he begins. He tells of crossing the sea, of Laṅkā on the southern shore, and of finding her in the pleasure grove guarded by rākṣasīs, wearing one garment, sleeping on the bare ground, her limbs pale like a lotus plant at the end of the cold season, her mind fixed on Rāma alone. He tells how she came to trust him, and how she rejoiced at the friendship of Rāma and Sugrīva. Then he gives the token she sent, the account of what happened near Rāma at Citrakūṭa, and the crest jewel she kept safe. He repeats her words, that beyond a month she will not live. Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa are comforted.
