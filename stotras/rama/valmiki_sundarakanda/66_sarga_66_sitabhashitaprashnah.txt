Blurb: This is a short sarga, and it holds one action only. Rāma takes the crest jewel that Sītā sent, presses it to himself and weeps. The poet says that his tears came as milk comes from a loving cow. He tells Lakṣmaṇa that this jewel was given to Vaidehī by his father-in-law Janaka at the time of the wedding, and that it used to shine upon her head. He says that seeing it he feels he has seen her herself. Then he asks Hanumān to tell again and again what Sītā said, for those words are the medicine by which he will live. He says that she will have lived long if she can hold on for a month, and he asks to be taken to the place where she was seen. He ends by asking how she, who was always timid, stands in the midst of fearful rākṣasas, and what that sweet lady of sweet speech said to him.

tel: ఓ హనుమంతుడా, సీతమ్మ ఏమి పలికెను? నేడు నాకు యథార్థముగా చెప్పుము. రోగి ఔషధముచే జీవించునట్లు నేను ఈ మాటలచేతనే జీవింతును.
hi: हे हनुमान जी, सीता जी ने क्या कहा? आज मुझे यथार्थ रूप से बताओ। जैसे रोगी औषध से जीवित रहता है, वैसे ही मैं इन्हीं वचनों से जीवित रहूँगा।

--- verse 15 | section: How does she live among the rākṣasas ---
deva:
मधुरा मधुरालापा किमाह मम भामिनी ।
मद्विहीना वरारोहा हनुमन्कथयस्व मे ॥
iast:
madhurā madhurālāpā kimāha mama bhāminī |
madvihīnā varārohā hanumankathayasva me ||
en: That sweet lady of sweet speech, my beautiful one — what did she say? She of fair form, parted from me — tell me, Hanumān.
tel: మధురమైనది, మధురముగా మాటలాడునది అగు నా భామిని ఏమి పలికెను? నా నుండి ఎడబాసిన ఆ వరారోహ మాటలను నాకు చెప్పుము, హనుమంతుడా.
hi: मधुर स्वभाव वाली और मधुर वचन बोलने वाली मेरी उस भामिनी ने क्या कहा? मुझसे बिछुड़ी हुई उस सुन्दरी की बात मुझे बताओ, हे हनुमान जी।
