Blurb: This is the last sarga of the Sundarakāṇḍa. Hanumān repeats to Rāma the close of what passed between him and Sītā. She asked him to stay near for a little while, because his going would leave her grief heavier than before. She then raised her doubt plainly. Only three beings can leap this ocean, Garuḍa, the wind and Hanumān himself, so how will the armies of vānaras and bears cross it, and how will the two sons of Daśaratha come. She said that he alone could carry her, but that she did not wish it, because she was taken from the forest by deceit and it would not be fitting for Rāma to recover her by stealth. She asked that Rāma come with his army and take her by his own valour. Hanumān repeats his answer. Sugrīva commands vānaras whose going is checked neither above nor below nor across, and among them there are many equal to him and better. He tells her that she will soon see Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa, the vānara host on the slopes of Mount Malaya, and Rāma’s exile at an end. The sarga closes with Sītā comforted, and with that the Sundarakāṇḍa ends.

deva:
इत्यार्षे श्रीमद्रामायणे वाल्मीकीये आदिकाव्ये सुन्दरकाण्डे अष्टषष्टितमः सर्गः
iast:
ityārṣe śrīmadrāmāyaṇe vālmīkīye ādikāvye sundarakāṇḍe aṣṭaṣaṣṭitamaḥ sargaḥ
en: Thus ends the sixty-eighth sarga, in the Sundarakāṇḍa of the holy Rāmāyaṇa of Vālmīki, the first poem, revealed by the sage.
tel: ఈ విధముగా ఋషిప్రోక్తమైన, ఆదికావ్యమైన శ్రీమద్వాల్మీకి రామాయణమునందలి సుందరకాండలో అష్టషష్టితమ సర్గ ముగిసినది.
hi: इस प्रकार ऋषिप्रणीत आदिकाव्य श्रीमद्वाल्मीकीय रामायण के सुन्दरकाण्ड में अष्टषष्टितम सर्ग समाप्त हुआ।
