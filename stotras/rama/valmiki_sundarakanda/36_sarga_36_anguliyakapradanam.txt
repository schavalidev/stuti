Blurb: The doubt ends with an object. Hanumān produces the ring marked with Rāma's name, and Sītā, taking it, is as glad as though she had gained her husband himself. Then she praises the messenger, observing shrewdly that Rāma would not have sent an untried man to her, so his being here is itself proof. What follows is the longest run of questions in the kāṇḍa. She asks whether Rāma is distressed, whether he falters in his tasks, whether he still keeps friends and is honoured by them, whether he uses the twofold and threefold means of policy, whether he holds to both human effort and destiny, and whether his love for her has been worn away by her long absence. The questions are those of a queen, not only of a wife. Hanumān answers with the detail that settles it: Rāma eats no flesh and takes no honey, does not brush the insects from his own body, sleeps and wakes saying her name, and speaks to her aloud when he sees a flower. The sarga ends with her grief lifted and yet made one with his, like a night in early autumn with the moon still half behind cloud.

tel: శ్రీరాముని కీర్తనముచే శోకము తొలగినను, శ్రీరాముని శోకముతో సమానమైన శోకము గల ఆ వైదేహపుత్రిక, శరదారంభమున మేఘశేషముతో కూడిన చంద్రుడు గల రాత్రి వలె ఆయెను.
hi: श्रीराम के कीर्तन से शोक दूर होने पर भी श्रीराम के शोक के समान शोक वाली वे वैदेहसुता शरद् के आरम्भ में मेघों में आधे ढके चन्द्रमा वाली रात्रि के समान हो गयीं।

iast:
ityārṣe śrīmadrāmāyaṇe vālmīkīye ādikāvye sundarakāṇḍe ṣaṭtriṁśaḥ sargaḥ
en: Thus ends the thirty-sixth sarga, in the Sundarakāṇḍa of the holy Rāmāyaṇa of Vālmīki, the first poem, revealed by the sage.
tel: ఈ విధముగా ఋషిప్రోక్తమైన, ఆదికావ్యమైన శ్రీమద్వాల్మీకి రామాయణమునందలి సుందరకాండలో షట్త్రింశ సర్గ ముగిసినది.
hi: इस प्रकार ऋषिप्रणीत आदिकाव्य श्रीमद्वाल्मीकीय रामायण के सुन्दरकाण्ड में षट्त्रिंश सर्ग समाप्त हुआ।
