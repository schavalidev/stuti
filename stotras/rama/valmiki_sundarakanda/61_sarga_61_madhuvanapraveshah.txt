Blurb: The vānaras accept Jāmbavān’s counsel and set out for Kiṣkindhā, glad and eager to carry the news. On the way they come upon Madhuvana, the honey grove kept for Sugrīva and guarded always by Dadhimukha, his maternal uncle. Aṅgada asks the leave of Jāmbavān and the elder vānaras, and it is given. What follows is a scene of plain delight. They drink the honey and become as though mad. Some sing, some dance, some bow down, some laugh, some weep, some fall against one another, some run from tree to tree and fall from the treetops. Dadhivaktra, the protector of the grove, tries to stop them. He speaks harshly to some and strikes others, and they surround him, scratch him with their nails, bite him and strike him, and he cannot hold them back.
