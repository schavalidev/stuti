Blurb: Eight verses, all in a long metre, on one thing. Hanumān stands in the middle of Rāvaṇa's house and looks at the Puṣpaka. The poet says what it is by saying what it is not: there is nothing in it that was not made with care, nothing that is not set with costly gems, nothing that is not of great distinction, and its excellences are not found even among the gods. Viśvakarman made it himself and called the work good. It was won by austerity and steadiness of mind, it goes where the mind directs, it cannot be stopped, and it moves like the wind. Night-rangers with earrings and rolling eyes bear it through the sky in their thousands. The last verse gives the image the sarga has been building towards. The car is lovelier to look at than a heap of spring blossom, and lovelier even than the month of spring itself.

hi: वह तप, मन के संयम और पराक्रम से अर्जित किया गया था; मन के संकल्प से ही चलता था; अनेक प्रकार की रचनाओं की विशेषताओं से बना था और हर ओर से समान विशेषता वाला दिखायी देता था।

--- verse 5 | section: How it was won, and how it moves ---

en: Setting the mind upon it, it goes swiftly; it cannot be stopped; it moves like the wind. It was as though the chief and glad abode of great ones who have done merit, of the rich and the famed.
tel: మనసు నిలుపగనే అది శీఘ్రముగా పోవును. దానిని ఆపుట శక్యము కాదు. వాయువు వలె అది కదలును. పుణ్యము చేసిన, సమృద్ధులైన, యశస్వులైన మహాత్ముల ప్రధానమైన ఆనందనిలయము వలె అది యుండెను.
hi: मन लगाते ही वह शीघ्र चलता था, उसे रोका नहीं जा सकता था और वह वायु के समान गति करता था। वह मानो पुण्यकर्मा, ऋद्धिमान् और यशस्वी महात्माओं का प्रमुख आनन्दधाम था।
