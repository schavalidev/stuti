Blurb: Rāvaṇa learns that the sons of his ministers have been killed. He hides what he feels and calls five commanders of his army: Virūpākṣa, Yūpākṣa, Durdhara, Praghasa and Bhāsakarṇa. His instruction to them is careful rather than angry. He tells them that this is no ordinary monkey. He has seen Vālin, Sugrīva, Jāmbavān, Nīla and Dvivida, and none of them had this gait, this radiance or this power to change shape. He warns them that a man who knows policy guards himself even when he is strong, because success in battle is uncertain. The five go out with chariots, elephants and horses, and find Hanumān seated on the archway, shining like the rising sun. Durdhara strikes him in the head with five arrows, and Hanumān springs into the sky roaring. He falls upon Durdhara’s chariot and kills him. Virūpākṣa and Yūpākṣa strike his chest with maces, and he kills them both with a sāla tree. Praghasa comes with a paṭṭasa and Bhāsakarṇa with a spear, and he kills them with a peak of the mountain. He then destroys what is left of the army, horse against horse and chariot against chariot, and takes his stand again on the archway.
