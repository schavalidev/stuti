Blurb: Having given the crest-jewel, Sītā sends her words. She asks that they be repeated exactly, which is the office of a messenger and the reason the whole sarga is speech. She urges Rāma to come before her time is spent, reproaches him gently for the delay, and recalls things that only he could know, so that the message itself carries its own proof. Hanumān answers by promising that Rāma will come, and asks her leave to go.
