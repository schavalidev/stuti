Blurb: Hanumān turns from the grove to the shrine-palace that stands in it, climbs to the top of it and roars so that the sound alone frightens those below. He tears out one of its pillars and whirls it until fire is struck from the friction, and with that fire he burns the building. The hundred guards set there attack him and he kills them with the same pillar. The sarga ends with his boast, which is also a report to be carried back: a thousand vānaras of his kind could level Laṅkā entire.
