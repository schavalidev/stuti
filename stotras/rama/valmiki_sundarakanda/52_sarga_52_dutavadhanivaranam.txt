Blurb: Rāvaṇa orders that Hanumān be killed, and his brother Vibhīṣaṇa stops him. The sarga is a plain argument about the law. Vibhīṣaṇa speaks gently. He tells Rāvaṇa that killing a vānara who has come as an envoy is against the dharma of kings, that it is condemned by the world, and that if learned men such as he are seized by anger then all their learning is of no use. Rāvaṇa answers that there is no sin in killing sinners. Vibhīṣaṇa then states the rule exactly: the punishments laid down for an envoy are disfiguring of a limb, beating, shaving of the head and branding with a mark, and the killing of an envoy has never been heard of. He adds the practical reason as well. This one was sent by others and speaks for another, so the fault is not his, and if he is killed there is no one left who can cross the sea and carry a word back. He closes by telling Rāvaṇa to send his army out against those who sent the envoy, rather than to kill the envoy. Rāvaṇa holds back the anger that has risen in him and honours his brother’s words.

असंशयं शत्रुरयं प्रवृद्धः
कृतं ह्यनेनाप्रियमप्रमेयम् ।

iast:
asaṁśayaṁ śatrurayaṁ pravṛddhaḥ
kṛtaṁ hyanenāpriyamaprameyam |
na dūtavadhyāṁ pravadanti santo
dūtasya dṛṣṭā bahavo hi daṇḍāḥ ||
en: Without doubt this one is a grown enemy, and he has done immeasurable harm. Yet the good do not speak of killing a messenger. Many other punishments are laid down for a messenger.
tel: సందేహము లేదు, ఇతడు పెరిగిన శత్రువే. ఇతడు కొలదికి మించిన అప్రియమును చేసెను. అయినను దూతను చంపవలెనని సజ్జనులు చెప్పరు. దూతకు అనేక దండములు కలవు.
hi: निःसन्देह यह बढ़ा हुआ शत्रु है और इसने अपार अप्रिय किया है। फिर भी सत्पुरुष दूत के वध की बात नहीं कहते। दूत के लिए और भी बहुत से दण्ड देखे गए हैं।

hi: अंगों का विरूप करना, कोड़ों से मारना, सिर मुँड़ाना और चिह्न अंकित करना — दूत के लिए ये ही दण्ड कहे गए हैं। दूत का वध तो हमने सुना तक नहीं।

hi: धर्म और अर्थ में जिनकी बुद्धि निपुण है, और जिन्होंने भले-बुरे का निश्चय कर रखा है, ऐसे आप जैसे पुरुष क्रोध के वश कैसे हो सकते हैं? सत्त्वशील पुरुष क्रोध को रोक लेते हैं।
