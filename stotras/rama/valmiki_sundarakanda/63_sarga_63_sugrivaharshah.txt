Blurb: Dadhimukha falls at Sugrīva’s feet, and Sugrīva tells him to rise and not to be afraid. Dadhimukha reports that the grove given by no king before has been plundered, that the guards were driven off, beaten with hands and knees, and that this was done while their lord stood by. Sugrīva does not grow angry. He draws the opposite conclusion, and Lakṣmaṇa asks him why the keeper of the grove has come in such distress. Sugrīva explains. Those who plundered the grove are the party returned from the southern quarter. Vānaras would not dare to touch Madhuvana unless the work had been done. He says that where Jāmbavān gives the counsel and Hanumān carries it out, the outcome cannot be otherwise. He then tells Dadhimukha that he is pleased, and that he wishes to see at once those forest dwellers led by Hanumān. Seeing the two princes glad, Sugrīva rejoices greatly.
