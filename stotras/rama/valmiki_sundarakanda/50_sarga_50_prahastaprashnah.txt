Blurb: Rāvaṇa, looking at the vānara standing before him, is filled with anger, and then with doubt. He wonders whether this is Nandī himself, who cursed him long ago on Kailāsa when he shook that mountain, or whether it is the asura Bāṇa. He tells Prahasta to question him. Prahasta speaks gently and offers safety: if he has been sent by Indra, or by Kubera, or by Yama, or by Varuṇa, or by Viṣṇu, let him say so and he will be set free, for his strength is not the strength of a vānara and only his shape is that of a vānara. Hanumān answers plainly. He belongs to none of them. He is a vānara by birth, and he came here because the lord of the rākṣasas is hard to see. He destroyed the grove so that he would be brought before him. He fought only to protect his own body. He could not have been bound at all, for the Grandfather gave him that boon, and he submitted to the weapon because he wished to see the king. He ends by naming himself the messenger of Rāghava, and asks that his words be heard, for they are spoken for Rāvaṇa’s good.

--- verse 13 | section: Hanumān answers ---

tel: నేను శక్రునివాడను కాను, యమునివాడను కాను, వరుణునివాడను కాను. ధనదుడైన కుబేరునితో నాకు స్నేహము లేదు. విష్ణువుచేత కూడ నేను పంపబడలేదు.

--- verse 16 | section: Hanumān answers ---
