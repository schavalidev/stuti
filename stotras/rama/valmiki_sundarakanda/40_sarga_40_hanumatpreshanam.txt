Blurb: Sītā sends Hanumān away, and the sarga is mostly her anxiety about what she cannot control. She says again that her life will not last beyond the month. Then she asks the practical question that has been under the whole embassy: the sea is a hundred yojanas, and if only one vānara could cross it, how will an army? Hanumān answers by naming the strength of the vānara host and assuring her that many among them are his equals and better. He takes his leave, and turns his mind northward.
