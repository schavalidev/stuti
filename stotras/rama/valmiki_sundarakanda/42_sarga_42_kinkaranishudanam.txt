Blurb: The rākṣasa women bring Rāvaṇa the news, and he sends eighty thousand of his own guards, the kiṅkaras. Before the fighting Hanumān does what an envoy does: he grows to a great size and declares who he is, the servant of Rāma and of Sugrīva, come as a messenger, and says plainly that he is ready for whatever Laṅkā can bring. Then he takes the iron bar from the archway and kills them all. The handful who live carry the news back to Rāvaṇa, and the sarga has done its work: it is the first measure Rāvaṇa has of what one vānara can do.
