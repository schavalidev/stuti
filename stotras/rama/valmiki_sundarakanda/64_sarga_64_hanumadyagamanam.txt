Blurb: Sugrīva sends Dadhimukha back, and he returns to Madhuvana and finds the vānara chiefs now sober. He tells Aṅgada to feel no anger about the guards, and that Sugrīva was glad, not angry, to hear of their coming and summons them. Aṅgada answers with courtesy. He says that although he is the crown prince he is not the one to give the order, and that he will do as all the vānara chiefs together tell him. They answer that this speech befits him and no one else, and that his humility declares his good birth. They spring into the sky together. Sugrīva, watching them come, tells Rāma to take comfort, because vānaras who had failed would not dare to plunder the honey grove, and Aṅgada would not come in this manner if he were not joyful. He says again that there is no cause of this success other than Hanumān. The party alights, and Hanumān tells Rāma that the goddess is steadfast and unharmed.
