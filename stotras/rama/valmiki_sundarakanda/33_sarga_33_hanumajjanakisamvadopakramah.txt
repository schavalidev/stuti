Blurb: Hanumān comes down from the tree, bows, and speaks to her directly for the first time. He begins by asking who she is, running through the possibilities like a man who does not want to presume: a goddess of the Rudras or Maruts or Vasus, Rohiṇī fallen from heaven, or Arundhatī who has angered Vasiṣṭha. Then he reasons from evidence, as he did in sarga 15. Her weeping and her touching the ground are not a goddess's; her marks are a queen's. So, he says, if you are she whom Rāvaṇa carried off from Janasthāna, then you are Sītā — tell me. She answers by naming her family and then telling the whole story from her own side: twelve years in Rāma's house, the consecration made ready in the thirteenth, Kaikeyī's threat to starve, the old king bewildered and weeping as he begged the kingdom of his own son, and Rāma accepting it in his mind before he accepted it with his voice. She ends with the two months Rāvaṇa has given her, and says that after them she will give up her life.

रक्षसाऽपहृता भार्या रावणेन दुरात्मना ।
द्वौ मासौ तेन मे कालो जीवितानुग्रहः कृतः ।
ऊर्ध्वं द्वाभ्यां तु मासाभ्यां ततस्त्यक्ष्यामि जीवितम् ॥
iast:
rakṣasā'pahṛtā bhāryā rāvaṇena durātmanā |
dvau māsau tena me kālo jīvitānugrahaḥ kṛtaḥ |
ūrdhvaṁ dvābhyāṁ tu māsābhyāṁ tatastyakṣyāmi jīvitam ||
en: Two months were granted me by him as a favour of life; and beyond those two months I shall give up my life.
tel: జీవితమునకు అనుగ్రహముగా రెండు నెలల కాలము అతనిచే నాకు ఇవ్వబడినది. ఆ రెండు నెలల తరువాత నేను జీవితమును విడిచెదను.
hi: जीवन की अनुकम्पा के रूप में उसने मुझे दो मास का समय दिया है; और उन दो मासों के बाद मैं प्राण त्याग दूँगी।

deva:
इत्यार्षे श्रीमद्रामायणे वाल्मीकीये आदिकाव्ये सुन्दरकाण्डे त्रयस्त्रिंशः सर्गः
iast:
ityārṣe śrīmadrāmāyaṇe vālmīkīye ādikāvye sundarakāṇḍe trayastriṁśaḥ sargaḥ
en: Thus ends the thirty-third sarga, in the Sundarakāṇḍa of the holy Rāmāyaṇa of Vālmīki, the first poem, revealed by the sage.
tel: ఈ విధముగా ఋషిప్రోక్తమైన, ఆదికావ్యమైన శ్రీమద్వాల్మీకి రామాయణమునందలి సుందరకాండలో త్రయస్త్రింశ సర్గ ముగిసినది.
hi: इस प्रकार ऋषिप्रणीत आदिकाव्य श्रीमद्वाल्मीकीय रामायण के सुन्दरकाण्ड में त्रयस्त्रिंश सर्ग समाप्त हुआ।
