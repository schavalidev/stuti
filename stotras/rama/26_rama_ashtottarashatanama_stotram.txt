Title: Śrī Rāma Aṣṭottaraśatanāma Stotram
Devanāgarī: श्रीराम-अष्टोत्तरशतनामस्तोत्रम्
Telugu: శ్రీ రామ అష్టోత్తరశతనామ స్తోత్రం

Author: Anonymous; Śrī Padma Purāṇa, Uttara-khaṇḍa, per the colophon of both stotra and nāmāvalī.

Source / recension: Primary text: **Gītā Press, Gorakhpur — Śatanāma Stotra Saṅgraha**, code 1850, pp. 62–65 (stotra pp. 62–63, nāmāvalī pp. 64–65), transcribed from the page images of the printed edition rather than from OCR. Entry 10 of the volume's forty. Both colophons read `इति श्रीपद्मपुराणे उत्तरखण्डे` — the same khaṇḍa the volume cites for its Viṣṇu aṣṭottara at entry 5 (`vishnu/26`), though the two are unrelated texts. Stotra and nāmāvalī are paired in one file as the publisher prints them, and were checked against each other name by name: they agree in order at all 108 positions, with two spelling divergences noted below. `rama/` was checked and holds 25 files plus a `sundarkand/` subfolder, none of them an aṣṭottaraśatanāma. **No second edition was collated**, and no web witness has been sought — a real gap for a text this widely circulated, since the Rāma aṣṭottara exists in several other recensions (the Ānanda Rāmāyaṇa and Agastya-saṁhitā lists among them) that a second witness would let us position this one against.

Recension note: **Prefix.** Gītā Press prints `ॐ` before each name; this corpus prints `श्री` for every nāmāvalī without exception (user, 2026-09-08). **The count is exactly 108. 107 distinct names in 108 slots:** `जितामित्राय` stands at both no. 11 and no. 49, and the repetition is in the stotra too (vv. 2 and 8), so it is the text's and is kept. **Two verse/nāmāvalī divergences, both retained as printed.** (a) V. 12 reads `अनादिरादिपुरुषो महापूरुष एव च` — short `पुरुष` in the first, long `पूरुष` in the second; the nāmāvalī at nos. 70–71 has it exactly the other way round, `आदिपूरुषाय` then `महापुरुषाय`. Both forms are good Sanskrit and the metre wants the long vowel where the verse puts it, so the verse is likely right and the list has transposed the quantity; each is kept where it stands. (b) V. 13 prints `स्मितवक्त्रो स्मिताभाषी`, with `-ो` before `स्` where the sandhi wants `स्मितवक्त्रः`. Kept as printed. **V. 18 is a single line.** The stotra's last verse is one line of four names, not a distich, and the volume numbers it `१८` all the same. It is given its own block rather than folded into v. 17, and the `Verse count:` of 19 blocks counts it as the print does. **Three names are printed as two words** and are kept that way: no. 22 `सदा हनुमदाश्रिताय`, no. 34 `भवरोगस्य भेषजाय` (a genitive inside the name, taken straight from v. 6's `भवरोगस्य भेषजम्`), and the run at nos. 98, 100, 101 — `परस्मै ब्रह्मणे`, `परस्मै ज्योतिषे`, `परस्मै धाम्ने` — where the neuters of v. 17 are properly declined. **No. 34 is neuter** (`भेषजम्`, the medicine), and the Hindi column keeps it as such rather than forcing a masculine stem. **The closing run on `पर-`** is the most conspicuous feature of the list: nos. 97–108 are twelve names in a row built on *para*, and the volume prints them as one unbroken sequence.

Verse count: 19

Name count: 108

Sections:
ध्यानम् — Dhyānam, one meditation verse (block 1, unnumbered in the print, glossed in a Hindi footnote)
स्तोत्रम् — Stotram (blocks 2–19 = printed vv. 1–18), the names in the nominative
- Colophon (1 block, unnumbered — excluded from the verse count)
Name index — all 108 names as numbered in the Gītā Press nāmāvalī, pp. 64–65

Blurb: The hundred and eight told as a life rather than a theology: Kausalyā's son, dear to Viśvāmitra, who broke Śiva's bow and ended Ahalyā's curse, cleared the Daṇḍaka, gathered the bears and the monkeys, built the bridge, took the ten heads off Rāvaṇa. Only at the very end does it lift away from the story — twelve names in a row on *para*, the supreme, the far shore, the highest. There is no phalaśruti; the hymn simply stops there.

--- verse 1 | section: Dhyānam ---
deva:
श्रीराघवं दशरथात्मजमप्रमेयं सीतापतिं रघुकुलान्वयरत्नदीपम् ।
आजानुबाहुमरविन्ददलायताक्षं रामं निशाचरविनाशकरं नमामि ॥
iast:
śrīrāghavaṁ daśarathātmajamaprameyaṁ sītāpatiṁ raghukulānvayaratnadīpam |
ājānubāhumaravindadalāyatākṣaṁ rāmaṁ niśācaravināśakaraṁ namāmi ||
en: I salute Rāma, the descendant of Raghu, son of Daśaratha, immeasurable, lord of Sītā, the jewel-lamp of Raghu's line; his arms reaching to his knees, his eyes long as lotus petals, the destroyer of the night-wanderers.
tel: శ్రీరఘునందనుడు, దశరథాత్మజుడు, అప్రమేయుడు, సీతాపతి, రఘుకులవంశమునకు రత్నదీపము, ఆజానుబాహుడు, అరవిందదళములవలె విశాలమైన నేత్రములు కలవాడు, రాక్షసవినాశకుడు అగు శ్రీరామునకు నమస్కరించుచున్నాను.
hi: श्रीरघुनन्दन, दशरथके पुत्र, अप्रमेय, रघुकुलकी वंशपरम्परामें रत्नदीपकसदृश, आजानुबाहु, कमलपत्रके समान विशाल नेत्रोंवाले तथा राक्षसोंके विनाशक सीतापति श्रीरामको मैं नमस्कार करता हूँ।
