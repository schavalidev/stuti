Title: Śrī Rāma Stutiḥ (Nārada Kṛtam)
Devanāgarī: श्री राम स्तुतिः (नारद कृतम्)

Author: traditional attribution to Nārada; the tenth verse is a signature verse in which the speaker addresses a king and says the preceding nine were "made by my own understanding". No purāṇic locus is given in the source.

Source / recension: stotranidhi.com, fetched via curl with browser User-Agent — IAST text at https://stotranidhi.com/en/narada-kruta-sri-rama-stuti-in-english/ and Devanāgarī text at https://stotranidhi.com/hi/narada-kruta-sri-rama-stuti-in-sanskrit/. Verse count: 10

Blurb: This is a hymn whose sound is its argument. Every verse runs on dense end-rhyme and internal rhyme of a kind that Sanskrit rarely permits itself. The opening line alone chimes śrīrāmaṁ, muniviśrāmaṁ, janasaddhāmaṁ and hṛdayārāmaṁ, four times in a single breath. All ten verses close with the same refrain: O Rāma, to you who clove the mighty tāla trees, I bow constantly with my head. That recalls the seven trees Rāma pierced with one arrow to convince Sugrīva. Two things make the hymn unusual. First, several of its epithets are drawn from Kṛṣṇa's cycle rather than Rāma's. In verse 1 Kālindī, the Yamunā, bows to him. The hymn therefore treats the two avatāras as one person, a habit it shares with the Śambhu Kṛta Rāma Stava also in this folder. Second, it does not end in the usual promise of fruit. Verse 10 turns aside to address a king directly, calls the preceding nine verses a navaka made by the poet's own understanding, and only then repeats the refrain. That is a signature rather than a phalaśruti.

en: Whose very form is consciousness; who has conquered mighty kings; before whom true devotees bow, before whom true kings bow; the lord of the earth waved round with lamps by the women born in the seven continents and their regions; the true sovereign well pleased by the many gifts of many princes — O Rāma, to you who clove the mighty tāla trees I bow constantly with my head.
tel: చిద్రూపుడు, గొప్ప రాజులను జయించినవాడు, సద్భక్తులు నమస్కరించువాడు, సద్రాజులు నమస్కరించువాడు; సప్తద్వీపములలోని వర్షములలో పుట్టిన స్త్రీలచే నీరాజనము పొందిన పృథ్వీశుడు; అనేక రాజులు అర్పించిన అనేక ఉపాయనములచే చక్కగా సంతోషింపబడిన సద్రాజు — ఓ రామా, తాళవృక్షములను ఖండించిన నీకు నేను శిరస్సుతో నిరంతరము నమస్కరించుచున్నాను.

--- verse 8 | section: Rama Stuti (Narada Krtam) ---

Recension note: The two stotranidhi editions (IAST and Devanāgarī) carry the identical 10-verse text with no variant readings. Several compounds in this hymn are unusually compressed and their reference is not certain — notably hṛdi sandhyāraṁ in v. 8 and mantrīmocita tatkālam / padmāsvādana samyakśikṣita tatkālaṁ in v. 9, whose narrative allusion is not identified in the source; the translations here render them at the level the grammar supports rather than assigning them to a specific episode. The refrain's cchedita sattālam is read as the seven tāla (or sāla) trees pierced by a single arrow in the Kiṣkindhākāṇḍa. No purāṇic locus, viniyoga, or phalaśruti is supplied; the tenth verse serves as the poet's signature instead.
