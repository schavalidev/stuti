Blurb: This is a turning point of the Sundarakāṇḍa, where the kāṇḍa stops being Hanumān's solitary adventure and becomes an army. It opens inside Sītā's message, with the aśoka grove speaking through Hanumān's mouth: clasp his feet, ask him what my offence was, tell him my eyes are the criminals that will not let my breath leave. Rāma weeps. Hanumān answers with the line on which the whole poem is built, that the only real calamity is a moment without remembrance of God. Then comes the extraordinary exchange in which Rāma tells a monkey that no god, no man and no sage has ever done him such a service, and that he cannot bring himself to face him. Hanumān, lifted from the feet he will not rise from, says that a monkey's whole prowess is jumping from branch to branch, and that everything else was your power, not mine. He asks for one thing only: unfailing devotion. Śiva, telling all this to Umā, loses himself in the memory of the Lord's palm upon the monkey's head. The last third turns outward: Sugrīva's muster; the bears and monkeys with mountains and trees in their hands and their nails for weapons; the elephants of the quarters staggering; and Śeṣa unable to bear the load, biting the tortoise's shell again and again, as though he were cutting the imperishable record of Rāma's march upon it.

--- verse 1 | section: Caupāī ---
deva:
अनुज समेत गहेहु प्रभु चरना।
दीन बंधु प्रनतारति हरना॥
मन क्रम बचन चरन अनुरागी।
केहिं अपराध नाथ हौं त्यागी॥
iast:
anuja sameta gahehu prabhu caranā|
dīna baṁdhu pranatārati haranā||
mana krama bacana carana anurāgī|
kehiṁ aparādha nātha hauṁ tyāgī||
en: (caupāī 2, following dohā 30) Hanumān goes on reporting Sītā's own message to Rāma: clasp the lord's feet, and his younger brother Lakṣmaṇa's with them, and say to him — you are the friend of the destitute, the one who takes away the sufferings of those who come to you for refuge; and I am a woman in love with your feet in thought, in act and in word. For what offence, then, my lord, have you cast me off?
tel: (చౌపాయి ౨, దోహా ౩౦ తరువాతిది) హనుమంతుడు సీతమ్మ సందేశాన్నే కొనసాగిస్తూ చెబుతున్నాడు: తమ్ముడైన లక్ష్మణునితో సహా ప్రభువు చరణాలను పట్టుకుని ఇలా చెప్పు — నీవు దీనబంధువు, శరణాగతుల దుఃఖాలను హరించువాడవు; నేనేమో మనసా వాచా కర్మణా నీ చరణాలయందే అనురాగము గలదానను. అలాంటప్పుడు, ఓ నాథా, ఏ అపరాధము చేసినందుకు నన్ను విడిచిపెట్టావు?
hi: (चौपाई २, दोहा ३० के बाद) हनुमान्जी सीताजी का संदेश आगे सुनाते हैं: छोटे भाई लक्ष्मण सहित प्रभु के चरण पकड़ना और कहना कि आप दीनबंधु हैं, शरणागत के दुःखों को हरने वाले हैं, और मैं मन, कर्म तथा वचन से आपके चरणों की अनुरागिणी हूँ। फिर हे स्वामी! आपने मुझे किस अपराध के कारण त्याग दिया?

--- verse 2 | section: Caupāī ---
deva:
अवगुन एक मोर मैं माना।
बिछुरत प्रान न कीन्ह पयाना॥
नाथ सो नयनन्हि को अपराधा।
निसरत प्रान करहिं हठि बाधा॥
iast:
avaguna eka mora maiṁ mānā|
bichurata prāna na kīnha payānā||
nātha so nayananhi ko aparādhā|
nisarata prāna karahiṁ haṭhi bādhā||
en: (caupāī 3) Sītā, still speaking through Hanumān: one fault of my own I do admit — that the moment I was parted from you my life-breath did not depart. But even that, my lord, is the eyes' crime, for it is they that stubbornly obstruct the breath as it tries to leave.
tel: (చౌపాయి ౩) సీతమ్మ మాటలే హనుమంతుని ద్వారా: ఒక్క దోషాన్ని మాత్రం నేను నా దోషంగా ఒప్పుకుంటాను — నీ ఎడబాటు కలిగిన క్షణమే నా ప్రాణాలు పోలేదని. అయితే ఓ నాథా, అది కూడా నా కన్నుల అపరాధమే; ప్రాణాలు వెళ్ళిపోబోతుంటే అవే మొండిగా అడ్డుపడుతున్నాయి.
hi: (चौपाई ३) सीताजी की ही वाणी हनुमान्जी के मुख से: एक दोष मैं अपना अवश्य मानती हूँ कि आपका वियोग होते ही मेरे प्राण नहीं चले गये। किंतु हे नाथ! वह भी नेत्रों का ही अपराध है, जो प्राणों के निकलने में हठपूर्वक बाधा डालते हैं।

--- verse 3 | section: Caupāī ---
deva:
बिरह अगिनि तनु तूल समीरा।
स्वास जरइ छन माहिं सरीरा॥
नयन स्रवहिं जलु निज हित लागी।
जरैं न पाव देह बिरहागी॥
iast:
biraha agini tanu tūla samīrā|
svāsa jarai chana māhiṁ sarīrā||
nayana sravahiṁ jalu nija hita lāgī|
jaraiṁ na pāva deha birahāgī||
en: (caupāī 4) Separation is the fire, the body is cotton, and the breath is the wind — with fire and wind meeting so, the body should burn away in a moment. But the eyes rain water for their own sake, so that they may go on beholding the lord's form and be glad of it; and because of that rain the body never quite manages to burn in the fire of separation. The ṭīkā takes the tears to be self-interested in exactly this sense — the eyes are not weeping out of grief alone but preserving the body that lets them see.
tel: (చౌపాయి ౪) విరహమే అగ్ని, శరీరమే దూది, శ్వాసయే గాలి; అగ్నీ గాలీ కలిసినప్పుడు ఈ శరీరం క్షణంలో కాలిపోవలసినదే. కానీ కన్నులు తమ స్వంత హితము కోసం — ప్రభువు రూపాన్ని చూసి సుఖించడం కోసం — నీటిని కురిపిస్తున్నాయి; ఆ కారణంగా విరహాగ్నికి కూడా ఈ దేహం కాలిపోలేకపోతున్నది. కన్నీరు ఇలా స్వార్థపూరితమైనదని గీతా ప్రెస్ టీక స్పష్టంగా చెబుతుంది.
hi: (चौपाई ४) विरह अग्नि है, शरीर रूई है और श्वास पवन है; अग्नि और पवन का ऐसा संयोग होने पर यह शरीर क्षणमात्र में जल जाना चाहिए। परंतु नेत्र अपने ही हित के लिये — प्रभु का स्वरूप देखकर सुखी होने के लिये — जल बरसाते हैं, जिससे विरह की आग से भी देह जलने नहीं पाती। टीका आँसुओं को इसी अर्थ में स्वार्थपूर्ण बताती है।

--- verse 4 | section: Caupāī ---
deva:
सीता कै अति बिपति बिसाला।
बिनहिं कहें भलि दीनदयाला॥
iast:
sītā kai ati bipati bisālā|
binahiṁ kaheṁ bhali dīnadayālā||
en: (caupāī 5) Hanumān breaks off, speaking now in his own voice: Sītā's misery is very great indeed — and it is better, O compassionate one, that it stay unspoken, for the telling of it would only cause you great anguish.
tel: (చౌపాయి ౫) ఇక్కడ హనుమంతుడు తన సొంత మాటగా చెబుతున్నాడు: సీతమ్మ ఆపద మిక్కిలి పెద్దది; ఓ దీనదయాళా, అది చెప్పకుండా ఉండటమే మేలు — చెబితే మీకు గొప్ప క్లేశం కలుగుతుంది.
hi: (चौपाई ५) यहाँ हनुमान्जी अपनी ओर से कहते हैं: सीताजी की विपत्ति बहुत बड़ी है; हे दीनदयालु! वह बिना कही ही अच्छी है, क्योंकि कहने से आपको बड़ा क्लेश होगा।

--- verse 5 | section: Dohā ---
deva:
निमिष निमिष करुनानिधि जाहिं कलप सम बीति।
बेगि चलिअ प्रभु आनिअ भुज बल खल दल जीति॥
सुनि सीता दुख प्रभु सुख अयना।
भरि आए जल राजिव नयना॥
बचन कायँ मन मम गति जाही।
सपनेहुँ बूझिअ बिपति कि ताही॥
iast:
nimiṣa nimiṣa karunānidhi jāhiṁ kalapa sama bīti|
begi calia prabhu ānia bhuja bala khala dala jīti||
suni sītā dukha prabhu sukha ayanā|
bhari āe jala rājiva nayanā||
bacana kāyam̐ mana mama gati jāhī|
sapanehum̐ būjhia bipati ki tāhī||
en: (dohā 31, with the caupāī group 1 that runs on from it) Hanumān: O treasury of compassion, each single instant passes for her like an aeon; so set out at once, my lord, conquer the host of the wicked by the strength of your arms, and bring her back. — Hearing of Sītā's suffering, the lord who is himself the abode of joy filled his lotus eyes with water, and said: one to whom I am the whole recourse in word, in body and in mind — can such a person meet with calamity even in a dream?
tel: (దోహా ౩౧, దానితో కలిసి వచ్చే ౧వ చౌపాయి సముదాయం) హనుమంతుడు: ఓ కరుణానిధీ, ఆమెకు ఒక్కొక్క నిమిషమూ ఒక కల్పంలా గడుస్తున్నది; కావున ప్రభూ, వెంటనే బయలుదేరండి, భుజబలంతో దుష్టుల సమూహాన్ని జయించి ఆమెను తీసుకురండి. — సీతమ్మ దుఃఖాన్ని విని, సుఖానికే నెలవైన ప్రభువు కమలనేత్రాలు నీటితో నిండిపోయాయి; ఆయన అన్నాడు: మనసా వాచా కర్మణా నేనే గతిగా ఉన్నవారికి కలలో అయినా విపత్తు కలుగుతుందా?
hi: (दोहा ३१, उसी के साथ चलने वाला १ चौपाई समूह) हनुमान्जी: हे करुणानिधान! उनका एक-एक पल कल्प के समान बीतता है; अतः हे प्रभु! तुरंत चलिये और अपनी भुजाओं के बल से दुष्टों के दल को जीतकर सीताजी को ले आइये। — सीताजी का दुःख सुनकर सुख के धाम प्रभु के कमलनेत्रों में जल भर आया और वे बोले: मन, शरीर और वचन से जिसे मेरी ही गति है, उसे क्या स्वप्न में भी विपत्ति हो सकती है?

--- verse 6 | section: Caupāī ---
deva:
कह हनुमंत बिपति प्रभु सोई।
जब तव सुमिरन भजन न होई॥
केतिक बात प्रभु जातुधान की।
रिपुहि जीति आनिबी जानकी॥
iast:
kaha hanumaṁta bipati prabhu soī|
jaba tava sumirana bhajana na hoī||
ketika bāta prabhu jātudhāna kī|
ripuhi jīti ānibī jānakī||
en: (caupāī 2) Hanumān said: my lord, the only calamity there is, is the time in which your remembrance and worship do not happen. And how much of a matter are the demons (jātudhāna, a Vedic word for rākṣasa)? You will conquer the enemy and bring Jānakī back.
tel: (చౌపాయి ౨) హనుమంతుడు అన్నాడు: ప్రభూ, విపత్తు అంటే ఏదైనా ఉన్నదంటే అది నీ స్మరణ, భజన జరగని కాలమే. ఇక రాక్షసుల (జాతుధాన) సంగతి ఎంత? శత్రువును జయించి జానకిని తీసుకువస్తారు.
hi: (चौपाई २) हनुमान्जी ने कहा: हे प्रभो! विपत्ति तो वही है जब आपका स्मरण और भजन न हो। हे प्रभो! राक्षसों (जातुधान) की बात ही कितनी है? आप शत्रु को जीतकर जानकीजी को ले आवेंगे।

--- verse 7 | section: Caupāī ---
deva:
सुनु कपि तोहि समान उपकारी।
नहिं कोउ सुर नर मुनि तनुधारी॥
प्रति उपकार करौं का तोरा।
सनमुख होइ न सकत मन मोरा॥
iast:
sunu kapi tohi samāna upakārī|
nahiṁ kou sura nara muni tanudhārī||
prati upakāra karauṁ kā torā|
sanamukha hoi na sakata mana morā||
en: (caupāī 3) Rāma now speaks: listen, monkey — of all embodied beings, god, man or sage, there is not one who has done me a kindness like yours. What return of kindness could I possibly make you? My mind cannot even bring itself to face you.
tel: (చౌపాయి ౩) ఇప్పుడు రాముడు మాట్లాడుతున్నాడు: విను కపీ — దేవతలలో గాని, మనుష్యులలో గాని, మునులలో గాని, శరీరధారులైన వారిలో నీవలె నాకు ఉపకారం చేసినవాడు ఒక్కడూ లేడు. నీకు ప్రత్యుపకారం ఏమి చేయగలను? నా మనసు నీ ఎదుట నిలువనైనా లేకపోతున్నది.
hi: (चौपाई ३) अब श्रीराम कहते हैं: हे हनुमान्, सुन; देवता, मनुष्य या मुनि — किसी भी शरीरधारी में तेरे समान मेरा उपकारी कोई नहीं है। मैं तेरा प्रत्युपकार क्या करूँ? मेरा मन तो तेरे सामने हो भी नहीं सकता।

--- verse 8 | section: Caupāī ---
deva:
सुनु सुत तोहि उरिन मैं नाहीं।
देखेउँ करि बिचार मन माहीं॥
पुनि पुनि कपिहि चितव सुरत्राता।
लोचन नीर पुलक अति गाता॥
iast:
sunu suta tohi urina maiṁ nāhīṁ|
dekheum̐ kari bicāra mana māhīṁ||
puni puni kapihi citava suratrātā|

en: (caupāī 4) Listen, son: I have thought it well over in my mind and seen that I cannot become free of my debt to you. — And the protector of the gods (suratrātā, Rāma) looked at the monkey again and again, tears of love standing in his eyes and his whole body thrilling with horripilation.

Recension note:

Language. This file is not Sanskrit. The Rāmcaritmānas is composed in Avadhī, the eastern
Hindi of Ayodhyā and its region, and the Devanāgarī above is Avadhī as Tulsīdās wrote it and as
Gītā Press prints it. The IAST is therefore a mechanical transliteration of Avadhī forms and has
deliberately not been Sanskritised: गहेहु is gahehu and not gṛhṇīhi, चरना is caranā and not caraṇā,
पच्छजुत is pacchajuta and not pakṣayuta, हौं is hauṁ, सपनेहुँ is sapanehum̐. Candrabindu is rendered
m̐ (hṛdayam̐, kahum̐, am̐ga, prabhāvam̐), anusvāra ṁ, the single daṇḍa |, the double daṇḍa ||, and
e/o are always plain. Because hi: is a modern-Hindi rendering of an Avadhī original, it is a real
translation here and not a copy of the deva: field; it follows Gītā Press's own bhāvārtha closely,
including the ṭīkā's explanatory parentheses where they carry the sense (the eyes weeping "for
their own good" at caupāī 4; the omens as līlā-maryādā at unit 20; the bite-marks read as an
inscription at chhand 2).

Division of the text. The mūl was separated from the interleaved Hindi ṭīkā by typeface, not by
editorial judgement: in the source file the verse is set in bold at 19.7pt and the bhāvārtha in
regular at 16pt, so the boundary between the two is the edition's own typographic decision,
mechanically recoverable, and nothing here rests on a modern editor's opinion about where a verse
ends. The unit division likewise follows the print: each dohā is printed together with the caupāī
half-line group numbered ॥१॥ that runs on from it, and that is how units 5, 9, 14, 18 and 25 are
constituted here — a dohā couplet plus its following caupāī group, section-labelled Dohā after the
kind the printed page gives. The two chhands after dohā 34 are printed unnumbered as verse and
numbered ॥१॥ and ॥२॥ only in the ṭīkā; they are given here as units 23 and 24. The closing verse
numeral (॥२॥, ॥३॥ …) has been stripped from every deva: field and carried into the unit header, per
corpus convention.

Divergences from the common web text, in this part specifically. (a) Unit 23 line 2: the print reads
मन हरष सभ गंधर्ब where much of the web text reads सब; सभ is kept. Both are attested Avadhī forms of
the same word and the sense is unaffected. (b) Unit 24 line 2: the print reads कमठ पृष्ट (pṛṣṭa)
where the standard spelling elsewhere is पृष्ठ; the print's पृष्ट is kept. (c) Unit 23 line 2 ends
with a space before the daṇḍa in the source setting; that is compositor spacing and has been
normalised away without any change to the text. Outside these, the collation against the DharmicData
corpus over this range showed no substantive divergence.

Overlap with the rest of the corpus. Nothing in dohās 31–35 duplicates a standalone stotra already
held in this corpus, so there is no entry to make in stotras/DEDUP_AUDIT.md for this file. The
nearest thing to an independently circulating unit here is Hanumān's line बिपति प्रभु सोई जब तव
सुमिरन भजन न होई (unit 6), widely quoted on its own but never as a separate text.

Language: Awadhi. The text is Awadhi, the language in which Tulsīdās composed the Rāmcaritmānas. It is not Sanskrit, and it is not modern Hindi. The `deva:` field carries the printed text exactly as it stands. The `iast:` field is a mechanical transliteration of it to corpus convention. The `hi:` field gives a plain modern-Hindi rendering of the sense, and `en:` and `tel:` do the same in English and Telugu. There is no Hindi form of this work, and none has been made here. A reader who wants the sense in modern Hindi should read the `hi:` field, which is a rendering of the Awadhi and not a second recension of it. In transliteration ड़ and ढ़ are written ṛ and ṛh, as Hindi and Awadhi require. The anusvāra before a stop is written ṁ, following the practice of this file (हनुमंत → hanumaṁta, सिंधु → siṁdhu); it is not assimilated to the homorganic nasal, which is a choice some other files in this corpus make and this one does not.
