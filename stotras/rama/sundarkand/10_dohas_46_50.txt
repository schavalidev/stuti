Blurb: This is Vibhīṣaṇa's acceptance, and the first council on how to cross the sea. Rāma seats the refugee beside him with Lakṣmaṇa, and asks, with something close to teasing, how a man of such scruple keeps his dharma alive day and night inside a company of the wicked. Better to live in hell, Vibhīṣaṇa answers, than in bad company. Out of that exchange comes the most quoted teaching of the kāṇḍa. No creature is safe, or has rest even in dream, until it stops serving desire, which is the house of grief, and turns to Rāma. Greed, delusion, envy, pride and conceit occupy a heart only for as long as Raghunātha with his bow and quiver has not occupied it. Possessiveness is a full dark night, in which the owls of attachment and aversion are happy, and it lasts exactly until the sun of the Lord's majesty rises. Rāma then states his own nature in the plainest terms he ever uses: let a man be the enemy of every living thing, and let him come afraid to my refuge and give up his deceits, and I will make him a saint at once. The coronation follows on the spot. Rāma crowns Vibhīṣaṇa king of Laṅkā before a single arrow has been shot at it, with flowers falling from the sky. The poet remarks that Śiva gave Rāvaṇa that same wealth for ten severed heads, while Raghunātha gave it away embarrassed at how small a gift it was. The passage closes with strategy: how is this sea to be crossed? Vibhīṣaṇa counsels petition rather than force. Rāma agrees, and Lakṣmaṇa, who wants the ocean dried up in anger, takes the answer badly.

--- verse 1 | section: Caupāī ---
deva:
अनुज सहित मिलि ढिग बैठारी।
बोले बचन भगत भयहारी॥
कहु लंकेस सहित परिवारा।
कुसल कुठाहर बास तुम्हारा॥
iast:
anuja sahita mili ḍhiga baiṭhārī|
bole bacana bhagata bhayahārī||
kahu laṁkesa sahita parivārā|
kusala kuṭhāhara bāsa tumhārā||
en: (caupāī 2, following dohā 45) Rāma embraced Vibhīṣaṇa together with his own younger brother Lakṣmaṇa, seated him close beside him, and spoke words that take away the fears of his devotees: tell me, lord of Laṅkā, how it goes with you and all your household — your dwelling is in a bad place.
tel: (చౌపాయి ౨, దోహా ౪౫ తరువాతిది) శ్రీరాముడు తమ్ముడైన లక్ష్మణునితో సహా విభీషణుని కౌగిలించుకుని, తన ప్రక్కనే కూర్చుండబెట్టుకుని, భక్తుల భయాన్ని హరించే మాటలు పలికాడు: ఓ లంకేశా, పరివారంతో సహా నీ కుశలము చెప్పు; నీ నివాసమైతే చెడ్డ చోట ఉన్నది.
hi: (चौपाई २, दोहा ४५ के बाद) छोटे भाई लक्ष्मण सहित गले मिलकर श्रीरामजी ने विभीषण को अपने पास बैठाया और भक्तों का भय हरने वाले वचन बोले: हे लंकेश! परिवार सहित अपनी कुशल कहो — तुम्हारा निवास तो बुरी जगह पर है।

--- verse 2 | section: Caupāī ---
deva:
खल मंडली बसहु दिनु राती।
सखा धरम निबहइ केहि भाँती॥
मैं जानउँ तुम्हारि सब रीती।
अति नय निपुन न भाव अनीती॥
iast:
khala maṁḍalī basahu dinu rātī|
sakhā dharama nibahai kehi bhām̐tī||
maiṁ jānaum̐ tumhāri saba rītī|
ati naya nipuna na bhāva anītī||
en: (caupāī 3) Rāma, continuing: day and night you live in a company of the wicked — in such a state, friend, how does your dharma keep going at all? I know your whole way of conduct: you are exceedingly skilled in right policy, and injustice does not please you.
tel: (చౌపాయి ౩) శ్రీరాముడు కొనసాగిస్తూ: రేయింబవళ్ళు దుష్టుల సమూహంలో నివసిస్తున్నావు; అలాంటి స్థితిలో, ఓ మిత్రమా, నీ ధర్మం ఎలా నిలుస్తున్నది? నీ ఆచార వ్యవహారాలన్నీ నాకు తెలుసు — నీవు నీతిలో మిక్కిలి నిపుణుడవు, అనీతి నీకు రుచించదు.
hi: (चौपाई ३) श्रीरामजी आगे कहते हैं: दिन-रात दुष्टों की मण्डली में बसते हो; ऐसी दशा में हे सखे! तुम्हारा धर्म किस प्रकार निभता है? मैं तुम्हारी सब रीति जानता हूँ — तुम अत्यन्त नीतिनिपुण हो, तुम्हें अनीति नहीं सुहाती।

--- verse 3 | section: Caupāī ---
deva:
बरु भल बास नरक कर ताता।
दुष्ट संग जनि देइ बिधाता॥
अब पद देखि कुसल रघुराया।
जौं तुम्ह कीन्हि जानि जन दाया॥
iast:
baru bhala bāsa naraka kara tātā|
duṣṭa saṁga jani dei bidhātā||
aba pada dekhi kusala raghurāyā|
jauṁ tumha kīnhi jāni jana dāyā||
en: (caupāī 4) Vibhīṣaṇa answers: dear one, it is better to dwell in hell — let the Creator never give anyone the company of the wicked. And now, Raghunātha, seeing your feet I am well, since you have taken me for your own servant and shown me this mercy.
tel: (చౌపాయి ౪) విభీషణుడు బదులిస్తాడు: ఓ తండ్రీ, నరకంలో నివసించడమే మేలు; దుష్టుల సాంగత్యాన్ని విధాత ఎవరికీ ఇవ్వకుండు గాక. ఇప్పుడైతే ఓ రఘునాథా, నీ చరణాలను చూసి నేను కుశలంగా ఉన్నాను — నన్ను నీ సేవకుడని తెలిసి నీవు దయ చూపావు కదా.
hi: (चौपाई ४) विभीषणजी उत्तर देते हैं: हे तात! नरक में रहना वरं अच्छा है, पर विधाता दुष्ट का संग कभी न दे। हे रघुनाथजी! अब आपके चरणों का दर्शन कर कुशल से हूँ, जो आपने अपना सेवक जानकर मुझ पर दया की।

--- verse 4 | section: Dohā ---
deva:
तब लगि कुसल न जीव कहुँ सपनेहुँ मन बिश्राम।
जब लगि भजत न राम कहुँ सोक धाम तजि काम॥
तब लगि हृदयँ बसत खल नाना।
लोभ मोह मच्छर मद माना॥
जब लगि उर न बसत रघुनाथा।
धरें चाप सायक कटि भाथा॥
iast:
taba lagi kusala na jīva kahum̐ sapanehum̐ mana biśrāma|
jaba lagi bhajata na rāma kahum̐ soka dhāma taji kāma||
taba lagi hṛdayam̐ basata khala nānā|
lobha moha macchara mada mānā||
jaba lagi ura na basata raghunāthā|
dhareṁ cāpa sāyaka kaṭi bhāthā||
en: (dohā 46, with the caupāī group 1 that runs on from it) There is no welfare for a living being, nor rest for its mind even in a dream, for as long as it does not give up desire — the very house of grief — and worship Rāma. — And the many wicked ones, greed, delusion, envy (matsara, the grudging of another's good), intoxication of pride and self-conceit, go on dwelling in the heart only for as long as Raghunātha, bow and arrow in hand and quiver at his waist, does not dwell there.
tel: (దోహా ౪౬, దానితో కలిసి వచ్చే ౧వ చౌపాయి సముదాయం) శోకానికి నెలవైన కామాన్ని విడిచి రాముని భజించనంత వరకు జీవునికి కుశలము లేదు, స్వప్నంలో కూడా మనసుకు విశ్రాంతి లేదు. — లోభము, మోహము, మత్సరము, మదము, మానము మొదలైన అనేక దుష్టులు హృదయంలో అప్పటి వరకే నివసిస్తారు — ధనుర్బాణాలను ధరించి, నడుమున అమ్ములపొదిని కట్టుకున్న రఘునాథుడు ఆ హృదయంలో నివసించనంత వరకే.
hi: (दोहा ४६, उसी के साथ चलने वाला १ चौपाई समूह) तब तक जीव की कुशल नहीं और न स्वप्न में भी उसके मन को विश्राम है, जब तक वह शोक के घर काम (विषय-कामना) को छोड़कर श्रीराम को नहीं भजता। — लोभ, मोह, मत्सर, मद और मान आदि अनेकों दुष्ट हृदय में तभी तक बसते हैं, जब तक धनुष-बाण और कमर में तरकस धारण किये हुए श्रीरघुनाथजी हृदय में नहीं बसते।

--- verse 5 | section: Caupāī ---
deva:
ममता तरुन तमी अँधिआरी।
राग द्वेष उलूक सुखकारी॥
तब लगि बसति जीव मन माहीं।
जब लगि प्रभु प्रताप रबि नाहीं॥
iast:
mamatā taruna tamī am̐dhiārī|
rāga dveṣa ulūka sukhakārī||
taba lagi basati jīva mana māhīṁ|
jaba lagi prabhu pratāpa rabi nāhīṁ||
en: (caupāī 2) Possessiveness — the sense of "mine" — is a dark night at its full, delightful to the owls of attachment and aversion; and that night goes on dwelling in the mind of the creature only for as long as the sun of the lord's majesty has not risen.
tel: (చౌపాయి ౨) మమత అనేది పూర్తిగా చీకటి పడిన రాత్రి; అది రాగద్వేషాలనే గుడ్లగూబలకు సుఖాన్నిచ్చేది. ఆ రాత్రి జీవుని మనసులో అప్పటి వరకే నివసిస్తుంది — ప్రభువు ప్రతాపమనే సూర్యుడు ఉదయించనంత వరకే.
hi: (चौपाई २) ममता पूर्ण अँधेरी रात है, जो राग-द्वेष रूपी उल्लुओं को सुख देने वाली है; वह रात्रि जीव के मन में तभी तक बसती है, जब तक प्रभु का प्रताप रूपी सूर्य उदय नहीं होता।

--- verse 6 | section: Caupāī ---
deva:
अब मैं कुसल मिटे भय भारे।
देखि राम पद कमल तुम्हारे॥
तुम्ह कृपाल जा पर अनुकूला।
ताहि न ब्याप त्रिबिध भव सूला॥
iast:
aba maiṁ kusala miṭe bhaya bhāre|
dekhi rāma pada kamala tumhāre||
tumha kṛpāla jā para anukūlā|
tāhi na byāpa tribidha bhava sūlā||
en: (caupāī 3) Vibhīṣaṇa: now, Rāma, having seen your lotus feet I am well, and my heavy fears are gone. Merciful one, whoever you are favourable to is not touched by the threefold pain of worldly existence — the ṭīkā glosses the three as the ādhyātmika, ādhidaivika and ādhibhautika afflictions, those arising from oneself, from the gods or fate, and from other creatures.
tel: (చౌపాయి ౩) విభీషణుడు: ఓ రామా, ఇప్పుడు నీ పాదపద్మాలను చూసి నేను కుశలంగా ఉన్నాను, నా భారీ భయాలు తొలగిపోయాయి. ఓ కృపాళూ, నీవు ఎవరిపై అనుకూలంగా ఉంటావో వానిని త్రివిధ భవశూలము తాకదు — ఆధ్యాత్మిక, ఆధిదైవిక, ఆధిభౌతిక తాపాలని టీక వివరిస్తుంది.
hi: (चौपाई ३) विभीषणजी: हे श्रीराम! आपके चरणकमलों का दर्शन कर अब मैं कुशल से हूँ, मेरे भारी भय मिट गये। हे कृपालु! आप जिस पर अनुकूल होते हैं, उसे तीनों प्रकार के भवशूल (आध्यात्मिक, आधिदैविक और आधिभौतिक ताप) नहीं व्यापते।

--- verse 7 | section: Caupāī ---
deva:
मैं निसिचर अति अधम सुभाऊ।
सुभ आचरनु कीन्ह नहिं काऊ॥
जासु रूप मुनि ध्यान न आवा।
तेहिं प्रभु हरषि हृदयँ मोहि लावा॥
iast:
maiṁ nisicara ati adhama subhāū|
subha ācaranu kīnha nahiṁ kāū||
jāsu rūpa muni dhyāna na āvā|
tehiṁ prabhu haraṣi hṛdayam̐ mohi lāvā||
en: (caupāī 4) Vibhīṣaṇa: I am a night-ranger, a rākṣasa of the basest nature, and I have never once performed an auspicious act; yet that lord whose form does not come even into the meditation of sages has gladly pressed me to his heart.
tel: (చౌపాయి ౪) విభీషణుడు: నేను అత్యంత అధమ స్వభావం గల రాక్షసుడను, ఎన్నడూ శుభ ఆచరణ చేయలేదు; అయినా ఎవరి రూపము మునుల ధ్యానానికి కూడా అందదో, ఆ ప్రభువే సంతోషించి నన్ను హృదయానికి హత్తుకున్నాడు.
hi: (चौपाई ४) विभीषणजी: मैं अत्यन्त नीच स्वभाव का राक्षस हूँ, मैंने कभी शुभ आचरण नहीं किया; फिर भी जिनका रूप मुनियों के ध्यान में भी नहीं आता, उन प्रभु ने हर्षित होकर मुझे हृदय से लगा लिया।

--- verse 8 | section: Dohā ---
deva:
अहोभाग्य मम अमित अति राम कृपा सुख पुंज।
देखेउँ नयन बिरंचि सिव सेब्य जुगल पद कंज॥
सुनहु सखा निज कहउँ सुभाऊ।
जान भुसुंडि संभु गिरिजाऊ॥
जौं नर होइ चराचर द्रोही।
आवै सभय सरन तकि मोही॥
iast:
ahobhāgya mama amita ati rāma kṛpā sukha puṁja|
dekheum̐ nayana biraṁci siva sebya jugala pada kaṁja||
sunahu sakhā nija kahaum̐ subhāū|
jāna bhusuṁḍi saṁbhu girijāū||
jauṁ nara hoi carācara drohī|
āvai sabhaya sarana taki mohī||
en: (dohā 47, with the caupāī group 1 that runs on from it) Vibhīṣaṇa: Rāma, mass of compassion and of joy, my good fortune is beyond all measure, that with these eyes I have seen the pair of lotus feet which Brahmā (Viraṁci) and Śiva serve. — Rāma answers: listen, friend, I will tell you my own nature, which Kākabhuśuṇḍi, Śambhu and Girijā (Pārvatī) also know. Though a man be the enemy of every moving and unmoving thing in creation, if he comes in fear and takes refuge in me —

Recension note:

Language. This is Awadhi, not Sanskrit. The IAST here transliterates the Awadhi exactly as printed
and does not Sanskritise it: बिभीषनु stands as bibhīṣanu and not vibhīṣaṇaḥ, सर्बग्य as sarbagya and
not sarvajña, दुष्ट alongside दुस्तर with the print's own spellings. Anunāsika is written m̐ and
anusvāra ṁ, the avagraha ', and the daṇḍa and double daṇḍa | and ||, per corpus convention; e and o
are always plain.

Division of the text. The mūl was separated from the Hindi ṭīkā mechanically, by typeface: in the
Gītā Press setting the verse is set in bold at 19.7pt and the bhāvārtha beneath it in regular 16pt,
so the division between verse and commentary reproduced here is the edition's own and involves no
editorial judgement of mine. The unit boundaries likewise follow the print's verse numerals. The
dohā units are constituted as the printed page constitutes them — a dohā couplet together with the
caupāī group numbered ॥१॥ that runs on from it, section-labelled Dohā after the kind the page gives
the couplet. Unit 17 (dohā 49क) is the one exception: the print gives it as a standalone couplet with
no caupāī group following, because dohā 49ख comes immediately after it. The closing verse numeral
(॥१॥, ॥२॥ …) has been stripped from every deva: field and carried into the unit header, per corpus
convention.

Numbering. This part contains six dohā units for five dohā numbers, because Gītā Press splits dohā
49 into 49(क) and 49(ख) — two separate couplets, each with its own bhāvārtha. The count of 22 units
reported above therefore does not contradict "dohās 46–50"; it describes the same stretch of text
counted by printed unit rather than by dohā number.

Divergences from the common web text, in this part specifically. The mūl over dohās 46–50 collated
cleanly: no substantive divergence from the DharmicData corpus was found anywhere in this range, and
every reading here is the Gītā Press reading. Three points are worth recording all the same. (a) Unit
12 line 1 prints दृढ़ with the nuqta, transliterated dṛṛha; some digital texts drop the nuqta and set
दृढ. The print's form is kept. (b) Unit 18 line 3 prints छाड़ि, again with the nuqta, where part of
the web text has छाँड़ि; the print's छाड़ि is kept. (c) The ṭīkā layer of this file was reported at the time of
writing to show a ligature-dropout pattern — हर्षत for हर्षित twice, अगिन for अग्नि, पुल्ल for पुंज in
the gloss on dohā 47. Those were not dropouts in the print but unmapped glyphs in this session's
Chanakya table, since mapped; the print reads हर्षित, अग्नि and पुञ्ज, and the volume now decodes
with zero malformed Devanāgarī sequences. The verse text reproduced here was unaffected either way.

Overlap with the rest of the corpus. Nothing in dohās 46–50 duplicates a standalone stotra already
held in this corpus, so there is no entry to make in stotras/DEDUP_AUDIT.md for this file. The
passages most often quoted on their own — the dohā तब लगि कुसल न जीव कहुँ (unit 4) and Rāma's
declaration of his own nature at units 8–11, sometimes circulated as a short "Rāma-svabhāva" reading
— are quotations from the Mānasa, never separately constituted texts.

Language: Awadhi. The text is Awadhi, the language in which Tulsīdās composed the Rāmcaritmānas. It is not Sanskrit, and it is not modern Hindi. The `deva:` field carries the printed text exactly as it stands. The `iast:` field is a mechanical transliteration of it to corpus convention.
