Blurb: This is a turning point of the Sundarkāṇḍ. Hanumān has found Sītā, and offers to carry her out of Laṅkā at once. She refuses, and doubts that a monkey so small could matter at all. He therefore swells into a body like a golden mountain, and then shrinks again, and she blesses him to be ageless, deathless and a treasury of virtues. What follows is deliberately comic before it turns grave. He is hungry. He asks permission to eat the fruit. The eating flattens the Aśoka grove, kills the guards, kills Akṣayakumāra, and at last brings Meghanāda with the brahmāstra. Tulsīdās stops the narrative at exactly that point, to have Śiva tell Pārvatī the theological joke of the whole episode: the servant of the one whose name cuts the bonds of birth and death cannot be bound by anything, and lets himself be bound only because the errand requires it. The passage ends with Hanumān standing unafraid in Rāvaṇa's court. There the gods and the guardians of the directions are watching the ten-headed king's eyebrows in fear, and Rāvaṇa laughs at him, until the memory of his fallen son sours the laugh.

--- verse 1 | section: Hanumān reassures Sītā ---
deva:
अबहिं मातु मैं जाउँ लवाई।
प्रभु आयसु नहिं राम दोहाई॥
कछुक दिवस जननी धरु धीरा।
कपिन्ह सहित अइहहिं रघुबीरा॥
iast:
abahiṁ mātu maiṁ jāum̐ lavāī|
prabhu āyasu nahiṁ rāma dohāī||
kachuka divasa jananī dharu dhīrā|
kapinha sahita aihahiṁ raghubīrā||
en: (caupāī 2 after dohā 15) Hanumān to Sītā: Mother, I would carry you away from here this very moment — but I swear by Rāma himself, I have no order from my lord to do it. So hold your patience a few days more, mother: the hero of Raghu's line will come here in person, and the monkey host with him. "Dohāī" is the Awadhi oath-word, to invoke a name as surety; Hanumān is not refusing out of weakness but out of obedience, and the point of the whole speech is that the rescue must be Rāma's, not his.
tel: (దోహా ౧౫ తరువాతి చౌపాయి ౨) హనుమంతుడు సీతమ్మతో: అమ్మా, ఈ క్షణమే నిన్ను ఇక్కడినుండి ఎత్తుకుని వెళ్ళిపోగలను — కాని శ్రీరాముని మీద ఒట్టు, అలా చేయమని ప్రభువు ఆజ్ఞ నాకు లేదు; అందుచేత అమ్మా, ఇంకా కొన్ని దినాలు ధైర్యం వహించు, రఘువీరుడు వానరసేనతో కూడి స్వయంగా ఇక్కడికి వస్తాడు. "దోహాई" అనేది అవధీ భాషలో ఒట్టు పెట్టుకునే మాట; హనుమంతుడు అశక్తతవలన కాదు, ఆజ్ఞ లేనందువలననే వెనుకంజ వేస్తున్నాడు — విడిపించవలసినది రాముడే కాని తానుకాదన్నదే ఈ మాటల సారం.
hi: (दोहा १५ के बाद चौपाई २) हनुमान्जी सीताजी से: हे माता! मैं आपको अभी इसी क्षण यहाँ से लिवा ले जाऊँ, पर श्रीरामचन्द्रजी की शपथ है — मुझे प्रभु की ऐसी आज्ञा नहीं है। इसलिए हे जननी! कुछ दिन और धीरज धरो; श्रीरघुवीर वानरों सहित स्वयं यहाँ आवेंगे। "दोहाई" अवधी में शपथ का शब्द है; हनुमान्जी असमर्थता से नहीं, आज्ञा न होने से रुक रहे हैं — उद्धार प्रभु के हाथों ही होना है।

--- verse 2 | section: Hanumān reassures Sītā ---
deva:
निसिचर मारि तोहि लै जैहहिं।
तिहुँ पुर नारदादि जसु गैहहिं॥
हैं सुत कपि सब तुम्हहि समाना।
जातुधान अति भट बलवाना॥
iast:
nisicara māri tohi lai jaihahiṁ|
tihum̐ pura nāradādi jasu gaihahiṁ||
haiṁ suta kapi saba tumhahi samānā|
jātudhāna ati bhaṭa balavānā||
en: (caupāī 3 after dohā 15) Hanumān: He will kill the night-rangers (nisicara, the rākṣasas) and take you away, and Nārada and the other sages will sing his fame through all three worlds. Sītā answers: Son, if all the monkeys are the size of you, and the jātudhānas — the demons — are such great and mighty warriors, how is it to be done? The line is Sītā's, though no speaker-tag is printed; the Gītā Press ṭīkā supplies the change of speaker at "हैं सुत", and glosses her sense as "all the monkeys will be little ones like you".
tel: (దోహా ౧౫ తరువాతి చౌపాయి ౩) హనుమంతుడు: ఆయన నిశాచరులను (రాక్షసులను) సంహరించి నిన్ను తీసుకువెళతాడు; నారదాది మునులు మూడు లోకాలలోనూ ఆయన కీర్తిని గానం చేస్తారు. అప్పుడు సీతమ్మ: కుమారా, వానరులందరూ నీలాంటి చిన్నవారే కదా, రాక్షసులేమో మహాబలవంతులైన యోధులు — అని. ఈ చరణంలో వక్త మారుతున్నట్లు ముద్రణలో సూచన లేకపోయినా, "హైं సుత" నుండి మాట సీతమ్మదేనని గీతా ప్రెస్ భావార్థం స్పష్టం చేస్తుంది.
hi: (दोहा १५ के बाद चौपाई ३) हनुमान्जी: वे राक्षसों को मारकर आपको ले जायँगे और नारद आदि ऋषि-मुनि तीनों लोकों में उनका यश गावेंगे। [सीताजी ने कहा—] हे पुत्र! सब वानर तो तुम्हारे ही समान (नन्हें-नन्हें से) होंगे, और राक्षस तो बड़े ही बलवान् योद्धा हैं। यहाँ वक्ता के बदलने की कोई मुद्रित सूचना नहीं है; "हैं सुत" से आगे की बात सीताजी की है, यह गीता प्रेस के भावार्थ से स्पष्ट होता है।

--- verse 3 | section: Hanumān reassures Sītā ---
deva:
मोरें हृदय परम संदेहा।
सुनि कपि प्रगट कीन्हि निज देहा॥
कनक भूधराकार सरीरा।
समर भयंकर अतिबल बीरा॥
iast:
moreṁ hṛdaya parama saṁdehā|
suni kapi pragaṭa kīnhi nija dehā||
kanaka bhūdharākāra sarīrā|
samara bhayaṁkara atibala bīrā||
en: (caupāī 4 after dohā 15) Sītā: So there is a very great doubt in my heart — how are monkeys like you to conquer such demons? Hearing this, Hanumān manifested his own true body: a frame shaped like a mountain of gold (bhūdhara, the earth-bearer, here Sumeru), a body that struck terror into the hearts of enemies in battle, of immense strength and valour.
tel: (దోహా ౧౫ తరువాతి చౌపాయి ౪) సీతమ్మ: అందుకే నా హృదయంలో గొప్ప సందేహం కలుగుతున్నది — నీవంటి వానరులు ఇటువంటి రాక్షసులను ఎలా జయిస్తారని. ఇది విని హనుమంతుడు తన నిజరూపాన్ని ప్రకటించాడు: బంగారు పర్వతము (సుమేరువు) ఆకారము గల విశాలదేహము, యుద్ధంలో శత్రువుల గుండెలు అదిరేలా చేసేది, అమిత బలము, వీరత్వము గలది.
hi: (दोहा १५ के बाद चौपाई ४) सीताजी: इसी से मेरे हृदय में बड़ा भारी सन्देह है कि तुम-जैसे वानर ऐसे राक्षसों को कैसे जीतेंगे। यह सुनकर हनुमान्जी ने अपना [असली] शरीर प्रकट किया — सोने के पर्वत (सुमेरु) के आकार का विशाल शरीर, जो युद्ध में शत्रुओं के हृदय में भय उत्पन्न करने वाला, अत्यन्त बलवान् और वीर था।

--- verse 4 | section: Hanumān reassures Sītā ---
deva:
सीता मन भरोस तब भयऊ।
पुनि लघु रूप पवनसुत लयऊ॥
iast:
sītā mana bharosa taba bhayaū|
puni laghu rūpa pavanasuta layaū||
en: (caupāī 5 after dohā 15) Then, seeing that form, confidence arose in Sītā's mind; and the son of the wind (Pavanasuta, Hanumān) took on his small shape again.
tel: (దోహా ౧౫ తరువాతి చౌపాయి ౫) ఆ రూపాన్ని చూసి అప్పుడు సీతమ్మ మనసులో నమ్మకం కలిగింది; పవనసుతుడు మళ్ళీ చిన్న రూపాన్నే ధరించాడు.
hi: (दोहा १५ के बाद चौपाई ५) तब [वह रूप देखकर] सीताजी के मन में विश्वास हुआ; और पवनसुत हनुमान्जी ने फिर छोटा रूप धारण कर लिया।

--- verse 5 | section: Sītā's blessing ---
deva:
सुनु माता साखामृग नहिं बल बुद्धि बिसाल।
प्रभु प्रताप तें गरुड़हि खाइ परम लघु ब्याल॥
मन संतोष सुनत कपि बानी।
भगति प्रताप तेज बल सानी॥
आसिष दीन्हि रामप्रिय जाना।
होहु तात बल सील निधाना॥
iast:
sunu mātā sākhāmṛga nahiṁ bala buddhi bisāla|
prabhu pratāpa teṁ garuṛahi khāi parama laghu byāla||
mana saṁtoṣa sunata kapi bānī|
bhagati pratāpa teja bala sānī||
āsiṣa dīnhi rāmapriya jānā|
hohu tāta bala sīla nidhānā||
en: (dohā 16, with the caupāī 1 that follows it) Hanumān: Listen, mother — we branch-beasts (sākhāmṛga, "tree-deer", the poem's standing epithet for monkeys) have no great strength or intellect of our own; but by the lord's majesty the very smallest snake can devour Garuḍa — that is, by his power the utterly weak can destroy the mightiest. Hearing that speech of the monkey, steeped in devotion, majesty, radiance and strength, Sītā's mind was satisfied; and knowing him to be dear to Rāma she gave him her blessing: may you be a treasury of strength and of good conduct, my child.
tel: (దోహా ౧౬, దాని తరువాతి చౌపాయి ౧తో కలిపి) హనుమంతుడు: అమ్మా విను, శాఖామృగాలకు (కొమ్మలలో తిరిగే మృగాలు, అనగా వానరులు) పెద్ద బలముకాని బుద్ధికాని ఉండవు; కాని ప్రభువు ప్రతాపము వలన అతిచిన్న పామైనా గరుడుని మింగగలదు — అనగా ఆయన శక్తితో అత్యంత బలహీనుడు కూడా మహాబలవంతుని సంహరించగలడు. భక్తి, ప్రతాపము, తేజస్సు, బలము నిండిన ఆ వానరుని మాట విని సీతమ్మ మనసు సంతోషించింది; ఇతడు రామునికి ప్రియుడని తెలిసి ఆమె ఆశీర్వదించింది — నాయనా, నీవు బలమునకూ శీలమునకూ నిధివి కమ్ము.
hi: (दोहा १६ तथा उसके बाद की चौपाई १) हनुमान्जी: हे माता! सुनो, वानरों में बहुत बल-बुद्धि नहीं होती; परन्तु प्रभु के प्रताप से बहुत छोटा साँप भी गरुड़ को खा सकता है — अर्थात् उनकी शक्ति से अत्यन्त निर्बल भी महान् बलवान् को मार सकता है। भक्ति, प्रताप, तेज और बल से सनी हुई हनुमान्जी की यह वाणी सुनकर सीताजी के मन में सन्तोष हुआ, और उन्हें श्रीरामजी का प्रिय जानकर उन्होंने आशीर्वाद दिया — हे तात! तुम बल और शील के निधान होओ।

--- verse 6 | section: Sītā's blessing ---
deva:
अजर अमर गुननिधि सुत होहू।
करहुँ बहुत रघुनायक छोहू॥
करहुँ कृपा प्रभु अस सुनि काना।
निर्भर प्रेम मगन हनुमाना॥
iast:
ajara amara gunanidhi suta hohū|
karahum̐ bahuta raghunāyaka chohū||
karahum̐ kṛpā prabhu asa suni kānā|
nirbhara prema magana hanumānā||
en: (caupāī 2 after dohā 16) Sītā: Be ageless, my son, be deathless, be a treasure-house of virtues; and may the lord of Raghu's line show you much favour (chohū, tenderness). The instant those words "may the lord show you grace" fell on his ears, Hanumān was drowned in unmixed, brimming love — the ṭīkā reads nirbhara as fullness, love with nothing else left in it.
tel: (దోహా ౧౬ తరువాతి చౌపాయి ౨) సీతమ్మ: కుమారా, నీవు అజరుడవు (ముసలితనము లేనివాడవు), అమరుడవు, గుణాలకు నిధి అయినవాడవు కమ్ము; రఘునాయకుడు నీపై ఎంతో దయ చూపుగాక. "ప్రభువు కృప చేయుగాక" అన్న ఆ మాట చెవిన పడగానే హనుమంతుడు నిండైన ప్రేమలో మునిగిపోయాడు — "నిర్భర" అనగా వేరొకటేదీ మిగలని పరిపూర్ణత అని భావార్థము.
hi: (दोहा १६ के बाद चौपाई २) सीताजी: हे पुत्र! तुम अजर (बुढ़ापे से रहित), अमर और गुणों के खजाने होओ; श्रीरघुनाथजी तुम पर बहुत कृपा करें। "प्रभु कृपा करें" — यह वचन कानों से सुनते ही हनुमान्जी पूर्ण (निर्भर) प्रेम में मगन हो गये; निर्भर का अर्थ यहाँ वह भरापूरापन है जिसमें और कुछ शेष ही न रहे।

--- verse 7 | section: Sītā's blessing ---
deva:
बार बार नाएसि पद सीसा।
बोला बचन जोरि कर कीसा॥
अब कृतकृत्य भयउँ मैं माता।
आसिष तव अमोघ बिख्याता॥
iast:
bāra bāra nāesi pada sīsā|
bolā bacana jori kara kīsā||
aba kṛtakṛtya bhayaum̐ maiṁ mātā|
āsiṣa tava amogha bikhyātā||
en: (caupāī 3 after dohā 16) Again and again the monkey (kīsa) bowed his head at Sītā's feet, and then with folded hands spoke: mother, now I have become kṛtakṛtya — one whose purpose is accomplished, who has nothing left to do; for it is famously known that your blessing is amogha, unfailing, an arrow that never misses.

परम सुभट रजनीचर भारी॥

sunahu mātu mohi atisaya bhūkhā|

en: (caupāī 4 after dohā 16) Hanumān: Listen, mother — seeing these trees with their beautiful fruit, a very great hunger has come upon me. --- verse 10 | section: Leave to eat the fruit ---

देखि बुद्धि बल निपुन कपि कहेउ जानकीं जाहु।
रघुपति चरन हृदयँ धरि तात मधुर फल खाहु॥

रहे तहाँ बहु भट रखवारे।
कछु मारेसि कछु जाइ पुकारे॥
iast:
dekhi buddhi bala nipuna kapi kaheu jānakīṁ jāhu|
raghupati carana hṛdayam̐ dhari tāta madhura phala khāhu||

en: (dohā 17, with the caupāī 1 that follows it) Seeing the monkey accomplished in both intelligence and strength, Jānakī said: go — and holding the feet of Raghupati in your heart, eat the sweet fruit, child. Many warriors were posted there as guards: some of them he killed, and some went off to raise the alarm.

--- verse 11 | section: The Aśoka grove destroyed ---
deva:

en: (caupāī 2 after dohā 17) The guards to Rāvaṇa: Lord, one huge monkey has come, and he has laid the Aśoka grove waste — he has eaten the fruit, torn the trees up by their roots, and crushed the watchmen and flung them on the ground. --- verse 19 | section: The brahmāstra and the binding ---

en: (dohā 19, with the caupāī 1 that follows it) At last Meghanāda fitted the brahmāstra to his bow, and the monkey reflected in his mind: if I do not honour the arrow of Brahmā, its boundless glory will be wiped out. So he let it strike — the weapon's power over him is a courtesy, not a defeat. Meghanāda loosed the brahma-arrow at him, and Hanumān fell from the tree; but even as he fell (paratihuṁ bāra) he destroyed a great part of the army. en: (dohā 20, with the caupāī 1 that follows it) Looking at the monkey, the ten-faced one laughed loudly, hurling abuse; then the memory of his son's killing came to him, and grief welled up in his heart. The lord of Laṅkā said: who are you, monkey? On whose strength did you lay the grove waste and ruin it? Or have you never heard of me with your own ears? Fool, I see you standing here altogether without fear.

Recension note:
This is Awadhi (Avadhī), the eastern Hindi literary dialect of Tulsīdās, not Sanskrit. The IAST above transliterates the Devanāgarī mechanically and as printed — abahiṁ, jāuṁ, kachuka, aihahiṁ, gaihahiṁ, kīnhisi, paratihuṁ — and deliberately does not Sanskritise the forms; Awadhi verb endings (-esi, -eu, -ahiṁ, -auṁ), the Awadhi lexicon (kīsa, markaṭa, sākhāmṛga for the monkey; nisicara, rajanīcara, jātudhāna for the demon), and the poem's tadbhava spellings (bisāla for viśāla, biṭapa for viṭapa, gyānī for jñānī, byāla for vyāla) are all kept exactly as the edition sets them.

The division between mūl and ṭīkā in this file is the edition's own, not editorial. In the Gītā Press setting the verse is printed in bold at 19.7pt and the Hindi bhāvārth in regular weight at 16pt, and the two were separated by that typeface distinction when the text layer was decoded; every line placed in a deva: field is a line the press itself set as verse, and nothing that the press set as commentary has been promoted into the mūl. The unit boundaries and the closing verse-numbers likewise follow the printed numerals, which is why the dohā and the caupāī numbered 1 after it stand together as one unit: that is how this edition sets them.

The caupāīs that complete dohā 15 are included here because the file boundary falls at dohā 16; dohā 15 itself and its first caupāī belong to the preceding file, and the two files together carry the sequence without a gap or an overlap.
