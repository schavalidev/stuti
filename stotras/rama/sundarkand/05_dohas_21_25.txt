Blurb: This is a turning point of the Sundarakāṇḍa. Bound and dragged into the court, Hanumān answers Rāvaṇa's sneer not with a boast about himself but with a single enormous relative clause about his master. It is he whose power māyā borrows to build the universes; by whose strength Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Śiva create, sustain and destroy; upon whose strength the thousand-hooded Śeṣa carries the cosmic egg with its mountains and forests. Only at the end of it does he say: I am his messenger, and his wife is the woman you carried off. What follows is the most patient piece of counsel in the poem, a monkey preaching bhakti to the emperor of the rākṣasas. Give her back. Take the shelter of Rāma. Do not become the stain on the clear moon of Pulastya's fame. Rāvaṇa laughs, calls him a very learned guru, and orders the tail set on fire. The poem's oldest reversal then closes: the fire will not touch the messenger of the one who made fire, and the city burns instead.

pāi jāsu bala biracati māyā||
en: (caupāī 2, following dohā 20) Rāvaṇa continues: For what offence did you kill my night-rangers (nisicara, the rākṣasas)? Speak, fool — have you no fear of losing your life? [Hanumān replied:] Listen, Rāvaṇa — he whose strength māyā receives before she fashions the whole array of universes;

hi: (दोहा २० के बाद की चौपाई २) रावण आगे कहता है: तूने किस अपराध से राक्षसों को मारा? रे मूर्ख, बता — क्या तुझे प्राण जाने का भय नहीं है? [हनुमान्‌जी ने कहा—] हे रावण, सुन; जिनका बल पाकर माया सम्पूर्ण ब्रह्माण्डों के समूहों की रचना करती है;

--- verse 5 | section: Rāvaṇa's challenge answered ---
deva:
जाके बल लवलेस तें जितेहु चराचर झारि।
तासु दूत मैं जा करि हरि आनेहु प्रिय नारि॥
जानउँ मैं तुम्हारि प्रभुताई।
सहसबाहु सन परी लराई॥
समर बालि सन करि जसु पावा।

jāke bala lavalesa teṁ jitehu carācara jhāri|

en: (dohā 21, then caupāī 1) By the merest particle of whose strength you conquered the whole world of moving and unmoving beings, and whose beloved wife you carried off by stealth — of him I am the messenger. [Caupāī:] I know your lordship well: you had a fight with Sahasrabāhu (the thousand-armed Kārtavīryārjuna), and you won your fame by making war on Vālī. Hearing the monkey's pointed words, Rāvaṇa laughed and turned the matter aside.
tel: (దోహా ౨౧, పిమ్మట చౌపాయి ౧) ఎవరి బలములోని లేశమాత్రముచేత నీవు చరాచర జగత్తునంతటిని జయించితివో, ఎవరి ప్రియపత్నిని నీవు [దొంగతనముగా] హరించి తెచ్చితివో — ఆయనకే నేను దూతను. [చౌపాయి:] నీ ప్రభుత్వమును నేను బాగుగా ఎరుగుదును — సహస్రబాహువుతో (కార్తవీర్యార్జునునితో) నీకు యుద్ధము జరిగినది, వాలితో పోరాడి యశము పొందితివి. కపి యొక్క ఆ మర్మపు మాటలు విని రావణుడు నవ్వి మాటను దాటవేసెను.
hi: (दोहा २१, फिर चौपाई १) जिनके लेशमात्र बल से तुमने समस्त चराचर जगत् को जीत लिया और जिनकी प्रिय पत्नी को तुम [चोरी से] हर लाये हो, मैं उन्हीं का दूत हूँ। [चौपाई:] मैं तुम्हारी प्रभुता को खूब जानता हूँ — सहस्रबाहु से तुम्हारी लड़ाई हुई थी और बालि से युद्ध करके तुमने यश प्राप्त किया था। हनुमान्‌जी के मार्मिक वचन सुनकर रावण ने हँसकर बात टाल दी।

Recension note:
(a) The language of this text is Awadhi, not Sanskrit. The `hi:` field is therefore a genuine translation into modern Hindi, not a re-transcription of the `deva:` line, and it follows the Gītā Press edition's own printed भावार्थ rather than an independent construal of the Awadhi. The IAST likewise transliterates the Awadhi as printed — forms such as `jānaum̐`, `bām̐dheum̐`, `toreum̐`, `mūṛha`, `baṛa`, `caṛheu`, `bhaiṁ` are left exactly as the edition sets them and have not been normalised toward Sanskrit or toward Khaṛī Bolī spellings. Candrabindu is rendered `m̐` and anusvāra `ṁ`, so that the edition's own distinction between the two survives in transliteration.

(b) The mūl was separated from the ṭīkā mechanically, by typeface rather than by editorial judgement: in this edition the verse is set in bold at 19.7pt and the Hindi commentary in regular weight at 16pt, and the split was made on that font signature alone. The division of the text into the units below is therefore the edition's own — its printed unit boundaries and its own closing numerals — and nothing here reflects a decision by this corpus about where a caupāī begins or ends.
