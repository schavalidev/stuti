Title: Pārāyaṇa-vidhi — the order of worship prefixed to the Sundarakāṇḍa
Devanāgarī: पारायण-विधि (श्रीरामचरितमानस – सुन्दरकाण्ड के पूर्व)
Telugu: పారాయణ-విధి (శ్రీరామచరితమానస – సుందరకాండకు పూర్వం)

Author: not a composed stotra but a prayoga — the Gītā Press, Gorakhpur editors' own pārāyaṇa sequence for this Sundarakāṇḍa volume (Gītā Press code 1349), assembled from traditional material. The eleven āvāhana verses are anonymous Sanskrit invocation ślokas of the kind printed before a Mānasa recitation (unit 9 is a sragdharā-class dhyāna in a longer metre, the rest anuṣṭubh); the viniyoga names Śiva, Kākabhuśuṇḍi, Yājñavalkya and Gosvāmī Tulsīdās as the ṛṣis of the Mānasa itself. The six nyāsa half-lines and the nine dhyāna couplets are not Sanskrit at all but Awadhi lines lifted out of the Rāmacaritamānasa and put to ritual use, which is this vidhi's most distinctive feature. Gosvāmī Tulsīdās is the author of the Awadhi; the arrangement is the press's.

Blurb: This is ritual procedure rather than text. It is the four printed pages that stand between the title page and the first śloka of the Sundarakāṇḍa. They tell a reciter who intends a formal pārāyaṇa exactly what to do before opening the book. The order is unusual and deserves notice: the poets are installed before the gods. Tulsīdās is invoked first and seated in the south-west, and Vālmīki in the north-east. Śiva is seated in the south-east. Then Lakṣmaṇa, Śatrughna and Bharata are each seated, each 'with your beloved', at the compass points around the seat. Hanumān is seated in the east. Only then is Rāma seated with Jānakī in the centre, for the sixteen-fold pūjā. What follows is the ordinary tāntrika apparatus, namely viniyoga, ācamana, prāṇāyāma, karanyāsa, aṅganyāsa and dhyāna, but with the mantras replaced by Awadhi. Six half-lines of the Mānasa are placed on the thumbs, the forefingers, the middle fingers, the ring fingers, the little fingers and the palms. The identical six are then placed again on the heart, the head, the śikhā, the armour, the eyes and the weapon. The repetition is the rite, and it is left standing here rather than collapsed. The section ends with nine couplets of dhyāna. In them Rāma is addressed as lotus-eyed, as dark as the blue lotus, as the bee at the honey of the devotee's heart-lotus, and finally as the destroyer of the filth of the Kali age and of the sense of 'I and mine'. The last words are: Tulsīdās's Lord, protect this suppliant.

|| śrīhariḥ||
pārāyaṇa-vidhi
vidhipūrvaka pāṭha karanevāle mahānubhāvoṁko pāṭhārambhake pūrva śrītulasīdāsajī, śrīvālmīkijī, śrīśivajī tathā śrīhanumānjīkā āvāhana-pūjana karaneke paścāt tīnoṁ bhāiyoṁsahita śrīsītārāmajīkā āvāhana, ṣoḍaśopacāra-pūjana aura dhyāna karanā cāhiye| tadanantara pāṭhakā ārambha karanā cāhiye| sabake āvāhana, pūjana aura dhyānake mantra kramaśaḥ nīce likhe jāte haiṁ—
en: Śrī Hari. The order of a recitation. Those good people who mean to recite according to the prescribed procedure should, before beginning the reading, first perform the invocation and worship of Śrī Tulsīdāsjī, Śrī Vālmīkijī, Śrī Śivajī and Śrī Hanumānjī, and after that perform the invocation, the sixteen-fold worship (ṣoḍaśopacāra) and the meditation of Śrī Sītā-Rāmajī together with the three brothers; only then should the reading itself begin. The mantras for the invocation, worship and meditation of all of them are written out below in order. — This opening rubric is the Gītā Press editors' own modern Hindi, and it fixes the sequence that the rest of the section carries out: the poets and Śiva and Hanumān first, then Rāma with Sītā and the brothers, then the pāṭha.
tel: శ్రీహరి. పారాయణ విధి. విధిపూర్వకంగా పాఠం చేయదలచిన మహానుభావులు, పాఠారంభానికి ముందు మొదట శ్రీతులసీదాసుజీ, శ్రీవాల్మీకిజీ, శ్రీశివుడు, శ్రీహనుమంతుల ఆవాహన-పూజనం చేసి, ఆ తరువాత ముగ్గురు సోదరులతో కూడిన శ్రీసీతారాములకు ఆవాహనం, షోడశోపచార పూజ, ధ్యానం చేయాలి. ఆ పిమ్మటనే పాఠాన్ని ఆరంభించాలి. అందరి ఆవాహన, పూజన, ధ్యాన మంత్రాలు వరుసగా క్రింద ఇవ్వబడుతున్నాయి. — ఈ ప్రారంభ సూచన గీతా ప్రెస్ సంపాదకుల స్వంత ఆధునిక హిందీ గద్యం; ఈ ప్రకరణమంతా అనుసరించే క్రమాన్ని అదే నిర్ణయిస్తుంది — ముందు కవులు, శివుడు, హనుమంతుడు; తరువాత సీతారాములు, సోదరులు; ఆ తరువాత పాఠం.
hi: श्रीहरिः। पारायण-विधि। विधिपूर्वक पाठ करने वाले महानुभावों को पाठ आरंभ करने से पहले पहले श्रीतुलसीदासजी, श्रीवाल्मीकिजी, श्रीशिवजी तथा श्रीहनुमानजी का आवाहन एवं पूजन करना चाहिए, उसके बाद तीनों भाइयों सहित श्रीसीतारामजी का आवाहन, षोडशोपचार-पूजन और ध्यान करना चाहिए; तदनन्तर ही पाठ का आरंभ करना चाहिए। सबके आवाहन, पूजन और ध्यान के मंत्र क्रमशः नीचे लिखे जा रहे हैं। — यह आरंभिक सूचना गीताप्रेस के सम्पादकों का अपना आधुनिक हिंदी गद्य है, और यही आगे के समस्त प्रकरण का क्रम निश्चित करती है।

en: Tulsī, salutation to you; come here, you of pure vows. Take your seat in the south-west (nairṛta) quarter and accept this worship. — Oṁ, salutation to Tulsīdāsa. The rite opens by installing the poet himself, and installing him first of all: the Sundarakāṇḍa is his composition, and the reciter approaches it through him. The vocative is printed "तुलसीक" (tulasīka), not the तुलसीश one would expect; the print's reading is kept — see the recension note. The line in the regular face is the namaḥ-formula recited while the flowers are offered, and it is part of the rite, not a caption.

en: Śrī Vālmīka, salutation to you; come here, giver of good. Stand in the middle of north and east (that is, the north-east, īśāna corner) and receive my worship. — Oṁ, salutation to Vālmīka. The ādi-kavi is seated second: the Mānasa is a retelling, and the Rāmāyaṇa's first author is honoured before the retelling is read. The print gives the stem as "श्रीवाल्मीक" (vocative of vālmīka) rather than the more usual वाल्मीकि, and it does so consistently in both the verse and the formula; the printed reading stands.

Dedup: the twelve nyāsa half-lines and the nine dhyāna couplets are all lines of the Rāmacaritamānasa quoted here for ritual use, and several of them are among the most widely circulated single lines of the poem (notably "उमा दारु जोषित की नाइं" and "सनमुख होइ जीव मोहि जबहीं"). They are not duplicates of any standalone stotra in this corpus, and their home kāṇḍas are outside the Sundarakāṇḍa files that follow, so nothing here overlaps 01_dohas_01_05.txt onward. No retirement is proposed; the overlap with the Mānasa proper is recorded for cross-reference only.
