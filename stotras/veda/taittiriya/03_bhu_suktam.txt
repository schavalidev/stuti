Blurb: This is the hymn to Bhū-devī, the Earth, recited fourth in the pañca-sūkta set at abhiṣeka. It is a compilation rather than a single hymn, and it praises the Earth under many names — Medinī, Vasundharā, Vasudhā, Mahī, Dharā. She is called the mother of the gods, the gandharvas, men, the forefathers and the asuras alike. The worshipper asks her to be kind, free of thorns, and a place of rest, and to grant wide shelter. Two later verses describe her form, holding a fresh sheaf of rice and a parrot, seated in a pavilion of gold and jewels.
