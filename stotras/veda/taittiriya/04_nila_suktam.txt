Title: Nīḷā Sūktam (Taittirīya, Kṛṣṇa-Yajurveda)
Devanāgarī: नीळासूक्तम् (तैत्तिरीयम्)
Telugu: నీళా సూక్తం (తైత్తిరీయం)

Author: No human author. Vedic — Taittirīya Saṁhitā 4.4.12, the anuvāka numbered 37 in the continuous numbering of that kāṇḍa. The devatā is the consort of Viṣṇu, called Nīḷā in the liturgical tradition, who is addressed in the text itself as `विष्णुपत्नी`.

Recension / śākhā: **Kṛṣṇa-Yajurveda, Taittirīya śākhā — the South Indian form.** The hymn belongs to the Śrīvaiṣṇava liturgy and has no counterpart in the Śukla-Yajurveda daily rite. Per the śākhā rule in the repo-root `CLAUDE.md`, it is not collated against, or supplied from, any other śākhā.

Source / recension: Base text: **stotranidhi.com**, `https://stotranidhi.com/hi/neela-suktam-in-hindi/` (accented Devanāgarī). Independent collation: the **accented Taittirīya Saṁhitā on sanskritdocuments.org** (`doc_veda/taittirIyasamhitA.itx`, category "veda, svara"), TS 4.4.12, anuvāka 37. Recension note: **Two defects in the base text were corrected from the Taittirīya Saṁhitā, and are the only changes made.** (1) In ṛc 3 stotranidhi prints `अ॒स्येश्या॑ना`, which is not a word; the Saṁhitā reads `अ॒स्येशा॑ना` (`asyeśānā`, "the ruler of this"). stotranidhi itself prints the correct `ईशाना` at the same word in ṛc 2, so this is a slip in its own page rather than a variant reading, and the Saṁhitā form has been restored. (2) In ṛc 2 stotranidhi prints a stray hyphen inside `बृह॒स्पति॑-र्मात॒रिश्वो॒त`; the hyphen has been removed. No other character was altered and no accent mark was added or changed. **On the extent of the text.** The Saṁhitā's anuvāka continues past the point where stotranidhi stops, with `विश्वव्यचा इषयन्ती सुभूतिः` and `वैश्वानरो न ऊत्या`. The recited Nīḷā Sūkta is the shorter liturgical excerpt, and this file prints what the base text prints rather than extending it from the Saṁhitā. **On the first word.** `गृणाहि` belongs at the end of the preceding anuvāka in the Saṁhitā, where it completes `अभि नो गृणाहि`. The liturgical text begins with it, and stotranidhi prints it as the opening; it is kept here in that position. **In the `iast:` field accent marks are omitted**, following `vidhi/taittiriya/01_krishna_yajurveda_sandhyavandanam.txt`. **Cross-references:** this is the last hymn of the pañca-sūkta set — `veda/taittiriya/01_purusha_suktam.txt`, `02_narayana_suktam.txt`, `devi/lakshmi/06_sri_suktam.txt`, `03_bhu_suktam.txt` and this file.

Verse count: 4 — the three ṛcs of the sūkta, with the closing śānti-pāṭha as the fourth unit. The sūkta proper is three ṛcs.

Sections:
नीळासूक्तम् — Nīḷā Sūktam (the three ṛcs, TS 4.4.12)
शान्तिपाठः — Śānti-pāṭha (the closing peace invocation)

Blurb: This is the shortest of the five sūktas recited at abhiṣeka. It praises the consort of Viṣṇu. She stands firm among the directions, she is the pillar that holds up the sky and the support that holds up the earth, and she rules this moving world. The hymn asks Savitṛ that the quarters be rich in ghee and milk, and asks Bṛhaspati, Mātariśvan and the winds to join in the praise. She is called Nīḷā in the liturgy; the text itself calls her only the consort of Viṣṇu.

--- verse 1 | section: Nīḷā Sūktam ---
deva:
ओं गृ॒णा॒हि॒ ।
घृ॒तव॑ती सवित॒राधि॑पत्यै॒ः पय॑स्वती॒रन्ति॒राशा॑नो अस्तु ।
iast:
oṁ gṛṇāhi |
ghṛtavatī savitarādhipatyaiḥ payasvatīrantirāśāno astu |
en: TS 4.4.12, ṛc 1. Sing forth. O Savitṛ, through your lordship may the quarters be rich in ghee and rich in milk, and may they stand near to us.
tel: తైత్తిరీయ సంహిత 4.4.12, మొదటి ఋక్కు. స్తుతింపుము. ఓ సవితృదేవా, నీ ఆధిపత్యము వలన దిక్కులు ఘృతముతోను పాలతోను నిండియుండును గాక. అవి మాకు దగ్గఱగా ఉండును గాక.
hi: तैत्तिरीय संहिता 4.4.12, पहली ऋचा। स्तुति कीजिए। हे सवितृ देव, आपके आधिपत्य से दिशाएँ घृत और दूध से भरी रहें, और वे हमारे समीप रहें।

--- verse 2 | section: Nīḷā Sūktam ---
deva:
ध्रु॒वा दि॒शां विष्णु॑प॒त्न्यघो॑रा॒ऽस्येशा॑ना॒सह॑सो॒या म॒नोता᳚ ।
बृह॒स्पति॑र्मात॒रिश्वो॒त वा॒युस्स॑न्धुवा॒नावाता॑ अ॒भि नो॑ गृणन्तु ।
iast:
dhruvā diśāṁ viṣṇupatnyaghorā'syeśānāsahasoyā manotā |
bṛhaspatirmātariśvota vāyussandhuvānāvātā abhi no gṛṇantu |
en: Ṛc 2. She stands firm among the directions. She is the consort of Viṣṇu. She is not fierce. She is the ruler, and she is the thought of strength. May Bṛhaspati, Mātariśvan, Vāyu and the winds blowing together sing to us.
tel: రెండవ ఋక్కు. ఆమె దిక్కులయందు స్థిరముగా నిలిచియున్నది. ఆమె విష్ణుపత్ని. ఆమె ఉగ్రురాలు కాదు. ఆమె ఈశానురాలు. ఆమె బలమునకు మనసు వంటిది. బృహస్పతియు, మాతరిశ్వుడును, వాయువును, కలిసి వీచు వాయువులును మనకు స్తుతి చేయుదురు గాక.
hi: दूसरी ऋचा। वे दिशाओं में स्थिर हैं। वे विष्णु की पत्नी हैं। वे उग्र नहीं हैं। वे ईशाना हैं, और बल की मनीषा हैं। बृहस्पति, मातरिश्वा, वायु और साथ बहने वाली वायुएँ हमारे लिए स्तुति करें।

en: Ṛc 3. She is the pillar of heaven and the support of the earth. She is the ruler of this moving world. She is the consort of Viṣṇu.

hi: तीसरी ऋचा। वे द्युलोक का स्तम्भ हैं। वे पृथ्वी का आधार हैं। वे इस चराचर जगत् की ईशाना हैं। वे विष्णु की पत्नी हैं।
