Title: Gāyatrī-mālā — the Gāyatrī mantras of the deities (Taittirīya, Kṛṣṇa-Yajurveda)
Devanāgarī: गायत्रीमाला — देवतागायत्रीमन्त्राः (तैत्तिरीयम्)
Telugu: గాయత్రీమాలా — దేవతా గాయత్రీ మంత్రాః (తైత్తిరీయం)

Author: No human author. Vedic — Taittirīya Āraṇyaka, prapāṭhaka 10, anuvāka 1, units 5 to 7. Prapāṭhaka 10 is the Mahānārāyaṇa Upaniṣad. Each mantra is addressed to a different devatā.

Recension / śākhā: **Kṛṣṇa-Yajurveda, Taittirīya śākhā — the South Indian form.** This is not a universal text. Other traditions recite deity gāyatrīs of their own, and their wording and accent are not the same. Per the śākhā rule in the repo-root `CLAUDE.md`, this file is not collated against Śukla-Yajurveda or any other tradition and does not stand for them.

Source / recension: Base text: **stotranidhi.com**, `https://stotranidhi.com/hi/vividha-gayatri-mantrani-in-hindi/` (accented Devanāgarī, eighteen mantras). Independent collation against **two further witnesses, both accented and both in this same śākhā**: (1) the **Taittirīya Āraṇyaka on sanskritdocuments.org**, `doc_veda/taittirIyaAraNyaka.itx`, at TĀ 10.1.5–7, which is the Āraṇyaka itself with its pāda numbering; and (2) the **Mahānārāyaṇa Upaniṣad with Vedic accent** on the same site, `doc_upanishhat/mahAnArAyaNopaniShatsasvarA.itx`, encoded by different volunteers from a different printed exemplar, where these stand as units 22 to 33. The two sanskritdocuments files are hosted together but are not copies of one another — they disagree at three places, listed below, which is what shows them to be separate transcriptions. **Result of the collation: with accent marks stripped, the eleven mantras common to all three witnesses agree word for word, apart from the three places recorded in the note below.** Gītā Press was checked on archive.org and publishes no edition of this text; its Gāyatrī volumes are on the Sāvitrī Gāyatrī and on sandhyā, not on the deity gāyatrīs.

Recension note: **One mantra is present in two witnesses and absent from the base text.** The Āraṇyaka and the Mahānārāyaṇa Upaniṣad both open the garland with `पुरु॑षस्य विद्म सहस्रा॒क्षस्य॑ महादे॒वस्य॑ धीमहि` at TĀ 10.1.5; stotranidhi's page begins at the next mantra. It is printed here as unit 1, and its accent is transcribed from the Āraṇyaka witness — the same śākhā, so the svara rule permits it. Nothing was inferred or reconstructed. **Three word-level differences, printed in round brackets in the line itself.** At unit 9 the base text and the Upaniṣad read `महद्द्युतिक॒राय॑` while the Āraṇyaka reads `महद्युतिक॒राय॑` with a single `द्`; the corpus's own `puja/smarta/11_surya_nitya_puja.txt` prints a third reading, `महाद्युतिकराय`, and that file is not touched here. At unit 11 the base text and the Āraṇyaka read `कन्यकु॒मारि॑` while the Upaniṣad reads `कन्याकु॒मारि॑`. At unit 18 the base text reads `तन्नो॑ सर्पः` and the Upaniṣad reads `तन्नः सर्पः`; the second is the form sandhi requires before `स्`, and it is given in brackets. In each place the base reading stands first. **On the six mantras of the second section.** The Mahānārāyaṇa Upaniṣad prints them under the heading `पाठभेदः`, that is, as a variant reading, and the Āraṇyaka does not carry them at all. They are therefore kept in a section of their own and are not presented as part of TĀ 10.1. **On their accent.** stotranidhi marks only the two words `वि॒द्महे॑` and `प्रचो॒दया᳚त्` in these six and leaves the rest bare; the Upaniṣad prints all six wholly without accent. The base text is transcribed exactly as it stands, and no mark was added to it or taken from it. **On saṁhitā sandhi.** The Āraṇyaka runs unit 5 together as `तन्न॑ष्षण्मुखः`, where the base text prints `तन्नः षण्मुखः`. That is a pāṭha difference inside one śākhā, not a disagreement of reading. **On the eighteenth mantra of the base page.** stotranidhi's page ends with the Lakṣmī gāyatrī, `महादेव्यै च विद्महे विष्णुपत्नी च धीमहि`. Neither Vedic witness carries it, because it does not belong to TĀ 10.1 — it belongs to the Śrī Sūkta. It is therefore not printed here. The corpus already holds it, at the end of `devi/lakshmi/06_sri_suktam.txt`. **On the word `लालीलाय` at unit 10.** All three witnesses print it and the commentators explain it variously. It is left untranslated in the three translation fields, since no rendering of it is certain. **Cross-references:** the tāntric chapter of fifty-four deity gāyatrīs from the Mantra-mahārṇava stands at `devi/gayatri/17_gayatri_patala_mantra_maharnava.txt`, and several of these mantras stand there in another wording; neither file supersedes the other. The Sūrya gāyatrī of unit 9 is recited in `puja/smarta/11_surya_nitya_puja.txt`, and a Gaṇapati gāyatrī with `एकद॒न्ताय॑` in place of `तत्पुरु॑षाय` in `puja/smarta/04_ganapati_nitya_puja.txt`. Neither file is altered by this one.

Verse count: 18 — twelve mantras from TĀ 10.1.5–7, and the six of the `पाठभेदः` block. stotranidhi's page prints eighteen as well, but a different eighteen: it omits the first of the twelve and adds the Lakṣmī gāyatrī at the end.

Sections:
गायत्रीमाला — Gāyatrī-mālā (the twelve mantras of TĀ 10.1.5–7)
पाठभेदः — Pāṭhabheda (six further mantras, printed as a variant reading)

Blurb: These are the Gāyatrī mantras of the deities. Each keeps the shape of the Sāvitrī Gāyatrī — first "may we know", then "may we meditate", then "may that deity impel us" — and fills it with the name and the mark of one god. Rudra, Gaṇeśa, Nandi, Skanda, Garuḍa, Brahman, Viṣṇu, Narasiṁha, Sūrya, Agni and Durgā are named in the first section, and Brahmā, Bhānu, Vaiśvānara, Bhagavatī, Gaurī and the serpent in the second. A worshipper recites the mantra of the deity being worshipped, and the priest uses them in the āvāhana and in japa.

--- verse 1 | section: गायत्रीमाला ---
deva:
पुरु॑षस्य विद्म सहस्रा॒क्षस्य॑ महादे॒वस्य॑ धीमहि ।
तन्नो॑ रुद्रः प्रचो॒दया᳚त् ॥
iast:
puruṣasya vidma sahasrākṣasya mahādevasya dhīmahi |
tanno rudraḥ pracodayāt ||
en: Rudra, the first of two. This is not the familiar Rudra gāyatrī. That one is the next unit. The Āraṇyaka prints this mantra immediately before it, at TĀ 10.1.5, and both end with the same words. May we know the Puruṣa. May we meditate on the great god of a thousand eyes. May Rudra impel us.
tel: రుద్రుడు, రెండింటిలో మొదటిది. ఇది సుపరిచితమైన రుద్ర గాయత్రి కాదు; అది తరువాతి మంత్రము. ఆరణ్యకము ఈ మంత్రమును దానికి ముందు తైత్తిరీయ ఆరణ్యకము 10.1.5 నందు ముద్రించును. రెండును ఒకే మాటలతో ముగియును. పురుషుని ఎరుగుదుము. వేయి కన్నులు కల మహాదేవుని ధ్యానించుదుము. ఆ రుద్రుడు మమ్ము ప్రేరేపించుగాక.
hi: रुद्र, दो में पहला। यह परिचित रुद्र गायत्री नहीं है। वह अगला मन्त्र है। आरण्यक इस मन्त्र को उससे ठीक पहले तैत्तिरीय आरण्यक 10.1.5 में छापता है। दोनों का अन्त एक ही शब्दों से होता है। पुरुष को हम जानें। सहस्र नेत्रों वाले महादेव का हम ध्यान करें। वे रुद्र हमें प्रेरित करें।

तन्नो॑ दुर्गिः प्रचो॒दया᳚त् ॥
iast:
kātyāyanāya vidmahe kanyakumāri(kanyākumāri) dhīmahi |
tanno durgiḥ pracodayāt ||
en: Durgā. May we know Kātyāyana. May we meditate on Kanyākumārī, the maiden. May Durgi impel us.
tel: దుర్గ. కాత్యాయనుని ఎరుగుదుము. కన్యాకుమారిని ధ్యానించుదుము. ఆ దుర్గి మమ్ము ప్రేరేపించుగాక.
hi: दुर्गा। कात्यायन को हम जानें। कन्याकुमारी का हम ध्यान करें। वे दुर्गि हमें प्रेरित करें।

--- verse 13 | section: पाठभेदः ---
deva:
चतुर्मुखाय वि॒द्महे॑ कमण्डलुधराय धीमहि ।
तन्नो॑ ब्रह्मा प्रचो॒दया᳚त् ॥
iast:
caturmukhāya vidmahe kamaṇḍaludharāya dhīmahi |
tanno brahmā pracodayāt ||
en: Brahmā. May we know the four-faced one. May we meditate on the one who holds the water-pot. May Brahmā impel us.
tel: బ్రహ్మ. చతుర్ముఖుని ఎరుగుదుము. కమండలువు ధరించినవానిని ధ్యానించుదుము. ఆ బ్రహ్మ మమ్ము ప్రేరేపించుగాక.
hi: ब्रह्मा। चतुर्मुख को हम जानें। कमण्डलु धारण करने वाले का हम ध्यान करें। वे ब्रह्मा हमें प्रेरित करें।

--- verse 14 | section: पाठभेदः ---
deva:
आदित्याय वि॒द्महे॑ सहस्रकिरणाय धीमहि ।
तन्नो॑ भानुः प्रचो॒दया᳚त् ॥
iast:
ādityāya vidmahe sahasrakiraṇāya dhīmahi |
tanno bhānuḥ pracodayāt ||
en: Sūrya. May we know Āditya. May we meditate on the one of a thousand rays. May Bhānu impel us.
tel: సూర్యుడు. ఆదిత్యుని ఎరుగుదుము. వేయి కిరణములు కలవానిని ధ్యానించుదుము. ఆ భానుడు మమ్ము ప్రేరేపించుగాక.
hi: सूर्य। आदित्य को हम जानें। सहस्र किरणों वाले का हम ध्यान करें। वे भानु हमें प्रेरित करें।

--- verse 15 | section: पाठभेदः ---
deva:
पावकाय वि॒द्महे॑ सप्तजिह्वाय धीमहि ।
तन्नो॑ वैश्वानरः प्रचो॒दया᳚त् ॥
iast:
pāvakāya vidmahe saptajihvāya dhīmahi |
tanno vaiśvānaraḥ pracodayāt ||
en: Agni. May we know Pāvaka, the one who purifies. May we meditate on the one of seven tongues. May Vaiśvānara impel us.
tel: అగ్ని. పవిత్రము చేయు పావకుని ఎరుగుదుము. ఏడు నాలుకలు కలవానిని ధ్యానించుదుము. ఆ వైశ్వానరుడు మమ్ము ప్రేరేపించుగాక.
hi: अग्नि। पवित्र करने वाले पावक को हम जानें। सात जिह्वाओं वाले का हम ध्यान करें। वे वैश्वानर हमें प्रेरित करें।

--- verse 16 | section: पाठभेदः ---
deva:
महाशूलिन्यै वि॒द्महे॑ महादुर्गायै धीमहि ।
तन्नो॑ भगवती प्रचो॒दया᳚त् ॥
iast:
mahāśūlinyai vidmahe mahādurgāyai dhīmahi |
tanno bhagavatī pracodayāt ||
en: Bhagavatī. May we know her who bears the great trident. May we meditate on Mahādurgā. May Bhagavatī impel us.
tel: భగవతి. మహాశూలము ధరించిన తల్లిని ఎరుగుదుము. మహాదుర్గను ధ్యానించుదుము. ఆ భగవతి మమ్ము ప్రేరేపించుగాక.
hi: भगवती। महाशूल धारण करने वाली को हम जानें। महादुर्गा का हम ध्यान करें। वे भगवती हमें प्रेरित करें।

--- verse 17 | section: पाठभेदः ---
deva:
सुभगायै वि॒द्महे॑ कमलमालिन्यै धीमहि ।
तन्नो॑ गौरी प्रचो॒दया᳚त् ॥
iast:
subhagāyai vidmahe kamalamālinyai dhīmahi |
tanno gaurī pracodayāt ||
en: Gaurī. May we know Subhagā, the one of good fortune. May we meditate on her who wears a garland of lotuses. May Gaurī impel us.
tel: గౌరి. సుభగను ఎరుగుదుము. కమలముల మాల ధరించిన తల్లిని ధ్యానించుదుము. ఆ గౌరి మమ్ము ప్రేరేపించుగాక.
hi: गौरी। सुभगा को हम जानें। कमलों की माला धारण करने वाली का हम ध्यान करें। वे गौरी हमें प्रेरित करें।

--- verse 18 | section: पाठभेदः ---
deva:
नवकुलाय वि॒द्महे॑ विषदन्ताय धीमहि ।
तन्नो॑(तन्नः) सर्पः प्रचो॒दया᳚त् ॥
iast:
navakulāya vidmahe viṣadantāya dhīmahi |
tanno(tannaḥ) sarpaḥ pracodayāt ||
en: The serpent. May we know the one of the nine serpent families. May we meditate on the one whose fangs hold poison. May the serpent impel us.
tel: సర్పము. నవకులముల వానిని ఎరుగుదుము. విషపు కోఱలు కలవానిని ధ్యానించుదుము. ఆ సర్పము మమ్ము ప్రేరేపించుగాక.
hi: सर्प। नव कुलों वाले को हम जानें। विष भरे दाँतों वाले का हम ध्यान करें। वे सर्प हमें प्रेरित करें।
