Devanāgarī: नारायणसूक्तम् (तैत्तिरीयम्)

Author: No human author. Vedic — Taittirīya Āraṇyaka, prapāṭhaka 10, anuvāka 13 (the units numbered 29 and 30 in the continuous numbering of the Āraṇyaka). Prapāṭhaka 10 is the Mahānārāyaṇa Upaniṣad. The devatā is Nārāyaṇa.

Recension note: **One difference between the two witnesses.** At `नारायण परो ज्योतिरात्मा नारायणः परः` the sanskritdocuments file prints `नारयणः` with a short second vowel. stotranidhi's `नारायणः` is followed, because the short form is not a word and no printed edition carries it. Nothing else differs. **On the divisions.** The Āraṇyaka prints the sūkta as two continuous units; stotranidhi prints it as twenty-six lines, each a complete half-verse, and this file follows stotranidhi's line division, one line to a verse. The śloka `ऋतं सत्यं परं ब्रह्म` and the Nārāyaṇa gāyatrī are given two lines each, as they are printed. **In the `iast:` field accent marks are omitted**, following `vidhi/taittiriya/01_krishna_yajurveda_sandhyavandanam.txt`; the `deva:` field carries them in full. The Taittirīya nasal written `ग्ं` before a sibilant is rendered `ṁ` in IAST. **Cross-references:** this is the second hymn of the pañca-sūkta set — `veda/taittiriya/01_purusha_suktam.txt`, this file, `devi/lakshmi/06_sri_suktam.txt`, `veda/taittiriya/03_bhu_suktam.txt` and `04_nila_suktam.txt`.

Verse count: 30 — the twenty-six lines of the sūkta proper, the `ऋतं सत्यं` śloka, the Nārāyaṇa gāyatrī, and the śānti-pāṭha printed before and after, counted as the first and last units.

Sections:
शान्तिपाठः — Śānti-pāṭha (the opening and closing peace invocation)
नारायणसूक्तम् — Nārāyaṇa Sūktam (the twenty-six lines, TĀ 10.13)
उत्तरभागः — Closing portion (the `ऋतं सत्यं` śloka, TĀ 10.22, and the Nārāyaṇa gāyatrī, TĀ 10.1)

Blurb: This hymn says that Nārāyaṇa is everything. He is the universe, the supreme light, the supreme brahman, and both the one who meditates and the meditation itself. The middle part of the hymn turns inward. It describes the heart within the body, shaped like a lotus bud hanging downward, and the fine flame standing in it like lightning within a dark cloud. In the middle of that flame the supreme Self abides, and he is named as Brahmā, Śiva, Hari and Indra together. It is recited second in the pañca-sūkta set used at abhiṣeka.

tel: శాంతి పాఠం. ఆయన మా ఇరువురిని రక్షించును గాక. మా ఇరువురిని పోషించును గాక. మేము కలిసి బలముతో కృషి చేయుదుము గాక. మేము చదివినది తేజస్సుతో నిండియుండును గాక. మేము ఒకరితో ఒకరు విరోధింపకుందుము గాక. ఓం శాంతిః శాంతిః శాంతిః.
hi: शान्ति-पाठ। वे हम दोनों की रक्षा करें। वे हम दोनों का पोषण करें। हम साथ मिलकर बल के साथ कार्य करें। हमारा पढ़ा हुआ तेजस्वी हो। हम आपस में विरोध न करें। ॐ शान्ति, शान्ति, शान्ति।

--- verse 20 | section: Nārāyaṇa Sūktam ---

--- verse 21 | section: Nārāyaṇa Sūktam ---
