Author: No human author. Vedic — Ṛgveda 1.1, nine ṛcs. The ṛṣi is Madhucchandas Vaiśvāmitra. The devatā is Agni. The metre is gāyatrī. The traditional index of the Ṛgveda records the hymn in the words `अग्निं नव मधुच्छन्दा वैश्वामित्रः` — "nine to Agni, by Madhucchandas Vaiśvāmitra".

Recension / śākhā: **Ṛgveda, Śākala saṁhitā.** The Śākala is the recitation tradition in which the Ṛgveda survives complete, so the cross-śākhā question that governs `veda/taittiriya/` does not arise for this hymn. The accent printed here is Śākala accent, taken from Śākala sources, as the svara rule in the repo-root `CLAUDE.md` requires.

Source / recension: **This file exists to carry the accent, which the corpus's existing Agni Sūktam does not have.** See `misc_vedic/01_agni_suktam.txt`, which is the same nine ṛcs without svara. Three witnesses were used here, three of them accented. **(1) Base text: sa.wikisource.org, `ऋग्वेदः सूक्तं १.१`** — the accented saṁhitā-pāṭha printed with Sāyaṇa's bhāṣya, with the accented pada-pāṭha of all nine ṛcs on the same page. This is the same source used for the corpus's other Ṛgvedic file, `pitr/04_pitr_suktam_rigveda.txt`. **(2) stotranidhi.com**, `https://stotranidhi.com/hi/agni-suktam-in-hindi/`, accented Devanāgarī — a separate lineage. **(3) sanskritdocuments.org, `doc_veda/r01.itx`**, category "veda, rigveda, svara" — the Aufrecht edition of 1877 as digitised by van Nooten and Holland, **accented**, a European critical lineage separate from both of the above. **(4) GRETIL's Ṛgveda, mandala 1** (`rv_hn01u.htm`), the same Aufrecht text in IAST and **unaccented**, used only as a check on the letters. **Result of the collation: all four agree in every word, and all three accented witnesses agree on every one of the 97 accent marks.** The Aufrecht text settles each of the three readings adjudicated below in the same direction as the pada-pāṭha: `.N` for the anunāsika in `स देवाँ एह`, `~Ngi` for the class nasal in `अङ्गिरः`, and `ratna\`dhAta\'mam` with final `म्`. Where a mark appears one character apart between them, it is because the two spell a nasal or a visarga differently; the accent falls on the same syllable in both. The differences are listed in the note below. **No Gītā Press witness exists** — archive.org holds no Gītā Press edition of the Ṛgveda saṁhitā.

Recension note: **Every difference between the witnesses is orthographic, and each is listed here.** **(1) Word-final `म्` written as anusvāra.** The base text's saṁhitā lines print `ऋ॒त्विजं॑`, `रत्न॒धात॑मं`, `वी॒रव॑त्तमं` and `व॒यं` before the daṇḍa. Its own pada-pāṭha on the same page, stotranidhi and Aufrecht all give `म्`. The `म्` form is printed here. The base text writes `दीदि॑विम्` with `म्` in ṛc 8, so this is an inconsistency in that page rather than a feature of its edition. **(2) Class nasal written as anusvāra.** The base text prints `यदं॒ग`, `स॒त्यमं॑गिरः` and `भरं॑त`; the pada-pāṭha, stotranidhi and Aufrecht give `अ॒ङ्ग`, `अ॒ङ्गि॒रः` and `भर॑न्तः`. The class nasal is printed here. The same page writes `राज॑न्तम` with `न्त` in ṛc 8. **(3) The Ṛgvedic `ळ`.** The base text reads `अ॒ग्निमी॑ळे` and Aufrecht `agnim īḷe`; stotranidhi prints `ल`. `ळ` is the Śākala orthography and is kept. **(4) Two defects in stotranidhi, neither adopted.** It keys the visarga as an **ASCII colon** (`वि॒श्वत॑:`, `स न॑:`, U+003A and not `ः` U+0903) — the encoding defect already recorded for that site; and in ṛc 2 it prints `स दे॒वा।ण् एह` for `स दे॒वाँ एह`, a garbled anunāsika. `misc_vedic/01_agni_suktam.txt` records the same garble in its own source and corrects it in the same way. **(5) `र्ऋ` against `रृ`.** In ṛc 2 the base text spells the sandhi of `र्` and `ऋ` with the full vowel letter, `पूर्वे॑भि॒र्ऋषि॑भिः`; stotranidhi uses the vowel sign, `रृ`. The base text's spelling is kept. **(6) The opening `ॐ`.** The plain reading text at the top of the base-text page begins `ॐ अग्निमीळे`; the accented saṁhitā text does not, and `ॐ` is not printed here. **(7)** Accent marks are carried in `deva:` and omitted from `iast:`, following `pitr/04_pitr_suktam_rigveda.txt` and the files in `veda/taittiriya/`. No accent mark was added, inferred or moved. **Cross-reference:** `misc_vedic/01_agni_suktam.txt` holds this hymn without accent. Its header says the svara of its source was dropped on purpose, under the convention this corpus followed before 2026-09-08. That file was not rewritten and its text was not edited; the two record the same hymn in two forms, and neither supersedes the other. The corpus's other Ṛgvedic hymn is `pitr/04_pitr_suktam_rigveda.txt` (ṚV 10.15), which is accented and sits in `pitr/` for historical reasons.

Sections:
अग्निसूक्तम् — Agni Sūktam (the nine ṛcs, ṚV 1.1.1–9)

Blurb: This is the first hymn of the Ṛgveda, and so the opening of the whole Vedic corpus. Nine short verses in gāyatrī metre praise Agni. He is called the purohita of the sacrifice, the priest who stands in front. He is both the ṛtvij who performs the rite and the hotṛ who offers the oblation into the fire. The fire carries the offering up to the gods and brings the gods down to the offering. The hymn asks for wealth that grows day by day and for brave sons. It ends by asking Agni to be as easy to approach as a father is to his son.

--- verse 1 | section: Agni Sūktam ---
deva:
अ॒ग्निमी॑ळे पु॒रोहि॑तं य॒ज्ञस्य॑ दे॒वमृ॒त्विज॑म् ।
होता॑रं रत्न॒धात॑मम् ॥
iast:
agnimīḷe purohitaṁ yajñasya devamṛtvijam |
hotāraṁ ratnadhātamam ||
en: ṚV 1.1.1. I praise Agni. He is the purohita of the sacrifice, the priest who stands in front. He is the god who serves as ṛtvij, and he is the hotṛ who offers the oblation. He gives treasure most of all.
tel: ఋగ్వేదం 1.1.1. అగ్నిని నేను స్తుతించుచున్నాను. ఆయన యజ్ఞమునకు పురోహితుడు. ఆయన దైవమైన ఋత్విక్కు, ఆహుతిని సమర్పించు హోత. ఆయన శ్రేష్ఠమైన సంపదను ఇచ్చువాడు.
hi: ऋग्वेद 1.1.1. मैं अग्नि की स्तुति करता हूँ। वे यज्ञ के पुरोहित हैं। वे दिव्य ऋत्विक् हैं और आहुति अर्पित करने वाले होता हैं। वे सबसे अधिक धन देने वाले हैं।

hi: ऋग्वेद 1.1.3. अग्नि के द्वारा मनुष्य धन प्राप्त करता है। वह धन दिन-प्रतिदिन बढ़ता है। वह यश देता है और वीर संतान देता है।
