Title: Rātri Sūktam (Ṛgveda 10.127, Śākala) — with Vedic accent
Devanāgarī: रात्रिसूक्तम् (ऋग्वेदः १०.१२७, शाकलम्)
Telugu: రాత్రి సూక్తం (ఋగ్వేదం 10.127, శాకలం)

Author: No human author. Vedic — Ṛgveda 10.127, eight ṛcs. The ṛṣi is Kuśika Saubhara; the tradition also names Rātri herself, and Bhāradvājī. The devatā is Rātri, the goddess Night. The metre is gāyatrī. The hymn is recited at the reading of the Devī Māhātmya.

Recension / śākhā: **Ṛgveda, Śākala saṁhitā.** The Ṛgveda survives complete in this one recitation tradition, so the cross-śākhā question that governs `veda/taittiriya/` does not arise here. The accent printed below is Śākala accent, taken from Śākala sources.

Source / recension: **This file exists to carry the accent, which the corpus's existing Rātri Sūktam does not have.** See `devi/durga/39_vedokta_ratri_suktam.txt`, which is the same eight ṛcs as the Gītā Press *Durgā Saptaśatī* prints them — without svara, because that volume prints the whole Saptaśatī unaccented. **Three witnesses were used here, all three accented and all three Śākala.** **(1) Base text: sa.wikisource.org, `ऋग्वेदः सूक्तं १०.१२७`** — the accented saṁhitā-pāṭha printed with Sāyaṇa's bhāṣya, with the accented pada-pāṭha of all eight ṛcs on the same page. **(2) sanskritdocuments.org, `doc_veda/r10.itx`**, category "veda, rigveda, svara" — the Aufrecht edition of 1877 as digitised by van Nooten and Holland, a European critical lineage entirely separate from the Indian printed edition above. **(3) stotranidhi.com**, `https://stotranidhi.com/hi/ratri-suktam-in-sanskrit/`, accented Devanāgarī. **Result of the collation: all three agree letter for letter, and all three agree on every accent mark.** Where a mark appears one character apart between them, it is because the editions order the mark and a following anusvāra or visarga differently; the accent falls on the same syllable in all three. **No Gītā Press witness to the accent exists** — Gītā Press publishes this hymn only inside the Saptaśatī, unaccented, and archive.org holds no Gītā Press edition of the Ṛgveda saṁhitā.

Recension note: **The hymn proper is printed here, and nothing else.** The Gītā Press Saptaśatī prints it inside a liturgical frame — a heading, a prose viniyoga naming ṛṣi, devatā and chandas, and an `ॐ` before the first ṛc. Those belong to the recitation and not to the saṁhitā, and they are in the existing file, not repeated here. **Compared with the existing file, letter for letter.** The two texts agree in every letter of all eight ṛcs, with three differences, none of them a difference of sense. **(1)** The Saptaśatī prefixes `ॐ` to ṛc 1. **(2)** The kampa numerals `१` in ṛcs 1, 2 and 6 — `दे॒व्य१॒॑क्षभिः॑`, `दे॒व्यु१॒॑द्वतः॑`, `वृ॒क्यं१॒॑` — are part of Ṛgvedic accent notation and mark an independent svarita, so a text printed without accent has no place for them. They are kept here in `deva:` as the base text prints them, and omitted from `iast:`, following `pitr/04_pitr_suktam_rigveda.txt`. **(3) One reading differs.** In ṛc 4 the Gītā Press Saptaśatī reads `नि ते यामन्नविक्षमहि`; all three accented witnesses read `नि ते॒ याम॒न्नवि॑क्ष्महि`, with the conjunct `क्ष्म`. The saṁhitā reading is printed here. The existing file was not edited — the rule in `CLAUDE.md` against changing another session's file covers this, and the difference is recorded in both files instead. **On stotranidhi.** Its page keys the visarga as an **ASCII colon** (`तम॑:`, U+003A and not `ः` U+0903), the encoding defect already recorded for that site. It was used as a witness to the accent, not as the base text, and the colon was not carried over. **Cross-reference:** `devi/durga/39_vedokta_ratri_suktam.txt` holds this hymn unaccented, in its Saptaśatī liturgical frame; the two record the same hymn in two forms and neither supersedes the other. The Tāntric hymn of the same name, `devi/durga/14_tantrokta_ratri_suktam.txt`, is a different text altogether — it is Purāṇic, not Vedic, and carries no accent because none belongs to it. Sections:
रात्रिसूक्तम् — Rātri Sūktam (the eight ṛcs, ṚV 10.127.1–8)

Blurb: This is the Ṛgveda's hymn to Night. The goddess Rātri comes and looks out over the world with her eyes, which are the stars, and puts on all her glories. She fills the low places and the high, and her own light keeps the darkness back. She makes room for her sister, the dawn. The villages settle down to rest, and the animals that walk and the birds that fly settle with them. The worshipper asks her to keep the wolf and the thief away, and to let him pass through the night in safety. The hymn is recited at the reading of the Devī Māhātmya.

tel: ఋగ్వేదం 10.127.1. రాత్రి వచ్చినది. తన కన్నులతో ఆమె అనేక చోట్లను చూచినది. ఆమె తన సమస్త శోభలను ధరించినది.
