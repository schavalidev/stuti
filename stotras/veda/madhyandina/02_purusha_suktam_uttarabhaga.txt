Telugu: పురుష సూక్తం — ఉత్తరభాగం (మాధ్యందినీయం)

Author: No human author. Vedic — Śukla-Yajurveda, Vājasaneyi Saṁhitā (Mādhyandina) 31.17–22, the six mantras that close adhyāya 31. The devatā is the Puruṣa. Tradition holds the hymn to have been seen, not composed.

Recension / śākhā: **Śukla-Yajurveda, Vājasaneyi Saṁhitā, Mādhyandina śākhā — the North Indian form.** This is NOT the universal form. The Taittirīya (Kṛṣṇa-Yajurveda) tradition recites the corresponding mantras as the Uttaranārāyaṇa portion of its own Puruṣa Sūkta, and its text differs — at the very first mantra it reads `अद्भ्यः संभूतः` where this recension reads `अ॒द्भ्यः सम्भृ॑तः`. Per the śākhā rule in the repo-root `CLAUDE.md`, these are the recitations of different communities. They are not merged, not collated against each other, and not used to fill each other's gaps. A reader who follows the Taittirīya tradition should know that this file is not theirs.

Source / recension: Base text and accent: the **accented Mādhyandina Vājasaneyi Saṁhitā on sa.wikisource.org** (`शुक्लयजुर्वेदः/अध्यायः ३१`, the saṁhitā text inside the Uvaṭa–Mahīdhara commentary blocks, fetched through the MediaWiki API) — **the same śākhā**, so the svara rule permits the accent. **Independent collation**: the **TITUS edition of the Vājasaneyi-Saṁhitā (Mādhyandina)**, `titus.uni-frankfurt.de`, part 31, accented IAST — a scholarly digital edition of a separate editorial lineage. With accent marks stripped, the two agree letter for letter in all six mantras except at two single words, which are printed as bracketed variants in the lines themselves and explained below. **No printed edition was available for these six mantras.** The Khelāṛīlāl lithograph used for the companion file `01_purusha_suktam.txt` closes at mantra 16 and does not carry them, and no Gītā Press edition of the Mādhyandina Puruṣa Sūkta was located. The witnesses here are therefore two digital editions, not a print and a digital edition, and this file rests on a narrower base than its companion.

Recension note: **Two one-word differences between the witnesses, both printed in brackets in the line.** (1) Mantra 5 (VS 31.21): sa.wikisource reads `रु॒चं ब्रा॒ह्मं`; TITUS reads `rucáṃ brāhmyáṃ`. Both mean the brahmic radiance. The sa.wikisource reading is the base. (2) Mantra 6 (VS 31.22): sa.wikisource reads `व्यात्त॑म्`; TITUS reads `vyā́ttām`. The sa.wikisource reading is the base. **The bracketed readings are printed without accent marks.** TITUS supplies them in IAST only, and no accented Mādhyandina witness for either was found. Per the svara rule, accent is transcribed or absent and never derived, so these two words stay unaccented rather than carrying marks reconstructed from the surrounding text. **Kept exactly as both witnesses print it**: the visarga written as an ASCII colon on the sa.wikisource page has been written as `ः` here; that is a character-encoding habit of the page, not a reading. **In the `iast:` field accent marks are omitted**, following `vidhi/taittiriya/01_krishna_yajurveda_sandhyavandanam.txt` and `pitr/03_pitr_suktam_vajasaneyi.txt`; the `deva:` field carries them in full. **Cross-references:** `veda/madhyandina/01_purusha_suktam.txt` carries VS 31.1–16, the Puruṣa Sūkta as the printed recitation book gives it; these two files together are the whole of adhyāya 31, and neither supersedes the other. `veda/taittiriya/01_purusha_suktam.txt` is the Taittirīya hymn of twenty-four ṛcs, whose closing ṛcs cover the same ground in a different recension.

Verse count: 6 — VS 31.17–22, numbered 1 to 6 in this file, with the Vājasaneyi number given in the `en:` field of each.

Sections:
उत्तरभागः — Uttara-bhāga (the six mantras that complete Vājasaneyi Saṁhitā adhyāya 31)

Blurb: These six mantras complete the thirty-first adhyāya of the Vājasaneyi Saṁhitā, after the sixteen mantras of the Puruṣa Sūkta proper. The Puruṣa is gathered from the waters and from the essence of the earth, and Tvaṣṭṛ shapes his form. The worshipper then declares that he knows this great Puruṣa, bright as the sun and beyond darkness, and that knowing him is the only way past death. Prajāpati moves within the womb and is born in many forms, and all the worlds rest in him. The last two mantras turn to worship: reverence is offered to the radiance that shines upon the gods, and the Puruṣa is addressed as the one whose consorts are Śrī and Lakṣmī, whose sides are day and night, and whose form is the stars.

--- verse 1 | section: Uttara-bhāga ---
deva:
अ॒द्भ्यः सम्भृ॑तः पृथिव्यै॒ रसा॑च्च वि॒श्वक॑र्मण॒ः सम॑वर्त॒ताग्रे॑ ।
तस्य॒ त्वष्टा॑ वि॒दध॑द्रू॒पमे॑ति॒ तन्मर्त्य॑स्य देव॒त्वमा॒जान॒मग्रे॑ ॥
iast:
adbhyaḥ sambhṛtaḥ pṛthivyai rasācca viśvakarmaṇaḥ samavartatāgre |
tasya tvaṣṭā vidadhadrūpameti tanmartyasya devatvamājānamagre ||
en: VS 31.17. He was gathered from the waters and from the essence of the earth. He came into being at the beginning from Viśvakarman. Tvaṣṭṛ shapes his form and moves toward it. That was the divine nature of the mortal at the beginning.
tel: వా.సం. 31.17. ఆయన జలముల నుండియు, భూమి యొక్క రసము నుండియు సమకూర్చబడెను. మొదట విశ్వకర్మ నుండి ఆయన ఆవిర్భవించెను. త్వష్ట ఆయన రూపమును నిర్మించుచు దాని వైపు సాగును. మొదట మర్త్యునికి కలిగిన దేవత్వము అదియే.
hi: वा.सं. 31.17। वे जल से और पृथ्वी के रस से एकत्र किए गए। आदि में विश्वकर्मा से वे प्रकट हुए। त्वष्टा उनका रूप रचते हुए उसकी ओर बढ़ते हैं। आदि में मर्त्य को प्राप्त देवत्व यही था।

--- verse 2 | section: Uttara-bhāga ---
deva:
वेदा॒हमे॒तं पुरु॑षं म॒हान्त॑मादि॒त्यव॑र्णं॒ तम॑सः प॒रस्ता॑त् ।
तमे॒व वि॑दि॒त्वाति॑ मृ॒त्युमे॑ति॒ नान्यः पन्था॑ विद्य॒तेऽय॑नाय ॥
iast:
vedāhametaṁ puruṣaṁ mahāntamādityavarṇaṁ tamasaḥ parastāt |
tameva viditvāti mṛtyumeti nānyaḥ panthā vidyate'yanāya ||
en: VS 31.18. I know this great Puruṣa, of the colour of the sun, beyond the darkness. Knowing him alone, a person goes past death. There is no other path to walk upon.
tel: వా.సం. 31.18. ఆదిత్యుని వర్ణము గల, చీకటికి ఆవల నున్న ఈ మహాపురుషుని నేను ఎరుగుదును. ఆయననే తెలిసికొని మనుజుడు మృత్యువును దాటును. వెళ్ళుటకు వేరొక మార్గము లేదు.
hi: वा.सं. 31.18। मैं इस महान् पुरुष को जानता हूँ, जो सूर्य के समान वर्ण वाले हैं और अन्धकार से परे हैं। उन्हीं को जानकर मनुष्य मृत्यु को पार करता है। जाने के लिए दूसरा कोई मार्ग नहीं है।

--- verse 3 | section: Uttara-bhāga ---
deva:
प्र॒जाप॑तिश्च॒रति॒ गर्भे॑ अ॒न्तरजा॑यमानो बहु॒धा वि जा॑यते ।
तस्य॒ योनिं॒ परि॑ पश्यन्ति॒ धीरा॒स्तस्मि॑न् ह तस्थु॒र्भुव॑नानि॒ विश्वा॑ ॥
iast:
prajāpatiścarati garbhe antarajāyamāno bahudhā vi jāyate |
tasya yoniṁ pari paśyanti dhīrāstasmin ha tasthurbhuvanāni viśvā ||
en: VS 31.19. Prajāpati moves within the womb. Though he is not born, he is born in many forms. The wise see his source. All the worlds have their place in him.
tel: వా.సం. 31.19. ప్రజాపతి గర్భములో సంచరించును. ఆయన పుట్టనివాడైనను అనేక రూపములలో పుట్టును. ధీరులు ఆయన మూలమును దర్శింతురు. సమస్త భువనములు ఆయనయందే నిలిచియున్నవి.
hi: वा.सं. 31.19। प्रजापति गर्भ के भीतर विचरते हैं। वे अजन्मा होकर भी अनेक रूपों में जन्म लेते हैं। धीर पुरुष उनके मूल को देखते हैं। सम्पूर्ण भुवन उन्हीं में स्थित हैं।

deva:
रु॒चं ब्रा॒ह्मं(ब्राह्म्यं) ज॒नय॑न्तो दे॒वा अग्रे॒ तद॑ब्रुवन् ।
यस्त्वै॒वं ब्रा॑ह्म॒णो वि॒द्यात्तस्य॑ दे॒वा अ॑स॒न् वशे॑ ॥

en: VS 31.21. Bringing forth the brahmic radiance, the gods said this at the beginning: the brāhmaṇa who knows him in this way — the gods shall be under his command.
tel: వా.సం. 31.21. బ్రహ్మతేజస్సును ప్రకటించుచు దేవతలు మొదట ఇట్లు పలికిరి — ఈ విధముగా ఎరిగిన బ్రాహ్మణునకు దేవతలు వశులై యుందురు.
hi: वा.सं. 31.21। ब्राह्म तेज को प्रकट करते हुए देवताओं ने आदि में यह कहा — जो ब्राह्मण इस प्रकार जानता है, देवता उसके वश में रहते हैं।

en: VS 31.22. Śrī and Lakṣmī are your two consorts. Day and night are your two sides. The stars are your form. The two Aśvins are your open mouth. Grant what is desired. Grant me that. Grant me the whole world.

hi: वा.सं. 31.22। श्री और लक्ष्मी आपकी दो पत्नियाँ हैं। दिन और रात आपके दो पार्श्व हैं। नक्षत्र आपका रूप हैं। दोनों अश्विनी आपका खुला मुख हैं। जो अभीष्ट है, वह प्रदान कीजिए। वह मुझे प्रदान कीजिए। सम्पूर्ण लोक मुझे प्रदान कीजिए।
