Blurb: The chapter opens with Arjuna asking Kṛṣṇa to explain seven terms used at the close of the previous chapter. These are brahman, adhyātma, karma, adhibhūta, adhidaiva and adhiyajña, together with the question of how the disciplined know him at the hour of departure. Kṛṣṇa answers all seven in two brief verses. He then gives the rest of the chapter to the last question. What a person remembers when leaving the body is what he becomes; therefore remember me, and fight. From there the chapter describes the yogic manner of dying, with the breath drawn up to the brow and oṁ pronounced as the single syllable. It then widens into cosmology, with the day and night of Brahmā, each of a thousand yugas, in which the worlds dissolve and return. It closes with the two paths of departure, the bright and the dark: one from which a person returns, and one from which he does not.

hi: श्रीभगवान ने कहा: अक्षर ही परम ब्रह्म है; उसका अपना स्वभाव अध्यात्म कहलाता है; और भूतों के भाव को उत्पन्न करने वाला जो विसर्ग है, वही कर्म नाम से जाना जाता है।

--- verse 18 | section: Brahmā's day and night; the unmanifest beyond ---
