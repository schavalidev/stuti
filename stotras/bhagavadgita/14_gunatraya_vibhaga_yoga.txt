Blurb: Kṛṣṇa turns from prakṛti and puruṣa to the three strands that prakṛti spins out of them: sattva, rajas and tamas, which bind the deathless Self to a body as happiness, as craving, as inertia. The chapter is unusually systematic — it names the great brahman as womb and Kṛṣṇa as the seed-giving father, then works the triad through three parallel grids: how each guṇa binds, what each looks like when it rises, and where each carries a person who dies under it, upward, level, or down. Arjuna asks how one who has crossed the three is recognized, and the answer is a portrait of unshaken evenness, closing on bhakti as the way across and on brahman standing beneath it all.

--- verse 17 | section: Death under each guṇa; fruits and rebirth ---

सत्त्वात् सञ्जायते ज्ञानं रजसो लोभ एव च ।

--- verse 18 | section: Death under each guṇa; fruits and rebirth ---

जघन्यगुणवृत्तिस्था अधो गच्छन्ति तामसाः ॥
iast:
ūrdhvaṁ gacchanti sattvasthā madhye tiṣṭhanti rājasāḥ |
jaghanyaguṇavṛttisthā adho gacchanti tāmasāḥ ||
en: Those established in sattva go upward; those of rajas remain in the middle; those of tamas, abiding in the workings of the lowest guṇa, go downward.

--- verse 19 | section: Seeing past the guṇas to the deathless ---
deva:

iast:
nānyaṁ guṇebhyaḥ kartāraṁ yadā draṣṭānupaśyati |
guṇebhyaśca paraṁ vetti madbhāvaṁ so'dhigacchati ||
en: When the seer perceives no agent other than the guṇas, and knows that which is beyond the guṇas, he attains my being.
tel: ద్రష్ట గుణాలు తప్ప వేరే కర్తను చూడనప్పుడు, మరియు గుణాలకు అతీతమైన దానిని తెలుసుకున్నప్పుడు, అతడు నా భావాన్ని పొందుతాడు.
hi: जब द्रष्टा गुणों के अतिरिक्त किसी अन्य कर्ता को नहीं देखता, और गुणों से परे जो है उसे जान लेता है, तब वह मेरे भाव को प्राप्त होता है।

--- verse 20 | section: Seeing past the guṇas to the deathless ---

गुणानेतानतीत्य त्रीन् देही देहसमुद्भवान् ।

janmamṛtyujarāduḥkhairvimukto'mṛtamaśnute ||

--- verse 21 | section: Arjuna asks; marks of the guṇātīta ---

kimācāraḥ kathaṁ caitāṁstrīn guṇānativartate ||

--- verse 22 | section: Arjuna asks; marks of the guṇātīta ---

--- verse 24 | section: Arjuna asks; marks of the guṇātīta ---

--- verse 25 | section: Arjuna asks; marks of the guṇātīta ---
