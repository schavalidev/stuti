Blurb: Arjuna asks what happens to people who worship with faith but without the rules of śāstra, and Kṛṣṇa answers by making faith itself the classifier: faith follows a person's innate constitution, and a person is what their faith is. From that principle the chapter runs a systematic sorting of ordinary life through sattva, rajas and tamas — the objects of worship, then the food people relish, then sacrifice, then austerity of body, speech and mind, then giving — each triad compact and concrete, down to stale food and gifts made with contempt. It closes on the threefold designation of brahman, oṁ tat sat, and the blunt verdict that whatever is done without faith is asat, availing nothing here or hereafter.

--- verse 19 | section: Austerity and giving in three grades ---

--- verse 20 | section: Austerity and giving in three grades ---

--- verse 23 | section: Oṁ tat sat, and faithless action ---

tel: ఈ విధంగా బ్రహ్మవిద్యయు యోగశాస్త్రమునగు శ్రీమద్భగవద్గీతా ఉపనిషత్తులలో, శ్రీకృష్ణార్జున సంవాదంలో, శ్రద్ధాత్రయవిభాగయోగం అనే పదిహేడవ అధ్యాయం సమాప్తం.
hi: इस प्रकार ब्रह्मविद्या तथा योगशास्त्र रूप श्रीमद्भगवद्गीता उपनिषद् में, श्रीकृष्ण और अर्जुन के संवाद में, श्रद्धात्रयविभागयोग नामक सत्रहवाँ अध्याय समाप्त हुआ।
