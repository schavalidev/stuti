Blurb: Chapter 7 opens the Gītā's middle six chapters by shifting from practice to the nature of the one practised toward. Kṛṣṇa promises knowledge together with its direct realisation, notes how few even try and fewer still know him truly, then divides his being into a lower prakṛti of eight elements and a higher one that becomes all life. A string of "I am" identifications follows — taste in water, light in moon and sun, fragrance in earth, strength in the strong — before the guṇas are said to issue from him without binding him. The chapter then sorts devotees into four kinds, ranks the jñānī highest, and explains why worshippers of lesser gods reach lesser, ending ends.

॥ इति श्रीमद्भगवद्गीतासूपनिषत्सु ब्रह्मविद्यायां योगशास्त्रे श्रीकृष्णार्जुनसंवादे ज्ञानविज्ञानयोगो नाम सप्तमोऽध्यायः ॥
iast:
|| iti śrīmadbhagavadgītāsūpaniṣatsu brahmavidyāyāṁ yogaśāstre śrīkṛṣṇārjunasaṁvāde jñānavijñānayogo nāma saptamo'dhyāyaḥ ||
en: Thus ends the seventh chapter, named the Yoga of Knowledge and Discernment, in the Upaniṣads of the glorious Bhagavad Gītā — the knowledge of brahman, the scripture of yoga — in the dialogue of Śrī Kṛṣṇa and Arjuna.
tel: ఈ విధంగా బ్రహ్మవిద్యయు యోగశాస్త్రమునగు శ్రీమద్భగవద్గీతా ఉపనిషత్తులలో, శ్రీకృష్ణార్జున సంవాదంలో, జ్ఞానవిజ్ఞానయోగం అనే ఏడవ అధ్యాయం సమాప్తం.
hi: इस प्रकार ब्रह्मविद्या तथा योगशास्त्र रूप श्रीमद्भगवद्गीता उपनिषद् में, श्रीकृष्ण और अर्जुन के संवाद में, ज्ञानविज्ञानयोग नामक सातवाँ अध्याय समाप्त हुआ।
