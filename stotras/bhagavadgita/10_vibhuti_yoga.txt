Blurb: Kṛṣṇa resumes his "supreme word" by claiming to be the source no god or sage can trace back, since he is their beginning; from him arise intellect, forgiveness, fear, fame and their opposites alike. Arjuna, overwhelmed, asks to be told the divine self-manifestations by which he might actually meditate on Kṛṣṇa — and the chapter answers with its famous catalogue: Viṣṇu among Ādityas, the sun, Śaṅkara, Meru, the Gaṅgā, the letter A, the syllable oṁ, spring, the gambling of cheats, silence, the seed of all beings. The list breaks off unfinished: one fragment of him upholds the entire world.
