# Every vrata in the corpus — inventory, 7 October 2026

Read off the data files, not from the plan: `design_handoff_stuti/app/stuti-vrata-data.js`,
`stuti-cycle-data.js`, `stuti-parva-data.js`, `stuti-nomu-data.js`, and the kalpa files under
`stotras/puja/`. Counts below are what those files actually hold today.

Two columns matter for the question of what is still to be written:

- **Guide** — `full` means the page carries samagri, vidhi, naivedya and the rest; `brief` means
  the page prints "Samagri and vidhi for this one are still being written" and shows little else.
- **Why it is kept** — whether the entry has a `significance:` block, the prose section the reader
  meets first on a page that has no vidhānam.

## 3. Nomulu — 102 entries, complete

`stuti-nomu-data.js`. These use their own schema and do not want a `significance:` block: the
`forwhat` field carries why the nomu is kept. Every entry has all seven fields — name, when,
forwhat, how, katha, vayanam, udyapana. The blanks that exist are faithful to Balantrapu
Venkataravu's print: thirteen with no stated duration, two with no stated fruit, four with no
method, three with no kathā, five with no vāyanam, four with no udyāpana.
