Blurb: A garland-refrain hymn — each of the eight verses ends "tvadīyapādapaṅkajaṁ namāmi devi narmade" ("I bow to your lotus feet, O goddess Narmadā") — that praises the river as a haven from Yama's terror, a refuge for sages from Vasiṣṭha to Nārada, and a stream capable, by Śiva's grace, of dissolving even cosmic-scale sin; the closing phalaśruti promises those who recite it thrice daily freedom from evil rebirth.

--- verse 1 | section: Aṣṭakam ---
deva:

en:
O goddess Narmadā, I bow to your lotus feet — delightful with the joyful breaking of waves upon your dotted waters, that carry away the very seeds of sin among your enemies, and that grant, like armor, the removal of the terror inspired by Yama's messengers and the agents of death.

--- verse 9 | section: Phalaśruti ---
deva:
इदं तु नर्मदाष्टकं त्रिकालमेव ये सदा
पठन्ति ते निरन्तरं न यान्ति दुर्गतिं कदा ।
सुलभ्यदेहदुर्लभं महेशधामगौरवं
पुनर्भवा नरा न वै विलोकयन्ति रौरवम् ॥
iast:
idaṁ tu narmadāṣṭakaṁ trikālameva ye sadā
paṭhanti te nirantaraṁ na yānti durgatiṁ kadā |
sulabhyadehadurlabhaṁ maheśadhāmagauravaṁ
punarbhavā narā na vai vilokayanti rauravam ||
en:
Those who recite this Narmadāṣṭakam always, at all three junctures of the day, never at any time fall into misfortune; having easily won the rare glory of Maheśa's abode through this readily attained body, such people are never again born to behold the hell called Raurava.
tel:
ఈ నర్మదాష్టకాన్ని త్రికాలములయందు నిత్యం పఠించువారు ఎన్నడూ దుర్గతిని పొందరు; ఈ సులభమైన శరీరముతో దుర్లభమైన మహేశధామ గౌరవాన్ని పొంది, అటువంటివారు మరల జన్మించి రౌరవ నరకాన్ని చూడరు.
hi:
जो लोग इस नर्मदाष्टकम् को त्रिकाल में सदा पढ़ते हैं, वे कभी दुर्गति को प्राप्त नहीं होते; इस सुलभ शरीर से दुर्लभ महेशधाम का गौरव पाकर, ऐसे मनुष्य पुनः जन्म लेकर रौरव नरक को कभी नहीं देखते।
