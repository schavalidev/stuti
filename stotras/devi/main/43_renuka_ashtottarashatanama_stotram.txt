Title: Śrī Reṇukā Aṣṭottaraśatanāma Stotram
Devanāgarī: श्रीरेणुका-अष्टोत्तरशतनामस्तोत्रम्
Telugu: శ్రీ రేణుకా అష్టోత్తరశతనామ స్తోత్రం

Author: The colophon ascribes it to the great sage **Śāṇḍilya**, who is also named as the seer in the viniyoga. The sanskritdocuments witness gives its source as the *Śrī Reṇukātantra* (Pitambara Peeth, Datia).

Source / recension: Primary text: **Gītā Press, Gorakhpur — Śatanāma Stotra Saṅgraha**, code 1850, pp. 182–186 (stotra pp. 182–184, nāmāvalī pp. 185–186; PDF pp. 183–187), transcribed from the page images of the printed edition. Entry 35 of the volume's forty. **Collated against sanskritdocuments.org `reNukA108nAmastotra.itx`** (encoded and proofread by Kaushal S. Kaloo, from the *Śrī Reṇukātantra*, Pitambara Peeth, Datia). This is the **same text**, with the same viniyoga, the same three nyāsas, the same dhyāna and the same Śāṇḍilya colophon. The witness is independent of Gītā Press. Stotra and nāmāvalī are paired in one file as the publisher prints them, and were checked against each other name by name.

Recension note: **Gītā Press followed; the sanskritdocuments witness (SD) is recorded, not adopted.** **SD lacks Gītā Press v. 12 entirely** (`शाकम्भरी तथा सूक्ष्मा शाङ्करी परमेश्वरी । ईशानी नैर्ऋती सौम्या माहेन्द्री वारुणी तथा ॥`). Its verses therefore run 15 to Gītā Press's 16. Without that verse the stotra falls nine names short of the nāmāvalī, which keeps all nine (nos. 86–94). Other differences (Gītā Press → SD): v. 3 `चन्द्रायुधधरा शुभा` → `candrāyudhadharāśubhā` (run together); v. 8 `नित्यानित्यतपःक्रिया` → `nityānityatapaḥpriyā`. SD also prints the nyāsa sections as separate lines headed `ṣaḍaṅganyāsaḥ` where Gītā Press heads the second `हृदयादिन्यास`, and it closes with a digbandhana and śānti mantra that Gītā Press does not print. **Stotra and nāmāvalī agree**, with these forms: v. 8 `निर्माया` → no. 62 `निर्मायै` (as printed); v. 14 `महातेजा` → no. 102 `महातेजसे` (the consonant stem). **Name count 111, not 108.** The nāmāvalī counts every word the stotra offers as a name. No. 42 `चिन्तामणिरूपधारिण्यै` is broken across two lines in the print but is one name. **The quarter-goddesses.** Nos. 90–94 (Īśānī, Nairṛtī, Saumyā, Māhendrī, Vāruṇī) are the śaktis of the regents of the directions. **The Paraśurāma cycle.** She is the "mother of Rāma" (no. 14), which here means Paraśurāma, and "beloved of the sage" (no. 17), Jamadagni. She is the "daughter of Reṇu" (no. 105) and the one "whose hand was severed" (no. 69), a name that reflects the account of her beheading. **Prefix.** Gītā Press prints `ॐ` before each name; this corpus prints `श्री` for every nāmāvalī without exception (user, 2026-09-08).

Blurb: Reṇukā, the Ekavīrā of the Deccan: Jamadagni's wife and Paraśurāma's mother, "whose hand was severed", but here also every great goddess at once, from Kālī and Lakṣmī to Kāmākṣī and Mīnākṣī. She is flame-mouthed and blazing, a remover of leprosy, and beyond peace itself.

--- verse 1 | section: Viniyogaḥ ---
deva:
अस्य श्रीरेणुकादेव्यष्टोत्तरशतनामस्तोत्रमहामन्त्रस्य शाण्डिल्य ऋषिः अनुष्टुप्छन्दः श्रीजगदम्बा रेणुकादेवता ॐ बीजं नमः शक्तिः ॐ महादेवीति कीलकं श्रीजगदम्बारेणुकाप्रसादसिद्ध्यर्थं सर्वपापक्षयद्वारा श्रीजगदम्बारेणुकाप्रीत्यर्थं सर्वाभीष्टफलप्राप्त्यर्थं च जपे विनियोगः ।
iast:
asya śrīreṇukādevyaṣṭottaraśatanāmastotramahāmantrasya śāṇḍilya ṛṣiḥ anuṣṭupchandaḥ śrījagadambā reṇukādevatā oṁ bījaṁ namaḥ śaktiḥ oṁ mahādevīti kīlakaṁ śrījagadambāreṇukāprasādasiddhyarthaṁ sarvapāpakṣayadvārā śrījagadambāreṇukāprītyarthaṁ sarvābhīṣṭaphalaprāptyarthaṁ ca jape viniyogaḥ |
en: Of this great mantra, the hymn of the hundred and eight names of the goddess Śrī Reṇukā, the seer is Śāṇḍilya, the metre anuṣṭubh, the deity Śrī Jagadambā Reṇukā; the seed is Oṁ, the power namaḥ, the pin 'Oṁ Mahādevī'. It is applied in japa to win the grace of Śrī Jagadambā Reṇukā, to please her through the destruction of every sin, and to obtain every desired fruit.
tel: ఈ శ్రీరేణుకాదేవి అష్టోత్తరశతనామస్తోత్రమహామంత్రమునకు శాండిల్యుడు ఋషి, అనుష్టుప్ ఛందస్సు, శ్రీజగదంబా రేణుక దేవత, ఓం బీజము, నమః శక్తి, ఓం మహాదేవీ అని కీలకము; శ్రీజగదంబారేణుకాప్రసాదసిద్ధికై, సర్వపాపక్షయముద్వారా ఆమె ప్రీతికై, సర్వాభీష్టఫలప్రాప్తికై జపమునందు వినియోగము.
hi: इस श्रीरेणुकादेवी-अष्टोत्तरशतनामस्तोत्ररूपी महामन्त्रके शाण्डिल्य ऋषि, अनुष्टुप् छन्द, श्रीजगदम्बा रेणुका देवता, ॐ बीज, नमः शक्ति और 'ॐ महादेवी' कीलक हैं; श्रीजगदम्बा रेणुकाके प्रसादकी सिद्धिके लिये, समस्त पापोंके क्षयद्वारा उनकी प्रीतिके लिये तथा सभी अभीष्ट फलोंकी प्राप्तिके लिये जपमें इसका विनियोग है।
