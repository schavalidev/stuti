Title: Śrī Jvālāmukhī Stotram — 2
Devanāgarī: श्री ज्वालामुखी स्तोत्रम् – २
Telugu: శ్రీ జ్వాలాముఖీ స్తోత్రం – 2

Author: unattributed. The text carries no viniyoga and no author-colophon — it closes simply with "iti śrī jvālāmukhī stotram" — and unlike the Stotram 1 of this pair it is not framed as the speech of any purāṇic or tantric speaker.

Source / recension: https://stotranidhi.com/en/sri-jwalamukhi-stotram-2-in-english/ (IAST), https://stotranidhi.com/hi/sri-jwalamukhi-stotram-2-in-sanskrit/ (Devanāgarī), https://stotranidhi.com/sri-jwalamukhi-stotram-2-in-telugu/ (Telugu script). All three were fetched via curl with a browser user-agent (stotranidhi blocks direct WebFetch) and compared verse by verse; they agree throughout on all sixteen verses. No independent second site carrying this particular text was located, so the cross-check rests on agreement among Stotranidhi's three language editions. Four printing details are preserved from the source rather than emended, each flagged at the verse where it occurs: layāgri for expected layāgni (v. 7), the short-vowel jvalāmukhi for jvālāmukhi (v. 8), a missing avagraha in rahitosmi (v. 12), and another in 'vijñopi (v. 16). The source also marks a syllable boundary with an explicit hyphen inside kṣityab-hutāśa (v. 4) to keep b+h from being read as bh; that hyphen is kept. Where a word straddles a pāda break the source hyphenates it across the two lines, and that hyphenation is reproduced here.

Verse count: 16

Sections:
స్తోత్రం — Stotram (vv. 1–15)
ఫలశ్రుతి — Phalaśruti, the fruits of recitation (v. 16)

Blurb: This is the second and quite different Jvālāmukhī hymn on Stotranidhi: where Stotram 1 is a tantric mantroddhāra spoken by Bhairava, this one is sixteen verses of vasantatilakā by a poet speaking entirely for himself. Its organising idea is that the goddess is fire, and so everything in the poem is worked in fire: sin is a blaze fanned by the winds of bad karma that leaves the speaker rolling on the ground burnt (v. 6); the goddess is a crore of dissolution-fires (v. 7) and the blaze of consciousness against which the poet is only "the incurable disease" to her crown of healing herbs, the moon (v. 8); and the pivot of the whole hymn (v. 11) is the request that, having once made sun, moon and fire to end outer darkness, she now let the inner flame shine. Verse 13 breaks frame entirely to scold the poet's own mind — "listen, you fool, drunk and blind" — and verse 9's image of a hundred griefs scattering like jackals before the lion of meditation is the hymn's best single stroke.

--- verse 1 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
जाज्वल्यमानवपुषा दशदिग्विभागान्
सन्दीपयन्त्यभयपद्मगदावराढ्या ।
सिंहस्थिता शशिकलाभरणा त्रिनेत्रा
ज्वालामुखी हरतु मोहतमः सदा नः ॥
iast:
jājvalyamānavapuṣā daśadigvibhāgān
sandīpayantyabhayapadmagadāvarāḍhyā |
siṁhasthitā śaśikalābharaṇā trinetrā
jvālāmukhī haratu mohatamaḥ sadā naḥ ||
en: With her blazing body she sets alight all ten divisions of space; she is rich in the abhaya (fearlessness) gesture, the lotus, the mace and the boon-giving hand; she stands upon a lion, wears the crescent moon as her ornament, and has three eyes — may Jvālāmukhī take away from us, always, the darkness of delusion.
tel: మండుతున్న తన దేహకాంతితో పది దిక్కులనూ ప్రకాశింపజేయునది, అభయముద్ర, పద్మం, గద, వరదముద్రలతో అలరారునది, సింహంపై నిలిచినది, చంద్రకళను ఆభరణంగా ధరించినది, మూడు కన్నులు కలది అయిన జ్వాలాముఖి మా మోహాంధకారాన్ని ఎల్లప్పుడూ హరించుగాక.
hi: अपने जाज्वल्यमान शरीर से दसों दिशाओं को प्रकाशित करने वाली, अभय, पद्म, गदा और वरद से समृद्ध, सिंह पर स्थित, चंद्रकला को आभूषण रूप में धारण किए हुए, त्रिनेत्रा — वह ज्वालामुखी हमारे मोह-अंधकार को सदा हरे।

deva:

ज्वालामुखीं कनककुण्डलशोभितांसां

ābrahmakīṭajananīṁ mahiṣīṁ śivasya

jvālāmukhīṁ kanakakuṇḍalaśobhitāṁsāṁ
vande punaḥ punarapīha sahasrakṛtvaḥ ||
en: Mother of everything from Brahmā down to the worm; queen of Śiva; her smile artless and sweet; her light that of a crore of suns at the world's dissolution; her shoulders lit by golden earrings — Jvālāmukhī I salute, here, again and again, a thousand times over.
tel: బ్రహ్మ మొదలుకొని క్రిమి వరకు సర్వానికీ తల్లి అయినది, శివుని మహిషి, ముగ్ధమైన చిరునవ్వు కలది, ప్రళయకాలపు కోటి సూర్యుల ప్రకాశం కలది, బంగారు కుండలాలతో భుజాలు శోభిల్లునది అయిన జ్వాలాముఖిని ఇక్కడ మరల మరల, వేయిసార్లు వందనం చేస్తున్నాను.
hi: ब्रह्मा से लेकर कीट तक सबकी जननी, शिव की महिषी, मुग्ध मुस्कान वाली, प्रलयकालीन कोटि सूर्यों के समान प्रकाश वाली, स्वर्ण कुंडलों से शोभित कंधों वाली — उस ज्वालामुखी को मैं यहाँ बार-बार, सहस्र बार वंदन करता हूँ।

--- verse 4 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
क्षित्यब्हुताशपवनाम्बरसूर्यचन्द्र-
-यष्ट्राख्यमूर्तिममलानपि पावयन्तीम् ।
ज्वालामुखीं प्रणतकल्पलतां शिवस्य
साम्राज्यशक्तिमतुलां महतीं नमामि ॥
iast:
kṣityab-hutāśapavanāmbarasūryacandra-
-yaṣṭrākhyamūrtimamalānapi pāvayantīm |
jvālāmukhīṁ praṇatakalpalatāṁ śivasya
sāmrājyaśaktimatulāṁ mahatīṁ namāmi ||
en: She purifies even the already spotless — the eight forms named earth, water, fire, wind, ether, sun, moon and the sacrificer, which are Śiva's aṣṭamūrti; she is the wish-granting creeper of those who bow; she is Śiva's own incomparable, vast power of sovereign rule. To Jvālāmukhī I bow. (The hyphen in kṣityab-hutāśa is the source's, marking that ap "water" combines as -ab- before hutāśa "fire," so that b and h are not to be read as a single bh.)
tel: పృథివి, జలం, అగ్ని, వాయువు, ఆకాశం, సూర్యుడు, చంద్రుడు, యజమాని అనే (శివుని అష్టమూర్తులైన) నిర్మలమైన రూపాలను సైతం పవిత్రం చేయునది, ప్రణమిల్లినవారికి కల్పలత అయినది, శివుని సామ్రాజ్యశక్తియైన అతులమైన మహాశక్తి అయిన జ్వాలాముఖికి నమస్కరిస్తున్నాను.
hi: पृथ्वी, जल, अग्नि, वायु, आकाश, सूर्य, चंद्र और यजमान — शिव की इन अष्टमूर्तियों को, जो स्वयं निर्मल हैं, उन्हें भी पवित्र करने वाली, प्रणत जनों के लिए कल्पलता, शिव की साम्राज्य-शक्ति रूप अतुल महाशक्ति — उस ज्वालामुखी को मैं नमन करता हूँ।

deva:
नौमीश्वरीं त्रिजगतोऽभयदानशौण्डां
ज्वालामहार्यभवजालहरां नमामि ।

naumīśvarīṁ trijagato'bhayadānaśauṇḍāṁ

en: I praise the Sovereign Lady, adept at granting fearlessness to the three worlds; I bow to the Flame, the great and noble one, who tears away the net of worldly existence; and in my mind I remember always the divine Bhagavatī, whose radiance, when it dispels the darkness of delusion, is the spotless moon's.
tel: ముల్లోకాలకూ అభయమిచ్చుటలో నేర్పరియైన ఈశ్వరిని స్తుతిస్తున్నాను; భవజాలాన్ని హరించే మహనీయమైన ఆ జ్వాలకు నమస్కరిస్తున్నాను; మోహాంధకారాన్ని పోగొట్టుటలో నిర్మల చంద్రకాంతి వంటిదైన ఆ భగవతీ దేవిని మనసా ఎల్లప్పుడూ స్మరిస్తున్నాను.
hi: तीनों लोकों को अभय देने में निपुण ईश्वरी की मैं स्तुति करता हूँ; भव के जाल को हरने वाली उस महान् आर्या ज्वाला को नमन करता हूँ; और मोह-अंधकार के हरण में निर्मल चंद्रकांति सरीखी उस भगवती देवी का मन से सदा स्मरण करता हूँ।

Recension note: Only Stotranidhi's tradition of this text could be located; its IAST, Devanāgarī and Telugu-script editions agree word for word across all sixteen verses and the closing line, so no variant readings are recorded. The four printing irregularities flagged in the verses above (layāgri v. 7, jvalāmukhi v. 8, rahitosmi v. 12, 'vijñopi v. 16) appear identically in all three editions and therefore belong to the shared source, not to any single one; all are left as printed rather than emended. The metre throughout is vasantatilakā (fourteen syllables per pāda), which is why several compounds run across the pāda break; the source's hyphenation of those words is reproduced. This text should not be confused with the Śrī Jvālāmukhī Stotram – 1 of the Devīrahasya (file 24 in this section), which is an entirely different, tantric hymn.
