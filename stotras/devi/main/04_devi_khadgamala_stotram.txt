Title: Śrī Devī Khaḍgamālā Stotram
Devanāgarī: श्री देवी खड्गमाला स्तोत्रम्
Telugu: శ్రీ దేవీ ఖడ్గమాలా స్తోత్రం

Author: traditional and anonymous; the closing colophon places it in the Vāmakeśvara Tantra, in the dialogue of Umā and Maheśvara (iti śrī vāmakeśvaratantre umāmaheśvarasaṁvāde devīkhaḍgamālāstotraratnaṁ samāptam). The viniyoga names Upasthendriyādhiṣṭhāyī Varuṇāditya as ṛṣi, Devī Gāyatrī as chandas, and Mahākāmeśvarī Śrī Lalitā Bhaṭṭārikā — seated on Kāmeśvara's lap on the sāttvika Ka-kāra Bhaṭṭāraka pīṭha — as devatā.

Source / recension: Base text is vignanam.org's śuddha-devanāgarī edition (https://vignanam.org/devanagari/sri-devi-khadgamala-stotram.html), whose Telugu-script edition (https://vignanam.org/telugu/sri-devi-khadgamala-stotram.html) agrees with it name for name. Two further independent witnesses were obtained in full and collated line by line: sanskritdocuments.org's khadgamala.itx (doc_devii; transliterated by Sarada Susarla, marked "Vāmakeśvara Tantra"), and stotranidhi.com's IAST edition (https://stotranidhi.com/en/devi-khadgamala-stotra-in-english/, the text printed in their Prabhāta Stotranidhi). Cross-checking is therefore full three-source, not reduced. The three witnesses diverge at roughly a dozen points, all itemised in the recension note below; nothing has been harmonised, and where they differ the vignanam reading stands in the deva/iast fields with the variant recorded in the note.

Verse count: Not a metrical verse stotra and not verse-numbered in any of the three sources. It is a nāmāvalī — a continuous string of names in the vocative and dative — divided by the source's OWN seventeen group headings (śrī devī sambodhanaṁ, nyāsāṅgadevatāḥ, tithinityādevatāḥ, the three guru-oghas, the nine śrīcakra āvaraṇas, navacakreśvarī nāmāni, śrīdevī viśeṣaṇāni). Those headings are used here as both sections and verse-block divisions; no divisions have been invented and none of the source's have been merged. Counting the names in the base text gives 181 (1 + 6 + 16 + 7 + 4 + 8 + 31 + 18 + 10 + 16 + 12 + 12 + 10 + 9 + 3 + 9 + 9); sanskritdocuments' recension yields 180, because it drops garimāsiddhe from the first āvaraṇa (see note). vignanam prints counts only for the first six groups — (1), (6), (16), (7), (4), (8) — and gives no count for any āvaraṇa; sanskritdocuments and stotranidhi print a count for every group. Around this name-string stand one prārthanā verse, a prose viniyoga, a dhyāna unit of one anuṣṭubh couplet plus one sragdharā-type verse, a six-line pañcapūjā, a phalaśruti of eleven anuṣṭubh couplets plus one closing hemistich, and the colophon. Every one of these carries its own unnumbered block below.

Blurb: This is a nāmāvalī used in ritual. It walks the nine āvaraṇas, or enclosures, of the Śrī Cakra from the outer bhūpura inward to the bindu. In each enclosure it calls every resident goddess by name, addressing her directly. In the rampart it names the siddhis, the mātṛkās and the mudrā-devatās. In the sixteen-petalled lotus it names the sixteen ākarṣiṇī śaktis. It then names the eight Anaṅga śaktis, the fourteen śaktis and the two sets of ten śaktis of the triangles, the eight Vāgdevatās, the weapon-goddesses, and the three kūṭa-devīs of the central triangle. At the centre it names Lalitā herself in the bindu. Each enclosure is sealed by its own cakreśvarī and its own yoginī. The completed recitation is itself the garland of the sword, which is what khaḍgamālā means. The phalaśruti states that it places an unseen sword in the reciter's hand.

deva:
श्री देवी प्रार्थन
ह्रीङ्कारासनगर्भितानलशिखां सौः क्लीं कलां बिभ्रतीं
सौवर्णाम्बरधारिणीं वरसुधाधौतां त्रिनेत्रोज्ज्वलाम् ।
वन्दे पुस्तकपाशमङ्कुशधरां स्रग्भूषितामुज्ज्वलां
त्वां गौरीं त्रिपुरां परात्परकलां श्रीचक्रसञ्चारिणीम् ॥
iast:
śrī devī prārthana
hrīṅkārāsanagarbhitānalaśikhāṁ sauḥ klīṁ kalāṁ bibhratīṁ
sauvarṇāmbaradhāriṇīṁ varasudhādhautāṁ trinetrojjvalām |
vande pustakapāśamaṅkuśadharāṁ sragbhūṣitāmujjvalāṁ
tvāṁ gaurīṁ tripurāṁ parātparakalāṁ śrīcakrasañcāriṇīm ||
en: I bow to you, Gaurī, Tripurā, the kalā beyond the beyond, who moves within the Śrī Cakra: you who bear the flame of fire held within the seat of the hrīṁkāra, and bear the kalā (portion, phase) of sauḥ and klīṁ; who wear golden garments, who are bathed in the finest nectar, brilliant with three eyes; who hold the book, the noose and the goad, adorned with a garland and shining. Three bījas are named here rather than described: hrīṁ, the māyā-bīja whose "seat" is the yantra itself; sauḥ, the parā-bīja; and klīṁ, the kāma-bīja — sauḥ and klīṁ together with aiṁ constitute the Bālā Tripurasundarī mantra that the viniyoga will name next.
tel: ఓ గౌరీ, త్రిపురా, పరాత్పరకళా, శ్రీచక్రంలో సంచరించే తల్లీ, నీకు నమస్కారం: హ్రీంకారమనే ఆసనం లోపల ఇమిడిన అగ్నిశిఖను ధరించినదానా, సౌః క్లీం అనే కళను ధరించినదానా; బంగారు వస్త్రాలు ధరించి, శ్రేష్ఠమైన అమృతంతో అభిషిక్తమై, మూడు కన్నులతో ప్రకాశించేదానా; పుస్తకం, పాశం, అంకుశం ధరించి, మాలతో అలంకరించబడి ప్రకాశించేదానా. ఇక్కడ మూడు బీజాలను వర్ణించకుండా పేర్కొన్నారు — హ్రీం అనే మాయాబీజం, దాని "ఆసనం" యంత్రమే; సౌః అనే పరాబీజం; క్లీం అనే కామబీజం — సౌః, క్లీం, ఐం కలిసి బాలా త్రిపురసుందరీ మంత్రం అవుతుంది, దానినే వినియోగంలో పేర్కొంటారు.
hi: हे गौरी, त्रिपुरा, परात्पर कला, श्रीचक्र में संचरण करने वाली माँ, तुम्हें प्रणाम: जो ह्रींकार रूपी आसन के भीतर समाई अग्निशिखा को धारण करती हो, तथा सौः और क्लीं की कला को धारण करती हो; जो स्वर्ण वस्त्र पहने, श्रेष्ठ अमृत से धौत, तीन नेत्रों से उज्ज्वल हो; जो पुस्तक, पाश और अंकुश धारण किए, माला से भूषित और देदीप्यमान हो। यहाँ तीन बीजों का वर्णन नहीं, नामकरण है — ह्रीं माया-बीज, जिसका "आसन" स्वयं यंत्र है; सौः परा-बीज; और क्लीं काम-बीज — सौः, क्लीं तथा ऐं मिलकर बाला त्रिपुरसुंदरी मंत्र बनते हैं, जिसे विनियोग आगे नामित करता है।

उपस्थेन्द्रियाधिष्ठायी वरुणादित्य ऋषयः

iast:
asya śrī śuddhaśaktimālāmahāmantrasya
upasthendriyādhiṣṭhāyī varuṇāditya ṛṣayaḥ
devī gāyatrī chandaḥ

en: For this great mantra, the Śrī Śuddhaśakti-mālā: Varuṇāditya, presiding over the organ of generation, is the ṛṣi (seer); Devī Gāyatrī is the chandas (metre); the devatā is Śrī Lalitā Bhaṭṭārikā, Mahākāmeśvarī, who dwells on Kāmeśvara's lap seated on the sāttvika Ka-kāra Bhaṭṭāraka pīṭha. The bīja is aiṁ (the vāgbhava seed), the śakti is klīṁ (the kāma seed), the kīlaka — the pin that locks the mantra — is sauḥ (the parā or śakti seed); these three together are exactly the Bālā Tripurasundarī mantra. The viniyoga (ritual application) is for japa undertaken to attain the khaḍga-siddhi (the "sword," the invisible weapon this text confers) and every desired attainment. One should then perform ṣaḍaṅganyāsa with the mūlamantra. Note that the mūlamantra itself — the Pañcadaśākṣarī, whose first cluster is the ka-kūṭa referred to by "Ka-kāra Bhaṭṭāraka pīṭha," and its Ṣoḍaśī extension — is deliberately never written out in this stotra: it is pointed to, not printed, being received only by initiation.
tel: ఈ శ్రీ శుద్ధశక్తిమాలా మహామంత్రానికి: ఉపస్థేంద్రియానికి అధిష్ఠాన దేవత అయిన వరుణాదిత్యుడు ఋషి; దేవీ గాయత్రి ఛందస్సు; సాత్విక కకార భట్టారక పీఠంపై కామేశ్వరుని అంకంలో నిలిచిన మహాకామేశ్వరీ శ్రీ లలితా భట్టారిక దేవత. ఐం బీజం (వాగ్భవ బీజం), క్లీం శక్తి (కామబీజం), సౌః కీలకం (పరాబీజం) — ఈ మూడూ కలిసి బాలా త్రిపురసుందరీ మంత్రమే. ఖడ్గసిద్ధి కోసం, సర్వాభీష్టసిద్ధి కోసం చేసే జపంలో దీని వినియోగం. తరువాత మూలమంత్రంతో షడంగన్యాసం చేయాలి. గమనించదగినది: మూలమంత్రం — పంచదశాక్షరీ, దాని మొదటి కూటమే "ककార భట్టారక పీఠం" సూచించే కకూటం, మరియు దాని షోడశీ రూపం — ఈ స్తోత్రంలో ఎక్కడా వ్రాయబడలేదు; అది సూచించబడుతుందే తప్ప ముద్రించబడదు, ఉపదేశం ద్వారానే లభిస్తుంది.
hi: इस श्री शुद्धशक्तिमाला महामंत्र के: उपस्थेंद्रिय के अधिष्ठाता वरुणादित्य ऋषि हैं; देवी गायत्री छंद है; सात्विक ककार भट्टारक पीठ पर कामेश्वर की गोद में स्थित महाकामेश्वरी श्री ललिता भट्टारिका देवता हैं। ऐं बीज (वाग्भव बीज), क्लीं शक्ति (काम बीज), सौः कीलक (परा बीज) — ये तीनों मिलकर ठीक बाला त्रिपुरसुंदरी मंत्र हैं। खड्ग-सिद्धि तथा सर्वाभीष्ट-सिद्धि हेतु किए जाने वाले जप में इसका विनियोग है। तत्पश्चात् मूलमंत्र से षडंगन्यास करना चाहिए। ध्यातव्य: मूलमंत्र स्वयं — पंचदशाक्षरी, जिसका प्रथम कूट ही "ककार भट्टारक पीठ" से संकेतित क-कूट है, तथा उसका षोडशी विस्तार — इस स्तोत्र में कहीं लिखा नहीं गया; उसकी ओर संकेत भर है, वह उपदेश से ही प्राप्त होता है।

en: This mālāmantra is declared to be the supreme secret and the supreme abode. The Śakti-mālā is fivefold, and the Śiva-mālā likewise — that is, this garland has five recensional forms on the Śakti side and five corresponding forms on the Śiva side, of which the present text is one.
tel: ఈ మాలామంత్రం పరమ గుహ్యమనీ, పరమ ధామమనీ చెప్పబడింది. శక్తిమాల ఐదు విధాలు, శివమాలా అలాగే — అంటే ఈ మాలకు శక్తిపక్షాన ఐదు రూపాలూ, శివపక్షాన అనుగుణంగా ఐదు రూపాలూ ఉన్నాయి; ఇక్కడి పాఠం వాటిలో ఒకటి.
hi: यह मालामंत्र परम गुह्य तथा परम धाम कहा गया है। शक्तिमाला पाँच प्रकार की है, और शिवमाला भी वैसी ही — अर्थात् इस माला के शक्ति-पक्ष में पाँच रूप और शिव-पक्ष में तदनुरूप पाँच रूप हैं, जिनमें से एक यह प्रस्तुत पाठ है।

तस्माद्गोप्यतराद्गोप्यं रहस्यं भुक्तिमुक्तिदम् ॥
iast:

en: Therefore it is to be kept more secret than the most secret — a mystery that gives both enjoyment and liberation. The phalaśruti closes on this single hemistich rather than a full couplet; the base text prints it so, with a double daṇḍa, and it is reproduced unchanged.
tel: కనుక అత్యంత గోప్యమైనదానికన్నా ఎక్కువగా దీన్ని గోప్యంగా ఉంచాలి — ఇది భుక్తినీ ముక్తినీ రెంటినీ ఇచ్చే రహస్యం. ఫలశ్రుతి పూర్తి శ్లోకంతో కాక ఈ ఒక్క అర్ధశ్లోకంతోనే ముగుస్తుంది; మూలపాఠం దాన్ని ద్విదండంతో అలాగే ముద్రించింది, ఇక్కడ మార్పు లేకుండా ఉంచాము.
hi: अतः इसे गोप्यतम से भी अधिक गोपनीय रखना चाहिए — यह वह रहस्य है जो भुक्ति और मुक्ति दोनों देता है। फलश्रुति पूर्ण श्लोक से नहीं, अपितु इसी एक अर्धश्लोक से समाप्त होती है; मूल पाठ ने उसे द्विदंड सहित ऐसे ही छापा है, और यहाँ अपरिवर्तित रखा गया है।

deva:
॥ इति श्री वामकेश्वरतन्त्रे उमामहेश्वरसंवादे देवीखड्गमालास्तोत्ररत्नं समाप्तम् ॥
iast:
|| iti śrī vāmakeśvaratantre umāmaheśvarasaṁvāde devīkhaḍgamālāstotraratnaṁ samāptam ||
en: Thus ends the jewel of a stotra, the Devī Khaḍgamālā, in the Śrī Vāmakeśvara Tantra, in the dialogue of Umā and Maheśvara. The Vāmakeśvara Tantra (whose best-known portion is the Nityāṣoḍaśikārṇava) is the principal scriptural authority for Śrī Vidyā, so the colophon is simultaneously an attribution and a claim to canonical standing.
tel: ఈ విధంగా శ్రీ వామకేశ్వర తంత్రంలో, ఉమామహేశ్వర సంవాదంలో ఉన్న దేవీ ఖడ్గమాలా స్తోత్రరత్నం సమాప్తం. వామకేశ్వర తంత్రం (దీనిలో అత్యంత ప్రసిద్ధమైన భాగం నిత్యాషోడశికార్ణవం) శ్రీవిద్యకు ప్రధాన ఆగమప్రమాణం; కనుక ఈ ఉపసంహారం రచనాధారాన్ని చెప్పడమే కాక, ప్రామాణికతను కూడా ప్రకటిస్తుంది.
hi: इस प्रकार श्री वामकेश्वर तंत्र में, उमा-महेश्वर संवाद में स्थित देवी खड्गमाला स्तोत्ररत्न समाप्त होता है। वामकेश्वर तंत्र (जिसका सर्वाधिक प्रसिद्ध अंश नित्याषोडशिकार्णव है) श्रीविद्या का प्रधान आगम-प्रमाण है; अतः यह पुष्पिका एक साथ रचना-स्रोत का निर्देश और प्रामाणिकता का दावा दोनों है।

Recension note: Three witnesses were obtained in full and collated line by line — (a) vignanam.org's śuddha-devanāgarī edition, used as the base text here, with its Telugu-script edition agreeing name for name; (b) sanskritdocuments.org's khadgamala.itx in doc_devii (transliterated and proofread by Sarada Susarla with Anantanarayanan, Sunder Hattangadi and Ajit Krishnan, headed "Vāmakeśvara Tantra," last updated May 2025), fetched via browser since the site's mod_security returns 406 to command-line clients; and (c) stotranidhi.com's IAST edition, the text printed in their Prabhāta Stotranidhi, likewise fetched via browser since it blocks scripted extraction. Cross-checking is therefore full, not reduced. The recensions do diverge, and nothing below has been harmonised into the base text.

Divergences in the frame material. Prārthanā, first pāda: vignanam and stotranidhi read hrīṅkārāsana-, "seated within the hrīṁkāra"; sanskritdocuments reads hrīṅkārānana-, "within the face of the hrīṁkāra." Both are old and both are defensible; the base reading stands. vignanam prints trinetrojjvalām in the prārthanā and triṇetrām in the dhyāna verse; stotranidhi prints exactly the reverse (triṇetrojjvalām / trinetrām); sanskritdocuments has trinetra- in both. Viniyoga: vignanam has varuṇāditya ṛṣayaḥ (plural); both other witnesses have ṛṣiḥ (singular). vignanam and sanskritdocuments have devī gāyatrī chandaḥ; stotranidhi has daivī gāyatrī chandaḥ. vignanam has sātvika; stotranidhi sāttvika.

Structural divergence in the dhyāna. The couplet tādṛśaṁ khaḍgamāpnoti… appears twice in vignanam — once under the dhyānam heading and again inside the phalaśruti — and both occurrences are reproduced above, since the base text prints both and the spec forbids silent tidying. sanskritdocuments prints it only under dhyānam and omits it from the phalaśruti; stotranidhi prints it in neither place, opening its dhyānam directly with āraktābhāṁ. This is the single largest structural difference among the three.

Divergences in the name-lists. Divyaughaguravaḥ: vignanam reads the seven as parameśvara, parameśvarī, mitreśamayī, ṣaṣṭhīśamayī, caryānāthamayī, lopāmudramayī, agastyamayī — that is, it splits Parameśvara and Parameśvarī into two entries and has no Uḍḍīśa. Both other witnesses read parameśvaraparameśvari as a single compound entry and insert uḍḍīśamayi after ṣaṣṭhīśamayi, still reaching seven. This is a substantive difference in the guru-lineage, not an orthographic one, and the base reading is left as printed. Siddhaughaguravaḥ: vignanam and sanskritdocuments have kālatāpaśamayī; stotranidhi has kālatāpanamayi. vignanam has lopāmudramayī where the others have lopāmudrāmayi.

Prathamāvaraṇa: vignanam prints garimāsiddhe inline in the siddhi-list, giving eleven siddhis and thirty-one names in the enclosure. sanskritdocuments removes it from the running list and parks it in a parenthesis reading "garimāsiddhe — not used although counted by some," giving ten siddhis and thirty names. stotranidhi prints it inline but in square brackets, [garimāsiddhe], while still heading the group "-30". All three thus record the same known uncertainty about whether garimā belongs to this list; the base text's inline reading is kept, and the discrepancy between vignanam's actual thirty-one names and the other editions' stated count of thirty is flagged rather than corrected.

Caturthāvaraṇa: vignanam and sanskritdocuments have sarvavidrāvinī with a dental n at this one place (though both spell the same name sarvavidrāviṇī with a retroflex ṇ in the first āvaraṇa); stotranidhi has sarvavidrāviṇi in both. vignanam and sanskritdocuments have sarvasampattipūriṇī; stotranidhi has sarvasampattipūraṇi. Navamāvaraṇa: vignanam and stotranidhi have parāpararahasyayoginī; sanskritdocuments has parāparātirahasyayoginī. Navacakreśvarī nāmāni: vignanam has tripurasiddhe and tripurāmbā; both other witnesses have tripurāsiddhe and tripurāmba.

Phalaśruti: vignanam reads lalitāyā mahaujanaḥ where both other witnesses read mahaujasaḥ, which is the grammatical form and is almost certainly what vignanam's mahaujanaḥ is a corruption of; the base reading is nonetheless printed as found, and the English renders the sense of the attested variant. Similarly vignanam runs lalitāyāmaheśānyā and mālā vidyā mahīyasī where stotranidhi divides lalitāyā maheśānyā and joins vidyāmahīyasī. sanskritdocuments alone reads navāvarṇadevīnāṁ (for navāvaraṇa-), which is a plain typo in that file. vignanam has kūṣmāṇḍaje, stotranidhi kūśmāṇḍaje. sanskritdocuments has paraṁ guhyāṁ for paraṁ guhyaṁ. Colophon: vignanam and sanskritdocuments end with samāptam; stotranidhi ends with śrī devīkhaḍgamālāstotraratnam and no samāptam.

Systematic orthographic divergence. sanskritdocuments and stotranidhi normalise every name in the garland to the correct Sanskrit feminine vocative in short -i (brāhmi, kāmeśvari, sarvākarṣiṇi, prakaṭayogini). vignanam prints these with long -ī throughout (brāhmī, kāmeśvarī, sarvākarṣiṇī, prakaṭayoginī), which reflects widespread South Indian printing practice for nāmāvalīs but is not the strict vocative. Because vignanam is the base text and the spec forbids silently smoothing a variant, its long -ī forms stand in the deva and iast fields; readers should know that the two other editions differ here at essentially every one of the 181 names, and that the short -i is the grammatically correct address form. vignanam's own text is internally inconsistent on this point — it prints kaumāri, manojadevamayi, sarvamṛtyupraśamani and kaulini with short -i while spelling their neighbours long — and those inconsistencies are reproduced as printed rather than regularised.

Section-heading irregularities in the base text, all reproduced unchanged: the sixth āvaraṇa is headed śrīcakra ṣaṣṭāvaraṇadevatāḥ for ṣaṣṭhāvaraṇa (both other editions have ṣaṣṭhā-); the penultimate heading is printed namaskāranavākṣarīca as one word, with the enclitic ca joined to the preceding word. vignanam supplies parenthesised counts only for the first six groups — (1), (6), (16), (7), (4), (8) — and none at all for the nine āvaraṇas, the navacakreśvarī list or the viśeṣaṇāni; the other two editions number every group. The base text's counts are reproduced inside the deva and iast fields exactly as printed, and the missing ones have not been supplied.

Transliteration choices. Anusvāra is rendered ṁ throughout per corpus convention. Sanskrit e and o are plain, never ē/ō, so vignanam's own transliteration page (which uses ē/ō, cha for ca, and Ch for cch) was not used as the IAST source; the iast fields were derived directly from the base Devanāgarī, with word division matching it exactly — including the places where the source runs a phalaśruti hemistich together as one long compound (apasmārajvaravyādhimṛtyukṣāmādijebhaye, aṣṭādaśamahādvīpasamrāḍbhoktābhaviṣyati, sarvopadravanirmuktassākṣācchivamayobhavet, lalitāyāmaheśānyā, pañcadhāsyācchivamālā), which are left unsplit. Where vignanam's Telugu-script edition writes ళ (ḷa) for the Devanāgarī ल — కళాం for कलां, పరాత్పరకళాం for परात्परकलां, సర్వమంగళే for सर्वमङ्गले, కళ్యాణదేవమయీ for कल्याणदेवमयी, కౌళిని for कौलिनि — the Devanāgarī l has been followed, since these are Sanskrit words and the ళ is a Telugu orthographic habit rather than a variant reading.

Bīja and mantra content. The bījas are reproduced exactly as printed and are named rather than translated: hrīṁ, sauḥ and klīṁ in the prārthanā; aiṁ (bīja), klīṁ (śakti) and sauḥ (kīlaka) in the viniyoga, which together are the Bālā Tripurasundarī mantra; the bhūta-bījas laṁ haṁ yaṁ raṁ vaṁ saṁ in the pañcapūjā; and the sambodhana string oṁ aiṁ hrīṁ śrīṁ aiṁ klīṁ sauḥ oṁ, which prefixes aiṁ hrīṁ śrīṁ to the Bālā mantra. It should be stated plainly that this recension does NOT spell out either the Pañcadaśī or the Ṣoḍaśī: the text refers to the mūlamantra twice (mūlamantreṇa ṣaḍaṅganyāsaṁ kuryāt; yathāśakti mūlamantraṁ japet) and gestures at it obliquely through the phrase kakārabhaṭṭārakapīṭha — the ka-kūṭa, i.e. the kādimata form of the Pañcadaśākṣarī — and through the three kūṭa-goddesses Mahākāmeśvarī, Mahāvajreśvarī and Mahābhagamālinī in the eighth āvaraṇa, but nowhere prints the syllables. All three witnesses agree in this omission, which is deliberate: the mūlamantra is transmitted by initiation, not in print. No Pañcadaśī or Ṣoḍaśī string has been supplied here from any other source.

Nothing in this text is verse-numbered in any witness, so no numbering has been invented; every block above is marked "verse none" and carries the source's own group heading as its section name. No portion of the name-list has been abridged.
