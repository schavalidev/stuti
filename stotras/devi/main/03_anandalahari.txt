Title: Ānandalaharī
Devanāgarī: आनन्दलहरी
Telugu: ఆనందలహరీ

This is not a separate file. In the dominant Telugu-tradition reading, which this corpus follows, "Ānandalaharī" names the first forty-one verses of the hundred-verse Saundaryalaharī, also written Soundarya Laharī, which is attributed to Ādi Śaṅkarācārya. That reading matches how vignanam.org, sanskritdocuments.org and stotranidhi.com all print and index the text. Those forty-one verses are the dense Śrī Vidyā and tantric portion, which tradition says Śiva himself dictated. Verses 42 to 100 are the Saundaryalaharī proper, and the same tradition holds that Śaṅkara composed those himself, after the god's own dictation was cut short. The full text, with this same division stated in its own header and Sections, is at:

./stotras/devi/lalita/14_saundarya_lahari.txt (verses 1-41 = Ānandalaharī; verses 42-100 = Saundaryalaharī proper)

A minority of editions, and some regional printings, do treat Ānandalaharī as a free-standing work of forty-one verses in its own right, with its own circulation and its own commentary tradition. That is noted here, but not adopted. The base sources this corpus follows for lalita/14 print and index the whole hundred verses as one continuous text under the one title, with Ānandalaharī marked only as its first section, rather than issued as a separate document. No verse content is duplicated here for that reason — duplicating the forty-one verses into a second file would mean keying the same Sanskrit twice under two titles, which this corpus avoids. A future need may arise for the ānandalaharī portion to be treated as fully independent, with its own header, its own recension note, and separate citation from the saundaryalaharī proper. If so, that should be done as a deliberate addition, scoped on its own, rather than folded silently into this pointer.
