Title: Śrī Śakti Aṣṭottaraśata Divyasthānīya Nāma Stotram
Devanāgarī: श्रीशक्ति-अष्टोत्तरशतदिव्यस्थानीयनामस्तोत्रम्
Telugu: శ్రీ శక్తి అష్టోత్తరశత దివ్యస్థానీయ నామ స్తోత్రం

Author: The colophon places it in the **Matsya Mahāpurāṇa**; the sanskritdocuments witness gives adhyāya 13. The speaker is the Goddess herself (v. 30, "whoever … beholds me", `पश्यति मां`), listing where she dwells. In the Matsya Purāṇa this is Satī, in the Dakṣa episode.

Source / recension: Primary text: **Gītā Press, Gorakhpur — Śatanāma Stotra Saṅgraha**, code 1850, pp. 198–202 (stotra pp. 198–200, nāmāvalī pp. 201–202; PDF pp. 199–203), transcribed from the page images of the printed edition. Entry 38 of the volume's forty. **Collated against sanskritdocuments.org `shaktyaShTottarashatadivyasthAnIyanAmAvaliH.itx`** (encoded and proofread by Karthik Raman; source given as Matsya Purāṇa, adhyāya 13). This is the **nāmāvalī only**, with 108 names in the same order and the same Matsya colophon. It is independent of Gītā Press. The file notes a "corresponding stotram", but sanskritdocuments' index lists no such file, so no independent witness of the verses was found. The verses are checked against Gītā Press's own nāmāvalī and, through it, against SD. All 108 names were compared mechanically. Stotra and nāmāvalī are paired in one file as the publisher prints them.

Recension note: **Gītā Press followed; the sanskritdocuments nāmāvalī (SD) is recorded, not adopted.** Differences (Gītā Press → SD): no. 51 `मथुरायां देविकायै` → `mayūrāyāṁ devikāyai` (Gītā Press's v. 13 also reads `मथुरायां`); no. 66 `माहेश्वरे पुरे स्वाहायै` → `maheśvare pure` (Gītā Press's v. 17 also reads `माहेश्वरे`); no. 95 `कुशद्वीपे` → `kṛśadvīpe`; no. 90 `चन्द्रभागायां` → `cadrabhāgāyāṁ` (an SD slip). No. 32 `संताने` is written with the anusvāra in the print, where SD has `santāne`. **Stotra and nāmāvalī agree throughout.** **Form of the names.** Each name is a place in the locative followed by the Goddess who dwells there in the dative, e.g. no. 1 `वाराणस्यां विशालाक्ष्यै`. The internal space is kept as printed. The last few are not places but classes or realms: "among the Mothers" (no. 105), "among faithful wives" (no. 106), "in the minds of all embodied beings" (no. 108). **No. 108** is printed `सर्वशरीरिणां चित्ते ब्रह्मकलानामशक्त्यै` in both witnesses. It folds the verse's `ब्रह्मकला नाम शक्तिः` ("the power called Brahmakalā") into one dative and is kept exactly as printed. **Repeated names at different places:** Puṣṭi (nos. 82, 85), Amṛtā (nos. 64, 92), Lalitā (nos. 3 as Lalitādevī, 32), the Mother (nos. 78 with Lakṣmī, 87). **Place names.** Many are the classical śākta pīṭhas and tīrthas (Vārāṇasī, Naimiṣa, Prayāga, Kedāra, Śrīśaila, Karavīra = Kolhāpur, Jālandhara, Kashmir). Others are not certain. They are transliterated as printed, not identified. **Prefix.** Gītā Press prints `ॐ` before each name; this corpus prints `श्री` for every nāmāvalī without exception (user, 2026-09-08). **Cross-reference:** `devi/main/11_ashtadasa_shaktipitha_stotram.txt` lists eighteen śakti-pīṭhas and is a different work. `shiva/33_shiva_ashtottarashata_divyasthaniya_nama_stotram.txt` is the companion list for Śiva in the same volume.

Blurb: The Goddess's own map of where to find her: Viśālākṣī at Vārāṇasī, Lalitā at Prayāga, Kāmākṣī on Gandhamādana, Mahālakṣmī at Karavīra, Rādhā in Vṛndāvana, Sītā at Citrakūṭa, and on across a hundred tīrthas. It ends beyond geography: Gāyatrī in the mouth of the Veda, Prabhā in the sun's disc, and the power called Brahmakalā in the mind of every living being. Bathe at these places and see her, she says, and you will live a kalpa in Śiva's city.

en: At Vārāṇasī she is Viśālākṣī, at Naimiṣa Liṅgadhāriṇī; at Prayāga the goddess Lalitā, on Gandhamādana Kāmākṣī.
tel: వారాణసియందు విశాలాక్షి, నైమిషమునందు లింగధారిణి; ప్రయాగయందు లలితాదేవి, గంధమాదనమునందు కామాక్షి.

|| iti śrīmatsyamahāpurāṇe śrīśaktyaṣṭottaraśatadivyasthānīyanāmastotraṁ sampūrṇam ||
en: Thus, in the Śrī Matsya Mahāpurāṇa, the Śrī Śakti Aṣṭottaraśata Divyasthānīya Nāma Stotram is complete.
tel: ఇట్లు శ్రీమత్స్యమహాపురాణమునందు శ్రీశక్త్యష్టోత్తరశతదివ్యస్థానీయనామస్తోత్రము సంపూర్ణము.
hi: इस प्रकार श्रीमत्स्यमहापुराणमें श्रीशक्ति-अष्टोत्तरशतदिव्यस्थानीयनामस्तोत्र सम्पूर्ण हुआ।
