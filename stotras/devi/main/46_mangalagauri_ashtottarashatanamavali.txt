Blurb: The hundred and eight names of Maṅgaḷagaurī, the auspicious Gaurī whom a married woman worships on the Tuesdays of Śrāvaṇa. The names call her the mother of Gaṇeśa and of Guha, the daughter of the mountain and of Menakā, the half of Śiva's body, the giver of married happiness, of sons and grandsons, and of every wished-for thing; they name her Annapūrṇā, Sarasvatī, Durgā, Kātyāyanī and Śyāmalā, and end in the names of Śrīvidyā: Śrīcakravāsinī, Tripurā, Sundarī, Svarṇagaurī, the deity of the sixteen syllables. The three Maṅgaḷagaurī vrata files in puja point to this list at the worship with a hundred and eight names.
