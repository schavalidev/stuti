Title: Śrī Garbharakṣāmbikā Stotram
Devanāgarī: श्री गर्भरक्षाम्बिका स्तोत्रम्
Telugu: శ్రీ గర్భరక్షాంబికా స్తోత్రం

Author: Brahmaśrī Anantarāma Dīkṣita (Anantarama Dikshitar), who signs himself inside verse 6 as "dīkṣitānantarāmeṇa" and states there that he composed the stotra "for the Devī's pleasure"; the closing colophon on both sources repeats the attribution — "iti brahmaśrī anantarāmadīkṣitavaryaviracitaṁ garbharakṣāmbikā-stotram."

Source / recension: Base text is vignanam.org's śuddha-devanāgarī edition (https://vignanam.org/devanagari/garbha-rakshambika-stotram.html), collated against its own Telugu-script edition and its IAST English edition, all three of which agree with one another. This was then cross-checked line by line against an independently sourced text — sanskritdocuments.org's garbharakShAmbikAstotram (doc_devii, encoded and proofread by PSA Easwaran with Rajani Arjun Shankar, last updated 22 April 2023). Full two-source verification was achieved for all six verses; four small readings differ between the two, all recorded in the recension note below. stotranidhi.com carries the same text under the same title and attribution but its page body could not be extracted through automated fetching, so it corroborates the title, author and verse count rather than supplying a third collation.

Verse count: 6 numbered verses, each in the same four-pāda shape, plus a one-line refrain (dhruvapada) beginning "śrīmādhavī kānanasthe" which vignanam prints once before verse 1 and again after every verse, and which sanskritdocuments.org instead prints as the closing line inside each verse. The refrain is given here once as its own unnumbered block and then again as the printed closing line of each verse, following the sources; that is a layout difference between the two editions, not a difference of text.

Sections:
ధ్రువపదం — Dhruvapada (the one-line refrain to Garbharakṣāmbikā of the mādhavī grove, repeated throughout)
స్తోత్రం — Stotram (the six numbered verses proper, the last of which carries the poet's signature and the phalaśruti)
సమాప్తి వాక్యం — Colophon (the unnumbered closing line naming Śrī Anantarāma Dīkṣita as composer)

Blurb: This is a temple hymn of six verses to the goddess of Thirukarukavur. At that shrine a pregnancy is the thing actually asked for. The hymn locates her precisely: on the bank of the tank, at the left side of Śiva as Vāmadeva, in the mādhavī grove that gives the town its name, and in the town itself, which is called Garbharakṣāpura, meaning the city of womb-protection. Verse 4 recalls the founding legend of the temple by naming the woman Vedikā, whose womb she guarded. The hymn describes her anointing with sandal paste on a Friday in the month of Āṣāḍha, and her chariot procession at the brahmotsava. It asks her in the plainest words to protect the living beings within the womb, called garbhastha-jantūn, and the children who are brought to her feet for that very reason. In the vignanam recension the reciter names herself in the refrain, in the feminine, as 'this devotee who praises you'.

--- verse none | section: Dhruvapada ---
deva:
श्रीमाधवी काननस्थे गर्भरक्षाम्बिके पाहि भक्तां स्तुवन्तीम् ॥
iast:
śrīmādhavī kānanasthe garbharakṣāmbike pāhi bhaktāṁ stuvantīm ||
en: O Garbharakṣāmbikā ("Mother who protects the womb"), you who dwell in the sacred mādhavī grove — protect this devotee who stands praising you. Both the noun and the participle here are feminine (bhaktāṁ stuvantīm) in the Telugu-tradition printing, so the refrain puts the words in the mouth of a woman: it is the expectant mother herself who is speaking. The mādhavī grove is the shrine's own sacred wood at Thirukarukavur, and this line is repeated after every verse of the stotra.
tel: ఓ గర్భరక్షాంబికా — పవిత్రమైన మాధవీ వనంలో వేంచేసియున్న తల్లీ, నిన్ను స్తుతిస్తూ నిలిచిన ఈ భక్తురాలిని రక్షించు. ఇక్కడ "భక్తాం స్తువంతీమ్" అనే పదాలు స్త్రీలింగంలో ఉన్నాయి — అంటే ఈ ప్రార్థన చేస్తున్నది గర్భవతి అయిన స్త్రీయే. మాధవీ వనం అంటే తిరుక్కరుకావూరు క్షేత్రంలోని స్థలవృక్ష వనం. ఈ పంక్తి ప్రతి శ్లోకం తరువాత పునరావృతమవుతుంది.
hi: हे गर्भरक्षांबिका — पवित्र माधवी वन में विराजमान माता, तुम्हारी स्तुति करती हुई इस भक्तिनी की रक्षा करो। यहाँ "भक्तां स्तुवन्तीम्" शब्द स्त्रीलिंग में हैं — अर्थात् यह प्रार्थना करने वाली स्वयं गर्भवती स्त्री है। माधवी वन तिरुक्करुकावूर क्षेत्र का अपना स्थल-वृक्ष वन है। यह पंक्ति प्रत्येक श्लोक के बाद दोहराई जाती है।

--- verse 1 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
वापीतटे वामभागे
वामदेवस्य देवस्य देवि स्थिता त्वम् ।
मान्या वरेण्या वदान्या
पाहि गर्भस्थजन्तून् तथा भक्तलोकान् ॥
श्रीमाधवी काननस्थे गर्भरक्षाम्बिके पाहि भक्तां स्तुवन्तीम् ॥
iast:
vāpītaṭe vāmabhāge
vāmadevasya devasya devi sthitā tvam |
mānyā vareṇyā vadānyā
pāhi garbhasthajantūn tathā bhaktalokān ||
śrīmādhavī kānanasthe garbharakṣāmbike pāhi bhaktāṁ stuvantīm ||
en: On the bank of the temple tank, at the left side of the god Vāmadeva (Śiva, under the name of one of his five faces — the goddess's shrine at Thirukarukavur stands to his left), there you are established, O Devī: honoured, most excellent, open-handed. Protect the beings that lie within the womb (garbhastha-jantūn), and likewise the whole company of your devotees. O Garbharakṣāmbikā of the mādhavī grove, protect this devotee who praises you.
tel: దేవాలయ పుష్కరిణి ఒడ్డున, వామదేవుడైన శివుని (శివుని పంచముఖాలలో ఒకదాని పేరు; తిరుక్కరుకావూరులో అమ్మవారి సన్నిధి ఆయనకు ఎడమవైపున ఉంటుంది) వామభాగంలో, ఓ దేవీ, నీవు నెలకొని ఉన్నావు — మాన్యురాలవు, వరేణ్యురాలవు, వదాన్యురాలవు. గర్భంలో ఉన్న జీవులను రక్షించు, అలాగే భక్తలోకాన్ని కూడా రక్షించు. ఓ మాధవీవనవాసినీ గర్భరక్షాంబికా, నిన్ను స్తుతించే ఈ భక్తురాలిని రక్షించు.
hi: मंदिर की वापी (पुष्करिणी) के तट पर, वामदेव नामक शिव के (शिव के पंच मुखों में से एक का नाम; तिरुक्करुकावूर में देवी का सन्निधान उनके बाईं ओर है) वाम भाग में, हे देवी, तुम स्थित हो — माननीया, वरेण्या, वदान्या। गर्भ में स्थित जीवों की रक्षा करो, और उसी प्रकार समस्त भक्तजनों की भी। हे माधवी वन में निवास करने वाली गर्भरक्षांबिका, तुम्हारी स्तुति करती इस भक्तिनी की रक्षा करो।

--- verse 2 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
श्रीगर्भरक्षापुरे या
दिव्यसौन्दर्ययुक्ता सुमाङ्गल्यगात्री ।
धात्री जनित्री जनानां
दिव्यरूपां दयार्द्रां मनोज्ञां भजे त्वाम् ॥
श्रीमाधवी काननस्थे गर्भरक्षाम्बिके पाहि भक्तां स्तुवन्तीम् ॥
iast:
śrīgarbharakṣāpure yā
divyasaundaryayuktā sumāṅgalyagātrī |
dhātrī janitrī janānāṁ
divyarūpāṁ dayārdrāṁ manojñāṁ bhaje tvām ||
śrīmādhavī kānanasthe garbharakṣāmbike pāhi bhaktāṁ stuvantīm ||
en: She who dwells in Śrī Garbharakṣāpura — the "city of womb-protection," the Sanskrit name of Thirukarukavur — endowed with divine beauty, her very body made of auspiciousness (sumāṅgalya), the sustainer and the birth-giver of all people: you of divine form, wet with compassion, altogether lovely, I worship you. O Garbharakṣāmbikā of the mādhavī grove, protect this devotee who praises you.
tel: శ్రీగర్భరక్షాపురంలో — తిరుక్కరుకావూరుకు సంస్కృత నామం, అంటే "గర్భరక్షణ నగరం" — వేంచేసియున్న ఆమె, దివ్యసౌందర్యంతో కూడినది, సుమాంగల్యమే శరీరంగా కలది, జనులకు ధాత్రి (పోషించునది), జనిత్రి (జన్మనిచ్చునది); దివ్యరూపిణీ, దయతో ఆర్ద్రమైన హృదయం కలదానా, మనోహరీ — నిన్ను భజిస్తున్నాను. ఓ మాధవీవనవాసినీ గర్భరక్షాంబికా, నిన్ను స్తుతించే ఈ భక్తురాలిని రక్షించు.
hi: जो श्रीगर्भरक्षापुर में — तिरुक्करुकावूर का संस्कृत नाम, अर्थात् "गर्भरक्षा की नगरी" — विराजमान हैं, दिव्य सौंदर्य से युक्त, जिनका शरीर ही सुमांगल्य है, जो जनों की धात्री (पालनकर्त्री) और जनित्री (जन्मदात्री) हैं; हे दिव्यरूपा, करुणा से आर्द्र, मनोज्ञा — मैं तुम्हारा भजन करता/करती हूँ। हे माधवी वन में निवास करने वाली गर्भरक्षांबिका, तुम्हारी स्तुति करती इस भक्तिनी की रक्षा करो।

--- verse 3 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
आषाढमासे सुपुण्ये
शुक्रवारे सुगन्धेन गन्धेन लिप्ता ।
दिव्याम्बराकल्पवेषा
वाजपेयादियागस्थभक्तैः सुदृष्टा ॥
श्रीमाधवी काननस्थे गर्भरक्षाम्बिके पाहि भक्तां स्तुवन्तीम् ॥
iast:
āṣāḍhamāse supuṇye
śukravāre sugandhena gandhena liptā |
divyāmbarākalpaveṣā
vājapeyādiyāgasthabhaktaiḥ sudṛṣṭā ||
śrīmādhavī kānanasthe garbharakṣāmbike pāhi bhaktāṁ stuvantīm ||
en: In the greatly meritorious month of Āṣāḍha, on a Friday, she is anointed with fragrant sandal-paste, dressed in divine garments and ornaments and full ceremonial attire, and beheld at her best by the devotees assembled at the Vājapeya and the other śrauta sacrifices. The verse is describing the shrine's own festival calendar: Āṣāḍha Fridays are the Devī's special days there, and the anointing (gandha-lepana) is the temple ritual the worshipper would actually be attending. O Garbharakṣāmbikā of the mādhavī grove, protect this devotee who praises you.
tel: మహాపుణ్యప్రదమైన ఆషాఢమాసంలో, శుక్రవారం నాడు, సుగంధభరితమైన గంధంతో లేపనం చేయబడినది; దివ్యమైన వస్త్రాలంకారాలతో, పూర్ణమైన అలంకారవేషంతో ఉన్నది; వాజపేయము మొదలైన యాగాలలో పాల్గొన్న భక్తులచే చక్కగా దర్శించబడినది. ఈ శ్లోకం క్షేత్రపు ఉత్సవక్రమాన్నే వర్ణిస్తోంది — అక్కడ ఆషాఢ శుక్రవారాలు అమ్మవారికి విశేషదినాలు, గంధలేపనం భక్తులు ప్రత్యక్షంగా చూసే ఆలయ సేవ. ఓ మాధవీవనవాసినీ గర్భరక్షాంబికా, నిన్ను స్తుతించే ఈ భక్తురాలిని రక్షించు.
hi: महापुण्यमय आषाढ़ मास में, शुक्रवार के दिन, सुगंधित चंदन से लिप्त; दिव्य वस्त्र-आभूषणों तथा पूर्ण अलंकार-वेष से युक्त; वाजपेय आदि यागों में स्थित भक्तों द्वारा भली भाँति दर्शित। यह श्लोक क्षेत्र के अपने उत्सव-क्रम का ही वर्णन करता है — वहाँ आषाढ़ के शुक्रवार देवी के विशेष दिन हैं, और गंध-लेपन वह मंदिर-सेवा है जिसे उपासक साक्षात् देखता है। हे माधवी वन में निवास करने वाली गर्भरक्षांबिका, तुम्हारी स्तुति करती इस भक्तिनी की रक्षा करो।

--- verse 4 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
कल्याणदात्रीं नमस्ये
वेदिकाढ्यस्त्रिया गर्भरक्षाकरीं त्वाम् ।
बालैस्सदा सेविताङ्घ्रिं
गर्भरक्षार्थमारादुपेतैरुपेताम् ॥
श्रीमाधवी काननस्थे गर्भरक्षाम्बिके पाहि भक्तां स्तुवन्तीम् ॥
iast:
kalyāṇadātrīṁ namasye
vedikāḍhyastriyā garbharakṣākarīṁ tvām |
bālaissadā sevitāṅghriṁ
garbharakṣārthamārādupetairupetām ||
śrīmādhavī kānanasthe garbharakṣāmbike pāhi bhaktāṁ stuvantīm ||
en: I bow to you, giver of every good thing, who performed the protection of the womb for the woman Vedikā — the sage Nidhruva's wife, whose unborn child the goddess is said in the temple legend to have kept alive after another sage's curse had struck it, the event on which the whole shrine is founded. Your feet are forever served by the children who have come close to you, drawn near for the sake of just that womb-protection, and you are surrounded by them. (The sanskritdocuments reading vedikākhya-striyā, "the woman named Vedikā," makes the reference to her explicit; the vignanam reading vedikāḍhya-striyā is discussed in the recension note.) O Garbharakṣāmbikā of the mādhavī grove, protect this devotee who praises you.
tel: సమస్త కల్యాణాలను ప్రసాదించే నీకు నమస్కరిస్తున్నాను — వేదిక అనే స్త్రీకి గర్భరక్షణ చేసిన తల్లీ. ఆమె నిధ్రువ మహర్షి భార్య; మరొక ముని శాపం తగిలి ఆమె గర్భస్థ శిశువు నశించబోతుంటే అమ్మవారు ఆ గర్భాన్ని కాపాడిందని క్షేత్రగాథ చెబుతుంది — ఈ సంఘటనపైనే ఈ ఆలయమే నిలిచింది. ఆ గర్భరక్షణ కోసమే నీ దగ్గరకు తీసుకురాబడిన బాలలచేత నీ పాదాలు నిత్యం సేవించబడుతున్నాయి, వారితోనే నీవు చుట్టుముట్టబడి ఉన్నావు. (సంస్కృతడాక్యుమెంట్స్ పాఠం "వేదికాఖ్యస్త్రియా" = "వేదిక అనే పేరుగల స్త్రీ" అని స్పష్టంగా చెబుతుంది; విజ్ఞానం పాఠం "వేదికాఢ్యస్త్రియా" గురించి రిసెన్షన్ నోట్‌లో చర్చించబడింది.) ఓ మాధవీవనవాసినీ గర్భరక్షాంబికా, నిన్ను స్తుతించే ఈ భక్తురాలిని రక్షించు.

Recension note: This is the plain "Garbharakṣāmbikā Stotram" of Anantarāma Dīkṣita, six verses with a refrain — deliberately distinguished here from the other texts that circulate for the same Thirukarukavur shrine, of which a Garbharakṣāmbikai Kavacam is the most widely printed (temple-published Tamil-tradition material also carries an aṣṭakam and Tamil devotional pieces for the goddess). None of those were used, and nothing from them has been mixed into this file.

Two independent sources were collated in full: vignanam.org (Devanāgarī, Telugu and IAST editions, which agree with each other) as the base, and sanskritdocuments.org's garbharakShAmbikAstotram (doc_devii; encoded by PSA Easwaran, proofread by PSA Easwaran and Rajani Arjun Shankar, updated 22 April 2023). The verse order, verse count and numbering agree exactly across both; there are no numbering irregularities. Four readings differ:

(1) The refrain. vignanam prints pāhi bhaktāṁ stuvantīm, with both words feminine — "protect this devotee (f.) who is praising you"; sanskritdocuments prints pāhi bhaktaṁ stuvantam, masculine. The feminine is retained here because it is the Telugu-tradition printing that is this corpus's base, and because it matches the text's actual use: the stotra is recited by expectant mothers. The variant is substantive and worth knowing, so it is recorded rather than smoothed away.

(2) Verse 4, second pāda. vignanam reads vedikāḍhyastriyā; sanskritdocuments reads vedikākhyastriyā, "the woman named Vedikā." The latter is the better reading — it names the sage Nidhruva's wife of the temple legend outright, and -ākhya "named" is the ordinary idiom — and the ḍhya/khya confusion is an easy Devanāgarī-to-Devanāgarī slip. The base-text reading is kept in deva/iast per the sourcing rule, with the variant flagged here and glossed inside the verse's own en field so the reference to Vedikā is not lost.

(3) Verse 6, second pāda. vignanam devyāśca tuṣṭyai; sanskritdocuments devyāḥ sutuṣṭyai. No difference in sense.

(4) Verse 1, third word of pāda 2. vignanam's Devanāgarī has the vocative devi (देवि); sanskritdocuments has the nominative devī (देवी). The vocative is adopted, since the whole verse addresses the goddess in the second person (sthitā tvam).

Layout differs between the two editions without the text differing: vignanam prints the refrain as a free-standing line before verse 1 and again after each of the six verses, while sanskritdocuments folds it in as the third line inside verses 1–5 and drops it after verse 6. This file follows vignanam — a standalone opening refrain block, then the refrain as the closing printed line of every verse including verse 6 — and notes that the closing repetition after verse 6 is absent from the sanskritdocuments text.

Transliteration choices: IAST here uses plain e and o throughout per the corpus convention, so vignanam's own transliteration (which prints ē/ō) has been normalised; anusvāra is written ṁ, normalising vignanam's ṁ; ca is written ca, not vignanam's cha. One script-level variant: verse 2's sumāṅgalyagātrī is spelt with ल in both Devanāgarī sources and so transliterated with l, but vignanam's Telugu edition prints సుమాంగళ్య with ళ (ḷa), a normal Telugu/Tamil orthographic preference rather than a different word.

The text carries no viniyoga, nyāsa, dhyāna or pañcapūjā apparatus — it is a short temple hymn, not a ritual manual, and no such sections have been invented for it. Note also that the text nowhere mentions the shrine's well-known ghee (ney) prasādam given to pregnant women; the temple practices it does name are the Āṣāḍha-Friday sandal-paste anointing (verse 3) and the brahmotsava chariot procession through the brahmins' street (verse 5), and only those are reported in the en/tel/hi fields. stotranidhi.com hosts the same stotra under the same title and attribution, but its page body resisted automated extraction, so it confirms the identification without adding a third collation. No verse is unverified.
