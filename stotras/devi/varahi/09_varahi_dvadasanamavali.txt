Title: Śrī Vārāhī Dvādaśanāmāvalī
Devanāgarī: श्री वाराही द्वादशनामावली
Telugu: శ్రీ వారాహీ ద్వాదశనామావళిః

Author: traditional. The twelve names collected here are the same twelve given in verse-form in the companion text Śrī Vārāhī Dvādaśanāma Stotram, whose colophon frames them as Brahmāṇḍa Purāṇa, Lalitopākhyāna, chapter 17 (iti śrībrahmāṇḍapurāṇe lalitopākhyāne saptadaśodhyāye śrī vārāhī dvādaśanāma stotram): Hayagrīva there teaches the names to Agastya (addressed as ghaṭodbhava, "pot-born") with the promise that Vārāhī becomes gracious merely on their being heard. A second recension of that verse-stotram, at sanskritdocuments.org, instead opens with a viniyoga naming Aśvānana (a horse-faced epithet of Hayagrīva) as ṛṣi, anuṣṭubh as chandas, and Vārāhī as devatā. This bare āvalī itself, as printed at the source used here, carries neither of those framings — no viniyoga, ṛṣi, or chandas is attached to it; it simply strings the twelve names into "oṁ [name]āyai namaḥ" formulas for archana/japa use.

Source / recension: Primary source is stotranidhi.com's śrī vārāhī dvādaśanāmāvalī, read across its own parallel Devanāgarī edition (stotranidhi.com/hi/sri-varahi-dwadasa-namavali-in-sanskrit/), Telugu edition (stotranidhi.com/sri-varahi-dwadasa-namavali-in-telugu/), and English-IAST edition (stotranidhi.com/en/sri-varahi-dwadasa-namavali-in-english/) — all three agree exactly on the twelve names and their order. Independently cross-checked against the separate verse-form Śrī Vārāhī Dvādaśanāma Stotram at sanskritdocuments.org/doc_devii/vArAhIdvAdashanAmastotram.html, whose two ślokas (pañcamī daṇḍanāthā ca saṅketā samayeśvarī | tathā samayasaṅketā vārāhī potriṇī śivā || vārtālī ca mahāsenāpyājñācakreśvarī tathā | arighnī ceti samproktaṁ nāma dvādaśakaṁ mune ||) list the identical twelve names in the identical order, confirming the set from a source independent of stotranidhi.com. vignanam.org, this corpus's usual primary source, does NOT appear to carry this specific dvādaśanāmāvalī — only its Vārāhī Sahasranāmāvalī and Aṣṭottараśatanāmāvalī turned up there under several guessed slugs (varahi-dwadasa-namavali, varahi-dvadasa-namavali, sri-varahi-dwadasa-namavali, all returning empty/AccessDenied) and via targeted search — so this is the spec's anticipated fallback case, flagged accordingly. The twelve per-name explanatory glosses folded into the en/tel fields below are adapted from the explanatory notes stotranidhi.com prints beneath its own Telugu-script edition of this āvalī; no independently authored gloss-text for these could be located, so that explanatory layer (as opposed to the name-list itself, which is doubly attested) carries a single-source caveat. Two minor orthographic points, not treated as errors: (1) the source spells the Om-praṇava as ओं / ఓం (o + anusvāra) rather than as the ligature ॐ used elsewhere in this corpus's kavaca material (e.g. 07_subrahmanya_kavaca_stotram.txt) — both notations are standard and phonetically identical, and this file follows the source's own ओं spelling rather than silently normalizing it; (2) name 9 appears as वार्ताल्यै (vārtālyai, plain la) in the Devanāgarī edition but as వార్తాళ్యై (vārtāḷyai, retroflex ḷa) in the Telugu edition — both la/ḷa spellings of Vārtālī are attested for this name in circulation, and each script field below simply follows its own source page rather than forcing agreement.

Verse count: 12 names, not metrical verses. The source is one continuous, otherwise unnumbered string of "oṁ [name]āyai namaḥ" formulas; it marks a running tally only after name 6 and again after name 12 (a japa/archana counting-checkpoint, e.g. "...vārāhyai namaḥ | 6" and "...arighnyai namaḥ | 12" in the English edition, ఓం వారాహ్యై నమః । ౬ and ఓం అరిఘ్న్యై నమః । ౧౨ in Telugu) rather than sequential 1-12 numbering of each individual name. Per the spec's guidance on nāmāvalī material that the source itself leaves undivided, this file gives each of the twelve names its own "verse none" block rather than inventing a numbering the source does not use; those two tally-checkpoint numerals are themselves omitted from the deva/iast fields as editorial marks, per the hard rule against verse numbers inside the text, and are recorded here instead.

Sections:
నామావళి — Nāmāvalī (the twelve "oṁ [name]āyai namaḥ" formulas themselves; the source attaches no viniyoga, dhyāna, or phalaśruti of its own to this āvalī, unlike the companion verse-stotram)

Blurb: The bare twelve-name archana/japa list of the boar-faced Śrī Vidyā power Vārāhī — Pañcamī through Arighnī — reformatted from the same Purāṇic name-set that the companion Vārāhī Dvādaśanāma Stotram gives in verse, here stripped to the twelve "oṁ [name]āyai namaḥ" formulas used to offer flowers/kuṅkuma one name at a time in worship.

deva:
ओं पञ्चम्यै नमः ।
iast:
oṁ pañcamyai namaḥ |
en: Salutations to her who is Pañcamī, "the fifth" — she is counted as the fifth of the sapta-mātṛkās (seven mother-goddesses), and, as the fifth power-form of Śiva corresponding to the sadāśiva-tattva, she carries this name.
tel: పంచమి అనే పేరు గల ఆమెకు నమస్కారం — ఈమె సప్తమాతృకలలో ఐదవది; అలాగే పరమశివుని ఐదవ శక్తిస్వరూపమైన సదాశివతత్త్వం కాబట్టి ఈమెను పంచమి అని పిలుస్తారు.
hi: पञ्चमी अर्थात् "पाँचवीं" नाम वाली उनको नमस्कार — ये सप्त मातृकाओं में पाँचवीं गिनी जाती हैं, और शिव के पाँचवें शक्ति-स्वरूप सदाशिव-तत्त्व के रूप में भी इसी नाम से जानी जाती हैं।

oṁ daṇḍanāthāyai namaḥ |
en: Salutations to Daṇḍanāthā, "mistress of the rod" — she is the commander-in-chief of Lalitā Parameśvarī's army, so named because she bears the daṇḍa (staff/rod of command) in hand to punish the wicked.
tel: దండనాథా అనే ఆమెకు నమస్కారం — ఈమె లలితా పరమేశ్వరి సైన్యానికి సైన్యాధ్యక్షురాలు; దుష్టులను శిక్షించడానికి చేతిలో దండాన్ని ధరించినది కాబట్టి దండనాథా అని పేరు.
hi: दण्डनाथा अर्थात् "दण्ड की स्वामिनी" उनको नमस्कार — ये ललिता परमेश्वरी की सेना की सेनाध्यक्षा हैं, दुष्टों को दण्ड देने के लिए हाथ में दण्ड धारण करने के कारण इन्हें यह नाम मिला है।
