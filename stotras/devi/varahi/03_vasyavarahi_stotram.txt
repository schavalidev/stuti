Title: Śrī Vaśyavārāhī Stotram
Devanāgarī: श्री वश्यवाराही स्तोत्रम्
Telugu: శ్రీ వశ్యవారాహీ స్తోత్రం

Author: traditional; the viniyoga names Nārada as the ṛṣi of the mantra, anuṣṭubh as the chandas, and Śrī Vaśyavārāhī as the devatā; the closing colophon states the text is drawn "iti atharvaśikhāyām" — from the Atharvaśikhā, an Atharvavedic ancillary text — no human poet-signature is given.

Source / recension: composed primarily from manblunder.com's full ritual edition of the "Śrī Vaśyavārāhī Stotram" (there sourced to the Durlabha Stotra Saṅgraha, and printed with its complete viniyoga, three nyāsas, dhyāna, pañcapūjā, the 29-syllable mantra, the eleven-verse stotra, a repeated uttaranyāsa, and a closing samarpaṇa verse), cross-checked against stotranidhi.com's "śrī vaśyavārāhī stotram" page (IAST only, no Devanāgarī; covers the viniyoga through the eleven verses but omits the closing nyāsa-repetition and samarpaṇa) and against bhaktipath.wordpress.com's Devanāgarī posting (covers only the eleven-verse stotra core, without any ritual apparatus). The text was NOT found on vignanam.org despite searching the /english/, /devanagari/ and /telugu/ slug patterns there for "vasya-varahi-stotram" and variants — flagged. It was also not found as a dedicated file in sanskritdocuments.org's doc_devii catalog (checked both by browsing the catalog page and by targeted search; the catalog carries a kirātavārāhīstotram and Vārāhī-sahasranāma material but no vaśyavārāhī-titled file), so cross-checking here rests on three independently-typed web editions rather than on a scanned or manuscript source — flagged as reduced cross-checking overall, with the closing samarpaṇa verse (found only in manblunder) additionally flagged as single-sourced. Where the three editions disagree on substance, the reading attested by two of the three, or the more grammatically coherent reading where only two editions cover a passage, is used, and every such departure is recorded in the recension note at the end of this file. A handful of evident single-letter OCR-type slips in the manblunder edition (kaniṣṭikhābhyāṃ → kaniṣṭhikābhyāṃ; tvat-pasādāt- → tvatprasādād-; abhīṣṭhaṃ → abhīṣṭaṃ) have been silently normalized since no source attests the misspelling as a genuine variant.

Verse count: 11 numbered stotra verses, framed by a prose viniyoga, three nyāsa units (ṛṣyādi-nyāsa, karanyāsa, ṣaḍaṅga-nyāsa), one dhyāna verse, a pañcapūjā unit, and the navaviṁśatyakṣara (29-syllable) mantra — none of which carry verse numbers in the source. The ṣaḍaṅga-nyāsa, dhyāna and pañcapūjā are then repeated verbatim after the stotra as uttaranyāsa (closing with digvimoka, "release of the directions," in place of the opening digbandha, "binding of the directions"), followed by a closing samarpaṇa verse offering water to the deity. 23 blocks in total.

Sections:
వినియోగం — Viniyoga (prose, assigning the mantra to Nārada as ṛṣi, anuṣṭubh chandas, Śrī Vaśyavārāhī as devatā, and its bīja/śakti/kīlaka)
ఋష్యాదిన్యాసః — Ṛṣyādi-nyāsa (assigning the ṛṣi, chandas, devatā, bīja, śakti, kīlaka and the viniyoga itself to the head, mouth, heart, secret place, feet, navel and whole body)
కరన్యాసః — Karanyāsa (assigning the bīja-mantra syllables to the fingers and palms)
షడంగన్యాసః — Ṣaḍaṅga-nyāsa (assigning the same syllables to the six limbs, closing with digbandha)
ధ్యానం — Dhyānam (one verse visualizing the goddess as Tāre Tāriṇi, universal mother and giver of dominance)
పంచపూజ — Pañcapūjā (five-fold mental worship with gandha, puṣpa, dhūpa, dīpa, naivedya, sarvopacāra)
మంత్రః — Mantra (the 29-syllable Vaśyavārāhī mantra)
స్తోత్రం — Stotram (the eleven verses proper, closing with its own colophon)
ఉత్తరషడంగన్యాసః — Uttaranyāsa: Ṣaḍaṅga-nyāsa repeated, closing with digvimoka instead of digbandha
ఉత్తరధ్యానం — Uttaranyāsa: Dhyānam repeated
ఉత్తరపంచపూజ — Uttaranyāsa: Pañcapūjā repeated
సమర్పణం — Samarpaṇam (closing verse offering water, sealing the japa)

Blurb: This is a stotra of vaśīkaraṇa, that is, of subjugation and attraction, addressed to Vārāhī in her Aśvārūḍhā aspect, as the commander of Śrī Lalitā's cavalry, mounted upon a horse. It is built around a mantra of twenty-nine syllables: 'oṁ aiṁ klīṁ glauṁ aśvārūḍhāṁ sarvavaśyavārāhyai mama sarvavaśaṅkari kuru kuru ṭhaḥ ṭhaḥ'. Its eleven verses ask her, verse after verse, to bring under the reciter's control kings, wealth, grain, royal insignia, animals, birds, and the entire world. They ask her also to make loved ones and strangers alike remember him and follow him.

--- verse none | section: Viniyoga ---
deva:
अस्य श्रीवश्यवाराहीस्तोत्रमहामन्त्रस्य नारद ऋषिः अनुष्टुप्छन्दः श्रीवश्यवाराही देवता
ऐं बीजं क्लीं शक्तिः ग्लौं कीलकं
मम सर्ववशार्थे जपे विनियोगः ॥
iast:
asya śrīvaśyavārāhīstotramahāmantrasya nārada ṛṣiḥ anuṣṭupchandaḥ śrīvaśyavārāhī devatā
aiṁ bījaṁ klīṁ śaktiḥ glauṁ kīlakaṁ
mama sarvavaśārthe jape viniyogaḥ ||
en: For this great mantra, the Śrī Vaśyavārāhī Stotra, Nārada is the ṛṣi (seer), the anuṣṭubh is the chandas (metre), Śrī Vaśyavārāhī is the devatā (deity); aiṁ is the bīja (seed-syllable) — the Vāgbhava/Sarasvatī bīja, associated with speech and knowledge; klīṁ is the śakti (power-syllable) — the Kāma bīja, associated with the arousal and fulfillment of desire; glauṁ is the kīlaka (the pin that locks the mantra's power) — the Pṛthvī bīja, associated with the earth and material gain; the viniyoga (ritual application) of this japa is for bringing everything and everyone under one's control (sarvavaśārtha).
tel: ఈ మహామంత్రమైన శ్రీ వశ్యవారాహీ స్తోత్రానికి నారదుడు ఋషి, అనుష్టుప్పు ఛందస్సు, శ్రీ వశ్యవారాహి దేవత; "ఐం" బీజం — వాక్కుకు, జ్ఞానానికి సంబంధించిన సరస్వతీ బీజం; "క్లీం" శక్తి — కోరికల సిద్ధికి సంబంధించిన కామబీజం; "గ్లౌం" కీలకం — భూమికి, భౌతిక సంపదకు సంబంధించిన పృథ్వీబీజం; సర్వులనూ వశపరచుకొనే నిమిత్తం చేసే ఈ జపంలో దీని వినియోగం.
hi: इस महामंत्र, श्री वश्यवाराही स्तोत्र के, नारद ऋषि हैं, अनुष्टुप् छंद है, श्री वश्यवाराही देवता हैं; "ऐं" बीज है — यह वाणी और ज्ञान से जुड़ा सरस्वती-बीज है; "क्लीं" शक्ति है — यह इच्छाओं की पूर्ति से जुड़ा काम-बीज है; "ग्लौं" कीलक है — यह पृथ्वी और भौतिक संपदा से जुड़ा पृथ्वी-बीज है; सबको वश में करने के प्रयोजन से किए जाने वाले इस जप में इसका विनियोग है।

--- verse none | section: Ṛṣyādi-nyāsa ---
deva:
नारद ऋषये नमः शिरसि ।
अनुष्टुप्छन्दसे नमः मुखे ।
श्रीवश्यवाराहीदेवतायै नमः हृदि ।
ऐं बीजाय नमः गुह्ये ।
क्लीं शक्तये नमः पादयोः ।
ग्लौं कीलकाय नमः नाभौ ।
मम सर्ववशार्थे जपे विनियोगाय नमः सर्वाङ्गे ।
इति ऋष्यादिन्यासः ॥
iast:
nārada ṛṣaye namaḥ śirasi |
anuṣṭupchandase namaḥ mukhe |
śrīvaśyavārāhīdevatāyai namaḥ hṛdi |
aiṁ bījāya namaḥ guhye |
klīṁ śaktaye namaḥ pādayoḥ |
glauṁ kīlakāya namaḥ nābhau |
mama sarvavaśārthe jape viniyogāya namaḥ sarvāṅge |
iti ṛṣyādinyāsaḥ ||
en: The ṛṣyādi-nyāsa touches, in turn: reverence to Nārada the ṛṣi at the head, to the anuṣṭubh chandas at the mouth, to the devatā Śrī Vaśyavārāhī at the heart, to the bīja aiṁ at the secret place (guhya), to the śakti klīṁ at the feet, to the kīlaka glauṁ at the navel, and finally to the viniyoga itself — the intent to bring everything under one's control — over the whole body; each element of the mantra's formal apparatus (seer, metre, deity, seed, power, pin, purpose) is thereby physically placed on the practitioner before japa begins.
tel: ఋష్యాదిన్యాసంలో వరుసగా: నారద ఋషికి శిరస్సు వద్ద, అనుష్టుప్ఛందస్సుకు ముఖం వద్ద, శ్రీ వశ్యవారాహీ దేవతకు హృదయం వద్ద, ఐం బీజానికి గుహ్యస్థానం వద్ద, క్లీం శక్తికి పాదాల వద్ద, గ్లౌం కీలకానికి నాభి వద్ద నమస్కరిస్తారు; చివరగా సర్వులనూ వశపరచుకొనే వినియోగానికి శరీరమంతటా న్యాసం చేస్తారు — మంత్రానికి సంబంధించిన ప్రతి అంశాన్నీ (ఋషి, ఛందస్సు, దేవత, బీజం, శక్తి, కీలకం, ప్రయోజనం) జపానికి ముందు సాధకుని శరీరం మీద భౌతికంగా ప్రతిష్ఠించడం ఇందులోని ఉద్దేశం.
hi: ऋष्यादिन्यास में क्रमशः: नारद ऋषि को शिर पर, अनुष्टुप् छंद को मुख पर, श्री वश्यवाराही देवता को हृदय पर, ऐं बीज को गुह्य स्थान पर, क्लीं शक्ति को पैरों पर, ग्लौं कीलक को नाभि पर नमन करते हुए न्यास किया जाता है; अंत में सबको वश में करने के विनियोग का न्यास सम्पूर्ण शरीर पर किया जाता है — इस प्रकार मंत्र के हर अंग (ऋषि, छंद, देवता, बीज, शक्ति, कीलक, प्रयोजन) को जप से पहले साधक के शरीर पर प्रतिष्ठित किया जाता है।

--- verse none | section: Karanyāsa ---
deva:
ॐ ऐं अङ्गुष्ठाभ्यां नमः ।
ॐ क्लीं तर्जनीभ्यां नमः ।
ॐ ग्लौं मध्यमाभ्यां नमः ।
ॐ अश्वारूढां अनामिकाभ्यां नमः ।
ॐ सर्ववश्यवाराह्यै कनिष्ठिकाभ्यां नमः ।
ॐ मम सर्ववशङ्करि कुरु कुरु ठः ठः करतलकरपृष्ठाभ्यां नमः ।
इति करन्यासः ॥
iast:
oṁ aiṁ aṅguṣṭhābhyāṁ namaḥ |
oṁ klīṁ tarjanībhyāṁ namaḥ |
oṁ glauṁ madhyamābhyāṁ namaḥ |
oṁ aśvārūḍhāṁ anāmikābhyāṁ namaḥ |
oṁ sarvavaśyavārāhyai kaniṣṭhikābhyāṁ namaḥ |
oṁ mama sarvavaśaṅkari kuru kuru ṭhaḥ ṭhaḥ karatalakarapṛṣṭhābhyāṁ namaḥ |
iti karanyāsaḥ ||
en: The karanyāsa assigns, in turn, the syllables aiṁ, klīṁ and glauṁ to both thumbs, both forefingers and both middle fingers; the name Aśvārūḍhā ("she mounted on a horse," Vārāhī's cavalry-commander aspect) to the ring fingers; the epithet "to Sarvavaśyavārāhī" (Vārāhī who subjugates all) to the little fingers; and the mantra's imperative core — "mama sarvavaśaṅkari kuru kuru ṭhaḥ ṭhaḥ," "O all-subjugator, do it for me, do it, ṭhaḥ ṭhaḥ" — to the front and back of both palms.
tel: కరన్యాసంలో వరుసగా: ఐం, క్లీం, గ్లౌం బీజాలను రెండు బొటనవేళ్ళు, రెండు చూపుడువేళ్ళు, రెండు మధ్యవేళ్ళకు; అశ్వారూఢా (గుర్రంపై అధిరోహించిన వారాహి సేనాధిపతి రూపం) అనే పేరును ఉంగరపువేళ్ళకు; సర్వవశ్యవారాహికి అనే సంబోధనను చిటికెనవేళ్ళకు; మంత్రంలోని ఆజ్ఞాపూర్వక భాగమైన "మమ సర్వవశంకరి కురు కురు ఠః ఠః" (అందరినీ వశపరచేదానా, నా కోసం చేయి, చేయి) అనేదాన్ని అరచేతుల ముందు వెనుక భాగాలకు న్యాసం చేస్తారు.
hi: करन्यास में क्रमशः: ऐं, क्लीं, ग्लौं बीजों को दोनों अंगूठों, दोनों तर्जनियों, दोनों मध्यमाओं पर; अश्वारूढा (घोड़े पर आरूढ़ वाराही की सेनापति रूप) नाम को अनामिकाओं पर; सर्ववश्यवाराही को यह संबोधन कनिष्ठिकाओं पर; तथा मंत्र के आज्ञासूचक भाग "मम सर्ववशंकरि कुरु कुरु ठः ठः" (हे सबको वश करने वाली, मेरे लिए करो, करो) को दोनों हथेलियों के आगे-पीछे के भाग पर न्यास किया जाता है।

--- verse none | section: Ṣaḍaṅga-nyāsa ---
deva:
ॐ ऐं हृदयाय नमः ।
ॐ क्लीं शिरसे स्वाहा ।
ॐ ग्लौं शिखायै वषट् ।
ॐ अश्वारूढां कवचाय हुम् ।
ॐ सर्ववश्यवाराह्यै नेत्रत्रयाय वौषट् ।
ॐ मम सर्ववशङ्करि कुरु कुरु ठः ठः अस्त्राय फट् ।
इति षडङ्गन्यासः ।
भूर्भुवस्सुवरोमिति दिग्बन्धः ॥
iast:
oṁ aiṁ hṛdayāya namaḥ |
oṁ klīṁ śirase svāhā |
oṁ glauṁ śikhāyai vaṣaṭ |
oṁ aśvārūḍhāṁ kavacāya hum |
oṁ sarvavaśyavārāhyai netratrayāya vauṣaṭ |
oṁ mama sarvavaśaṅkari kuru kuru ṭhaḥ ṭhaḥ astrāya phaṭ |
iti ṣaḍaṅganyāsaḥ |
bhūrbhuvassuvaromiti digbandhaḥ ||

Recension note: This file is assembled from three independently-typed web editions, since the text was not located on vignanam.org (searched under /english/, /devanagari/ and /telugu/ with several slug variants) nor as a dedicated file in sanskritdocuments.org's doc_devii catalog (browsed directly and searched by name; the catalog holds a kirātavārāhīstotram and Vārāhī-sahasranāma material but nothing titled vaśyavārāhī). The base text and overall structure (viniyoga, three nyāsas, dhyāna, pañcapūjā, the 29-syllable mantra, eleven stotra verses, a repeated uttaranyāsa closing in digvimoka, and a closing samarpaṇa verse) follow manblunder.com's edition, which is the only one of the three carrying the full ritual apparatus and the only one with a matching Devanāgarī text throughout. This was cross-checked against stotranidhi.com's IAST-only edition (viniyoga through the eleven verses, no closing nyāsa-repetition or samarpaṇa) and against bhaktipath.wordpress.com's Devanāgarī posting (eleven-verse stotra core only, no ritual apparatus). Substantive places where the three disagree, and how each was resolved:
- Verse 4, "vyāpāreṣu subhāṣu ca" (manblunder) vs. "vyāpāreṣu sabhāsu ca" (stotranidhi and bhaktipath, majority) — this file reads sabhāsu ("in assemblies").
- Verse 7, "tvat-pasādād-dayāṁ" (manblunder, apparently missing an akṣara) vs. "tvatprasādāddayāṁ" (stotranidhi and bhaktipath, majority) — this file reads tvatprasādāddayāṁ.
- Verse 10, "bhaktyāpattinivāraṇam" (manblunder and bhaktipath, majority) vs. "bhaktyāpaddhinivāraṇam" (stotranidhi) — this file reads bhaktyāpattinivāraṇam; also "bāṇāstraṁ" (manblunder, stotranidhi) vs. bhaktipath's likely misprint "vāṇāstraṁ" — this file reads bāṇāstraṁ.
- Verse 3, "yathātvaṁ vaśamānaya"/capitalized "Vārāhī" (manblunder) vs. vocative "vārāhi" (stotranidhi, bhaktipath, and grammatically expected) — this file reads vārāhi.
- Verse 6, "ramā" (manblunder) vs. "rāmā" (stotranidhi, bhaktipath) — this file reads rāmā (a generic word for "a beloved/passionate woman" here, distinct from the proper name Ramā/Lakṣmī used correctly, and unanimously, as "ramāṁ" in verse 11).
- Verse 1, bhaktipath's "aśvārūḍhaṁ" (accusative-looking) is very likely its own typo for the vocative "aśvārūḍhe" read identically by manblunder and stotranidhi; this file follows the majority.
- Verses 2, 8: manblunder's "vaśīkaraṇa kāryārtha" (missing final anusvāra) vs. stotranidhi/bhaktipath's "vaśīkaraṇakāryārthaṁ" — this file uses the anusvāra-complete, majority form.
- Verse 11: bhaktipath's "prāpnuyādyakto" is a clear misprint for "prāpnuyādbhakto," read that way by both other sources.
- Dhyānam: manblunder's "vipakṣapakṣadalini" and "sandāyini" vs. stotranidhi's "vipakṣayakṣadalini" and "sandhāyini" — only these two sources cover the dhyāna (bhaktipath omits the ritual portions), so this file keeps manblunder's readings as base text and flags stotranidhi's as the alternate. Similarly the closing pāda "rūpaṁ dehi yaśaśca satataṁ" (manblunder) vs. "rūpaṁ dehi yaśaśca dehi satataṁ vaśyaṁ" (stotranidhi) — this file adopts stotranidhi's fuller reading with the repeated dehi, which better fits the verse's long metre.
- Throughout the nyāsas and the mantra, the name of Vārāhī's cavalry-commander aspect appears as "aśvarūḍhāṁ"/"aśvarūḍhaṁ" (manblunder, itself inconsistent between a long-ā spelling in the nyāsas and a short-a spelling in the mantra) versus "aśvārūḍhā" (stotranidhi, nominative, no anusvāra, consistent throughout). This file standardizes to "aśvārūḍhāṁ" (long ā, anusvāra-final) at every occurrence for internal consistency. None of the attested forms is the grammatically expected dative "aśvārūḍhāyai" before namaḥ; this may reflect a genuine crux in the mantra's oral/print transmission rather than a simple typo, so it is flagged rather than silently corrected to the "expected" grammar.
- The closing samarpaṇa verse ("guhyātiguhyagoptrī...") is attested only in manblunder's edition among the three checked here and is flagged as single-sourced; it was not contradicted by the other two, which simply end their transcriptions earlier (stotranidhi ends after the eleven verses; bhaktipath likewise).
A small number of evident single-letter OCR-type slips in manblunder (kaniṣṭikhābhyāṁ → kaniṣṭhikābhyāṁ; abhīṣṭhaṁ → abhīṣṭaṁ at verses 5 and 11) have been silently normalized to the standard spelling, since no source attests the misspelling itself as a genuine variant. Verse 11's closing phrase "yathāpi vaḥ" is printed identically by all three sources but its precise grammatical force is not fully certain; it is translated here in its plainest available sense rather than smoothed into a more confident reading. All Sanskrit content, including the vaśīkaraṇa material naming specific classes of beings (kings, animals, birds, "jewels among women") to be brought under the reciter's control, is translated plainly as historical Tantric devotional material per the corpus's Śākta-handling guidance, without softening or added commentary.
