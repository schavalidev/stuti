Blurb: The messenger returns to Śumbha with the Devī's refusal, and the war begins. Śumbha orders his general Dhūmralocana to drag her back by the hair and to kill anyone — god, yakṣa or gandharva — who tries to save her. Dhūmralocana rides out with sixty thousand asuras, finds her on the snow mountain, and repeats the demand; she answers only that she cannot stop him. When he charges, she reduces him to ash with a single huṅkāra, then rains arrows, spears and axes on his army, and her lion tears through the rest with claws, jaws and paws. Hearing this, Śumbha sends Caṇḍa and Muṇḍa with larger forces.

Recension note: Verse numbering follows the Saptaśatī's own ritual division, in which standalone "uvāca" speaker-lines are counted as verses and some ślokas are split, so that the thirteen adhyāyas total exactly 700 — the saptaśatī of the title. GRETIL's Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa prints the same words divided into 587 verses across its adhyāyas 81–93, so the two cannot be aligned by verse number and were collated by content. Gītā Press prints the 700-division followed here. This file was re-collated against the printed Gītā Press edition after an earlier round had used only a degraded archive.org OCR of it. That pass confirmed 62 of the 63 readings the OCR round had adopted and reversed one, and corrected further readings, front matter and verse divisions besides; where the print and the other witnesses disagree, the print is followed unless our reading is the one Gītā Press itself prints and the alternative sits in its footnotes. The Gītā Press pages for adhyāya 6 are legible enough to be genuinely decisive here, which is not always the case. Its Devanāgarī OCR is badly degraded in the usual ways — धूप्रलोचनः for धूम्रलोचनः, हुंकारेणेव for हुङ्कारेणैव, केषाचित् for केषाञ्चित्, ख for स, पहासुरान् for महासुरान्, and the Hindi commentary and running heads bleeding into the verse block — so it was read for readings and verse division only, never quoted as text. What it settled: its per-verse numbering (अ० ६।२ through ६।२४) confirms all 24 verses including the four उवाच lines, and its colophon tally (उवाच ४, श्लोकाः २०, एवम् २४, एवमादितः ४९२) confirms the count exactly. Its editorial footnotes were the most valuable evidence in the chapter: at 6.16 it prints आक्रम्य in the main text and demotes the base's आक्रान्त्या to a pāṭhāntara, and separately records three competing forms of स जघान — settling a diff GRETIL alone could only have made suspicious. It also confirms अपरः at 6.5, ततः at 6.13, and the presence of v8 that GRETIL drops. One thing it did not settle: its chapter title reads शुम्भनिशुम्भसेनानीधूम्रलोचनवधो नाम, longer than the JSON colophon's धूम्रलोचनवधो नाम; per instructions the JSON colophon was followed for the title. 2 divergence groups were left as printed, being orthographic or damage in the comparison text — GRETIL orthographic and typing damage across the chapter: Grouped verdict for the remaining diffs, all of them GRETIL corruption rather than variant readings: paṣṭyā for ṣaṣṭyā (v7), talaprihāreṇa for talaprahāreṇa (v17), sṛtāstena for kṛtāstena (v18), kṛtstraṁ for kṛtsnaṁ (v20). Base kept. 3 readings were corrected — v. 5: The base's वोत्तिष्ठतेः is not Sanskrit — a visarga stranded where a word was lost, leaving the pāda a syllable short and कश्चित् without its correlate.
