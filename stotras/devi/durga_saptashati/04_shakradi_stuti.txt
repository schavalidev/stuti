Blurb: Mahiṣāsura has just been killed. This chapter is almost entirely the hymn the freed gods then sing — twenty-two verses in vasantatilakā naming the Devī as the power by which the world is stretched out, as Śrī and Alakṣmī, Svāhā and Svadhā, the three Vedas, Medhā, Durgā and Gaurī, and reading even her slaughter of the asuras as a mercy that sends them to heaven. It closes in anuṣṭubh: a prayer for protection in every direction, the Devī's offer of a boon, the gods' request that she come when remembered, and her vanishing.
