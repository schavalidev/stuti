Blurb: The fifth adhyāya opens the Uttama Carita. Śumbha and Niśumbha have seized the three worlds and the gods' shares of sacrifice; the dispossessed gods go to Himavat and praise Viṣṇumāyā in the great litany that names her in every being as consciousness, sleep, hunger, shadow, power, thirst, forbearance, modesty, peace, faith, beauty, fortune, memory, compassion and delusion. Pārvatī, come to bathe in the Gaṅgā, hears it; Ambikā steps out of her sheath as Kauśikī, and Pārvatī herself darkens into Kālikā. Caṇḍa and Muṇḍa report her beauty, Śumbha sends the envoy Sugrīva with an offer of empire, and she answers with the vow that only the man who defeats her in battle will have her hand. Verses 8-82 circulate separately in this corpus as the Aparājitā Stotram (file 06).
