Blurb: With Śumbha slain, the gods led by Agni stand before Kātyāyanī, their faces opened like lotuses, and praise her. The chapter carries the Nārāyaṇī Stuti. After opening verses in the upajāti metre, sixteen ślokas salute her form after form: Brahmāṇī, Māheśvarī, Kaumārī, Vaiṣṇavī, Vārāhī, Nārasiṁhī, Aindrī, Śivadūtī and Cāmuṇḍā. Each closes with the words nārāyaṇi namo'stu te. Those sixteen verses, numbers 8 to 23, also circulate on their own, and they stand separately in this corpus as file 34. The gods then invoke her face, her trident, her bell and her sword, and ask that she always put an end to their enemies. She consents, and foretells her coming descents as Raktadantikā, Śatākṣī, Śākambharī, Durgā, Bhīmā and Bhrāmarī.

No insertion; GRETIL's omission of the speaker lines देवा ऊचुः (v. 38) and देव्युवाच: GRETIL, editing the Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa, absorbs or drops bare speaker lines — which is why it gives 51 units to the Saptaśatī's 55. Gītā Press counts all four uvāca lines as verses (उवाच ४). Base kept; Orthographic trivia — anusvāra vs conjunct nasal (सम्मोहितं v. 5), ū/u (भूयश्च v. 46), -i vs -e vocatives (प्रदायिनि v. 9; पुष्टि, महारात्रि v. 22), कृत्स्न-/अलङ्घ्य- v. 4, आत्ममूर्तिं v. 30, भीमं रूपं v. 51: All spelling or sandhi-notation, metre-neutral; Gītā Press matches the base wherever legible. On the vocatives it prints the -i forms in the main text and gives -e only as footnoted variants (पा०--पुष्टे, पा०--रात्रे), so the base is the authority reading; Plain GRETIL damage: kṛtstram (v. 4), maghe for anaghe (v. 15), aśeṣāsuraśūdanam (v. 26), noparibhīteḥ (v. 34), viśvārtihāriṇī (v. 35), suragaṇa (v. 37), sudānavān (v. 44), punīnāṁ (v. 51), cokāḥ for lokāḥ (v. 54): Typing/OCR errors of the same family as GRETIL's known "bhupatiḥ" for "bhūpatiḥ"; several are nonsense words. Gītā Press confirms the base at every one where the scan is readable (अनघे, सूदनम्, नोऽरिभीतेः, महासुरान्, मुनीनां, लोकास्तदा). 6 readings were corrected — v. 7: Gītā Press prints स्वर्गमुक्तिप्रदायिनी in the main text and relegates भुक्ति to a footnote (पा० भुक्ति), agreeing with GRETIL. A real variant, not orthography, and the authority edition settles it against the base; v. 25: Gītā Press reads पातु नः सर्वभीतिभ्यः, with GRETIL. "Guard us from all fears" also parallels भीतेः in the very next verse. The scan is clear here; v. 33: Gītā Press prints धारयसीति विश्वम्, agreeing with GRETIL against the base's धारयसीह; legible in the scan; v. 43: Gītā Press has वैप्रचित्तांस्तु दानवान् (the conjunct is OCR-mangled but the तु is unambiguous), with GRETIL. GRETIL's own following सुदानवान् for महासुरान् in v. 44 is its corruption and was not followed; v. 47: Gītā Press reads निरीक्षिष्यामि यन्मुनीन्, matching GRETIL; the base's अहं looks like a smoothing of the harder यत्; v. 49: Gītā Press has यास्याम्यहं (yā-, "I shall attain" that renown), with GRETIL; the base's यस्याम्यहं is a dropped mātrā, not a reading. Part of this chapter also exists in the corpus as a standalone stotra — `devi/durga/34_narayani_stuti.txt` (the Nārāyaṇī Stuti) — because the tradition recites and anthologises it separately as well as in situ. Neither file supersedes the other; the renderings were aligned so the two do not disagree on the same verse. Section divisions in the header are editorial — the Sanskrit carries no internal headings — and are given so a passage can be located; the numbered verses, the front matter and the colophon are as printed in the source.
