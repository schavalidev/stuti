Title: Śrī Durgā Saptaśatī — Mūrti Rahasyam
Devanāgarī: श्री दुर्गा सप्तशती — मूर्तिरहस्यम्
Telugu: శ్రీ దుర్గా సప్తశతీ — మూర్తిరహస్యమ్

Author: Anonymous and undated; the Mūrti Rahasyam is one of the three Rahasyams appended to the Durgā Saptaśatī and is not part of the Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa proper, though the Gītā Press colophon prints it within the Sāvarṇika Manvantara frame of that Purāṇa. It is framed internally as the continued teaching of the ṛṣi Medhas to King Suratha, addressed as vasudhādhipa, on the Devī's mūrtis; the tradition offers no separate author, and none should be assigned.

Blurb: A doctrinal appendix, not narrative: the ṛṣi tells Suratha what the Devī's mūrtis actually look like, so that the forms promised in the Māhātmyam can be meditated on and worshipped correctly. Four are given in turn — Nandā, gold-hued and four-armed on a golden lotus, identified with Lakṣmī; Raktadantikā, red from garments to teeth, nursing her devotees, called Raktacāmuṇḍā and Yogeśvarī; Śākambharī, dark blue, holding vegetables, flowers and a bow, and equated with Śatākṣī, Durgā, Umā, Gaurī, Satī, Caṇḍī, Kālikā and Pārvatī; and Bhīmā with Bhrāmarī, who is Mahāmārī. The chapter closes by calling the teaching secret, forbidding its indiscriminate transmission, and promising release from grave sin through recitation.

--- verse 1 | section: Nandā, the golden Devī ---
deva:
ऋषिरुवाच ।
नन्दा भगवती नाम या भविष्यति नन्दजा ।
स्तुता सा पूजिता भक्त्या वशीकुर्याज्जगत्त्रयम् ॥
iast:
ṛṣiruvāca |
nandā bhagavatī nāma yā bhaviṣyati nandajā |
stutā sā pūjitā bhaktyā vaśīkuryājjagattrayam ||
en: The ṛṣi said: She who will be born of Nanda and be known as the blessed Nandā — praised and worshipped with devotion, she brings the three worlds under her sway.
tel: ఋషి పలికెను: నందుని ఇంట జన్మించి నందా భగవతి అను పేరుతో ప్రసిద్ధురాలు కాబోవు ఆమెను భక్తితో స్తుతించి పూజించినచో ఆమె ముల్లోకములను వశము చేయును.
hi: ऋषि ने कहा: जो नन्द के यहाँ जन्म लेकर नन्दा भगवती नाम से प्रसिद्ध होंगी, उनकी भक्तिपूर्वक स्तुति और पूजा करने पर वे तीनों लोकों को वश में कर देती हैं।

hi: उनका ध्यान इस प्रकार किया जाता है — उत्तम स्वर्ण के समान कान्ति वाली, सुन्दर कान्ति वाले स्वर्णमय वस्त्र धारण किए हुए, स्वर्ण के वर्ण से प्रकाशित तथा उत्तम स्वर्णाभूषणों से विभूषित वह देवी।

en: Thus the Mūrti Rahasyam is complete.
tel: ఇట్లు మూర్తి రహస్యము సంపూర్ణము.
hi: इस प्रकार मूर्ति रहस्य सम्पूर्ण हुआ।

---
Recension note: The three Rahasyams — Prādhānika, Vaikṛtika and Mūrti — are appended to the Saptaśatī and stand outside the Devī Māhātmya's thirteen adhyāyas and its seven-hundred-verse count. They are doctrinal rather than narrative, expounding the Devī's prakṛti, her vikṛtis and her mūrtis, and share a single viniyoga covering the rahasya-traya as a set. Recensions of these three diverge more than the Māhātmya's own text does, so Gītā Press was given decisive weight. This file was re-collated against the printed Gītā Press edition after an earlier round had used only a degraded archive.org OCR of it. That pass confirmed 62 of the 63 readings the OCR round had adopted and reversed one, and corrected further readings, front matter and verse divisions besides; where the print and the other witnesses disagree, the print is followed unless our reading is the one Gītā Press itself prints and the alternative sits in its footnotes. The Gītā Press pages for this Rahasyam are among the better-preserved in the scan and settled a good deal. Verse-final numbering "(दु° मू°। १–२५)" survives intact throughout, which fixed both the verse division and the total of 25 independently of the base. Individual pādas are legible enough at the word level to decide four of the substantive forks — भक्त्या not ध्याता at v1, वृत्तपीनपयोधरा at v18, याः ख्याता at v21, and the presence of v16 and of vv. 23–25 in the base's form, against the second witness which drops or rewrites them. Its consonant clusters and sibilants are still badly mangled (शाकम्भरी printed as जाकस्भरी and हाकम्भरी, शृणु as च्णु, विशाल as विराल, दंष्ट्रा as रंष्र), so nothing was quoted from it as text and no purely orthographic decision rests on it. This edition carries no numbered "१. पा०—" footnote for the Mūrti Rahasyam; instead it brackets supplementary matter inline — a half-verse भुक्त्वा भोगान् यथाकामं देवीसायुज्यमाप्नुयात् after v10, and a three-line coda (एतस्यास्त्वं प्रसादेन… / सर्वरूपमयी देवी… / अतोऽहं विश्वरूपां…) after v25 — which is its way of demoting them; both are left out of the numbered text here, as they are there. Its own v14 differs from the base at two points, but the line is too damaged to act on. 3 divergence groups were left as printed, being orthographic or damage in the comparison text — sanskritdocuments' omission of v16 and its rewriting of vv. 23–25: The second witness drops v16 (विशोका दुष्टदमनी…) entirely and substitutes अधीष्वावहितः स्वयम् / देव्या ध्यानं तवाख्यातम् for the base's अभीष्टफलदायकम् / तस्मात् सर्वप्रयत्नेन देवीं जप निरन्तरम् block, losing v24 with it. Gītā Press prints v16 and prints vv. 23–25 exactly as the base has them, numbered २३–२५. These are genuine recensional divergences in the second witness, and Gītā Press decides against it. Base kept throughout; sanskritdocuments' minor divergences at vv. 3, 6, 8, 9, 13, 15, 17, 22: Grouped verdict on the remaining diffs: कनकाङ्कुश for कमलाङ्कुश (v3), रक्तदंष्ट्रिका for रक्तदन्तिका (v6), सपाययेत्/कामदुधौ for सम्पाययेत्/कामदुघौ (vv. 8–9), शिलीमुखैः for शिलीमुखा° (v13), बिभर्ति for बिभ्रती and स्यात् for सा (v15), अक्षव्यम् and अन्नपानादि सर्वशः for अक्षय्यम् and अन्नपानामृतं फलम् (v17), यस्य कस्यचित् for कस्यचित्त्वया (v22). Gītā Press supports the base at every one of these where its OCR is legible, and the rest are dropped anusvāras or plain corruption in the comparison text. Base kept; v14 — Gītā Press's own variants मूलकादि and क्षुत्तृष्णामृत्युभयापहम्: Gītā Press here reads पुष्पपल्लवमूलकादि° and °भयापहम् against the base's मूलादि° and °जरापहम्. मूलकादि adds a syllable and breaks the anuṣṭubh pāda, so it is set aside as a printing slip rather than a reading; भयापहम् for जरापहम् is metrically sound but the OCR at that line is heavily damaged (क्षुत्तणमु्युभयापहम्) and cannot be read with confidence, so the base is kept and the variant recorded rather than adopted. 4 readings were corrected — v. 2: The base text carries a bracketed ध्याता as a first line of v2; sanskritdocuments instead has ध्याता inside v1 in place of भक्त्या ("पूजिता ध्याता"). Gītā Press prints v1 with भक्त्या and begins v2 straight at कनकोत्तमकान्तिः, with no ध्याता anywhere. The word is a displaced variant reading, not text; it is deleted and v1 stands as the base has it; v. 18: Gītā Press reads पयोधरा here, which also avoids the verbatim repetition of v13's वृत्तपीनघनस्तनी four verses earlier — the base's घनस्तनी looks like contamination from that line. Metre is unaffected. Replaced on Gītā Press's authority; v. 21: Gītā Press reads याः ख्याता; the OCR is clean at this point. व्याख्याता is likely anticipation of व्याख्यानं at the head of v23. Replaced on Gītā Press's authority. Gītā Press also confirms the base's चित्रभ्रमरपाणिः against sanskritdocuments' चित्रभ्रमरसंकाशा; v. 25: The base's ध्यानम् followed by a space before मया is a malformed nasal, not a variant; Gītā Press prints ध्यानं. Normalised to the anusvāra. This also settles the collation against sanskritdocuments' तवाख्यातं for मया ख्यातं, where Gītā Press supports the base. Section divisions in the header are editorial — the Sanskrit carries no internal headings — and are given so a passage can be located; the numbered verses, the front matter and the colophon are as printed in the source.
