Title: Gāyatrī-paṭala — the Gāyatrī mantras of the deities (Mantra-mahārṇava)
Devanāgarī: गायत्रीपटलम् — देवतागायत्रीमन्त्राः (मन्त्रमहार्णवे)
Telugu: గాయత్రీపటలము — దేవతా గాయత్రీ మంత్రాః (మంత్రమహార్ణవే)

Author: No single author. The Gāyatrī-paṭala is a chapter of the **Mantra-mahārṇava**, a tāntric compendium that gathers mantras from many earlier tantras. Its own first section cites the Śāradātilaka. The mantras are purāṇic and tāntric, not Vedic, and are printed without accent.

Source / recension: Base text: **Mantra-mahārṇava, edited by Mādhavarāya Vaidya, Śrī Veṅkaṭeśvara Steam Press, Mumbai, 1924** — the Gāyatrī-paṭala, pages 274 to 280 of the second khaṇḍa (the running heads read `म० खं० २ । गा० तं० । तरं० १`). **The text here was read from the page images of that print**, archive.org item `ntkg-mantra-maharnava-by-madhava-raya-vaidya-sanskrit-t`, leaves n603 to n615, **not from the item's OCR**, which is heavily corrupt and was used only to find the chapter. A second printing of the same work was consulted — *Mantra-mahārṇava, Pūrva Khaṇḍa*, Khemrāj Śrīkṛṣṇadāsa, Śrī Veṅkaṭeśvara Steam Press (archive.org `yepa_mantra-maharnava-purva-khanda...`), which carries the same chapter under the same numbering. **That is the same publishing house and it is not an independent witness**; it was used only as a check on the reading of the page. Independent collation: the **Puraścaryārṇava** of Mahārāja Pratāpasiṁha of Nepal, ṣaṣṭha-taraṅga, sandhyā-vidhi, pages 504 to 508 (Digital Library of India scan, archive.org `in.ernet.dli.2015.487362`). That is a different work of a different lineage, and it names its own sources for each mantra — Merutantra, Yāmala, Kumārī-tantra, Bhairava-tantra, Kālikārṇava, Jñānārṇava, Vīracūḍāmaṇi, Mahākāla-saṁhitā. The results of that collation are set out below, mantra by mantra. **Gītā Press was checked on archive.org and publishes no collection of deity gāyatrīs.**

Recension note: **What the Puraścaryārṇava confirms.** Six mantras stand in it word for word as they stand here: Gaṇeśa (which it takes from the Yāmala), the third Viṣṇu form, Hayagrīva, Chinnamastā, Rudra, and the Kāma mantra that this print gives in its footnote. Its Lakṣmī mantra, `महालक्ष्म्यै च विद्महे महाश्रियै च धीमहि । तन्नः श्रीः प्रचोदयात्`, is likewise the alternative that this print gives in its own footnote. **Two word-level differences, printed in round brackets in the line itself**, per the bracket rule in the repo-root `CLAUDE.md`: at unit 23 the Puraścaryārṇava reads `काममालिन्यै` for `काममालायै`, and at unit 41 it reads `कन्याकुमारी` for `कन्याकुमार्यै`. The base reading stands first in each. **Two further bracketed readings come from the base print's own footnotes**: at unit 13 `तन्त्रान्तरे तु पक्षायेति पाठः`, giving `सुवर्णपक्षाय`; and at unit 27 `महाद्युतिकरायेति पाठः`. **Where the two works give different mantras for the same deity, both stand, and neither is merged into the other.** The Puraścaryārṇava's Sūrya is `ॐ सप्ततुरगाय विद्महे सहस्रकिरणाय धीमहि । तन्नो रविः प्रचोदयात्` (Merutantra); its Garuḍa is `ॐ सुपर्णाय विद्महे पक्षिराजाय धीमहि । तन्नो गरुडः प्रचोदयात्`; its **Hanumān is `ॐ हनुमते विद्महे आञ्जनेयाय धीमहि । तन्नो वीरः प्रचोदयात्`**, which is a different mantra from the one printed here; its Nṛsiṁha is `ॐ नृसिंहाय विद्महे तीक्ष्णदंष्ट्राय धीमहि । तन्नः सिंहः प्रचोदयात्`; its Gopāla is `ॐ दामोदराय विद्महे वासुदेवाय धीमहि । तन्नः कृष्णः प्रचोदयात्`; and its Mātaṅgī is `ॐ शुकप्रियायै विद्महे श्रीकामेश्वर्यै धीमहि । तन्नः श्यामा प्रचोदयात्`. It also carries a Sudarśana gāyatrī, `ॐ सुदर्शनाय विद्महे महाज्वालाय धीमहि । तन्नश्चक्रः प्रचोदयात्`, which this chapter does not have. **The base print's other footnotes**, which give whole alternative mantras rather than single words, are recorded here and are not printed in the lines: for Lakṣmī, `ॐ महादेवी च विद्महे विष्णुपत्न्यै च धीमहि`; for Kṛṣṇa, `ॐ श्रीकृष्णाय विद्महे दामोदराय धीमहि । तन्नो विष्णुः प्रचोदयात्`; for Nṛsiṁha, `ॐ वज्रनखाय विद्महे तीक्ष्णदंष्ट्राय धीमहि । तन्नो नारसिंहः प्रचोदयात्`; for Sūrya, `ॐ आदित्याय विद्महे मार्तण्डाय धीमहि । तन्नः सूर्यः प्रचोदयात्`; for Agni, `ॐ वैश्वानराय विद्महे लालीलाय धीमहि । तन्नोऽग्निः प्रचोदयात्`; for Kāma, `ॐ कामदेवाय विद्महे पुष्पबाणाय धीमहि । तन्नोऽनंगः प्रचोदयात्`; for Jayadurgā, `ॐ महादेव्यै च विद्महे दुर्गायै च धीमहि । तन्नो देवी प्रचोदयात्`; and for Ṣoḍaśī, both `ॐ क्लीं त्रिपुरादेव्यै विद्महे कामेश्वर्यै धीमहि । तन्नः क्लिन्ने प्रचोदयात्` and a Bālā gāyatrī, `ऐं वागीश्वर्यै विद्महे क्लीं कामेश्वर्यै धीमहि । सौः तन्नः शक्तिः प्रचोदयात्`. **One reading worth flagging.** The Lakṣmaṇa mantra at unit 11 reads `अलबेलाय`, which is not an ordinary Sanskrit word. It is printed twice on the same page, in the mantra and again in the śikhā-nyāsa, so it is what the edition has. No second witness for it was found, and it has not been corrected. **Numbering.** The chapter numbers its mantras 2 to 53, and its colophon calls itself `श्रीत्र्यधिकपञ्चाशद्देवतागायत्रीमन्त्रे गायत्रीपटलतन्त्रम्` — the Gāyatrī-paṭala of fifty-three deity gāyatrīs. Its number 1 is the Brahma-gāyatrī puraścaraṇa-prayoga, whose mantra is the Sāvitrī Gāyatrī itself; that section is a rule of practice, not a deity gāyatrī, and it is not reprinted here. Its number 5, Viṣṇu, carries three forms under one number, and they are three units here. That is why this file has fifty-four units where the print has fifty-two numbers. **On the nyāsa.** Each mantra in the print is followed by its ṣaḍaṅga-nyāsa and its mūla-mantra. Those are the apparatus of the mantra, not the gāyatrī, and they are not transcribed here. **Cross-references.** The Vedic garland of deity gāyatrīs stands at `veda/taittiriya/06_gayatri_mala_mantrah.txt`, and the gāyatrīs of the nine planets at `navagraha/39_navagraha_gayatri_mantrah.txt`. Several mantras appear in more than one of the three files in different wording, and none of the three supersedes another. The Lakṣmī gāyatrī of unit 7 also stands at the end of `devi/lakshmi/06_sri_suktam.txt`. Those files are not altered by this one.

Verse count: 54 — the fifty-two numbered mantras of the chapter, with the three Viṣṇu forms counted separately.

Blurb: These are the Gāyatrī mantras of the deities, as one tāntric compendium gathers them. Each keeps the shape of the Sāvitrī Gāyatrī — first "may we know", then "may we meditate", then "may that deity impel us" — and fills it with the name and the mark of one god or goddess. The chapter is wide. It holds Rāma, Sītā, Lakṣmaṇa and Hanumān together, Kṛṣṇa with Gopāla and Rādhikā, Śiva with Gaurī, Gaṇeśa and Nandī, the sun and moon, the five elements, the guru, the tulasī plant, and the ten Mahāvidyās. A worshipper recites the mantra of the deity being worshipped, in japa and in pūjā.

--- verse 1 | section: ब्रह्मादयः ---
deva:
ॐ परमहंसाय विद्महे महातत्त्वाय धीमहि ॥
तन्नो हंसः प्रचोदयात् ॥

en: Haṁsa. May we know the supreme swan. May we meditate on the great principle. May Haṁsa impel us.
tel: హంస. పరమహంసుని ఎరుగుదుము. మహాతత్త్వమును ధ్యానించుదుము. ఆ హంస మమ్ము ప్రేరేపించుగాక.

--- verse 49 | section: शाक्ताः ---
deva:
ॐ वैरोचन्यै च विद्महे छिन्नमस्तायै धीमहि ॥
तन्नो देवी प्रचोदयात् ॥
iast:
oṁ vairocanyai ca vidmahe chinnamastāyai dhīmahi ||
tanno devī pracodayāt ||
en: Chinnamastā. May we know Vairocanī. May we meditate on Chinnamastā. May the goddess impel us.
tel: ఛిన్నమస్త. వైరోచనిని ఎరుగుదుము. ఛిన్నమస్తను ధ్యానించుదుము. ఆ దేవి మమ్ము ప్రేరేపించుగాక.
hi: छिन्नमस्ता। वैरोचनी को हम जानें। छिन्नमस्ता का हम ध्यान करें। वे देवी हमें प्रेरित करें।

--- verse 50 | section: शाक्ताः ---
deva:
ॐ धूमावत्यै च विद्महे संहारिण्यै च धीमहि ॥
तन्नो धूमा प्रचोदयात् ॥
iast:
oṁ dhūmāvatyai ca vidmahe saṁhāriṇyai ca dhīmahi ||
tanno dhūmā pracodayāt ||
en: Dhūmāvatī. May we know Dhūmāvatī. May we meditate on her who draws all things back into herself. May Dhūmā impel us.
tel: ధూమావతి. ధూమావతిని ఎరుగుదుము. సంహారిణిని ధ్యానించుదుము. ఆ ధూమ మమ్ము ప్రేరేపించుగాక.
hi: धूमावती। धूमावती को हम जानें। संहारिणी का हम ध्यान करें। वे धूमा हमें प्रेरित करें।

--- verse 53 | section: शाक्ताः ---
deva:
ॐ महिषमर्दिन्यै विद्महे दुर्गायै च धीमहि ॥
तन्नो देवी प्रचोदयात् ॥
iast:
oṁ mahiṣamardinyai vidmahe durgāyai ca dhīmahi ||
tanno devī pracodayāt ||
en: Mahiṣamardinī. May we know her who slew the buffalo demon. May we meditate on Durgā. May the goddess impel us.
tel: మహిషమర్దిని. మహిషమర్దినిని ఎరుగుదుము. దుర్గను ధ్యానించుదుము. ఆ దేవి మమ్ము ప్రేరేపించుగాక.
hi: महिषमर्दिनी। महिषमर्दिनी को हम जानें। दुर्गा का हम ध्यान करें। वे देवी हमें प्रेरित करें।

--- verse 54 | section: शाक्ताः ---
deva:
ॐ त्वरिता देव्यै विद्महे महानित्यायै धीमहि ॥
तन्नो देवी प्रचोदयात् ॥
iast:
oṁ tvaritā devyai vidmahe mahānityāyai dhīmahi ||
tanno devī pracodayāt ||
en: Tvaritā. May we know the goddess Tvaritā. May we meditate on the great eternal one. May the goddess impel us.
tel: త్వరిత. త్వరితా దేవిని ఎరుగుదుము. మహానిత్యను ధ్యానించుదుము. ఆ దేవి మమ్ము ప్రేరేపించుగాక.
hi: त्वरिता। त्वरिता देवी को हम जानें। महानित्या का हम ध्यान करें। वे देवी हमें प्रेरित करें।
