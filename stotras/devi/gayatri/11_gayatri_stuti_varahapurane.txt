Title: Śrī Gāyatrī Stutiḥ (Varāha Purāṇe, Maheśvara-kṛtā)
Devanāgarī: श्री गायत्रीस्तुतिः (वराहमहापुराणे महेश्वरकृता)
Telugu: శ్రీ గాయత్రీ స్తుతిః (వరాహమహాపురాణే మహేశ్వరకృతా)

Author: Ascribed within the text to Maheśvara (Śiva) himself; Varāha Mahāpurāṇa, chapter 28, verses 27–33

Source / recension: sanskritdocuments.org, two independently prepared files that transmit the same passage and were compared verse by verse for this edition — https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_devii/gAyatrIstutiH.itx (proofread by Aruna Narayanan, taken from the Devīstotraratnākara anthology, where the passage circulates as a standalone stuti in eight verses) and https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_devii/maheshvarakRRitaMgAyatrIdevIstotram.itx (proofread by PSA Easwaran, taken directly from the Varāha Purāṇa, where it carries the chapter's own numbering 27–33). The Devanāgarī rendering of each was checked against its ITRANS source and against the other file. Three genuine variant readings emerged; they are recorded in the recension note below. This text is not carried by stotranidhi.com, whose Gāyatrī category was exhausted before reaching it.

Verse count: 8

Sections:
स्तुतिः — Stuti, Maheśvara's praise of the Goddess (vv. 1–7)
निगमनम् — Nigamana, the narrator's closing frame (v. 8)
समाप्तिवाक्यम् — Colophon (the unnumbered closing sentence naming the work and its Purāṇic home)

Blurb: This is the oldest and shortest text in this folder. It shows most plainly how Gāyatrī was worshipped before the tantric kavacas took her over. It is seven verses of nothing but direct address. Śiva does not ask her for anything. He simply calls her by name. The names arrive in threes and in ever longer compounds: three worlds, three principles, three fires; great Māyā, great Goddess, great being, great festivity. The last verse leaves address behind for a run of plain identifications, which is the heart of the piece: you are polity, you are speech, you are the earth, you are the imperishable syllable, you are understanding, you are prosperity, you are Oṁ. What deserves notice is the company she keeps here. She is addressed as Sarasvatī directly, as Svāhā and Svadhā, and as Ambikā. She is also addressed, in the middle of a Vedic mother's litany, as Kirātinī, the tribal huntress, with a terrible face and a terrible eye.

--- verse 1 | section: Stuti ---
deva:
महेश्वर उवाच ।
जयस्व देवि गायत्रि महामाये महाप्रभे ।
महादेवि महाभागे महासत्त्वे महोत्सवे ॥
iast:
maheśvara uvāca |
jayasva devi gāyatri mahāmāye mahāprabhe |
mahādevi mahābhāge mahāsattve mahotsave ||
en: Maheśvara said: Be victorious, O Goddess Gāyatrī — O great Māyā, O you of great radiance; O great Goddess, greatly blessed, of great being, O you who are great festivity itself.
tel: మహేశ్వరుడు పలికెను: జయమగుగాక, ఓ గాయత్రీదేవీ — ఓ మహామాయా, ఓ మహాప్రభా; ఓ మహాదేవీ, మహాభాగా, మహాసత్త్వా, మహోత్సవస్వరూపిణీ.
hi: महेश्वर ने कहा: हे गायत्री देवी, तुम्हारी जय हो — हे महामाये, हे महाप्रभे; हे महादेवि, महाभागे, महासत्त्वे, हे महोत्सवस्वरूपे।

--- verse 2 | section: Stuti ---
deva:
दिव्यगन्धानुलिप्ताङ्गि दिव्यस्रग्दामभूषिते ।
वेदमातर्नमस्तुभ्यं त्र्यक्षरस्थे महेश्वरि ॥
iast:
divyagandhānuliptāṅgi divyasragdāmabhūṣite |
vedamātarnamastubhyaṁ tryakṣarasthe maheśvari ||
en: O you whose limbs are anointed with divine fragrance, adorned with divine garlands and wreaths; O Mother of the Vedas, salutation to you, O Maheśvarī who abides within the three syllables.
tel: దివ్యగంధములతో అంగములు అలదబడినదానా, దివ్యమైన మాలలతో, దండలతో అలంకరించబడినదానా; ఓ వేదమాతా, నీకు నమస్కారము, మూడు అక్షరములయందు నెలకొనియున్న ఓ మహేశ్వరీ.
hi: हे दिव्य गंध से अंगों को अनुलिप्त किए हुए, दिव्य माला और स्रग् से भूषित; हे वेदमाता, तुम्हें नमस्कार है, हे तीन अक्षरों में स्थित महेश्वरी।

--- verse 3 | section: Stuti ---
deva:
त्रिलोकस्थे त्रितत्त्वस्थे त्रिवह्निस्थे त्रिशूलिनि ।
त्रिनेत्रे भीमवक्त्रे च भीमनेत्रे भयानके ॥
iast:
trilokasthe tritattvasthe trivahnisthe triśūlini |
trinetre bhīmavaktre ca bhīmanetre bhayānake ||
en: O you who abide in the three worlds, in the three principles, in the three sacrificial fires; O bearer of the trident; O three-eyed one, of terrible face and terrible eye, O fearsome one.
tel: మూడు లోకములయందు, మూడు తత్త్వములయందు, మూడు అగ్నులయందు నెలకొనియున్నదానా, త్రిశూలము ధరించినదానా; ఓ త్రినేత్రీ, భీమవక్త్రా, భీమనేత్రా, భయానకా.
hi: हे तीनों लोकों में, तीनों तत्त्वों में, तीनों अग्नियों में स्थित, हे त्रिशूलधारिणी; हे त्रिनेत्रे, भीमवक्त्रे और भीमनेत्रे, हे भयानके।

--- verse 4 | section: Stuti ---
deva:
कमलासनजे देवि सरस्वति नमोऽस्तु ते ।
नमः पङ्कजपत्राक्षि महामायेऽमृतस्रवे ॥
iast:
kamalāsanaje devi sarasvati namo'stu te |
namaḥ paṅkajapatrākṣi mahāmāye'mṛtasrave ||
en: O Goddess born of the lotus-seated one, O Sarasvatī, salutation be to you. Salutation, O you whose eyes are the petals of the lotus, O great Māyā from whom nectar flows.
tel: ఓ కమలాసనుని (బ్రహ్మ) నుండి పుట్టినదానా, ఓ దేవీ, ఓ సరస్వతీ, నీకు నమస్కారము. ఓ కమలదళముల వంటి కన్నులు కలదానా, అమృతమును స్రవించు ఓ మహామాయా, నమస్కారము.
hi: हे कमलासन (ब्रह्मा) से उत्पन्न देवि, हे सरस्वति, तुम्हें नमस्कार हो। हे कमलपत्र के समान नेत्रों वाली, हे अमृत बहाने वाली महामाये, नमस्कार है।

--- verse 5 | section: Stuti ---

सर्वगे सर्वभूतेशि स्वाहाकारे स्वधेऽम्बिके ।
सम्पूर्णे पूर्णचन्द्राभे भास्वराङ्गे भवोद्भवे ॥
iast:
sarvage sarvabhūteśi svāhākāre svadhe'mbike |
sampūrṇe pūrṇacandrābhe bhāsvarāṅge bhavodbhave ||
en: O all-pervading one, O ruler of every being; O you who are the very utterance Svāhā, O Svadhā, O Ambikā; O complete one, radiant as the full moon, of shining limbs, O source from which all becoming arises.
tel: ఓ సర్వగతా, సర్వభూతేశ్వరీ; ఓ స్వాహాకారస్వరూపిణీ, ఓ స్వధా, ఓ అంబికా; ఓ సంపూర్ణా, పూర్ణచంద్రకాంతీ, ప్రకాశించు అంగములు కలదానా, సర్వము పుట్టు మూలమైనదానా.
hi: हे सर्वगे, हे सर्वभूतेशि; हे स्वाहाकारस्वरूपे, हे स्वधे, हे अम्बिके; हे सम्पूर्णे, पूर्णचंद्र के समान आभा वाली, भास्वर अंगों वाली, हे सब उत्पत्ति के मूल।

--- verse 6 | section: Stuti ---
deva:
महाविद्ये महावेद्ये महादैत्यविनाशिनि ।
महाबुद्ध्युद्भवे देवि वीतशोके किरातिनि ॥

mahāvidye mahāvedye mahādaityavināśini |
mahābuddhyudbhave devi vītaśoke kirātini ||
en: O great knowledge, O great object of all knowing, O destroyer of the great demons; O Goddess from whom great intelligence arises, O you in whom sorrow has ceased, O huntress of the forest.
tel: ఓ మహావిద్యా, మహావేద్యా, మహాదైత్యవినాశినీ; ఓ మహాబుద్ధి ఉద్భవించు మూలమైన దేవీ, శోకరహితా, ఓ కిరాతినీ.

--- verse 7 | section: Stuti ---
deva:
त्वं नीतिस्त्वं महाभागे त्वं गीस्त्वं गौस्त्वमक्षरम् ।
त्वं धीस्त्वं श्रीस्त्वमोङ्कारस्तत्त्वे चापि परिस्थिता ।
सर्वसत्त्वहिते देवि नमस्ते परमेश्वरि ॥
iast:
tvaṁ nītistvaṁ mahābhāge tvaṁ gīstvaṁ gaustvamakṣaram |
tvaṁ dhīstvaṁ śrīstvamoṅkārastattve cāpi paristhitā |
sarvasattvahite devi namaste parameśvari ||
en: You are right conduct, O greatly blessed one; you are speech, you are the earth, you are the imperishable syllable; you are understanding, you are prosperity, you are Oṁkāra, and you stand established within reality itself. O Goddess intent on the welfare of every living being, salutation to you, O supreme Īśvarī.
tel: ఓ మహాభాగా, నీవే నీతివి; నీవే వాక్కువు, నీవే గోవు (భూమి)వి, నీవే అక్షరమవు; నీవే ధీవి, నీవే శ్రీవి, నీవే ఓంకారమవు, తత్త్వమునందే నిలిచియున్నదానవు. సమస్త ప్రాణుల హితమును కోరు ఓ దేవీ, ఓ పరమేశ్వరీ, నీకు నమస్కారము.
hi: हे महाभागे, तुम्हीं नीति हो; तुम्हीं वाणी हो, तुम्हीं गौ (पृथ्वी) हो, तुम्हीं अक्षर हो; तुम्हीं धी हो, तुम्हीं श्री हो, तुम्हीं ओंकार हो, और तत्त्व में ही स्थित हो। हे समस्त प्राणियों का हित करने वाली देवि, हे परमेश्वरि, तुम्हें नमस्कार है।

tel: ఈ విధముగా పరమేష్ఠియైన భవునిచే (శివునిచే) దేవి స్తుతింపబడెను; దేవతలును "జయము" అని ఉచ్చస్వరముతో ఆ పరమేశ్వరిని కీర్తించిరి.

Recension note: The two files used here descend from different intermediaries — one from the Devīstotraratnākara anthology, where the passage travels as a self-contained eight-verse stuti, and one from the Varāha Purāṇa itself, where it is chapter 28.27–33 and is divided into seven units rather than eight. The verse division adopted here is the anthology's, which keeps each half-verse pair intact; the Purāṇic file instead runs verse 3 into the first line of verse 4 and verse 5 into the first line of verse 6, producing the same text with different breaks. Three genuine variant readings distinguish them. In verse 2 the anthology reads divyasragdāma- (from sraj, "garland") against the Purāṇic file's divyastragdāma-, which is a scribal doubling with no separate meaning; sragdāma is adopted. In verse 4 the anthology reads amṛtasrave ("from whom nectar flows," from √sru) against the Purāṇic amṛtastrave, again a doubling; srave is adopted. In verse 7 the anthology reads sarvasattvahite ("intent on the welfare of all beings") against the Purāṇic sarvasattvāhite; the long ā would negate the sense, and the reading is almost certainly an error for the same word, so sattvahite is adopted. In each case the two witnesses agree on everything but the disputed cluster, and the anthology's reading is the grammatically sound one. The two also close differently. The colophon adopted here, and given in the Colophon block above, is the anthology's: इति श्रीवराहमहापुराणे महेश्वरकृता गायत्रीस्तुतिः सम्पूर्णा ॥ (iti śrīvarāhamahāpurāṇe maheśvarakṛtā gāyatrīstutiḥ sampūrṇā ||), "Thus is completed the Gāyatrī Stuti made by Maheśvara, in the Śrī Varāha Mahāpurāṇa." The Purāṇic file ends instead with इति वराहपुराणे अष्टाविंशाध्यायान्तर्गतं महेश्वरकृतं गायत्रीदेवीस्तोत्रं समाप्तम् ॥ (iti varāhapurāṇe aṣṭāviṁśādhyāyāntargataṁ maheśvarakṛtaṁ gāyatrīdevīstotraṁ samāptam ||), "Thus ends the Gāyatrīdevī Stotra made by Maheśvara, contained in the twenty-eighth chapter of the Varāha Purāṇa" — calling the piece a stotra rather than a stuti and locating it by chapter, as one would expect of a file transmitted inside the Purāṇa itself rather than through an anthology. This is a difference of editorial framing and not of the hymn's text.
