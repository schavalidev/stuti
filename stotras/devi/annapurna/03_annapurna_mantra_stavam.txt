Title: Śrī Annapūrṇā Mantra Stavaḥ
Devanāgarī: श्री अन्नपूर्णा मन्त्र स्तवः
Telugu: శ్రీ అన్నపూర్ణా మంత్ర స్తవః

Author: traditional; no poet-signature. The closing colophon assigns the text to the Śrī Mahātripurasiddhānta ("iti śrīmahātripurasiddhānte annapūrṇā mantrastavaḥ"), and the opening rubric makes Śrī Dakṣiṇāmūrti the speaker, addressing Pārvatī (named directly in vv. 9 and 18, and as Īśvarī / Parameśvarī in vv. 1 and 14). Within the text, verse 4 gives the mantra's own ṛṣyādi: Brahmā is the ṛṣi, gāyatrī the chandas, Annapūrṇeśvarī the devatā.

Source / recension: stotranidhi.com's edition, used as the base text here, in all three of its parallel scripts — Telugu (stotranidhi.com/sri-annapurna-mantra-stava-in-telugu/), Devanāgarī (stotranidhi.com/hi/sri-annapurna-mantra-stava-in-sanskrit/) and IAST (stotranidhi.com/en/sri-annapurna-mantra-stava-in-english/) — which agree with one another word for word, including in their shared irregularities; the Devanāgarī and IAST pages were each fetched directly and cross-checked verse by verse, and match exactly. This text is NOT on vignanam.org: nine candidate slugs were probed there and a site-scoped search returned only the ordinary Annapūrṇā Stotram, the Aṣṭottaraśatanāma Stotram and the Nāmāvalī, none of them this work. Verse count: 18

Sections:
Mantroddhāra — derivation of the mantra (vv. 1–5)
Dhyānam — the two meditation verses (vv. 6–7)
Prayoga-phalaśruti — application and its fruits (vv. 8–18)
Colophon — the one-line closing title-statement (unnumbered)
(Editorial: the source prints the text as one continuous stava with no internal headings; the divisions above are editorial, following the divisions the text itself makes in words rather than rubrics — the mantroddhāra of vv. 2–3, the ṛṣyādi-nyāsa of vv. 4–5, the dhyāna pair of vv. 6–7 closed by "evaṁ dhyātvā," and the prayoga section opened at v. 9 by "evaṁ siddhasya mantrasya prayogācchṛṇu")

Blurb: A mantroddhāra text rather than a hymn of praise — Dakṣiṇāmūrti spells out the Annapūrṇā mūlamantra one unit at a time by code-name in vv. 2–3 (praṇava = oṁ, māyā = hrīṁ, śrī = śrīṁ, kāma = klīṁ, then the words namo bhagavati māheśvari annapūrṇe, and last "Agni's beloved" = svāhā, yielding oṁ hrīṁ śrīṁ klīṁ namo bhagavati māheśvari annapūrṇe svāhā), calls it a pratyaṅga or subsidiary limb of the Śrīvidyā, assigns its bīja, śakti and kīlaka (hrīṁ, śrīṁ, klīṁ) in v. 5, supplies the two dhyāna verses to be visualised before japa, and then devotes vv. 8–18 entirely to prayoga — the exact japa counts, homa substances (rice, palāśa flowers, milk, haviṣya) and their specific yields in grain, cattle, wealth and the ending of dāridrya.

--- verse 1 | section: Mantroddhāra ---
deva:
श्री दक्षिणामूर्तिरुवाच ।
अन्नपूर्णामनुं वक्ष्ये विद्याप्रत्यङ्गमीश्वरी ।
यस्य श्रवणमात्रेण अलक्ष्मीर्नाशमाप्नुयात् ॥
iast:
śrī dakṣiṇāmūrtiruvāca |
annapūrṇāmanuṁ vakṣye vidyāpratyaṅgamīśvarī |
yasya śravaṇamātreṇa alakṣmīrnāśamāpnuyāt ||
en: Śrī Dakṣiṇāmūrti said — the rubric that opens the text, marking the whole stava as Dakṣiṇāmūrti's instruction to Pārvatī, who is addressed directly throughout and named at verses 9 and 18. O Īśvarī, I shall now declare the Annapūrṇā manu (mantra), which is a pratyaṅga — a subsidiary limb — of the Vidyā, that is, an auxiliary mantra attached to the Śrīvidyā itself; merely by hearing it, alakṣmī (misfortune, the absence of Lakṣmī, destitution) is destroyed.
tel: శ్రీ దక్షిణామూర్తి పలికెను — ఈ వాక్యంతో స్తవం ఆరంభమవుతుంది; ఇది మొత్తం స్తవాన్ని పార్వతికి దక్షిణామూర్తి చేసిన ఉపదేశంగా నిర్దేశిస్తుంది. పార్వతిని అంతటా సంబోధిస్తూ, తొమ్మిదవ మరియు పద్దెనిమిదవ శ్లోకాలలో పేరుతో పిలుస్తారు. ఓ ఈశ్వరీ, ఇప్పుడు నేను అన్నపూర్ణా మనువును (మంత్రాన్ని) చెప్పబోతున్నాను; ఇది విద్యకు — శ్రీవిద్యకు — ప్రత్యంగం, అనగా దానికి అనుబంధమైన అంగమంత్రం; దీనిని వినినంత మాత్రాన అలక్ష్మి (దారిద్ర్యం, లక్ష్మీవిహీనత) నశిస్తుంది.
hi: श्री दक्षिणामूर्ति ने कहा — इस वाक्य से स्तव आरंभ होता है; यह पूरे स्तव को पार्वती के प्रति दक्षिणामूर्ति के उपदेश के रूप में निर्दिष्ट करता है। पार्वती को सर्वत्र संबोधित किया गया है तथा श्लोक ९ और १८ में नाम लेकर पुकारा गया है। हे ईश्वरी, अब मैं अन्नपूर्णा मनु (मंत्र) कहूँगा, जो विद्या का — अर्थात् श्रीविद्या का — प्रत्यंग है, उससे जुड़ा हुआ अंग-मंत्र है; इसके श्रवण मात्र से अलक्ष्मी (दरिद्रता, लक्ष्मी का अभाव) नष्ट हो जाती है।

--- verse 2 | section: Mantroddhāra ---
deva:
प्रणवं पूर्वमुच्चार्य मायां श्रियमथोच्चरेत् ।
कामं नमः पदं प्रोक्तं पदं भगवतीत्यथ ॥
iast:
praṇavaṁ pūrvamuccārya māyāṁ śriyamathoccaret |
kāmaṁ namaḥ padaṁ proktaṁ padaṁ bhagavatītyatha ||
en: This is the mantroddhāra, the extraction of the mantra by code-names for its bījas. Having first uttered the praṇava — the bīja oṁ — one should then utter māyā, that is the bīja hrīṁ, and then śrī, the bīja śrīṁ; then kāma, the bīja klīṁ; then the word "namaḥ" is prescribed, and then the word "bhagavati." No bīja here is a word to be translated: māyā, śrī and kāma are the standard Śākta cover-names for hrīṁ, śrīṁ and klīṁ respectively.
tel: ఇది మంత్రోద్ధారం — బీజాక్షరాలను సంకేతనామాలతో వెలికితీయడం. ముందుగా ప్రణవాన్ని — అనగా ఓం బీజాన్ని — ఉచ్చరించి, తరువాత మాయను, అనగా హ్రీం బీజాన్ని, ఆ పైన శ్రీని, అనగా శ్రీం బీజాన్ని ఉచ్చరించాలి; తరువాత కామం, అనగా క్లీం బీజం; ఆ తరువాత "నమః" పదం చెప్పబడింది, ఆ పైన "భగవతి" పదం. ఇక్కడ ఏ బీజాన్నీ అర్థంగా అనువదించరాదు: మాయ, శ్రీ, కామ అనేవి వరుసగా హ్రీం, శ్రీం, క్లీం బీజాలకు శాక్త సంప్రదాయంలోని ప్రామాణిక సంకేతనామాలు.
hi: यह मंत्रोद्धार है — बीजाक्षरों को सांकेतिक नामों द्वारा निकालना। पहले प्रणव — अर्थात् ॐ बीज — का उच्चारण करके, फिर माया अर्थात् ह्रीं बीज, तत्पश्चात् श्री अर्थात् श्रीं बीज का उच्चारण करे; फिर काम अर्थात् क्लीं बीज; तदनंतर "नमः" पद कहा गया है, और उसके बाद "भगवति" पद। यहाँ किसी बीज का अर्थानुवाद नहीं करना चाहिए: माया, श्री और काम क्रमशः ह्रीं, श्रीं और क्लीं के शाक्त परंपरा में प्रचलित सांकेतिक नाम हैं।

--- verse 3 | section: Mantroddhāra ---
deva:
माहेश्वरी पदं पश्चादन्नपूर्णेत्यथोच्चरेत् ।
उत्तरे वह्निदयितां मन्त्र एष उदीरितः ॥
iast:
māheśvarī padaṁ paścādannapūrṇetyathoccaret |
uttare vahnidayitāṁ mantra eṣa udīritaḥ ||
en: After that comes the word "māheśvari," and then one should utter "annapūrṇe"; at the end, "the beloved of Vahni" — that is svāhā, Agni's wife, the standard code-name for the oblation-syllable. So the mantra has been declared. Assembled, it reads: oṁ hrīṁ śrīṁ klīṁ namo bhagavati māheśvari annapūrṇe svāhā — the Annapūrṇā mūlamantra, which the whole remainder of the stava is about.
tel: ఆ తరువాత "మాహేశ్వరి" పదం, ఆ పైన "అన్నపూర్ణే" అని ఉచ్చరించాలి; చివరన "వహ్నిదయిత" — అనగా అగ్ని భార్య అయిన స్వాహా, ఆహుతిబీజానికి ప్రామాణిక సంకేతనామం. ఈ విధంగా మంత్రం చెప్పబడింది. కూర్చినపుడు అది: ఓం హ్రీం శ్రీం క్లీం నమో భగవతి మాహేశ్వరి అన్నపూర్ణే స్వాహా — ఇదే అన్నపూర్ణా మూలమంత్రం, స్తవంలోని మిగిలినదంతా దీని గురించే.
hi: उसके बाद "माहेश्वरि" पद, तत्पश्चात् "अन्नपूर्णे" का उच्चारण करे; अंत में "वह्निदयिता" — अर्थात् अग्नि की पत्नी स्वाहा, आहुति-बीज का प्रचलित सांकेतिक नाम। इस प्रकार मंत्र कह दिया गया। संयुक्त रूप में वह है: ॐ ह्रीं श्रीं क्लीं नमो भगवति माहेश्वरि अन्नपूर्णे स्वाहा — यही अन्नपूर्णा मूलमंत्र है, जिसके विषय में शेष सारा स्तव है।

--- verse 4 | section: Mantroddhāra ---
deva:
ऋषिः ब्रह्मास्य मन्त्रस्य गायत्री छन्द ईरितम् ।
अन्नपूर्णेश्वरीदेवी देवता प्रोच्यते बुधैः ॥
iast:
ṛṣiḥ brahmāsya mantrasya gāyatrī chanda īritam |
annapūrṇeśvarīdevī devatā procyate budhaiḥ ||
en: For this mantra Brahmā is declared to be the ṛṣi (the seer to whom it was revealed) and gāyatrī the chandas (metre); the goddess Annapūrṇeśvarī is declared by the wise to be its devatā. This is the standard ṛṣyādi-nyāsa formula that must precede japa of any mantra.
tel: ఈ మంత్రానికి బ్రహ్మ ఋషి అని, గాయత్రి ఛందస్సు అని చెప్పబడింది; అన్నపూర్ణేశ్వరీదేవి దీని దేవత అని పండితులు చెబుతారు. ఏ మంత్రజపానికైనా ముందుగా చేయవలసిన ప్రామాణిక ఋష్యాదిన్యాస సూత్రం ఇది.
hi: इस मंत्र के ब्रह्मा ऋषि तथा गायत्री छंद कहे गए हैं; अन्नपूर्णेश्वरी देवी इसकी देवता हैं, ऐसा विद्वान् कहते हैं। यह वही प्रामाणिक ऋष्यादि-न्यास सूत्र है जो किसी भी मंत्र के जप से पूर्व किया जाता है।

--- verse 5 | section: Mantroddhāra ---
deva:
मायाबीजं बीजमाहुः लक्ष्मीशक्तिरितीरिता ।
कीलकं मदनं प्राहुर्मायया न्यासमाचरेत् ॥
iast:
māyābījaṁ bījamāhuḥ lakṣmīśaktiritīritā |
kīlakaṁ madanaṁ prāhurmāyayā nyāsamācaret ||
en: They call the māyābīja — hrīṁ — the bīja of this mantra; Lakṣmī, that is śrīṁ, is declared to be its śakti; and Madana (Kāma), that is klīṁ, they call its kīlaka, the pin that locks the mantra's power. The nyāsa is to be performed with māyā, that is with hrīṁ. The three bījas extracted in verse 2 are thus assigned their three ritual offices here.
tel: ఈ మంత్రానికి మాయాబీజాన్ని — హ్రీం — బీజమని అంటారు; లక్ష్మి, అనగా శ్రీం, శక్తి అని చెప్పబడింది; మదనుడు (కాముడు), అనగా క్లీం, కీలకమని అంటారు — మంత్రశక్తిని బంధించే మేకు అది. న్యాసాన్ని మాయతో, అనగా హ్రీంతో ఆచరించాలి. రెండవ శ్లోకంలో వెలికితీసిన మూడు బీజాలకు ఇక్కడ మూడు ఆచార పదవులు కేటాయించబడ్డాయి.
hi: इस मंत्र का माया-बीज — ह्रीं — बीज कहा जाता है; लक्ष्मी अर्थात् श्रीं शक्ति कही गई है; और मदन (काम) अर्थात् क्लीं कीलक कहा जाता है, जो मंत्र की शक्ति को कीलित रखने वाली कील है। न्यास माया से, अर्थात् ह्रीं से करना चाहिए। श्लोक २ में निकाले गए तीन बीजों को यहाँ उनके तीन अनुष्ठानिक पद सौंपे गए हैं।

--- verse 6 | section: Dhyānam ---
deva:
रक्तां विचित्रवासनां नवचन्द्रजूटाम्
अन्नप्रदाननिरतां स्तनभारनम्राम् ।
अन्नप्रदाननिरतां नवहेमवस्त्राम्
अम्बां भजे कनकमौक्तिकमाल्यशोभाम् ॥
iast:
raktāṁ vicitravāsanāṁ navacandrajūṭām
annapradānaniratāṁ stanabhāranamrām |
annapradānaniratāṁ navahemavastrām
ambāṁ bhaje kanakamauktikamālyaśobhām ||
en: The first of the two dhyāna verses. I worship the Mother — red of body, wearing garments of many colours, the new crescent moon in her piled hair, wholly intent on the giving of food, bending under the weight of her breasts, wholly intent on the giving of food, clothed in fresh gold, resplendent with garlands of gold and pearl. The pāda annapradānaniratāṁ ("intent on the giving of food") stands twice in this verse in every script of the source; see the recension note.
tel: రెండు ధ్యానశ్లోకాలలో మొదటిది. ఎర్రని దేహం కలదానిని, రకరకాల రంగుల వస్త్రాలు ధరించినదానిని, జడలో నవచంద్రవంకను ధరించినదానిని, అన్నదానంలో నిమగ్నమైనదానిని, స్తనభారంతో వంగినదానిని, అన్నదానంలో నిమగ్నమైనదానిని, నూతన బంగారు వస్త్రం ధరించినదానిని, బంగారు ముత్యాల హారాలతో ప్రకాశించే అంబను నేను భజిస్తాను. Recension note: This is the mantra-stava, not the well-known Annapūrṇā Stotram ("nityānandakarī varābhayakarī..."). The two are wholly distinct texts and share no verse: this one opens "annapūrṇāmanuṁ vakṣye," is spoken by Dakṣiṇāmūrti to Pārvatī rather than by Śaṅkara in his own voice, and is a mantroddhāra-and-prayoga manual rather than a hymn. No source consulted printed the two on a single page, so no boundary question arose. It is likewise genuinely distinct from the separately-transmitted "Annapūrṇā Stavaḥ" (see 04_annapurna_stavam.txt, composed from Stotras Samāhāra Part 2, in this same folder): those two share only the deity and general subject, not a single verse.

Base text is stotranidhi.com in its Telugu, Devanāgarī and IAST editions, which agree word for word — including in every one of the irregularities recorded below, which is itself evidence they descend from one setting rather than three independent ones. The text is absent from vignanam.org: nine plausible slugs were probed across its english/, devanagari/ and telugu/ paths and a site-scoped search surfaced only the ordinary Annapūrṇā Stotram, the Aṣṭottaraśatanāma Stotram and the Nāmāvalī. It is also absent from sanskritdocuments.org, whose doc_devii catalog carries eight Annapūrṇā items, none of them this one; its nearest by title, annapUrNAstavaH (from Stotras Samahara Part 2, ed. K. Raghavan Pillai), is a different work in āryā metre — though its verse 5 performs the same mantroddhāra by the same code-names ("vedādir bhuvanāhṛd bhagavati māheśvarīti cānnānte pūrṇe'gnivallabhā syād ukto'yaṁ manuḥ"), which independently confirms the mūlamantra reconstructed here from vv. 2–3 and confirms that vahnivallabhā/vahnidayitā means svāhā. Cross-checking is therefore REDUCED and is flagged as such.

The one true cross-check obtained is hariome.com's parallel edition, which has the same 18 verses, the same speaker rubric and the same Mahātripurasiddhānta colophon. It is, however, an ad-hoc roman transliteration with heavy corruption (e.g. "bhagavalityatha" for bhagavatītyatha, "japtvaikalaṣmaṇam jnnonnet" for japtvaikalakṣaṁ mantrajño hunet, "bhaktestu kayakah" for bhakteṣṭadāyakaḥ), so it establishes the identity and extent of the text but settles no readings. Notably, hariome distributes the pādas of vv. 11–12 differently, closing 11 after "natra samshayako" and folding "annarāśim..." and "sahasram prajaped..." into a three-hemistich v. 12 — that is, the two witnesses disagree precisely at the point where stotranidhi's own numbering breaks down.

Structure and metre: 18 numbered verses, closed by the unnumbered colophon. Verses 1–5 and 8–18 are anuṣṭubh; verses 6–7, the dhyāna pair, are vasantatilakā. The unnumbered prose speaker-rubric "śrī dakṣiṇāmūrtiruvāca" is carried at the head of verse 1's deva/iast rather than as a separate unnumbered block, and is glossed there. Numbering irregularity, reproduced not corrected: verse 12 is printed in all three stotranidhi scripts with an unclosed number — "|| 12", "॥ १२", "|| ౧౨" — lacking the closing double daṇḍa that every other verse carries. Verse numbers are omitted from the deva/iast fields per the corpus format, so the defect is recorded here rather than shown. Verse 18 has three hemistichs where the metre would lead one to expect two; this is in all witnesses and is not an error.

Textual corruptions in the source, reproduced as printed and glossed in the en/tel/hi fields where they occur: (a) v. 6 repeats the pāda annapradānaniratāṁ in both its second and third lines, in every script — one of the two almost certainly displaces a lost reading; (b) v. 7 indiśakalābharaṇaṁ is for induśakalābharaṇaṁ (adorned with the crescent moon); (c) v. 7 hyaṣṭāṁ is unconstruable and is evidently hṛṣṭāṁ (delighted); (d) v. 7 suvarṇadarpaṁ is for suvarṇadarvīṁ, the golden ladle that is Annapūrṇā's standard attribute — hariome reads "suvarnadrpam," equally corrupt but corrupt differently, which suggests the damage is old; (e) v. 12 svamalavyāpinīm cannot be construed and has been left untranslated with the difficulty stated in the gloss rather than smoothed over.

Minor script-level variants between stotranidhi's own editions, all orthographic and none affecting sense: the Telugu edition writes ళ where the Devanāgarī has ल in kalahāvaliḥ (v. 13, తెలుగు కలహావళిః) and kālikaḥ (v. 15, కాళికః); IAST has been normalised to the Devanāgarī in both places. Also v. 15 jagatrayam stands for jagattrayam in all witnesses and has been left as printed.

Deliberate editorial choices: the corpus convention of ṁ for anusvāra and plain e/o has been applied throughout, so stotranidhi's ē/ō (śravaṇamātrēṇa, prōktaṁ, dēvatā) appear here as e/o, and the source's mixed anusvāra / final-m spellings have been normalised to uniform ṁ. The vasantatilakā verses 6 and 7 are printed in the source with a soft hyphen at each line-wrap (navacandrajūṭā- -mannapradāna-); per the corpus rule these have been joined, so the pāda breaks fall after jūṭām and after navahemavastrām, which is where the metre puts them. One avagraha has been supplied that the source omits: v. 18 अन्नपूर्णाप्रयोगोयम् is given here as अन्नपूर्णाप्रयोगोऽयम् / annapūrṇāprayogo'yam. No other emendation has been made anywhere in the text.

The mūlamantra assembled from vv. 2–3 — oṁ hrīṁ śrīṁ klīṁ namo bhagavati māheśvari annapūrṇe svāhā — is given in the gloss to v. 3 rather than inserted into the deva/iast fields, since the poem deliberately withholds it and states it only by the code-names praṇava, māyā, śrī, kāma and vahnidayitā. Those code-names have been named as bījas and identified, not translated: māyā is hrīṁ, śrī is śrīṁ, kāma/madana is klīṁ, and vahnidayitā ("Agni's beloved") is svāhā. No syllable-count for the mantra is asserted here: the verses themselves state none, and the two write-ups merged into this file disagreed, one calling it seventeen-syllabled and the other ten-syllable.
