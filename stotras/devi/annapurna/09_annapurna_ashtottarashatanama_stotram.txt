Author: The colophon places it in the **Śivarahasya**. The sanskritdocuments witness locates it more exactly: Śivarahasya, Ugra-aṁśa (the seventh), adhyāya 29, in a dialogue of Śiva and Gaurī on the glory of Kāśī, and also in the Brahmottara-khaṇḍa. The viniyoga names **Brahmā** as the seer.

Blurb: These are the one hundred and eight names of the goddess of the kitchens of Kāśī, who feeds even Śiva. She is of the colour of vermilion. She holds the noose and the goad. She is full-breasted and young. The list names her as the mother of everyone: of Viṣṇu, of Brahmā, of Gaṇeśa and of Kumāra. It then settles into the theme of bliss. She is the giver of bliss, she is bliss in form, she is the mother of bliss, and she is an ocean of goodness and of bliss. At the end she is simply the wife of Sadāśiva, beautiful in every limb for ever.

deva:
अस्य श्रीअन्नपूर्णाष्टोत्तरशतनामस्तोत्रमहामन्त्रस्य ब्रह्मा ऋषिः, अनुष्टुप्छन्दः, श्रीअन्नपूर्णेत्यधिदेवता, स्वधा बीजम्, स्वाहा शक्तिः, ओं कीलकम्, मम सर्वाभीष्टप्रसादसिद्ध्यर्थे जपे विनियोगः ।
iast:
asya śrīannapūrṇāṣṭottaraśatanāmastotramahāmantrasya brahmā ṛṣiḥ, anuṣṭupchandaḥ, śrīannapūrṇetyadhidevatā, svadhā bījam, svāhā śaktiḥ, oṁ kīlakam, mama sarvābhīṣṭaprasādasiddhyarthe jape viniyogaḥ |
en: Of this great mantra, the hymn of the hundred and eight names of Śrī Annapūrṇā, the seer is Brahmā, the metre anuṣṭubh, the presiding deity Śrī Annapūrṇā; svadhā is the seed, svāhā the power, oṁ the pin; it is employed in japa for the attainment of her grace in all that I desire.
tel: ఈ శ్రీఅన్నపూర్ణాష్టోత్తరశతనామస్తోత్రమహామంత్రమునకు బ్రహ్మ ఋషి, అనుష్టుప్ ఛందస్సు, శ్రీఅన్నపూర్ణ అధిదేవత, స్వధా బీజము, స్వాహా శక్తి, ఓం కీలకము; నా సర్వాభీష్టప్రసాదసిద్ధికై జపమునందు వినియోగము.
hi: इस श्रीअन्नपूर्णा-अष्टोत्तरशतनामस्तोत्ररूप महामन्त्रके ऋषि ब्रह्मा, छन्द अनुष्टुप्, अधिदेवता श्रीअन्नपूर्णा, बीज स्वधा, शक्ति स्वाहा और कीलक ओं है; मेरी समस्त अभीष्ट कृपाकी सिद्धिके लिये जपमें इसका विनियोग है।
