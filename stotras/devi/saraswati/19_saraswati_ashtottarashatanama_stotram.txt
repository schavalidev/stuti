Title: Śrī Sarasvatī Aṣṭottaraśatanāma Stotram
Devanāgarī: श्रीसरस्वती-अष्टोत्तरशतनामस्तोत्रम्
Telugu: శ్రీ సరస్వతీ అష్టోత్తరశతనామ స్తోత్రం

Author: Traditional. The Gītā Press table of contents marks the text `संकलित` ("compiled"), and the colophon names no source. There is no speaker, frame or phalaśruti; the stotra is the name list alone, closed by a count line.

Source / recension: Primary text: **Gītā Press, Gorakhpur — Śatanāma Stotra Saṅgraha**, code 1850, pp. 159–163 (stotra pp. 159–161, nāmāvalī pp. 162–163; PDF pp. 160–164), transcribed from the page images of the printed edition. Entry 30 of the volume's forty. **Collated against two independent witnesses on sanskritdocuments.org:** (1) `sarasvati108St.itx` (proofread by Sunder Hattangadi), the **same verse text**, 15 verses in the same numbering; (2) `sarasvatii108-5.itx` (proofread by Varun Mandawara), the **same name list**, 108 names in the same order. Stotra and nāmāvalī are paired in one file as the publisher prints them, and were checked against each other name by name.

Recension note: **Gītā Press followed; the witnesses are recorded, not adopted.** This is the widely recited Sarasvatī aṣṭottara (`सरस्वती महाभद्रा महामाया वरप्रदा`), and the three texts agree almost exactly. **Stotra and nāmāvalī diverge once, in Gītā Press itself:** v. 6 of the stotra prints `वसुदा` ("giver of wealth"), while the nāmāvalī no. 42 prints `वसुधायै` ("to the earth"). Both witnesses read `vasudhā` in both places, so the stotra's `वसुदा` may be a misprint; it is kept as printed, and the index keeps the nāmāvalī's `वसुधायै`. Other differences (Gītā Press → stotra witness): v. 12 `कालरात्रिः` → `kālarātrī`; v. 13 `विद्याधरसुपूजिता` (the witness notes a variant `vidyādharī supūjitā`); v. 14 `रक्तमध्या` → `raktamadyā` (a witness slip). Gītā Press → nāmāvalī witness: no. 8 `पद्मवक्त्रकायै` → `padmavaktrāyai`; no. 53 `विन्ध्यवासायै` → `vindhyāvāsāyai`. **Duplicates** (all in the verses too): `महाभद्रायै` (nos. 2 and 44), `वैष्णव्यै` (nos. 33 and 56) and `वाग्देव्यै` (nos. 41 and 89). **The dhyāna** (`या कुन्देन्दुतुषारहारधवला …`) is the famous Sarasvatī dhyāna, prefixed by the volume and glossed in a Hindi footnote. **Prefix.** Gītā Press prints `ॐ` before each name; this corpus prints `श्री` for every nāmāvalī without exception (user, 2026-09-08). **Cross-reference:** the other Sarasvatī texts in `devi/saraswati/` are separate works; no Sarasvatī aṣṭottara was in the corpus before this file.

Blurb: The goddess of learning, white as jasmine and snow, holding a book and showing the gesture of knowledge — and then, surprisingly, a warrior: Mahākālī with noose and goad, slayer of Śumbha, Raktabīja, Muṇḍa and Dhūmralocana, Cāmuṇḍā and Vārāhī. The list keeps both sides, Brahmā's wife and Śiva's younger sister, the Vindhya-dweller and the swan-rider, and ends by calling her Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Śiva at once.

--- verse 1 | section: Dhyānam ---
deva:
या कुन्देन्दुतुषारहारधवला या शुभ्रवस्त्रावृता
या वीणावरदण्डमण्डितकरा या श्वेतपद्मासना ।
या ब्रह्माच्युतशङ्करप्रभृतिभिर्देवैः सदा वन्दिता
सा मां पातु सरस्वती भगवती निःशेषजाड्यापहा ॥
iast:
yā kundendutuṣārahāradhavalā yā śubhravastrāvṛtā
yā vīṇāvaradaṇḍamaṇḍitakarā yā śvetapadmāsanā |
yā brahmācyutaśaṅkaraprabhṛtibhirdevaiḥ sadā vanditā
sā māṁ pātu sarasvatī bhagavatī niḥśeṣajāḍyāpahā ||
en: She who is white as jasmine, the moon, snow and a pearl necklace, who is clad in white, whose hands are graced with the excellent vīṇā, who sits on a white lotus, who is ever revered by Brahmā, Acyuta, Śaṅkara and the other gods — may that Blessed Sarasvatī, remover of all dullness, protect me.
tel: కుందపుష్పము, చంద్రుడు, మంచు, ముత్యాలహారమువలె ధవళమైనది, శుభ్రవస్త్రము ధరించినది, శ్రేష్ఠమైన వీణాదండముచే అలంకృతమైన చేతులు గలది, శ్వేతపద్మాసనస్థ, బ్రహ్మ విష్ణు శంకరాది దేవతలచే ఎల్లప్పుడు వందింపబడునది, సమస్తజడత్వమును పోగొట్టునది అయిన భగవతి సరస్వతి నన్ను కాపాడుగాక.
hi: जो कुन्दके फूल, चन्द्रमा, बर्फ और हारके समान श्वेत हैं, जो शुभ्र कपड़े पहनती हैं, जिनके हाथ उत्तम वीणासे सुशोभित हैं, जो श्वेत कमलासनपर बैठती हैं, ब्रह्मा, विष्णु, महेश आदि देव जिनकी सदा स्तुति करते हैं और जो सब प्रकारकी जड़ता हर लेती हैं, वे भगवती सरस्वती मेरा पालन करें।

iast:
śvetānanā nīlabhujā caturvargaphalapradā |

en: white-faced, dark-armed, giver of the fruit of the four aims; sovereign of the four-faced Brahmā, red at the waist, the stainless;

--- verse 16 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
हंसासना नीलजङ्घा ब्रह्मविष्णुशिवात्मिका ।
एवं सरस्वतीदेव्या नाम्नामष्टोत्तरं शतम् ॥ १५ ॥
iast:
haṁsāsanā nīlajaṅghā brahmaviṣṇuśivātmikā |
evaṁ sarasvatīdevyā nāmnāmaṣṭottaraṁ śatam || 15 ||
en: seated on a swan, dark-shanked, whose self is Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Śiva. Such are the hundred and eight names of the goddess Sarasvatī.
tel: హంసాసన, నీలజంఘ, బ్రహ్మవిష్ణుశివాత్మిక. ఈ విధముగా సరస్వతీదేవి యొక్క అష్టోత్తరశతనామములు.
hi: हंसासना, नीलजङ्घा, ब्रह्मविष्णुशिवात्मिका। इस प्रकार सरस्वतीदेवीके एक सौ आठ नाम हैं।
