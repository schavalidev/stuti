Title: Śrī Sarasvatī Kavacam (Brahmavaivarta Purāṇa)
Devanāgarī: श्री सरस्वती कवचम् (ब्रह्मवैवर्तपुराणे)
Telugu: శ్రీ సరస్వతీ కవచం (బ్రహ్మవైవర్తపురాణే)

Author: Traditional; the text presents itself as Brahmā's teaching to Bhṛgu (Brahmavaivarta Purāṇa, Prakṛtikhaṇḍa, Nārada–Nārāyaṇa dialogue, chapter 4), originally spoken by Kṛṣṇa in Goloka

Source / recension: https://stotranidhi.com/en/sri-saraswati-kavacham-in-english/ (IAST), https://stotranidhi.com/hi/sri-saraswati-kavacham-in-sanskrit/ (Devanāgarī), https://stotranidhi.com/sri-saraswati-kavacham-in-telugu/ (Telugu script)

Verse count: 32

Sections:
Prastāvanā — Introduction: Bhṛgu's request and Brahmā's account of the kavaca's power and lineage (vv. 1–13)
Kavacam — The armor proper: bīja-mantras assigned to protect each part of the body and each direction (vv. 14–26)
Phalaśruti — The fruits of recitation and rules for its use (vv. 27–32)

Blurb: A kavaca ("armor") text from the Brahmavaivarta Purāṇa that frames Sarasvatī's protection not as abstract praise but as a body-mapping ritual — a distinct bīja-mantra and epithet assigned to the head, eyes, throat, chest, back, each of the eight directions, and above and below, so that the reciter is enclosed in her protection from every side. Its opening section catalogues Bṛhaspati, Śukra, Vālmīki, Vyāsa, Pāṇini and other sages and grammarians as past masters who owed their eloquence and learning to this same armor.
