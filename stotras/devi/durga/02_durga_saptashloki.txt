Title: Śrī Durgā Saptaślokī
Devanāgarī: श्री दुर्गा सप्तश्लोकी
Telugu: శ్రీ దుర్గా సప్తశ్లోకీ

Author: No human author is named. The viniyoga names Nārāyaṇa as the ṛṣi (seer) of this stotra-mantra, the anuṣṭubh (or, in one recension checked, "the metres beginning with anuṣṭubh") as its chandas, and Mahākālī, Mahālakṣmī and Mahāsarasvatī as its devatās. The text's own frame narrative presents all seven core verses as spoken by the Devī to Śiva, who asks her for a means by which the fruit of the entire Devī Māhātmyam (Durgā Saptaśatī) can be won in the Kali age without reciting all seven hundred of its verses; every source checked for this file gives the questioner as Śiva, not Himavat — see the recension note.

Source / recension: Base text is vignanam.org's śuddha-devanāgarī edition ("SrI Durga Sapta Shloki"), cross-checked word for word against its own parallel English/IAST and Telugu-script editions on the same site (all three agree). Independently cross-checked against sanskritdocuments.org's doc_devii catalog file durga7.itx ("durgāsaptaślokī," transliterated by Kapila Shankaran Love, proofread by Shriramana Sharma) and against greenmesg.org's annotated edition, which cites each of the seven verses to its exact chapter and verse in the Devī Māhātmyam (see per-verse notes below). All three sources agree on the substance of every verse; a few small wording differences were found and are resolved and flagged in the recension note rather than silently smoothed. This is a well-attested, widely circulated text, so cross-checking here is on firmer footing than for rarer items in this corpus.

Verse count: 7 numbered verses (1-7), the saptaślokī proper. These are framed by two unnumbered introductory verses (Śiva's question and the Devī's reply promising to speak), one unnumbered viniyoga passage in prose, and a one-line closing colophon — none of which carry verse numbers in any source consulted. No source consulted here prints an eighth, phalaśruti-type verse; the text closes immediately after verse 7 with "iti śrī durgā saptaślokī."

Sections:
కథాపీఠిక — Frame narrative (Śiva's question to the Devī, and the Devī's reply promising to reveal the stuti)
వినియోగం — Viniyoga (naming the ṛṣi, chandas and devatās, and stating the ritual purpose)
సప్తశ్లోకీ — the Saptaślokī proper: seven verses lifted verbatim from seven different passages of the Devī Māhātmyam
ముగింపు — Colophon (the one-line closing formula; no separate phalaśruti verse follows it here)

Blurb: A seven-verse digest, said to be the Devī's own answer to Śiva's question, that lifts one verse each from widely separated points in the Devī Māhātmyam/Durgā Saptaśatī (Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa) — Brahmā's praise of Yoganidrā in ch.1, the gods' hymn after Mahiṣāsura's death in ch.4, and five verses from the great Nārāyaṇī-stuti in ch.11 — so that reciting these seven is traditionally held to carry the fruit of reciting the entire seven-hundred-verse text.

--- verse none | section: Frame narrative ---
deva:
शिव उवाच ।
देवी त्वं भक्तसुलभे सर्वकार्यविधायिनि ।
कलौ हि कार्यसिद्ध्यर्थमुपायं ब्रूहि यत्नतः ॥
iast:
śiva uvāca |
devī tvaṁ bhaktasulabhe sarvakāryavidhāyini |
kalau hi kāryasiddhyarthamupāyaṁ brūhi yatnataḥ ||
en: Śiva speaks: O Devī, you who are easily accessible to your devotees, ordainer of every undertaking — in the Kali age, tell me earnestly the means to accomplish any task.
tel: శివుడు పలికెను: ఓ దేవీ, నీవు భక్తులకు సులభంగా లభించేదానవు, ప్రతి కార్యాన్ని నిర్వహించేదానవు; కలియుగంలో కార్యసిద్ధి కోసం ఉపాయాన్ని శ్రద్ధగా నాకు తెలుపుము.
hi: शिव बोले: हे देवी, आप भक्तों को सुलभ हैं, प्रत्येक कार्य की विधायिनी हैं; कलियुग में कार्यसिद्धि के लिए उपाय मुझे यत्नपूर्वक बताइए।

--- verse none | section: Frame narrative ---
deva:
देव्युवाच ।
शृणु देव प्रवक्ष्यामि कलौ सर्वेष्टसाधनम् ।
मया तवैव स्नेहेनाप्यम्बास्तुतिः प्रकाश्यते ॥
iast:
devyuvāca |
śṛṇu deva pravakṣyāmi kalau sarveṣṭasādhanam |
mayā tavaiva snehenāpyambāstutiḥ prakāśyate ||
en: The Devī replies: Listen, O God; I shall declare the means to attain every desired end in the Kali age. Out of my very affection for you, this praise of the Mother (ambā) is now being revealed.
tel: దేవి పలికెను: ఓ దేవా, వినుము; కలియుగంలో సర్వకోరికలను సాధించే ఉపాయాన్ని నేను తెలియజేస్తాను. నీపై గల నా ప్రేమతోనే ఈ అంబాస్తుతిని ఇప్పుడు ప్రకటిస్తున్నాను.
hi: देवी बोलीं: हे देव, सुनिए; कलियुग में समस्त इच्छाओं की सिद्धि का साधन मैं बताऊँगी। आपके प्रति अपने स्नेह के कारण ही यह अंबा-स्तुति अब प्रकट की जा रही है।

--- verse none | section: Viniyoga ---
deva:
अस्य श्री दुर्गा सप्तश्लोकी स्तोत्रमन्त्रस्य नारायण ऋषिः, अनुष्टुप् छन्दः, श्री महाकाली महालक्ष्मी महासरस्वत्यो देवताः, श्री दुर्गा प्रीत्यर्थं सप्तश्लोकी दुर्गापाठे विनियोगः ।
iast:
asya śrī durgā saptaślokī stotramantrasya nārāyaṇa ṛṣiḥ, anuṣṭup chandaḥ, śrī mahākālī mahālakṣmī mahāsarasvatyo devatāḥ, śrī durgā prītyarthaṁ saptaślokī durgāpāṭhe viniyogaḥ |
en: For this great mantra, the Śrī Durgā Saptaślokī Stotra, Nārāyaṇa is the ṛṣi (seer); the anuṣṭubh is the chandas (metre) — one cross-checked recension instead says "the metres beginning with anuṣṭubh," which is technically the more accurate statement, since verses 2 and 6 below are not actually in anuṣṭubh but in longer metres; Śrī Mahākālī, Mahālakṣmī and Mahāsarasvatī are the devatās (deities addressed). The viniyoga (ritual application) is for the recitation of the Saptaślokī Durgā, undertaken for the pleasure of Śrī Durgā.
tel: ఈ మహామంత్రమైన శ్రీ దుర్గా సప్తశ్లోకీ స్తోత్రానికి నారాయణ ఋషి; అనుష్టుప్పు ఛందస్సు — ఒక పాఠాంతరంలో దీనికి బదులు "అనుష్టుప్పు మొదలైన పలు ఛందస్సులు" అని ఉంది, ఇదే సాంకేతికంగా సరైనది, ఎందుకంటే కింద కనిపించే 2, 6 శ్లోకాలు అనుష్టుప్పులో కాక పొడవైన ఛందస్సులలో ఉన్నాయి; శ్రీ మహాకాళీ మహాలక్ష్మీ మహాసరస్వతులు దేవతలు. శ్రీ దుర్గా ప్రీతి కోసం సప్తశ్లోకీ దుర్గా పారాయణలో దీని వినియోగం.
hi: इस महामंत्र, श्री दुर्गा सप्तश्लोकी स्तोत्र के, नारायण ऋषि हैं; अनुष्टुप् छंद है — एक जाँची गई प्रति में इसके स्थान पर "अनुष्टुप् आदि अनेक छंद" कहा गया है, जो तकनीकी रूप से अधिक सही है, क्योंकि नीचे के श्लोक 2 और 6 वास्तव में अनुष्टुप् में नहीं, बल्कि दीर्घतर छंदों में हैं; श्री महाकाली, महालक्ष्मी, महासरस्वती देवताएँ हैं। श्री दुर्गा की प्रीति हेतु सप्तश्लोकी दुर्गा के पाठ में इसका विनियोग है।

--- verse 1 | section: Saptaślokī ---
deva:
ज्ञानिनामपि चेतांसि देवी भगवती हि सा ।
बलादाकृष्य मोहाय महामाया प्रयच्छति ॥
iast:
jñānināmapi cetāṁsi devī bhagavatī hi sā |
balādākṛṣya mohāya mahāmāyā prayacchati ||
en: Even the minds of the wise, that blessed Devī — Mahāmāyā, the great power of illusion — forcibly draws away and delivers over to delusion. (Devī Māhātmyam 1.55: Brahmā's praise of Yoganidrā, in the frame-story of the Madhu-Kaiṭabha episode.)
tel: జ్ఞానులైన వారి మనస్సులను సైతం ఆ భగవతీ దేవి, మహామాయ, బలవంతంగా ఆకర్షించి మోహానికి గురిచేస్తుంది. (దేవీమాహాత్మ్యం 1.55 — మధుకైటభ కథా సందర్భంలో బ్రహ్మదేవుని స్తుతి నుండి.)
hi: ज्ञानियों के मन को भी वह भगवती देवी, महामाया, बलपूर्वक आकर्षित कर मोह में डाल देती है। (देवीमाहात्म्यम् 1.55 — मधु-कैटभ प्रसंग में ब्रह्मा की स्तुति से।)

--- verse 2 | section: Saptaślokī ---
deva:
दुर्गे स्मृता हरसि भीतिमशेषजन्तोः
स्वस्थैः स्मृता मतिमतीव शुभां ददासि ।
दारिद्र्यदुःखभयहारिणि का त्वदन्या
सर्वोपकारकरणाय सदार्द्रचित्ता ॥
iast:
durge smṛtā harasi bhītimaśeṣajantoḥ
svasthaiḥ smṛtā matimatīva śubhāṁ dadāsi |
dāridryaduḥkhabhayahāriṇi kā tvadanyā
sarvopakārakaraṇāya sadārdracittā ||
en: O Durgā, remembered in danger you take away the fear of every creature; remembered by those at ease, you grant an exceedingly auspicious understanding. Remover of poverty, sorrow and fear — who but you, your heart ever moist with compassion, acts for the good of all? (Devī Māhātmyam 4.17: from the gods' hymn after the slaying of Mahiṣāsura.)
tel: ఓ దుర్గా, ఆపదలో స్మరించినంతనే సమస్త ప్రాణుల భయాన్ని పోగొడతావు; సుఖంగా ఉన్నవారు స్మరిస్తే అత్యంత శుభకరమైన బుద్ధిని ప్రసాదిస్తావు. దారిద్ర్యం, దుఃఖం, భయం పోగొట్టేదానా, సదా కరుణతో తడిసిన హృదయంతో అందరికీ మేలు చేసే నీవు తప్ప మరెవరు ఉన్నారు? (దేవీమాహాత్మ్యం 4.17 — మహిషాసుర వధానంతరం దేవతల స్తుతి నుండి.)
hi: हे दुर्गे, संकट में स्मरण करते ही समस्त प्राणियों का भय हर लेती हो; सुखी जनों द्वारा स्मरण किए जाने पर अत्यंत शुभ बुद्धि प्रदान करती हो। दरिद्रता, दुःख और भय हरने वाली, तुम्हारे अतिरिक्त और कौन सदा करुणा से आर्द्र हृदय से सबका उपकार करती है? (देवीमाहात्म्यम् 4.17 — महिषासुर-वध के पश्चात् देवताओं की स्तुति से।)

--- verse 3 | section: Saptaślokī ---
deva:
सर्वमङ्गलमाङ्गल्ये शिवे सर्वार्थसाधिके ।
शरण्ये त्र्यम्बके गौरि नारायणि नमोऽस्तु ते ॥
iast:
sarvamaṅgalamāṅgalye śive sarvārthasādhike |
śaraṇye tryambake gauri nārāyaṇi namo'stu te ||
en: Auspiciousness itself among all auspicious things, benign one, accomplisher of every aim, giver of refuge, three-eyed one, Gaurī — salutations to you, Nārāyaṇī. (Devī Māhātmyam 11.10, part of the gods' Nārāyaṇī-stuti after Śumbha and Niśumbha are slain.)
tel: సర్వమంగళాలలో మంగళస్వరూపిణివి, శుభకారిణివి, సర్వార్థసాధికవు; శరణు కోరదగినదానవు, త్రినేత్రవు, గౌరీ — నారాయణీ, నీకు నమస్కారము. (దేవీమాహాత్మ్యం 11.10 — శుంభనిశుంభ వధానంతర నారాయణీస్తుతి నుండి.)
hi: समस्त मंगलों में मंगलस्वरूपिणी, कल्याणकारिणी, सर्वार्थसाधिका; शरण देने योग्य, त्र्यंबका, गौरी — नारायणी, तुम्हें नमस्कार है। (देवीमाहात्म्यम् 11.10 — शुंभ-निशुंभ वध के पश्चात् नारायणी-स्तुति से।)

--- verse 4 | section: Saptaślokī ---
deva:
शरणागतदीनार्तपरित्राणपरायणे ।
सर्वस्यार्तिहरे देवि नारायणि नमोऽस्तु ते ॥
iast:
śaraṇāgatadīnārtaparitrāṇaparāyaṇe |
sarvasyārtihare devi nārāyaṇi namo'stu te ||
en: Intent upon protecting those who are humble and afflicted and have come to you for refuge, remover of everyone's suffering, O Devī — salutations to you, Nārāyaṇī. (Devī Māhātmyam 11.12.)
tel: శరణు కోరిన దీనులను, బాధితులను రక్షించడంలో నిమగ్నురాలివి, అందరి బాధలను తొలగించేదానవు, ఓ దేవీ — నారాయణీ, నీకు నమస్కారము. (దేవీమాహాత్మ్యం 11.12.)
hi: शरणागत, दीन, आर्तजनों की रक्षा में सदैव तत्पर, सबकी पीड़ा हरने वाली, हे देवी — नारायणी, तुम्हें नमस्कार है। (देवीमाहात्म्यम् 11.12.)

--- verse 5 | section: Saptaślokī ---
deva:
सर्वस्वरूपे सर्वेशे सर्वशक्तिसमन्विते ।
भयेभ्यस्त्राहि नो देवि दुर्गे देवि नमोऽस्तु ते ॥
iast:
sarvasvarūpe sarveśe sarvaśaktisamanvite |
bhayebhyastrāhi no devi durge devi namo'stu te ||
en: You who are the form of everything, the ruler of everything, endowed with every power — protect us from all fears, O Devī Durgā; salutations to you. (Devī Māhātmyam 11.24.)
tel: సర్వస్వరూపిణివి, సర్వేశ్వరివి, సర్వశక్తులతో కూడినదానవు; భయాల నుండి మమ్మల్ని రక్షించు, ఓ దుర్గాదేవీ — నీకు నమస్కారము. (దేవీమాహాత్మ్యం 11.24.)
hi: सर्वस्वरूपा, सर्वेश्वरी, समस्त शक्तियों से युक्त; हे देवि दुर्गे, हमें भयों से रक्षा करो — तुम्हें नमस्कार है। (देवीमाहात्म्यम् 11.24.)

--- verse 6 | section: Saptaślokī ---
deva:
रोगानशेषानपहंसि तुष्टा
रुष्टा तु कामान् सकलानभीष्टान् ।
त्वामाश्रितानां न विपन्नराणां
त्वामाश्रिता ह्याश्रयतां प्रयान्ति ॥
iast:
rogānaśeṣānapahaṁsi tuṣṭā
ruṣṭā tu kāmān sakalānabhīṣṭān |
tvāmāśritānāṁ na vipannarāṇāṁ
tvāmāśritā hyāśrayatāṁ prayānti ||
en: When pleased, you take away every disease; but when displeased, you cut off every desired wish. Those who have taken refuge in you never come to ruin — indeed, those who take refuge in you attain the very state of being a refuge (for others). (Devī Māhātmyam 11.29.)
tel: నీవు ప్రసన్నురాలవైతే సమస్త రోగాలను తొలగిస్తావు; కోపించితే కోరిన కోరికలన్నింటినీ తీసివేస్తావు. నిన్ను ఆశ్రయించినవారు ఎన్నడూ ఆపదలో పడరు — నిన్ను ఆశ్రయించినవారే ఇతరులకు ఆశ్రయమయ్యే స్థితిని పొందుతారు. (దేవీమాహాత్మ్యం 11.29.)

Recension note: Base text is vignanam.org's śuddha-devanāgarī "SrI Durga Sapta Shloki," which agrees word for word with its own parallel English (IAST) and Telugu-script editions. This was independently cross-checked against sanskritdocuments.org's doc_devii/durga7.itx ("durgāsaptaślokī") and against greenmesg.org's annotated edition, which supplies an exact Devī Māhātmyam chapter-and-verse citation for each of the seven verses (1.55, 4.17, 11.10, 11.12, 11.24, 11.29, 11.39 respectively) — these citations are reproduced in the en/tel/hi fields above and match what is independently known of the Devī Māhātmyam's structure (v.1 from Brahmā's praise of Yoganidrā in ch.1, v.2 from the gods' hymn after Mahiṣāsura's death in ch.4, vv.3-7 from the Nārāyaṇī-stuti after Śumbha-Niśumbha's death in ch.11).

Variants found and how they were resolved: (1) The viniyoga's chandas is given as "anuṣṭup chandaḥ" (singular) in vignanam.org, but as "anuṣṭubhādīni chandāṁsi" / "anuṣṭupādīni chandāṁsi" ("the metres beginning with anuṣṭubh," plural) in both sanskritdocuments.org and greenmesg.org; the plural is retained as the primary reading's gloss and the vignanam wording is also given, since the plural is the technically accurate statement (verses 2 and 6 are in a longer metre, not anuṣṭubh). (2) Verse 7's opening compound is "sarvabādhāpraśamanam" in vignanam.org but "sarvābādhāpraśamanam" in sanskritdocuments.org and greenmesg.org; vignanam's reading (without the extra ā) is kept as the base text since it is the grammatically simpler and more commonly printed form, and the variant is flagged here rather than silently adopted or dropped. (3) Verse 3's "gaurī" is printed as nominative-length ī in vignanam.org's devanāgarī, but as the grammatically expected vocative "gauri" (short i) in both sanskritdocuments.org and greenmesg.org; the vocative form is used here as the evident correct reading, with vignanam's ī treated as a printing slip. (4) In verse 2, vignanam.org's typeset editions print a visible space inside the compounds "dāridryaduḥkha bhayahāriṇi" and "sadārdra chittā"; sanskritdocuments.org prints both as unbroken compounds ("dAridryaduHkhabhayahAriNi," "sadA.a.ardrachittA," i.e. sadārdracittā by sandhi), which is grammatically what the compound requires, so the unbroken forms are used here and the vignanam spacing is treated as a typesetting artifact rather than a real reading. (5) Verse 6 is printed across four lines in vignanam.org with a hyphen at the end of "tuṣṭā-", continuing onto "ruṣṭā" on the next line; per this corpus's convention that a soft line-wrap is not a pāda break, the hyphen is dropped here and the word is left as the two grammatically distinct words tuṣṭā and ruṣṭā that it always was, laid out following vignanam's own four-line display.

On the assignment note about Himavat: every source consulted for this file — vignanam.org, sanskritdocuments.org, and greenmesg.org — opens the frame narrative with "śiva uvāca" (Śiva speaks) and "devyuvāca" (the Devī replies), i.e. the questioner is Śiva, not Himavat. No recension naming Himavat as the questioner was found after a genuine search; this is noted here rather than silently forcing the header to say "Himavat" or silently ignoring the discrepancy.

No eighth, phalaśruti-type verse was found in any of the three sources; all three end the text immediately after verse 7 with the single colophon line "iti śrī durgā saptaślokī" ("thus concludes..."), with no benefits-of-recitation verse attached.
