Title: Śrī Durgā Stotram (Yudhiṣṭhira-kṛtam)
Devanāgarī: श्री दुर्गा स्तोत्रम् (युधिष्ठिर कृतम्)

Author: traditional; embedded in the Mahābhārata as a prayer spoken by Yudhiṣṭhira, with the goddess's own reply (devyuvāca) forming vv. 28–36

Sections:

దేవీవచనం — the goddess's own reply and blessing (vv. 28–36)

Blurb: This hymn is set at the moment when the Pāṇḍavas approach the city of Virāṭa, to begin their year of living unrecognised, which is called ajñātavāsa. It tells a story as well as offering praise. In verses 1 to 27 Yudhiṣṭhira praises the Goddess and recalls her birth, which is linked to that of Śrī Kṛṣṇa. An infant girl was exchanged for the child Kṛṣṇa. Kaṁsa struck her against a stone, but she rose into the sky as the Goddess herself. In verse 18 the praise turns fierce, and speaks of a Kālī who dwells in the Vindhya hills and who accepts liquor, meat and sacrificial animals. Yudhiṣṭhira then asks for refuge during the exile. The Goddess replies in her own words in verses 28 to 36, marked devyuvāca. She grants two definite boons: victory in the coming war, and that no one in the kingdom of Virāṭa will recognise the Pāṇḍavas. These boons shape the rest of the Virāṭaparva. Verse 21 is often quoted: 'durgāt tārayase durge, tat tvaṁ durgā smṛtā janaiḥ', meaning 'O Durgā, you carry your devotees across every difficulty; for this reason people remember you as Durgā'. It is among the best known verses explaining her name.

--- verse 1 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
विराटनगरं रम्यं गच्छमानो युधिष्ठिरः ।
अस्तुवन्मनसा देवीं दुर्गां त्रिभुवनेश्वरीम् ॥
iast:
virāṭanagaraṁ ramyaṁ gacchamāno yudhiṣṭhiraḥ |
astuvanmanasā devīṁ durgāṁ tribhuvaneśvarīm ||
en: While journeying to the lovely city of Virāṭa, Yudhiṣṭhira began, in his mind, to praise the goddess Durgā, ruler of the three worlds.
tel: విరాటనగరమనే రమ్యమైన పట్టణానికి వెళ్తూ, యుధిష్ఠిరుడు తన మనసులో ముల్లోకాలకు అధీశ్వరి అయిన దుర్గాదేవిని స్తుతించడం మొదలుపెట్టాడు.
hi: सुरम्य विराटनगर की ओर जाते हुए, युधिष्ठिर ने मन ही मन त्रिभुवनेश्वरी देवी दुर्गा की स्तुति करना आरम्भ किया।

deva:

शिलातटविनिक्षिप्तामाकाशं प्रति गामिनीम् ॥

śilātaṭavinikṣiptāmākāśaṁ prati gāminīm ||
en: You who terrified Kaṁsa, who brought destruction upon the asuras — flung down onto a rocky ledge, you rose instead into the sky. This recalls the well-known story of the infant girl swapped for the newborn Kṛṣṇa: Kaṁsa dashed her against a stone, and she flew up into the sky as the goddess herself, revealing that his true slayer had already been born elsewhere.
tel: కంసుని భయకంపితుని చేసినదానా, రాక్షసులను నశింపజేసినదానా — శిలపై కొట్టబడినను, నీవు ఆకాశానికి ఎగిరిపోయితివి. కృష్ణునికి బదులుగా అప్పగించబడిన శిశువును కంసుడు రాతిపై కొట్టగా, ఆ శిశువు దేవిగా ఆకాశానికి ఎగిరిన ప్రసిద్ధ కథను ఇది గుర్తుచేస్తుంది.
hi: कंस को भयभीत करने वाली, असुरों का नाश करने वाली — शिला पर पटके जाने पर भी तुम आकाश की ओर उड़ गई। यह उस प्रसिद्ध कथा का स्मरण कराता है जिसमें कृष्ण के बदले सौंपी गई कन्या को कंस ने पत्थर पर पटका, और वह देवी के रूप में आकाश में उड़ गई।
