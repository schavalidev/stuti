Title: Śrī Kumārī Stotram
Devanāgarī: श्री कुमारी स्तोत्रम्
Telugu: శ్రీ కుమారీ స్తోత్రం

Author: traditional; no poet-signature or ṛṣi/chandas/devatā viniyoga is given in the stotra text itself. A Telugu-language editorial note carried alongside stotranidhi.com's edition (not part of the Sanskrit verses) describes the stotra as spoken by Śrī Kṛṣṇa to Dharmarāja (Yudhiṣṭhira); the specific Purāṇa or parvan this exchange is drawn from is not named there, so this attribution is reported as traditional framing rather than a verified textual pedigree.

Source / recension: Base text is stotranidhi.com's "Śrī Kumārī Stotram," whose Devanāgarī, Telugu, and English (IAST) editions agree word for word with one another. Independently cross-checked against bhaktinidhi.com's English edition, whose verse 1 and verse 9 (the portion retrievable in full) match stotranidhi's text verbatim, confirming this is the same widely-circulated nine-verse text rather than a stotranidhi-only reading. Verse count: 9 numbered verses (१-९ in the source), closing with a one-line prose colophon ("iti śrī kumārī stotram"). No viniyoga, nyāsa, dhyāna, or phalaśruti unit appears in the Sanskrit stotra text itself.

Sections:
కుమారీ స్తోత్రం — the stotra proper (nine verses of worship, each closing on one of the Goddess's names; the source carries no separate viniyoga, nyāsa, dhyāna, or phalaśruti unit)
సమాప్తి వాక్యం — Colophon (the one-line closing title-statement, unnumbered)

Blurb: This is a nine-verse mānasa pūjā, that is, a worship performed in the mind. It is addressed to the Goddess as Kaumārī. Each verse is made of words that begin with the same sound, repeated through the verse. The sounds used are ja, tri, kalā or kalyā, a, kāma or kāla, caṇḍa, sa or śā, dur, and su. Each verse ends in one of nine names under which she is worshipped: Kaumārī, Trimūrti, Kalyāṇī, Rohiṇī, Kālikā, Caṇḍikā, Śāmbhavī, Durgā and Subhadrā. In Telugu practice it is recited during the Kumārī Pūjā. This is the Navarātri rite in which a young unmarried girl is worshipped as the living form of the Devī. By tradition this stotra was taught by Śrī Kṛṣṇa to Dharmarāja. The introductory note on the source page at stotranidhi.com records this practice.

--- verse 1 | section: Kumārī Stotram ---
deva:
जगत्पूज्ये जगद्वन्द्ये सर्वशक्तिस्वरूपिणि ।
पूजां गृहाण कौमारि जगन्मातर्नमोऽस्तु ते ॥
iast:
jagatpūjye jagadvandye sarvaśaktisvarūpiṇi |
pūjāṁ gṛhāṇa kaumāri jaganmātarnamo'stu te ||
en: O you worshipped by the whole world (jagatpūjye), saluted by the whole world (jagadvandye), whose very form is every power (sarvaśaktisvarūpiṇi) — accept this worship, O Kaumārī; O Mother of the universe (jaganmātaḥ), salutation be to you. The verse's four "ja-" words set the alliterative pattern this stotra repeats in each verse, here culminating in the name Kaumārī.
tel: సర్వలోకముచే పూజింపబడు దానా, జగత్తుచే వందనము పొందు దానా, సమస్త శక్తులకు స్వరూపమైన ఓ కౌమారీ దేవీ! నా పూజను స్వీకరించుము. జగన్మాతా, నీకు నమస్కారము.
hi: हे संपूर्ण जगत् द्वारा पूजित, जगत् द्वारा वंदित, समस्त शक्तियों की साक्षात् स्वरूपिणी कौमारी देवी! मेरी इस पूजा को स्वीकार करो। हे जगन्माता, तुम्हें नमस्कार हो।

--- verse 2 | section: Kumārī Stotram ---
deva:
त्रिपुरां त्रिगुणाधारां त्रिवर्गज्ञानरूपिणीम् ।
त्रैलोक्यवन्दितां देवीं त्रिमूर्तिं पूजयाम्यहम् ॥
iast:
tripurāṁ triguṇādhārāṁ trivargajñānarūpiṇīm |
trailokyavanditāṁ devīṁ trimūrtiṁ pūjayāmyaham ||
en: You are Tripurā, support of the three guṇas (triguṇādhārā), the very form of the wisdom belonging to the three worldly aims dharma, artha and kāma (trivargajñānarūpiṇī), saluted by the three worlds (trailokyavanditā) — I worship you, O Devī, as Trimūrti, the one embodying the three forms (of Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Rudra). The "tri-" alliteration running through the verse culminates here in the name Trimūrti.
tel: మూడు పురములకు (త్రిపుర) అధిదేవతవు, సత్త్వ-రజో-తమో గుణములకు ఆధారమైనదానవు, ధర్మార్థకామములనెడి మూడు పురుషార్థముల జ్ఞానస్వరూపిణివి, ముల్లోకములచే వందనము పొందునదానవు అయిన నిన్ను, త్రిమూర్తుల స్వరూపముగా భావించి పూజించుచున్నాను.
hi: तुम त्रिपुरा हो, तीनों गुणों (सत्त्व-रज-तम) की आधारभूता हो, धर्म-अर्थ-काम इन तीनों पुरुषार्थों के ज्ञान की साक्षात् स्वरूपिणी हो, तीनों लोकों से वंदित हो — हे देवी, तुम्हें त्रिमूर्ति-स्वरूपिणी मानकर मैं पूजता हूँ।

--- verse 3 | section: Kumārī Stotram ---
deva:
कलात्मिकां कलातीतां कारुण्यहृदयां शिवाम् ।
कल्याणजननीं देवीं कल्याणीं पूजयाम्यहम् ॥
iast:
kalātmikāṁ kalātītāṁ kāruṇyahṛdayāṁ śivām |
kalyāṇajananīṁ devīṁ kalyāṇīṁ pūjayāmyaham ||
en: You who consist of the kalās (the sixteen digits/arts of manifestation) yet stand beyond them (kalātmikā, kalātītā), whose heart is compassion, auspicious (śivā), the mother from whom all auspiciousness is born (kalyāṇajananī) — I worship you, O Devī, as Kalyāṇī. The "kalā-/kalyā-" alliteration culminates in the name Kalyāṇī.
tel: కళలచే నిండినదానవు, కళలను మించినదానవు, కరుణతో నిండిన హృదయము కలదానవు, శుభస్వరూపిణివి (శివా), సకల శుభములకు జననివైన నిన్ను కల్యాణీ రూపముగా భావించి పూజించుచున్నాను.
hi: तुम कलाओं से युक्त हो और साथ ही कलाओं से परे भी हो, करुणा से भरे हृदय वाली, मंगलस्वरूपा (शिवा), समस्त कल्याण की जननी हो — हे देवी, तुम्हें कल्याणी रूप में मैं पूजता हूँ।

--- verse 4 | section: Kumārī Stotram ---
deva:
अणिमादिगुणाधरामकाराद्यक्षरात्मिकाम् ।
अनन्तशक्तिकां लक्ष्मीं रोहिणीं पूजयाम्यहम् ॥
iast:
aṇimādiguṇādharāmakārādyakṣarātmikām |
anantaśaktikāṁ lakṣmīṁ rohiṇīṁ pūjayāmyaham ||
en: You who hold the power of aṇimā and the other siddhis (aṇimādiguṇādharā — the eight yogic perfections, of which aṇimā, the power to become atom-sized, is named first), whose very self is the syllable "a" and the letters beginning with it (akārādyakṣarātmikā), possessed of infinite power, O Lakṣmī — I worship you as Rohiṇī. Here the alliterating syllable is "a-" itself, made explicit in the verse's own naming of the akāra (the letter "a").
tel: అణిమాది అష్టసిద్ధులను ఆధారముగా కలిగినదానవు, 'అ' కారము మొదలగు అక్షరములకు ఆత్మస్వరూపిణివి, అనంతమైన శక్తి కలదానవు అయిన ఓ లక్ష్మీదేవీ, నిన్ను రోహిణీ రూపముగా పూజించుచున్నాను.
hi: जो अणिमा आदि अष्ट सिद्धियों को धारण करने वाली हो, 'अ' कार आदि अक्षरों की आत्मस्वरूपिणी हो, अनंत शक्ति से युक्त हो, हे लक्ष्मी — तुम्हें रोहिणी रूप में मैं पूजता हूँ।

--- verse 5 | section: Kumārī Stotram ---
deva:
कामचारीं शुभां कान्तां कालचक्रस्वरूपिणीम् ।
कामदां करुणोदारां कालिकां पूजयाम्यहम् ॥
iast:
kāmacārīṁ śubhāṁ kāntāṁ kālacakrasvarūpiṇīm |
kāmadāṁ karuṇodārāṁ kālikāṁ pūjayāmyaham ||
en: You who move as you please (kāmacārī), auspicious, lovely (kāntā), whose very form is the wheel of time (kālacakrasvarūpiṇī), granter of desires (kāmadā), of generous compassion (karuṇodārā) — I worship you as Kālikā. The alliteration here plays on "kāma-" and "kāla-" together, culminating in the name Kālikā.
tel: స్వేచ్ఛగా సంచరించునదానవు (కామచారిణి), శుభస్వరూపిణివి, మనోహరమైనదానవు, కాలచక్ర స్వరూపిణివి, కోరిన కోర్కెలు తీర్చునదానవు, ఉదారమైన కరుణ కలిగినదానవైన నిన్ను కాళికాదేవిగా పూజించుచున్నాను.
hi: जो अपनी इच्छा से विचरण करने वाली हो (कामचारिणी), मंगलमयी, मनोहर (कान्ता), कालचक्र-स्वरूपिणी, इच्छाओं की दात्री (कामदा), उदार करुणा वाली हो — तुम्हें कालिका रूप में मैं पूजता हूँ।

--- verse 6 | section: Kumārī Stotram ---
deva:
चण्डवीरां चण्डमायां चण्डमुण्डप्रभञ्जिनीम् ।
पूजयामि सदा देवीं चण्डिकां चण्डविक्रमाम् ॥
iast:
caṇḍavīrāṁ caṇḍamāyāṁ caṇḍamuṇḍaprabhañjinīm |
pūjayāmi sadā devīṁ caṇḍikāṁ caṇḍavikramām ||
en: Fierce heroine (caṇḍavīrā), of terrible māyā (caṇḍamāyā), destroyer of the demons Caṇḍa and Muṇḍa (caṇḍamuṇḍaprabhañjinī) — I ever worship the goddess Caṇḍikā, of fierce valor (caṇḍavikramā). This is the one verse in the set whose alliterating syllable is also the name of the demon pair she slays, doubling as both sound-play and narrative reference to the Caṇḍa-Muṇḍa episode of the Devī-māhātmya.
tel: ఉగ్రవీరురాలవు (చండవీర), భయంకరమైన మాయ కలదానవు, చండ-ముండులనెడి రాక్షసులను సంహరించినదానవు అయిన చండికాదేవిని, భీకర పరాక్రమము గల ఆమెను, నేను నిత్యము పూజించుచున్నాను.
hi: उग्र वीरता वाली (चण्डवीरा), भयंकर माया वाली (चण्डमाया), चण्ड-मुण्ड नामक राक्षसों का संहार करने वाली — इस चण्डिका देवी को, जिसका पराक्रम अत्यंत उग्र है, मैं सदा पूजता हूँ।

--- verse 7 | section: Kumārī Stotram ---
deva:
सदानन्दकरीं शान्तां सर्वदेवनमस्कृताम् ।
सर्वभूतात्मिकां लक्ष्मीं शाम्भवीं पूजयाम्यहम् ॥
iast:
sadānandakarīṁ śāntāṁ sarvadevanamaskṛtām |
sarvabhūtātmikāṁ lakṣmīṁ śāmbhavīṁ pūjayāmyaham ||
en: Ever bestower of bliss (sadānandakarī), serene (śāntā), saluted by all the gods (sarvadevanamaskṛtā), the self dwelling within all beings (sarvabhūtātmikā), O Lakṣmī — I worship you as Śāmbhavī (consort of Śambhu/Śiva). The verse alliterates loosely on "sa-/sar-/śā-" sounds, closing on Śāmbhavī.
tel: ఎల్లప్పుడూ ఆనందమును కలిగించునదానవు, ప్రశాంత స్వరూపిణివి, సమస్త దేవతలచే నమస్కరింపబడునదానవు, సర్వప్రాణులలో అంతర్యామిగా ఉన్న ఓ లక్ష్మీదేవీ, నిన్ను శాంభవీ రూపముగా పూజించుచున్నాను.
hi: जो सदा आनंद देने वाली हो, शांतस्वरूपा हो, समस्त देवताओं द्वारा नमस्कृत हो, समस्त प्राणियों में अंतर्यामिनी हो, हे लक्ष्मी — तुम्हें शांभवी रूप में मैं पूजता हूँ।

--- verse 8 | section: Kumārī Stotram ---
deva:
दुर्गमे दुस्तरे कार्ये भवदुःखविनाशिनीम् ।
पूजयामि सदा भक्त्या दुर्गां दुर्गार्तिनाशिनीम् ॥
iast:
durgame dustare kārye bhavaduḥkhavināśinīm |
pūjayāmi sadā bhaktyā durgāṁ durgārtināśinīm ||
en: In difficult and insurmountable undertakings (durgame dustare kārye), destroyer of worldly sorrow (bhavaduḥkhavināśinī) — I ever worship with devotion Durgā, remover of the afflictions of the distressed (durgārtināśinī). The "dur-" alliteration here is also an etymological play on Durgā's own name (durga, a fort or difficulty She removes).
tel: అత్యంత కఠినమైన, దాటుటకు వీలుకాని కార్యములలో సంసార దుఃఖములను నశింపజేయు దానవైన దుర్గాదేవిని, ఆపదలో ఉన్నవారి బాధను తొలగించు దానవైన ఆమెను, నేను నిత్యము భక్తితో పూజించుచున్నాను.
hi: अत्यंत कठिन और दुस्तर कार्यों में सांसारिक दुःख का नाश करने वाली, संकटग्रस्तों की पीड़ा को दूर करने वाली दुर्गा देवी को मैं सदा भक्तिपूर्वक पूजता हूँ।

--- verse 9 | section: Kumārī Stotram ---
deva:
सुन्दरीं स्वर्णवर्णाभां सुखसौभाग्यदायिनीम् ।
सुभद्रजननीं देवीं सुभद्रां पूजयाम्यहम् ॥
iast:
sundarīṁ svarṇavarṇābhāṁ sukhasaubhāgyadāyinīm |
subhadrajananīṁ devīṁ subhadrāṁ pūjayāmyaham ||
en: Beautiful (sundarī), of golden radiance (svarṇavarṇābhā), giver of happiness and good fortune (sukhasaubhāgyadāyinī), mother from whom good fortune is born (subhadrajananī) — I worship you, O Devī, as Subhadrā. The closing "su-" alliteration culminates the set of nine names on Subhadrā, ending the stotra as it began, with a benedictory name.
tel: మిక్కిలి సుందరమైనదానవు, బంగారు వర్ణ కాంతితో ప్రకాశించునదానవు, సుఖసౌభాగ్యములను అనుగ్రహించునదానవు, శుభమునకు జననివైన ఓ దేవీ, నిన్ను సుభద్రా రూపముగా పూజించుచున్నాను.
hi: अति सुंदर, स्वर्ण के समान कांतिमयी, सुख-सौभाग्य की दात्री, समस्त शुभ की जननी हे देवी! तुम्हें सुभद्रा रूप में मैं पूजता हूँ।

Recension note: Base text is stotranidhi.com's Śrī Kumārī Stotram (Devanāgarī, Telugu, and English/IAST editions, which agree with one another word for word). Cross-checked against bhaktinidhi.com's English edition; its verse 1 and verse 9 (the portion independently retrieved) match stotranidhi's text verbatim, so this is confirmed as the same widely-circulated nine-verse text rather than a single-site reading, though a full line-by-line collation of all nine verses against bhaktinidhi.com was not completed. This text could not be located on vignanam.org after checking /english/, /devanagari/, and /telugu/ under several plausible slugs and via a general site search — it appears vignanam.org simply does not carry this particular stotra — so the corpus's usual vignanam base text was replaced by stotranidhi.com here, and this reduced cross-checking is flagged per the sourcing brief rather than silently upgraded. sanskritdocuments.org's doc_devii catalog does not list a plain "kumArIstotram"; it separately lists a differently-titled kumArIstavaH (Rudrayāmalottaratantra), kumArIkavacham, kumArItarpaNAtmakastotram, and kumArIsahasranAmastotram, none of which is this text, so no collation against sanskritdocuments.org was possible. More than one distinct text circulates under Kumārī-related titles: this nine-verse "Kumari Stotram" (opening jagatpūjye jagadvandye) is a separate, shorter composition from the Kumārī Kavacham (a protective armor-text) and the Kumārī Sahasranāma Stotram (a thousand-name text), both listed alongside it on stotram.co.in's Kumari category page and in sanskritdocuments.org's catalogue; that distinction is preserved here rather than conflated. The traditional attribution to a dialogue between Śrī Kṛṣṇa and Dharmarāja, and the framing of this stotra as recited during Kumārī Pūjā, both come from stotranidhi.com's Telugu-language editorial introduction to the text (present only on its Telugu-script page, not on its Devanāgarī or English pages), and are reported in the header as traditional/source framing rather than as the stotra's own colophon, since the Sanskrit verses themselves carry no viniyoga or attribution. No internal numbering irregularities were found within the source text (verses run continuously 1-9). The apparent typographic artifact ":'stu" in some online IAST renderings of verse 1 (for what should be the sandhi namo'stu) has been normalized here to namo'stu, matching the unambiguous Devanāgarī नमोऽस्तु. No bīja or seed-syllable mantras appear anywhere in this text; its distinguishing device is the verse-by-verse alliterative (anuprāsa) chain of names noted in each verse's en field, not mantric encoding.
