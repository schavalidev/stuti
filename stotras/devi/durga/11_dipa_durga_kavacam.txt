Title: Śrī Dīpa Durgā Kavacam
Devanāgarī: श्री दीप दुर्गा कवचम्
Telugu: శ్రీ దీప దుర్గా కవచం

Author: traditional; no human poet-signature. The text frames itself as words spoken by Bhairava to Devī, and its closing colophon assigns it to the Rudrayāmala Tantra: "iti śrī rudrayāmalatantre śrī bhairavadevī saṁvāde śrī dīpadurgā kavaca stotram" ("thus, in the Śrī Rudrayāmala Tantra, in the dialogue of Śrī Bhairava and Devī, the Śrī Dīpa Durgā Kavaca Stotra").

Source / recension: Base text is stotranidhi.com's Devanāgarī edition (stotranidhi.com/hi/sri-deepa-durga-kavacham-in-sanskrit/) together with its own IAST-diacritic edition (stotranidhi.com/en/sri-deepa-durga-kavacham-in-english/), which agree with each other word for word except for South-Indian-style ē/ō in the IAST page (silently normalized to plain e/o per this corpus's convention). Cross-checked against sanskritdocuments.org's independently prepared ITX edition (sanskritdocuments.org/doc_devii/dIpadurgAkavacham.itx, catalogued under doc_devii, last updated 2020-04-11), which matches almost verbatim; the handful of places the two disagree are minor (a verb-number slip, a single consonant) and are resolved and flagged below rather than silently picked. This text was searched for on vignanam.org under several plausible slugs (dipa-durga-kavacham, deepa-durga-kavacham, dipadurgakavacham, in its english/devanagari/telugu directories) and via web search; no vignanam.org page under this title was found, so vignanam.org is not part of the cross-check for this item — a genuine gap, not an oversight. hariome.com, hindunidhi.com and stotraveda.com all carry a "Deepa/Dipa Durga Kavacham" under this same title, confirming it circulates widely under this name, but were not used as source text.

DISAMBIGUATION from this folder's other Durgā kavacas: this text opens "śrī bhairava uvāca | śṛṇu devi jaganmātar jvālādurgāṁ bravīmyaham" ("Śrī Bhairava said: listen, O Devī, mother of the world — I shall speak of Jvālā Durgā") — a Bhairava-to-Devī dialogue from the Rudrayāmala Tantra, addressed to a goddess named Jvālā Durgā ("Flame Durgā") whom its own colophon titles Dīpa Durgā ("Lamp Durgā"), treating flame and lamp as the same form; it closes by naming her Jvālāmukhī, "the flame-faced one" (v.28). This is unrelated in opening, frame-narrative, and content to 05_devi_kavacam.txt (the Mārkaṇḍeya-Purāṇa/Saptaśatī Devī Kavacam, opening "asya śrīcaṇḍikādevyāḥ kavacasyaiśāna ṛṣiḥ..." and structured as a body-armor list without embedded bīja-strings), and to whatever recensions appear in this batch as 09 (a plain Durgā Kavacam) and 10 (Durgā Kavacam, Brahmāṇḍamohanam) — this text is identifiable independent of those by its title's explicit "Dīpa," by its Rudrayāmala/Bhairava-Devī frame (rather than a Mārkaṇḍeya or Śiva-Pārvatī frame), and by its distinctive method: verses 11-14 march the entire Sanskrit consonant alphabet (mātṛkā), varga by varga, string by string, down the body from shoulders to the secret parts — a structural signature none of the other three kavacas in this folder share.

Verse count: 34 numbered verses (śloka, with two anuṣṭubh-and-a-half lines forming v.34), opening with a six-verse Bhairava-Devī dialogue frame (vv.1-6), the body-and-directions armor proper (vv.7-28), and a phalaśruti (vv.29-34), followed by one unnumbered prose colophon line. The source prints no separate headed sections (no viniyoga/nyāsa/dhyānam blocks set off from the numbering) — the whole thing is one continuously numbered sequence — so this file keeps it as a single "Kavacam" section per the source's own layout, and gives the colophon its own unnumbered block.

Sections:
కవచం — Kavacam (the full 34-verse text: Bhairava's opening address to Devī on the secrecy and power of this kavaca called Trailokyavijaya "Victory over the Three Worlds" (vv.1-6); the armor proper, which strings bīja-syllables — including, in vv.11-14, a complete pass through the Sanskrit consonant alphabet taken varga by varga — to each body part from head to secret parts and beyond (vv.7-22); a set of guardian-goddess assignments across most of the compass directions, a couple of place-types, and "all times" (vv.23-27); and the phalaśruti describing the kavaca's power and how it is to be used, given, and inscribed (vv.28-34))
(unnumbered) — Colophon (the closing "iti" line naming the text's source-tantra and its title)

Blurb: This kavaca comes from the Rudrayāmala Tantra, where Bhairava speaks it to the Devī. It is addressed to the goddess Jvālā Durgā, whose name means Flame Durgā. The colophon of the text calls her Dīpa Durgā, that is, Lamp Durgā. The kavaca guards her limb by limb, placing a bīja syllable on each part of the body. In verses 11 to 14 it uses the whole Sanskrit consonant alphabet, called the mātṛkā, group by group, from the shoulders down to the private parts. It then extends this protection to most of the directions through a set of fierce guardian goddesses. These are Vārāhī, Nārasiṁhī, Kaumārī, Mātaṅgī, Bhadrakālikā, Ugratārā, Dakṣiṇakālikā and Tripurasundarī. At the close it names her Jvālāmukhī, which means the one whose face is flame. It then gives directions for writing the kavaca in vermilion upon birch-bark, using wine and semen, for the tantric powers known as stambhana, mohana, māraṇa and vaśīkaraṇa.

--- verse 1 | section: Kavacam ---
deva:
श्री भैरव उवाच ।
शृणु देवि जगन्मातर्ज्वालादुर्गां ब्रवीम्यहम् ।
कवचं मन्त्रगर्भं च त्रैलोक्यविजयाभिदम् ॥
iast:
śrī bhairava uvāca |
śṛṇu devi jaganmātarjvālādurgāṁ bravīmyaham |
kavacaṁ mantragarbhaṁ ca trailokyavijayābhidam ||
en: Śrī Bhairava said: Listen, O Devī, mother of the world — I shall speak of Jvālā Durgā ("Flame Durgā"); [I shall speak] the kavaca that carries a mantra hidden within it (mantragarbha, "mantra-wombed"), called by the name Trailokyavijaya, "Victory over the Three Worlds."
tel: శ్రీ భైరవుడు పలికెను: దేవీ, జగన్మాతా, వినుము — జ్వాలాదుర్గను గురించి పలుకుతున్నాను; మంత్రగర్భమైన (లోపల మంత్రాన్ని దాచుకున్న) ఈ కవచాన్ని, త్రైలోక్యవిజయం అనే పేరుగలదానిని, చెప్పుచున్నాను.
hi: श्री भैरव ने कहा: हे देवि, जगन्माता, सुनो — मैं ज्वालादुर्गा के विषय में कहता हूँ; मंत्रगर्भ (जिसके भीतर मंत्र छिपा है) इस कवच को, जिसका नाम त्रैलोक्यविजय है, कहता हूँ।

--- verse 2 | section: Kavacam ---
deva:
अप्रकाश्यं परं गुह्यं न कस्य कथितं मया ।
विनामुना न सिद्धिः स्यात्कवचेन महेश्वरि ॥
iast:
aprakāśyaṁ paraṁ guhyaṁ na kasya kathitaṁ mayā |
vināmunā na siddhiḥ syātkavacena maheśvari ||
en: It is not to be made public, the supreme secret; I have not told it to just anyone. Without this [kavaca] (vinā amunā, sandhi-joined to vināmunā) there can be no siddhi (spiritual attainment) through the kavaca, O Maheśvarī.
tel: బహిర్గతం చేయరానిది, పరమ రహస్యమైనది — దీనిని నేను ఎవరికి పడితే వారికి చెప్పలేదు. దీనిని (వినా అమునా, సంధి చేరి వినామునా అయినది) లేకుండా కవచం ద్వారా సిద్ధి కలుగదు, మహేశ్వరీ.
hi: प्रकट न करने योग्य, परम गुह्य है यह — इसे मैंने जिसे-तिसे नहीं बताया। इस [कवच] के बिना (विना अमुना, संधि से विनामुना) कवच द्वारा सिद्धि नहीं होती, हे महेश्वरि।

--- verse 3 | section: Kavacam ---
deva:
अवक्तव्यमदातव्यं दुष्टायासाधकाय च ।
निन्दकायान्यशिष्याय न वक्तव्यं कदाचन ॥
iast:
avaktavyamadātavyaṁ duṣṭāyāsādhakāya ca |
nindakāyānyaśiṣyāya na vaktavyaṁ kadācana ||
en: It is not to be spoken of, not to be given, to a wicked person or to a non-practitioner (asādhaka); it is never to be told to a slanderer or to a disciple belonging to another [teacher].
tel: దుష్టునికి, సాధకుడు కాని వానికి (అసాధకుడు) దీనిని చెప్పరాదు, ఇవ్వరాదు; నిందకునికిగాని, వేరొక గురువుకు చెందిన శిష్యునికిగాని దీనిని ఎన్నడూ చెప్పరాదు.
hi: दुष्ट को, साधक न होने वाले (असाधक) को यह न कहना चाहिए, न देना चाहिए; निंदक को अथवा अन्य गुरु के शिष्य को इसे कभी न कहना चाहिए।

--- verse 4 | section: Kavacam ---
deva:
श्री देव्युवाच ।
त्रैलोक्यनाथ वद मे बहुधा कथितं मया ।
स्वयं त्वया प्रसादोऽयं कृतः स्नेहेन मे प्रभो ॥
iast:
śrī devyuvāca |
trailokyanātha vada me bahudhā kathitaṁ mayā |
svayaṁ tvayā prasādo'yaṁ kṛtaḥ snehena me prabho ||
en: Śrī Devī said: O Lord of the Three Worlds, tell it to me — much has already passed between us in this telling; you yourself have shown me this favor out of affection, O Lord. (This couplet's compressed dialogue-frame phrasing leaves "bahudhā kathitaṁ mayā" somewhat elliptical in the source; rendered here at the level of its evident sense rather than word-for-word certainty.)
tel: శ్రీదేవి పలికెను: త్రైలోక్యనాథా, నాకు చెప్పుము — ఇంతవరకు చాలా విషయాలు మనమధ్య చెప్పుకొనబడినవి; నీవు స్వయంగా ప్రేమతో ఈ అనుగ్రహాన్ని నాకు చేసితివి, ప్రభూ. (ఈ శ్లోకంలోని సంభాషణ-శైలి కొంత సంక్షిప్తంగా ఉండటం వలన "बहुधा कथितं मया" అనే పదబంధం స్పష్టంగా అన్వయించదు; భావాన్ని బట్టి అనువదించబడింది.)
hi: श्री देवी ने कहा: हे त्रैलोक्यनाथ, मुझे बताओ — इस कथन में हमारे बीच अब तक बहुत कुछ कहा जा चुका है; तुमने स्वयं स्नेहवश मुझ पर यह अनुग्रह किया है, हे प्रभो। (इस श्लोक की संक्षिप्त संवाद-शैली के कारण "बहुधा कथितं मया" पद पूर्णतः स्पष्ट अन्वयवाला नहीं है; यहाँ भाव के अनुसार अनुवाद किया गया है।)

--- verse 5 | section: Kavacam ---
deva:
श्री भैरव उवाच ।
प्रभाते चैव मध्याह्ने सायङ्कालेऽर्धरात्रके ।
कवचं मन्त्रगर्भं च पठनीयं परात्परम् ॥
iast:
śrī bhairava uvāca |
prabhāte caiva madhyāhne sāyaṅkāle'rdharātrake |
kavacaṁ mantragarbhaṁ ca paṭhanīyaṁ parātparam ||
en: Śrī Bhairava said: At dawn, at midday, at evening, and at midnight, this kavaca that carries a mantra hidden within it, the supreme among supreme things, should be recited.
tel: శ్రీ భైరవుడు పలికెను: ఉదయమున, మధ్యాహ్నమున, సాయంకాలమున, అర్ధరాత్రియందు — మంత్రగర్భమైన, పరాత్పరమైన ఈ కవచాన్ని పఠించవలెను.
hi: श्री भैरव ने कहा: प्रातःकाल, मध्याह्न, सायंकाल तथा अर्धरात्रि में — मंत्रगर्भ, परात्पर इस कवच का पाठ करना चाहिए।

--- verse 6 | section: Kavacam ---
deva:
मधुना मत्स्यमांसादिमोदकेन समर्चयेत् ।
देवतां परया भक्त्या पठेत्कवचमुत्तमम् ॥
iast:
madhunā matsyamāṁsādimodakena samarcayet |
devatāṁ parayā bhaktyā paṭhetkavacamuttamam ||
en: One should worship the goddess with honey, fish, meat, and sweet cakes (modaka) and the like, and recite this best of kavacas with supreme devotion. (Offerings of meat and fish belong to this text's Tantric register, translated here plainly and without softening, per the corpus convention for ugra/left-current material.)
tel: తేనె, చేప, మాంసం, మోదకం మొదలైనవాటితో దేవతను అర్చించి, పరమభక్తితో ఈ ఉత్తమమైన కవచాన్ని పఠించవలెను. (మాంస-మత్స్య నైవేద్యాలు ఈ తాంత్రిక గ్రంథపు లక్షణం; వీటిని మార్చకుండా యథాతథంగా అనువదించడమైనది.)
hi: मधु, मत्स्य, मांस, मोदक आदि से देवता की अर्चना करके, परम भक्ति से इस उत्तम कवच का पाठ करना चाहिए। (मांस-मत्स्य नैवेद्य इस तांत्रिक ग्रंथ की विशेषता है; इसे बिना किसी परिवर्तन के यथावत् अनूदित किया गया है।)

--- verse 7 | section: Kavacam ---
deva:
ओं ह्रीं मे पातु मूर्धानं ज्वाला द्व्यक्षरमातृका ।
ओं ह्रीं श्रीं मेऽवतात्फालं त्र्यक्षरी विश्वमातृका ॥
iast:
oṁ hrīṁ me pātu mūrdhānaṁ jvālā dvyakṣaramātṛkā |
oṁ hrīṁ śrīṁ me'vatātphālaṁ tryakṣarī viśvamātṛkā ||
en: May [the two-syllabled bīja-string] oṁ hrīṁ — Jvālā, the two-syllabled Mātṛkā ("Mother-seed") — protect my head; may [the three-syllabled bīja-string] oṁ hrīṁ śrīṁ — Viśvamātṛkā, "Mother of the Universe," the three-syllabled one — protect my forehead.
tel: రెండక్షరాల బీజమైన ఓం హ్రీం — జ్వాల అనే ద్వ్యక్షర మాతృక — నా శిరస్సును రక్షించుగాక; మూడక్షరాల బీజమైన ఓం హ్రీం శ్రీం — త్ర్యక్షరి, విశ్వమాతృక — నా నుదుటిని రక్షించుగాక.
hi: दो अक्षरों वाले बीज ओं ह्रीं — ज्वाला नामक द्व्यक्षर मातृका — मेरे सिर की रक्षा करे; तीन अक्षरों वाले बीज ओं ह्रीं श्रीं — त्र्यक्षरी, विश्वमातृका — मेरे ललाट की रक्षा करे।

--- verse 8 | section: Kavacam ---
deva:
ओं ऐं क्लीं सौः ममाव्यात्सा देवी माया भ्रुवौ मम ।
ओं अं आं इं ईं सौः पायान्नेत्रा मे विश्वसुन्दरी ॥
iast:
oṁ aiṁ klīṁ sauḥ mamāvyātsā devī māyā bhruvau mama |
oṁ aṁ āṁ iṁ īṁ sauḥ pāyānnetrā me viśvasundarī ||
en: May [the bīja-string] oṁ aiṁ klīṁ sauḥ — that goddess Māyā — protect my eyebrows; may [the bīja-string] oṁ aṁ āṁ iṁ īṁ sauḥ — Viśvasundarī, "Beauty of the Universe" — protect my eyes.
tel: ఓం ఐం క్లీం సౌః అనే బీజం — మాయాదేవి — నా కనుబొమలను రక్షించుగాక; ఓం అం ఆం ఇం ఈం సౌః అనే బీజం — విశ్వసుందరి — నా కళ్ళను రక్షించుగాక.
hi: ओं ऐं क्लीं सौः बीज — वह देवी माया — मेरी भौंहों की रक्षा करे; ओं अं आं इं ईं सौः बीज — विश्वसुंदरी — मेरे नेत्रों की रक्षा करे।

--- verse 9 | section: Kavacam ---
deva:
ओं ह्रीं ह्रीं सौः पुत्र नासां उं ऊं कर्णौ च मोहिनी ।
ऋं ॠं लृं लॄं सौः मे बाला पायाद्गण्डौ च चक्षुषी ॥
iast:
oṁ hrīṁ hrīṁ sauḥ putra nāsāṁ uṁ ūṁ karṇau ca mohinī |
ṛṁ ṝṁ ḷṁ ḹṁ sauḥ me bālā pāyādgaṇḍau ca cakṣuṣī ||
en: May [the bīja-string] oṁ hrīṁ hrīṁ sauḥ protect the nose; may [the bīja-pair] uṁ ūṁ — Mohinī, "the Bewitching one" — [protect] the ears; may [the string of vocalic-vowel bījas] ṛṁ ṝṁ ḷṁ ḹṁ sauḥ — Bālā, "the Girl [goddess]" — protect my cheeks and eyes. The word "putra" ("son") in the first pāda does not fit the grammar of the surrounding pātu-formula and is attested identically in both stotranidhi.com and sanskritdocuments.org's independent transcriptions; it is reproduced here as printed rather than silently corrected, and is likely an inherited corruption (perhaps originally "pātu," "may it protect") rather than an intended word.

tel: గ్రహపీడ మొదలైన కాలాలలో దీనిని పఠించవలెను, అన్నీ శాంతిస్తాయి. ఇది, దేవీ, మంత్రగర్భమైన, దేవతలచే పూజింపబడిన కవచం.
hi: ग्रहपीड़ा आदि के समय इसका पाठ करना चाहिए, सब कुछ शांत हो जाता है। यह, हे देवि, मंत्रगर्भ, देवताओं द्वारा पूजित कवच है।

--- verse 34 | section: Kavacam ---
deva:

iast:
yasya kasya na dātavyaṁ vinā śiṣyāya pārvati |
māsenaikena bhavetsiddhirdevānāṁ yā ca durlābhā |

en: It is not to be given to just anyone, except to a [worthy] disciple, O Pārvatī; within a single month one attains the siddhi (accomplishment) that even the gods find hard to obtain; a mortal who recites it for three months attains the direct vision (darśana) of the Goddess.
tel: దీనిని ఎవరిపడితే వారికి ఇవ్వరాదు, యోగ్యుడైన శిష్యుడు తప్ప, పార్వతీ; ఒక్క మాసంలోనే దేవతలకు కూడా దుర్లభమైన సిద్ధి లభిస్తుంది; మూడు మాసాలు పఠించే మనుష్యుడు దేవీదర్శనాన్ని పొందుతాడు.
hi: इसे जिसे-तिसे नहीं देना चाहिए, योग्य शिष्य के अतिरिक्त, हे पार्वति; एक ही मास में देवताओं को भी दुर्लभ सिद्धि प्राप्त होती है; तीन मास पाठ करने वाला मनुष्य देवी का साक्षात् दर्शन प्राप्त करता है।
