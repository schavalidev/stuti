Title: Śrī Caṇḍikā Dhyānam
Devanāgarī: श्री चण्डिका ध्यानम्
Telugu: శ్రీ చండికా ధ్యానం

Author: Traditional; unattributed. Transmitted as one of the pūrvāṅga (preliminary) pieces recited before a Durgā Saptaśatī pārāyaṇa, after the Devī Kavaca–Argalā–Kīlaka triad and immediately before the reading of the text proper.

Verse count: 2 (an extended five-line anuṣṭubh dhyāna, followed by one śārdūlavikrīḍita verse)

Blurb: This is the verse of meditation that sets the form of the Goddess in the reciter's mind before the Saptaśatī is read. Its description follows the tantric tradition rather than the purāṇic one. She is of the colour of the scarlet bandhūka flower. She is seated on the pañcamuṇḍa āsana, a seat of five skulls. She wears a garland of skulls and has three eyes. Her four hands do not hold weapons. They hold a book and a rosary, and they show the gestures of granting boons and of giving freedom from fear. The verse names her as the one honoured in the Uttarāmnāya, which is the northern Kaula transmission. The second verse turns to the story. It recalls the whole Saptaśatī in short form, naming Madhu and Kaiṭabha, Mahiṣa, Dhūmralocana, Caṇḍa and Muṇḍa, Raktabīja, and Śumbha and Niśumbha. It then asks the Goddess to protect the reciter together with her nine crore forms. In this way the whole text about to be recited is called upon beforehand as a single protecting presence.

--- verse 1 ---
deva:
ओं बन्धूककुसुमाभासां पञ्चमुण्डाधिवासिनीम् ।
स्फुरच्चन्द्रकलारत्नमुकुटां मुण्डमालिनीम् ॥
त्रिनेत्रां रक्तवसनां पीनोन्नतघटस्तनीम् ।
पुस्तकं चाक्षमालां च वरं चाभयकं क्रमात् ॥
दधतीं संस्मरेन्नित्यमुत्तराम्नायमानिताम् ।
iast:
oṁ bandhūkakusumābhāsāṁ pañcamuṇḍādhivāsinīm |
sphuraccandrakalāratnamukuṭāṁ muṇḍamālinīm ||
trinetrāṁ raktavasanāṁ pīnonnataghaṭastanīm |
pustakaṁ cākṣamālāṁ ca varaṁ cābhayakaṁ kramāt ||
dadhatīṁ saṁsmarennityamuttarāmnāyamānitām |
en: Om. One should ever meditate on her who shines with the radiance of the scarlet bandhūka flower; who is seated upon the five skulls; whose jeweled crown flashes with the crescent moon; who wears a garland of skulls; who is three-eyed and clad in red, with full, high, pitcher-like breasts; who holds, in order, a book, a rosary, the boon-giving gesture and the gesture of fearlessness; and who is honored in the Uttarāmnāya, the northern transmission.
tel: ఓం. ఎర్రని బంధూక పుష్పపు కాంతితో ప్రకాశించునది, పంచముండాసనంపై కూర్చున్నది, మెరిసే చంద్రకళతో కూడిన రత్నకిరీటం ధరించినది, ముండమాలను ధరించినది, మూడు కన్నులు కలిగి ఎర్రని వస్త్రం ధరించినది, పుష్టమైన ఎత్తైన కుంభస్తనాలు కలిగినది, వరుసగా పుస్తకం, అక్షమాల, వరముద్ర, అభయముద్రలను ధరించినది, ఉత్తరామ్నాయంలో పూజింపబడునది అయిన ఆ దేవిని నిత్యం స్మరించవలెను.
hi: ॐ। जो लाल बन्धूक पुष्प की आभा से प्रकाशित हैं, जो पाँच मुण्डों के आसन पर विराजमान हैं, जिनका रत्नमुकुट चन्द्रकला से देदीप्यमान है, जो मुण्डमाला धारण करती हैं, जो त्रिनेत्रा हैं और लाल वस्त्र पहनती हैं, जिनके स्तन पुष्ट, उन्नत और घट के समान हैं, जो क्रमशः पुस्तक, अक्षमाला, वरमुद्रा और अभयमुद्रा धारण करती हैं, और जो उत्तराम्नाय में पूजित हैं — उनका नित्य स्मरण करना चाहिए।

--- verse 2 ---
deva:
या चण्डी मधुकैटभादिदलनी या माहिषोन्मूलिनी
या धूम्रेक्षणचण्डमुण्डमथनी या रक्तबीजाशनी ।
शक्तिः शुम्भनिशुम्भदैत्यदलनी या सिद्धिदात्री परा
सा देवी नवकोटिमूर्तिसहिता मां पातु विश्वेश्वरी ॥
iast:
yā caṇḍī madhukaiṭabhādidalanī yā māhiṣonmūlinī
yā dhūmrekṣaṇacaṇḍamuṇḍamathanī yā raktabījāśanī |
śaktiḥ śumbhaniśumbhadaityadalanī yā siddhidātrī parā
sā devī navakoṭimūrtisahitā māṁ pātu viśveśvarī ||
en: She who is Caṇḍī, crusher of Madhu, Kaiṭabha and the rest; she who tore up Mahiṣa by the root; she who ground down Dhūmralocana, Caṇḍa and Muṇḍa; she who devoured Raktabīja; the Śakti who split apart the demons Śumbha and Niśumbha; she who is the supreme giver of attainment — may that Goddess, the Ruler of the universe, together with her nine crore forms, protect me.
tel: మధు కైటభాదులను సంహరించిన చండి ఎవరో, మహిషాసురుని సమూలంగా పెకిలించినది ఎవరో, ధూమ్రలోచన చండముండులను మథించినది ఎవరో, రక్తబీజుని భక్షించినది ఎవరో, శుంభనిశుంభ దైత్యులను ఛేదించిన శక్తి ఎవరో, పరమమైన సిద్ధిదాత్రి ఎవరో — తొమ్మిది కోట్ల మూర్తులతో కూడిన ఆ విశ్వేశ్వరి అయిన దేవి నన్ను రక్షించుగాక.
hi: जो चण्डी मधु, कैटभ आदि का संहार करने वाली हैं, जो महिषासुर को जड़ से उखाड़ने वाली हैं, जो धूम्रलोचन, चण्ड और मुण्ड का मर्दन करने वाली हैं, जो रक्तबीज का भक्षण करने वाली हैं, जो शुम्भ-निशुम्भ दैत्यों का विदारण करने वाली शक्ति हैं, जो परम सिद्धिदात्री हैं — नौ करोड़ मूर्तियों सहित वह विश्वेश्वरी देवी मेरी रक्षा करें।

Recension note: Single-source text (stotranidhi.com), verified across that site's Devanāgarī and IAST editions with no divergence. The piece is short and circulates in several closely related shapes: some Saptaśatī pārāyaṇa manuals print only the second (śārdūlavikrīḍita) verse under the name Caṇḍikā Dhyānam, others print only the anuṣṭubh dhyāna, and Bengali-tradition editions attach the anuṣṭubh portion to the Caṇḍī-pāṭha's Uttarāmnāya section rather than to the dhyāna. The two-verse arrangement given here is the one printed by this source. Note also that the closing verse ("yā caṇḍī madhukaiṭabhādidalanī") is used independently in many Devī liturgies and appears in some printings with māhiṣonmūlinī replaced by māhiṣapramathinī; no such variant occurred in the sources consulted here.
