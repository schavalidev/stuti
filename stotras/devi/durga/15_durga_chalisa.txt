Sections:
చాలీసా — Cālīsā, the forty numbered caupāī-couplets proper (vv. 1–40): Devī's universal light and fierce iconography (1–4); her cosmic power and her forms as Annapūrṇā and the eternal Bālā (5–7); her presence within Śiva, Brahmā, Viṣṇu and as Sarasvatī (8–9); her Narasiṁha, Prahlāda-protecting and Lakṣmī forms (10–12); Kṣīrasāgara, the Śakti Pīṭha shrines of Hiṅgalāj, Nagarkoṭ and Jvālā, and a run of Daśamahāvidyā names (13–20); her three canonical demon-slayings — Śumbha-Niśumbha, Raktabīja, Mahiṣāsura (21–25); the fruits of devotion and meditation (26–29); the cautionary legend of Ācārya Śaṅkara's austerities toward Śiva failing until he turns to her Śakti (30–34); and the poet's personal petitions and the phalaśruti (35–40)
సమాపన పద్యం — Closing couplet, the unnumbered signature-verse naming the poet Devīdās

Blurb: This is a cālīsā in Hindi and Awadhi, written in caupāī couplets. It praises Durgā in the wide manner of the Śākta and purāṇic tradition. It takes the Narasiṁha avatāra of Viṣṇu and the goddess Lakṣmī as her own forms. It names six of the ten Mahāvidyās, two or three in a couplet: Mātaṅgī, Dhūmāvatī, Bhuvaneśvarī, Bagalā, Bhairavī who is also called Tārā, and Chinnamastā. It names the places of her Śakti Pīṭha shrines, such as Hiṅgalāj, Nagarkoṭ and Jvālāmukhī. It retells her three well-known victories, over Śumbha and Niśumbha, over Raktabīja, and over Mahiṣāsura. In the middle it tells a story of warning. Even the austerities of Ācārya Śaṅkara towards Śiva did not succeed, until he understood that yoga cannot be attained without her Śakti. The cālīsā closes with the prayer of the poet Devīdās, and with the fruit promised to those who recite it daily.

deva:
नमो नमो दुर्गे सुख करनी ।
नमो नमो अंबे दुःख हरनी ॥
iast:
namo namo durge sukha karanī |
namo namo aṁbe duḥkha haranī ||
en: Salutations again and again to Durgā, the giver of happiness; salutations again and again to Ambā, the remover of sorrow.
tel: దుర్గాదేవికి మాటిమాటికి నమస్కారం, ఆమె సుఖాన్ని ప్రసాదించేది; అంబకు మాటిమాటికి నమస్కారం, ఆమె దుఃఖాన్ని తొలగించేది.
hi: हे दुर्गा! तुम्हें बार-बार प्रणाम, तुम सुख देने वाली हो; हे अंबा! तुम्हें बार-बार प्रणाम, तुम दुःख हरने वाली हो।

Recension note: This is a Hindi/Awadhi devotional cālīsā, NOT Sanskrit, in caupāī-metre couplets, part of the same popular North Indian genre as the Hanumān Cālīsā and Śiva Cālīsā. Base recension: vignanam.org's Hindi edition (सरल देवनागरी, simplified anusvāra) and its शुद्ध देवनागरी edition, which differ from each other only in anusvāra-class spelling (अंबे/अम्बे, शंकर/शङ्कर, हिंगलाज/हिङ्गलाज, मातंगी/मातङ्गी, डंका/डङ्का, शुंभ निशुंभ/शुम्भ निशुम्भ, जगदंब/जगदम्ब), never in wording — cross-checked word for word against vignanam's own Telugu-script transliteration page (agrees exactly with the सरल edition) and against stotranidhi.com's independently-hosted Devanāgarī edition (agrees with vignanam's content in every line). This is genuine two-independent-site agreement, called Family A here, and is the text followed throughout above.

sanskritdocuments.org DOES carry this text, despite the shared spec's caution that it might not (being primarily a Sanskrit repository) — not in its usual doc_devii Devī catalog, but at doc_z_otherlang_hindi/durga40.html, a page which itself prints two texts: an English-annotated version at the top, and a second "मूल पाठ" (base-text) version below it, not identical to each other in every line. Both agree closely with hindi.webdunia.com's edition, and all three differ from Family A at the same handful of points, forming a second well-attested recension, called Family B here. Family A is used as the base text, matching this collection's usual sourcing method; Family B's variants are recorded, not adopted:
— v.1 line 2: Family A "नमो नमो अंबे दुःख हरनी" (repeating Ambe); Family B "नमो नमो दुर्गे दुःख हरनी" (repeating Durge).
— v.5: Family A "शक्ति लय कीना" ("gathered/absorbed the power"); Family B "शक्ति लै कीना" ("took the power") — near-synonymous.
— v.15: Family A "मातंगी धूमावति माता" (eight syllables); Family B "मातंगी अरु धूमावति माता" (adds अरु, "and").
— v.19: Family A "तोहे कर में अस्त्र त्रिशूला"; Family B "सोहै अस्त्र और त्रिशूला" — different wording, same sense.
— v.24: Family A "पडी भीढ संतन पर"; Family B "परी गाढ़ संतन पर" — भीढ़ ("press of trouble") vs गाढ़ ("a deep trouble"), near-synonyms.
— v.36 line 2: Family A "रिपु मूरख मोहि अति दर पावैं" ("the foolish enemy fills me with great fear"); Family B "मोह मदादिक सब बिनशावै" ("may you destroy all delusion, pride and the like in me") — the largest divergence in the text, a genuinely different half-line rather than a spelling variant, flagged rather than silently smoothed.
— v.11: every witness prints "हिरण्याक्ष" (Hiraṇyākṣa) except sanskritdocuments's own "मूल पाठ", which alone prints the garbled "हिरणाकुश". In every case the name given for the demon sent to heaven in the Narasiṁha-Prahlāda verse is properly Hiraṇyakaśipu (Prahlāda's father, slain by Narasiṁha), not Hiraṇyākṣa (a different demon, slain earlier by Varāha); this is a well-known name-confusion in the popular printed Cālīsā, reproduced here as printed and flagged in v.11's en/tel/hi fields rather than silently corrected.

Some print and web editions (e.g. greenmesg.org) prepend the widely-attached stock invocation "या देवी सर्वभूतेषु मातृरूपेण संस्थिता..." before the Cālīsā; this Devī-māhātmya verse is used before many different Durgā texts, is not part of the Cālīsā itself, and is absent from both vignanam.org and stotranidhi.com, so it is not included here. Several sites, including the sanskritdocuments.org page used above, likewise print the ārtī "जय अंबे गौरी" immediately after the Cālīsā; that is a separate, independently authored composition and is excluded here for the same reason.

Numbering is regular in the base recension: 40 caupāī-couplets numbered 1 to 40 with no gap or repeat, followed by one closing couplet, unnumbered in every witness, carrying the signature "देवीदास" — the sole basis for the traditional attribution of authorship. No other biographical fact about a poet named Devīdās is agreed upon across sources; some casual web pages misattribute the whole work to Tulsīdās (author of the metrically similar Hanumān Cālīsā), an attribution not supported by any source consulted here and not adopted.

Transliteration choices for this Hindi/Awadhi text, stated explicitly per the shared spec's instruction and following the pattern set by file 09 of this collection (Abhirāmi Andādi) for a non-Sanskrit text: (1) Anusvāra (ं) and candrabindu (ँ) are both romanized ṁ, without distinction — the corpus-wide convention that anusvāra is always ṁ is extended here to candrabindu, since Hindi's nasalized-vowel candrabindu and its anusvāra represent the same nasalization and IAST has no separate standard sign for candrabindu; a form like "तिहूँ" (candrabindu) and a form like "संसार" (anusvāra) are both rendered with ṁ. (2) The inherent "a" (schwa) of Devanāgarī is transliterated literally wherever the script carries it, exactly as vignanam.org's own IAST edition does (करनी is karanī, not the colloquially schwa-deleted karnī; सुखदाता is sukhadātā, not sukhdātā) — this is a deliberate choice not to apply modern spoken-Hindi schwa-deletion, which would require phonological judgment calls this file does not make; the transliteration follows the letters printed, not the modern spoken pronunciation. (3) Retroflexes ट ठ ड ढ ण are marked ṭ ṭh ḍ ḍh ṇ as usual; the source carries no nuqtā dots anywhere (ड़/ढ़/फ़/ज़/क़ do not occur), so no dotted-consonant convention was needed. (4) Following this collection's Sanskrit-text convention, plain e and o are used, never ē/ō — unlike the Tamil item (file 09), standard Hindi has no phonemic short/long e-o contrast requiring the distinction. (5) The hi field is NOT a repetition of the verse, which is already Hindi verse, but an independent plain Hindi PROSE paraphrase of each couplet's sense; stated once here rather than in every block.

Two further printing artifacts were silently corrected and are flagged here per this collection's practice of never smoothing without saying so: vignanam.org's सरल-devanāgarī and Telugu-transliteration pages consistently print a small number of verb-endings that should carry an anusvāra/candrabindu nasal (गावें, ध्यावें, माहीं, समाहीं, पावैं, जियूँ, पावूँ, सुनावूँ, तिहूँ) instead as an unnasalized vowel followed by a stray halant-म (गावेम्, ध्यावेम्, माहीम्, समाहीम्, पावैम्, जियू, पावू, सुनावू, तिहू) — a font/input-method artifact, confirmed because stotranidhi.com's independently-typeset edition prints the expected nasalized forms at every one of these points; stotranidhi's forms are used here. Likewise both vignanam.org and stotranidhi.com print "मॊहि" in v.36 using the vowel sign ॊ (properly a Kannada-influenced short-o sign, not standard Hindi orthography) rather than the expected "मोहि"; this is corrected silently to मोहि/mohi here as a shared mechanical rendering fault rather than a genuine variant — both independently-hosted sources make the identical error at the identical word, which points to a shared underlying digital source file rather than an attested alternate reading.
