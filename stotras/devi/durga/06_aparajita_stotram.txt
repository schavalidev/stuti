Title: Devī Aparājitā Stotram
Devanāgarī: देवी अपराजिता स्तोत्रम्
Telugu: దేవీ అపరాజితా స్తోత్రం

Author: No individual poet is named. vignanam.org files this text under both its "Devī" and "Ṛṣi Mārkaṇḍeya" categories, consistent with its content: it is the well-known "namo devyai mahādevyai" hymn embedded in the Devī Māhātmya (Durgā Saptaśatī) of the Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa, where the assembled gods praise Mahāmāyā/Viṣṇumāyā — here invoked, in her un-defeated aspect, as Aparājitā. The 28-verse unit itself carries no separate viniyoga, ṛṣi/chandas/devatā framing, or colophon of its own in either source consulted.

Source / recension: Base text is vignanam.org's śuddha-devanāgarī edition of "Devi Aparajita Stotram," cross-checked word for word against its own parallel Telugu-script and English/IAST editions on the same site (all three agree exactly). Independently cross-checked against stotranidhi.com's English/IAST edition, "Aparajitha Stotram" (which states it is printed from the Prabhāta Stotranidhi book, in Telugu, Kannada, Devanāgarī and English/IAST) — fetched via curl with a browser user-agent since stotranidhi.com blocks direct WebFetch; its 28 verses match the vignanam text exactly, verse for verse and word for word. This gives a full, independent two-source cross-check for the entire text, one of the more solidly attested items in this corpus. vignanam.org's own "meaning" page for this title is not yet populated ("Coming soon..."), so the en/tel/hi glosses below are original translations made for this file rather than reproductions of a published meaning.

A second, substantially different text also circulates under the same title: sanskritdocuments.org's doc_devii catalog carries aparAjitAstotra.itx (transliterated by Dinesh Agarwal, proofread by Dinesh Agarwal, PSA Easwaran and Rama Prakasha), headed internally "śrī trailokyavijayā aparājitā stotram." That is a much longer, heavily tantric text — 46 numbered verses interleaved with long unnumbered prose passages of hrīṁ-type bīja strings, apotropaic formulas against disease, enemies, bad omens and childbirth complications, narrated by Mārkaṇḍeya to a disciple addressed as "vatsa," and stated within its own text to have been composed by Viṣṇu. It shares only the title and the general theme (Aparājitā as an invincible protective goddess, and a shared opening invocation "namo 'parājitāyai") with the vignanam/stotranidhi text; the verse text itself is entirely different from verse 1 onward. That text was NOT used as the base here, since the vignanam/stotranidhi recension is the one that matches this corpus's primary Telugu-tradition sourcing (vignanam.org base + stotranidhi.com cross-check) and is the text actually filed on both sites under the plain title "Devi/Aparajita Stotram." The Trailokyavijayā recension is flagged here as the other text circulating under this title, per the sourcing instructions, and was not collated verse-by-verse against the base text since the two are not textually related beyond their shared subject and title.

Verse count: 28 verses, numbered 1-28 continuously and without gap or repeat in both sources consulted.

Sections: The source prints this as one continuous, undivided stotra with no internal headers, viniyoga, dhyāna or phalaśruti of its own — so it is kept here as a single section, matching the source's own undivided presentation. Internally the hymn moves in two unmarked movements: verses 1-5 salute the Goddess by a sequence of names and epithets (Śivā, Prakṛti, Bhadrā, Durgā, Śarvāṇī and others), and verses 6-28 are an anaphoric litany, "yā devī sarvabhūteṣu ... rūpeṇa saṁsthitā" ("the Goddess who abides in all beings in the form of —"), naming in turn the specific faculty or state she takes in every living being.
స్తోత్రం — Stotram (the continuous 28-verse hymn proper; no further division in the source)

Blurb: This hymn repeats one sentence again and again: 'to the Goddess who dwells in all beings in the form of X, salutation to her'. In place of X it names twenty-one faculties and conditions of ordinary life. These include consciousness, sleep, hunger, memory, modesty, delusion and motherhood. In this way the hymn teaches that the same undefeated Goddess, here called Viṣṇumāyā and Aparājitā, is present within everyday experience itself. She is not only a deity outside the worshipper, to whom prayers are addressed.

--- verse 1 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
नमो देव्यै महादेव्यै शिवायै सततं नमः ।
नमः प्रकृत्यै भद्रायै नियताः प्रणताः स्मताम् ॥
iast:
namo devyai mahādevyai śivāyai satataṁ namaḥ |
namaḥ prakṛtyai bhadrāyai niyatāḥ praṇatāḥ smatām ||
en: Salutation to the Goddess, to the great Goddess, to Śivā (the auspicious one) — constant salutation; salutation to Prakṛti (primordial nature) and to Bhadrā (the gracious one) — ever disciplined and bowed down, we salute Her.
tel: దేవికి, మహాదేవికి, శివ (శుభకరి)కి నమస్కారం — నిత్యనమస్కారం; ప్రకృతికి (మూలప్రకృతి), భద్రకి (శుభప్రదాత్రి)కి నమస్కారం — నియమంతో ఉండి, వంగి ఆమెకు నమస్కరిస్తున్నాము.
hi: देवी को, महादेवी को, शिवा (कल्याणकारिणी) को नमस्कार — सतत नमस्कार; प्रकृति (मूल प्रकृति) को, भद्रा (मंगलमयी) को नमस्कार — नियमपूर्वक झुककर हम उन्हें नमस्कार करते हैं।

--- verse 2 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
रौद्रायै नमो नित्यायै गौर्यै धात्र्यै नमो नमः ।
ज्योत्स्नायै चेन्दुरूपिण्यै सुखायै सततं नमः ॥
iast:
raudrāyai namo nityāyai gauryai dhātryai namo namaḥ |
jyotsnāyai cendurūpiṇyai sukhāyai satataṁ namaḥ ||
en: Salutation to Raudrā (the fierce one), to the eternal one, to Gaurī, to Dhātrī (the sustainer/creatrix) — salutation again and again; salutation to Jyotsnā (moonlight) and to her whose very form is the moon, to Sukhā (bliss) — constant salutation.
tel: రౌద్రకి (ఉగ్రస్వరూపిణి), నిత్యురాలికి, గౌరికి, ధాత్రికి (సృష్టికర్త్రి) నమస్కారం — మళ్ళీ మళ్ళీ నమస్కారం; జ్యోత్స్నకి (వెన్నెల), చంద్రరూపిణికి, సుఖకి నమస్కారం — నిత్యనమస్కారం.
hi: रौद्रा (उग्ररूपिणी) को, नित्या को, गौरी को, धात्री (सृष्टिकर्त्री) को नमस्कार — बार-बार नमस्कार; ज्योत्स्ना (चाँदनी) को, चंद्ररूपिणी को, सुखा को नमस्कार — सतत नमस्कार।

--- verse 3 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
कल्याण्यै प्रणता वृद्ध्यै सिद्ध्यै कुर्मो नमो नमः ।
नैरृत्यै भूभृतां लक्ष्म्यै शर्वाण्यै ते नमो नमः ॥
iast:
kalyāṇyai praṇatā vṛddhyai siddhyai kurmo namo namaḥ |
nairṛtyai bhūbhṛtāṁ lakṣmyai śarvāṇyai te namo namaḥ ||
en: Bowed down, we offer salutation again and again to Kalyāṇī (the auspicious one), to Vṛddhi (increase, prosperity) and to Siddhi (accomplishment); salutation to Nairṛtī, to the Lakṣmī of those who bear the earth (bhūbhṛtām — kings, or mountains), to Śarvāṇī (Śiva's consort) — salutation to you again and again.
tel: కల్యాణికి, వృద్ధికి, సిద్ధికి వంగి మళ్ళీ మళ్ళీ నమస్కరిస్తున్నాము; నైరృతికి, భూభృతుల (రాజుల/పర్వతాల) లక్ష్మికి, శర్వాణికి నీకు మళ్ళీ మళ్ళీ నమస్కారం.
hi: कल्याणी को, वृद्धि को, सिद्धि को झुककर बार-बार नमस्कार करते हैं; नैरृती को, भूभृतों (राजाओं/पर्वतों) की लक्ष्मी को, शर्वाणी को आपको बार-बार नमस्कार।

--- verse 4 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
दुर्गायै दुर्गपारायै सारायै सर्वकारिण्यै ।
ख्यात्यै तथैव कृष्णायै धूम्रायै सततं नमः ॥
iast:
durgāyai durgapārāyai sārāyai sarvakāriṇyai |
khyātyai tathaiva kṛṣṇāyai dhūmrāyai satataṁ namaḥ ||
en: To Durgā, to her who ferries devotees across hardship (durgapārā), to Sārā (the essence) and to her who accomplishes everything (sarvakāriṇī); likewise to Khyāti (fame/renown), to Kṛṣṇā (the dark one), to Dhūmrā (the smoke-hued one) — constant salutation.
tel: దుర్గకి, దుర్గం (కష్టం) దాటించే దుర్గపారకి, సారకి (సారభూతురాలికి), సర్వకారిణికి (అన్నీ చేసేదానికి) నమస్కారం; అలాగే ఖ్యాతికి, కృష్ణకి (శ్యామవర్ణి), ధూమ్రకి (పొగరంగు గలది) నిత్యనమస్కారం.
hi: दुर्गा को, कठिनाई से पार उतारने वाली दुर्गपारा को, सारा (सारभूता) को, सर्वकारिणी को नमस्कार; इसी तरह ख्याति को, कृष्णा (श्यामवर्णा) को, धूम्रा (धूम्रवर्णा) को सतत नमस्कार।

--- verse 5 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
अतिसौम्यातिरौद्रायै नतास्तस्यै नमो नमः ।
नमो जगत्प्रतिष्ठायै देव्यै कृत्यै नमो नमः ॥
iast:
atisaumyātiraudrāyai natāstasyai namo namaḥ |

en: Bowed down, we offer salutation again and again to her who is at once utterly gentle and utterly fierce; salutation to the Goddess who is the foundation of the world, to Kṛtyā (ritual power, the effective deed itself) — salutation again and again.
tel: మిక్కిలి సౌమ్యురాలు, మిక్కిలి ఉగ్రురాలు అయిన ఆమెకు వంగి మళ్ళీ మళ్ళీ నమస్కరిస్తున్నాము; జగత్తుకు ప్రతిష్ఠ (ఆధారం) అయిన దేవికి, కృత్యకి (కర్మశక్తికి) మళ్ళీ మళ్ళీ నమస్కారం.
hi: अत्यंत सौम्य और अत्यंत रौद्र, दोनों रूपों वाली उन्हें झुककर बार-बार नमस्कार करते हैं; जगत् की प्रतिष्ठा (आधार) रूप देवी को, कृत्या (कर्मशक्ति) को बार-बार नमस्कार।
