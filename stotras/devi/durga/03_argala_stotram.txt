Title: Śrī Argalā Stotram
Devanāgarī: श्री अर्गला स्तोत्रम्
Telugu: శ్రీ అర్గళా స్తోత్రం

Author: traditional; frame-narrated as spoken by the sage Mārkaṇḍeya ("mārkaṇḍeya uvāca") within the Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa, the text in which the Devī Māhātmyam itself is set; the viniyoga names Viṣṇu as the ṛṣi of the mantra, anuṣṭubh as its chandas, and Śrī Mahālakṣmī as its devatā. Colophon: "iti śrīmārkaṇḍeyapurāṇe argalāstotraṁ samāptam" ("thus, in the Śrī Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa, the Śrī Argalā Stotra is concluded").

Source / recension: Base text cross-checked across three independently sourced editions that agree with each other word for word: sanskritdocuments.org's doc_devii diplomatic transcription (argalaastotra.itx, transliterated by Smt. K. Shankaran, proofread by Sunder Hattangadi, fetched via the browser tools), stotranidhi.com's Devanāgarī edition (stotranidhi.com/hi/argala-stotram-in-sanskrit/, fetched via curl with a browser User-Agent since the site blocks direct WebFetch), and greenmesg.org's Devanāgarī-with-English-translation edition. vignanam.org's Devanāgarī, English (IAST) and Plain-English pages (devi-mahatmyam-argala-stotram / -argalaa-) were fetched as a fourth cross-check; they carry the identical 27 verses' worth of wording but group two of the padas differently at verses 2-3, and read one word differently at verse 3 — both are flagged below rather than silently harmonized. All four sources agree on the total of 27 numbered units and on every other verse.

Verse count: 27 numbered verses. Verses 1-2 and 27 do not carry the refrain; verses 3-26 (24 verses) each close with the refrain "rūpaṁ dehi jayaṁ dehi yaśo dehi dviṣo jahi." These are framed by a prose viniyoga, a two-line namaskāra-plus-speaker-attribution ("oṁ namaś caṇḍikāyai," "mārkaṇḍeya uvāca"), and two dhyāna verses (a primary dhyāna and an alternate "athavā" dhyāna) — none of which carry verse numbers in any source consulted — and closed by an unnumbered colophon line.

Sections:
వినియోగం — Viniyoga (prose ritual application: ṛṣi, chandas, devatā and purpose of the japa)
ధ్యానం — Dhyānam (two visualization verses: a tantric pañcamuṇḍāsana form, and an alternate verse identifying the goddess with every deed of the Devī Māhātmyam)
నమస్కారం — Namaskāra (homage to Caṇḍikā, and the "mārkaṇḍeya uvāca" speaker-tag that frames the whole stotra as the sage's own speech)
అర్గళా స్తోత్రం — the twenty-seven numbered verses proper
సమాప్తి — Samāpti (closing colophon)

Blurb: This stotra has twenty-seven verses. It is recited just before the Devī Māhātmyam, which is also called the Durgā Saptaśatī. It is one of three short texts recited before that work. The other two are the Devī Kavacam and the Kīlaka Stotram. Its place in the order of recitation is as important as its words. The stotra names the chief demons whom Devī defeated in the Saptaśatī: Madhu and Kaiṭabha, Mahiṣāsura, Dhūmralocana, Raktabīja, and Śumbha and Niśumbha. Each name is addressed to her directly. After each one comes the same prayer of four parts: 'rūpaṁ dehi jayaṁ dehi yaśo dehi dviṣo jahi', which means 'give me beauty, give me victory, give me fame, and destroy my enemies'. The word argalā means a bolt or a latch. By tradition, reciting this stotra draws back that bolt, so that the Saptaśatī may then be recited.

--- verse none | section: Viniyoga ---
deva:
ॐ अस्य श्री अर्गलास्तोत्रमहामन्त्रस्य विष्णुः ऋषिः । अनुष्टुप् छन्दः । श्री महालक्ष्मीर्देवता ।
श्री जगदम्बाप्रीतये सप्तशतीपाठाङ्गत्वेन जपे विनियोगः ॥
iast:
oṁ asya śrī argalāstotramahāmantrasya viṣṇuḥ ṛṣiḥ | anuṣṭup chandaḥ | śrī mahālakṣmīrdevatā |
śrī jagadambāprītaye saptaśatīpāṭhāṅgatvena jape viniyogaḥ ||
en: For this great mantra, the Śrī Argalā Stotra, Viṣṇu is the ṛṣi (seer), anuṣṭubh is the chandas (metre), Śrī Mahālakṣmī is the devatā (deity); its japa, undertaken as an aṅga (auxiliary limb) attached to the recitation of the Saptaśatī, is for the pleasure of Śrī Jagadambā — this is its viniyoga (ritual application).
tel: ఈ మహామంత్రమైన శ్రీ అర్గళా స్తోత్రానికి విష్ణువు ఋషి, అనుష్టుప్పు ఛందస్సు, శ్రీ మహాలక్ష్మి దేవత; సప్తశతీ పారాయణానికి అంగంగా చేసే ఈ జపం శ్రీ జగదంబ ప్రీతి కోసం — ఇదే దీని వినియోగం.
hi: इस महामंत्र, श्री अर्गला स्तोत्र के, विष्णु ऋषि हैं, अनुष्टुप् छंद है, श्री महालक्ष्मी देवता हैं; सप्तशती पाठ के अंग रूप में किए जाने वाले इस जप का प्रयोजन श्री जगदम्बा की प्रसन्नता है — यही इसका विनियोग है।

स्फुरच्चन्द्रकलारत्नमुकुटां मुण्डमालिनीम् ॥

अथवा
या चण्डी मधुकैटभादिदैत्यदलनी या माहिषोन्मूलिनी
या धूम्रेक्षणचण्डमुण्डमथनी या रक्तबीजाशनी ।

oṁ bandhūkakusumābhāsāṁ pañcamuṇḍādhivāsinīm |
sphuracchandrakalāratnamukuṭāṁ muṇḍamālinīm ||
trinetrāṁ raktavasanāṁ pīnonnataghaṭastanīm |
pustakaṁ cākṣamālāṁ ca varaṁ cābhayakaṁ kramāt ||
dadhatīṁ saṁsmarennityamuttarāmnāyamānitām ||

yā caṇḍī madhukaiṭabhādidaityadalanī yā māhiṣonmūlinī

en: We contemplate her thus: glowing red like the bandhūka flower, seated upon (or dwelling amid) five skulls — pañcamuṇḍādhivāsinī, a tantric āsana — her crown flashing with a jewelled digit of the moon, garlanded with skulls (muṇḍamālinī), three-eyed, red-clothed, her breasts full and high like water-jars, holding in due order a book, a rosary (akṣamālā), and the boon-granting and fear-not gestures; one should constantly remember her so, as she is honoured in the Uttarāmnāya (the tantric "northern" transmission). Alternatively (athavā): she who is Caṇḍī — destroyer of Madhu, Kaiṭabha and the other demons, uprooter of Mahiṣa, slayer of the dull-eyed Caṇḍa and Muṇḍa, devourer of the duplicates born from Raktabīja's blood, the śakti who destroyed the demons Śumbha and Niśumbha, the supreme granter of siddhi (accomplishment) — may that Devī, present in nine crore (ninety million) forms, Viśveśvarī, mistress of the universe, protect me.

hi: हम उनका ऐसे ध्यान करते हैं: बंधूक पुष्प के समान लाल आभा वाली, पंचमुण्डाधिवासिनी (पाँच कपालों के आसन पर विराजमान — एक तांत्रिक आसन) रत्नजड़ित चंद्रकला से चमकते मुकुट वाली, मुण्डमालिनी (कपालों की माला धारण करने वाली), त्रिनेत्रा, रक्त वस्त्र धारण करने वाली, घट के समान पुष्ट-उन्नत स्तनों वाली, क्रमशः पुस्तक, अक्षमाला, वरदमुद्रा और अभयमुद्रा धारण करने वाली — उत्तराम्नाय (तांत्रिक उत्तर-परंपरा) में सम्मानित उस देवी का नित्य स्मरण करना चाहिए। अथवा: जो चण्डी मधु-कैटभ आदि दैत्यों की संहारक हैं, जिन्होंने महिषासुर को समूल उखाड़ फेंका, जिन्होंने मंदबुद्धि चण्ड-मुण्ड का वध किया, जो रक्तबीज के रक्त से उत्पन्न प्रतिरूपों को निगल गईं, जो शुम्भ-निशुम्भ दैत्यों की संहारक शक्ति हैं, जो परम सिद्धिदात्री हैं — वह नवकोटि रूपों वाली विश्वेश्वरी देवी मेरी रक्षा करें।

deva:
ॐ नमश्चण्डिकायै ।
मार्कण्डेय उवाच ।
iast:
oṁ namaścaṇḍikāyai |
mārkaṇḍeya uvāca |
en: "Oṁ, homage to Caṇḍikā." The sage Mārkaṇḍeya speaks — the frame in which the whole Devī Māhātmyam is set, Mārkaṇḍeya being its narrator within the Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa.
tel: "ఓం చండికాదేవికి నమస్కారము." మార్కండేయ మహర్షి పలుకుతున్నాడు — యావత్తు దేవీ మాహాత్మ్యం మార్కండేయ పురాణంలో ఈ మార్కండేయుని కథనంగానే నడుస్తుంది.
hi: "ॐ चण्डिका देवी को नमस्कार।" मार्कण्डेय महर्षि कह रहे हैं — संपूर्ण देवी माहात्म्य मार्कण्डेय पुराण में इसी मार्कण्डेय के कथन के रूप में चलता है।

--- verse 1 | section: Argalā Stotram ---
deva:
ॐ जय त्वं देवि चामुण्डे जय भूतापहारिणि ।
जय सर्वगते देवि कालरात्रि नमोऽस्तु ते ॥
iast:
oṁ jaya tvaṁ devi cāmuṇḍe jaya bhūtāpahāriṇi |
jaya sarvagate devi kālarātri namo'stu te ||
en: Victorious are You, O Devī Cāmuṇḍā — victory to You, remover of the afflictions of created beings; victory to You, O Devī present everywhere, O Kālarātri ("night of time/death," one of Devī's fierce forms) — homage to You.
tel: విజయం నీదే, దేవి చాముండా — భూతముల బాధలను తొలగించే నీకు జయము; అంతటా వ్యాపించిన దేవీ, కాలరాత్రీ (కాలస్వరూప భయంకర దేవీ రూపం), నీకు నమస్కారము.
hi: जय है तुम्हारी, हे देवी चामुण्डा — प्राणियों की पीड़ा हरने वाली तुम्हें जय हो; सर्वव्यापी देवी, हे कालरात्रि (काल-स्वरूपा भयंकर देवी-रूप), तुम्हें नमस्कार है।

deva:
मधुकैटभविध्वंसि विधातृवरदे नमः ।

en: Homage to You, destroyer of Madhu and Kaiṭabha, granter of a boon to Vidhātṛ (Brahmā, whom the two demons had threatened) — O Devī, give me beauty, give me victory, give me fame, destroy my enemies.

hi: मधु-कैटभ का विनाश करने वाली, विधाता (ब्रह्मा) को वरदान देने वाली तुम्हें नमस्कार है — हे देवी, मुझे रूप दो, विजय दो, यश दो, शत्रुओं का नाश करो।

Recension note: The base Sanskrit text above follows sanskritdocuments.org's doc_devii diplomatic edition (argalaastotra.itx), cross-checked word for word against stotranidhi.com's Devanāgarī edition (fetched by curl with a browser User-Agent, since the site returns 403 to direct WebFetch) and against greenmesg.org's Devanāgarī-with-translation edition; all three agree exactly, including the pada-grouping of verses 2 and 3. vignanam.org's Devanāgarī, English (IAST) and Plain-English pages were fetched as a fourth source and confirm the same 27 verses' worth of wording, but with two flagged differences: (1) vignanam groups the padas at verses 2-3 differently — it prints "madhukaiṭabhavidrāvi vidhātṛvarade namaḥ" together with "jayantī maṅgalā kālī bhadrakālī kapālinī" as its verse 2 (no refrain), and "durgā śivā kṣamā dhātrī svāhā svadhā namo'stu te" together with the first refrain as its verse 3, whereas sanskritdocuments/stotranidhi/greenmesg all pair "jayantī maṅgalā...svāhā svadhā namo'stu te" as verse 2 and "madhukaiṭabhavidhvaṁsi vidhātṛvarade namaḥ" plus the refrain as verse 3; the latter grouping, being the majority reading and the one matching the widely-printed Durgā Saptaśatī editions, is used here. (2) At the same line, vignanam reads "madhukaiṭabhavidrāvi" (routed/scattered) where the other three sources read "madhukaiṭabhavidhvaṁsi" (destroyed) — a genuine lexical variant, not a transcription slip on either side; "vidhvaṁsi" is used here as the majority reading. vignanam's viniyoga paragraph is also longer than the one used here, adding a bīja/śakti clause ("mantroditā devyo bījaṁ, navārṇo mantra śaktiḥ...") not present in sanskritdocuments' or stotranidhi's viniyoga; that clause also contains what looks like OCR corruption in vignanam's own text (e.g. "jagadandā" where "jagadambā" is expected), so it is treated as unreliable and not adopted, though its existence is flagged here rather than silently dropped. vignanam's verse 13 additionally prints "dhehi" in place of "dehi" twice within that verse's refrain (रूपं धेहि जयं देहि यशो धेहि द्विषो जहि); this reads as a typo against all three of the other sources and against the verse's own metre, so "dehi" is used throughout verse 13 here, consistent with the refrain's wording everywhere else in the text.
