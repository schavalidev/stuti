Verse count: 19 numbered verses — a five-verse dhyāna (vv. 1–5) plus a one-verse narrative bridge (v. 6); Śiva's seven-verse hymn (vv. 7–13); and six further verses of phalaśruti and worship-history (vv. 14–19) — followed by a one-line prose colophon

Sections:

శంకర స్తోత్రం — Śiva's hymn, built on repeated wordplay on maṅgala, "auspicious" (vv. 7–13)

Blurb: This hymn addresses the Goddess as Maṅgalacaṇḍikā, which means the auspicious Caṇḍikā. It comes from the Prakṛti-khaṇḍa of the Brahmavaivarta Purāṇa. The hymn is presented as the very words that Lord Śiva used when he prayed to her in a time of distress. Verse 6 describes him as composed even while seized by calamity. Verses 7 to 12 are built almost wholly from compounds of the single word maṅgala. That word carries three meanings at once: auspiciousness, the planet Mars, and Tuesday, which is called maṅgalavāra. The same kind of wordplay is used in the Śrī Kumārī Stotram in this corpus, at `12_kumari_stotram.txt`, where each verse repeats one sound and ends in a name. Verses 15 and 16 give a short history of her worship. She was worshipped first by Śiva, then by the planet Maṅgala in person, then by a king named Maṅgala, and then by women and by men on Tuesdays. This also explains why Tuesday remains her special day of worship.

pratimaṅgalavāre ca pūjāṁ kṛtvā gataḥ śivaḥ ||
en: With this very hymn Śambhu praised Maṅgalacaṇḍikā; and having performed her worship on every Tuesday, Śiva departed content.
tel: ఈ స్తోత్రముతో శంభుడు మంగళచండికను స్తుతించి, ప్రతి మంగళవారము పూజ చేసి, [సంతృప్తుడై] వెళ్ళిపోయెను.
hi: इस स्तोत्र से शंभु ने मंगलचंडिका की स्तुति करके, प्रत्येक मंगलवार पूजा करके, [संतुष्ट होकर] प्रस्थान किया।

deva:
देव्याश्च मङ्गलस्तोत्रं यः शृणोति समाहितः ।

--- verse 15 | section: Phalaśruti ---
deva:
प्रथमे पूजिता देवी शम्भुना सर्वमङ्गला ।
द्वितीये पूजिता देवी मङ्गलेन ग्रहेण च ॥
iast:
prathame pūjitā devī śambhunā sarvamaṅgalā |
dvitīye pūjitā devī maṅgalena graheṇa ca ||
en: First, the all-auspicious goddess was worshipped by Śambhu; second, the goddess was worshipped by the planet Maṅgala (Mars) as well.
tel: మొదట శంభునిచే సర్వమంగళయైన దేవి పూజింపబడెను; రెండవసారి మంగళగ్రహము (అంగారకుడు) చేత పూజింపబడెను.
hi: पहले शंभु द्वारा सर्वमंगला देवी पूजित हुईं; दूसरी बार मंगल ग्रह (अंगारक) द्वारा पूजित हुईं।

--- verse 16 | section: Phalaśruti ---
deva:
तृतीये पूजिता भद्रा मङ्गलेन नृपेण च ।
चतुर्थे मङ्गले वारे सुन्दरीभिश्च पूजिता ।
पञ्चमे मङ्गलाकाङ्क्षैर्नरैर्मङ्गलचण्डिका ॥
iast:
tṛtīye pūjitā bhadrā maṅgalena nṛpeṇa ca |
caturthe maṅgale vāre sundarībhiśca pūjitā |
pañcame maṅgalākāṅkṣairnarairmaṅgalacaṇḍikā ||
en: Third, the gracious one was worshipped by a king named Maṅgala; fourth, on Tuesday (maṅgalavāra) itself, she was worshipped by beautiful women; fifth, Maṅgalacaṇḍikā was worshipped by men desiring auspiciousness for themselves. This brief worship-history — Śiva, then the planet Mars, then a King Maṅgala, then women and men on Tuesdays — doubles as the stotra's own account of why Tuesday is her day.
tel: మూడవసారి మంగళుడనే రాజుచే భద్రాదేవి పూజింపబడెను; నాలుగవసారి మంగళవారమునాడు సుందరీమణులచే పూజింపబడెను; ఐదవసారి మంగళమును కోరు మానవులచే మంగళచండిక పూజింపబడెను. ఈ సంక్షిప్త పూజాచరిత్ర — శివుడు, తరువాత అంగారక గ్రహము, తరువాత మంగళుడనే రాజు, తరువాత మంగళవారమునాడు స్త్రీపురుషులు — మంగళవారము ఆమెకు ప్రత్యేకమైనదిగా ఎందుకు మారినదో స్తోత్రమే స్వయంగా వివరించు కథనము.
hi: तीसरी बार मंगल नामक राजा द्वारा भद्रा देवी पूजित हुईं; चौथी बार मंगलवार को सुंदरियों द्वारा पूजित हुईं; पाँचवीं बार मंगल चाहने वाले मनुष्यों द्वारा मंगलचंडिका पूजित हुईं। यह संक्षिप्त पूजा-इतिहास — पहले शिव, फिर मंगल ग्रह, फिर मंगल नामक राजा, फिर मंगलवार को स्त्री-पुरुष — यही स्तोत्र का अपना विवरण है कि मंगलवार उनका विशेष दिन क्यों बना।

--- verse 17 | section: Phalaśruti ---
deva:

tataḥ sarvatra sampūjya sā babhūva sureśvarī ||

--- verse 19 | section: Phalaśruti ---
