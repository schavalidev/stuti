Title: Śrī Durgā Āryā Stavam
Devanāgarī: श्री दुर्गा आर्या स्तवम्
Telugu: శ్రీ దుర్గా ఆర్యా స్తవం

Author: Traditionally spoken by Vaiśampāyana (as composed/arranged by Vyāsa)

Source / recension: stotranidhi.com — Devanāgarī: stotranidhi.com/hi/sri-durga-arya-stavam-in-sanskrit/ ; IAST: stotranidhi.com/en/sri-durga-arya-stavam-in-english/ ; Telugu: stotranidhi.com/sri-durga-arya-stavam-in-telugu/. All three script versions carry identical verse text and numbering (1–36) and were cross-checked against one another; the colophon in all three reads "iti śrīmahābhārate khilabhāge harivaṁśe viṣṇuparvaṇi tṛtīyo'dhyāye āryā stavam," placing the text in the Harivaṁśa (Khila-parvan) appended to the Mahābhārata, Viṣṇuparva, chapter 3. One minor source-side typographic irregularity was preserved as found: verse 15's first line in the source carries a stray extra daṇḍa before the verse-15 numeral (sāmyātrikāṇāṁ tu vēlā tvaṁ sāgarasya ca ॥ |), evidently a print artifact rather than a real half-verse break; it has been left untouched rather than silently "corrected" against un-fetched manuscripts.

Verse count: 36

Blurb: This hymn comes from the Harivaṁśa. Vaiśampāyana speaks it to Janamejaya. Within the story it is Śrī Kṛṣṇa who praises Yoganidrā, also called Ādyāśakti, before she is born as the daughter of Devakī to protect the newborn Kṛṣṇa from Kaṁsa. The hymn is a long list of identifications, each of the form 'you are this, you are that'. It identifies the Goddess with holy places, the seasons, the tithis, the worship offered by forest tribes, the planets, the parts of the Vedic sacrifice, and the wives and mothers of sages. It identifies her also with qualities such as tṛpti, dhṛti and kṣudhā, that is, satisfaction, steadiness and hunger. It closes with Kṛṣṇa's prophecy that she will be born as his own sister and will confound Kaṁsa. The hymn does not describe her outward form. It answers the question of who Durgā is by identifying her with the whole universe, both moving and unmoving, which is called sthāvara-jaṅgama.
