Title: Śrī Nārāyaṇī Stuti
Devanāgarī: श्री नारायणी स्तुति
Telugu: శ్రీ నారాయణీ స్తుతి

Author: Traditional; unattributed. The hymn is the concluding stuti of the Durgā Saptaśatī (Devī Māhātmya, Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa), chapter 11, sung by the assembled gods after the slaying of Śumbha and Niśumbha. The same site catalogues it twice — once as a standalone stuti and once as the Saptaśatī's eleventh chapter — with identical opening verses.

Verse count: 16

Blurb: The great closing hymn of the Durgā Saptaśatī, and structurally one of the most deliberate in the corpus: it takes the battle apart and puts it back together. The opening five verses address the Goddess in wholly abstract terms — the intelligence seated in every person's heart, the divisions of time by which things change, the power operating at the dissolution of the world, the ground of the three guṇas. Then verses 6 to 14 do something the abstract opening has already prepared for: they walk one by one through the Mātṛkās who actually fought the war, and salute each as a form of the one Nārāyaṇī. Brahmāṇī on her swan-drawn chariot with her kuśa-water; Māheśvarī with trident, moon and serpent on the bull; Kaumārī attended by peacock and cock; Vaiṣṇavī with conch, discus, mace and bow; Vārāhī lifting the earth on her tusk; the Man-Lion form; Aindrī thousand-eyed with the thunderbolt; Śivadūtī with her terrible roar; and Cāmuṇḍā, fanged and garlanded with severed heads. The plurality of the battlefield is thus systematically collapsed back into a single addressee. The last two verses drop iconography altogether and name her as the plain, unspectacular powers that hold ordinary life together — modesty, faith, nourishment, memory, restraint.

--- verse 1 ---
deva:
सर्वस्य बुद्धिरूपेण जनस्य हृदि संस्थिते ।
स्वर्गापवर्गदे देवि नारायणि नमोऽस्तु ते ॥
iast:
sarvasya buddhirūpeṇa janasya hṛdi saṁsthite |
svargāpavargade devi nārāyaṇi namo'stu te ||
en: O you who are seated in the heart of every person in the form of intelligence; O Goddess who bestows both heaven and release — Nārāyaṇī, salutations to you.
tel: ప్రతి మనిషి హృదయంలో బుద్ధిరూపంలో నెలకొని ఉన్నదానా; స్వర్గాన్నీ మోక్షాన్నీ ప్రసాదించే దేవీ — నారాయణీ, నీకు నమస్కారం.
hi: जो सब मनुष्यों के हृदय में बुद्धि के रूप में स्थित हो; हे स्वर्ग और अपवर्ग दोनों देने वाली देवी — नारायणि, तुम्हें नमस्कार हो।

--- verse 15 ---
deva:
लक्ष्मि लज्जे महाविद्ये श्रद्धे पुष्टि स्वधे ध्रुवे ।
महारात्रि महामाये नारायणि नमोऽस्तु ते ॥
iast:
lakṣmi lajje mahāvidye śraddhe puṣṭi svadhe dhruve |
mahārātri mahāmāye nārāyaṇi namo'stu te ||
en: O Lakṣmī; O Modesty; O Great Knowledge; O Faith; O Nourishment; O Svadhā; O Unshakeable One; O Great Night; O Great Illusion — Nārāyaṇī, salutations to you.
tel: ఓ లక్ష్మీ, ఓ లజ్జా, ఓ మహావిద్యా, ఓ శ్రద్ధా, ఓ పుష్టీ, ఓ స్వధా, ఓ ధ్రువా; ఓ మహారాత్రీ, ఓ మహామాయా — నారాయణీ, నీకు నమస్కారం.
hi: हे लक्ष्मि, हे लज्जे, हे महाविद्ये, हे श्रद्धे, हे पुष्टि, हे स्वधे, हे ध्रुवे; हे महारात्रि, हे महामाये — नारायणि, तुम्हें नमस्कार हो।

--- verse 16 ---
deva:
मेधे सरस्वति वरे भूति बाभ्रवि तामसि ।
नियते त्वं प्रसीदेशे नारायणि नमोऽस्तु ते ॥
iast:
medhe sarasvati vare bhūti bābhravi tāmasi |
niyate tvaṁ prasīdeśe nārāyaṇi namo'stu te ||
en: O Retentive Intellect; O Sarasvatī; O Excellent One; O Prosperity; O Bābhravī; O Dark One; O Restraint — be gracious, O Ruler: Nārāyaṇī, salutations to you.
tel: ఓ మేధా, ఓ సరస్వతీ, ఓ వరా, ఓ భూతీ, ఓ బాభ్రవీ, ఓ తామసీ; ఓ నియతీ, ప్రసన్నురాలవు కమ్ము, ఓ ఈశీ — నారాయణీ, నీకు నమస్కారం.
hi: हे मेधे, हे सरस्वति, हे वरे, हे भूति, हे बाभ्रवि, हे तामसि; हे नियते, प्रसन्न हो, हे ईशे — नारायणि, तुम्हें नमस्कार हो।

Recension note: Single-source text (stotranidhi.com), verified across that site's Devanāgarī and IAST editions verse for verse with no divergence in wording. Two purely orthographic normalizations were applied to bring the text in line with this corpus's conventions, neither of which changes a word: (1) in vv. 10 and 14 the source prints दम्ष्ट्र / damṣṭra with a full म् before the sibilant cluster, which is not valid Sanskrit orthography for this word; it is given here as दंष्ट्र / daṁṣṭra, the standard anusvāra form of daṁṣṭrā, "tusk, fang." (2) In vv. 7 and 16 the source's IAST runs the refrain together as namo'stute; it is spaced as namo'stu te throughout, matching the same site's own spelling in the other fourteen verses. Readers collating against printed Saptaśatī editions should note that this hymn is chapter 11 of the Devī Māhātmya and is a wholly different text from the Tantrokta Devī Sūktam of chapter 5 ("namo devyai mahādevyai"), which circulates under the name Aparājita Stotram and is already present in this corpus at 06_aparajita_stotram.txt; the two are sometimes conflated because both are built on a namas refrain. Some Saptaśatī recensions carry additional verses in this chapter before and after the sixteen given here, including the gods' introductory verse and the Devī's reply; this source presents only the stuti proper.
