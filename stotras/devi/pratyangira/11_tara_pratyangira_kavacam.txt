Title: Śrī Tārā Pratyaṅgirā Kavacam
Devanāgarī: श्री तारा प्रत्यङ्गिरा कवचम्
Telugu: శ్రీ తారా ప్రత్యంగిరా కవచం

Author: Traditional — the closing colophon assigns it to the Rudrayāmala Tantra as "the Pratyaṅgirā Kavaca of the venerable Ugratarā"; the sanskritdocuments recension frames it as spoken by Īśvara (Śiva).

Source / recension: stotranidhi.com — Devanāgarī: https://stotranidhi.com/hi/sri-tara-pratyangira-kavacham-in-sanskrit/ ; IAST: https://stotranidhi.com/en/sri-tara-pratyangira-kavacham-in-english/ ; Telugu script: https://stotranidhi.com/sri-tara-pratyangira-kavacham-in-telugu/ . Devanāgarī and IAST were checked verse-by-verse and agree throughout, with continuous numbering 1–15; IAST is stotranidhi's own transliteration, normalized to this corpus's conventions (ē/ō → e/o, ṃ → ṁ, r̥ → ṛ), with the stray colon artifact "vā:'pi" corrected to "vā'pi". The text was additionally cross-checked against a second, independent recension — sanskritdocuments.org, tArApratyangirA.itx (transliterated and proofread by "KM", last updated 5 April 2025, https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_devii/tArApratyangirA.itx) — and the two agree substantively, with these differences recorded here: (i) in verse 1 the fourth śakti is jṛmbhiṇī in stotranidhi but hastinī in sanskritdocuments; jṛmbhiṇī is adopted, since both recensions use jṛmbhiṇī in the formula sequence itself (v. 6); (ii) sanskritdocuments reverses the order of the mohinī and kṣobhiṇī formulas (vv. 4–5); (iii) verse 10's closing runs "sarvatra māṁ rakṣa rakṣa svāhā" here against "sarvatra rakṣa rakṣa māṁ svāhā" there; (iv) verse 11's "hanyācchatrūn" is transmitted corruptly at sanskritdocuments ("hyanyācchatrūn"), and stotranidhi's reading is followed; (v) sanskritdocuments prefixes an invocation "oṁ pratyaṅgirāyai namaḥ" and a speaker-tag "īśvara uvāca" that stotranidhi lacks, runs vv. 13–14 together as one unit, and carries a doublet of the closing verse ("sarvataḥ sukham āpnoti… / na kvāpi bhayam āpnoti sarvatra sukham āpnuyāt") that stotranidhi conflates into the single verse 15. Two words are corrupt in both recensions and are printed as transmitted: verse 2's "śaktayoraṣṭau" (for śaktayo hy aṣṭau, "these eight śaktis," as sanskritdocuments' śaktayo haṣṭau also shows) and verse 15's "dhīyāno" (evidently for dhyāyāno, "meditating"). In verse 15 stotranidhi supplies the relative pronoun यो / yo in brackets; it is adopted into the verse text here without brackets. No prose viniyoga or nyāsa apparatus appears in either recension. The sanskritdocuments file carries a modern contributor's note arguing that this kavaca is not abhicārika and that "śatru" should be read as the sādhaka's own inner senses; that is an editorial gloss, not part of the text, and the translations below render the Sanskrit as it stands.

Verse count: 15

Sections:
కవచం — Kavacam, the eight śaktis of Tārā and their formulas (vv. 1–10)
ఫలశ్రుతిః — Phalaśruti, the fruits of bearing and reciting the vidyā (vv. 11–15)

Blurb: This is the Rudrayāmala's Pratyaṅgirā kavaca for Ugratārā, the fierce dark form of the Mahāvidyā Tārā, who is also called Nīlasarasvatī. It is built on exactly the same plan as the Bagalā Pratyaṅgirā Kavacam in this collection. Eight śaktis are named in a single opening verse, and each is then given her own imperative formula, turned against the enemy. Only the last of the eight breaks the pattern. Tāriṇī, meaning 'she who ferries across', is asked not to attack anyone, but to protect the reciter from every calamity and from every being. Its phalaśruti is unusually concrete about where the kavaca is to be worn and used: in battle, in a royal court, and inside a besieged fort. It ends by folding the whole fierce apparatus back into Tārā's own mantra, 'oṁ hrīṁ ugratārāyai nīlasarasvatyai namaḥ'.

--- verse 1 | section: Kavacam ---
deva:
तारायाः स्तम्भिनी देवी मोहिनी क्षोभिणी तथा ।
जृम्भिणी भ्रामिणी रौद्री संहारिण्यपि तारिणी ॥
iast:
tārāyāḥ stambhinī devī mohinī kṣobhiṇī tathā |
jṛmbhiṇī bhrāmiṇī raudrī saṁhāriṇyapi tāriṇī ||
en: Of Tārā these are the powers: Stambhinī the goddess, "she who paralyses"; Mohinī, "she who bewilders"; and Kṣobhiṇī, "she who convulses"; Jṛmbhiṇī, "she who makes the limbs gape and slacken"; Bhrāmiṇī, "she who sets whirling"; Raudrī, "the ferocious"; Saṁhāriṇī, "she who annihilates"; and Tāriṇī too, "she who ferries across."
tel: తారాదేవి యొక్క శక్తులు ఇవి — స్తంభిని (స్తంభింపజేయు దేవి), మోహిని (మోహింపజేయునది), క్షోభిణి (కలవరపరచునది), జృంభిణి (అవయవములను వదలిపోవునట్లు చేయునది), భ్రామిణి (తిప్పివేయునది), రౌద్రి (భయంకరురాలు), సంహారిణి (సంహరించునది), తారిణి (తరింపజేయునది) కూడ.
hi: तारा की ये शक्तियाँ हैं — स्तम्भिनी देवी (जड़ कर देने वाली), मोहिनी (मोहित करने वाली), क्षोभिणी (विचलित करने वाली), जृम्भिणी (अंगों को शिथिल करने वाली), भ्रामिणी (घुमा देने वाली), रौद्री (भयंकर), संहारिणी (संहार करने वाली), और तारिणी भी (तार देने वाली)।

hi: ये आठ शक्तियाँ इसी क्रम से शत्रु-पक्ष पर नियुक्त की गई हैं; साधकश्रेष्ठ द्वारा धारण की गई यह विद्या समस्त शत्रुओं का निवारण करती है।

deva:
ओं तारिणी स्त्रीं स्त्रीं सर्वापद्भ्यः सर्वभूतेभ्यः सर्वत्र मां रक्ष रक्ष स्वाहा ॥

--- verse 11 | section: Phalaśruti ---
deva:

en: Whoever bears this vidyā upon his person, or recites it at the three junctures of the day — he, having cast sorrow far away from himself, will strike down his enemies; of this there is no doubt.
tel: ఈ విద్యను ధరించువాడు, లేక త్రిసంధ్యలయందు దీనిని పఠించువాడు — దుఃఖమును దూరముగా విడిచి శత్రువులను సంహరించును; ఇందు సందేహము లేదు.
hi: जो इस विद्या को धारण करता है, अथवा तीनों सन्ध्याओं में इसका पाठ करता है — वह दुःख को दूर छोड़कर शत्रुओं का हनन करता है; इसमें संशय नहीं।

en: In battle, in the king's court, in a fortress, in great terror, in disasters — this Pratyaṅgirā vidyā guards a man on every side.
tel: యుద్ధమునందు, రాజసభయందు, దుర్గమునందు, మహాభయమునందు, ఆపదలయందు — ఈ ప్రత్యంగిరా విద్య మనుష్యుని అన్నివైపుల నుండి రక్షించును.

tel: ఈ విద్యచేత రక్షను చేసికొని పఠించు బుద్ధిమంతుడు — మంత్రాక్షరమునైనను ధ్యానించుచు — నీలసరస్వతిని చింతింపవలెను.

iast:
idaṁ kavacaṁ dhīyāno nityaṁ yo dhārayennaraḥ |
na kvāpi bhayamāpnoti sarvatra jayamāpnuyāt ||
iti śrīrudrayāmale śrīmadugratarā pratyaṅgirā kavacam |
en: The man who, meditating upon her, bears this kavaca daily meets with fear nowhere at all, and wins victory everywhere. Thus, in the Śrī Rudrayāmala, the Pratyaṅgirā Kavaca of the venerable Ugratarā. (The participle transmitted as dhīyāno is corrupt in both recensions and is rendered here from its evident sense, dhyāyāno, "meditating.")
tel: ధ్యానము చేయుచు ఈ కవచమును నిత్యము ధరించు మనుష్యుడు ఎచ్చటను భయమును పొందడు, అంతటను జయమును పొందును. ఇట్లు శ్రీ రుద్రయామలమునందలి శ్రీమదుగ్రతరా ప్రత్యంగిరా కవచము. (మూలములోని "ధీయానో" అను రూపము రెండు పాఠములలోను చెడిపోయియున్నది; "ధ్యాయానో" (ధ్యానించుచు) అను స్పష్టమైన అర్థమునే ఇచ్చట గ్రహించితిమి.)
hi: जो मनुष्य ध्यान करते हुए इस कवच को नित्य धारण करता है, वह कहीं भी भय को प्राप्त नहीं होता और सर्वत्र विजय पाता है। इस प्रकार श्री रुद्रयामल में श्रीमद् उग्रतरा प्रत्यङ्गिरा कवच। (मूल का "धीयानो" रूप दोनों पाठों में विकृत है; यहाँ उसका स्पष्ट अभिप्राय "ध्यायानो" (ध्यान करते हुए) लिया गया है।)
