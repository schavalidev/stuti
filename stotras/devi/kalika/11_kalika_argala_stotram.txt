Title: Śrī Kālikā Argala Stotram
Devanāgarī: श्री कालिका अर्गल स्तोत्रम्
Telugu: శ్రీ కాళికా అర్గల స్తోత్రం

Author: Traditional (unattributed); the opening viniyoga names Bhairava as the ṛṣi of the hymn, and the colophon reads "iti śrī kālī argala stotram"

Source / recension: https://stotranidhi.com/en/sri-kalika-argala-stotram-in-english/ (IAST); https://stotranidhi.com/hi/sri-kalika-argala-stotram-in-sanskrit/ (Devanāgarī); slug confirmed against stotranidhi.com/post-sitemap*.xml. The Devanāgarī on the /hi/ page was checked verse for verse against the IAST on the /en/ page and matches exactly, with no variant readings found. The source opens with a line of prose ritual apparatus before the verses begin — the viniyoga: "asya śrī kālikārgala stotrasya bhairava ṛṣiranuṣṭup chandaḥ śrīkālikā devatā mama sarvasiddhisādhane viniyogaḥ" ("Of this Śrī Kālikā Argala Stotra, Bhairava is the ṛṣi, anuṣṭubh the meter, Śrī Kālikā the deity; its application is for my accomplishment of every siddhi"). Following this corpus's practice, that prose has been kept out of the numbered verse sequence and is recorded here instead. The whole hymn proper is in anuṣṭubh, and its sentences frequently run across verse boundaries (vv. 7–8, 17–18, 27–29); the verse divisions follow the source's numbering. Two small corruptions are left as printed: "vājīṁ" in v. 7 where the sense requires vāṇīṁ (speech), and "śavavāhinā" in v. 2 where the vocative śavavāhini is expected.

Verse count: 30

Sections:
అర్గలం — Argala, the bolt of protective installations (vv. 1–29)
ఫలశ్రుతి — Phalaśruti, the fruits of recitation (v. 30)

Blurb: An argala is literally a bolt or a door-bar, and it is the companion piece to a kavaca. This one works by fastening a named form of the Goddess, or a named mantra of hers, onto each part of the reciter's body in turn. The sequence runs from head to foot and is unusually specific. The paired Kūrca seeds go on the forehead, and the paired Bhuvaneśvarī seeds on the brows. Light anoints the eyes. The Lajjā seeds go on the ears. Hrīṁ Hrīṁ guards the nostrils. The first of the three seeds goes on the teeth, the lips and the tongue. Svāhā stands steady in the uvula. Then the four-syllabled vidyā goes in the heart-lotus, the six-syllabled in the arms, the seven-syllabled in the breasts, the eight-syllabled in the belly, the ten-syllabled in the navel, and the fourteen-syllabled in the liṅga. Guhyakālī goes in the secret place, and Bhadrakālī in every limb. The mantras are never spelled out as bare strings of syllables. They are always named by their code-words and by the number of their syllables, so that the whole text remains readable as verse. Its second half widens into two full pantheons. First come the attendant śaktis of the Kālī-kula: Kullā, Kurukullā, Virodhinī, Vipracittā, Ugrā, Prabhā, Dīptā, Nīlā, Ghanā, Balākā, Mātrā, Mudrā and Mitā, stationed in the pores of the hair, the head, the road, the directions, the netherworld and the sky. Then come the Mātṛkās: Brāhmī, Nārāyaṇī, Māheśvarī, Cāmuṇḍā, Kaumārī, Aparājitā, Vārāhī and Nārasiṁhī, each posted at the station proper to her. A single closing verse promises the reciter every siddhi, and the state of a khecara, one who moves through the sky.

--- verse 30 | section: Phalaśruti ---
deva:
य इदं चार्गलं देवि यः पठेत्कालिकार्चने ।
सर्वसिद्धिमवाप्नोति खेचरो जायते तु सः ॥
iast:
ya idaṁ cārgalaṁ devi yaḥ paṭhetkālikārcane |
sarvasiddhimavāpnoti khecaro jāyate tu saḥ ||
en: Whoever recites this Argala, O Devī, in the course of the worship of Kālikā obtains every accomplishment, and becomes a khecara, one who moves through the sky.
tel: ఓ దేవీ, ఎవడు కాళికార్చనయందు ఈ అర్గలమును పఠించునో, అతడు సర్వసిద్ధిని పొందును, మఱియు ఖేచరుడగును.
hi: हे देवि, जो कोई कालिका की अर्चना में इस अर्गल का पाठ करता है, वह सर्व सिद्धि प्राप्त करता है और खेचर हो जाता है।
