Devanāgarī: श्री ललिता-अष्टोत्तरशतनामावलिः
Telugu: శ్రీ లలితా అష్టోత్తరశతనామావళిః

Author: Traditional; the source names no Purāṇa or composer. This is the widely recited Lalitā aṣṭottara nāmāvalī beginning `रजताचलशृङ्गाग्रमध्यस्थायै`, whose long compound names are chanted with `नमो नमः`.

Source / recension: Primary text: **a Telugu-script nāmāvalī collection, Smārtha e-books (smarthamitra.in), "108–1008"** (scanned PDF, page images only, PDF metadata author "kprss", 2016), printed pp. 124–129 (PDF pp. 128–133). **Not a Gītā Press edition; Gītā Press's *Śatanāma Stotra Saṅgraha* (code 1850) has no list of this form.** Transcribed from the Telugu page images into Devanāgarī. **Collated against sanskritdocuments.org `lalitaa108naam.itx`** (transliterated and proofread by Sujatha Beladakere), an independent witness of the same list; the two agree name for name, in the same order. Three other sanskritdocuments Lalitā lists (`lalitaa108`, `lalitAaShTottarashatanAmAvalI`, `lalitAShTottarashatanAmAvalI2`) were checked and are **different works**, not witnesses here.

Recension note: **The Smārtha nāmāvalī is followed; the Beladakere witness (SD) is recorded, not adopted.** Differences (Smārtha → SD): no. 33 `सचामररमावाणीविराजितायै` → `sañcāramararamāvāṇīvījitāyai`; no. 35 `भूतालिङ्गनोद्भूतपुलकाङ्ग्यै` → `bhūteśāliṅganodbhūtapulakāṅgyai` (the Smārtha print lacks `ईश`, giving no clear sense; kept as printed, and the gloss marked); no. 44 `…दुर्वासपूजितायै` → `…durvāsaḥpūjitāyai`; no. 83 `भक्तहंसपरीमुख्यवियोगायै` → `…parimukhya…`; no. 100 `श्रीवीरभक्तविज्ञाननिधानायै` → `…vidhānāyai`; no. 103 `हयमेधाग्रसम्पूज्यमहिम्ने` (masculine-stem dative) → `…mahimāyai`. **Formula.** Every name in the print ends `నమో నమః` ("salutation, salutation") rather than the single `నమః`; the index gives the dative alone, as for every nāmāvalī in the corpus. The print opens with a bīja line, `ఓం – ఐం – హ్రీం – శ్రీం`, under the heading `శ్రీ బాలాత్రిపురసుందర్యై నమః`; it is recorded here, not numbered. SD gives the same bīja line and adds a dhyāna (`सिन्दूरारुणविग्रहां …`), which the Smārtha print lacks. **Count:** 108; the print's tally marks and SD agree. **Prefix.** This corpus prints `श्री` for every nāmāvalī without exception (user, 2026-09-08). **Cross-reference:** `devi/lalita/36_lalita_sahasranama_stotram.txt` is the sahasranāma, a separate work.

Blurb: A portrait painted from head to foot in long compounds: the emerald-bright body that is half of Śiva's, the musk mark and line of ash on the brow, the campaka nose, the pomegranate-seed teeth, the betel-reddened smile, the marriage thread tied by Girīśa. Then the throne in the lotus forest of the jewel island, the war against Bhaṇḍa, and at last the goddess who is waking, dreaming and deep sleep's witness, pleased by nothing more than being thought of.
