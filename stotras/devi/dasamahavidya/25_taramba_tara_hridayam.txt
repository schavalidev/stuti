Title: Śrī Tārāmbā Hṛdayam
Devanāgarī: श्री ताराम्बा हृदयम्
Telugu: శ్రీ తారాంబా హృదయం

Author: Anonymous. The colophon places the text in the Bhairavī Tantra as a dialogue between Śiva and Pārvatī — "iti śrī bhairavītantre śivapārvatī saṁvāde śrīmad-ugratārā-hṛdayaṁ" — and verse 3 attributes the hymn's original composition to Indra, in the ancient war between gods and demons.

Source / recension: stotranidhi.com, Devanāgarī page (stotranidhi.com/hi/sri-taramba-tara-hrudayam-in-sanskrit/) cross-checked verse-by-verse against the IAST page (stotranidhi.com/en/sri-taramba-tara-hrudayam-in-english/). The two agree at every verse, bīja-strings included; no variant readings were found. Between verses 3 and 4 the source prints prose ritual apparatus which is kept out of the numbered verse sequence below: a viniyoga (Bhairava as ṛṣi, anuṣṭubh as metre, Ugratārā as devatā, strīṁ as bīja, hūṁ as śakti, namaḥ as kīlaka, the purpose being the destruction of all enemies), followed by a karanyāsa and an aṅganyāsa on the syllables strīṁ, hrīṁ, hūṁ, trīṁ, aiṁ, haṁsaḥ. The dhyāna verse that follows that apparatus is verse content and is retained here as verse 4.

Sections:
ఉపోద్ఘాతం — Prologue, the frame dialogue (vv. 1–3)
ధ్యానం — Dhyānam, the meditation verse (v. 4)
హృదయం — Hṛdayam, the Heart proper (vv. 5–14)
ప్రయోగాః — Prayogas, the ritual applications (vv. 15–20)
ఫలశ్రుతి — Phalaśruti, the fruits of recitation (vv. 21–24)

Blurb: A hṛdaya is the heart, or inner core, of a deity's cult. It is a form that packs the essential names and uses of a mantra into a short frame. This one is built in three distinct movements. First comes a secret garland of twelve names, in verses 9 to 12. There Tārā is identified in turn with rajas and creation, with tamas and Mahāmāyā, and with time and dissolution. Then come six prayoga verses, in verses 15 to 20, which read as a working manual. Each pairs a particular string of bījas with a particular substance, namely water, sandal paste, cremation ash, a flower, unbroken rice, or lamp-black, and states the effect intended. Last comes a phalaśruti, which closes with the plain prohibition 'na prakāśyam idaṁ kvacit', meaning 'this must never anywhere be revealed'.

--- verse 1 | section: Prologue ---
deva:
शृणु पार्वति भद्रं ते लोकानां हितकारकं ।
कथ्यते सर्वदा गोप्यं ताराहृदयमुत्तमम् ॥
iast:
śṛṇu pārvati bhadraṁ te lokānāṁ hitakārakaṁ |
kathyate sarvadā gopyaṁ tārāhṛdayamuttamam ||
en: Śrī Śiva said: Listen, Pārvatī — may it go well with you. The supreme Heart of Tārā, which works the good of the worlds and is at all times to be kept secret, is now declared.
tel: శ్రీ శివుడు పలికెను: విను పార్వతీ — నీకు మంగళమగుగాక. లోకాలకు హితాన్ని కలిగించునది, ఎల్లప్పుడూ గోప్యంగా ఉంచదగినది అయిన ఉత్తమమైన తారా హృదయం ఇప్పుడు చెప్పబడుతున్నది.
hi: श्री शिव ने कहा: सुनो पार्वति — तुम्हारा कल्याण हो। लोकों का हित करने वाला, सदा गोपनीय, उत्तम तारा-हृदय अब कहा जा रहा है।

--- verse 4 | section: Dhyānam ---

dhyāyetkoṭidivākaradyutinibhāṁ bālenduyukchekharāṁ

tel: కోటి సూర్యుల కాంతికి సమానమైనది, శిరస్సున బాలచంద్రుని ధరించినది, ఎర్రని అవయవాలు కలది, వికటరూపిణి, గాఢమైన ఎర్రని వస్త్రాలు ధరించినది, పూర్ణచంద్రబింబం వంటి ముఖం కలది, నాలుగు భుజాలలో పాశం, ఖడ్గం, మహాంకుశం మొదలైనవి ధరించినది, అనేక ఆభరణాలతో అలంకరించబడినది అయిన జగత్తారిణి భగవతి తారను ధ్యానించాలి.
hi: कोटि सूर्यों के समान द्युति वाली, शिर पर बाल चंद्र धारण किए, रक्त अंगों वाली, विकट रूपा, गाढ़े लाल वस्त्र पहने, पूर्णचंद्र-बिम्ब सदृश मुख वाली, चार भुजाओं में पाश, खड्ग, महांकुश आदि धारण किए, अनेक आभूषणों से विभूषित, जगत् को तारने वाली भगवती तारा का ध्यान करना चाहिए।

--- verse 5 | section: Hṛdayam ---
deva:
एवं ध्यात्वा शुभां तारां ततस्तु हृदयं पठेत् ।
तारिणी तत्त्वनिष्ठानां सर्वतत्त्वप्रकाशिका ॥
iast:
evaṁ dhyātvā śubhāṁ tārāṁ tatastu hṛdayaṁ paṭhet |
tāriṇī tattvaniṣṭhānāṁ sarvatattvaprakāśikā ||
en: Having thus meditated on the auspicious Tārā, one should then recite the Heart. She is the deliverer of those grounded in the truth, the revealer of every principle.
tel: ఈ విధంగా శుభమైన తారను ధ్యానించి, ఆ తరువాత హృదయాన్ని పఠించాలి. ఆమె తత్త్వనిష్ఠులను తరింపజేయునది, సమస్త తత్త్వాలను ప్రకాశింపజేయునది.
hi: इस प्रकार शुभा तारा का ध्यान करके, तत्पश्चात् हृदय का पाठ करे। वह तत्त्वनिष्ठों को तारने वाली, समस्त तत्त्वों को प्रकाशित करने वाली है।

Recension note: Only one recension was available — stotranidhi's Devanāgarī and IAST pages, two script-renderings of a single underlying text, agreeing at every verse. Verse 24 is a half-verse in the source and has been kept as such rather than padded. Verse 6's compound rāmābhinnāpadāśakti admits more than one division; it is read here as rāmā-abhinnā + āpad-ā-śakti, "she whose power is inseparable from Rāmā (Lakṣmī) and who lifts one out of calamity." The source prints the conjunct म्श rather than an anusvāra in samśodhya and samśayaḥ (v. 18) and prāṇasamśaye (v. 22), and this has been transliterated as printed. The prayoga verses (15–20) include abhicāra applications directed at enemies; they are translated as they stand, as part of the historical text. The IAST page uses ē and ō for the long vowels, rendered here as plain e and o per the conventions of this corpus, a right curly quote for the avagraha (pradā'naghā, japtvā'bhiṣecayet), rendered as an apostrophe, and decomposed r̥, given here as precomposed ṛ; its ẏ (य़) in the nyāsa prose was read as य.
