Title: Śrī Bhuvaneśvarī Pañjara Stotram
Devanāgarī: श्री भुवनेश्वरी पञ्जर स्तोत्रम्
Telugu: శ్రీ భువనేశ్వరీ పంజర స్తోత్రం

Author: Anonymous tantric text. The closing verses address Pārvatī directly, placing the hymn in the usual Śiva-to-Pārvatī frame of the pañjara literature; the colophon gives only "iti śrībhuvaneśvarī pañjarastotram sampūrṇam" with no source work named.

Source / recension: stotranidhi.com, Devanāgarī page (stotranidhi.com/hi/sri-bhuvaneshwari-panjara-stotram-in-sanskrit/) cross-checked line-by-line against the IAST page (stotranidhi.com/en/sri-bhuvaneshwari-panjara-stotram-in-english/). The two agree throughout, bīja-strings included. The source numbers the text in three separate runs — 1 to 2 for the introduction, a fresh 1 to 13 for the directional invocations, and a fresh 1 to 7 for the phalaśruti — and the numbering here is continuous, 1 to 22. Three points of the printed text are worth flagging. First, the phalaśruti (v. 16) speaks of caturdaśa diṅmanavaḥ, fourteen directional mantras, while only thirteen are actually printed; a fourteenth appears to have dropped out of this recension. Second, the thirteenth invocation (v. 15, sarvataḥ, "on every side") is printed without the bīja-string that heads the other twelve — reproduced here as printed. Third, small typographic slips in the Devanāgarī that the IAST page corrects have been normalized silently: शत्रुन् to शत्रून् in the second through thirteenth invocations, and देव देवि to देवि देवि in the third. In verse 17 the printed na bhaktāya is almost certainly for abhaktāya, and the translation follows that sense.

Verse count: 22

Sections:
ఉపోద్ఘాతం — Introduction, the promise of the Pañjara (vv. 1–2)
పంజరం — Pañjaram, the thirteen directional invocations (vv. 3–15)
ఫలశ్రుతి — Phalaśruti, the fruits of recitation (vv. 16–22)

Blurb: A pañjara is a cage. It is a protective enclosure built by placing the deity, armed and named, at each point of the compass, and then above, below, in front, behind, and finally all around. The reciter ends by standing within a sealed lattice of her presence. The thirteen invocations here are identical except in two words: the direction, and the weapon she holds there. She is Cakriṇī in the east with the discus, Gadinī in the south-east with the mace, Śaṅkhinī in the south, Khaḍginī in the south-west with the sword, Pāśinī in the west with the noose, and so on around to Sāyudhā, the armed one, on every side at once. What makes this text unusual in its genre is its refrain. It does not so much ask for protection as demand attention: 'uttiṣṭha puruṣe kiṁ svapiṣi bhayaṁ me samupasthitam', meaning 'rise up; why do you sleep? Fear has come upon me'. A gāyatrī of the Enchantress of the three worlds follows. The phalaśruti instructs the reciter to guard the text as one would hide a mother's lover, which shows how closely it was held.

--- verse 1 | section: Introduction ---
deva:
इदं श्री भुवनेश्वर्याः पञ्जरं भुवि दुर्लभम् ।
येन संरक्षितो मर्त्यो बाणैः शस्त्रैर्न बाध्यते ॥
iast:
idaṁ śrī bhuvaneśvaryāḥ pañjaraṁ bhuvi durlabham |
yena saṁrakṣito martyo bāṇaiḥ śastrairna bādhyate ||
en: This Pañjara of Śrī Bhuvaneśvarī is hard to come by upon the earth. The mortal who is fully guarded by it is not assailed by arrows or by any weapon.
tel: శ్రీ భువనేశ్వరి యొక్క ఈ పంజరము భూమిపై దుర్లభమైనది. దీనిచేత చక్కగా రక్షింపబడిన మనుజుడు బాణములచేతను ఆయుధములచేతను బాధింపబడడు.
hi: श्री भुवनेश्वरी का यह पंजर भूमि पर दुर्लभ है। जो मनुष्य इससे भली-भाँति रक्षित है, वह बाणों और शस्त्रों से बाधित नहीं होता।

hi: प्रातःकाल, मध्याह्न, सन्ध्या और अर्धरात्रि में — जो मनुष्य एकाग्रचित्त होकर देवी का ध्यान करके पंजर करता है —

--- verse 22 | section: Phalaśruti ---
deva:
कालमृत्युमपि प्राप्तं जयेदत्र न संशयः ।
ब्रह्मास्त्रादीनि शस्त्राणि तद्गात्रं न लगन्ति च ।
पुत्रवान् धनवान् लोके यशस्वी जायते नरः ॥
iast:
kālamṛtyumapi prāptaṁ jayedatra na saṁśayaḥ |
brahmāstrādīni śastrāṇi tadgātraṁ na laganti ca |
putravān dhanavān loke yaśasvī jāyate naraḥ ||
en: — conquers even death itself when its hour has come upon him; of that there is no doubt here. Weapons, the Brahmāstra among them, do not touch his body; and that man becomes, in this world, blessed with sons, with wealth and with fame.
tel: — వచ్చిపడిన కాలమృత్యువును కూడ జయించును; ఇందు సందేహము లేదు. బ్రహ్మాస్త్రము మొదలగు ఆయుధములు అతని శరీరమునకు తగులవు; ఆ మనుజుడు లోకమునందు పుత్రవంతుడు, ధనవంతుడు, యశస్వి అగును.
hi: — आ पड़े हुए कालमृत्यु को भी जीत लेता है; इसमें संशय नहीं। ब्रह्मास्त्र आदि शस्त्र उसके शरीर को नहीं लगते; और वह मनुष्य लोक में पुत्रवान्, धनवान् तथा यशस्वी होता है।
