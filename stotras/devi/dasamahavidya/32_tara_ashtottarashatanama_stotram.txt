Title: Śrī Tārā Aṣṭottaraśatanāma Stotram
Devanāgarī: श्रीतारा-अष्टोत्तरशतनामस्तोत्रम्
Telugu: శ్రీ తారా అష్టోత్తరశతనామ స్తోత్రం

Author: The colophon places it in the **Svarṇamālā Tantra**; **Śiva** speaks it to the Devī (`श्रीशिव उवाच`). Many of the names turn on the root *tṛ*, "to cross over" — Tāriṇī, Tarī (the boat), Tīrarūpā (the far shore), Bhadratāriṇī — the etymology of Tārā as "she who carries across".

Source / recension: Primary text: **Gītā Press, Gorakhpur — Śatanāma Stotra Saṅgraha**, code 1850, pp. 110–114 (stotra pp. 110–112, nāmāvalī pp. 113–114; PDF pp. 111–115), transcribed from the page images of the printed edition. Entry 20 of the volume's forty. **Collated against two independent witnesses on sanskritdocuments.org:** (1) `tArAshatanAmastotram.itx` (transliterated by Shree Devi Kumar, proofread by P. S. A. Easwaran, with acknowledgement to the Mahaperiaval Trust), the **same Svarṇamālā Tantra verse text**, 21 verses in the same numbering and with the same colophon; its note adds that vv. 1–10 recur in the Muṇḍamālā Tantra, paṭala 13, with small variations. (2) `tArAshatanAmAvalI.itx`, from the printed *Daśamahāvidyā Aṣṭottaraśatanāmāvalī* (ed. Rāmadatta Śukla and Ṛtaśīlā Śarmā), the name list. Stotra and nāmāvalī are paired in one file as the publisher prints them, and were checked against each other name by name.

Recension note: **Gītā Press followed; the witnesses are recorded, not adopted.** Verse text, Gītā Press → the Svarṇamālā stotra witness (SD): v. 1 `तीररूपा` ("whose form is the far shore") → `tīvrarūpā` ("fierce in form"; the Śukla–Śarmā nāmāvalī also has `tīvrarūpāyai`); v. 2 `तरुणा` → `taralā`, with `taruṇā` noted as its variant; v. 3 `उग्रा उग्रप्रभा` → `ugrā cograprabhā`; v. 5 `चरास्यादितिपूजिता` → `carāsyā ditipūjitā` (so SD has Diti, and Gītā Press Aditi; the Gītā Press nāmāvalī no. 39 reads `अदितिपूजितायै`); v. 5 `सगुणासगुणाराध्या` → `saguṇā saguṇārādhyā` (Gītā Press divides this as `सगुणा असगुणा आराध्या`, nos. 40–42); v. 6 `दुर्धा` → `durgā`; v. 7 `ऊर्ध्वकेशेश्वरी केशा केशवा सविभूषिता` → `ardhakeśeśvarī keśā keśavāsavibhūṣitā`; v. 11 `तत्त्वरूपा तत्त्वप्रिया` → SD reverses the pair; v. 13 `शक्तिभक्तानुरागिनी` → `śaktirbhaktānurāgiṇī` (two names in SD, one in Gītā Press no. 96). The name list, Gītā Press → the Śukla–Śarmā nāmāvalī: `तनुक्षीणपयोधरायै` (no. 9) is split there as `tanukṣīṇāyai payodharāyai`, so its numbering runs one ahead from that point. **Stotra and nāmāvalī agree throughout.** **Duplicate:** `रक्तप्रियायै` (nos. 44 and 68), which the verses also repeat (vv. 6 and 9). **The dhyāna** is the volume's, glossed in a Hindi footnote; `कर्तृ` in it is glossed there as `कर्तरि (कैंची)`, "shears". **V. 21 is a tristich.** **Prefix.** Gītā Press prints `ॐ` before each name; this corpus prints `श्री` for every nāmāvalī without exception (user, 2026-09-08). **Cross-reference:** `devi/dasamahavidya/06_tarashtakam.txt`, `21_tara_kavacam.txt` and `25_taramba_tara_hridayam.txt` are separate Tārā texts, not this one.

Blurb: The goddess who carries across, named by her crossing: the boat, the far shore, the one who ferries to good. She is dark and slender, with a single matted lock, a severed brow and a blood-red mouth; she is Blue Sarasvatī, loud-laughing, fond of offerings and of copper vessels, and she is the three channels of the subtle body. Then, unexpectedly, the list claims Balarāma and Rāma for her too.

--- verse 17 | section: Phalaśrutiḥ ---

śrutvā mokṣamavāpnoti tārādevyāḥ prasādataḥ || 16 ||

tel: ఓ దేవీ, ఈ పరమాద్భుతమైన రహస్యము చెప్పబడినది. దీనిని విని తారాదేవి ప్రసాదముచే మోక్షము పొందును.
hi: हे देवि! इस प्रकार यह परम अद्भुत रहस्य कहा गया। इसे सुनकर मनुष्य तारादेवीकी कृपासे मोक्ष प्राप्त करता है।

--- verse 18 | section: Phalaśrutiḥ ---
deva:

tel: తారాస్తుతిరహస్యమైన ఈ స్తోత్రమును ఎవడు పఠించునో వాడు సర్వసిద్ధియుతుడై భూమండలమునందు విహరించును.
hi: जो इस तारास्तुतिरहस्यरूप स्तोत्रका पाठ करता है, वह सर्वसिद्धियुक्त होकर पृथ्वीपर विचरण करता है।

--- verse 19 | section: Phalaśrutiḥ ---
deva:

भवत्येव महामाये सत्यं सत्यं न संशयः ॥ १८ ॥
iast:
tasyaiva mantrasiddhiḥ syānmama siddhiranuttamā |
bhavatyeva mahāmāye satyaṁ satyaṁ na saṁśayaḥ || 18 ||
en: For him alone there is mastery of the mantra, and my own unsurpassed attainment comes to be, O Mahāmāyā — truly, truly, without doubt.
tel: వానికే మంత్రసిద్ధి కలుగును; ఓ మహామాయే, నా అనుత్తమసిద్ధియు కలుగును — ఇది సత్యము సత్యము, సంశయము లేదు.
hi: उसीको मन्त्रसिद्धि होती है और हे महामाये! मेरी अनुत्तम सिद्धि भी प्राप्त होती है — यह सत्य है, सत्य है, इसमें संशय नहीं।

--- verse 20 | section: Phalaśrutiḥ ---
deva:

tasyaiva mantrasiddhiḥ syād gāṇapatyaṁ labheta saḥ || 19 ||
en: Whoever, self-restrained, recites it at night on a Saturday or Tuesday gains mastery of the mantra and attains lordship over the gaṇas.
tel: శనివారమునను మంగళవారమునను రాత్రియందు నియతుడై ఎవడు పఠించునో వానికే మంత్రసిద్ధి కలుగును; వాడు గాణపత్యమును పొందును.
hi: जो संयमपूर्वक शनिवार और मंगलवारको रात्रिमें इसका पाठ करता है, उसीको मन्त्रसिद्धि होती है और वह गाणपत्य पद प्राप्त करता है।

--- verse 22 | section: Phalaśrutiḥ ---

en: By a thousand recitations, O Devī, one gains the fruit of the preparatory rite. Those who, ever joined in this way, worship you in meditation, O Maheśānī, have fulfilled their purpose on the road of death and saṁsāra.
tel: ఓ దేవీ, సహస్రావర్తనముచే పురశ్చర్యాఫలమును పొందును. ఈ విధముగా ఎల్లప్పుడు యుక్తులై నిన్ను ధ్యానించుచు ఉపాసించువారు, ఓ మహేశానీ, మృత్యుసంసారమార్గమునుండి కృతార్థులగుదురు.
hi: हे देवि! एक हजार पाठसे पुरश्चरणका फल प्राप्त होता है। इस प्रकार जो निरन्तर युक्त होकर ध्यानपूर्वक तुम्हारी उपासना करते हैं, हे महेशानि! वे मृत्युरूप संसारमार्गसे कृतार्थ हो जाते हैं।
