Title: Śrī Tripurabhairavī Aṣṭottaraśatanāma Stotram
Devanāgarī: श्रीत्रिपुरभैरवी-अष्टोत्तरशतनामस्तोत्रम्
Telugu: శ్రీ త్రిపురభైరవీ అష్టోత్తరశతనామ స్తోత్రం

Author: The colophon places it in the **Śāktapramoda**. The Devī asks Śiva on Kailāsa for Bhairavī's hundred and eight names, which she has "not heard", and **Śiva** gives them.

Source / recension: Primary text: **Gītā Press, Gorakhpur — Śatanāma Stotra Saṅgraha**, code 1850, pp. 125–129 (stotra pp. 125–127, nāmāvalī pp. 128–129; PDF pp. 126–130), transcribed from the page images of the printed edition. Entry 23 of the volume's forty. **Collated against sanskritdocuments.org `bhairavyaShTottarashatanAmastotra.itx`** (encoded and proofread by Gopal Upadhyay; titled there "athavā śrītripurabhairavī aṣṭottaraśatanāmastotram"), an independent witness of the **same verse text**, with the same Devī–Śiva frame and the same opening (`कैलासवासिन् भगवन्`). sanskritdocuments' `bhairavyaShTottarashatanAmastotram2` is from the Kālīvilāsa Tantra and is a different work. Stotra and nāmāvalī are paired in one file as the publisher prints them, and were checked against each other name by name.

Recension note: **Gītā Press followed; the sanskritdocuments witness (SD) is recorded, not adopted.** **SD HAS THREE MORE VERSES**, vv. 21–23: a Kaula phalaśruti directing worship "with the five m's" (`देवीं ध्यात्वा सुरां पीत्वा मकारपञ्चकैः प्रिये`), a promise of becoming "equal to Gaṇanātha within six months", and a prohibition on giving the text to others' disciples or to scoffers. **Gītā Press ends at v. 20** and prints none of them. They are recorded here and not added. SD also has no colophon source (it has only `iti śrībhairavyaṣṭottaraśatanāmastotraṁ sampūrṇam`), where Gītā Press names the Śāktapramoda. Differences within the shared text (Gītā Press → SD): v. 4 `आर्या` → `kāryyā`; v. 5 `मालती माला` (two names in the Gītā Press nāmāvalī, nos. 13–14) → `mālatīmālā` (one compound, "garland of jasmine"); v. 15 `महोन्मत्ता` → `mahottamā`; v. 16 `क्वणन्नूपुरधारिणी` → `kaṅkaṇannūpuradhāriṇī` ("wearing bracelets and anklets"). **Stotra and nāmāvalī agree throughout.** **The frame runs across a verse boundary:** the Devī's request ends in the first line of v. 2, and Śiva's reply begins in its second (`श्रीशिव उवाच` printed mid-verse); the block is kept whole as printed. **Prefix.** Gītā Press prints `ॐ` before each name; this corpus prints `श्री` for every nāmāvalī without exception (user, 2026-09-08). **Cross-reference:** `devi/dasamahavidya/13_tripura_bhairavi_stotram.txt`, `14_tripura_bhairavi_kavacam.txt` and `28_tripura_bhairavi_hridayam.txt` are separate texts, not this one.

Blurb: Bhairavī, bright as a thousand rising suns and garlanded with heads, named by her wrath: red-eyed with anger, pot-bellied, long-eared, long-fanged, long-nosed, laughing in peals, with anklets ringing. But she is also Devakī and the mother of the gods, beloved of Dāmodara and Keśava, the milk-ocean's daughter, and the girl — and she both gives delusion and takes it away.
