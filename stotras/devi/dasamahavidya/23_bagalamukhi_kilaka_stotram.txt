Title: Śrī Bagalāmukhī Kīlaka Stotram
Devanāgarī: श्री बगलामुखी कीलक स्तोत्रम्

Author: Anonymous. No ṛṣi or source-text is named in the colophon, which reads simply "iti śrī bagalāmukhī kīlaka stotram"; the piece belongs to the tantric Bagalāmukhī cycle.

Source / recension: stotranidhi.com, Devanāgarī page (stotranidhi.com/hi/sri-bagalamukhi-keelaka-stotram-in-sanskrit/) cross-checked verse-by-verse against the IAST page (stotranidhi.com/en/sri-bagalamukhi-keelaka-stotram-in-english/). Verse count: 6

Sections:
స్తోత్రం — Stotram (vv. 1–5)

Blurb: A kīlaka — literally a "pin" or "wedge," the short text recited to unlock a mantra whose power is otherwise held sealed. What makes this one unusual is that it is not a hymn with seed-syllables inserted but a hymn generated out of them: each bīja is immediately answered by a vocative built on the same sound, so that hlīṁ produces "you whose voice is the hlīṁ-sound," jrauṁ produces jātarūpe ("golden-formed"), ṭhaṁ produces ṭhaṅkāraveśe, and ḍāṁ pulls in both the Ḍākinīs and the drumbeat ḍimaka-ḍima-ḍimaṁ of the ḍamaru. The five long sragdharā-type stanzas are therefore as much sound-architecture as description, and the closing mālinī verse steps out of that texture entirely to promise the reciter pre-eminence among debaters.

deva:

Hasrauṁ, hasrauṁ — O you whose own nature is that sound, delighting in the field of battle, of divine form, self-existent. Jrauṁ, jrauṁ, jrauṁ — O golden-formed one, strike down, strike down my misfortune, O you of Jambha's might and of majesty. O Kālī of the skeletal form, crusher of the enemy folk, grant me the supreme attainment.

hi: क्रौं, क्रौं — ईश-रूपा, शत्रु-कुल का हनन करने वाली, देह में गड़ी हुई कील, हे कपालधारिणी। हस्रौं, हस्रौं — उसी नाद का स्वरूप धारण करने वाली, समर में रत, दिव्यरूपा, स्वरूपा। ज्रौं, ज्रौं, ज्रौं — हे स्वर्णरूपा, मेरे दुरित का नाश करो, नाश करो; हे जम्भरूपा, हे प्रभावशालिनी। हे कंकालरूपा काली, शत्रुजनों का दलन करने वाली, मुझे परम सिद्धि प्रदान करो।

--- verse 5 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
वाणीं व्याख्यानदात्रीं रिपुमुखखनने वेदशास्त्रार्थपूतां
मातः श्रीबगले परात्परतरे वादे विवादे जयम् ।
देहि त्वं शरणागतोऽस्मि विमले देवि प्रचण्डोद्धृते ।
माङ्गल्यं वसुधासु देहि सततं सर्वस्वरूपे शिवे ॥
iast:
vāṇīṁ vyākhyānadātrīṁ ripumukhakhanane vedaśāstrārthapūtāṁ
mātaḥ śrībagale parātparatare vāde vivāde jayam |
dehi tvaṁ śaraṇāgato'smi vimale devi pracaṇḍoddhṛte |

en: You are Speech herself, giver of exposition, purified by the meaning of Veda and śāstra, and you dig out the mouth of the foe. O Mother Śrī Bagalā, higher than the highest — grant me victory in argument and in dispute. I have come to your shelter, O stainless one, O Goddess of fierce and towering power. Grant me auspiciousness always upon this earth, O you whose form is all things, O Śivā.
tel: నీవే వాణివి, వ్యాఖ్యానాన్ని ప్రసాదించునదానివి, వేదశాస్త్రార్థంతో పవిత్రమైనదానివి, శత్రువు ముఖాన్ని త్రవ్వివేయునదానివి. ఓ తల్లీ శ్రీబగళా, పరాత్పరతరా — వాదంలో, వివాదంలో నాకు జయాన్ని ప్రసాదించు. ఓ విమలా, ప్రచండోద్ధృతా, దేవీ, నేను శరణాగతుడనైనాను. ఓ సర్వస్వరూపిణీ, శివా, ఈ భూమిపై నాకు ఎల్లప్పుడూ మంగళాన్ని ప్రసాదించు.

bhavati parameśo vādināmagragaṇyaḥ ||
en: O Goddess, whoever recites this kīlaka day after day and through the night — this text served by all the sages, which paralyzes every enemy, is here ever devoted to peace, and is the very heart-form of those who know — becomes a supreme lord, and is counted first among all who debate.
tel: ఓ దేవీ, సమస్త మునులచే సేవించబడునది, సర్వశత్రువులను స్తంభింపజేయునది, ఇచట నిత్యం శమమునందు నిరతమైనది, జ్ఞానుల హృదయస్వరూపమైనది అయిన ఈ కీలకాన్ని ప్రతిదినం, రాత్రియందు ఎవరు పఠిస్తారో — వారు పరమేశులై, వాదులందరిలో అగ్రగణ్యులవుతారు.
hi: हे देवि, समस्त मुनियों द्वारा सेवित, सब शत्रुओं को स्तंभित करने वाले, यहाँ नित्य शम में रत, ज्ञानियों के हृदय-स्वरूप इस कीलक का जो प्रतिदिन और रात्रि में पाठ करता है — वह परमेश हो जाता है और वादियों में अग्रगण्य गिना जाता है।

Recension note: Only one recension of this short text was available — stotranidhi's Devanāgarī and IAST pages, which are two script-renderings of a single underlying text and agreed at every syllable, bīja-strings included. The layout of daṇḍas on the source page is irregular in verses 3 and 5, where a single daṇḍa closes the third pāda as well as the second; this has been reproduced rather than regularised. Verse 2's opening cluster is printed as a single word krauṁmīśarūpe, which is read here as krauṁ (the third repetition of the bīja) followed by īśarūpe. The IAST page uses a right curly quote where the Devanāgarī has the doubled avagraha ऽऽ in duṣṭacittā''dalana (v. 3); the avagrahas are given here as apostrophes. The IAST page for this text already prints plain e and o rather than ē and ō, matching the conventions of this corpus; its decomposed ṛ has been given as precomposed ṛ.
