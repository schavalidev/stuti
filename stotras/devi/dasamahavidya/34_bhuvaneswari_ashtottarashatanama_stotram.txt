Blurb: On Kailāsa, Śiva is asked for a hymn greater than the thousand names. He gives the names of the queen of the worlds. He names her Māyā and the great knowledge. He names the goddesses of every family: Brahmāṇī, Vaiṣṇavī, Indrāṇī, Vārāhī and Caṇḍikā. He then names the world itself: water, sky and every element, the river Narmadā, the three twilights, and the lunar days one by one. The phalaśruti promises something to every station of life: sons to the childless, learning to the fool, and even a good end to the thief.

bālaravidyutimindukirīṭāṁ tuṅgakucāṁ nayanatrayayuktām |
smeramukhīṁ varadāṅkuśapāśābhītikarāṁ prabhaje bhuvaneśīm ||
en: I worship Bhuvaneśī, radiant as the rising sun, crowned with the moon, high-breasted and three-eyed, smiling, her hands showing boon, goad, noose and fearlessness.
tel: బాలసూర్యకాంతి గలది, చంద్రకిరీటము గలది, ఉన్నతకుచములు, మూడు నేత్రములు గలది, చిరునవ్వు ముఖము గలది, వరద అంకుశ పాశ అభయ హస్తములు గల భువనేశిని భజించుచున్నాను.
hi: मैं भुवनेश्वरीदेवीका ध्यान करता हूँ। उनके श्रीअंगोंकी आभा प्रभातकालके सूर्यके समान है और मस्तकपर चन्द्रमाका मुकुट है। वे उभरे हुए स्तनों और तीन नेत्रोंसे युक्त हैं। उनके मुखपर मुसकानकी छटा छायी रहती है और हाथोंमें वरद, अंकुश, पाश एवं अभय-मुद्रा शोभा पाते हैं।
