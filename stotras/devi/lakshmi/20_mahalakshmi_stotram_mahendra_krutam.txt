Title: Śrī Mahālakṣmī Stotram (Mahendra Kṛtam)
Devanāgarī: श्री महालक्ष्मी स्तोत्रम् (महेन्द्र कृतम्)
Telugu: శ్రీ మహాలక్ష్మీ స్తోత్రం (మహేంద్ర కృతం)

Author: Framed as composed and spoken by Mahendra (Indra), within the Nārada-Nārāyaṇa dialogue of the Prakṛti-khaṇḍa (chapter 39) of the Brahmavaivarta Purāṇa, per the text's own closing colophon. Distinct from this corpus's own Indra-attributed Śrī Mahālakṣmyaṣṭakam (file 15), a much shorter eight-verse hymn preserved separately in the Brahmāṇḍa Purāṇa — the two share only their attribution to Indra and their general subject, not a single verse.

Verse count: 29

Sections:
స్తుతిః — Stuti, Mahendra's chain of salutations and petitions to Mahālakṣmī (vv. 1–21)
కథాసమాప్తిః — the narrative frame's resolution: Mahendra's tearful bow, the gods' rites of propitiation, and Lakṣmī's and the gods' return to their places (vv. 22–26)
ఫలశ్రుతి — Phalaśruti, the fruits of recitation (vv. 27–29)

Blurb: This is a long chain of verses of salutation, in which Mahendra, who is Indra, identifies Mahālakṣmī with almost every form that prosperity can take. She is the Mahālakṣmī of Vaikuṇṭha, the Lakṣmī of the Ocean of Milk, the Lakṣmī of heaven in Indra's own house, the royal Lakṣmī of a king, and the household Lakṣmī of a householder. She is even Surabhi, the mother of cows, and Dakṣiṇā, the fee given at a sacrifice. The hymn then turns to direct and urgent petition: grant me kingship, grant me majesty, grant me strength. Verse 18 admits that we are without kin and reduced to beggars, stripped of every kind of prosperity. This places the hymn within the familiar purāṇic theme of Indra begging Lakṣmī's grace from a position of present loss. The same theme lies behind the much shorter Mahālakṣmyaṣṭakam attributed to Indra, which is file 15 in this corpus. Unusually for a stotra, this one closes with five verses of narrative resolution before its phalaśruti: Mahendra's tearful bow, the gods' rites of propitiation, and Lakṣmī's gift of a garland to Viṣṇu.

--- verse 14 | section: Stuti ---

--- verse 15 | section: Stuti ---

hi: शैशव में स्तनपान करते शिशुओं की जैसे माता होती है, वैसे ही तुम सदा समस्त विश्व में सबकी माता हो।

--- verse 16 | section: Stuti ---

--- verse 17 | section: Stuti ---

--- verse 18 | section: Stuti ---

--- verse 19 | section: Stuti ---

--- verse 24 | section: Kathāsamāptiḥ ---

--- verse 25 | section: Kathāsamāptiḥ ---

--- verse 29 | section: Phalaśruti ---
