Title: Śrī Sūktam
Devanāgarī: श्री सूक्तम्
Telugu: శ్రీ సూక్తం

Author: No human author — this is a Vedic khila (a supplementary hymn attached to the Ṛgveda, also transmitted independently within the Taittirīya Āraṇyaka of the Kṛṣṇa Yajurveda). It has no poet-signature or purāṇic frame-story; tradition holds it to have been "seen" (dṛṣṭa), not composed. The text's own closing portion names its own anukramaṇī (seer-and-deity note): "ānandaḥ kardamaścaiva ciklīta iti viśrutāḥ / ṛṣayaste trayaḥ putrāḥ svayaṁ śrīreva devatā" — "Ānanda, Kardama, and Ciklīta, thus known, are the [three] ṛṣis, themselves her sons; Śrī herself is the devatā." So the seers are Ānanda, Kardama, and Ciklīta (also addressed by name within the hymn, in the uttara-khila verses to Kardama and Ciklīta), the devatā is Śrī/Lakṣmī herself, and the chandas is mixed — most ṛcs are anuṣṭubh, with ṛcs 4-6 in a longer (triṣṭubh-type) meter.

Source / recension: Primary text is vignanam.org's śuddha-devanāgarī edition (vignanam.org/devanagari/sri-suktam.html), which explicitly states it prints "the right anusvaras marked" and carries full Vedic svara (pitch-accent) marks — udātta (॑, a mark above the syllable), anudātta (॒, a mark below), and the double-svarita (᳚) — throughout. Its parallel IAST edition (vignanam.org/english/sri-suktam.html) carries the same accents transliterated with combining diacritics, and was used to build the iast field below; its Telugu-script edition (vignanam.org/telugu/sri-suktam.html) was checked to confirm the same text and used to confirm the title. vignanam's IAST page uses a non-standard "ch" for च (e.g. "chandrāṁ" for candrām, "prachodayāt" for pracodayāt) and ē/ō macrons on every e/o; both have been normalized here to this corpus's plain c/e/o and ṁ conventions — the accent diacritics themselves were left untouched. Cross-checked word-for-word against stotranidhi.com's accented IAST edition (stotranidhi.com/en/sri-suktam-in-english/), which prints the identical set and order of verses (through ṛc 15, the japa-vidhi verse, the ṛṣi-verse, the phalaśruti stotra-verses, the Lakṣmī-gāyatrī, one closing verse, and the śānti formula), with only trivial wording variants (stotranidhi reads "padmāsane padma ūrū" where vignanam reads "padmānane padma ūrū" in the first phalaśruti verse; stotranidhi reads "svayaṁ śrīdevi devatā" in the ṛṣi-verse where vignanam reads "svayaṁ śrīreva devatā" — both noted, vignanam's reading followed since it is the primary text). Also cross-checked against sanskritdocuments.org's two Rigveda-khila files: doc_veda/s-sukta.itx (unaccented, titled "mantrapuṣpam and others") and doc_veda/s-sukta-accent.itx (accented, titled "śrīsūktopaniṣad", transliterated by Avinash Sathaye and Anant Upadhyayula). Both sanskritdocuments files print a FULLER text than vignanam/stotranidhi: after the "sarvamaṅgala māṅgalye" verse they add five more units before reaching the Lakṣmī-gāyatrī and closing material — a "sarasijanilaye sarojahaste..." verse (shared with the Kanakadhārā-stotra tradition), a "viṣṇupatnīṁ kṣamāṁ devīm mādhavīm mādhavapriyām" salutation verse, a second Lakṣmī-gāyatrī variant ("mahālakṣmī ca vidmahe viṣṇupatnī ca dhīmahi"), an apotropaic ṛṇa-roga-dāridrya verse ("ṛṇarogādidāridrya-pāpakṣudapamṛtyavaḥ..."), the genuine khila verse "śriye jātā śriya āniryāyā..." (also known from the Taittirīya tradition), and a closing brāhmaṇa-style prose passage ("śriya evainaṁ tacchriyām ādadhāti..."), before repeating the same Lakṣmī-gāyatrī vignanam prints and the same śānti close. This fuller sanskritdocuments text is NOT reproduced in the file below — since vignanam.org is this corpus's designated primary source and its own print stops at the "śrīrvarcasyam..." verse followed directly by the śānti formula, this file follows vignanam's own scope exactly rather than silently merging in the extra sanskritdocuments material. greenmesg.org's unaccented edition (greenmesg.org/stotras/lakshmi/sri_suktam.php) was also checked and confirms the same verse content and order as vignanam/stotranidhi through the "sarvamaṅgala māṅgalye" verse, but numbers the ṛṣi-verse and all the phalaśruti verses continuously as vv.17-31 rather than leaving them unnumbered — this file does not adopt that continuous numbering (see Verse count below). No verse here is unverified; every verse printed is attested identically (accents aside) across at least two of the four sources checked.

Verse count: 15 numbered core ṛcs (ṛc 1-15, the mūla-sūkta proper — self-confirmed by the text's own v.16 below, which calls itself a recitation of "pañcadaśarcam," fifteen ṛcs) plus one bracketed interpolated line after ṛc 9 ("śrīrme bhajatu, alakṣmīrme naśyatu") that vignanam itself sets off in brackets as a traditional insertion, not part of the ṛc count — plus 20 further units printed by vignanam.org after its own visual divider line, none of which are numbered by either vignanam or stotranidhi: the japa-vidhi verse (v.16, "yaḥ śuciḥ..."), the ṛṣi-identification verse, fourteen further phalaśruti-style stotra verses (mostly anuṣṭubh couplets, two in a longer meter), the Lakṣmī-Nārāyaṇī gāyatrī, one closing benedictory verse ("śrīrvarcasyam..."), and the closing triple śānti formula. Total: 15 numbered blocks + 21 "verse none" blocks (the bracketed interpolation plus the 20 uttara-khila units) = 36 blocks.

Blurb: This is a Vedic hymn of invocation. It calls Śrī, who is Lakṣmī, by a sequence of concrete images. She is golden in hue and bright as the moon. She is heralded by elephants. She is throned upon the lotus. She is moist and fruitful like a field that has been manured. The hymn calls her for a stated purpose: to drive her elder sister Alakṣmī, who is misfortune, hunger and want, out of the reciter's house. A phalaśruti portion is appended. It contains further verses in praise of Lakṣmī's various forms, a gāyatrī of Lakṣmī-Nārāyaṇī, and a benediction for wealth and long life.

च॒न्द्रां हि॒रण्म॑यीं-लँ॒क्ष्मी-ञ्जात॑वेदो म॒माव॑ह ॥

en: O Jātavedas (Fire, the knower of all born things), bring to me that Lakṣmī who is golden-hued, tawny and graceful, garlanded with strings of gold and silver, moon-bright, and made of gold.

hi: हे जातवेदा (सभी जन्म लेने वालों के साक्षी अग्नि), स्वर्ण-वर्ण वाली, हरिणी के समान सुंदर, स्वर्ण-रजत की मालाओं से सुसज्जित, चंद्रमा के समान कांतिमान, स्वर्णमयी उस लक्ष्मी को मेरे लिए ले आओ।
