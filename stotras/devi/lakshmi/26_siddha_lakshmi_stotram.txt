Title: Śrī Siddhalakṣmī Stotram
Devanāgarī: श्री सिद्धलक्ष्मी स्तोत्रम्
Telugu: శ్రీ సిద్ధలక్ష్మీ స్తోత్రం

Author: No human author. The closing colophon places the text in the Brahma Purāṇa, spoken within a dialogue between Īśvara (Śiva) and Viṣṇu — "iti śrībrahmapurāṇe īśvaraviṣṇusaṁvāde śrī siddhalakṣmī stotram" — and v.22 confirms the ascription from inside the text itself: "īśvareṇa kṛtaṁ stotram," the stotra was made by Īśvara. The viniyoga names Hiraṇyagarbha as the ṛṣi of the mantra, anuṣṭubh as its metre, and Mahākālī, Mahālakṣmī and Mahāsarasvatī jointly as its presiding deities — the Devī Māhātmya's three-goddess scheme, applied here to Lakṣmī.

Blurb: This is a hymn that praises a goddess by praising the sounds she is made of. Its central movement (vv. 3-12) walks through Oṁ, Śrīṁ, Hrīṁ and Klīṁ one at a time, hailing each syllable as a form of Lakṣmī and then naming which Lakṣmī it is — Oṁ is Siddha-, Mokṣa- and Ādyalakṣmī; Śrīṁ is Satyalakṣmī; Hrīṁ is Bhogalakṣmī; Klīṁ is the restless Cañcalā and Kātyāyanī — before v.6 takes Oṁ apart into A, U and M and assigns Lakṣmī, Viṣṇu and the unmanifest Puruṣa to the three letters. Two things are worth noticing: the goddess meditated on in v.2 is not the seated queen of most Lakṣmī hymns but a bālakumārikā, a young girl, six-armed and four-faced and armed with sword and trident; and v.13 is the celebrated "sarvamaṅgalamāṅgalye" of the Devī Māhātmya, quoted here verbatim, which shows how thoroughly this Lakṣmī has been read through the Durgā tradition.

en: Of this Śrī Siddhalakṣmī stotra-mantra, Hiraṇyagarbha is the seer, anuṣṭubh the metre, and Śrī Mahākālī, Mahālakṣmī and Mahāsarasvatī the deities. Śrīṁ is the seed-syllable, hrīṁ the power, klīṁ the pin that fastens it. Its application is in the recitation of the Śrī Siddhilakṣmī Stotra, for the removal of all my afflictions and torments, for the destruction of all sorrow and poverty, and for the accomplishment of every undertaking. Both editions spell the title siddhi-lakṣmī here while the heading and colophon read siddha-lakṣmī; the inconsistency is in the source and is left as printed.

en: The placing of the seer and the rest: salutations to the seer Hiraṇyagarbha — on the head; to the anuṣṭubh metre — on the mouth; to the deities Mahākālī, Mahālakṣmī and Mahāsarasvatī — on the heart; to śrīṁ the seed — at the generative centre; to hrīṁ the power — at the feet; to klīṁ the pin — at the navel; to the application — on all the limbs. The placing on the hands assigns six mantra-names to the six parts of the hands, from thumbs to palms and backs. The placing on the body assigns the same six to heart, head, tuft, armour, three eyes and weapon, with their proper ritual exclamations (svāhā, vaṣaṭ, hum, vauṣaṭ, phaṭ). Then, with "oṁ śrīṁ hrīṁ klīṁ śrīṁ, salutations to Siddhalakṣmī," the directions are sealed (digbandha). This block is ritual procedure rather than praise, and is reproduced as printed for completeness rather than paraphrased away; a reader may go straight to the dhyāna at verse 1.

ओं श्रीं सिद्धलक्ष्म्यै हृदयाय नमः ।
ओं ह्रीं विष्णुतेजसे शिरसे स्वाहा ।
ओं क्लीं अमृतानन्दायै शिखायै वषट् ।
ओं श्रीं दैत्यमालिन्यै कवचाय हुम् ।
ओं ह्रीं तेजः प्रकाशिन्यै नेत्रत्रयाय वौषट् ।
ओं क्लीं ब्राह्म्यै वैष्णव्यै रुद्राण्यै अस्त्राय फट् ॥
ओं श्रीं ह्रीं क्लीं श्रीं सिद्धलक्ष्म्यै नमः इति दिग्विमोकः ॥
iast:
uttaranyāsaḥ –
oṁ śrīṁ siddhalakṣmyai hṛdayāya namaḥ |
oṁ hrīṁ viṣṇutejase śirase svāhā |
oṁ klīṁ amṛtānandāyai śikhāyai vaṣaṭ |
oṁ śrīṁ daityamālinyai kavacāya hum |
oṁ hrīṁ tejaḥ prakāśinyai netratrayāya vauṣaṭ |
oṁ klīṁ brāhmyai vaiṣṇavyai rudrāṇyai astrāya phaṭ ||

en: The closing placement: the same six assignments to heart, head, tuft, armour, three eyes and weapon are repeated after the recitation, and then, with "oṁ śrīṁ hrīṁ klīṁ śrīṁ, salutations to Siddhalakṣmī," the directions that were sealed before the recitation are released (digvimoka). The rite is thus symmetrically closed: what was bound at the start is loosed at the end.
tel: ఉత్తరన్యాసము: పఠనానంతరము అవే ఆరు న్యాసములను హృదయ-శిరో-శిఖా-కవచ-నేత్రత్రయ-అస్త్రములయందు మరల చేసి, పిమ్మట "ఓం శ్రీం హ్రీం క్లీం శ్రీం సిద్ధలక్ష్మ్యై నమః" అని పూర్వము బంధించిన దిక్కులను విడిచిపెట్టుట (దిగ్విమోకము). ఆరంభమున బంధించినదానిని అంతమున విడుచుట — ఇట్లు క్రియ సమతుల్యముగా ముగియును.
