Title: Mahālakṣmyaṣṭakam
Devanāgarī: महालक्ष्म्यष्टकम्
Telugu: మహాలక్ష్మ్యష్టకం

Author: Traditionally spoken by Indra — the text opens "indra uvāca" ("Indra said") and its own closing colophon names him as composer: "iti śrī indrakṛtaṁ mahālakṣmyaṣṭakastotraṁ sampūrṇam," "thus ends the Mahālakṣmyaṣṭaka composed by Śrī Indra." It is popularly described as belonging to the Padma Purāṇa / Skanda Purāṇa stotra literature, though none of the editions collated here print a purāṇa-chapter citation alongside the text itself, and no separate viniyoga or nyāsa apparatus is attached to it in any source consulted.

Source / recension: Base text is vignanam.org's edition of "Maha Lakshmi Ashtakam," collated across its Devanāgarī, Telugu (saraḷa) and English (IAST) pages, which agree with one another verse for verse. Cross-checked against sanskritdocuments.org's doc_devii/mahaalakshmyashhTakaM.html (encoded by Aikya Param, fetched via the browser tools), which prints a sārtha (with-English-meaning) recension and, appended beneath it, a second recension titled "atha mahālakṣmyaṣṭakam" (colophon: "iti indrakṛtaṁ śrī mahālakṣmīstavam"); and against stotranidhi.com's "Sri Mahalakshmi Ashtakam" page (fetched by curl with a browser user-agent, since the site blocks WebFetch/browser navigation). All three sources independently agree on the same eight verses and the same three phalaśruti couplets in the same order. Wording differs only in a handful of minor points, given in the recension note below; three evident typesetting artifacts in the vignanam.org text (a misplaced space, a stray hyphen, and a dropped syllable) have been silently corrected against the concurring sanskritdocuments.org/stotranidhi.com reading and are also logged below.

Verse count: 8 numbered verses (the aṣṭaka proper), followed by 3 unnumbered phalaśruti couplets and a one-line prose colophon — none of which the base text (vignanam.org) numbers. Two of the three cross-check sources (sanskritdocuments.org's second appended recension, and stotranidhi.com) do number the phalaśruti couplets as verses 9–11, continuing the sequence; this file follows the base text's own unnumbered presentation of that material rather than silently adopting the alternate numbering — see the recension note.

Sections:
స్తోత్రం — Stotram (इन्द्र उवाच, the eight namaste-refrain verses proper)
ఫలశ్రుతి — Phalaśruti (three couplets on the fruit of reciting once/twice/thrice daily)
సమాప్తి — Samāpti (one-line closing colophon naming Indra as composer)

Blurb: This hymn has eight verses and a refrain of salutation. In it Indra addresses Mahālakṣmī through an unbroken chain of epithets. She is Mahāmāyā enthroned upon the Śrīpīṭha. She is mounted on Garuḍa and is the terror of the demon Kolāsura. She is the beginningless Ādiśakti, born of yoga. She is seated upon the lotus and is the very embodiment of Brahman. Every verse closes with the same refrain, 'mahālakṣmi namo'stu te'. A phalaśruti is then added. It promises increasing fruit according to the number of recitations each day: the destruction of sin for one recitation, wealth and grain for two, and the destruction of enemies for three.

--- verse 1 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
नमस्तेऽस्तु महामाये श्रीपीठे सुरपूजिते ।
शङ्खचक्र गदाहस्ते महालक्ष्मि नमोऽस्तु ते ॥
iast:
namaste'stu mahāmāye śrīpīṭhe surapūjite |
śaṅkhacakra gadāhaste mahālakṣmi namo'stu te ||
en: Salutations to you, Mahāmāyā — the great cosmic power of illusion — enthroned as Śrīpīṭha, the seat and source of Śrī (fortune) itself, and worshipped by the suras (gods); holding the conch, discus and mace in your hands, Mahālakṣmī, salutations to you.
tel: సకల ఐశ్వర్యానికి మూలమైన శ్రీపీఠంపై ఆసీనురాలవై, దేవతలచే పూజింపబడే మహామాయా (జగత్తుకు మూలమైన మాయాశక్తి) స్వరూపమా, నీకు నమస్కారం; శంఖము, చక్రము, గదను చేతుల్లో ధరించిన మహాలక్ష్మీ, నీకు నమస్కారం.
hi: समस्त ऐश्वर्य के आधार श्रीपीठ पर विराजमान, देवताओं द्वारा पूजित महामाया-स्वरूपिणी देवी, तुम्हें नमस्कार है; शंख, चक्र और गदा हाथों में धारण करने वाली महालक्ष्मी, तुम्हें नमस्कार है।

--- verse 2 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
नमस्ते गरुडारूढे कोलासुर भयङ्करि ।
सर्वपापहरे देवि महालक्ष्मि नमोऽस्तु ते ॥
iast:
namaste garuḍārūḍhe kolāsura bhayaṅkari |
sarvapāpahare devi mahālakṣmi namo'stu te ||
en: Salutations to you, mounted on Garuḍa, terror of the demon Kolāsura; O Goddess who removes every sin, Mahālakṣmī, salutations to you.
tel: గరుడవాహనంపై ఆసీనురాలవై, కోలాసురుడనే రాక్షసునికి భయంకరిగా ఉండే దేవీ, సకల పాపాలను హరించే మహాలక్ష్మీ, నీకు నమస్కారం.
hi: गरुड़ पर आरूढ़, कोलासुर नामक असुर के लिए भयंकरी, समस्त पापों का नाश करने वाली देवी महालक्ष्मी, तुम्हें नमस्कार है।

--- verse 3 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
सर्वज्ञे सर्ववरदे सर्व दुष्ट भयङ्करि ।
सर्वदुःख हरे देवि महालक्ष्मि नमोऽस्तु ते ॥
iast:
sarvajñe sarvavarade sarva duṣṭa bhayaṅkari |
sarvaduḥkha hare devi mahālakṣmi namo'stu te ||
en: All-knowing, granter of every boon, terror to every wicked being, remover of every sorrow, O Goddess, Mahālakṣmī, salutations to you.
tel: సర్వజ్ఞురాలవు, అందరికీ వరాలను ఇచ్చేదానివి, సకల దుష్టులకు భయంకరివి, సకల దుఃఖాలను హరించే దేవీ, మహాలక్ష్మీ, నీకు నమస్కారం.
hi: सर्वज्ञ, सबको वर देने वाली, समस्त दुष्टों के लिए भयंकरी, समस्त दुःखों का नाश करने वाली देवी महालक्ष्मी, तुम्हें नमस्कार है।

--- verse 4 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
सिद्धि बुद्धि प्रदे देवि भुक्ति मुक्ति प्रदायिनि ।
मन्त्र मूर्ते सदा देवि महालक्ष्मि नमोऽस्तु ते ॥
iast:
siddhi buddhi prade devi bhukti mukti pradāyini |
mantra mūrte sadā devi mahālakṣmi namo'stu te ||
en: O Goddess who grants siddhi (spiritual accomplishment) and buddhi (understanding), giver of both bhukti (worldly enjoyment) and mukti (liberation), ever the very embodiment of mantra itself, Mahālakṣmī, salutations to you. Sanskritdocuments.org records an alternate reading "mantrapūte" ("purified by mantra") in place of "mantramūrte" here.
tel: సిద్ధి బుద్ధులను ప్రసాదించే దేవీ, భుక్తి ముక్తులను అనుగ్రహించే తల్లీ, ఎల్లప్పుడూ మంత్రస్వరూపిణివైన దేవీ, మహాలక్ష్మీ, నీకు నమస్కారం.
hi: सिद्धि और बुद्धि प्रदान करने वाली देवी, भुक्ति और मुक्ति देने वाली, सदा मंत्र-स्वरूपिणी देवी महालक्ष्मी, तुम्हें नमस्कार है।

--- verse 5 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
आद्यन्त रहिते देवि आदिशक्ति महेश्वरि ।
योगज्ञे योग सम्भूते महालक्ष्मि नमोऽस्तु ते ॥
iast:
ādyanta rahite devi ādiśakti maheśvari |
yogajñe yoga sambhūte mahālakṣmi namo'stu te ||
en: O Goddess without beginning or end, the primordial power (Ādiśakti), the great sovereign Maheśvarī; knower of yoga (yogajñe) and born of yoga itself, Mahālakṣmī, salutations to you. sanskritdocuments.org and stotranidhi.com both read "ādyaśakti" for "ādiśakti" and "yogaje" ("born of yoga," redundant with the following "yoga sambhūte") for "yogajñe" ("knower of yoga") — vignanam.org's "yogajñe" is followed here as the semantically sharper reading, with the variant flagged.
tel: ఆద్యంతరహితురాలవైన దేవీ, ఆదిశక్తివి, మహేశ్వరివి; యోగాన్ని ఎరిగినదానివి, యోగం నుండి ఆవిర్భవించినదానివి, మహాలక్ష్మీ, నీకు నమస్కారం.
hi: आदि-अंत रहित देवी, आदिशक्ति, महेश्वरी; योग को जानने वाली और योग से उत्पन्न होने वाली देवी महालक्ष्मी, तुम्हें नमस्कार है।

--- verse 6 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
स्थूल सूक्ष्म महारौद्रे महाशक्ति महोदरे ।
महा पाप हरे देवि महालक्ष्मि नमोऽस्तु ते ॥
iast:
sthūla sūkṣma mahāraudre mahāśakti mahodare |
mahā pāpa hare devi mahālakṣmi namo'stu te ||
en: Fierce (mahāraudre) in both gross and subtle forms, great power, vast-wombed one (mahodare — she who holds the whole universe within her), remover of great sin, O Goddess, Mahālakṣmī, salutations to you.
tel: స్థూల సూక్ష్మ రూపాలలో మహారౌద్రివి, మహాశక్తివి, విశ్వాన్ని తనలో దాచుకున్న మహోదరివి, మహాపాపాలను హరించే దేవీ, మహాలక్ష్మీ, నీకు నమస్కారం.
hi: स्थूल और सूक्ष्म रूपों में महारौद्री, महाशक्ति, संपूर्ण विश्व को अपने भीतर धारण करने वाली महोदरी, महापापों का नाश करने वाली देवी महालक्ष्मी, तुम्हें नमस्कार है।

--- verse 7 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
पद्मासन स्थिते देवि परब्रह्म स्वरूपिणि ।
परमेशि जगन्मातः महालक्ष्मि नमोऽस्तु ते ॥
iast:
padmāsana sthite devi parabrahma svarūpiṇi |
parameśi jaganmātaḥ mahālakṣmi namo'stu te ||
en: Seated on the lotus-throne, O Goddess whose true nature is the Supreme Brahman itself, supreme sovereign mistress, mother of the universe, Mahālakṣmī, salutations to you.
tel: పద్మాసనంపై ఆసీనురాలవైన దేవీ, పరబ్రహ్మ స్వరూపిణివి, పరమేశ్వరివి, జగన్మాతా, మహాలక్ష్మీ, నీకు నమస్కారం.
hi: पद्मासन पर विराजमान देवी, परब्रह्म-स्वरूपिणी, परमेश्वरी, जगन्माता महालक्ष्मी, तुम्हें नमस्कार है।

--- verse 8 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
श्वेताम्बरधरे देवि नानालङ्कार भूषिते ।
जगस्थिते जगन्मातः महालक्ष्मि नमोऽस्तु ते ॥
iast:
śvetāmbaradhare devi nānālaṅkāra bhūṣite |
jagasthite jaganmātaḥ mahālakṣmi namo'stu te ||
en: Clad in white garments, O Goddess adorned with manifold ornaments, abiding within the universe, mother of the universe, Mahālakṣmī, salutations to you. sanskritdocuments.org and stotranidhi.com both read "jagatsthite" for "jagasthite" here — the same sense ("abiding in the world"), with the consonant of "jagat" retained before the following word.
tel: శ్వేతవస్త్రాలను ధరించిన దేవీ, నానావిధ ఆభరణాలతో అలంకరింపబడినదానివి, జగత్తులో వ్యాపించి ఉన్నదానివి, జగన్మాతా, మహాలక్ష్మీ, నీకు నమస్కారం.
hi: श्वेत वस्त्र धारण करने वाली देवी, नाना प्रकार के आभूषणों से सुशोभित, संपूर्ण जगत् में व्याप्त, जगन्माता महालक्ष्मी, तुम्हें नमस्कार है।

--- verse none | section: Phalaśruti ---
deva:
महालक्ष्म्यष्टकं स्तोत्रं यः पठेद् भक्तिमान् नरः ।
सर्वसिद्धिमवाप्नोति राज्यं प्राप्नोति सर्वदा ॥
iast:
mahālakṣmyaṣṭakaṁ stotraṁ yaḥ paṭhed bhaktimān naraḥ |
sarvasiddhimavāpnoti rājyaṁ prāpnoti sarvadā ||
en: Whatever devoted person recites this eight-verse hymn, the Mahālakṣmyaṣṭaka, attains every siddhi (accomplishment) and forever obtains sovereignty (rājya).
tel: భక్తితో ఈ మహాలక్ష్మ్యష్టక స్తోత్రాన్ని పఠించే నరుడు సర్వసిద్ధులను పొందుతాడు, ఎల్లప్పుడూ రాజ్యాన్ని పొందుతాడు.
hi: जो भक्तिमान् पुरुष इस महालक्ष्म्यष्टक स्तोत्र का पाठ करता है, वह सर्व सिद्धियाँ प्राप्त करता है और सदा राज्य प्राप्त करता है।

--- verse none | section: Phalaśruti ---
deva:
एककाले पठेन्नित्यं महापाप विनाशनम् ।
द्विकालं यः पठेन्नित्यं धन धान्य समन्वितः ॥
iast:
ekakāle paṭhennityaṁ mahāpāpa vināśanam |
dvikālaṁ yaḥ paṭhennityaṁ dhana dhānya samanvitaḥ ||
en: One who recites it once a day constantly destroys great sin (mahāpāpa); one who recites it twice a day is ever endowed with wealth and grain (dhanadhānya).
tel: రోజుకు ఒక్కసారి పఠించేవాడు నిత్యం మహాపాపాలను నశింపజేసుకుంటాడు; రోజుకు రెండుసార్లు పఠించేవాడు ధనధాన్యాలతో నిండి ఉంటాడు.
hi: जो प्रतिदिन एक बार पाठ करता है, वह नित्य महापाप का नाश करता है; जो प्रतिदिन दो बार पाठ करता है, वह सदा धन-धान्य से युक्त रहता है।

--- verse none | section: Phalaśruti ---
deva:
त्रिकालं यः पठेन्नित्यं महाशत्रु विनाशनम् ।
महालक्ष्मीर्भवेन्नित्यं प्रसन्ना वरदा शुभा ॥
iast:
trikālaṁ yaḥ paṭhennityaṁ mahāśatru vināśanam |
mahālakṣmīrbhavennityaṁ prasannā varadā śubhā ||
en: One who recites it three times a day constantly destroys great enemies, and Mahālakṣmī — auspicious and boon-granting — becomes ever pleased with him.
tel: రోజుకు మూడుసార్లు పఠించేవాడు నిత్యం మహాశత్రువులను నశింపజేసుకుంటాడు; శుభప్రదాయిని, వరదాయిని అయిన మహాలక్ష్మి అతనిపై ఎల్లప్పుడూ ప్రసన్నురాలవుతుంది.
hi: जो प्रतिदिन तीन बार पाठ करता है, वह नित्य महाशत्रुओं का नाश करता है; शुभ, वरदायिनी महालक्ष्मी सदा उस पर प्रसन्न रहती हैं।

--- verse none | section: Colophon ---
deva:
इति श्री इन्द्रकृतं महालक्ष्म्यष्टकस्तोत्रं सम्पूर्णम् ।
iast:
iti śrī indrakṛtaṁ mahālakṣmyaṣṭakastotraṁ sampūrṇam |
en: Thus ends the Mahālakṣmyaṣṭaka-stotram composed by Śrī Indra.
tel: ఇది శ్రీ ఇంద్రుడు రచించిన మహాలక్ష్మ్యష్టక స్తోత్రం సంపూర్ణం.
hi: इस प्रकार श्री इंद्र द्वारा रचित महालक्ष्म्यष्टक स्तोत्र सम्पूर्ण होता है।

Minor wording variants noted at the relevant verses above: v.4 mantramūrte/mantrapūte; v.5 ādiśakti/ādyaśakti and yogajñe/yogaje; v.8 jagasthite/jagatsthite (also v.7-8 jaganmātaḥ vs. the sandhi-joined jaganmātar- before the following word, a spacing/sandhi difference only, not a wording variant, and not separately flagged verse by verse). vignanam.org additionally prints several of its compound epithets (e.g. śaṅkhacakra gadāhaste, siddhi buddhi prade, sthūla sūkṣma mahāraudre) with spaces between components, where sanskritdocuments.org and stotranidhi.com print the same compounds solid; this is a legibility convention on vignanam's part, not a wording difference, and vignanam's own spacing is kept throughout this file for that reason.

Three further points in the vignanam.org source text were corrected here as evident typesetting artifacts, each confirmed against the sanskritdocuments.org/stotranidhi.com concurring reading rather than left as printed: (1) the phalaśruti's self-reference to the stotra's own title was printed "महालक्ष्मष्टकं" (missing the "य" that the correct sandhi of mahālakṣmī + aṣṭakam requires, and that the title itself carries at the head of the document) — corrected to "महालक्ष्म्यष्टकं"; (2) "सर्व सिद्धि मवाप्नोति" carried a stray space that split the accusative "सिद्धिम्" in the wrong place, stranding "म" against "अवाप्नोति" — corrected to the grammatically continuous "सर्वसिद्धिमवाप्नोति"; (3) "महालक्ष्मी र्भवेन्-नित्यं" carried a stray space and a stray hyphen — corrected to "महालक्ष्मीर्भवेन्नित्यं". None of these three affect meaning; all three are simply restored to the reading both cross-check sources already carry cleanly.

The base text (vignanam.org) leaves the three phalaśruti couplets and the closing colophon unnumbered, following straight on from verse 8. sanskritdocuments.org prints two recensions on the same page: the first (sārtha, with English meaning) also leaves the phalaśruti unnumbered, closing "iti indrakṛtaṁ mahālakṣmyaṣṭakaṁ sampūrṇam"; the second, appended below it under the heading "atha mahālakṣmyaṣṭakam," numbers the same three phalaśruti couplets as verses 9, 10 and 11 and closes with a different colophon, "iti indrakṛtaṁ śrī mahālakṣmīstavam." stotranidhi.com likewise numbers them 9-11. This file follows the base text's unnumbered presentation rather than silently adopting either alternate numbering, per the corpus convention of not inventing or erasing a source's own numbering choices. No viniyoga, nyāsa, dhyāna or pañcapūjā apparatus accompanies this stotra in any of the three sources consulted; the text runs directly from "indra uvāca" into the eight verses, which is why the section list above has only three members. No verse was left unverifiable in this case — all eight numbered verses and all three phalaśruti couplets are attested identically (modulo the noted minor variants) across all three independent sources.
