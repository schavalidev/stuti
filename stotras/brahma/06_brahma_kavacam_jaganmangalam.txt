Verse count: 10

Blurb: This is a short protective kavacam from the third ullāsa of the Mahānirvāṇa Tantra. Its own opening verse gives it the name Jaganmaṅgalam, which means the auspiciousness of the universe. It is not an armour in which many deities guard the limbs one by one. It is built almost entirely around the formless Brahman, described as paramātmā, cinmaya and sanātanam. That Brahman guards the reciter's head, heart, throat, face, hands and feet. The later verses turn from protection to method. The kavacam may be written on birch bark and worn as an amulet, encased in gold at the throat or upon the right arm, and the wearer is then said to become lord of all siddhis. The text then states that neither a written amulet nor spoken recitation matters as much as bhāva-śuddhi, which is purity of inner intention, in the worship of the Supreme. It closes by saying that sincerity of body, of speech or of mind is what truly completes this worship.

--- verse 1 ---
deva:
अस्य श्रीजगन्मङ्गलनामक कवचस्य सदाशिव ऋषिरनुष्टुप् छन्दः परब्रह्म देवता धर्मार्थकाममोक्षावाप्तये पाठे विनियोगः ॥
कवचं शृणु चार्वङ्गि जगन्मङ्गलनामकम् ।
पठनाद्धारणाद्यस्य ब्रह्मज्ञो जायते ध्रुवम् ॥
iast:
asya śrījaganmaṅgalanāmaka kavacasya sadāśiva ṛṣiranuṣṭup chandaḥ parabrahma devatā dharmārthakāmamokṣāvāptaye pāṭhe viniyogaḥ ||
kavacaṁ śṛṇu cārvaṅgi jaganmaṅgalanāmakam |
paṭhanāddhāraṇādyasya brahmajño jāyate dhruvam ||
en:
Of this armor named "Jaganmaṅgalam," Sadāśiva is the sage, anuṣṭubh is the meter, the supreme Brahman is the deity; its recitation is applied for the attainment of dharma, artha, kāma and mokṣa. Listen, O fair-limbed one, to this armor named Jaganmaṅgalam, by reading or wearing which one surely becomes a knower of Brahman.
tel:
ఈ శ్రీ జగన్మంగళనామక కవచమునకు సదాశివుడు ఋషి, అనుష్టుప్ ఛందస్సు, పరబ్రహ్మ దేవత; ధర్మార్థకామమోక్షముల ప్రాప్తికై పఠనమునందు వినియోగము. చారువంగీ, జగన్మంగళనామక ఈ కవచమును వినుము — దీనిని పఠించుటచే, ధరించుటచే ధ్రువముగా బ్రహ్మజ్ఞుడగును.
hi:
इस श्री जगन्मंगल नामक कवच के सदाशिव ऋषि हैं, अनुष्टुप् छंद है, परब्रह्म देवता है; धर्म-अर्थ-काम-मोक्ष की प्राप्ति के लिए पाठ में विनियोग है। हे चारुअंगी, जगन्मंगल नामक इस कवच को सुनो — जिसके पठन और धारण से निश्चय ही ब्रह्मज्ञ हो जाता है।

--- verse 2 ---
deva:
परमात्मा शिरः पातु हृदयं परमेश्वरः ।
कण्ठं पातु जगत्पाता वदनं सर्वदृग्विभुः ॥
iast:

en:
May the supreme self protect my head, may the supreme lord protect my heart; may the protector of the world protect my throat, may the all-seeing lord protect my face.
tel:
పరమాత్మ శిరస్సును రక్షించుగాక, పరమేశ్వరుడు హృదయమును రక్షించుగాక; జగత్పాత కంఠమును రక్షించుగాక, సర్వదృగ్విభుడు వదనమును రక్షించుగాక.
hi:
परमात्मा सिर की रक्षा करें, परमेश्वर हृदय की रक्षा करें; जगत्पाता कंठ की रक्षा करें, सर्वदृग्विभु मुख की रक्षा करें।
