Blurb: This is a short stuti in which the gods address Brahmā. They address him as the oṁkāra made manifest at the dawn of creation. They trace how he showed himself forth, from the cosmic egg to the parting of heaven and earth. They identify him with the principles of Sāṅkhya, that is, the seven subtle tattvas and the eighth which is their cause. They describe him as the single ground that runs through the gross and the subtle, and through the manifest and the unmanifest. The stuti ends with an appeal to him as refuge, as protector and as guardian.

संहारेच्छोस्ते नमो रुद्रमूर्ते ॥

en:
The Devas said: You are the oṁkāra, born as the very sprout of this universe — its own Self — prior to the beginning of its endless differentiation; and after it has come into being, O embodiment of pure existence, when you desire its dissolution, salutations to you in your Rudra-form.

ఆ స్థూలమైన పురాతనరూపముల ద్వారా, ఉద్భవించు జీవులందరి భూతభవిష్యత్తులుగా తెలియబడుచు, ఆయా భావములయందు ధ్యానింపబడుచు, వ్యక్తభావములను ఒక్కొక్కటిగా విడిచి నీవు వారితో ఐక్యమగుదువు. ఓ దేవా, భక్తులకు శరణ్యుడవైన అనంతమూర్తీ, నీవే మా రక్షకుడవు, పరిరక్షకుడవు కమ్ము.

उन स्थूल, पुरातन रूपों के द्वारा उत्पन्न होने वाले सभी प्राणियों के भूत और भविष्य के रूप में जाने जाकर, हर-हर भाव में ध्याए जाकर, व्यक्त भावों को एक-एक कर त्यागते हुए तुम उनसे सदा युक्त रहते हो। हे देव, भक्तों के शरण्य, अनंतमूर्ते, तुम ही हमारे त्राता और रक्षक बनो।
