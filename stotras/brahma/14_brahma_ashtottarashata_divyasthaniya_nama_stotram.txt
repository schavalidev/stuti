Title: Śrī Brahmā Aṣṭottaraśata Divyasthānīya Nāma Stotram
Devanāgarī: श्रीब्रह्मा-अष्टोत्तरशतदिव्यस्थानीयनामस्तोत्रम्
Telugu: శ్రీ బ్రహ్మా అష్టోత్తరశత దివ్యస్థానీయ నామ స్తోత్రం

Author: The colophon places it in the **Skanda Mahāpurāṇa, Prabhāsa Khaṇḍa**, and calls it spoken by **Mahādeva** (`महादेवप्रोक्तं`). The Wikisource witness gives the exact place: Skanda Purāṇa 7 (Prabhāsa Khaṇḍa), 1 (Prabhāsakṣetra Māhātmya), adhyāya 107, vv. 72–109, a chapter on the glory of Brahmā in the form of a child (`बालरूपी`) at Prabhāsa. The frame is layered. Śiva tells the Goddess (v. 1, `देवि`) what Brahmā once told Viṣṇu when Viṣṇu asked him (v. 5–6), and inside it Brahmā speaks to Viṣṇu as Madhusūdana and Kṛṣṇa (vv. 33–34).

Recension note: **Gītā Press followed; the Wikisource text (WS) is recorded, not adopted.** **Name count 100, not 108.** V. 32 says "a hundred and eight names", but Gītā Press's stotra, its nāmāvalī and WS all give a hundred places. **Differences of reading** (Gītā Press → WS): v. 12 `उड्डियाने देवकर्ता` (nāmāvalī no. 23) → `उच्छ्रियाणे देवकर्त्ता`; v. 13 `गोमदे स्थविराकार` ("at Gomada, the one in the form of an elder", no. 27) → `गोनर्दः स्थविराकारे` ("at Sthavirākāra, Gonarda"), where place and name are exchanged; v. 20 `मार्कण्डे च मधुप्रियः` (no. 54) → `मर्कटे च मधुप्रियः`; v. 21 `पम्पायां च सुदर्शनः` (no. 60) → `चंपायां सुरमर्दनः`. With WS's reading Campā would occur twice, since v. 26 (no. 80) has `चम्पायां गरुडप्रियः` in both. **Verse division.** Gītā Press prints v. 31 as a single line and v. 33 as three lines. WS runs the same words as regular couplets (WS 102–104), so the text is identical and only the numbering differs. **WS continues** after Gītā Press's last verse (WS 109). Vv. 110–119 cover the Kārtikī full moon at Prabhāsa and the Padmaka yoga, and Īśvara closes the chapter. Gītā Press does not print them. Smaller WS differences (orthographic or WS slips): v. 1 `ब्रह्मणः बालरूपिणः` (unsandhied); v. 5 `स्तोत्रमुत्त्मम्`; v. 17 `कौंकणे`; v. 33 `तेषामपि` (a syllable short); v. 36 `पुष्पोपहौरर्धूपैश्च`. **Stotra and nāmāvalī agree**, with these forms: v. 14 `विरञ्चिः` → no. 31 `विरञ्चये`; v. 30 `पापहा` → no. 95 `पापघ्ने`; v. 11 `कर्णाटस्य पुरे ब्रह्मा` → no. 19 `कर्णाटस्य पुरे ब्रह्मणे` (three words, kept as printed). **Form of the names.** Each name is a place in the locative followed by the form of Brahmā that dwells there in the dative, e.g. no. 1 `पुष्करे सुरश्रेष्ठाय`. The internal space is kept as printed. Many of the forms are other gods' names (Viṣṇu, Rāghava, Śaṅkara, Govinda, Vināyaka, Vāsuki). The hymn's point is that Brahmā is all of them. **Repeated forms at different places:** Suraśreṣṭha (nos. 1, 33), Janānanda (nos. 17, 42), Padmahasta (nos. 15, 36), Surapriya (nos. 8, 92), Govinda (nos. 39, 89), Mahādeva (nos. 29, 69), Sudarśana (nos. 60, 96). Arbuda occurs twice as a place (nos. 36, 49). **Place names** are transliterated as printed, not identified. **Prefix.** Gītā Press prints `ॐ` before each name; this corpus prints `श्री` for every nāmāvalī without exception (user, 2026-09-08). **Cross-reference:** the other files in `brahma/` are hymns *to* Brahmā and are different works. `shiva/33`, `devi/main/44` and `vishnu/29` are the companion divyasthāna lists in the same volume.

Blurb: Brahmā answers Viṣṇu's question "where should I think of you?" with a hundred places. He is the best of the gods at Puṣkara and a child at Prabhāsa; at Kāñcī he is dear to the people, in Kashmir he rides the swan, in Laṅkā he is Paulastya, in Pātāla Vāsuki, and in the sky the moon. Wherever the list is recited, he says, he is present at the three junctures of the day.

prabhāsakṣetramāhātmyaṁ brahmaṇo bālarūpiṇaḥ || 1 ||
en: Thus, Goddess, I have told you the excellent glory of the worship, and the glory of the holy field of Prabhāsa, where Brahmā has the form of a child.
tel: దేవీ, ఇట్లు నీకు ఉత్తమమగు పూజామాహాత్మ్యమును, బాలరూపియగు బ్రహ్మ యొక్క ప్రభాసక్షేత్రమాహాత్మ్యమును చెప్పితిని.
hi: देवि! इस प्रकार मैंने तुमसे उत्तम पूजामाहात्म्य और बालरूपी ब्रह्माके प्रभासक्षेत्रका माहात्म्य कहा।
