Blurb: The Vaikhānasa tradition is the older of the two priestly systems that serve Viṣṇu temples in the south, the other being the Pāñcarātra. It traces itself to the sage Vikhanas, who is held to be Viṣṇu's own manifestation and the teacher of Bhṛgu, Marīci, Atri and Kaśyapa. This hymn of eight verses in the vasantatilakā metre is that tradition's portrait of its founder. What it shows most clearly is how completely it joins guru and deity. Vikhanas is the bee at Vāsudeva's feet in one line, and the form of the supreme Viṣṇu in the next. He is the beloved son of Lakṣmī's lord in verse 2, and the good son of Kamalā who grants the fruit in verse 9. Verse 8 carries the doctrinal weight. It points to the viṣṇubali rite of his own Sūtra, by which a Vaikhānasa is a Vaiṣṇava already in the womb. That is the claim on which the school's distinct identity rests.

Colophon (unnumbered): iti śrī vikhanasāṣṭakam || — "Thus the Śrī Vikhanasāṣṭakam."

Recension note: The hymn is in vasantatilakā throughout, including the phalaśruti. Two small points from the source have been handled as follows. In verse 2 both script versions print भपबन्ध / bhapabandha; no such word exists, and the sense plainly requires भवबन्ध / bhavabandha, "the bonds of worldly existence," which is how it is rendered above; the printed reading is preserved in the text. In verse 9 the source omits the daṇḍa at the end of the second line in both versions; it has been supplied. Verse 8 alludes to the viṣṇubali, the Vaikhānasa saṁskāra performed for the child while still in the womb, which is the basis of the school's claim that its practitioners are Vaiṣṇavas by birth rather than by later initiation — the point on which Vaikhānasa self-definition turns, and the reason the verse is phrased as an appeal to Vikhanas's own Sūtra as authority. Vālmīki in verse 1 and Bhṛgu in verse 4 place Vikhanas at the head of the sage-lineage; Bhṛgu, Marīci, Atri and Kaśyapa are traditionally counted as his four disciples, from whom the four branches of Vaikhānasa literature descend.
