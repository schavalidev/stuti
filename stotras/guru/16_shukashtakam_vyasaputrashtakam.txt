Verse count: 9 (8 verses of Advaitic description, each closing on the same refrain, plus a closing phalaśruti verse)

Blurb: This is a dense and purely philosophical aṣṭakam addressed to Śuka, the son of Vyāsa, as the pattern of jīvanmukti, that is, of liberation while living. It describes, through a series of comparisons, the state of one for whom the distinction between doer and deed, and between merit and sin, has simply ceased to exist. The comparisons are rivers merging into the ocean, gold returning to gold in fire, and salt dissolving in the sea. Every verse ends with the same question: 'nistraiguṇye pathi vicaratāṁ ko vidhiḥ ko niṣedhaḥ', meaning 'for one who walks the path beyond the three guṇas, what rule applies, and what prohibition?' A closing verse promises the reciter swift passage towards liberation.

deva:
सत्यं सत्यं परमममृतं शान्ति कल्याणहेतुं
मायारण्ये दहनममलं शान्तिनिर्वाणदीपम् ।
तेजोराशिं निगमसदनं व्यासपुत्राष्टकं यः
प्रातःकाले पठति सहसा याति निर्वाणमार्गम् ॥
iast:
satyaṁ satyaṁ paramamamṛtaṁ śānti kalyāṇahetuṁ
māyāraṇye dahanamamalaṁ śāntinirvāṇadīpam |
tejorāśiṁ nigamasadanaṁ vyāsaputrāṣṭakaṁ yaḥ
prātaḥkāle paṭhati sahasā yāti nirvāṇamārgam ||
en:
Whoever recites, at dawn, this Vyāsaputrāṣṭakam — true, utterly true, supremely immortal, the very cause of peace and auspiciousness, a stainless fire burning away the forest of māyā, a lamp of peace and liberation, a mass of splendor, the very dwelling-place of the Vedas — swiftly sets out upon the path to liberation.
tel:
సత్యమైనది, పరమామృతమైనది, శాంతికల్యాణహేతువైనది, మాయారణ్యమును దహించు నిర్మలాగ్నివంటిది, శాంతినిర్వాణదీపమువంటిది, తేజోరాశి, వేదనిలయమైన ఈ వ్యాసపుత్రాష్టకమును ఎవడు ప్రాతఃకాలమున పఠించునో, వాడు శీఘ్రముగా నిర్వాణమార్గమును పొందును.

सत्य, परम अमृत, शांति-कल्याण का हेतु, माया-वन को जलाने वाली निर्मल अग्नि, शांति-निर्वाण का दीपक, तेजोराशि, वेदों का निवासस्थान — इस व्यासपुत्राष्टक को जो प्रातःकाल पढ़ता है, वह शीघ्र ही निर्वाण-मार्ग को प्राप्त होता है।
