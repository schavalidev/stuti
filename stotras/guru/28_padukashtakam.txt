పాదుకాష్టకం — Pādukāṣṭakam, the meditation verses ending "prātar eva hi mānase gurupādukādvayam āśraye" (vv. 1–9)

Blurb: This is titled an aṣṭaka but is printed with nine verses, and it is unlike any other pādukā hymn in this section. Here the guru's two sandals are not an object of outward homage at all. They are the seat of the guru's feet within the practitioner's own subtle body. Every epithet is drawn from the vocabulary of inner yoga: nāda, bindu and kalā; the ten inner sounds; the anāhata; the cakrarāja; the five sheaths and three guṇas that the object of meditation is not; and the space behind the brows. The refrain fixes the practice in time and place: at dawn itself, within my mind, I take refuge in the pair of the guru's sandals. The text is dense, and in places it has come down damaged. The translations below stay deliberately close to the words, rather than smoothing over what is uncertain.

--- verse 1 | section: Pādukāṣṭakam ---
deva:
श्रीसमञ्चितमव्ययं परमप्रकाशमगोचरं
भेदवर्जितमप्रमेयमनन्तमुझ्झितकल्मषम् ।
निर्मलं निगमान्तमद्भुतमप्यतर्क्यमनुत्तमं
प्रातरेव हि मानसे गुरुपादुकाद्वयमाश्रये ॥
iast:
śrīsamañcitamavyayaṁ paramaprakāśamagocaraṁ
bhedavarjitamaprameyamanantamujhjhitakalmaṣam |
nirmalaṁ nigamāntamadbhutamapyatarkyamanuttamaṁ
prātareva hi mānase gurupādukādvayamāśraye ||
en:
Graced with Śrī, imperishable, the supreme light, beyond the reach of the senses; free of all division, immeasurable, without end, with every stain cast away; stainless, the very end of the Veda, wondrous, beyond reasoning, unsurpassed — at dawn itself, within my mind, I take refuge in the pair of the guru's sandals.
tel:
శ్రీచేత సమన్వితమైనది, అవ్యయమైనది, పరమప్రకాశమైనది, ఇంద్రియములకు అగోచరమైనది; భేదరహితమైనది, అప్రమేయమైనది, అనంతమైనది, కల్మషము తొలగినది; నిర్మలమైనది, నిగమాంతమైనది, అద్భుతమైనది, తర్కమునకు అందనిది, అనుత్తమమైనది — ప్రాతఃకాలముననే నా మనస్సునందు గురుపాదుకాద్వయమును నేను ఆశ్రయించుచున్నాను.

श्री से समन्वित, अव्यय, परम प्रकाश, इंद्रियों से अगोचर; भेदरहित, अप्रमेय, अनंत, कल्मष से रहित; निर्मल, निगमांत, अद्भुत, अतर्क्य, अनुत्तम — प्रातःकाल में ही अपने मन में मैं गुरु-पादुका-युगल की शरण लेता हूँ।

--- verse 2 | section: Pādukāṣṭakam ---
deva:

मन्त्रराजपराजितं निजमण्डलान्तरभासितम् ।
पञ्चवर्णमखण्डमद्भुतमादिकारणमच्युतं

Colophon (unnumbered): iti pādukāṣṭakam | — "Thus the Pādukāṣṭakam."

Recension note: Nine verses are printed despite the title "aṣṭakam," which is a common enough discrepancy in this genre. The metre runs eighteen syllables to the pāda, breaking regularly into a phrase of eight and a phrase of ten; it is not one of the standard named akṣara metres of the stotra repertory and has not been identified here. The text is difficult, and several readings are irregular in ways that survive identically on both source pages and have therefore been kept rather than emended: verse 1 उझ्झित / ujhjhita for ujjhita; verse 2 मन्त्रराजपराजितं / mantrarājaparājitaṁ, taken here as "unvanquished, itself the king of mantras," though a reading as mantrarāja-aparājitam is also possible; verse 3 अतत्परं / atatparaṁ, rendered "beyond even That"; verse 4 त्रिदैवकूटस्थ / tridaivakūṭastha and निजशुद्धसख्यम् / nijaśuddhasakhyam, both of uncertain construction; verse 5 अभीकरम् / abhīkaram, read as "fear-dispelling" rather than "fearsome"; verse 8 तैजप / taijapa, plainly a slip for taijasa, the second of the three states of the Māṇḍūkya, which is how it is translated; and verse 9 चन्द्रकान्तरतारकैरवम् / candrakāntaratārakairavam, whose internal division is unsettled. The translations above stay close to the words and do not smooth over these places. Throughout, the "pair of the guru's sandals" is understood in the internal, yogic sense — the seat of the guru's feet in the practitioner's own subtle body — which is why the epithets are drawn from nāda-bindu-kalā, the ten inner sounds, the anāhata and cakrarāja, the five kośas and three guṇas, the three states of Viśva, Taijasa and Prājña, and the space behind the brow (niṭalāntarāla).
