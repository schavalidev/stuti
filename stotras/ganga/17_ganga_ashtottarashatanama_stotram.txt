Title: Śrī Gaṅgā Aṣṭottaraśatanāma Stotram
Devanāgarī: श्रीगङ्गा-अष्टोत्तरशतनामस्तोत्रम्
Telugu: శ్రీ గంగా అష్టోత్తరశతనామ స్తోత్రం

Author: The colophon places it in the **Mahābhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa**. **Nārada** asks, and **Mahādeva** answers that these hundred and eight are "the best among the thousand names" of Jāhnavī.

Source / recension: Primary text: **Gītā Press, Gorakhpur — Śatanāma Stotra Saṅgraha**, code 1850, pp. 168–172 (stotra pp. 168–170, nāmāvalī pp. 171–172; PDF pp. 169–173), transcribed from the page images of the printed edition. Entry 32 of the volume's forty. **Collated against sanskritdocuments.org `gangAShTottarashatanAmastotram.itx`** (encoded and proofread by Karthik Raman), the **same text**: the same dhyāna (`सितमकरनिषण्णां …`), the Nārada–Mahādeva frame, 29 verses in the same numbering, and the same Mahābhāgavata colophon. Stotra and nāmāvalī are paired in one file as the publisher prints them, and were checked against each other name by name.

Recension note: **Gītā Press followed; the sanskritdocuments witness (SD) is recorded, not adopted.** The two agree almost exactly. The one substantive difference (Gītā Press → SD): v. 29 `सागरसङ्गमे` ("at the meeting with the ocean") → `sagarasaṅgame` ("at Sagara's confluence", Gaṅgāsāgar). Gītā Press prints `ॐ` before v. 3's first name; SD does not. **Stotra and nāmāvalī agree throughout.** The nāmāvalī keeps some phrases whole as single names: no. 2 `त्रिपथगादेव्यै` (v. 3 `त्रिपथगा देवी`), no. 60 `पुंसां निर्वाणपददायिन्यै` (the genitive `पुंसां`, "to men", kept inside the name), and no. 108 `सर्वदेवाधिदेवैः परिपूज्यपदाम्बुजायै` (the whole second line of v. 21, instrumental included). They are kept as printed; in the index they are written without the internal space so that each remains one entry. **Epithets of Umā** — `पार्वती`, `शिवपत्नी` (nos. 36–37) — are given to Gaṅgā, her co-wife and sister in the myth; `गौरीभगिनी` (no. 73) names that sisterhood. **Prefix.** Gītā Press prints `ॐ` before each name; this corpus prints `श्री` for every nāmāvalī without exception (user, 2026-09-08). **Cross-reference:** `ganga/16_ganga_sahasranama_stotram.txt` is a Gaṅgā sahasranāma (Skanda Purāṇa, Kāśīkhaṇḍa) — a different work, not the Mahābhāgavata thousand this list claims to be drawn from; the other files in `ganga/` are separate hymns.

Blurb: The river told as her own story: born from Viṣṇu's foot, caught in Brahmā's water-pot, falling into Śiva's matted locks, swallowed by the sage Jahnu and let out through his knee, splitting golden peaks and great mountains, running down to the sea and on into the underworld to carry Sagara's sons across. The phalaśruti promises that wherever you bathe, reciting these names makes it a bath in the Gaṅgā.

--- verse 1 | section: Dhyānam ---
deva:
सितमकरनिषण्णां शुभ्रवर्णां त्रिनेत्रां
करधृतकलशोद्यत्सोत्पलामत्यभीष्टाम् ।
विधिहरिहररूपां सेन्दुकोटीरचूडां
कलितसितदुकूलां जाह्नवीं तां नमामि ॥
iast:
sitamakaraniṣaṇṇāṁ śubhravarṇāṁ trinetrāṁ
karadhṛtakalaśodyatsotpalāmatyabhīṣṭām |
vidhiharihararūpāṁ sendukoṭīracūḍāṁ
kalitasitadukūlāṁ jāhnavīṁ tāṁ namāmi ||
en: I bow to Jāhnavī, seated on a white makara, bright in colour, three-eyed, holding in her hands a brimming pot and lotuses, most dear to devotees, in the form of Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Śiva, her crown set with the moon, clad in white silk.
tel: తెల్లని మకరముపై కూర్చున్నది, శుభ్రవర్ణ, త్రినేత్ర, చేతులలో నిండుకలశము ఉత్పలములు ధరించినది, అత్యంతాభీష్ట, బ్రహ్మవిష్ణుమహేశ్వరరూప, చంద్రునితో కూడిన కిరీటము గలది, తెల్లని పట్టువస్త్రము ధరించిన ఆ జాహ్నవికి నమస్కరించుచున్నాను.
hi: श्वेत मकरपर विराजित, शुभ्रवर्णवाली, तीन नेत्रोंवाली, दो हाथोंमें भरे हुए कलश तथा दो हाथोंमें सुन्दर कमल धारण किये हुए, भक्तोंके लिये परम इष्ट; ब्रह्मा, विष्णु तथा महेश तीनोंका रूप अर्थात् तीनोंके कार्य करनेवाली, मस्तकपर सुशोभित चन्द्रजटित मुकुटवाली तथा सुन्दर श्वेत वस्त्रोंसे विभूषित माँ गंगाको मैं प्रणाम करता हूँ।

deva:

śrīnārada uvāca |

en: Śrī Nārada said: O supreme lord, the name 'Gaṅgā' has been declared supremely holy. Tell me, how many names of Gaṅgā are praised?
tel: శ్రీనారదుడు పలికెను: ఓ పరమేశ్వరా, గంగ అను నామము పరమపుణ్యమని చెప్పబడినది. గంగ యొక్క ప్రశస్తనామములు ఎన్ని? నాకు చెప్పుము.
hi: श्रीनारदजी बोले — हे परमेश्वर! गंगा नाम परम पुण्यमय कहा गया है। गंगाके कितने नाम प्रशस्त हैं, मुझे बताइये।
