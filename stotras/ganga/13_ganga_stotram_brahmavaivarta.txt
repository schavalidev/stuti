Blurb: This Gaṅgā hymn is built as a description of the cosmos. Thirteen consecutive verses give the river's breadth and length in yojanas at each successive level of the universe, as she encircles it. Goloka comes first, where she is ten million yojanas across. Then come Vaikuṇṭha, Śivaloka, Dhruva's station, the moon, the sun, the Satya, Tapo and Jana worlds, Kailāsa, Indra's heaven, where she is called Mandākinī, and the netherworld, where she is Bhogavatī. The figures shrink at every step, down to the last of them: on earth she is one krośa wide, and in places dwindled away, and there she is called Alakanandā. Every one of those verses closes with the same refrain, 'tāṁ gaṅgāṁ praṇamāmy aham'. A second scale then runs alongside the first, this one of time. She is milk-white in the Satya age, milk-like in Tretā, sandal-hued in Dvāpara, and in the Kali age merely the colour of water. Its account of her origin is the Brahmavaivarta one. She arose as the liquefied body of Kṛṣṇa, when he melted at Śiva's singing in the rāsa circle of Goloka, on the full moon of Kārttika.

tel: శ్రీనారాయణుడు పలికెను: శివుని సంగీతానికి పూర్తిగా మైమరచిన శ్రీకృష్ణుని దేహం నుండి ఉద్భవించినది, రాధాదేహమే అన్నట్లు ఆయనతో కలిసినది — ఆ గంగకు నేను ప్రణమిల్లుతున్నాను. బ్రహ్మవైవర్త ప్రకారం శివసంగీతానికి కృష్ణుడే ద్రవించి జలరూపమయ్యాడు.
hi: श्रीनारायण ने कहा: शिव के संगीत से पूर्णतः मुग्ध हुए श्रीकृष्ण के अंग से जो उत्पन्न हुई, और जो राधा के अंग की भाँति उनसे संयुक्त हुई — उस गंगा को मैं प्रणाम करता हूँ। यह ब्रह्मवैवर्त का उत्पत्ति-वर्णन है: शिव के संगीत से स्वयं कृष्ण ही द्रवित होकर जल हो गए।

en: The obscure become renowned, and a fool becomes a learned man. For whoever rises at dawn and recites this auspicious hymn daily, even an evil dream turns auspicious, and he gains the fruit of bathing in Gaṅgā.

hi: अस्पष्ट कीर्ति वाला सुयशस्वी हो जाता है, मूर्ख पण्डित हो जाता है। जो प्रातः उठकर इस शुभ स्तोत्र को नित्य पढ़ता है, उसका दुःस्वप्न भी शुभ हो जाता है और उसे गंगा-स्नान का फल मिलता है।

Recension note: Single-source text (sanskritdocuments.org, gangAstotram2.itx, encoded and proofread by Anil Kumar Pandey), with its Devanāgarī taken from the site's own rendering of that encoding; the two are by construction one text, so what was verified here is that the ITRANS and the rendered Devanāgarī agree word for word, and the IAST was derived from the Devanāgarī. No second recension was located for collation, and none is claimed. This copy carries an unusual density of evident printing corruptions, all of which have been left exactly as printed rather than emended, and all of which are listed here: v. 4 गोम्पिभिः for गोपीभिः; v. 3 सत्रिधाने for सन्निधाने; v. 5 दैयैः and vv. 6–15 दैध्ये / दैध्यें, all for दैर्घ्ये ("in length"), which is how they are translated; v. 16 दीघीं for दीर्घा; v. 17 अलकनान्दाख्या for अलकनन्दाख्या; v. 18 क्षीरसत्रिभा for क्षीरसन्निभा; v. 23 भवेद्धवम् for भवेद् ध्रुवम्, so translated as "assuredly"; v. 24 मूखों for मूर्खो; and the colophon's ब्रह्मवैवोक्त for ब्रह्मवैवर्तोक्त. Verses 18, 19, 20 and 24 are printed as three-line units rather than two, and the source's own v. 21 calls the hymn a set of twenty-one verses (गङ्गापद्यैकविंशतिः) against a printed numbering that runs to twenty-four — taken together these indicate that the line-division of the transmitted copy has slipped somewhere in vv. 18–20; the printed numbering has nevertheless been followed here rather than reconstructed. The final line शुभं भवेच्च दुःस्वप्नो गङ्गास्नानफलं लभेत् stands unnumbered after ॥२४॥ in the source; because it syntactically completes the sentence begun in v. 24 it is set within that verse here, and the fact is recorded rather than concealed. The whole text is in anuṣṭubh. Distinctness was established at the opening pāda and then verse by verse against 03_ganga_stotram.txt (the Śaṅkara-tradition "देवि सुरेश्वरि भगवति गङ्गे") and 04_ganga_stavah.txt (Kalki Purāṇa), and against gangAstotram3, gangAstotraM6, gangAstotram10, siddhakRRitaMgangAstotram11, kaNvamaharShiNAkRRitaMgangAstotram09 and gangAstotramtapovanam on the same site. No pāda is shared with any of them, and the cosmographic catalogue of vv. 5–17 — with its yojana figures and its per-world names Mandākinī, Bhogavatī and Alakanandā — occurs in no other Gaṅgā text held in this folder.
