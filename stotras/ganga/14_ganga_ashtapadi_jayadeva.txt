Title: Śrī Gaṅgāṣṭapadī (Jayadeva-kṛtā)
Devanāgarī: गङ्गाष्टपदी (कविश्रीजयदेवकृता)
Telugu: శ్రీ గంగాష్టపది (కవి శ్రీ జయదేవ కృతా)

Author: Kavi Śrī Jayadeva, as stated both in the source header and in the closing colophon: "इति जयदेवविरचितगीतगोविन्दे कविश्रीजयदेवकृतौ गङ्गाष्टपदी सम्पूर्णा". The poet also signs himself inside the last verse — धीरजयदेवविरचितं — which is the bhaṇitā convention of the Gīta Govinda's own aṣṭapadīs. The colophon places the piece in the Gīta Govinda, though it is not among that work's twenty-four canonical aṣṭapadīs; it is best described as an aṣṭapadī transmitted under Jayadeva's name and in his manner, and no stronger claim is made here.

Source / recension: sanskritdocuments.org, doc_devii/gangAShTapadI.itx (transliterated and proofread by Shree Devi Kumar, latest update 3 November 2019), read through the site's own Devanāgarī rendering at doc_devii/gangAShTapadI.html. The IAST here was generated from that Devanāgarī and checked line by line against the underlying ITRANS encoding; no wording was altered, and the copy's several evident corruptions are listed in the recension note rather than repaired. Three items of apparatus stand before verse 1 and are kept out of the numbered verse sequence: the invocation "॥ श्रीगङ्गायै नमः ॥"; the musical heading "॥ रामगंरारागे यत्तिताले ॥", plainly a corruption of रामगिरीरागे यतिताले, "in the rāga Rāmagirī, in yati-tāla" — the same rāga-and-tāla rubric that heads every aṣṭapadī of the Gīta Govinda; and the marker ध्रुवपदं, printed after the refrain in verse 1 to identify it as the recurring dhruvapada. Sections:
गङ्गाष्टपदी — The eight sung stanzas, each closing on the dhruvapada (vv. 1–8)

Blurb: This is a Gaṅgā lyric in the exact manner of the Gīta Govinda. It is headed with its rāga and tāla. It is built on a dhruvapada that returns at the end of every stanza. It is signed within the final verse by 'steadfast Jayadeva', as that poet signs all his songs. The refrain is the plainest thing in it: 'namo devi gaṅge, namo mātar gaṅge', meaning 'homage, goddess Gaṅgā; homage, mother Gaṅgā', followed by the request that she carry off every sin. Around that plainness the verses run in Jayadeva's dense internal rhyme, and they keep reaching for the same figure, in which scale is reversed. A mere mouthful of her water is already deep enough for makaras and heavy with waves, in verse 7. She splits the stone of the golden mountain, and with the same motion fills the ocean, in verse 3. The last stanza names the moment it wants remembered, which is Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa bowing to the river at Viśvāmitra's word. It then asks her to ferry the poet himself across.

en: In your moving water, on the wave where the waves lie hushed, you carry the noble image of the slayer of Madhu and a bow strung of moonbeams. These last two lines are the dhruvapada, and the source marks them as such; they return at the close of every stanza.

नमो देवि गङ्गे नमो मातङ्गगे ॥
iast:
iti rāmalakṣmaṇai kauśikānujñayā surasaritkṛtanamaskāre |
vahati sukṛtamudāraṁ dhīrajayadevaviracitaṁ kavatārayasi nava jaladhipāre |
namo devi gaṅge namo mātaṅgage ||
en: Thus, O river of the gods, to whom Rāma and Lakṣmaṇa made obeisance at Kauśika's bidding — this song composed by steadfast Jayadeva carries noble merit, and you bring the poet across to the far shore of the fresh sea. Homage, goddess Gaṅgā; homage, mother Gaṅgā. The signature inside the last stanza is Jayadeva's standard bhaṇitā; the last words are corrupt in this copy and are left as printed.
tel: ఈ విధంగా, ఓ దేవనదీ, కౌశికుని అనుజ్ఞతో రామలక్ష్మణులు నమస్కరించిన నీకు — ధీరుడైన జయదేవుడు రచించిన ఈ గీతం ఉదారమైన సుకృతాన్ని మోస్తున్నది, నీవు కవిని నూతన సముద్రపు అవతలి తీరానికి చేరుస్తావు. నమస్కారం దేవీ గంగా, నమస్కారం తల్లీ గంగా. చివరి చరణంలోని ఈ సంతకమే జయదేవుని భణితా; చివరి పదాలు ఈ ప్రతిలో దూషితమై ఉన్నాయి, ముద్రితరీతిలోనే ఉంచబడ్డాయి.
hi: इस प्रकार, हे सुरसरिते, जिसे कौशिक की अनुज्ञा से राम और लक्ष्मण ने नमस्कार किया — धीर जयदेव द्वारा रचित यह गीत उदार सुकृत वहन करता है, और तुम कवि को नए जलधि के पार उतारती हो। नमस्कार हे देवि गंगे, नमस्कार हे मातर् गंगे। अन्तिम चरण का यह हस्ताक्षर जयदेव की भणिता है; अन्तिम शब्द इस प्रति में भ्रष्ट हैं और जैसे छपे हैं वैसे ही रखे गए हैं।

Recension note: Single-source text (sanskritdocuments.org, gangAShTapadI.itx, encoded and proofread by Shree Devi Kumar), with its Devanāgarī taken from the site's own rendering of that encoding; the two are by construction one text, so what was verified here is that the ITRANS and the rendered Devanāgarī agree word for word, and the IAST was derived from the Devanāgarī. No second recension was located for collation, and none is claimed. The copy is corrupt in a number of places, and every doubtful reading has been left exactly as printed and is listed here rather than emended: the musical heading रामगंरारागे यत्तिताले for रामगिरीरागे यतिताले; v. 1 गजदृत, which does not yield a confident sense and is left untranslated in the rendering of that compound; v. 2 सप्तरुचिकेलि, taken here as the play of her seven streams, which is probable rather than certain; v. 3 सङ्गतविन्ध्यगिरिग्राहे, rendered conservatively; v. 5 यास्तावगाहिता, almost certainly a corruption of या त्वम् अवगाहिता and translated on that assumption; v. 7 परपाङ्खरसि, which yields no confident sense and is passed over in the rendering; v. 8 रामलक्ष्मणै for रामलक्ष्मणैः, कवतारयसि for कविम् अवतारयसि (so translated), and the closing नमो मातङ्गगे for नमो मातर्गङ्गे, which the refrain requires and which every earlier stanza has. The dhruvapada is printed in full only in vv. 1–5; vv. 6 and 7 carry an abbreviated form and v. 8 the full one, and that variation is reproduced as printed. Distinctness rests on both form and wording: this is the only sung aṣṭapadī with a dhruvapada in this folder, and its refrain नमो देवि गङ्गे नमो मातर्गङ्गे appears nowhere else among the Gaṅgā texts here or among those consulted on the same site. The attribution to Jayadeva is the source's and the colophon's; the piece is not one of the twenty-four aṣṭapadīs of the received Gīta Govinda, and this file makes no claim about its authenticity beyond reporting what the colophon says.
