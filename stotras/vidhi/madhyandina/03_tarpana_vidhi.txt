Recension / śākhā: **North Indian smārta, Vājasaneyi (Śukla-Yajurveda) based — the Mādhyandina-tradition form.** This is important and is not a formality: tarpaṇa practice is śākhā-specific, and this file is NOT the universal or only form of the rite. Gītā Press, Gorakhpur is a North Indian house, and this volume's Vedic citations are overwhelmingly to the Vājasaneyi Saṁhitā (यजु० = VS 36.3, 11.50–52, 20.20–21, 7.41–42 and the rest), which is the Śukla Yajurveda followed in the north in its Mādhyandina śākhā. Several core mantras are cited instead to the **Taittirīya Āraṇyaka** (तै० आ० प्र० १०) — the prāṇāyāma mantra, sūryaśca mā manyuśca, āpaḥ punantu, agniśca mā manyuśca, uttame śikhare. That is not a defect in the book: TĀ prapāṭhaka 10 (the Mahānārāyaṇa Upaniṣad) is the common source of sandhyā mantras across traditions. But it means this file must be described as Vājasaneyi-based rather than Mādhyandina throughout.

**Southern (Taittirīya / Kṛṣṇa-Yajurveda) practice differs and is deliberately NOT represented here.** A user following the Taittirīya tarpaṇa-vidhi should not use this file: the mantra selection, the sequence and the accented recitation all differ. That recension belongs in its own file, from its own source, and must never be merged into or harmonised with this one. See the śākhā rule in the repo-root `CLAUDE.md`.

Blurb: Tarpaṇa is the daily rite in which water is offered with respect to the gods, to the sages and to the forefathers. The water is offered together with sesame seeds. The rite follows a fixed order. It begins with the gods and the sages. Next comes the offering to the divine forefathers and to the fourteen names of Yama. After this the worshipper offers water to his own forefathers, calling each one by name. This is done in three gotra lines: the line of his father, the line of his mother's father, and the line of his wife and children. Each forefather is offered three handfuls of water. At the end, water is offered to all those who have no family left to offer it for them. This includes the beings in every hell, those who were never his relatives, those who were his relatives in an earlier birth, and those who died without children and for whom no piṇḍa is offered any more. Water with sesame is offered to them because no one else will offer it. Through the whole rite the yajñopavīta, that is the sacred thread, is moved from one shoulder to the other. The book gives footnotes showing the correct position of the thread for each group.

--- unit 1 | section: Saṅkalpa and invocation ---
vidhi:
At dawn, in the brāhmī hour, rise from bed, finish the ablutions, bathe at a river, tank or well as convenience allows, put on clean bright cloth and sit on a kuśa seat facing east. Do ācamana three times, then, after the sandhyopāsana and the daily homa, wear the pavitrī on the ring finger of the left and right hands, reciting 'pavitre stho vaiṣṇavyau…' in full. Then take in the hand three kuśa blades, barley, unbroken rice and water, and recite this saṅkalpa. In place of 'amuka' insert the actual saṁvatsara, month, pakṣa, tithi, weekday and gotra; a brāhmaṇa uses 'śarmā', a kṣatriya 'varmā', a vaiśya 'gupta'.
deva:
ॐ विष्णवे नमः ३ । हरिः ॐ तत्सदद्यैतस्य श्रीब्रह्मणो द्वितीयपरार्धे श्रीश्वेतवाराहकल्पे जम्बूद्वीपे भरतखण्डे आर्यावर्तैकदेशे कलियुगे कलिप्रथमचरणे अमुकसंवत्सरे अमुकमासे अमुकपक्षे अमुकतिथौ अमुकवासरे अमुकगोत्रोत्पन्नः अमुकशर्मा अहं श्रीपरमेश्वरप्रीत्यर्थं देवर्षिमनुष्यपितृतर्पणं करिष्ये ।
iast:
oṁ viṣṇave namaḥ 3 | hariḥ oṁ tatsadadyaitasya śrībrahmaṇo dvitīyaparārdhe śrīśvetavārāhakalpe jambūdvīpe bharatakhaṇḍe āryāvartaikadeśe kaliyuge kaliprathamacaraṇe amukasaṁvatsare amukamāse amukapakṣe amukatithau amukavāsare amukagotrotpannaḥ amukaśarmā ahaṁ śrīparameśvaraprītyarthaṁ devarṣimanuṣyapitṛtarpaṇaṁ kariṣye |
en: Salutation to Viṣṇu, three times. Hari Oṁ Tat Sat. It is now the second half of the life of Brahmā, in the Śveta-Vārāha kalpa, in the Kali age, in its first quarter. The place is the continent Jambū, the land of Bhārata, in a region of Āryāvarta. The date is such a year, month, fortnight, lunar day and weekday. I, so-and-so Śarmā, born of such a gotra, will now perform the tarpaṇa of the gods, the ṛṣis, men and the forefathers. I do this for the pleasure of the supreme Lord.
tel: విష్ణువునకు నమస్కారము — మూడుసార్లు. హరిః ఓం తత్సత్. ఇప్పుడు బ్రహ్మదేవుని ద్వితీయపరార్ధమున, శ్వేతవరాహకల్పమున, జంబూద్వీపమున, భరతఖండమున, ఆర్యావర్తప్రదేశమున, కలియుగ ప్రథమచరణమున, ఫలానా సంవత్సర-మాస-పక్ష-తిథి-వాసరములందు, ఫలానా గోత్రమున జన్మించిన ఫలానా శర్మ అను నేను, పరమేశ్వరుని ప్రీతి కొరకు దేవర్షిమనుష్యపితృతర్పణమును చేయుచున్నాను.
hi: ॐ विष्णवे नमः (तीन बार)। हरिः ॐ तत्सत्। आज श्रीब्रह्माजीके द्वितीय परार्धमें, श्वेतवाराहकल्पमें, जम्बूद्वीपके भरतखण्डमें, आर्यावर्तके एक देशमें, कलियुगके प्रथम चरणमें, अमुक संवत्सर-मास-पक्ष-तिथि-वारमें, अमुक गोत्रमें उत्पन्न मैं अमुक शर्मा श्रीपरमेश्वरकी प्रसन्नताके लिये देवर्षिमनुष्यपितृतर्पण करूँगा।

--- unit 2 | section: Saṅkalpa and invocation ---
vidhi:
Then in a copper or silver vessel place white sandal, rice, fragrant flowers and tulasī leaves; lay across the vessel three kuśa blades a hand or a span long with their tips to the east; fill the vessel with water for the tarpaṇa; take the three kuśa blades with the tulasī, cupped, in the right hand and cover them with the left, and invoke the gods with this mantra and the two that follow. [The vessel for tarpaṇa should be gold, silver, copper or bell-metal, never clay, as Pitāmaha's verse says: gold, silver, copper or bell-metal vessels are to be used for the tarpaṇa of the forefathers; a clay one must be avoided.]
deva:
ॐ विश्वेदेवास आगत शृणुता म इमꣳ हवम् । एदं बर्हिर्निषीदत ॥
iast:
oṁ viśvedevāsa āgata śṛṇutā ma imaṁ havam | edaṁ barhirniṣīdata ||
en: O All-gods, come; hear this call of mine; sit down here upon this sacred grass.
tel: విశ్వేదేవులారా! రండు; నా ఈ ఆహ్వానమును వినుడు; ఈ దర్భాసనమునందు కూర్చుండుడు.

--- unit 5 | section: Tarpaṇa of gods, ṛṣis and divine forefathers ---
vidhi:
Devatarpaṇa. With the yajñopavīta on the left shoulder (upavīta), facing east, take up water mixed with rice from the first vessel with the tips of the fingers of the right hand — that is, from the deva-tīrtha — and let one aṅjali fall into the second vessel for each of Brahmā and the rest, reciting their name-mantras in turn. [One aṅjali for each god, two for each man, three for each forefather; and, per Vyāsa and Śāṅkhāyana, three each for one's mother, paternal grandmother and paternal great-grandmother, two each for a stepmother and the ācārya's wife, and one for every other woman. The gods' tarpaṇa is done with the tips of the kuśa, men's with the middle, the forefathers' with the root, facing east, north and south respectively.]

--- unit 26 | section: Wringing of the cloth, and Bhīṣma-tarpaṇa ---
vidhi:
Vastra-niṣpīḍana. Then fold the cloth four times over, dip it in water, take it out, and wring it out on the ground to your left, apasavya, reciting this mantra; let the wrung water fall into the water already offered in the tarpaṇa. [If an annual śrāddha or similar rite for a departed member of the household is being performed, the vastra-niṣpīḍana must not be done.] Per Yogiyājñavalkya, the water that comes from the wrung cloth is the share of those beings who live on the bather's leavings, so it is not to be wrung at the bathing place itself; per Vṛddhayogī, one must not wring the cloth until the tarpaṇa of the ṛṣis and forefathers has been done, since until then the bather himself is not properly bathed. Another smṛti: fold the cloth four times, wring it outside the water, place it on the left, do ācamana twice, and one becomes pure.
deva:

te gṛhṇantu mayā dattaṁ vastraniṣpīḍanodakam ||
en: Those born in our line who died sonless, of our gotra — let them receive this water of the wrung cloth, given by me.

--- unit 28 | section: Arghyadāna ---
vidhi:
Arghyadāna. Then place the yajñopavīta on the left shoulder, face east, do ācamana with pure water and perform prāṇāyāma. Fill a vessel with pure water, and with the ring finger draw a six-petalled lotus in the middle of it; put white sandal, unbroken rice, flowers and tulasī leaves into it. In a second vessel draw a six-petalled lotus with sandal, invoke and worship Brahmā and the other gods in it in the order of the directions beginning with the east, and then offer arghya to those worshipped gods with the water of the first vessel. These are the six arghya mantras (units 28–33), each closed with its own namaḥ and pūjā formula.

2. ॐ विष्णवे नमः ।
3. ॐ विष्णवे नमः ।
॥ ॐ तत्सद्ब्रह्मार्पणमस्तु ॥
iast:
anena yathāśaktikṛtena devarṣimanuṣyapitṛtarpaṇākhyena karmaṇā bhagavān mama samastapitṛsvarūpī janārdanavāsudevaḥ prīyatāṁ na mama |
1. oṁ viṣṇave namaḥ |
2. oṁ viṣṇave namaḥ |
3. oṁ viṣṇave namaḥ |
|| oṁ tatsadbrahmārpaṇamastu ||
en: By this act called the tarpaṇa of gods, ṛṣis, men and forefathers, done to the best of my power, may the Lord Janārdana Vāsudeva, who is the very form of all my forefathers, be pleased; it is not mine. Salutation to Viṣṇu, salutation to Viṣṇu, salutation to Viṣṇu. Oṁ tat sat — let it be an offering to Brahman.

hi: यथाशक्ति किये हुए इस देवर्षिमनुष्यपितृतर्पण नामक कर्मसे मेरे समस्त पितृस्वरूपी भगवान् जनार्दन वासुदेव प्रसन्न हों; यह मेरा नहीं है। ॐ विष्णवे नमः। ॐ विष्णवे नमः। ॐ विष्णवे नमः। ॐ तत्सत् ब्रह्मार्पण हो।
