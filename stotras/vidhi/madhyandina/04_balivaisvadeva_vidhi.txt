Recension / śākhā: **North Indian smārta, Vājasaneyi (Śukla-Yajurveda) based — the Mādhyandina-tradition form.** This is important and is not a formality: bali-vaiśvadeva practice is śākhā-specific, and this file is NOT the universal or only form of the rite. Gītā Press, Gorakhpur is a North Indian house, and this volume's Vedic citations are overwhelmingly to the Vājasaneyi Saṁhitā (यजु० = VS 36.3, 11.50–52, 20.20–21, 7.41–42 and the rest), which is the Śukla Yajurveda followed in the north in its Mādhyandina śākhā. Several core mantras are cited instead to the **Taittirīya Āraṇyaka** (तै० आ० प्र० १०) — the prāṇāyāma mantra, sūryaśca mā manyuśca, āpaḥ punantu, agniśca mā manyuśca, uttame śikhare. That is not a defect in the book: TĀ prapāṭhaka 10 (the Mahānārāyaṇa Upaniṣad) is the common source of sandhyā mantras across traditions. But it means this file must be described as Vājasaneyi-based rather than Mādhyandina throughout.

**Southern (Taittirīya / Kṛṣṇa-Yajurveda) practice differs and is deliberately NOT represented here.** A user following the Taittirīya bali-vaiśvadeva-vidhi should not use this file: the mantra selection, the sequence and the accented recitation all differ. That recension belongs in its own file, from its own source, and must never be merged into or harmonised with this one. See the śākhā rule in the repo-root `CLAUDE.md`.

As with the tarpaṇa name-lists, these take reference-list treatment: the whole numbered run is kept as one unit in Devanāgarī and IAST, with the `en`/`tel`/`hi` fields describing the run rather than translating each formula separately. संकल्प एवं अग्निध्यान — Saṅkalpa and the meditation on Agni (units 1–3)
पञ्चमहायज्ञ — The five great sacrifices: deva, bhūta, pitṛ, manuṣya, brahma (units 4–9)

Blurb: Bali-vaiśvadeva is the daily duty of the householder to feed others before he takes his own food. First, five offerings are made into the fire, to Brahmā and to the other deities. Next, twenty morsels of food without salt are placed at twenty marked points on a square drawn on the ground. Each morsel has its own mantra. These offerings are made to the elements, to the directions, to the sun, to the Viśvedevas, to the dawn, and to Prajāpati, the lord of beings. After this the Gāyatrī mantra is recited at least three times as the brahmayajña. Then comes the pañcabali, which is the set of five offerings of food. Food is placed for cows, for dogs, for crows, for the gods along with the ghosts and demons, and last of all for the ants. The sacred thread is shifted to the correct position for each of these offerings. The mantra for the ants says that these creatures are bound by their karma and are hungry, and prays that they may be satisfied. Every mantra in this section ends with the words 'na mama', which mean 'not mine'.

--- unit 1 | section: Saṅkalpa and meditation on Agni ---
vidhi:
The bali-vaiśvadeva is done thus. First perform ācamana and prāṇāyāma, and wear the kuśa pavitrī on the ring finger of the right hand with the mantra 'pavitre stho vaiṣṇavyau'. Then read this saṅkalpa. (This saṅkalpa may also be made mentally.) The place, time and name are to be filled in as in the sandhyā and tarpaṇa.

Then salute Agni, take unsalted cooked food in a vessel, keep the yajñopavīta in the savya position, place the right knee on the ground and offer these five oblations into the fire one by one, in order. --- unit 5 | section: The five great sacrifices ---
vidhi:
Bhūtayajña. Then, near the fire, draw a square figure on the ground with water, its door to the east, and place twenty morsels of food at the marked positions in order. The diagram the book prints shows the fire-place (agnisthāna) at the top left and the food vessel (annapātra) at the top right, with 'east' across the top, 'west' at the bottom, 'north' at the left and 'south' at the right; the twenty numbered positions run 1, 2, 3 across the top toward the east, 5, 9, 11, 12, 13 down the eastern side, 14, 15 and 16, 17 in two inner columns, 4, 8, 18 along the southern edge, and 6, 10, 19, 20 along the northern. Only the numbers are marked in the figure: place the first morsel where 1 stands, the second where 2 stands, and so on to the twentieth. Recite one mantra for each morsel. For this section wear the yajñopavīta savya, in the seventeen-morsel maṇḍala, and drop each morsel at its proper place.

Pitṛyajña, the eighteenth morsel. Make the yajñopavīta apasavya, place the left knee on the ground, face south, take sesame with the food if possible, and place the morsel at the position marked in the maṇḍala of cooked food.

१८ ॐ पितृभ्यः स्वधा नमः, इदं पितृभ्यः स्वधा न मम ।
iast:
18 oṁ pitṛbhyaḥ svadhā namaḥ, idaṁ pitṛbhyaḥ svadhā na mama |
en: To the forefathers, svadhā and salutation; this svadhā is for the forefathers, not mine.
tel: పితరులకు స్వధా, నమస్కారము; ఈ స్వధ పితరులదే, నాది కాదు.
hi: पितरोंको स्वधा, नमस्कार; यह स्वधा पितरोंकी ही है, मेरी नहीं।

--- unit 7 | section: The five great sacrifices ---
vidhi:
Nirṇejanam, the nineteenth. Make the yajñopavīta savya, wash the food vessel, and drop that water at the place marked nineteen in the maṇḍala while reciting this.
deva:
१९ ॐ यक्ष्मैतत्ते निर्णेजनं नमः, इदं यक्ष्मणे न मम ।
iast:
19 oṁ yakṣmaitatte nirṇejanaṁ namaḥ, idaṁ yakṣmaṇe na mama |
en: O Yakṣma (wasting disease), this is your cleansing water; salutation. This is for Yakṣma, not mine.
tel: ఓ యక్ష్మా! ఇది నీ నిర్ణేజనము; నమస్కారము. ఇది యక్ష్మునిదే, నాది కాదు.
hi: हे यक्ष्मा! यह तुम्हारा निर्णेजन (धोवन) है, नमस्कार। यह यक्ष्माका ही है, मेरा नहीं।

--- unit 8 | section: The five great sacrifices ---
vidhi:
Manuṣyayajña, the twentieth. Wear the yajñopavīta round the neck like a garland (nivīta), face north, and drop the morsel at the place marked twenty in the maṇḍala of cooked food while reciting this.
deva:
२० ॐ हन्त ते सनकादिमनुष्येभ्यो नमः, इदं हन्त ते सनकादिमनुष्येभ्यो न मम ।
iast:
20 oṁ hanta te sanakādimanuṣyebhyo namaḥ, idaṁ hanta te sanakādimanuṣyebhyo na mama |
en: Here, take it — salutation to Sanaka and the other divine men; this is theirs, not mine.
tel: ఇదిగో, గ్రహించుడు — సనకాది మనుష్యులకు నమస్కారము; ఇది వారిదే, నాది కాదు.
hi: लो, ग्रहण करो — सनक आदि मनुष्योंको नमस्कार; यह उन्हींका है, मेरा नहीं।

--- unit 9 | section: The five great sacrifices ---
vidhi:
Brahmayajña. Facing east, with the yajñopavīta savya, recite the Gāyatrī at least three times. This completes the five great sacrifices; no separate mantra is given for it beyond the Gāyatrī itself.
deva:
ॐ भूर्भुवः स्वः तत्सवितुर्वरेण्यं भर्गो देवस्य धीमहि । धियो यो नः प्रचोदयात् ॥
iast:
oṁ bhūrbhuvaḥ svaḥ tatsaviturvareṇyaṁ bhargo devasya dhīmahi | dhiyo yo naḥ pracodayāt ||
en: Oṁ, earth, mid-air, heaven: we meditate on that adorable splendour of the god Savitṛ; may he impel our understandings.
tel: ఓం భూర్భువః స్వః. సవితృదేవుని ఆ వరణీయమైన తేజస్సును ధ్యానించుచున్నాము; అతడు మా బుద్ధులను ప్రేరేపించుగాక.
hi: हम स्थावर-जंगमरूप सम्पूर्ण विश्वको उत्पन्न करनेवाले उन निरतिशय प्रकाशमय परमेश्वरके भजनेयोग्य तेजका ध्यान करते हैं, जो हमारी बुद्धियोंको सत्कर्मोंकी ओर प्रेरित करते हैं।

--- unit 10 | section: The five bali offerings ---
vidhi:
Gobali, the offering to cows. With the yajñopavīta in the savya position, offer the bali to the cows while reciting this mantra.
deva:
ॐ सौरभेयः सर्वहिताः पवित्राः पुण्यराशयः ।
प्रतिगृह्णन्तु मे ग्रासं गावस्त्रैलोक्यमातरः ॥
इदं गोभ्यो न मम ।
iast:
oṁ saurabheyaḥ sarvahitāḥ pavitrāḥ puṇyarāśayaḥ |
pratigṛhṇantu me grāsaṁ gāvastrailokyamātaraḥ ||
idaṁ gobhyo na mama |
en: Sprung from Surabhi, benefactors of all, pure, heaps of merit — let the cows, the mothers of the three worlds, accept this morsel of mine. This is for the cows, not mine.
tel: సురభి సంతానము, సర్వహితకారిణులు, పవిత్రులు, పుణ్యరాశులునైన త్రైలోక్యమాతలగు గోవులు నా ఈ గ్రాసమును గ్రహించెదరు గాక. ఇది గోవులదే, నాది కాదు.
hi: सुरभिसे उत्पन्न, सबका हित करनेवाली, पवित्र, पुण्यकी राशिरूपा तथा तीनों लोकोंकी माता गौएँ मेरे इस ग्रासको ग्रहण करें। यह गौओंका ही है, मेरा नहीं।

--- unit 11 | section: The five bali offerings ---
vidhi:
Kukkurabali, the offering to dogs. Wear the yajñopavīta round the neck like a garland and give the morsel to the dogs with this mantra.
deva:
ॐ द्वौ श्वानौ श्यामशबलौ वैवस्वतकुलोद्भवौ ।
ताभ्यामन्नं प्रदास्यामि स्यातामेतावहिंसकौ ॥
इदं श्वभ्यां न मम ।
iast:
oṁ dvau śvānau śyāmaśabalau vaivasvatakulodbhavau |
tābhyāmannaṁ pradāsyāmi syātāmetāvahiṁsakau ||
idaṁ śvabhyāṁ na mama |
en: The two dogs, the dark and the dappled, born of the house of Vaivasvata (Yama) — to those two I shall give food; may they be harmless. This is for the two dogs, not mine.
tel: వైవస్వతకులమున పుట్టిన శ్యామ, శబల అను రెండు కుక్కలకు నేను అన్నమును ఇత్తును; అవి హింసారహితములగుగాక. ఇది కుక్కలదే, నాది కాదు.
hi: वैवस्वत (यम)-के कुलमें उत्पन्न श्याम और शबल—ये दो कुत्ते हैं, उन दोनोंको मैं अन्न प्रदान करूँगा; ये दोनों अहिंसक हों। यह कुत्तोंका ही है, मेरा नहीं।

--- unit 12 | section: The five bali offerings ---
vidhi:
Kākabali, the offering to crows. Make the yajñopavīta apasavya again and place the morsel on the ground for the crows, reciting this.
deva:
ॐ ऐन्द्रवारुणवायव्या याम्या वै नैर्ऋतास्तथा ।
वायसाः प्रतिगृह्णन्तु भूमौ पिण्डं मयोज्झितम् ॥
इदं वायसेभ्यो न मम ।
iast:
oṁ aindravāruṇavāyavyā yāmyā vai nairṛtāstathā |
vāyasāḥ pratigṛhṇantu bhūmau piṇḍaṁ mayojjhitam ||
idaṁ vāyasebhyo na mama |
en: The crows belonging to Indra, Varuṇa and Vāyu, to Yama and to Nirṛti — let them accept this ball of food cast down by me on the ground. This is for the crows, not mine.
tel: ఐంద్ర, వారుణ, వాయవ్య, యామ్య, నైరృత సంబంధులైన కాకులు నేను భూమిపై ఉంచిన ఈ పిండమును గ్రహించెదరు గాక. ఇది కాకులదే, నాది కాదు.
hi: इन्द्र, वरुण, वायु, यम तथा निर्ऋतिसे सम्बन्ध रखनेवाले कौए मेरे द्वारा भूमिपर छोड़े हुए इस पिण्डको ग्रहण करें। यह कौओंका ही है, मेरा नहीं।

--- unit 13 | section: The five bali offerings ---
vidhi:
Devādibali. Then, in the savya position, offer food to the gods and the rest with this mantra.
deva:
ॐ देवा मनुष्याः पशवो वयांसि सिद्धाः सयक्षोरगदैत्यसङ्घाः ।
प्रेताः पिशाचास्तरवः समस्ता ये चान्नमिच्छन्ति मया प्रदत्तम् ॥
इदमन्नं देवादिभ्यो न मम ।
iast:
oṁ devā manuṣyāḥ paśavo vayāṁsi siddhāḥ sayakṣoragadaityasaṅghāḥ |
pretāḥ piśācāstaravaḥ samastā ye cānnamicchanti mayā pradattam ||
idamannaṁ devādibhyo na mama |
en: Gods, men, cattle, birds, siddhas, and the hosts of yakṣas, serpents and daityas; pretas, piśācas and all the trees — and whoever else desires the food given by me. This food is for the gods and the rest, not mine.
tel: దేవతలు, మనుష్యులు, పశువులు, పక్షులు, సిద్ధులు, యక్ష-ఉరగ-దైత్య సంఘములు, ప్రేతలు, పిశాచములు, సమస్త వృక్షములు — నేనిచ్చిన అన్నమును కోరువారందరు. ఈ అన్నము దేవాదులదే, నాది కాదు.
hi: देवता, मनुष्य, पशु, पक्षी, सिद्ध, यक्ष, नाग तथा दैत्योंके समूह, प्रेत, पिशाच और समस्त वृक्ष—तथा जो भी मेरे दिये हुए अन्नको चाहते हैं। यह अन्न देवता आदिका ही है, मेरा नहीं।

--- unit 14 | section: The five bali offerings ---
vidhi:
Pipīlikādibali, the offering to ants and the like. In the same way give food to the ants and the rest with this mantra. This closes the bali-vaiśvadeva.
deva:
ॐ पिपीलिकाः कीटपतङ्गकाद्या बुभुक्षिताः कर्मनिबन्धबद्धाः ।
तेषां हि तृप्त्यर्थमिदं मयान्नं तेभ्यो विसृष्टं सुखिनो भवन्तु ॥
इदमन्नं पिपीलिकादिभ्यो न मम ।
iast:
oṁ pipīlikāḥ kīṭapataṅgakādyā bubhukṣitāḥ karmanibandhabaddhāḥ |
teṣāṁ hi tṛptyarthamidaṁ mayānnaṁ tebhyo visṛṣṭaṁ sukhino bhavantu ||
idamannaṁ pipīlikādibhyo na mama |
en: Ants, insects, moths and the rest are hungry, bound fast by the bondage of their karma. For their satisfaction this food has been set out by me for them — let them be happy. This food is for the ants and the rest, not mine.
tel: చీమలు, కీటకములు, పతంగములు మొదలగునవి ఆకలిగొనినవి, కర్మబంధములచేత కట్టబడినవి. వాటి తృప్తి కొరకే ఈ అన్నమును నేను వాటికి ఉంచితిని — అవి సుఖముగా ఉండుగాక. ఈ అన్నము చీమలు మొదలగువాటిదే, నాది కాదు.
hi: चींटी, कीट, पतङ्ग आदि भूखे हैं और कर्मबन्धनमें बँधे हुए हैं। उनकी तृप्तिके लिये ही यह अन्न मेरे द्वारा उनके लिये छोड़ा गया है—वे सुखी हों। यह अन्न चींटी आदिका ही है, मेरा नहीं।
