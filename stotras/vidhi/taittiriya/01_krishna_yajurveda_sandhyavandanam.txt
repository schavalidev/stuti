Title: Kṛṣṇa-Yajurveda Sandhyāvandanam (Taittirīya)
Devanāgarī: कृष्ण यजुर्वेद संध्यावन्दनम्
Telugu: కృష్ణ యజుర్వేద సంధ్యావందనం

Author: Anonymous (traditional Taittirīya smārta rite). No single compiler; the sequence is the received Āpastamba/Bodhāyana-tradition order of service, and the mantras are drawn from the Taittirīya corpus (Āraṇyaka, Brāhmaṇa, Saṁhitā) with paurāṇika verses interspersed.

Type: vidhi (ritual manual) — not a stotra. Uses the one-extra-field vidhi format documented in `../madhyandina/01_sandhyopasana_vidhi.txt`: a `vidhi:` field carrying the ritual instruction, then deva / iast / en / tel / hi.

Recension / śākhā: **Taittirīya (Kṛṣṇa-Yajurveda) — the South Indian form.** This is important and is not a formality: sandhyāvandana is śākhā-specific, and this file is NOT the universal or only form of the rite. Northern practice follows the Mādhyandina / Vājasaneyi (Śukla-Yajurveda) form, transcribed separately at `../madhyandina/01_sandhyopasana_vidhi.txt` from Gītā Press book 210. **The two are the observances of different communities and must never be merged, harmonised or collated against each other.** A user who follows the Mādhyandina rite should not use this file.

A demonstrated example of why, found while collating this file: the Gāyatrī dismissal verse reads **`उ॒त्तमे॑ शिख॑रे जा॒ते॒ भू॒म्यां`** here (*jāte*), against **`उत्तमे शिखरे देवि भूम्यां`** (*devi*) in the Mādhyandina file. Both are correct in their own recension. Likewise this recension reads `आपो॒ हिष्ठा` as one word where Mādhyandina splits `आपो हि ष्ठा`. **These are not variants to adjudicate and have deliberately not been reconciled.**

Accent: **present throughout, from the outset** (user, 2026-09-08), unlike the Mādhyandina set — accented recitation is normal in this tradition, and the source prints it. Udātta `॑` (U+0951) and anudātta `॒` (U+0952) as printed; the dīrgha/double svarita, which South Indian printing renders with a double-quote glyph `”`, is given as `᳚` (U+1CDA VEDIC TONE DOUBLE SVARITA); and the nasal-before-spirant, which the Telugu source writes `గ్ం` / `గ్‍ం` (ga + virama [+ ZWJ] + anusvāra), is given in Devanāgarī as `ग्ं` (U+0917 + U+094D + U+0902), the spelling this corpus uses for that sound. The Vedic-sign spelling `ᳪं᳭` is also attested — see `अग्निᳪं᳭ हृदयेन` in the accented Yajurveda — but it is not used here. The `tel:` field preserves the source's own `ఓగ్‍ం` byte-for-byte, ZWJ included: it marks a real recitational distinction (praṇava with guttural nasal, occurring before spirants — `ఓగ్‍ం సువః`, `ఓగ్‍ం సత్యమ్`, against plain `ఓం` elsewhere; rendered `ogṁ` in the `iast:` field, distinct from plain `oṁ`) and stripping it would collapse `ogṁ` into `oṁ`. **The corpus-wide "zero ZWJ" cleanliness check must therefore exempt the `tel:` field of this file.**

Source / recension: base text `https://stotranidhi.com/krishna-yajurveda-sandhya-vandanam-in-telugu/` (fetched 2026-09-09; note the slug carries no `/en/` prefix). **Corrections applied to the base text, with the count, because stotranidhi is not a critical edition.** The page carries a systematic character-level encoding defect: **29 occurrences of Latin `o` (U+006F) standing where the Telugu anusvāra `ం` (U+0C02) belongs** — in `రసోఽమృత॒o`, `అభో”జ్య॒o`, `దురి॑త॒o`, `సర్వ॑o`, `ఆయూగ్॑oషి`, `గర్భ॑o`, `త్వచ॑o`, `అస్త॒o`, `సకల॑o`, `పరమాత్మ॑o`, `అక్షర॑o`, `పా॒ప॒o` and the like. A Latin letter cannot occur in Telugu text and every instance sits on an anusvāra-final word after an accent mark, so the repair is unambiguous; all 29 were corrected. (28 were caught by a rule keyed on a preceding Telugu character or accent mark; a 29th, in `యాసా”o`, sits after the svarita glyph `”` and was caught only on a second pass — recorded here because a future run of the same rule would miss it too.) No other change was made to the source's readings.

Blurb: This is the twilight worship as followed in the South Indian Taittirīya tradition. It is kept as a separate file from the northern Mādhyandina rite, because the two belong to different communities. The difference between them can be seen at once. The Gītā Press Mādhyandina manual prints the mantras without accent marks and explains the actions in long Hindi prose. This text is meant for chanting, and every syllable carries its accent mark. The rite begins by requesting the bhūtas to leave the ground, and this is done before any Vedic word is spoken. At the end, the worshipper respectfully sends Gāyatrī Devī back to her mountain. Before doing so, he prays to her for long life on earth, for wealth, and for brahmavarcas, which is the radiance that comes from sacred learning. Between the beginning and the end come the twenty-four mudrās. The text states that without these mudrās the Gāyatrī mantra does not give its fruit.

--- unit 1 | section: Purification ---
vidhi:
The rite opens with the invocation to the teacher, and then the verse of purification. Recite it, then repeat the name of the lotus-eyed one three times.
deva:
श्री गुरुभ्यो नमः | हरिः ओं ||
अपवित्रः पवित्रो वा सर्वावस्थां गतोऽपि वा |
यः स्मरेत्पुंडरीकाक्षं स बाह्याभ्यन्तरश्शुचिः ||
पुंडरीकाक्ष पुंडरीकाक्ष पुंडरीकाक्षाय नमः ||
iast:
śrī gurubhyo namaḥ | hariḥ oṁ ||
apavitraḥ pavitro vā sarvāvasthāṁ gato'pi vā |
yaḥ smaretpuṁḍarīkākṣaṁ sa bāhyābhyantaraśśuciḥ ||
puṁḍarīkākṣa puṁḍarīkākṣa puṁḍarīkākṣāya namaḥ ||
en: Salutation to the teachers. Hari Oṁ. Impure or pure, or fallen into whatever state at all — whoever calls to mind the lotus-eyed Lord becomes clean outside and within. Lotus-eyed one, lotus-eyed one, salutation to the lotus-eyed one.
tel: శ్రీ గురువులకు నమస్కారము. హరిః ఓం. అపవిత్రుడైనను, పవిత్రుడైనను, ఏ అవస్థలో ఉన్నను — పుండరీకాక్షుని ఎవడు స్మరించునో, అతడు బాహ్యమునను అంతరమునను శుద్ధుడగును. పుండరీకాక్షా, పుండరీకాక్షా, పుండరీకాక్షునకు నమస్కారము.
hi: श्रीगुरुओंको नमस्कार। हरिः ॐ। मनुष्य अपवित्र हो या पवित्र अथवा किसी भी दशामें स्थित हो, जो पुण्डरीकाक्षका स्मरण करता है, वह बाहर और भीतर सब ओरसे शुद्ध हो जाता है। पुण्डरीकाक्ष, पुण्डरीकाक्ष, पुण्डरीकाक्षको नमस्कार है।

deva:

uttiṣṭhaṁtu bhūta piśācāḥ yete bhūmi bhārakāḥ |
eteṣāmavirodhena brahmakarma samārabhe ||
en: Let the bhūtas and piśācas who are a burden on this ground rise up and depart. Without hindrance from them, I begin the act of Brahman.
tel: ఈ భూమికి భారమైన భూతపిశాచములు లేచి పోవుగాక. వారి విరోధము లేకుండ బ్రహ్మకర్మను ఆరంభించుచున్నాను.

--- unit 7 | section: Saṅkalpa ---
vidhi:
The saṅkalpa. It is markedly longer than the Mādhyandina one and locates the worshipper far more precisely — by Meru and the south, by Śrīśaila, by the two rivers between which he stands. The ellipses are in the source, to be filled with the actual place, saṁvatsara, ayana, ṛtu, month, pakṣa, tithi, weekday, nakṣatra, yoga, karaṇa, gotra and name; and `prātaḥ / mādhyāhnika / sāyaṁ` is chosen according to the hour.
deva:
मम उपात्त समस्त दुरितक्षय द्वारा श्रीपरमेश्वरमुद्दिश्य श्रीपरमेश्वर प्रीत्यर्थं शुभाभ्यां शुभे शोभने मुहूर्ते श्री महाविष्णोराज्ञया प्रवर्तमानस्य अद्य ब्रह्मणः द्वितीय परार्थे श्वेतवराहकल्पे वैवस्वत मन्वंतरे कलियुगे प्रथमपादे जंबूद्वीपे भरतवर्षे भरतखंडे मेरोः दक्षिण दिग्भागे श्रीशैलस्य …… प्रदेशे ……, …… नद्योः मध्य प्रदेशे शोभन गृहे समस्त देवता ब्राह्मण आचार्य हरिहर गुरु चरण सन्निधौ अस्मिन् वर्तमान व्यावहारिक चांद्रमानेन श्री …… नाम संवत्सरे …… अयने …… ऋतौ …… मासे …… पक्षे …… तिथौ …… वासरे …… नक्षत्रे …… योगे …… करण एवं गुण विशेषण विशिष्टायां शुभायां शुभतिथौ श्रीमान् …… गोत्रः …… नामधेयः (श्रीमतः …… गोत्रस्य …… नामधेयस्य धर्मपत्नीसमेतस्य) श्री परमेश्वर प्रीत्यर्थं प्रातः / माध्याह्निक / सायं संध्यां उपासिष्ये ||
iast:
mama upātta samasta duritakṣaya dvārā śrīparameśvaramuddiśya śrīparameśvara prītyarthaṁ śubhābhyāṁ śubhe śobhane muhūrte śrī mahāviṣṇorājñayā pravartamānasya adya brahmaṇaḥ dvitīya parārthe śvetavarāhakalpe vaivasvata manvaṁtare kaliyuge prathamapāde jaṁbūdvīpe bharatavarṣe bharatakhaṁḍe meroḥ dakṣiṇa digbhāge śrīśailasya …… pradeśe ……, …… nadyoḥ madhya pradeśe śobhana gṛhe samasta devatā brāhmaṇa ācārya harihara guru caraṇa sannidhau asmin vartamāna vyāvahārika cāṁdramānena śrī …… nāma saṁvatsare …… ayane …… ṛtau …… māse …… pakṣe …… tithau …… vāsare …… nakṣatre …… yoge …… karaṇa evaṁ guṇa viśeṣaṇa viśiṣṭāyāṁ śubhāyāṁ śubhatithau śrīmān …… gotraḥ …… nāmadheyaḥ (śrīmataḥ …… gotrasya …… nāmadheyasya dharmapatnīsametasya) śrī parameśvara prītyarthaṁ prātaḥ / mādhyāhnika / sāyaṁ saṁdhyāṁ upāsiṣye ||
en: I make this resolve for the destruction of all the sin I have incurred. It is directed towards the supreme Lord and is for his pleasure. The time is an auspicious and beautiful muhūrta, in the course ordained by the command of Śrī Mahāviṣṇu. It is now the second half of the life of Brahmā, in the Śveta-Vārāha kalpa, in the Vaivasvata manvantara, in the Kali age, in its first quarter. The place is the continent Jambū, in the land of Bhārata, in the region of Bhārata, on the southern side of Meru, in the region of Śrīśaila, at such a place, in the land between such and such two rivers, in an auspicious house. This is done in the presence of all the gods, the brāhmaṇas, the teacher, Hari and Hara, and the feet of the guru. vidhi:
