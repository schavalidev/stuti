Title: Yajñopavīta-dhāraṇa Vidhi (Taittirīya)
Devanāgarī: यज्ञोपवीतधारणविधिः
Telugu: యజ్ఞోపవీత ధారణ విధిః

Author: Anonymous (traditional Taittirīya smārta rite). No compiler is named. The Vedic mantras are from the Taittirīya Āraṇyaka and the Taittirīya Brāhmaṇa, and the base text prints its own citations for two of them.

Type: vidhi (ritual manual) — not a stotra. Uses the one-extra-field vidhi format documented in `../madhyandina/01_sandhyopasana_vidhi.txt`: a `vidhi:` field carrying the ritual instruction, then deva / iast / en / tel / hi.

Recension / śākhā: **Taittirīya (Kṛṣṇa-Yajurveda) — the South Indian form.** This is not inferred: the base text cites `(तै.आ.४-४२-४)` for the water mantra and `(तै.ब्रा.३-७-६-२२)` for the mantra said to the sun, it writes the guttural nasal in the Taittirīya manner, and its ācamana is the twenty-four-name series of this tradition. **This file is not the universal form of the rite.** Northern practice follows the Mādhyandina form and uses the Pāraskara sequence; the corpus's Mādhyandina material is in `../madhyandina/`. The two are the practice of different communities and are not collated against each other. The sandhyāvandana of this same tradition is at `01_krishna_yajurveda_sandhyavandanam.txt` and its brahmayajña at `02_brahma_yajna_vidhi.txt`.

Accent: **present in the Vedic mantras — 298 marks.** Parity with the source was measured rather than assumed: 298 in, 298 out. Udātta `॑` (U+0951) and anudātta `॒` (U+0952) as printed, and the double svarita as `᳚` (U+1CDA). The guttural nasal before a spirant is written `ग्ं` (U+0917 + U+094D + U+0902), as in the two sibling files of this folder, and romanised `gṁ`. The base text writes it `ग्ं`, and once as `ग्` followed only by an accent (`गृध्रा॑णा॒ग्॒`); there are **eight such sites** and all eight are written `ग्ं` here, with any accent that stood inside the cluster placed after it. **No accent was added, inferred, moved between syllables or reconstructed at any point.** The ritual prose — the saṅkalpas, the invocations into the nine strands, the sixteen services and the verses said over the worn thread — is unaccented in the source and is unaccented here. There are no zero-width joiners and no zero-width non-joiners in this file.

Source / recension: base text `https://stotranidhi.com/hi/yagnopaveetha-dharana-vidhi-in-sanskrit/` (fetched 2026-09-12; native accented Devanāgarī, 5,514 Devanāgarī characters, 298 accent marks, 128 lines). The page exists in five scripts, and per the sourcing rule those are **one witness, not five**.

Its differences are listed in the Recension note.

**(2) The accented Taittirīya Āraṇyaka on sanskritdocuments.org** (`doc_veda/taittirIyaAraNyaka.html`, fetched 2026-09-12; 236,935 Devanāgarī characters, 37,630 accent marks), for the water mantra of unit 5, **at exactly the place the base text cites**: the Āraṇyaka numbers it `०। ४। ४२। ९२–९३`. Confirmed there.

**(3) The accented Taittirīya Brāhmaṇa on sanskritdocuments.org** (`doc_veda/taittirIyabrAhmaNam.html`, fetched 2026-09-12; 562,095 Devanāgarī characters, 90,803 accent marks), for the mantra said to the sun at unit 11, **again at exactly the place the base text cites** — the Brāhmaṇa numbers it `३। ७। ६। २२`. Confirmed there, with six differences listed below.

**No Gītā Press witness applies.** Gītā Press publishes the northern rite, and under the standing rule an edition in the wrong śākhā is not a weaker witness but no witness at all.

Corrections applied to the base text. **Ten occurrences of the ASCII colon `:` (U+003A) standing where the visarga `ः` (U+0903) belongs** — the systematic defect already recorded for that site — in `के॒तव॑:`, `तेज॑:`, `सुव॑:`, `न॑:`, `रस॑:` and the like. All ten corrected. No other change was made to the source's readings.

Recension note: **every difference found between the witnesses is listed here, and none has been reconciled away.**

**(1) The Brāhmaṇa and the manual differ in six places in the mantra to the sun**, at unit 11. The accented Brāhmaṇa reads `हृ॒द्रो॒गं` where the base text reads `हृद्रो॒गं`, one anudātta more; `हरि॒माण᳚म्` where the base text reads `हरि॒माणं᳚`, the same sign one letter earlier; **`हारिद्र॒वेषु॑` where the base text reads `हरिद्र॒वेषु॑`**, a difference of the word itself; `आदि॒त्यः । विश्वे॑न` where the base text runs the sandhi together as `आदि॒त्यो विश्वे॑न`; **`मम॑ र॒न्धयन्॑` where the base text reads `मह्यं॑ रं॒धय॒न्`**; and **`द्वि॒ष॒तो र॑धम्` where the base text reads `द्वि॒ष॒ते र॑धम्`**. The base text's readings are kept throughout, because it is a ritual manual quoting the passage in its own pāṭha, and the Brāhmaṇa's are given here so that a reciter who follows the pāṭha can find them. The Brāhmaṇa also continues the passage beyond where the manual stops.

**(2) The Āraṇyaka runs the water mantra together where the manual splits it.** The Āraṇyaka reads `म॑यो॒भुव॒स्ता न॑`, `यो वः॑ शि॒वत॑मो॒ रस॒स्तस्य॑` and `तस्मा॒ अरं॑ गमाम वो॒ यस्य॒`; the manual prints `म॑यो॒भुव॑ः । ता न॑`, `यो व॑श्शि॒वत॑मो॒ रस॑ः । तस्य॑` and `तस्मा॒ अरं॑ गमाम वः । यस्य॒`. **This is a pāṭha difference within one śākhā, not a variant reading**, and it is exactly the case the svara rule warns not to mistake for cross-śākhā contamination. One genuine accent difference stands beside it: the Āraṇyaka reads `मा॒तरः॑` where the manual reads `मा॒त॑रः`. Not reconciled.

**(3) The second manual is a much shorter rite.** It has nine numbered sections against this one's twenty-two units. **It does not have** the invocation of the nine deities into the nine strands, the three knot mantras to Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Rudra, the sixteen services offered to the thread, the showing of the thread to the sun, the second and third threads, the recitation of the Gāyatrī, or the long release mantra `समुद्रं गच्छ स्वाहा`. **It has** a driving-away of the spirits, a prāṇapratiṣṭhā using Ṛgveda 10.59.6 and `अमृतं वै प्राणाः`, an abhivādana, and a closing samarpaṇa with `यस्य स्मृत्या` and `कायेन वाचा`. **Nothing was imported from it in either direction.** This is a difference in the extent of two manuals of one tradition.

**(4) The two manuals give the mantra a different metre.** The base text reads `परमेष्ठी ऋषिः, परब्रह्म परमात्मा देवता, त्रिष्टुप् छंदः`; the second manual reads `परमेष्टि परब्रह्मर्​षिः, परमात्म देवता, देवी गायत्रीच्छन्दः`. **A real disagreement about the viniyoga, deliberately not reconciled.**

**(5) `(गृहस्थः प्रति – )`, "for the householder", is printed twice in the base text** — once before the second thread and once before the third — and the second of the two stands before the line `आचम्य`, where it reads oddly. It is transcribed as printed at units 14 and 15. The second manual has neither.

**(6) The base text writes `त्स` where the second manual and the Brāhmaṇa write `थ्स`** (`तत्सवितुर्` against `तथ्सवितुर्`). The same cluster is discussed in `02_brahma_yajna_vidhi.txt` in this folder, where two independent Taittirīya editions spell it `थ्स`; it is the tradition's own spelling of that sound, and each edition is left as it prints it.

Not collated: the long release mantra `समुद्रं गच्छ स्वाहा` at unit 21, which was searched for in both the accented Āraṇyaka and the accented Brāhmaṇa and found in neither, so it stands on the base text alone; the four verses said over the worn thread at units 19 and 20; the invocations into the nine strands and the three cords at unit 6; the sixteen services at unit 10; and the saṅkalpa sentences. These are ritual Sanskrit and paddhati prose rather than Veda, they are unaccented in the source, and no second edition of each was consulted. The Gāyatrī dhyāna verse at unit 17 is the same verse that stands at unit 46 of `../kanva/01_shukla_yajurveda_sandhyavandanam.txt`; the two files print it from their own sources and neither was used to correct the other.

Verse count: 22 units. Not a verse count in the stotra sense — the work is an order of service punctuated by mantras, and the numbering is this corpus's, not the source's.

Blurb: This is the rite for putting on a new sacred thread. It is performed once a year at the Upākarma, and again whenever the thread becomes worn or unclean. The order of the rite matters. The worshipper first sips water, controls the breath and states his intention. He then consecrates the new thread with water, invokes a deity into each of its nine strands and a Veda into each of its three cords, and invokes Brahmā, Viṣṇu and Rudra into its three knots. He worships the thread with the sixteen services, shows it to the sun, and only then puts it on. A householder wears a second thread, and a third in place of the upper garment. He recites the Gāyatrī ten times so that the mantra takes hold in the new thread. **The old thread is taken off last, never first**, so that he is never without one. He speaks to it with respect, thanks it for the years it has carried him, and lets it go.

यज्ञोपवीतं यदि जीर्णवंतं
वेदांत नित्यं परब्रह्म सत्यम् ।
आयुष्यमग्र्यं प्रतिमुंच शुभ्रं
यज्ञोपवीतं विसृजस्तुतेजः ॥
iast:
yajñopavītaṁ yadi jīrṇavaṁtaṁ
vedāṁta nityaṁ parabrahma satyam |
āyuṣyamagryaṁ pratimuṁca śubhraṁ
yajñopavītaṁ visṛjastutejaḥ ||
en: If the sacred thread has grown old — the Vedānta is eternal, the supreme Brahman is true. Put on the bright thread that is the first giver of long life, and let the old thread go with its splendour.
tel: యజ్ఞోపవీతము జీర్ణమైనచో — వేదాంతము నిత్యము, పరబ్రహ్మము సత్యము. ఆయువును ఇచ్చు శుభ్రమైన దానిని ధరించుము, పాత యజ్ఞోపవీతమును తేజస్సుతో విడుచుము.
hi: यदि यज्ञोपवीत जीर्ण हो गया हो — वेदांत नित्य है, परब्रह्म सत्य है। आयु देने वाले शुभ्र को धारण करो, और पुराने यज्ञोपवीत को उसके तेज सहित छोड़ो।

--- unit 21 | section: Giving up the old thread ---
vidhi:
Then say the long mantra of release and take the old thread off.
deva:
जीर्णयज्ञोपवीत विसर्जन मंत्रम् –
स॒मु॒द्रं ग॑च्छ॒ स्वाहा॑ऽन्तरि॑क्षं गच्छ॒ स्वाहा॑ दे॒वग्ं स॑वि॒तारं॑ गच्छ॒ स्वाहा॑ऽहोरा॒त्रे ग॑च्छ॒ स्वाहा॑ मि॒त्रावरु॑णौ गच्छ॒ स्वाहा॒ सोमं॑ गच्छ॒ स्वाहा॑ य॒ज्ञं ग॑च्छ॒ स्वाहा॒ छन्दाग्ं॑सि गच्छ॒ स्वाहा॒ द्यावा॑ पृथि॒वी ग॑च्छ॒ स्वाहा॒ नभो॑ दि॒व्यं ग॑च्छ॒ स्वाहा॒ऽग्निं वै᳚श्वान॒रं ग॑च्छ॒ स्वाहा॒ऽद्भ्यस्त्वौष॑धीभ्यो॒ मनो॑ मे॒ हार्दि॑ यच्छ त॒नूं त्वचं॑ पु॒त्रं नप्ता॑रमशीय॒ शुग॑सि॒ तम॒भि शो॑च॒ यो᳚ऽस्मान् द्वेष्टि॒ यं च॑ व॒यं द्वि॒ष्मो धाम्नो॑धाम्नो राजन्नि॒तो व॑रुण नो मुञ्च॒ यदापो॒ अंघ्नि॑या॒ वरु॒णेति॒ शपा॑महे॒ ततो॑ वरुण नो मुञ्च ॥
इति जीर्ण यज्ञोपवीतं विसृजेत् ।
iast:
jīrṇayajñopavīta visarjana maṁtram –
samudraṁ gaccha svāhā'ntarikṣaṁ gaccha svāhā devagṁ savitāraṁ gaccha svāhā'horātre gaccha svāhā mitrāvaruṇau gaccha svāhā somaṁ gaccha svāhā yajñaṁ gaccha svāhā chandāgṁsi gaccha svāhā dyāvā pṛthivī gaccha svāhā nabho divyaṁ gaccha svāhā'gniṁ vaiśvānaraṁ gaccha svāhā'dbhyastvauṣadhībhyo mano me hārdi yaccha tanūṁ tvacaṁ putraṁ naptāramaśīya śugasi tamabhi śoca yo'smān dveṣṭi yaṁ ca vayaṁ dviṣmo dhāmnodhāmno rājannito varuṇa no muñca yadāpo aṁghniyā varuṇeti śapāmahe tato varuṇa no muñca ||
iti jīrṇa yajñopavītaṁ visṛjet |
en: The mantra for releasing the worn sacred thread. Go to the ocean, svāhā. Go to the mid-region, svāhā. Go to the god Savitā, svāhā. Go to day and night, svāhā. Go to Mitra and Varuṇa, svāhā. Go to Soma, svāhā. Go to the sacrifice, svāhā. Go to the metres, svāhā. Go to heaven and earth, svāhā. Go to the divine sky, svāhā. Go to Agni Vaiśvānara, svāhā. From the waters and from the plants, give me back my mind and my heart, my body and my skin. May I obtain a son and a grandson. You are grief. Burn him who hates me and him whom I hate. O king Varuṇa, release us from every bond. Whatever we swear falsely by the waters, saying "by Varuṇa" — from that, O Varuṇa, release us. So let the worn sacred thread be given up.
tel: జీర్ణ యజ్ఞోపవీత విసర్జన మంత్రము. సముద్రమునకు వెళ్ళుము, స్వాహా. అంతరిక్షమునకు వెళ్ళుము, స్వాహా. సవితృ దేవునికి వెళ్ళుము, స్వాహా. అహోరాత్రములకు వెళ్ళుము, స్వాహా. మిత్రావరుణులకు వెళ్ళుము, స్వాహా. సోమునికి వెళ్ళుము, స్వాహా. యజ్ఞమునకు వెళ్ళుము, స్వాహా. ఛందస్సులకు వెళ్ళుము, స్వాహా. ద్యావాపృథివులకు వెళ్ళుము, స్వాహా. దివ్యమైన ఆకాశమునకు వెళ్ళుము, స్వాహా. అగ్ని వైశ్వానరునికి వెళ్ళుము, స్వాహా. జలముల నుండి ఓషధుల నుండి నా మనస్సును, హృదయమును, శరీరమును, చర్మమును నాకు ఇమ్ము. పుత్రుని పౌత్రుని పొందుదును గాక. నీవు శోకము. నన్ను ద్వేషించువానిని, నేను ద్వేషించువానిని దహించుము. ఓ వరుణ రాజా, ప్రతి బంధము నుండి మమ్ము విడిపించుము. జలములపై వరుణుని పేరు చెప్పి మేము ఏ అసత్యము శపథము చేసినను, దాని నుండి వరుణా, మమ్ము విడిపించుము. ఇట్లు జీర్ణ యజ్ఞోపవీతమును విడువవలెను.
hi: जीर्ण यज्ञोपवीत के विसर्जन का मंत्र। समुद्र को जाओ, स्वाहा। अंतरिक्ष को जाओ, स्वाहा। सविता देव को जाओ, स्वाहा। दिन और रात को जाओ, स्वाहा। मित्र और वरुण को जाओ, स्वाहा। सोम को जाओ, स्वाहा। यज्ञ को जाओ, स्वाहा। छंदों को जाओ, स्वाहा। द्यावापृथिवी को जाओ, स्वाहा। दिव्य आकाश को जाओ, स्वाहा। अग्नि वैश्वानर को जाओ, स्वाहा। जल और औषधियों से मेरा मन और हृदय, शरीर और त्वचा मुझे लौटा दो। मैं पुत्र और पौत्र प्राप्त करूँ। तुम शोक हो। जो मुझसे द्वेष करता है और जिससे मैं द्वेष करता हूँ, उसे जलाओ। हे राजा वरुण, हर बंधन से हमें मुक्त करो। जल पर वरुण के नाम से जो असत्य शपथ हम करते हैं, उससे हे वरुण हमें मुक्त करो। इस प्रकार जीर्ण यज्ञोपवीत का विसर्जन करना चाहिए।
