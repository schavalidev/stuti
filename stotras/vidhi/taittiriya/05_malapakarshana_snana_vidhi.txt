Recension note: **every difference found between the two witnesses is listed here, and none has been reconciled away.**

**(1) The guttural nasal is spelled differently.** The base text writes `शग्ग्` and `वयग्ग्` where the independent edition writes `śagṁ` and `vayagṁ`, and writes `अपकामग्` where that edition writes `apakāmagṁ`. This is one sound written two ways, not two readings. The base text's spelling is kept, because that is what this corpus does with the sibling Taittirīya files.

**(2) `रफ्सुषदः` against `apsuṣadaḥ`.** At ṛc 4 the base text prints `अ॒ग्नीग्ं र॑फ्सु॒षदो॑`, with `फ्`; the independent edition prints `agnīgṁrapsuṣado`, with `प्`. The word is the same, and the sense is the same — the fires that sit in the waters. The base text is printed as it stands. Not reconciled.

**(3) `अतिष्ठत्स्यन्दमानाः` against `atiṣṭhathsyandamānāḥ`.** At ṛc 8 the two editions write the same sound with `त्स्` and with `थ्स्`. The base text uses `थ्स्` itself elsewhere, at `कामा॒न्थ्सम॑र्धयन्तु`. This is a spelling convention of the tradition, not a reading.

**(4) One accent mark is missing from the base text, and it has not been supplied.** In the passage of TB 3.2.4 the base text prints `भ्रातृ॑व्येभ्यः प्रहृत्य॑`, with no anudātta on `प्र`; the independent edition reads `pra` with the anudātta, giving `प्र॒हृत्य॑`. Accent is transcribed or absent, never derived, and the rule against supplying it holds even when a second witness in the same śākhā shows what the mark should be. The line is printed as the base text prints it, and the difference is recorded here instead.

**(5) The word divisions differ in several places** — `नि धत्त` against `निधत्त`, `सं प्रयतीः` against `संप्रयतीः`, `अनु स्थन` against `अनुस्थन`, `सं भव` against `संभव`, `मधुपृचां अरंगमः` against `मधुपृचा मरंगमः`. The akṣaras are the same in each place. The base text's divisions are kept.

**(6) The pada-pāṭha is not carried.** The base text prints the pada-pāṭha of each ṛc between `//` marks below it. No file in this corpus carries a pada-pāṭha — neither the seven accented files of `../../veda/taittiriya/` nor the Mahānyāsa in this folder — and the reader recites the saṁhitā-pāṭha. It is therefore left out here as well, and this is recorded so that the next person knows it was in the source and was a decision rather than an oversight.

**(7) On Gītā Press.** No Gītā Press witness applies. Gītā Press publishes the northern rite, and under the standing rule an edition in the wrong śākhā is not a weaker witness but no witness at all.

**(8) On GRETIL.** GRETIL was checked with `bin/gretil.py` and holds no Taittirīya Saṁhitā, Brāhmaṇa or Āraṇyaka. There is no critical edition of this text.

Blurb: This is the bath that removes impurity. It is performed before the recitation of the Śrī Rudram, when the image is bathed with pure water. The worshipper does not pour the water in silence. He recites the hymn to the waters, and he speaks to the waters themselves. They are of golden colour, they are clear, and they make clean. He asks them to look upon him kindly, to touch his skin kindly, and to place lustre, strength and vigour in him. The hymn then tells how the rivers received their names. After the hymn comes the passage that explains why the water is taken, and then the pāvamānī verses, in which Agni, Savitṛ, the wind, heaven and earth, Indra, Soma, Varuṇa and Yama are each asked to make the worshipper clean. The rite closes by declaring that the waters are all this world, and by offering the bath to Rudra.

--- unit 16 | section: The cups of water ---
vidhi:
Recite the passage of the Saṁhitā that explains why the water is taken. It is not addressed to the waters. It states what the rite does.

अ॒पां ग्रहा᳚न्गृह्णात्ये॒तद्वाव रा॑ज॒सूयं॒ यदे॒ते ग्रहाः᳚ स॒वो᳚ऽग्निर्व॑रुणस॒वो रा॑ज॒सूय॑मग्निस॒वश्चित्य॒स्ताभ्या॑मे॒व सू॑य॒तेऽथो॑ उ॒भावे॒व लो॒काव॒भि ज॑यति॒ यश्च॑ राज॒सूये॑नेजा॒नस्य॒ यश्चा᳚ग्नि॒चित॒ आपो॑ भव॒न्त्यापो॒ वा अ॒ग्नेर्भ्रातृ॑व्या॒ यद॒पो᳚ऽग्नेर॒धस्ता॑दुप॒दधा॑ति॒ भ्रातृ॑व्याभिभूत्यै॒ भव॑त्या॒त्मना॒ परा᳚ऽस्य॒ भ्रातृ॑व्यो भवत्य॒मृतं॑ वा आप॒स्तस्मा॑द॒द्भिरव॑तान्तम॒भिषि॑ञ्चन्ति॒ नाऽऽर्ति॒मार्छ॑ति॒ सर्व॒मायु॑रेति॒ ॥
iast:
apāṁ grahāngṛhṇātyetadvāva rājasūyaṁ yadete grahāḥ savo'gnirvaruṇasavo rājasūyamagnisavaścityastābhyāmeva sūyate'tho ubhāveva lokāvabhi jayati yaśca rājasūyenejānasya yaścāgnicita āpo bhavantyāpo vā agnerbhrātṛvyā yadapo'gneradhastādupadadhāti bhrātṛvyābhibhūtyai bhavatyātmanā parā'sya bhrātṛvyo bhavatyamṛtaṁ vā āpastasmādadbhiravatāntamabhiṣiñcanti nā''rtimārchati sarvamāyureti ||
en: He takes the cups of water. This is the rājasūya, namely these cups. The pressing is Agni. Varuṇa's pressing is the rājasūya. Agni's pressing is the piling of the fire altar. By these two he is consecrated. He thus wins both worlds, the world of one who has performed the rājasūya and the world of one who has piled the fire. The waters come into being. The waters are the rivals of Agni. When he places the waters below Agni, it is for the overcoming of rivals. He remains himself, and his rival is driven away. The waters are that which does not die. Therefore they sprinkle a man with water to the very end. He does not come to harm. He lives out his full span of life.
tel: అతడు జలముల గ్రహములను గ్రహించును. ఈ గ్రహములే రాజసూయము. సవము అగ్ని. వరుణసవము రాజసూయము. అగ్నిసవము అగ్నిచయనము. ఈ రెండింటిచేతనే అతడు అభిషిక్తుడగును. అందువలన రాజసూయము చేసినవాని లోకమును, అగ్నిచయనము చేసినవాని లోకమును — ఈ రెండు లోకములను అతడు జయించును. జలములు కలుగును. జలములు అగ్నికి భ్రాతృవ్యములు. అగ్నికి క్రింద జలములను ఉంచుట భ్రాతృవ్యులను జయించుట కొరకే. అతడు తానుగా నిలుచును, అతని భ్రాతృవ్యుడు తొలగిపోవును. జలములు అమృతము. అందువలననే చివరివరకు జలముతో అభిషేకము చేయుదురు. అతడు ఆర్తిని పొందడు. అతడు పూర్ణాయుష్షును పొందును.
