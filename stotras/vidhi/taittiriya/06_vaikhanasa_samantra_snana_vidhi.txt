Recension note: two passages of this chapter also stand elsewhere in the corpus, from other editions, and the readings differ. **Nothing was reconciled, no brackets were used, and no other file was altered.**

**(1) The classification of baths.** Unit 1 gives the bath as twofold, gauṇa and mukhya, and then divides the chief kind, the vāruṇa, into six — nitya, naimittika, kāmya, kriyāṅga, malāpakarṣaṇa and kriyā. `../samanya/09_snana_bheda_bhasma_gomaya_mrittika_varuna_panchagavya.txt`, written from Gītā Press and the *Dharmasindhu*, gives a **sevenfold** classification instead, by the means used — māntra, bhauma, āgneya, vāyavya, divya, vāruṇa and mānasa. **These are two different classifications and not two readings of one.** One divides by the means, the other divides the water bath by the occasion. The vāruṇa bath is the single term the two share. The two files should be read together and neither supersedes the other.

**(2) The verse of the five limbs of the bath.** Unit 3 closes with it, and this volume reads `संकल्प सूक्त पठने मार्जनं चाघमर्षणं । तर्पणं पितृदेवानां स्नानं पंचांगमुच्यते ॥`. `../samanya/06_tungabhadra_pushkara_snana_sankalpa.txt` opens with the same verse from a Telugu Puṣkara booklet, which reads `संकल्पः सूक्तपठनं मार्जनं चाघमर्षणं । देवादि तर्पणं चैव स्नानं पंचांगमुत्तमं ॥`. The five limbs are the same five in both. The second half differs in two places — `तर्पणं पितृदेवानां` against `देवादि तर्पणं चैव`, and `पंचांगमुच्यते` against `पंचांगमुत्तमं`. **This is a difference between two editions and not a spelling**, so it is recorded here and is not bracketed, and the Puṣkara file was not touched.

**(3) A third overlap, smaller.** Unit 8 carries the river-invocation `गंगेच यमुने चैव गोदावरि सरस्वति`, which the corpus holds in several places, and which `../samanya/05_nadi_snana_sankalpa_ganga.txt` discusses at length and deliberately leaves out of that file, because Gītā Press puts it in the kalaśa-pūjā and not in the bath. **Here the verse belongs to the bath**, and the reason is that this volume puts it there: the worshipper draws a square on the water with his hand and calls the rivers into it. Both placements are correct, each in its own tradition.

Blurb: This file gives the daily bath as the Vaikhānasa tradition keeps it. It is not the smārta form. It opens with the verses that say what a man gains by bathing every day, and that no rite may be done without a bath. It then sets out the kinds of bath and says which bath is meant on which occasion. The worshipper wears the pavitrī rings on both hands, does ācamana twice, and makes the saṅkalpa, in which he says that he takes this bath as a limb of the worship of the Lord and at his command. He is told where to face and in what water to bathe. He then goes to the water and asks it to carry away his wrongdoing, bows to Varuṇa and asks for the tīrtha, sprinkles himself with water and earth, and enters the river. Standing in water up to the navel he goes under four times, meditating on Vāsudeva in the midst of the sun's orb and on the stream from his feet falling upon his own head. He draws a square on the water and calls the seven rivers into it, asks the Lord's leave to use his tīrtha, and goes under four times more. He then offers water to the gods, to the sages and to the forefathers, each from its own part of the hand, and comes out.

--- unit 1 | section: What the daily bath gives, and that no rite is done without it ---
vidhi:
These verses are not recited at the bath. They state what the daily bath gives and why no rite may be done without it, and they are given so that the worshipper knows what he is doing.

आयुष्य मारोग्यमलोलुपत्वं दुस्स्वप्ननाशंच तपश्चमेधां ॥
श्लो॥ याम्यंहि यातना दुःखं प्रातः स्नायी नपश्यति ।

शोक दुस्स्वप्नहंस्नानं मोक्षदंह्लादनं तथा ।

en: For a good man given to the daily bath there is beauty, brightness, good name, cleanness, long life, health, freedom from greed, the ending of bad dreams, austerity and understanding. He who bathes in the morning does not see the pain and torment of the world of Yama. Without bathing let him do no rite at all — no recitation, no offering into fire, nothing. The bath takes away sorrow and bad dreams, it gives release, and it gives ease. The bath is said to be of two kinds, the secondary and the chief. Of these the chief is the water bath, and that again is remembered as sixfold: the daily bath, the bath for an occasion, the bath taken for a wish, the bath that is a limb of a rite, the bath that removes dirt, and, as the sixth, the bath at the end of a rite. So the bath is declared to be of six kinds.

vidhi:
These verses define each of the six kinds of the water bath named in the previous unit. They are not recited. Read them to know which bath the occasion asks for.

स्नानार्हस्तुयदा स्नाति स्नानं नैमित्तिकं हितत् ।
वृष्ट्या स्नानादिकं यत्तु दैवज्ञ तिथिचोदितं ॥

en: Half of the morning bath is called the daily bath. When one who is fit to bathe bathes after touching an outcaste, a dead body, a śūdra or the like, or a woman in her season who has bathed, that is the bath for an occasion, and it is good. The bath and the rest that is prescribed for rain, or by an astrologer, or by a tithi, is declared to be the bath taken for a wish; one who has no such wish does not use it. When a man who wishes to recite, or to make the mantras pure, or to worship the gods and the forefathers, takes a bath, the wise know that to be the bath that is a limb of a rite. The bath that removes dirt is the one preceded by rubbing with oil. In all the rites for one who has departed, the bath at the end is spoken of.

--- unit 3 | section: The sūtra, the verse of Manu and the five limbs of the bath ---
vidhi:
The sūtra names the five kinds of bath for men of the four āśramas, and the bhāṣya says that the abhiṣeka meant here is the water bath, that is, bathing by going into the water. Wear the pavitrī rings on both hands and do ācamana twice. The verse of Manu says that a bath made without a saṅkalpa bears no fruit, and the verse after it names the five limbs of the bath.
deva:
सूत्रंः अथ चतुराश्रमिणां स्नानविधि रभिषेकं दिव्यं वा यव्यमाग्नेयं गुर्वनुज्ञा इति पंचधा भवंतीति । अभिषेकं वारुणं अवगाहन स्नानमिति भाष्ये तत्प्रकारमाह । उभय पवित्रपाणिः द्विराचम्य ॥
मनुः श्लो॥ असंकल्प्य तुयः कुर्यात् स्नानदानव्रतानिच ।
अन्यानि पुण्यकर्माणि विफलानि भवंति हि ॥
संकल्प सूक्त पठने मार्जनं चाघमर्षणं ।
तर्पणं पितृदेवानां स्नानं पंचांगमुच्यते ॥
iast:
sūtraṁḥ atha caturāśramiṇāṁ snānavidhi rabhiṣekaṁ divyaṁ vā yavyamāgneyaṁ gurvanujñā iti paṁcadhā bhavaṁtīti | abhiṣekaṁ vāruṇaṁ avagāhana snānamiti bhāṣye tatprakāramāha | ubhaya pavitrapāṇiḥ dvirācamya ||
manuḥ ślo|| asaṁkalpya tuyaḥ kuryāt snānadānavratānica |
anyāni puṇyakarmāṇi viphalāni bhavaṁti hi ||
saṁkalpa sūkta paṭhane mārjanaṁ cāghamarṣaṇaṁ |
tarpaṇaṁ pitṛdevānāṁ snānaṁ paṁcāṁgamucyate ||
en: The sūtra: now, for men of the four āśramas the rule of bathing is fivefold — the abhiṣeka, the divine, the yavya, the fiery, and by the leave of the teacher. In the bhāṣya it is said that the abhiṣeka is the water bath, that is, bathing by going into the water, and the manner of it is told there. With the pavitrī on both hands, having done ācamana twice. Manu says: he who does the bath, the gift and the vows, and the other meritorious acts, without first making a saṅkalpa, finds them all fruitless. The saṅkalpa, the reciting of the sūkta, the mārjana, the aghamarṣaṇa and the tarpaṇa of the forefathers and the gods — the bath is said to have these five limbs.
tel: సూత్రము — నాలుగు ఆశ్రమములవారికి స్నాన విధి అభిషేకము, దివ్యము, యవ్యము, ఆగ్నేయము, గురువు అనుజ్ఞ అని అయిదు విధములు. అభిషేకము అనగా వారుణము, అనగా నీటిలో మునిగి చేయు స్నానమని భాష్యమున చెప్పి దాని ప్రకారమును తెలిపిరి. రెండు చేతులయందు పవిత్రములు ధరించి రెండు సార్లు ఆచమనము చేయవలెను. మనువు చెప్పెను — సంకల్పము చేయకుండ స్నానము, దానము, వ్రతములు, ఇతర పుణ్య కర్మములు చేసినచో అవి విఫలములగును. సంకల్పము, సూక్త పఠనము, మార్జనము, అఘమర్షణము, పితృదేవతల తర్పణము — ఈ అయిదు అంగములు గల స్నానమే పంచాంగ స్నానమని చెప్పబడును.
hi: सूत्र — चारों आश्रमवालों के लिए स्नानविधि पाँच प्रकार की है: अभिषेक, दिव्य, यव्य, आग्नेय और गुरु की अनुज्ञा। भाष्य में कहा है कि अभिषेक वारुण है, अर्थात् जल में डूबकर किया जाने वाला स्नान, और वहीं उसकी विधि बताई है। दोनों हाथों में पवित्री धारण कर दो बार आचमन करे। मनु कहते हैं — जो संकल्प किए बिना स्नान, दान, व्रत और अन्य पुण्यकर्म करता है, उसके वे सब निष्फल हो जाते हैं। संकल्प, सूक्त का पाठ, मार्जन, अघमर्षण और पितृदेवताओं का तर्पण — इन पाँच अंगों वाला स्नान पंचांग स्नान कहा जाता है।

--- unit 5 | section: Where to face, and in what water ---
vidhi:
These verses state the rule. The householder and the forest-dweller bathe in the morning and at midday, the ascetic three times a day, and the student once. Face the current in a river, and in a tank face the side where the deity is. Elsewhere face the sun, and at night face the fire. Water near an image of Viṣṇu is as good as the Gaṅgā, whether it is clean or not.
deva:
श्लो॥ प्रातर्माध्यं दिन स्नानं वानप्रस्थ गृहस्थ योः ॥
यतेस्त्रि षवण स्नानंस कृत्तु ब्रह्मचारिणाम् ॥
स्रोतसोभिमुख स्तत्रखाते देवमुख स्तथा ।
श्लो॥ अन्यत्रार्क मुख स्स्नायाद्रात्रौचेदग्नि सन्निधौ ।
अप्रवाहोदक स्नानं विप्रपादावसेचनं ॥
गायत्र्याजपमर्घ्यंच आदित्याभि मुखश्चरेत् ।
जलं शुद्धमशुद्धंवा विष्णुवास्तु समीपतः ॥
विष्णु गंगासमंतोयं महापातक नाशनम् ।
नातिक्रमेद्ध दंसिंधुं नानुष्ठातुंन दींतरेत् ॥
iast:
ślo|| prātarmādhyaṁ dina snānaṁ vānaprastha gṛhastha yoḥ ||
yatestri ṣavaṇa snānaṁsa kṛttu brahmacāriṇām ||
srotasobhimukha statrakhāte devamukha stathā |
ślo|| anyatrārka mukha ssnāyādrātraucedagni sannidhau |
apravāhodaka snānaṁ viprapādāvasecanaṁ ||
gāyatryājapamarghyaṁca ādityābhi mukhaścaret |
jalaṁ śuddhamaśuddhaṁvā viṣṇuvāstu samīpataḥ ||
viṣṇu gaṁgāsamaṁtoyaṁ mahāpātaka nāśanam |
nātikrameddha daṁsiṁdhuṁ nānuṣṭhātuṁna dīṁtaret ||
en: The bath of the morning and of midday is for the forest-dweller and the householder. For the ascetic the bath is at three times of day, and for the student once. There he faces the current; in a dug tank he faces the side where the deity is. Elsewhere let him bathe facing the sun, and at night, if it is near a fire, facing that. Bathing in water that does not flow, washing the feet of a brāhmaṇa, the recitation of the Gāyatrī and the offering of arghya — these he does facing the sun. Water is clean or unclean, but if it is near an image of Viṣṇu, that water is equal to the Gaṅgā and destroys great wrongs. Let him not cross the flowing river beyond his measure, and let him not swim across while an observance is upon him.

--- unit 6 | section: Going to the water ---
vidhi:
Go towards the water saying this.
deva:
सूत्रंः इदमापः प्रवहते त्यभिगम्य -
मंत्रः इदमापः प्रवहता वद्यंच मलंचयत् । यद्वाभि रुद्रहान्नृता यद्वाशेपो अभीरुणं । आपो मातस्मादेनसो विश्वान्मुंचं त्वग्ं हसः । निर्मा मुंचामिशपथान्निर्मा वरुणादधि । निर्मायमस्य पड्बिशात् सर्वस्माद्देव किल्बिषात् । इदमापः प्रणीत भेषजं तन्मे वरुणं नमः ॥
इति जलसमीपं गत्वा । हिरण्य शृंगमिति प्रणम्य ॥
iast:
sūtraṁḥ idamāpaḥ pravahate tyabhigamya -
maṁtraḥ idamāpaḥ pravahatā vadyaṁca malaṁcayat | yadvābhi rudrahānnṛtā yadvāśepo abhīruṇaṁ | āpo mātasmādenaso viśvānmuṁcaṁ tvagṁ hasaḥ | nirmā muṁcāmiśapathānnirmā varuṇādadhi | nirmāyamasya paḍbiśāt sarvasmāddeva kilbiṣāt | idamāpaḥ praṇīta bheṣajaṁ tanme varuṇaṁ namaḥ ||
iti jalasamīpaṁ gatvā | hiraṇya śṛṁgamiti praṇamya ||
en: The sūtra: approach saying, this, O waters. Mantra: this, O waters, carry away — whatever in me is blameworthy and whatever is dirt. Whatever falsehood I have spoken, and whatever curse. O waters, mother, free me from that wrong, from all of it, from every sin. vidhi:
Stand facing east in water up to the navel, do ācamana once, and offer water to the gods from the tip of the fingers, holding the hand upwards and towards the east. Then wear the sacred thread in the ordinary way and offer to the sages from the side of the hand, holding it sideways and towards the north. Then wear the sacred thread over the right shoulder and offer to the forefathers from the base of the thumb, facing south and holding the hand downwards. Do ācamana after. Then take water in the joined hands, say the closing verse, and throw the water outside the river. Come out, sit facing south, and dry the head and the body with two separate dry cloths. Do not shake the hair out.
