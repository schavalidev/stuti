Title: Māgha-snāna Vidhi — the bathing rite of the month of Māgha
Devanāgarī: माघस्नानविधिः
Telugu: మాఘ స్నాన విధి

Author: Anonymous. The verses are the received formulae of the Purāṇas and the ritual manuals. No composer is named for any of them in the source.

Type: vidhi (ritual manual) — not a stotra. Uses the one-extra-field vidhi format documented in `../madhyandina/01_sandhyopasana_vidhi.txt`: a `vidhi:` field carrying the ritual instruction, then deva / iast / en / tel / hi.

Recension / paddhati: **not śākhā-specific**, which is why the file sits in `vidhi/samanya/`. Māgha-snāna is a naimittika observance of the month, its authority is the Purāṇic literature and the nibandhas, and it is kept in both the northern and the southern traditions. The source used is a northern one, and where it and the western nibandha differ, that is recorded below.

Accent: **none, in any witness.** These are Purāṇic ślokas. No accent belongs to them and none is supplied.

Source / recension: the authority and the base text is **Gītā Press, Gorakhpur — *Vrata-paricaya***, archive.org item `vrata-parichaya` (`_djvu.txt` OCR fetched 2026-09-12; 1,530,043 bytes). Its first entry under the vratas of Māgha is `(१) माघस्नान (नानापुराणादि)`, pages 196-198, and every verse in this file is taken from it.

The volume gives the rule of the month as well as the words. Māgha, Kārtika and Vaiśākha are the three months held most holy for bathing. The bath is best at sunrise, and its merit falls away the later it is taken. Kāśī and Prayāga are the best places for it; where the worshipper cannot go there, he bathes where he is and remembers them. The month runs either from the eleventh of the bright fortnight of Pauṣa to the eleventh of the bright fortnight of Māgha, or from the full moon of Pauṣa to the full moon of Māgha, or through the thirty days the sun stands in Makara. The volume states plainly that **every person may keep it** — of any of the four āśramas, of any of the four varṇas, at any age, and whether man, woman or neither.

Independent collation: **against the *Dharmasindhu* of Kāśīnātha Upādhyāya**, archive.org item `in.ernet.dli.2015.484720` (the edition with Marathi translation; `_djvu.txt` OCR fetched 2026-09-12; 5,483,144 bytes). This is a western nibandha lineage of 1790 and a genuinely different editorial line from the Gorakhpur volume. Its own Māgha-snāna section was read and compared verse by verse.

**Where the two agree.** The saṅkalpa verse at unit 5, `दुःखदारिद्र्यनाशाय श्रीविष्णोस्तोषणाय च। प्रातःस्नानं करोम्यद्य माघे पापविनाशनम्॥`, stands in both, word for word. The arghya verse at unit 6, `सवित्रे प्रसवित्रे च परं धाम जले मम। त्वत्तेजसा परिभ्रष्टं पापं यातु सहस्रधा॥`, stands in both, word for word, and both give it as the verse said to the sun every day of the month. The two witnesses therefore agree on the heart of the rite.

**Where they differ — two places, both recorded and neither adopted.** First, the *Dharmasindhu* prints the prayer to the water at unit 4 with the ending `वाङ्मनःकायकर्मजम्` — "done by speech, by mind, by body and by act" — where Gītā Press prints `वाङ्मनःकर्मभिः कृतम्`. Second, the *Dharmasindhu* prints after the saṅkalpa a further verse that Gītā Press does not carry at all: `मकरस्थे रवौ सार्धं गोविन्दाच्युत माधव। स्नानेनानेन मे देव यथोक्तफलदो भव॥`. It also opens its saṅkalpa with a verse of its own beginning `माघमासमिमं पूर्णं स्नास्येऽहं देव माधव`.

**One caution about that witness.** The *Dharmasindhu* scan runs its words together and drops conjuncts, so those readings are reported here as what the scan shows and are not offered as a corrected text. The two verses on which the witnesses agree came through both scans cleanly and are the firm part of this collation.

**Not collated.** The three verses at units 1 to 3 that call the tīrthas into the water were searched for in the *Dharmasindhu* and in the *Nirṇayasindhu* of Kamalākara Bhaṭṭa (archive.org `zajy-nirnayasindhu-with-commentary-of-krishnam-bhatta-b`, Chowkhambā, Benares 1930; `_djvu.txt` fetched 2026-09-12; 3,053,279 bytes). `पुष्करादीनि`, `हरिद्वारे` and `अयोध्या मथुरा` return zero occurrences in both. They rest on the Gītā Press volume alone.

Recension note: the Gītā Press readings are kept throughout, under the standing rule that a Gītā Press edition is the authority where one exists. The *Dharmasindhu*'s two differences are recorded above and were **not** adopted and **not** put in brackets — the first because the scan is not clean enough to make a bracketed reading trustworthy, and the second because a verse the other edition does not print at all is a recension difference and not a word.

`गंगे च यमुने चैव` at unit 3 is worth one line. The corpus's `05_nadi_snana_sankalpa_ganga.txt` leaves that verse out, because the volume used there prints it only for the invocation of the waters into the kalaśa at a pūjā. **Here it is different.** The Gītā Press *Vrata-paricaya* gives it in the bath itself, as one of the things said when the worshipper cannot reach Kāśī or Prayāga. So the verse enters this corpus in the place where a source actually puts it in a bath.

Cross-reference: `04_karthika_snana_vidhi.txt` is the bath of the month of Kārtika and `06_tungabhadra_pushkara_snana_sankalpa.txt` is the Puṣkara bath in the southern form. `05_nadi_snana_sankalpa_ganga.txt` is the daily river bath in the northern form. All four are separate rites with separate sources, and **none supersedes another**.

Blurb: Māgha is one of the three months in which a daily bath is held to carry great merit, and this file gives what is said at it. The bath is taken at sunrise, every day of the month, at a holy place if possible and at home if not. The worshipper first calls the holy waters to come into the water before him, naming Puṣkara and the rivers. He remembers the places where bathing is said to end rebirth, and the seven cities that give release. He says the verse that asks the Gaṅgā, the Yamunā, the Godāvarī, the Sarasvatī, the Narmadā, the Sindhu and the Kāverī to be present in this water. He then prays to the water itself to destroy the wrong he has done, states his intention to take the Māgha bath, and after bathing offers arghya to the sun.

--- unit 1 | section: Calling the holy waters ---
vidhi:
Say this at the water before bathing. It is said wherever the bath is taken, at a holy place or at home.
deva:
पुष्करादीनि तीर्थानि गंगाद्याः सरितस्तथा ।
आगच्छन्तु पवित्राणि स्नानकाले सदा मम ॥
iast:
puṣkarādīni tīrthāni gaṁgādyāḥ saritastathā |
āgacchantu pavitrāṇi snānakāle sadā mama ||
en: May Puṣkara and the other holy places, and the Gaṅgā and the other rivers, which make all things clean, come here always at the time of my bath.
tel: పుష్కరము మొదలైన తీర్థములు, గంగ మొదలైన నదులు — పవిత్రము చేయు ఆ జలములు అన్నియు నా స్నాన సమయమునందు సదా ఇచ్చటికి వచ్చునుగాక.
hi: पुष्कर आदि तीर्थ और गंगा आदि नदियाँ, जो सबको पवित्र करती हैं, मेरे स्नान के समय सदा यहाँ आएँ।

--- unit 2 | section: Remembering the holy places ---
vidhi:
Say this next. Kāśī and Prayāga are the best places for the Māgha bath. A worshipper who cannot go there bathes where he is and remembers them by these verses.
deva:
हरिद्वारे कुशावर्ते बिल्वके नीलपर्वते ।
स्नात्वा कनखले तीर्थे पुनर्जन्म न विद्यते ॥
अयोध्या मथुरा माया काशी कांची अवन्तिका ।
पुरी द्वारावती ज्ञेयाः सप्तैता मोक्षदायिकाः ॥
iast:
haridvāre kuśāvarte bilvake nīlaparvate |
snātvā kanakhale tīrthe punarjanma na vidyate ||
ayodhyā mathurā māyā kāśī kāṁcī avantikā |
purī dvārāvatī jñeyāḥ saptaitā mokṣadāyikāḥ ||
en: Bathing at Haridvāra, at Kuśāvarta, at Bilvaka, at Nīlaparvata and at the holy place of Kanakhala, there is no birth again. Ayodhyā, Mathurā, Māyā, Kāśī, Kāñcī, Avantikā, and Purī and Dvāravatī — these seven are known to be the cities that give release.
tel: హరిద్వారమునందు, కుశావర్తమునందు, బిల్వకమునందు, నీలపర్వతమునందు, కనఖల తీర్థమునందు స్నానము చేసినవానికి పునర్జన్మము లేదు. అయోధ్య, మథుర, మాయ, కాశి, కాంచి, అవంతిక, పురి, ద్వారావతి — ఈ ఏడు మోక్షమును ఇచ్చు పట్టణములుగా తెలియవలెను.
hi: हरिद्वार, कुशावर्त, बिल्वक, नीलपर्वत और कनखल तीर्थ में स्नान करने से पुनर्जन्म नहीं होता। अयोध्या, मथुरा, माया, काशी, कांची, अवन्तिका, पुरी और द्वारावती — ये सात मोक्ष देने वाली नगरियाँ जानी जाती हैं।

--- unit 3 | section: The seven rivers in this water ---
vidhi:
Say this over the water in which you are about to bathe.
deva:
गंगे च यमुने चैव गोदावरि सरस्वति ।
नर्मदे सिन्धु कावेरि जलेऽस्मिन् संनिधिं कुरु ॥
iast:
gaṁge ca yamune caiva godāvari sarasvati |
narmade sindhu kāveri jale'smin saṁnidhiṁ kuru ||
en: O Gaṅgā, O Yamunā, O Godāvarī, O Sarasvatī, O Narmadā, O Sindhu, O Kāverī — be present in this water.
tel: ఓ గంగా, ఓ యమునా, ఓ గోదావరీ, ఓ సరస్వతీ, ఓ నర్మదా, ఓ సింధూ, ఓ కావేరీ — ఈ జలమునందు సన్నిధి కలిగియుండుడు.
hi: हे गंगा, हे यमुना, हे गोदावरी, हे सरस्वती, हे नर्मदा, हे सिन्धु, हे कावेरी — इस जल में सन्निधि करो।

--- unit 4 | section: The prayer to the water ---
vidhi:
Say this at the beginning of the bath, as a prayer to the water.
deva:
आपस्त्वमसि देवेश ज्योतिषां पतिरेव च ।
पापं नाशय मे देव वाङ्मनःकर्मभिः कृतम् ॥
iast:
āpastvamasi deveśa jyotiṣāṁ patireva ca |
pāpaṁ nāśaya me deva vāṅmanaḥkarmabhiḥ kṛtam ||
en: You are the waters, O lord of the gods, and you are the lord of the lights. O god, destroy the wrong I have done by speech, by mind and by act.
tel: దేవదేవా, నీవే జలము, నీవే జ్యోతుల పతివి. దేవా, వాక్కుచేత, మనస్సుచేత, కర్మచేత నేను చేసిన పాపమును నశింపజేయుము.
hi: हे देवेश, आप ही जल हैं और आप ही ज्योतियों के पति हैं। हे देव, वाणी, मन और कर्म से किया हुआ मेरा पाप नष्ट कर दीजिए।

--- unit 5 | section: Saṅkalpa ---
vidhi:
Say this as the statement of intention, praying to the Lord before going into the water.
deva:
दुःखदारिद्र्यनाशाय श्रीविष्णोस्तोषणाय च ।
प्रातःस्नानं करोम्यद्य माघे पापविनाशनम् ॥
iast:
duḥkhadāridryanāśāya śrīviṣṇostoṣaṇāya ca |
prātaḥsnānaṁ karomyadya māghe pāpavināśanam ||
en: For the destruction of sorrow and poverty, and for the pleasure of Śrī Viṣṇu, I now take the morning bath of Māgha, which destroys wrongdoing.
tel: దుఃఖము, దారిద్ర్యము నశించుట కొరకు, శ్రీ విష్ణువు ప్రీతి కొరకు, పాపములను నశింపజేయు మాఘ ప్రాతః స్నానమును ఈ దినమున చేయుచున్నాను.
hi: दुःख और दरिद्रता के नाश के लिए और श्री विष्णु की प्रसन्नता के लिए, मैं आज माघ का प्रातःस्नान करता हूँ, जो पापों का नाश करने वाला है।

--- unit 6 | section: Arghya to the sun ---
vidhi:
Say this after the bath and offer arghya to the sun. Then worship Hari, or remember him.
deva:
सवित्रे प्रसवित्रे च परं धाम जले मम ।
त्वत्तेजसा परिभ्रष्टं पापं यातु सहस्रधा ॥
iast:
savitre prasavitre ca paraṁ dhāma jale mama |
tvattejasā paribhraṣṭaṁ pāpaṁ yātu sahasradhā ||
en: To the one who brings forth, to the one who sets in motion, whose highest abode is in this water of mine — may the wrong in me, burnt away by your light, go from me a thousandfold.
tel: ప్రసవించువాడవైన, ప్రేరేపించువాడవైన నీ పరమ ధామము ఈ నా జలమునందు కలదు. నీ తేజస్సుచేత దగ్ధమైన నా పాపము వేయి విధములుగా తొలగిపోవునుగాక.
hi: प्रसव करने वाले और प्रेरित करने वाले आपका परम धाम मेरे इस जल में है। आपके तेज से जला हुआ मेरा पाप सहस्र प्रकार से दूर हो जाए।
