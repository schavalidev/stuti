Title: Vaiśākha-snāna Vidhi — the bathing rite of the month of Vaiśākha
Devanāgarī: वैशाखस्नानविधिः
Telugu: వైశాఖ స్నాన విధి

Author: Anonymous. The three verses are Purāṇic — one from the Padma Purāṇa and two from the Skanda Purāṇa, as recorded below. No composer is named for any of them.

Type: vidhi (ritual manual) — not a stotra. Uses the one-extra-field vidhi format documented in `../madhyandina/01_sandhyopasana_vidhi.txt`: a `vidhi:` field carrying the ritual instruction, then deva / iast / en / tel / hi.

Recension / paddhati: **not śākhā-specific**, which is why the file sits in `vidhi/samanya/`. Vaiśākha-snāna is a naimittika observance of the month, its authority is the Purāṇic māhātmya literature, and it is kept alike in the northern and the southern traditions. The deity of the month is Madhusūdana, and every one of these verses is addressed to him.

Accent: **none, in any witness.** These are Purāṇic ślokas, not Vedic mantras. Under the standing rule accent is transcribed or absent, never derived, so none is supplied.

Source / recension: the authority and the base text is **Gītā Press, Gorakhpur — *Vaiśākha-Kārtika-Māgha-Māsa-Māhātmya*, book code GP1136**, archive.org item `gp1136-vaishakha-kartik-magha-maas-mahatmya` (`_djvu.txt` OCR fetched 2026-09-14; 1,168,618 bytes, 357,779 Devanāgarī characters). The volume prints two Vaiśākha-māhātmyas side by side, one from the Padma Purāṇa and one from the Skanda Purāṇa, and the mantras of the bath come from both:

- **Unit 1** is from the Padma Purāṇa māhātmya, in the chapter headed "वैशाखमासमें स्नान, तर्पण और श्रीमाधव-पूजनकी विधि एवं महिमा" (Ambarīṣa asks, Nārada answers), page 27. The volume prints the verse reference as `(८९।११)` and says in so many words that this śloka is uttered *before* the bath.
- **Units 2 and 3** are from the Skanda Purāṇa māhātmya, in the chapter headed "वैशाखमासमें विविध वस्तुओंके दानका महत्त्व तथा वैशाखस्नानके नियम", page 69. The volume introduces unit 2 as "वैशाखमासमें भगवान्‌को प्रार्थनाका मन्त्र इस प्रकार है" and unit 3 as "तत्पश्चात् निम्नांकित मन्त्रसे अर्घ्य प्रदान करे", so both are given as words to be said, not as narrative.

The volume also gives the rule of the month, which is what the `vidhi:` fields carry: the bath is taken every morning before sunrise while the sun stands in Meṣa, in a river, a river-tīrtha, a lake, a spring, a temple tank, a step-well or a well; a river running to the sea is better than a tank and one of the seven Gaṅgās better still, these being named as the Jāhnavī, the Vṛddhagaṅgā (Godāvarī), the Kālindī, the Sarasvatī, the Kāverī, the Narmadā and the Veṇī. After the arghya the worshipper bathes, dresses, completes sandhyā and tarpaṇa, worships Viṣṇu with the flowers of the season, and hears the māhātmya of the month.

Both are Vārāṇasī-and-Kurukṣetra commercial prints of the Sanskrit with a vernacular gloss, a different editorial line from the Gorakhpur volume, and they carry units 2 and 3 as verses 32 and 33 of their chapter. **Both agree with Gītā Press word for word on both verses**, allowing for what their scans do to conjuncts. The heart of this rite is therefore attested three times over.

**Unit 1 has no second witness and this is a real gap.** The Padma Purāṇa Pāṭāla-khaṇḍa and Uttara-khaṇḍa of the Khemrāj 1895–96 printing (archive.org `sri-padma-maha-purana-part-5-patal-khanda-khemraj-1896-sanskrti-printed-jangamwadi-math-collection` and `...part-6-uttar-khanda-printed-khemraj-1895-...`, both fetched 2026-09-14 with sound Devanāgarī OCR, 644,314 and 1,031,762 Devanāgarī characters) were searched and **do not contain this māhātmya at all** — `वैशाख` returns 19 and 3 occurrences respectively, and `वल्लभो मधुसूदन`, `माधवो मासो` and `पापहा भव` return zero in each. The verse is not in the two Skanda printings either, because it is not a Skanda verse. GRETIL holds no Padma Purāṇa, and its two Skanda files are the Revākhaṇḍa and the critical-edition adhyāyas 1–31, neither of which is the Vaiṣṇava-khaṇḍa. So unit 1 rests on the Gītā Press volume alone.

Corrections applied to the OCR, each with its reason:

- Unit 2, `रबो` → `रवौ`. The Gorakhpur scan reads `मेषगे रबो`; ब for व and ो for ौ are the commonest Devanāgarī OCR confusions. The Kurukṣetra witness prints `रवौ` plainly, the form is the locative of `रवि` required by `मेषगे`, and the volume's own Hindi renders it "सूर्यके मेषराशिमें स्थित". The correction is made on the witness, not on grammar alone.
- Unit 3, `परायण:` → `परायणः`. The Gorakhpur scan writes the visarga as an **ASCII colon (U+003A)**. Both Sanskrit witnesses print a true visarga. This is the same OCR defect this corpus corrects elsewhere.
- Unit 1, a zero-width non-joiner inside `तस्मिन्‌` was removed. It is a scan artefact, not a character of the text.

No other reading was altered. Nothing was supplied from memory, and no verse was reconstructed.

Recension note: **one verse stands in the independent witnesses and not in Gītā Press, and it was not adopted.**

Both Sanskrit printings carry, as verse 34 immediately after the arghya mantra, a fourth śloka in which the rivers and tīrthas are asked to accept the offering: `गङ्गायाः सरितः सर्वास्तीर्थानि च तथा ह्रदाः । प्रतिगृह्य मया दत्तमर्घ्यं सम्यक् प्रसीदत ॥` — "O Gaṅgā, and all the rivers, and the holy places and the pools: receive the arghya I have given and be gracious." The Gītā Press volume does not print it; `गङ्गायाः` and `प्रतिगृह्य` return zero occurrences in the whole book. Under the standing rule, a verse that one edition carries and the other does not is a recension difference and not a word, so it is **recorded here and not put in the text and not bracketed**. A later session with a cleaner Skanda edition may decide it belongs; that decision is not made here. The two witnesses' scans of it disagree in small ways (`सर्वास्तीर्थानि` against `सर्वतीर्थानि`), which is a further reason not to print it as settled text.

**On the numbering.** The Gītā Press volume cites unit 1 as `(८९।११)` of its Padma text and prints units 2 and 3 without numbers; the two Sanskrit witnesses number units 2 and 3 as 32 and 33 of their fourth adhyāya. The numbering below is this corpus's own and follows the order of the rite, not any edition.

Not independently collated: unit 1. See above.

Blurb: Vaiśākha is one of the three months in which a daily bath at dawn is held to carry great merit, and this file gives what is said at it. The month's deity is Madhusūdana, and all three verses are addressed to him. The bath is taken every morning before sunrise while the sun stands in Meṣa, in a river or a lake or a tank or a well, for the whole month. Before entering the water the worshipper tells the Lord that this month is dear to him and asks him to make the bath bear its fruit. He then states his intention to take the Vaiśākha bath and asks that it go without hindrance. He offers arghya with the third verse, and then bathes. Afterwards he dresses, completes his daily rites, worships Viṣṇu with the flowers of the season, and hears the story of the month.

--- unit 1 | section: Prayer before the bath ---
vidhi:
Go before sunrise to a river, a river-tīrtha, a lake, a spring, a temple tank, a step-well or a well, and say this verse before bathing, remembering Viṣṇu.
deva:
यथा ते माधवो मासो वल्लभो मधुसूदन ।
प्रातःस्नानेन मे तस्मिन् फलदः पापहा भव ॥
iast:
yathā te mādhavo māso vallabho madhusūdana |
prātaḥsnānena me tasmin phaladaḥ pāpahā bhava ||
en: O Madhusūdana, as the month of Mādhava is dear to you, so, when I take the morning bath in it, be to me the giver of its fruit and the destroyer of my wrongdoing.
tel: ఓ మధుసూదనా, మాధవ మాసము నీకు ఎట్లు ప్రియమో, ఆ మాసమునందు నేను ప్రాతఃస్నానము చేయుటచేత నాకు ఫలమును ఇచ్చువాడవును, పాపమును పోగొట్టువాడవును అగుము.
hi: हे मधुसूदन, जैसे माधव (वैशाख) मास आपको प्रिय है, वैसे ही उसमें प्रातःस्नान करने से आप मुझे शास्त्रोक्त फल देने वाले और पाप हरने वाले हों।

--- unit 2 | section: Saṅkalpa ---
vidhi:
Say this as the statement of intention, praying to the Lord before going into the water. It is said every day of the month, from the full moon of Caitra to the full moon of Vaiśākha, while the sun stands in Meṣa.
deva:
मधुसूदन देवेश वैशाखे मेषगे रवौ ।
प्रातःस्नानं करिष्यामि निर्विघ्नं कुरु माधव ॥
iast:
madhusūdana deveśa vaiśākhe meṣage ravau |
prātaḥsnānaṁ kariṣyāmi nirvighnaṁ kuru mādhava ||
en: O Madhusūdana, lord of the gods, I shall take the morning bath in Vaiśākha, while the sun stands in Meṣa. O Mādhava, let it be without hindrance.
tel: ఓ మధుసూదనా, దేవదేవా, సూర్యుడు మేష రాశియందు ఉండగా వైశాఖ మాసమునందు నేను ప్రాతఃస్నానము చేయుదును. ఓ మాధవా, దానిని నిర్విఘ్నముగా జరుగునట్లు చేయుము.
hi: हे मधुसूदन, हे देवेश, सूर्य के मेष राशि में रहते हुए वैशाख मास में मैं प्रातःस्नान करूँगा। हे माधव, इसे निर्विघ्न पूर्ण कीजिये।

--- unit 3 | section: Arghya to Madhusūdana ---
vidhi:
Offer arghya with this verse, and then bathe. After the bath dress, complete sandhyā, tarpaṇa and the other daily rites, worship Viṣṇu with the flowers of the season, and hear the māhātmya of the month.
deva:
वैशाखे मेषगे भानौ प्रातःस्नानपरायणः ।
अर्घ्यं तेऽहं प्रदास्यामि गृहाण मधुसूदन ॥
iast:
vaiśākhe meṣage bhānau prātaḥsnānaparāyaṇaḥ |
arghyaṁ te'haṁ pradāsyāmi gṛhāṇa madhusūdana ||
en: In Vaiśākha, while the sun stands in Meṣa, holding to the rule of the morning bath, I shall give you arghya. Receive it, O Madhusūdana.
tel: సూర్యుడు మేష రాశియందు ఉండగా వైశాఖ మాసమునందు ప్రాతఃస్నాన నియమమునందు నిష్ఠ కలిగి నేను నీకు అర్ఘ్యమును ఇచ్చెదను. ఓ మధుసూదనా, దానిని గ్రహించుము.
hi: सूर्य के मेष राशि पर रहते हुए वैशाख मास में प्रातःस्नान के नियम में लगा हुआ मैं आपको अर्घ्य देता हूँ। हे मधुसूदन, इसे ग्रहण कीजिये।
