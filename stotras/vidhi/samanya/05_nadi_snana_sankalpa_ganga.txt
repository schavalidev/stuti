Title: Nadī-snāna Saṅkalpa — the intention stated before bathing in the Gaṅgā
Devanāgarī: नदीस्नानसङ्कल्पः (गङ्गास्नाने)
Telugu: నదీ స్నాన సంకల్పము (గంగా స్నానమునందు)

Author: Anonymous. The saṅkalpa is the received formula of the ritual manuals, and the two verses are quoted in them from earlier texts. No composer is named for any part of it.

Type: vidhi (ritual manual) — not a stotra. Uses the one-extra-field vidhi format documented in `../madhyandina/01_sandhyopasana_vidhi.txt`: a `vidhi:` field carrying the ritual instruction, then deva / iast / en / tel / hi.

Recension / paddhati: **not śākhā-specific**, which is why the file sits in `vidhi/samanya/`. The daily bath and the intention stated before it are common to the traditions, and the source used prints them in the general chapters of its manual, before any śākhā-specific matter begins.

Accent: **none, in any witness.** The saṅkalpa is ritual prose and the two verses are Smṛti and Purāṇic ślokas. None of this is Vedic chant, so no accent belongs to it and none is supplied.

Source / recension: the authority and the base text is **Gītā Press, Gorakhpur — *Nityakarma-Pūjāprakāśa***, archive.org item `nitya-karma-puja-prakash-gita-press-gorakhpur` (`_djvu.txt` OCR fetched 2026-09-12; 1,214,814 bytes). Three passages were used, all from its general chapters. The saṅkalpa frame is from its "संकल्प" section, pages 35-36. The closing clause of the saṅkalpa, the mṛttikā verse and the twelve names of the Gaṅgā are from its "स्नान" section, pages 41-44.

**How the saṅkalpa was assembled, stated plainly.** The volume prints the frame once, in full, ending in the generic words `अमुक कर्म करिष्ये` — "I shall do such-and-such an act". Its snāna chapter then does not reprint the frame. It refers the reader back to it and gives only the ending: `प्रातः (मध्याह्ने, सायं) स्नानं करिष्ये`. What stands at unit 1 is therefore the full frame of pages 35-36 with the snāna chapter's own ending in place of the generic one, which is what the volume itself directs the reader to do. Nothing was supplied from outside the volume.

Independent collation: **one genuinely independent witness was found, and it agrees on one of the three passages and differs on another.**

**The witness — the *Dharmasindhu* of Kāśīnātha Upādhyāya**, archive.org item `in.ernet.dli.2015.484720` (the edition with Marathi translation; `_djvu.txt` OCR fetched 2026-09-12; 5,483,144 bytes). This is a western nibandha lineage of 1790 with no connection to the Gītā Press manual, so it is a real second editorial lineage and not the same text reprinted.

**(1) The mṛttikā verse — the witnesses differ, and the difference is real.** Gītā Press prints one verse, citing Dakṣa-smṛti 2.46 and the Padma Purāṇa: `अश्वक्रान्ते रथक्रान्ते विष्णुक्रान्ते वसुन्धरे। मृत्तिके हर मे पापं यन्मया दुष्कृतं कृतम्॥`. The Dharmasindhu prints **two** verses in its place, and assigns each to a separate act: the clay is first addressed with `अश्वक्रान्ते रथक्रान्ते विष्णुक्रान्ते वसुन्धरे शिरसा धारयिष्यामि रक्षस्व मां पदे पदे`, and it is then taken into the hand with `उद्धृतासि वराहेण कृष्णेन शतबाहुना। मृत्तिके हर मे पापं यन्मया दुष्कृतं कृतम्॥`. Gītā Press's single verse is the first half of the Dharmasindhu's first verse joined to the second half of its second. See the Recension note for what was done.
*(The scan reads `दातबाहुना` at that word. The syllable is damaged in the OCR and it was not relied on for anything.)*

**(2) The saṅkalpa frame — not independently collated.** The Dharmasindhu gives saṅkalpas for particular rites and not a general snāna frame, so it does not witness unit 1. No second edition of this frame was found.

**(3) The twelve names of the Gaṅgā — not independently collated.** Gītā Press attributes the verses to the *Ācāraprakāśa* and the *Ācārendu*, page 45. Two nibandha editions were searched for them and neither carries them: the Dharmasindhu above, and the *Nirṇayasindhu* of Kamalākara Bhaṭṭa with the commentary of Kṛṣṇaṁ Bhaṭṭa, archive.org item `zajy-nirnayasindhu-with-commentary-of-krishnam-bhatta-b` (Chowkhambā, Benares 1930; `_djvu.txt` fetched 2026-09-12; 3,053,279 bytes). The words `नलिनी`, `भोगवती`, `त्रिपथगामिनी` and `द्वादशैतानि` were each searched for in both files and returned zero occurrences in both. So unit 3 rests on the Gītā Press volume alone.

Recension note: at unit 2 the Gītā Press reading is kept, under the standing rule that a Gītā Press edition is the authority where one exists. The Dharmasindhu's two-verse form is recorded above and was **not** adopted and **not** put in brackets — brackets are for a word that differs, and this is a different number of verses assigned to two different acts, which is a recension difference and not a spelling.

`गङ्गे च यमुने चैव गोदावरि सरस्वति` is the verse most people associate with bathing. It **is** in this volume, twice, but in both places it belongs to the invocation of the waters into the kalaśa at a pūjā, not to the bath. It was therefore left out of this file rather than moved into a rite the source does not put it in.

Cross-reference: `04_karthika_snana_vidhi.txt` in this folder is the bath of the month of Kārtika and has its own saṅkalpa, its own prayers and its own source. It is a different rite and neither file supersedes the other. The stotras of the Gaṅgā herself stand in `../../ganga/`.

Blurb: Before bathing in a river the worshipper states what he is about to do, and this file gives what is said. He first cleans himself on the bank, because rubbing the body is not done in the river itself. He binds his hair, wears the pavitrī rings, does ācamana, and then takes water in his right hand and makes the saṅkalpa, naming the age, the place, the day, his lineage and his own name, and saying that he now takes the morning bath. He then smears clay on his limbs with the verse addressed to the earth. Before entering the water he recites the twelve names of the Gaṅgā, in which she promises that she comes to be present in whatever water a man remembers her by those names. He then goes in up to the navel, faces the current or the sun, and takes three, five, seven or twelve immersions.

Sections:

--- unit 1 | section: Saṅkalpa ---
vidhi:
Clean the body on the bank first, not in the river. Then bind the hair, wear the pavitrī rings on both hands, purify yourself with ācamana, take water in the right hand and say this. Fill each blank with what is true of you. If you are bathing at a tīrtha, put the name of that tīrtha in the blank; if you are in a town, the town's name; if in a village, the village's name. Say prātaḥ for the morning bath, madhyāhne at midday and sāyam in the evening.
deva:
1. ॐ विष्णवे नमः ।
2. ॐ विष्णवे नमः ।
3. ॐ विष्णवे नमः ।
ॐ अद्य ब्रह्मणोऽह्नि द्वितीयपरार्धे श्रीश्वेतवाराहकल्पे वैवस्वतमन्वन्तरेऽष्टाविंशतितमे कलियुगे कलिप्रथमचरणे बौद्धावतारे भूर्लोके जम्बूद्वीपे भरतखण्डे भारतवर्षे ......क्षेत्रे ......नगरे ......ग्रामे ......नामसंवत्सरे ......मासे ......पक्षे ......तिथौ ......वासरे ......गोत्रः ......शर्माहम् प्रातः सर्वकर्मसु शुद्ध्यर्थं श्रुतिस्मृतिपुराणोक्तफलप्राप्त्यर्थं श्रीभगवत्प्रीत्यर्थं च प्रातःस्नानं करिष्ये ॥
iast:
1. oṁ viṣṇave namaḥ |
2. oṁ viṣṇave namaḥ |
3. oṁ viṣṇave namaḥ |
oṁ adya brahmaṇo'hni dvitīyaparārdhe śrīśvetavārāhakalpe vaivasvatamanvantare'ṣṭāviṁśatitame kaliyuge kaliprathamacaraṇe bauddhāvatāre bhūrloke jambūdvīpe bharatakhaṇḍe bhāratavarṣe ......kṣetre ......nagare ......grāme ......nāmasaṁvatsare ......māse ......pakṣe ......tithau ......vāsare ......gotraḥ ......śarmāham prātaḥ sarvakarmasu śuddhyarthaṁ śrutismṛtipurāṇoktaphalaprāptyarthaṁ śrībhagavatprītyarthaṁ ca prātaḥsnānaṁ kariṣye ||
en: Salutations to Viṣṇu. Salutations to Viṣṇu. Salutations to Viṣṇu. Today, in the second half of the life of Brahmā, in the Śvetavārāha kalpa, in the age of Manu Vaivasvata, in the twenty-eighth Kali Yuga, in the first quarter of the Kali age, in the time of the Buddha avatāra, in the earth world, in the continent of Jambū, in the land of Bharata, in the country of India, at this holy place, in this town, in this village, in the year of this name, in this month, in this fortnight, on this lunar day, on this day of the week — I, of this gotra and of this name, for purity in all my acts, for the obtaining of the fruit that the Śruti, the Smṛti and the Purāṇas declare, and for the pleasure of the Lord, shall now take the morning bath.
tel: విష్ణువునకు నమస్కారము. విష్ణువునకు నమస్కారము. విష్ణువునకు నమస్కారము. ఈ దినమున, బ్రహ్మదేవుని ద్వితీయ పరార్ధమునందు, శ్రీ శ్వేతవరాహ కల్పమునందు, వైవస్వత మన్వంతరమునందు, ఇరువది ఎనిమిదవ కలియుగమునందు, కలి ప్రథమ చరణమునందు, బౌద్ధావతార కాలమునందు, భూలోకమునందు, జంబూ ద్వీపమునందు, భరత ఖండమునందు, భారత వర్షమునందు, ఈ క్షేత్రమునందు, ఈ నగరమునందు, ఈ గ్రామమునందు, ఈ పేరు గల సంవత్సరమునందు, ఈ మాసమునందు, ఈ పక్షమునందు, ఈ తిథియందు, ఈ వాసరమునందు — ఈ గోత్రము ఈ నామము కలవాడనై, సమస్త కర్మలయందు శుద్ధి కొరకు, శ్రుతి స్మృతి పురాణములు చెప్పిన ఫలము పొందుట కొరకు, భగవంతుని ప్రీతి కొరకు, ప్రాతః స్నానమును చేయుచున్నాను.

--- unit 2 | section: The verse of the clay ---
vidhi:
After the saṅkalpa, say this and smear clay on all the limbs.
deva:
अश्वक्रान्ते रथक्रान्ते विष्णुक्रान्ते वसुन्धरे ।
मृत्तिके हर मे पापं यन्मया दुष्कृतं कृतम् ॥
iast:
aśvakrānte rathakrānte viṣṇukrānte vasundhare |
mṛttike hara me pāpaṁ yanmayā duṣkṛtaṁ kṛtam ||
en: O earth, over whom horses have passed, over whom chariots have passed, whom Viṣṇu strode across. O clay, take away my wrongdoing, whatever wrong I have done.
tel: అశ్వములు నడచిన, రథములు నడచిన, విష్ణువు కొలిచిన ఓ వసుంధరా. ఓ మృత్తికా, నేను చేసిన పాపమును, ఏ దుష్కృతము చేసితినో దానిని, హరించుము.
hi: हे वसुन्धरा, जिस पर घोड़े चले, जिस पर रथ चले, जिसे विष्णु ने नापा। हे मृत्तिके, मेरा पाप हर लो, जो कुछ बुरा मैंने किया है।

--- unit 3 | section: The twelve names of the Gaṅgā ---
vidhi:
Say this before entering the water. In these verses the Gaṅgā speaks, and she says that she comes to be present in whatever water a man remembers her by these twelve names. After saying it, go into the water up to the navel, push the surface water aside, close the ears and the nose, face the current or face the sun, and take three, five, seven or twelve immersions.
deva:
नन्दिनी नलिनी सीता मालती च महापगा ।
विष्णुपादाब्जसम्भूता गङ्गा त्रिपथगामिनी ॥
भागीरथी भोगवती जाह्नवी त्रिदशेश्वरी ।
द्वादशैतानि नामानि यत्र यत्र जलाशये ॥
स्नानोद्यतः स्मरेन्नित्यं तत्र तत्र वसाम्यहम् ॥
iast:
nandinī nalinī sītā mālatī ca mahāpagā |
viṣṇupādābjasambhūtā gaṅgā tripathagāminī ||
bhāgīrathī bhogavatī jāhnavī tridaśeśvarī |
dvādaśaitāni nāmāni yatra yatra jalāśaye ||
snānodyataḥ smarennityaṁ tatra tatra vasāmyaham ||
en: Nandinī, Nalinī, Sītā, Mālatī, Mahāpagā, she who arose from the lotus feet of Viṣṇu, Gaṅgā who goes by three paths, Bhāgīrathī, Bhogavatī, Jāhnavī, Tridaśeśvarī — in whatever water a man about to bathe remembers these twelve names each day, there I am present.
tel: నందిని, నలిని, సీత, మాలతి, మహాపగ, విష్ణువు పాద పద్మముల నుండి పుట్టినది, మూడు మార్గములలో ప్రవహించు గంగ, భాగీరథి, భోగవతి, జాహ్నవి, త్రిదశేశ్వరి — ఈ పండ్రెండు నామములను స్నానమునకు సిద్ధపడినవాడు ఏ జలాశయమునందు ప్రతిదినము స్మరించునో, అచ్చట నేను ఉందును.
hi: नन्दिनी, नलिनी, सीता, मालती, महापगा, विष्णु के चरणकमलों से उत्पन्न, तीन मार्गों से जाने वाली गंगा, भागीरथी, भोगवती, जाह्नवी, त्रिदशेश्वरी — स्नान के लिए तैयार मनुष्य जिस जलाशय में इन बारह नामों का प्रतिदिन स्मरण करता है, वहीं मैं रहती हूँ।
