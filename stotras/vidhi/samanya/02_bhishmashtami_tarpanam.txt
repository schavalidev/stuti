Recension note: **one difference, and it is carried into the text in brackets as the corpus's variant rule directs.**

**`आजन्मब्रह्मचारिणे` and `आबाल्यब्रह्मचारिणे`, at unit 3.** The base text reads `अर्घ्यं ददामि भीष्माय आजन्मब्रह्मचारिणे` — "celibate from his birth". Gītā Press reads `अर्घ्य ददामि भीष्माय आबाल्यब्रह्मचारिणे` — "celibate from his boyhood". Both are well attested and both are recited, and the sense is not materially different. The base text's reading stands in the line and **the Gītā Press reading is given after it in round brackets**, in `deva:` and in `iast:` alike. The same word stands in unit 1 as `आजन्म ब्रह्मचारिणे`, where Gītā Press offers nothing to compare, so it is left alone there.

Sections:
भीष्मतर्पणम् — Offering of water to Bhīṣma (units 1–4)

Blurb: This is the yearly offering of water to Bhīṣma. It is made on the eighth day of the bright fortnight of Māgha, the day on which he is remembered. Bhīṣma left no sons, and so no descendant of his own offers him water. For that reason the tradition asks every twice-born man to make the offering in a son's place, whatever his own family may be. The verses name his family and his pravara, call him the son of Gaṅgā and of Śantanu, and speak of him as one who kept his vow of celibacy from the beginning of his life. The worshipper faces the south, takes water with sesame and darbha grass, and pours it out three times.

--- unit 1 | section: Offering of water to Bhīṣma ---
vidhi:
Face the south, take water with sesame and darbha grass, and offer it with each verse. The offering is made on the eighth day of the bright fortnight of Māgha.
deva:
वैयाघ्रपाद गोत्राय साङ्कृत्य प्रवराय च ।
गङ्गापुत्राय भीष्माय आजन्म ब्रह्मचारिणे ॥ १
iast:
vaiyāghrapāda gotrāya sāṅkṛtya pravarāya ca |
gaṅgāputrāya bhīṣmāya ājanma brahmacāriṇe || 1
en: To him of the Vaiyāghrapāda gotra and of the Sāṅkṛtya pravara, to Bhīṣma the son of Gaṅgā, who was celibate from his birth.
tel: వైయాఘ్రపాద గోత్రుడైన, సాంకృత్య ప్రవరుడైన, గంగాపుత్రుడైన, జన్మము మొదలు బ్రహ్మచారియైన భీష్మునికి.
hi: वैयाघ्रपाद गोत्र वाले, सांकृत्य प्रवर वाले, गंगा के पुत्र, जन्म से ब्रह्मचारी भीष्म को।

--- unit 2 | section: Offering of water to Bhīṣma ---
vidhi:
The second verse names him and asks that he receive what a son would give.
deva:
भीष्मः शान्तनवो वीरः सत्यवादी जितेन्द्रियः ।
आभिरद्भिरवाप्नोतु पुत्र पौत्रोचितां क्रियाम् ॥ २
iast:
bhīṣmaḥ śāntanavo vīraḥ satyavādī jitendriyaḥ |
ābhiradbhiravāpnotu putra pautrocitāṁ kriyām || 2
en: Bhīṣma the son of Śantanu, the hero, the speaker of truth, who had mastered his senses — may he obtain by this water the rite that is due to one who has sons and grandsons.
tel: శంతనుని కుమారుడు, వీరుడు, సత్యవాది, ఇంద్రియములను జయించినవాడు అయిన భీష్ముడు ఈ జలముల చేత పుత్ర పౌత్రులకు తగిన క్రియను పొందును గాక.
hi: शंतनु के पुत्र, वीर, सत्यवादी, जितेंद्रिय भीष्म इस जल से पुत्र-पौत्रों के योग्य क्रिया प्राप्त करें।

--- unit 3 | section: Offering of water to Bhīṣma ---
vidhi:
The third verse is said while the water is poured out. Gītā Press prints this verse alone of the three, and its reading of the last word is given in brackets.
deva:
वसूनामवताराय शन्तनोरात्मजाय च ।
अर्घ्यं ददामि भीष्माय आजन्म(आबाल्य)ब्रह्मचारिणे ॥ ३
iast:
vasūnāmavatārāya śantanorātmajāya ca |
arghyaṁ dadāmi bhīṣmāya ājanma(ābālya)brahmacāriṇe || 3
en: To him who is the descent of the Vasus, to the son of Śantanu, to Bhīṣma who was celibate from his birth, I give this water.
tel: వసువుల అవతారమైన, శంతనుని కుమారుడైన, జన్మము మొదలు బ్రహ్మచారియైన భీష్మునికి ఈ అర్ఘ్యమును ఇచ్చుచున్నాను.
hi: वसुओं के अवतार, शंतनु के पुत्र, जन्म से ब्रह्मचारी भीष्म को यह अर्घ्य देता हूँ।

--- unit 4 | section: Offering of water to Bhīṣma ---
vidhi:
Pour the water out three times as this is said.

भीष्माय नमः इदमर्घ्यं इदमर्घ्यं इदमर्घ्यम् ॥

hi: भीष्म को नमस्कार। यह अर्घ्य है। यह अर्घ्य है। यह अर्घ्य है।
