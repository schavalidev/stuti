Title: Puṣkara-nadī-tīre Pitṛ-tarpaṇam — the offering of water to the forefathers on the bank of a river in her Puṣkara, in the southern form
Devanāgarī: पुष्करनदीतीरे पितृतर्पणम्
Telugu: పుష్కర నదీ తీరే పితృ తర్పణము

Author: Anonymous. This is the received tarpaṇa of the southern ritual manuals. No composer is named for it, and none is claimed.

Type: vidhi (ritual manual) — not a stotra. Uses the one-extra-field vidhi format documented in `../madhyandina/01_sandhyopasana_vidhi.txt`: a `vidhi:` field carrying the ritual instruction, then deva / iast / en / tel / hi.

Recension / paddhati: **the southern smārta form, as the Telugu manuals print it.** It is not the northern form. The saṅkalpa names the place by the Puṣkara of the river, and the sacred thread is worn prācīnāvītī, over the right shoulder, as the southern manuals direct for a rite of the forefathers. A worshipper who follows the northern practice will not recognise it, and should use the files in `../madhyandina/` instead. The rite itself is naimittika and not śākhā-specific, which is why the file sits in `vidhi/samanya/`.

Accent: **the two opening mantras are printed with svara marks in the source, and the accent is not carried here.** The booklet marks them in the Telugu manner, with the anudātta as an underline and the svarita as an upright stroke above the letter. Accented text is lifted from a machine-readable source in the mantra's own śākhā and is never typed off a page image, because a single missed mark cannot be seen on review. No machine-readable accented witness of these two mantras in this śākhā was found, so the letters are given here without the marks. The rest of the file is ritual prose and Purāṇic ślokas, to which no accent belongs.

Source / recension: base text is the Telugu booklet *Puṣkara Tarpaṇa Vidhānaṁ*, one of three booklets published together as *Tuṅgabhadrā Puṣkarālu*, archive.org item `20210721_20210721_0639`. The rite runs across four pages, numbered 39 to 42 in the volume. **Those four pages were read as page images and transcribed from them, not from the OCR**, which is broken on Telugu conjuncts. The Devanāgarī column is a machine transliteration of that Telugu by `bin/tel2dev.py`, and the IAST is generated from the Devanāgarī by `bin/dev2iast.py`; neither tool changes a word.

The booklet fills its occasion clause with the Tuṅgabhadrā, because it was printed for that river. That clause is left standing at unit 2, since it shows how the river and the Puṣkara are named. A worshipper at another river puts his own river in its place.

Independent collation: **one genuinely independent witness was found, and it agrees on the shape of the rite and on the list of relations.**

**The witness — *Śrī Kṛṣṇā-nadī Puṣkara Māhātmyam*, Mohan Publications, Rajahmundry, 2016**, archive.org item `FACEBOOKmohanpublications_20160510`. This is a different publisher, a different river and a different editorial lineage from the Tuṅgabhadrā booklet. Its ritual chapter runs from page 59 to page 64, and pages 61 to 64 carry a tarpaṇa vidhi with its own list of relations. **Those pages were read as page images**, because the volume's text layer is in legacy Anu fonts and comes out as mojibake.

**(1) The list of relations — collated, and it agrees.** The Kṛṣṇā volume prints the same relations in the same two groups, the forefathers first and the others after, and it gives the same relations by the same Sanskrit names — duhitā, ātmabhaginī, dauhitra, bhāgineyaka, pitṛbhaginī, mātṛbhaginī, jāmātā, bhāvuka, snuṣā, śvaśura, śvaśrū, śyālaka and the rest — each followed by a blank for the gotra. Two differences of arrangement, neither of them a disagreement. The Kṛṣṇā volume sets the list out with dashes and not with numerals, and it closes the list by saying that a master, a friend and any others the worshipper wishes are added in the same way, where the Tuṅgabhadrā booklet stops at thirty-five. It also names the teacher as brahmībhūta where this booklet has guru.

**(2) The saṅkalpa and the five ślokas — not independently collated.** The Kṛṣṇā volume's saṅkalpa on its page 60 is for the tīrtha-śrāddha and not for the tarpaṇa, so it does not witness unit 2. No second edition of the five ślokas of the dust of the brāhmaṇas' feet was found.

Recension note: at unit 3 the booklet reads `समुत्थिता पत्कुलधूम केतवः` and `समुद्रहेतवः` where the verse is often printed `समुद्धृतापत्कुलधूमकेतवः` and `समुद्रसेतवः`. The booklet's readings are kept, because a file prints what its own source prints. The other readings are recorded here and are **not** put in brackets, because no second edition of this passage was actually fetched and a bracketed reading needs a named witness.

At unit 1 the booklet reads `परिवाजमासते` where the Ṛgvedic verse is `परि वाचमासते`, and it divides `अतप्ततनूर्णत दामोअश्नुते` where the verse divides `अतप्ततनूर्न तदामो अश्नुते`. Both were checked against the page image at high magnification and are what the booklet prints. They are kept as printed.

Blurb: When Jupiter enters a sign, the Puṣkara of the river belonging to that sign begins. During those days the worshipper offers water to his forefathers on the bank of that river. This file gives what is said. He first holds the pavitrī and says two mantras of purification. He then does ācamana and prāṇāyāma, wears the sacred thread over the right shoulder, and makes the saṅkalpa, naming the Puṣkara and saying that he now offers the tarpaṇa of sesame and water for the sake of his forefathers. He praises the dust of the feet of the brāhmaṇas, asks their leave to begin, and receives it. He then offers water three times to each relation in turn, naming the gotra and the name of each — first to the father, the grandfather and the great-grandfather and the women of both lines, and afterwards, as the kāruṇya-tarpaṇa, to his wife, his children, his brothers and sisters and the rest of his household. At the end he presses the water from his sacred thread for those of his line who left no son, and does ācamana.

Sections:
पवित्रधारणमन्त्रौ — The two mantras said while holding the pavitrī (unit 1)
सङ्कल्पः — Saṅkalpa (unit 2)
विप्रपादपांसुस्तुतिः — Praise of the dust of the feet of the brāhmaṇas (unit 3)
अनुज्ञा — Asking and receiving leave (unit 4)
तर्पणक्रमः — The order of the tarpaṇa (unit 5)

Unit count: 6 units — two mantras, one saṅkalpa, five ślokas, the asking of leave, the tarpaṇa list of thirty-five relations, and the closing verse.

--- unit 1 | section: The two mantras said while holding the pavitrī ---
vidhi:
Wear the pavitrī rings of darbha grass on both hands and say these two mantras.
deva:
पवित्रं धृत्वा ॥
पवित्रवंतः परिवाजमासते । पितैषां प्रत्नो अभिरक्षति ।
व्रतं महस्समुद्रं वरुणस्तिरोदधे । धीराइच्छेकुर्धरुणेष्वारभं ।
पवित्रंते विततं ब्रह्मणस्पते । प्रभुर्गात्राणि पर्येषि विश्वतः ।
अतप्ततनूर्णत दामोअश्नुते । शृतास इद्वहं तस्तत्समासत ॥
iast:
pavitraṁ dhṛtvā ||
pavitravaṁtaḥ parivājamāsate | pitaiṣāṁ pratno abhirakṣati |
vrataṁ mahassamudraṁ varuṇastirodadhe | dhīrāicchekurdharuṇeṣvārabhaṁ |
pavitraṁte vitataṁ brahmaṇaspate | prabhurgātrāṇi paryeṣi viśvataḥ |
ataptatanūrṇata dāmoaśnute | śṛtāsa idvahaṁ tastatsamāsata ||
en: Holding the pavitrī. Those who hold the pavitrī sit around the speech; their ancient father guards them. The vow, the great sea — Varuṇa has hidden himself; the wise ones alone were able to take hold among the supports. Your filter is spread out, O lord of prayer; you go around the limbs on every side. He whose body is not heated does not reach it; those who are made ready, bearing it, have obtained it.
tel: పవిత్రమును ధరించి చెప్పునవి. పవిత్రమును ధరించినవారు వాక్కును చుట్టుకొని కూర్చుండుదురు. వారి పురాతనుడైన తండ్రి వారిని రక్షించును. వ్రతము, మహా సముద్రము — వరుణుడు దాగియున్నాడు. ధీరులు మాత్రమే ఆధారములయందు పట్టుకొనగలిగిరి. ఓ బ్రహ్మణస్పతీ, నీ పవిత్రము విస్తరించియున్నది. నీవు అన్ని వైపుల నుండి అవయవములను చుట్టివచ్చుచున్నావు. తపింపని శరీరము కలవాడు దానిని పొందడు. సిద్ధమైనవారే దానిని మోసి పొందిరి.
hi: पवित्री धारण करके ये दो मन्त्र कहें। जो पवित्री धारण करते हैं, वे वाणी को घेरकर बैठते हैं; उनका प्राचीन पिता उनकी रक्षा करता है। व्रत, महान् समुद्र — वरुण छिप गया है; धीर पुरुष ही आधारों में पकड़ पा सके। हे ब्रह्मणस्पते, तुम्हारा पवित्र फैला हुआ है; तुम सब ओर से अंगों को घेरते हो। जिसका शरीर तपा नहीं है, वह उसे नहीं पाता; जो सिद्ध हैं, वे ही उसे धारण कर पाते हैं।

--- unit 2 | section: Saṅkalpa ---
vidhi:
Do ācamana and prāṇāyāma. Wear the sacred thread over the right shoulder. Fill the blanks with the year, the month, the fortnight, the tithi and the rest, in the usual way. Say the name of the river at whose Puṣkara you are standing in place of the Tuṅgabhadrā. Then take the dakṣiṇā with darbha and akṣata in the hand. Go round three times.
deva:
आचम्य । प्राणानायम्य । एवंगुण ...... पुण्यतिथौ ॥ प्राचीनावीति ॥
मृगागते देवगुरौ सार्धत्रिकोटि तीर्थ पुष्कर प्रयुक्त तुंगभद्रा पुण्यनदी तीरे - अस्मत्पितॄणां अक्षय पुण्यलोक फलावाप्त्यर्थं, अस्मत्पितॄनुद्दिश्य, अस्मत्पितॄणां प्रीत्यर्थं तीर्थ श्राद्ध काले तिल तर्पणं (सव्यं) करिष्ये । काले अधिकार सिद्ध्यर्थं यथा संभवत्प्रायश्चित्तं करिष्ये । दर्भाक्षतैस्सह यथाशक्ति परिषद्दक्षिणां गृहीत्वा ॥
iast:
ācamya | prāṇānāyamya | evaṁguṇa ...... puṇyatithau || prācīnāvīti ||
mṛgāgate devagurau sārdhatrikoṭi tīrtha puṣkara prayukta tuṁgabhadrā puṇyanadī tīre - asmatpitṝṇāṁ akṣaya puṇyaloka phalāvāptyarthaṁ, asmatpitṝnuddiśya, asmatpitṝṇāṁ prītyarthaṁ tīrtha śrāddha kāle tila tarpaṇaṁ (savyaṁ) kariṣye | kāle adhikāra siddhyarthaṁ yathā saṁbhavatprāyaścittaṁ kariṣye | darbhākṣataissaha yathāśakti pariṣaddakṣiṇāṁ gṛhītvā ||
en: Having done ācamana and prāṇāyāma, on this auspicious tithi so qualified, wearing the sacred thread over the right shoulder — when Jupiter has entered Mṛgaśira, on the bank of the holy river Tuṅgabhadrā in this Puṣkara of three and a half crore tīrthas — for the gaining of an unending world of merit by my forefathers, with my forefathers in mind, and for the pleasure of my forefathers, I shall now offer the tarpaṇa of sesame at the time of the tīrtha-śrāddha, with the sacred thread in the ordinary position. For the fitness to do it at this time, I shall do whatever expiation is possible. Having taken the dakṣiṇā of the assembly according to my means, together with darbha and akṣata.
tel: ఆచమనము, ప్రాణాయామము చేసి, ఈ గుణ విశేషణములతో కూడిన శుభ తిథియందు, ప్రాచీనావీతిగా — దేవగురువు మృగశిరలో ప్రవేశించినపుడు సార్ధ త్రికోటి తీర్థములతో కూడిన ఈ పుష్కరమునందు తుంగభద్రా పుణ్య నదీ తీరమునందు — నా పితరులకు అక్షయమైన పుణ్యలోక ఫలము కలుగుట కొరకు, నా పితరులను ఉద్దేశించి, నా పితరుల ప్రీతి కొరకు, తీర్థ శ్రాద్ధ కాలమునందు తిల తర్పణమును సవ్యముగా చేయుచున్నాను. ఈ కాలమునందు అధికార సిద్ధి కొరకు యథా సంభవముగా ప్రాయశ్చిత్తము చేయుచున్నాను. దర్భలతోను అక్షతలతోను కూడి యథాశక్తి పరిషద్దక్షిణను గ్రహించి.
hi: आचमन और प्राणायाम करके, इन गुणों से युक्त इस शुभ तिथि को, प्राचीनावीती होकर — देवगुरु के मृगशिरा में प्रवेश करने पर साढ़े तीन करोड़ तीर्थों वाले इस पुष्कर में तुंगभद्रा पुण्य नदी के तट पर — अपने पितरों को अक्षय पुण्यलोक की प्राप्ति के लिए, अपने पितरों को उद्देश्य करके, और अपने पितरों की प्रसन्नता के लिए, तीर्थश्राद्ध के समय तिल तर्पण सव्य रूप से करता हूँ। इस समय अधिकार की सिद्धि के लिए यथासम्भव प्रायश्चित्त करता हूँ। दर्भ और अक्षत के साथ यथाशक्ति परिषद् दक्षिणा लेकर।

--- unit 3 | section: Praise of the dust of the feet of the brāhmaṇas ---
vidhi:
Say these five verses in praise of the brāhmaṇas present.
deva:
समस्त संपत्समवाप्य हेतवः समुत्थिता पत्कुलधूम केतवः ।
अपार संसार समुद्रहेतवः पुनंतुमां ब्राह्मण पादपांसवः ॥
आपद्धनध्वांत सहस्रभासवः समीहितार्थार्पण कामधेनवः ।
समस्त तीर्थांबु पवित्रमूर्तयः रक्षंतुमां ब्राह्मण पादपांसवः ॥
आधिव्याधि हरंन्नृणां मृत्युदारिद्र्य नाशनं ।
श्री पुष्टि कीर्तिदं वंदे विप्रश्री पाद पंकजं ॥
विप्राघ्रि दर्शनात्सद्यः क्षीयंते पापराशयः ।
वंदनान्मंगळा वाप्तिः अर्चनादच्युतं पदं ॥
नमो ब्रह्मण्य देवाय गोब्राह्मण हितायच ।
जगद्धिताय कृष्णाय गोविंदाय नमोनमः ॥
iast:
samasta saṁpatsamavāpya hetavaḥ samutthitā patkuladhūma ketavaḥ |
apāra saṁsāra samudrahetavaḥ punaṁtumāṁ brāhmaṇa pādapāṁsavaḥ ||
āpaddhanadhvāṁta sahasrabhāsavaḥ samīhitārthārpaṇa kāmadhenavaḥ |
samasta tīrthāṁbu pavitramūrtayaḥ rakṣaṁtumāṁ brāhmaṇa pādapāṁsavaḥ ||
ādhivyādhi haraṁnnṛṇāṁ mṛtyudāridrya nāśanaṁ |
śrī puṣṭi kīrtidaṁ vaṁde vipraśrī pāda paṁkajaṁ ||
viprāghri darśanātsadyaḥ kṣīyaṁte pāparāśayaḥ |
vaṁdanānmaṁgaḻā vāptiḥ arcanādacyutaṁ padaṁ ||
namo brahmaṇya devāya gobrāhmaṇa hitāyaca |
jagaddhitāya kṛṣṇāya goviṁdāya namonamaḥ ||
en: May the dust of the feet of the brāhmaṇas purify me — that dust which is the cause of obtaining every kind of wealth, which is the comet that destroys the family of misfortunes when they rise, and which is the cause of crossing the boundless ocean of worldly life. May the dust of the feet of the brāhmaṇas protect me — that dust which is a thousand rays of light in the darkness of loss of wealth, which is the wish-giving cow that grants what is desired, and which is the holy form of the water of all the tīrthas. I bow to the lotus feet of the brāhmaṇas, which take away the sickness of body and mind of men, which destroy untimely death and poverty, and which give prosperity, health and good name. येके चास्मत्कुले जाता अपुत्रिनो गोत्रिनोमृताः ।
ते गृह्णंतु मयादत्तं सूत्रनिष्पीडनोदकम् ॥
आचमनं ।
iast:
yeke cāsmatkule jātā aputrino gotrinomṛtāḥ |
te gṛhṇaṁtu mayādattaṁ sūtraniṣpīḍanodakam ||
ācamanaṁ |
en: Those born in our family who have departed leaving no son and no one of their gotra — let them receive the water I now give, pressed from my sacred thread. Then ācamana.
tel: మన కులమునందు పుట్టి, పుత్రులు లేకయు, గోత్రము వారు లేకయు గతించిన వారెవరైనను కలరో, వారు నేను ఇచ్చు ఈ సూత్ర నిష్పీడన ఉదకమును గ్రహించుదురు గాక. పిమ్మట ఆచమనము.
hi: हमारे कुल में जो बिना पुत्र और बिना गोत्र वाले होकर चले गए हैं, वे मेरे द्वारा दिया गया यह सूत्रनिष्पीडन का जल ग्रहण करें। फिर आचमन।
