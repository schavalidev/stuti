Title: Śrī Lakṣmīnṛsiṁha Pañcaratnam
Devanāgarī: श्री लक्ष्मीनृसिंह पञ्चरत्नम्
Telugu: శ్రీ లక్ష్మీనృసింహ పంచరత్నం

Author: Ādi Śaṅkarācārya (per the text's own colophon, which names him disciple of Govinda Bhagavatpāda)

Source / recension: stotranidhi.com — Devanāgarī: https://stotranidhi.com/hi/lakshmi-narasimha-pancharatnam-in-sanskrit/ ; Telugu: https://stotranidhi.com/lakshmi-nrusimha-pancharatnam-in-telugu/ ; English/IAST cross-check: https://stotranidhi.com/en/lakshmi-narasimha-pancharatnam-in-english/ . All three recensions agree verse-for-verse; no variant readings found.

Verse count: 5

Blurb: Unlike most Nṛsiṁha stotras, this "five gems" hymn is addressed not to the deity directly but to the poet's own restless mind, apostrophized in every verse as "cetobhṛṅga" — the bee that is my mind — wandering pointlessly over the flavorless desert of worldly existence. Each verse offers a classic Vedāntic simile for that futility (silver mistaken in a seashell, a śālmali flower mistaken for a lotus, sense-pleasures like a fruit that sickens after it is eaten, the equal unreality of dream and waking) before the refrain turns the mind back toward the one worthwhile object: the nectar at Lakṣmīnṛsiṁha's stainless lotus feet.

चेतोभृङ्ग भ्रमसि वृथा भवमरुभूमौ विरसायां

iast:

cetobhṛṅga bhramasi vṛthā bhavamarubhūmau virasāyāṁ
bhaja bhaja lakṣmīnarasiṁhānaghapadasarasijamakarandam ||
en: If you desire what is truly dear to your own soul and its Lord, worship Narahari constantly — for one skilled at keeping a reflection adorned thereby extends that adornment to the very object reflected. O bee that is my mind, you wander in vain over the flavorless desert of worldly existence — worship, worship the nectar of Lakṣmīnarasiṁha's stainless lotus feet!

चेतोभृङ्ग भ्रमसि वृथा भवमरुभूमौ विरसायां
भज भज लक्ष्मीनरसिंहानघपदसरसिजमकरन्दम् ॥

ākṛtisāmyācchālmalikusume sthalanalinatvabhramamakaroḥ
gandharasāviha kimu vidyete viphalaṁ bhrāmyasi bhṛśavirase'smin |
cetobhṛṅga bhramasi vṛthā bhavamarubhūmau virasāyāṁ
bhaja bhaja lakṣmīnarasiṁhānaghapadasarasijamakarandam ||
en: Because its shape resembles one, you mistook the śālmali tree's flower for a land-lotus — but do fragrance and nectar actually exist in it? In vain you wander about in this utterly flavorless world. O bee that is my mind, you wander in vain over the flavorless desert of worldly existence — worship, worship the nectar of Lakṣmīnarasiṁha's stainless lotus feet!

गन्धफलीसदृशा ननु तेऽमी भोगानन्तरदुःखकृतः स्युः ।

cetobhṛṅga bhramasi vṛthā bhavamarubhūmau virasāyāṁ

en: Thinking sense-objects — garlands, sandal-paste, women, and the rest — to be givers of happiness, you sport among them; but surely, like the sweet-smelling fruit that sickens after it is eaten, these become causes of sorrow once the enjoyment is over. O bee that is my mind, you wander in vain over the flavorless desert of worldly existence — worship, worship the nectar of Lakṣmīnarasiṁha's stainless lotus feet!
tel: పూలదండలు, చందనము, స్త్రీలు మొదలైన విషయసుఖములను నిజమైన సుఖదాయకములని తలచి వాటియందు విహరిస్తున్నావు — కానీ తినిన తరువాత రోగము కలిగించు మధురఫలమువలె, ఇవి భోగానంతరము దుఃఖమునే కలిగించును. ఓ మనసు అనే తుమ్మెదా, రసహీనమైన ఈ సంసార ఎడారిలో వృథాగా తిరుగుతున్నావు — లక్ష్మీనరసింహుని నిర్మలమైన పాదపద్మ మకరందమును సేవించుము, సేవించుము.

--- verse 5 ---
deva:
तव हितमेकं वचनं वक्ष्ये शृणु सुखकामो यदि सततं
स्वप्ने दृष्टं सकलं हि मृषा जाग्रति च स्मर तद्वदिति ।
चेतोभृङ्ग भ्रमसि वृथा भवमरुभूमौ विरसायां
भज भज लक्ष्मीनरसिंहानघपदसरसिजमकरन्दम् ॥
इति श्रीमत्परमहंसपरिव्राजकाचार्यस्य श्रीगोविन्दभगवत्पूज्यपादशिष्यस्य श्रीमच्छङ्करभगवतः कृतौ श्रीलक्ष्मीनरसिंह पञ्चरत्नम् ।
iast:
tava hitamekaṁ vacanaṁ vakṣye śṛṇu sukhakāmo yadi satataṁ
svapne dṛṣṭaṁ sakalaṁ hi mṛṣā jāgrati ca smara tadvaditi |
cetobhṛṅga bhramasi vṛthā bhavamarubhūmau virasāyāṁ
bhaja bhaja lakṣmīnarasiṁhānaghapadasarasijamakarandam ||
iti śrīmatparamahaṁsaparivrājakācāryasya śrīgovindabhagavatpūjyapādaśiṣyasya śrīmacchaṅkarabhagavataḥ kṛtau śrīlakṣmīnarasiṁha pañcaratnam |
en: I shall speak one word that is good for you — listen, if you ever truly desire happiness: everything seen in a dream is false; know that it is exactly the same for what you see while awake. O bee that is my mind, you wander in vain over the flavorless desert of worldly existence — worship, worship the nectar of Lakṣmīnarasiṁha's stainless lotus feet! Thus ends the Śrī Lakṣmīnarasiṁha Pañcaratnam, a work of the venerable wandering monk-teacher Śrī Śaṅkara Bhagavat, disciple of the venerable Govinda Bhagavatpāda.
