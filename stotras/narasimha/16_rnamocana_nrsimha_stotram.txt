Title: Śrī Ṛṇamocana Nṛsiṁha Stotram
Devanāgarī: श्री ऋणमोचन नृसिंह स्तोत्रम्
Telugu: శ్రీ ఋణమోచన నృసింహ స్తోత్రం

Author: Unattributed; the source's colophon places it in the Nṛsiṁha Purāṇa — iti śrīnṛsiṁhapurāṇe ṛṇamocana śrī nṛsiṁha stotram.

Source / recension: stotranidhi.com, Devanāgarī page (hi/runa-vimochana-narasimha-stotram-in-sanskrit) and IAST page (en/runa-vimochana-narasimha-stotram-in-english), fetched directly and compared verse by verse; the two agree throughout, including in the two alternative readings the site prints in square brackets (digdanti in v. 6 and sañjñitam in v. 10). Those bracketed variants are kept out of the verse text here and recorded in the notes instead. The site's r̥ has been normalised to precomposed ṛ and its ē/ō to plain e/o per this corpus's conventions.

Verse count: 11

Sections:
ధ్యానం — Dhyāna, the meditation verse (v. 1)
స్తోత్రం — Stotram, the eight petitions (vv. 2–9)
ఫలశ్రుతి — Phalaśruti, the fruit of recitation (vv. 10–11)

Blurb: A hymn with an unusually plain and worldly request built into its refrain: every one of the eight stanzas ends namāmi ṛṇamuktaye, "I bow, for release from debt." Where most stotras name mokṣa as the goal and let material relief follow as a by-product, this one reverses the order — it asks for solvency and says so eight times, and its closing verse promises without euphemism that the reciter will quickly become debt-free and obtain abundant wealth. Verse 8 widens ṛṇa beyond money to affliction by cruel planets, which is how the text is most often used in practice.

यस्यास्ते हृदये संवित्तं नृसिंहमहं भजे ॥
iast:
vāgīśā yasya vadane lakṣmīryasya ca vakṣasi |
yasyāste hṛdaye saṁvittaṁ nṛsiṁhamahaṁ bhaje ||
en: In whose mouth is Vāgīśā, mistress of speech, and on whose chest is Lakṣmī; in whose heart abides pure awareness — that Nṛsiṁha I worship.
tel: ఎవని ముఖంలో వాగీశ్వరి ఉన్నదో, ఎవని వక్షస్థలంలో లక్ష్మి ఉన్నదో, ఎవని హృదయంలో సంవిత్తు నిలిచి ఉన్నదో — ఆ నృసింహుని నేను సేవిస్తున్నాను.
hi: जिनके मुख में वागीशा हैं, जिनके वक्ष पर लक्ष्मी हैं, जिनके हृदय में संवित् स्थित है — उन नृसिंह की मैं उपासना करता हूँ।

--- verse 6 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
सिंहनादेन महता दिग्विदिग्भयनाशनम् ।
श्रीनृसिंहं महावीरं नमामि ऋणमुक्तये ॥
iast:
siṁhanādena mahatā digvidigbhayanāśanam |
śrīnṛsiṁhaṁ mahāvīraṁ namāmi ṛṇamuktaye ||
en: To him whose mighty lion-roar destroys fear in every direction and every quarter between — to Śrīnṛsiṁha, the great hero, I bow, for release from debt. The source records an alternative reading digdanti here, which would make it the elephants of the quarters, rather than the quarters themselves, that are shaken.
tel: గొప్ప సింహనాదంతో దిక్కులలోను, విదిక్కులలోను భయాన్ని నశింపజేసేవానికి — శ్రీనృసింహునికి, మహావీరునికి, ఋణవిముక్తి కోసం నేను నమస్కరిస్తున్నాను. ఇక్కడ మూలంలో "దిగ్దంతి" అనే పాఠాంతరం ఉన్నది; అప్పుడు దిగ్గజాలు కంపించినట్లు అర్థం.
hi: महान सिंहनाद से दिशाओं और विदिशाओं का भय नष्ट करने वाले — उन श्रीनृसिंह महावीर को मैं ऋणमुक्ति के लिए नमन करता हूँ। स्रोत यहाँ "दिग्दंति" पाठांतर देता है, जिससे दिग्गजों का काँपना अर्थ बनता है।

--- verse 10 | section: Phalaśruti ---
deva:
इत्थं यः पठते नित्यं ऋणमोचन सिद्धये ।
अनृणो जायते शीघ्रं धनं विपुलमाप्नुयात् ॥
iast:
itthaṁ yaḥ paṭhate nityaṁ ṛṇamocana siddhaye |
anṛṇo jāyate śīghraṁ dhanaṁ vipulamāpnuyāt ||
en: Whoever recites in this way daily, for the accomplishment of release from debt, quickly becomes free of debt and obtains abundant wealth. The source records an alternative reading sañjñitam, by which the line would instead name the hymn — "this hymn called Ṛṇamocana."
tel: ఋణమోచన సిద్ధి కోసం ఈ విధంగా నిత్యం పఠించేవాడు వేగంగా ఋణరహితుడై విపులమైన ధనాన్ని పొందుతాడు. మూలంలో "సంజ్ఞితం" అనే పాఠాంతరం ఉన్నది; అప్పుడు ఈ పంక్తి స్తోత్రం పేరునే — "ఋణమోచనం అనే పేరుగల" — సూచిస్తుంది.
hi: ऋणमोचन की सिद्धि के लिए इस प्रकार जो नित्य पाठ करता है, वह शीघ्र ऋणमुक्त होकर विपुल धन प्राप्त करता है। स्रोत "संज्ञितम्" पाठांतर देता है, जिससे यह पंक्ति स्तोत्र का नाम ही बताती — "ऋणमोचन नामक"।

--- verse 11 | section: Phalaśruti ---

en: It bestows every attainment upon people and grants every kind of prosperity; therefore one should recite this hymn always, sparing no effort.

hi: यह मनुष्यों को समस्त सिद्धियाँ देने वाला और समस्त ऐश्वर्य प्रदान करने वाला है; अतः पूर्ण प्रयत्न से इस स्तोत्र का सदा पाठ करना चाहिए।

Recension note: The Devanāgarī and IAST pages on stotranidhi.com carry identical text and identical bracketed variants; all eleven blocks were compared line by line. The hymn is in anuṣṭubh throughout. Two alternative readings are printed by the source in square brackets after the affected line — digdanti for digvidik in the fifth stotram verse (v. 6 here) and sañjñitam for siddhaye in the first phalaśruti verse (v. 10 here). Both have been left out of the verse text and recorded in the corresponding en/tel/hi notes, so the deva and iast fields carry only the primary reading. Verse numbering in this file is sequential; the source numbers only the ten verses after the dhyāna, which it prints unnumbered under the heading dhyānam, followed by atha stotram.
