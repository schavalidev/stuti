Title: Pitṛ Sūktam (Ṛgveda 10.15)
Devanāgarī: पितृसूक्तम् (ऋग्वेदीय)
Telugu: పితృ సూక్తం (ఋగ్వేదీయం)

Author: Vedic — Ṛgveda 10.15, fourteen ṛks; ṛṣi Śaṅkha Yāmāyana, devatā the Pitṛs, metre triṣṭubh with ṛk 11 in jagatī (per the anukramaṇī as quoted in Sāyaṇa's bhāṣya on the sa.wikisource page).

Recension / śākhā: **Ṛgveda, Śākala saṁhitā.** This is NOT the Pitṛ Sūkta that Gītā Press prints for the śrāddha, which is the Śukla-Yajurveda's (VS 19.49–61), kept separately as `pitr/03_pitr_suktam_vajasaneyi.txt`. Four ṛks are shared outright (this file's 1, 3, 4 and 8 = VS 19.49, 56, 55 and 51), and a fifth in altered form (ṛk 14 against VS 19.60, where the Ṛgveda reads `अग्निदग्धाः`/`अनग्निदग्धाः`/`कल्पयस्व` and the Yajurveda `अग्निष्वात्ताः`/`अनग्निष्वात्ताः`/`कल्पयाति`). The two hymns are separate works of separate Vedas and are not collated against, merged with, or used to fill gaps in each other.

Source / recension: **No Gītā Press witness exists** for this form of the sūkta — the Gītā Press śrāddha volume prints the Yajurvedic one. Two digital witnesses, both accented and both Śākala: (1) **sa.wikisource.org, `ऋग्वेदः सूक्तं १०.१५`** — the saṁhitā text printed with Sāyaṇa's bhāṣya, taken as the text here; (2) **stotranidhi.com, "Pitṛ Sūktam"** (`/pitru-suktam-in-sanskrit/`), which is the accented Ṛgvedic hymn. Collated ṛk by ṛk by machine, twice: once with accents stripped (text) and once with accents kept (svara). **The two agree in every word and on every accent position.** The accent is therefore the Śākala accent, taken from a Śākala source, as the svara rule requires.

Recension note: **Every difference between the two witnesses is orthographic, and each is listed here.** **(1) stotranidhi keys the visarga as an ASCII colon** (`पि॒तर॑:` with U+003A, not ः U+0903) throughout — an encoding defect of the kind already recorded for that site in `vidhi/taittiriya/`, and a reason the Wikisource text was preferred as the base. **(2) Class nasal vs anusvāra:** Wikisource writes the anusvāra where stotranidhi writes the class nasal, consistently (`अ॒व॒ंतु`, `भजं॑त`, `ग॑मंतु॒`, `शंत॑मे`, `जु॒षध्वं॑`, `इंद्रे॑ण`, `मा॒दयं॑ते` against `अवन्तु`, `भजन्त`, `गमन्तु`, `शन्तमे`, `जुषध्वम्`, `इन्द्रेण`, `मादयन्ते`). Wikisource's spelling is kept as printed. **(3) The Ṛgvedic `ळ`**: Wikisource `ई॒ळि॒तो` (ṛk 12) and `स्व॒राळसु॑नीतिम्` (ṛk 14) against stotranidhi's `ल`; `ळ` is the Śākala orthography and is kept. **(4)** Wikisource `पि॒तॄन्त्सु॑वि॒दत्राँ॑` (ṛk 3) with the epenthetic `त्` against stotranidhi `पि॒तॄन्सु॑वि॒दत्रा॑ँ`; Wikisource kept. **(5) The kampa numeral** `ऊ॒त्य१॒॑र्वाग्` (ṛk 4), which marks an independent svarita in Ṛgvedic printing, is kept in `deva:` as Wikisource prints it (stotranidhi gives it in parentheses, `ऊ॒त्य(१॒॑ )र्वा`) and omitted from `iast:`. Accent marks are carried in `deva:` and omitted from `iast:`, following `vidhi/taittiriya/01_krishna_yajurveda_sandhyavandanam.txt`. **Usage.** Recited in Ṛgvedic śrāddha and aṣṭakā rites (Sāyaṇa cites Āśvalāyana Gṛhya 2.4.6 for its first eight ṛks at the aṣṭakā homa). The Gītā Press prohibition on `उदीरतामवर` in the preta-śrāddha, recorded in `pitr/03`, names the shared first mantra by its incipit and is noted here for the same reason.

Verse count: 14

Sections:
पितृसूक्तम् — Pitṛ Sūktam (ṛks 1–14 = ṚV 10.15.1–14)

Blurb: The Ṛgveda's hymn to the forefathers is gentler and stranger than that of the Yajurveda. It asks forgiveness in advance for whatever wrong the living may do them, 'being human'. It seats the forefathers in the lap of the red dawns. At the end it admits that it does not know them all: those who are here and those who are not, those we know and those we do not, those who were burnt and those who were not. It leaves the count to Agni, who does know.

en: ṚV 10.15.1. Let the forefathers rise up — the lower, the higher and the middle ones, the Soma-worthy; they who have gone to the life beyond, unharmed and knowing the order of truth — may those forefathers protect us at our invocations.
