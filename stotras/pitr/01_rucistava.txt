Title: Rucistavaḥ (Pitṛ Stotram of Ruci)
Devanāgarī: रुचिस्तवः (रुचिकृतं पितृस्तोत्रम्)
Telugu: రుచిస్తవః (రుచికృత పితృ స్తోత్రం)

Author: Prajāpati Ruci, in the narrative of Garuḍa Purāṇa, Ācāra-kāṇḍa, adhyāya 89 (GRETIL numbering 1.89.51–60), told by Mārkaṇḍeya to Krauñcuki. Ruci, commanded by his forefathers to marry and by Brahmā to beget progeny, does pitṛ-tarpaṇa on a river bank; a column of light fills the sky, and he kneels and sings this. The same narrative is traditionally placed also in the Mārkaṇḍeya Purāṇa (Raucya manvantara); that was **not verified here**, because GRETIL's Mārkaṇḍeya text stops at adhyāya 93.

Source / recension: Primary text: **Gītā Press, Gorakhpur — Antya-karma Śrāddha-prakāśa**, p. 418, under the heading `प्रजापति रुचि और उनके द्वारा की गयी पितरोंकी उपासना`, transcribed from the page image of the printed edition (archive.org `antya-karma-shraddha-prakash-gita-press-gorakhpur_202011`, scan leaf 431), not from its OCR; doubtful glyphs were settled on full-resolution crops. Collated verse by verse against three further witnesses: (1) **GRETIL, Garuḍa Purāṇa 1.89.51–60** — a scholarly digital edition, a genuinely independent lineage; (2) **Ṛṣipīṭham** (Telugu monthly), September 2014, pp. 12–13, in Telugu script, supplied by the user as a PDF and read from its page images — an independent printed witness, and one that agrees with GRETIL at every point where the witnesses split, so it is best treated as the same textual family as GRETIL rather than as a third family; (3) **stotranidhi.com**, where this text is "Pitṛ Stotram 2 (Ruci kṛtam)" — a convenience source.

Recension note: **Gītā Press is followed throughout; every place where it stands against the other witnesses is recorded here and none is silently resolved.** **(1) Verse 5, `देवर्षीणां जनितॄंश्च…`, is present in Gītā Press and stotranidhi and ABSENT from both GRETIL and Ṛṣipīṭham** — so the text runs to 11 verses in one family and 10 in the other. It is printed here because the authority prints it. **(2) v. 3 — two readings where Gītā Press stands alone:** `मन्वादीनां मुनीन्द्राणां` against `मन्वादीनां च नेतारः` (GRETIL, Ṛṣipīṭham, stotranidhi), and `पितॄनप्सूदधावपि` ("in the waters and the ocean too") against `पितॄनप्युदधावपि` (all three others). Gītā Press's own Hindi paraphrase on the same page renders both — "मुनीश्वरों" and "जल और समुद्रमें" — so these are deliberate readings of the edition and not misprints. **(3) v. 7** `स्वयम्भुवे` (Gītā Press, stotranidhi) against `स्वायम्भुवे` (GRETIL, Ṛṣipīṭham). **(4) v. 9** `अग्नीषोममयं` (Gītā Press, Ṛṣipīṭham) against `अग्निसोममयं` (GRETIL, stotranidhi) — the Vedic dual compound against its classical spelling. **(5) v. 10** `ये तु तेजसि` (Gītā Press) against `ये च तेजसि` (all three others). **(6) v. 11** `नमस्ते मे प्रसीदन्तु` (Gītā Press, parsed `नमः। ते मे प्रसीदन्तु` — "may they be gracious to me", which is how the book's Hindi takes it) against `नमस्तेऽस्तु प्रसीदन्तु` (all three others). **(7)** GRETIL's `saptarṣoṇām` in v. 2 is a keying slip for `सप्तर्षीणां`, which the other three all print. **Orthography as printed:** Gītā Press doubles the consonant after repha in `मूर्त्तानां` and `मूर्त्तिधरां`, and that is kept; `अक्षय्यस्य` is the book's spelling. **Relation to the other Ruci text.** This stotra is the second half of one continuous passage: Garuḍa 89.13–48 is Ruci's longer hymn beginning `नमस्येऽहं पितॄन्` (`pitr/02_ruci_pitr_stotram.txt`), then two verses of Mārkaṇḍeya's narration (89.49–50), then this. Ṛṣipīṭham prints the two together in that order; stotranidhi and this corpus catalogue them separately, as Gītā Press does by printing only this one — **the tradition treats them as two stotras and both files are kept, cross-referenced.** **Usage recorded by Gītā Press.** The same volume directs that this Rucistava be recited, with the Puruṣa Sūkta and Pitṛ Sūkta, before the brāhmaṇas at the meal of a śrāddha — and forbids exactly these recitations (`उदीरतामवर`, the Puruṣa Sūkta, `रुचिस्तव`) in the **preta-śrāddha**, the rites before sapiṇḍīkaraṇa, when the dead person has not yet joined the forefathers. An app presenting this text should carry that restriction.

Verse count: 11

Sections:
स्तोत्रम् — Stotram (vv. 1–11)

Blurb: This is a hymn sung upon the knees before a wall of light. Ruci was an ascetic who had refused to marry. His own forefathers told him that his refusal was dragging them down. He worshipped them on a river bank, the sky filled with radiance, and he sang. He sang not to his own grandfathers but to the forefathers as a cosmic order: the leaders of Indra, and the seven ṛṣis, and the sun and moon, in whom the whole universe is Agni and Soma. This is the hymn that Gītā Press prescribes at the śrāddha meal. The same volume forbids it before the departed have joined the forefathers.

--- verse 1 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
रुचिरुवाच ।
अर्चितानाममूर्त्तानां पितॄणां दीप्ततेजसाम् ।
नमस्यामि सदा तेषां ध्यानिनां दिव्यचक्षुषाम् ॥
iast:
ruciruvāca |
arcitānāmamūrttānāṁ pitṝṇāṁ dīptatejasām |
namasyāmi sadā teṣāṁ dhyānināṁ divyacakṣuṣām ||
en: Ruci said: I bow always to the forefathers — worshipped, formless, of blazing radiance, absorbed in meditation, possessed of divine sight.
tel: రుచి పలికెను: పూజింపబడినవారు, రూపరహితులు, దీప్తమైన తేజస్సు గలవారు, ధ్యానమగ్నులు, దివ్యదృష్టి గలవారు అయిన ఆ పితరులకు నేను సదా నమస్కరిస్తున్నాను.
hi: रुचि बोले — जो पूजित, अमूर्त, अत्यन्त तेजस्वी, ध्यानी और दिव्यदृष्टि-सम्पन्न हैं, उन पितरों को मैं सदा नमस्कार करता हूँ।

saptarṣīṇāṁ tathānyeṣāṁ tān namasyāmi kāmadān ||
en: They who lead Indra and the other gods, Dakṣa and Mārīca, the seven ṛṣis and others besides — to them, the granters of desires, I bow.
tel: ఇంద్రాది దేవతలకు, దక్షునికి, మారీచునికి, సప్తర్షులకు, ఇతరులకు కూడా నాయకులై కోరికలను తీర్చే ఆ పితరులకు నమస్కరిస్తున్నాను.
hi: जो इन्द्र आदि देवताओं, दक्ष, मारीच, सप्तर्षियों तथा अन्य लोगों के भी नेता हैं, कामनाएँ पूर्ण करने वाले उन पितरों को मैं नमस्कार करता हूँ।

--- verse 3 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
मन्वादीनां मुनीन्द्राणां सूर्याचन्द्रमसोस्तथा ।
तान् नमस्याम्यहं सर्वान् पितॄनप्सूदधावपि ॥

tel: మను మొదలైనవారికి, మునీంద్రులకు, సూర్యచంద్రులకు నాయకులైన ఆ పితరులందరికీ — జలాలలోను సముద్రంలోను ఉన్నవారికి కూడా — నేను నమస్కరిస్తున్నాను.
hi: जो मनु आदि के, मुनीश्वरों के तथा सूर्य और चन्द्रमा के भी नायक हैं, उन सभी पितरों को मैं जल में और समुद्र में भी नमस्कार करता हूँ।
