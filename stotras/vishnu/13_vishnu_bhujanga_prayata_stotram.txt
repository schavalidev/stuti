Blurb: Composed entirely in the bhujaṅgaprayāta ("serpent-gliding") meter, this hymn opens in the register of Vedāntic abstraction -- Viṣṇu as pure consciousness, beyond the guṇas, unmanifest, the fourth state -- then settles into an extended dhyāna (vv. 3-7) visualizing his form seated on a yogic lotus-seat, radiant, four-armed, richly ornamented, before returning to praise (v. 8-9) as householder of the three worlds with Śrī and Bhū. The final third turns sharply personal and unflinching: old age described as a devouring demoness, the terror of the death-rattle's final breath, and a plea to be granted, at that moment, the presence of mind to simply repeat Viṣṇu's names -- closing with the promise that reciting this hymn itself grants that same grace.

en: Salutations to you, whom the threefold Veda proclaims and knows -- utterly pure, auspicious, tranquil, without beginning or end, the very life of the universe, of the nature of light and bliss, not to be delimited by direction, place, or time.

hi: विशुद्ध, शिव, शांत, आदि-अंत रहित, जगज्जीवन, ज्योति-आनंदस्वरूप; दिशा, देश, काल से अविभाज्य — जिसे त्रयी वेद कहता और जानता है, उस तुम्हें नमस्कार।

गुणाहस्करे वह्निबिम्बार्धमध्ये
समासीनमोङ्कर्णिकेऽष्टाक्षराब्जे ॥

en: [I meditate on you] seated on the great, resplendent seat of yoga -- formed of the essential principles beginning with earth, endowed with śakti, the dispenser of the guṇas -- in the midst of half the orb of fire, upon the eight-syllabled lotus whose pericarp is the syllable Oṁ.
tel: ప్రకాశించే మహాయోగపీఠంపై, భూమి మొదలైన తత్త్వాత్మకమైన, శక్తియుక్తమైన, గుణాలను ప్రసాదించే, అగ్నిబింబం మధ్యభాగంలో, ఓంకారమే బొడ్డుగా కల అష్టాక్షరపద్మంపై ఆసీనుడైన నిన్ను ధ్యానిస్తున్నాను.
hi: देदीप्यमान महायोगपीठ पर, पृथ्वी आदि तत्त्वात्मक, शक्तियुक्त, गुणों को देने वाले, अग्नि के अर्ध-बिम्ब के मध्य में, ओंकार-कर्णिका वाले अष्टाक्षर कमल पर विराजमान तुम्हारा मैं ध्यान करता हूँ।

--- verse 14 ---

स मोहं विहायाशु युष्मत् प्रसादात्
समाश्रित्य योगं व्रजत्यच्युतं त्वाम् ॥
इति श्रीमत्परमहंसपरिव्राजकाचार्यस्य श्रीगोविन्दभगवत्पूज्यपादशिष्यस्य श्रीमच्छङ्करभगवतः कृतौ श्री विष्णु भुजङ्ग प्रयात स्तोत्रं सम्पूर्णम् ।

bhujaṅgaprayātaṁ paṭhedyastu bhaktyā

samāśritya yogaṁ vrajatyacyutaṁ tvām ||
iti śrīmatparamahaṁsaparivrājakācāryasya śrīgovindabhagavatpūjyapādaśiṣyasya śrīmacchaṅkarabhagavataḥ kṛtau śrī viṣṇu bhujaṅga prayāta stotraṁ sampūrṇam |
en: O Murāri, whoever recites this Bhujaṅgaprayāta hymn with devotion, holding you fixed in mind, quickly casts off delusion by your grace and, taking refuge in union with you, goes to you, the Unfallen One. Thus ends the Śrī Viṣṇu Bhujaṅga Prayāta Stotram, composed by the glorious Śaṅkara Bhagavatpāda, disciple of the glorious Govinda Bhagavatpūjyapāda, the great paramahaṁsa wandering ācārya.

hi: हे मुरारे, जो भक्तिपूर्वक तुम्हें चित्त में स्थिर कर इस भुजंगप्रयात स्तोत्र को पढ़ता है, वह तुम्हारी कृपा से शीघ्र मोह त्यागकर, योग का आश्रय लेकर, अच्युत तुम्हें प्राप्त होता है। इस प्रकार श्रीमत्परमहंस परिव्राजकाचार्य, श्रीगोविंद भगवत्पूज्यपाद के शिष्य श्रीमच्छंकर भगवत्पाद द्वारा रचित श्री विष्णु भुजंग प्रयात स्तोत्रम् सम्पूर्ण होता है।
