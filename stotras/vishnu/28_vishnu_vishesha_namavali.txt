Title: Śrī Viṣṇu Viśeṣanāmāvalī
Devanāgarī: अथ विशेषनामावलिः (श्रीविष्णु)
Telugu: అథ విశేష నామావళిః

Author: Traditional; the source names no Purāṇa or composer. This is a short supplementary archana list printed after the Viṣṇu aṣṭottara. Its first ten entries honour Viṣṇu's retinue and the sacred world in the plural — Garuḍa, the serpents, the holy places, forests and mountains, the quarters, the brāhmaṇas, the emperors, the sixteen great kings. The rest honour Viṣṇu as guru, father, mother, grandfather, maker and sustainer of all worlds.

Blurb: An appendix to worship rather than a hymn: after the hundred and eight names of Viṣṇu, the offering is carried outward to everything that belongs to him — Garuḍa, the serpents, Kāśī and Kurukṣetra, Naimiṣa and Daṇḍaka, Meru and Kailāsa, the sky and the earth, the holy trees, the brāhmaṇas and the ancient kings. Then it comes back to him as father, mother, grandfather and great-grandfather of every world.
