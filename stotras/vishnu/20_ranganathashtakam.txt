Blurb: This is a hymn to Raṅganātha of Śrīraṅgam. Its first seven verses hold one line unchanged: 'ramatāṁ mano me', meaning 'let my mind take its rest there'. The word before it turns over and over: in his form, his play, his dwelling, his adorableness, his kingship, his auspicious sleep, his reclining. Each verse then packs six more compounds ending in that same word, so that the hymn works less like an argument than like a litany rung upon a single note. The eighth verse abandons the refrain for a virtuoso yamaka, in which every line-end rhymes on -aṅga: raṅgaṁ, aṅgaṁ, śāṅgaṁ, rathāṅgaṁ, gāṅgaṁ, vihaṅgaṁ, bhujaṅgam. It makes its one theological claim there: whoever lays down the body at Śrīraṅgam takes no body again.

en: Whoever rises at dawn and recites this holy Raṅganātha Aṣṭaka obtains all that he desires, and attains union (sāyujya) with the Lord of Raṅga. Thus ends the Śrī Raṅganāthāṣṭakam composed by the venerable Śaṅkarācārya.
tel: పుణ్యప్రదమైన ఈ రంగనాథాష్టకమును ప్రాతఃకాలమున లేచి ఎవడు పఠించునో, అతడు సర్వకామములను పొంది, రంగనాథుని సాయుజ్యమును పొందును. ఇట్లు శ్రీమచ్ఛంకరాచార్యులచే రచింపబడిన శ్రీ రంగనాథాష్టకము సంపూర్ణము.
hi: जो प्रातःकाल उठकर इस पुण्य रंगनाथाष्टक का पाठ करता है, वह समस्त कामनाएँ प्राप्त करता है और रंगनाथ का सायुज्य पाता है। इस प्रकार श्रीमच्छंकराचार्य द्वारा रचित श्री रंगनाथाष्टकम् सम्पूर्ण हुआ।

---
Recension note: The stotranidhi pages head this text as "Raṅganāthāṣṭakam – 1"; a second, unrelated Raṅganāthāṣṭakam and a separate Raṅganātha Stotram circulate on the same site, so the numeral is part of their indexing rather than of the hymn. The title counts eight, and vv. 1–8 are indeed eight, with v. 9 an added phalaśruti in anuṣṭubh — a different metre from the upajāti of the body. In v. 7 both source pages read sacitraśāyī; some printed editions instead separate it as "sa citraśāyī", taking sa as the pronoun, which does not change the sense materially. Verse 8's compressed second pāda (punarnaśāṅgaṁ yadi śāṅgameti) is transmitted in this exact form on both pages and is read here as punar na aṅgaṁ … yadi śārṅgam eti, the rhyme forcing śāṅga for śārṅga.
