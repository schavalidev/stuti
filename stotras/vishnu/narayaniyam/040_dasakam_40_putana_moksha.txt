Blurb: This daśaka tells the first of the demon killings at Gokula. It opens away from the child altogether, with Nanda gone to the king's city to pay the yearly tax and Vasudeva meeting him there. Vasudeva knows what Kaṁsa's men are preparing, but he cannot say so; he says only that the birth of Nanda's son gives him the joy of a son of his own, and then warns that there are a hundred evil omens at the border of Vraja and Nanda should go back quickly. Nanda turns back at once, but Pūtanā reaches the house while he is still away. Pūtanā comes in the shape of a beautiful young woman with bees hovering at her hair, and the women of Vraja are so taken with her manner that they cannot stop her. She sits inside the house and gives the poisoned breast, and the Lord, angry at her for the killing of children, draws at it along with her life. She falls outside in her own terrible form with both arms flung wide. The daśaka closes as the frightened women take the child up and guard him with the Lord's own names, and the poet asks for the same protection for himself.

en: After that, your father Vasudeva, who knew fully what Kaṁsa's helpers were setting about, saw Nanda — the seat of no small good fortune — who had come to the king's city to pay the yearly tax, and spoke to him. — The daśaka begins with the two fathers meeting far from the child. Vasudeva has information and no way to act on it, and the whole of what follows turns on how he uses it.
tel: ఆ పిమ్మట, కంసుని సహాయకుల ప్రయత్నమును పూర్తిగా ఎరిగిన మీ తండ్రి వసుదేవుడు, అమందమైన శుభములకు నెలవైన నందుని — వార్షిక కరమును చెల్లించుటకు రాజధానికి వచ్చిన వానిని — చూచి అతనితో పలికెను. — దశకము శిశువునకు దూరముగా, ఇద్దరు తండ్రుల కలయికతో ఆరంభమగుచున్నది. వసుదేవునికి విషయము తెలియును, కాని చేయగలిగినది ఏమియు లేదు; ముందు జరుగునదంతయు అతడు ఆ విషయమును ఎట్లు వినియోగించుకొనునో దానిపైనే ఆధారపడియున్నది.
hi: इसके पश्चात् आपके पिता वसुदेवजी ने, जिन्हें कंस के सहायकों का उद्यम पूरी तरह ज्ञात था, अमन्द मंगल के आश्रय नन्दजी को — जो वार्षिक कर चुकाने के लिये राजधानी आये हुए थे — देखकर उनसे कहा। — दशक का आरम्भ बालक से दूर, दो पिताओं की भेंट से होता है। वसुदेवजी के पास समाचार है पर कुछ करने का उपाय नहीं, और आगे का सब कुछ इसी पर निर्भर है कि वे उस समाचार का उपयोग कैसे करते हैं।

en: "Friend, the birth of your son gives me joy today as the birth of a son of my own would." The poet's word is samadhiropya, he placed his own fatherhood upon Nanda, which is exactly what has happened in fact.

hi: "हे मित्र, आपके पुत्र का जन्म आज मुझे अपने ही पुत्र के जन्म के समान सुख दे रहा है।" यह कहकर उन्होंने आपके प्रति अपने पितृत्व को व्रजेश्वर नन्द पर आरोपित करके आदरपूर्वक उनसे कहा। — वसुदेवजी उस व्यक्ति से बात कर रहे हैं जो उनके पुत्र का पालन कर रहा है, और सत्य ऐसे रूप में कहते हैं जिससे कुछ प्रकट न हो। कवि का शब्द 'समधिरोप्य' है — अपना पितृत्व नन्द पर रख दिया, जो वस्तुतः हुआ भी यही है।

en: Then you climbed into her lap without any hesitation, angry at her for the destruction of children, and drew again and again at that breast, smeared with deadly poison, which was like a great mango fruit. — Aśaṅkita, without hesitation, answers the women's hesitation in verse 5. The anger named here is not the child's; it is the Lord's anger at what she had done to other children, and it is the reason the feeding becomes a killing.
tel: పిమ్మట నీవు ఏ సందేహమును లేక ఆమె ఒడిలోనికి ఎక్కి, బాలురను చంపినందుకు ఆమెపై కోపగించి, గొప్ప మామిడిపండువలె ఉన్న ఆ స్తనమండలమును — ఘోరమైన విషముచేత పూయబడినదానిని — మరల మరల చీకితివి. — "అశంకితుడవై" అను మాట, ఐదవ శ్లోకములోని స్త్రీల సందేహమునకు సమాధానము. ఇచట చెప్పబడిన కోపము శిశువుది కాదు; ఇతర బాలురపై ఆమె చేసిన దానికి భగవంతుని కోపము. అందుచేతనే ఆ స్తన్యపానము వధ అయినది.

deva:

en: As you drew at the breast along with her very life, she cried out with a sound like thunder, went back into her own terrifying body, flung both arms wide and fell. — Four things happen in one half-line: the cry, the return to her true form, the arms flung wide, the fall. She came in a borrowed shape, and she dies in her own.
