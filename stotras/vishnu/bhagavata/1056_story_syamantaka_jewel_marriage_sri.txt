deva:

iast:
dāsye duhitaraṁ tasmai strīratnaṁ ratnameva ca|

--- verse 43 ---
deva:

iast:
evaṁ vyavasito buddhyā satrājit svasutāṁ śubhām|

--- verse 44 ---
deva:
तां सत्यभामां भगवानुपयेमे यथाविधि।

iast:
tāṁ satyabhāmāṁ bhagavānupayeme yathāvidhi|

--- verse 45 ---
deva:
भगवानाह न मणिं प्रतीच्छामो वयं नृप।
तवास्तां देवभक्तस्य वयं च फलभागिनः॥
iast:
bhagavānāha na maṇiṁ pratīcchāmo vayaṁ nṛpa|
tavāstāṁ devabhaktasya vayaṁ ca phalabhāginaḥ||
