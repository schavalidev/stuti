The differences between the witnesses listed below were read as the verses were glossed; except where this note says otherwise, each is a spelling, a sandhi or a word order of the two editions, none of them changes the sense, and the print's reading therefore stands unbracketed. The print is followed throughout. The print numbers 57 verses and GRETIL numbers 57. Spacing is the print's, as its text layer gives it. The speaker lines (श्रीशुक उवाच and the others) are printed in italic above the verse they introduce, and are given as its first line. A footnote's numeral keys it to one word, and which word that is cannot be told from the note alone, so those readings stay unbracketed until the page itself is read. Three swallowed or broken word spaces were put right as the verses were read. At verse 21 justification had left a space inside the asura's name शङ्कुशिराः, and it is closed here; GRETIL reads the name whole. At verse 53 the space between तत्र and भगवान् had been lost, and it is restored; GRETIL reads तत्र भगवान् as two words, and तत्र carries no case ending that could make it the first member of a compound. In the colophon the space between देवासुरसंग्रामे and दशमोऽध्यायः had been lost, and it is restored; GRETIL carries no colophon, so the witness here is the print itself, which spaces the locative from the ordinal in the companion colophon of adhyāya 11 (देवासुरसंग्रामे एकादशोऽध्यायः) and in every other colophon of this skandha. Nothing else was changed. The print's line-break hyphens at verses 37, 38, 54 and 57 stay exactly where the print puts them. At verse 26 the print writes सह गणैः as two words where GRETIL compounds them as सहगणैः; the print's spacing reads as it stands and is kept. Of the differences listed above, verses 2 and 24 turned out to differ from GRETIL only in where the words are spaced, the letters being the same; verses 16 and 20 differ only in the spelling of a nasal (संख्ये, संतापो for saṅkhye, santāpo); verse 18 reads वालव्यजन for GRETIL's बालव्यजन, and both words mean a whisk of hair; verse 9 names the mount गौरमृग where GRETIL has गौरमुख, and either is one animal in a list of many; verse 47 reads महागजान् in the accusative, so that the lions, tigers and boars crush the great elephants, where GRETIL's nominative महागजाः leaves मर्दयन्तः without an object, and the print's reading is the clearer one; verse 7 reads निःस्वनो for निस्वनो and verse 57 writes the elided आ of आद्यः with the print's own double avagraha (अरिणाऽऽद्यः), which is left as printed. At verse 52 GRETIL's line is shorter than the print's and cannot be aligned with it at all, so the print alone carries that reading.

en: Śrī Śuka said: Thus, O king, the Dānavas and the Daiteyas did not obtain the nectar. They were engaged in the work and they laboured at it, but they had turned their faces away from Vāsudeva.
tel: శ్రీశుకుడు పలికెను: ఓ రాజా, ఈ విధముగా దానవులు, దైతేయులు అమృతమును పొందలేదు. వారు ఆ కార్యమునందు నిమగ్నులై శ్రమించిరి, కాని వాసుదేవుని నుండి ముఖము త్రిప్పుకొనియుండిరి.
hi: श्रीशुकदेवजी ने कहा: हे राजन्, इस प्रकार दानव और दैतेय अमृत को न पा सके। वे उस कार्य में लगे हुए थे और परिश्रम भी करते थे, परन्तु वासुदेव से मुख मोड़े हुए थे।

--- verse 43 ---
deva:

iast:

--- verse 44 ---
deva:

--- verse 45 ---

--- verse 50 ---

--- verse 52 ---

iast:

--- verse 53 ---
