en: Śrī Bādarāyaṇi said: Śiva, whose banner bears the bull, heard of this. Hari had taken the form of a woman, had deluded the Dānavas, and had made the hosts of the gods drink the nectar.

--- verse 24 ---

--- verse 25 ---

--- verse 26 ---
