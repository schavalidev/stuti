Title: Śrīmad Bhāgavata 9.4 — The story of Nābhāga and Ambarīṣa
Devanāgarī: श्रीमद्भागवतम् – नवमस्कन्धः – चतुर्थोऽध्यायः

Author: Vedavyāsa, by tradition. The Bhāgavata is spoken by the sage Śuka to King Parīkṣit on the bank of the Gaṅgā, and retold by Sūta to the sages gathered at Naimiṣāraṇya.

The differences between the witnesses listed below were read as the verses were glossed; except where this note says otherwise, each is a spelling, a sandhi or a word order of the two editions, none of them changes the sense, and the print's reading therefore stands unbracketed. Of the 71 verses GRETIL has under the same number, 43 agree with the print letter for letter once spacing and the spelling of nasals are set aside; the rest differ: 5, 7, 8, 10, 11, 17, 19, 25, 26, 27, 28, 32, 33, 37, 40, 41, 46, 49, 50, 51, 52, 54, 59, 65, 66, 67, 69, 71. Four words were put right as the verses were read. At verse 2 justification had left a space inside अभाङ्क्षुः, and it is closed here. At verses 39, 46 and 50 the space between two words had been swallowed — between मां and स्पृशेत्, between तया and स, and between अनुषक्तं and मुनिः — and each is restored. Several other places where the two editions space differently are the print's own and are left exactly as printed: the line-break hyphens at verses 18, 22, 27, 52 and 62; the older spelling समुद्रा-ल्ँलोकान् at verse 51, where GRETIL has समुद्रान् लोकान्; माव हि at verse 61; and साधु विप्रेभ्यो at verse 34. Three readings were settled as the verses were glossed. At verse 1 ततं is the father, whom the brothers allot to Nābhāga as his share, which is what verse 2 then reports. At verse 25 the न of verse 24 carries over to समर्द्धयन्ति, so the pleasures do not gladden the devotee; that is the only way the two verses read as one sentence. At verse 34 the print's own numeral न्यर्बुद is kept in the meanings and is not converted to crores.

--- verse 1 ---
deva:
श्रीशुक उवाच
नाभागो नभगापत्यं यं ततं भ्रातरः कविम्।
यविष्ठं व्यभजन् दायं ब्रह्मचारिणमागतम्॥
iast:
śrīśuka uvāca
nābhāgo nabhagāpatyaṁ yaṁ tataṁ bhrātaraḥ kavim|
yaviṣṭhaṁ vyabhajan dāyaṁ brahmacāriṇamāgatam||
en: Śrī Śuka said: Nābhāga was the son of Nabhaga. He was the youngest of the brothers and a learned man, and when he came home as a brahmacārin his brothers allotted their father to him as his share of the inheritance.
tel: శ్రీశుకుడు పలికెను: నాభాగుడు నభగుని కుమారుడు. అతడు సోదరులలో కనిష్ఠుడును విద్వాంసుడును. అతడు బ్రహ్మచారియై తిరిగి వచ్చినప్పుడు, అతని సోదరులు తమ తండ్రిని అతనికి వారసత్వ వంతుగా ఇచ్చిరి.
hi: श्रीशुकदेवजी ने कहा: नाभाग नभग के पुत्र थे। वे भाइयों में सबसे छोटे और विद्वान् थे। जब वे ब्रह्मचारी होकर लौटे, तब उनके भाइयों ने अपने पिता को ही उनका भाग दे दिया।

bhrātaro'bhāṅkta kiṁ mahyaṁ bhajāma pitaraṁ tava|

en: Nābhāga asked: Brothers, what have you allotted to me? They said: We allot our father to you. He then went to his father and said: Father, my elder brothers have allotted you to me. The father said: My child, do not take that to heart.
tel: నాభాగుడు అడిగెను: సోదరులారా, నాకు ఏమి వంతుగా ఇచ్చితిరి? వారు పలికిరి: మా తండ్రిని నీకు ఇచ్చితిమి. అపుడు అతడు తండ్రి చెంతకు పోయి పలికెను: తండ్రీ, నా అన్నలు నిన్ను నాకు వంతుగా ఇచ్చిరి. తండ్రి పలికెను: కుమారా, దానిని మనస్సునకు తీసుకొనవలదు.
hi: नाभाग ने पूछा: भाइयों, आपने मेरे लिये क्या भाग रखा है? उन्होंने कहा: हम अपने पिता को तुम्हें देते हैं। तब वे पिता के पास जाकर बोले: पिताजी, मेरे बड़े भाइयों ने आपको मेरा भाग दिया है। पिता ने कहा: बेटा, उसे मन में न लो।
