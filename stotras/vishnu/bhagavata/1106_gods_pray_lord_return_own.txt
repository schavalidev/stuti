Title: Śrīmad Bhāgavata 11.6 — The gods pray to the Lord to return to his own abode, and seeing the Yādavas prepare to go to Prabhāsa, Uddhava comes to the Lord
Devanāgarī: श्रीमद्भागवतम् – एकादशस्कन्धः – षष्ठोऽध्यायः
Telugu: శ్రీమద్భాగవతమ్ – ఏకాదశస్కంధః – షష్ఠోఽధ్యాయః

Author: Vedavyāsa, by tradition. The Bhāgavata is spoken by the sage Śuka to King Parīkṣit on the bank of the Gaṅgā, and retold by Sūta to the sages gathered at Naimiṣāraṇya.

At verse 31 the Lord says yāsyāmi bhavanaṁ brahman etad-ante tava; Śrīdhara takes bhavanam with tava, so the meaning reads it as Brahmā's abode, and the words also allow the Lord's own abode. The print reads tyajemahi at verse 45, āsvādya and paramamaṁgalam at verse 44, kulanandana at verse 39 and svavīryaṁ at verse 16 where GRETIL differs; the print is followed.
