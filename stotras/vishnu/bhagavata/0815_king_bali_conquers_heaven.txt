Title: Śrīmad Bhāgavata 8.15 — King Bali conquers heaven
Devanāgarī: श्रीमद्भागवतम् – अष्टमस्कन्धः – पञ्चदशोऽध्यायः

Spacing is the print's, as its text layer gives it. The speaker lines (श्रीशुक उवाच and the others) are printed in italic above the verse they introduce, and are given as its first line. A footnote's numeral keys it to one word, and which word that is cannot be told from the note alone, so those readings stay unbracketed until the page itself is read. One word was put right as the verses were read. At verse 33 the print's text layer had lost the space between निलीनेषु and बलिर्वैरोचनः, and it is restored here. GRETIL reads the two words apart, and निलीनेषु carries its own locative ending, so it cannot be the first member of a compound. The print's spaces after a virāma (एतद् वेदितुम्, अभजद् भृगून्, समन्ताद् रुरुधे) are the print's own and are left as printed, and the line-break hyphens in verses 6, 7, 20, 21 and 23 stay where the print puts them. Where the two editions read differently the print stands: at 8 संनह्य against GRETIL's सन्नह्य, at 21 मुरजर्ष्टि against मुरजेष्ट, and at 28 मघवञ्छत्रोः against मघवन् छत्रोः. Two places have a wrong letter rather than a wrong space, which is an emendation and outside this pass, so the lines are left exactly as printed and the meanings follow the only possible reading: at 1 the print reads भूत्वेश्वरः where GRETIL reads भूतेश्वरः, and the meaning is that he is the Lord of all beings; at 10 the print reads दहद्भि where GRETIL reads दहद्भिः, and the meaning is that the leaders burned the horizons with their eyes. The print's verse 29 holds the second halves of GRETIL's verses 29 and 30, and the two half-verses GRETIL places first are absent from the print; that is why GRETIL numbers 37 verses to the print's 36, and from the print's verse 30 onward GRETIL's number is one higher. At 21 the kind of instrument the print's ऋष्टि names is not certain, and the meaning keeps the name in the place the list gives it.

rājovāca

en: The king said: Why did Hari beg three paces of ground from Bali? He is the Lord of all beings, yet he begged like a poor man. And why, though he had obtained what he asked for, did he bind Bali?
tel: రాజు పలికెను: హరి బలి నుండి మూడు అడుగుల భూమిని ఎందుకు అడిగెను? ఆయన సర్వభూతములకు ఈశ్వరుడు, అయినను దీనుని వలె అడిగెను. అడిగినది లభించినను బలిని ఎందుకు బంధించెను?
hi: राजा ने कहा: श्रीहरि ने बलि से तीन पग भूमि क्यों माँगी? वे सब भूतों के ईश्वर हैं, फिर भी दीन के समान माँगा। और माँगी हुई वस्तु पा लेने पर भी उन्होंने बलि को क्यों बाँधा?

en: Śrī Śuka said: O king, Bali had lost his fortune and his life as well at Indra's hands, and the Bhṛgus brought him back to life. That great-souled one then served those Bhṛgus with his whole heart as their disciple, and offered all his wealth to them.
