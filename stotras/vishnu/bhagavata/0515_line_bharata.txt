hi: श्रीशुक ने कहा: भरत का एक पुत्र हुआ जो सुमति नाम से कहा जाता है। वे ऋषभ के मार्ग का अनुसरण करते थे। कलियुग में कुछ पाखण्डी, जो आर्य नहीं हैं, अपनी पापयुक्त बुद्धि से उन्हें ऐसा देवता मान बैठेंगे जिसका वेद में नाम नहीं है।

He was a part of the blessed Viṣṇu himself, who takes up the quality of sattva in order to protect the world, and by his self-control and his other marks he reached the state of a great Person.

en: He guarded the earth by his own duty, whose marks are the protection, the feeding, the pleasing, the caring for and the ruling of the people, and by sacrifice and the other rites. By serving the feet of those who know Brahman he gained the yoga of devotion to the Lord. He turned this over in his mind again and again, and his understanding became wholly pure. He gave up all regard for what is not the self. The experience of Brahman as his own self arose in him of itself, and even so he guarded the earth without any pride at all.
