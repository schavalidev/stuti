Title: Śrīmad Bhāgavata 10.89 — Bhṛgu tests the three gods, and the Lord brings back the dead sons of a brāhmaṇa
Devanāgarī: श्रीमद्भागवतम् – दशमस्कन्धः – एकोननवतितमोऽध्यायः
Telugu: శ్రీమద్భాగవతమ్ – దశమస్కంధః – ఏకోననవతితమోఽధ్యాయః

Author: Vedavyāsa, by tradition. The Bhāgavata is spoken by the sage Śuka to King Parīkṣit on the bank of the Gaṅgā, and retold by Sūta to the sages gathered at Naimiṣāraṇya.

Śrīdhara takes राजन्यबन्धुः as the subject and the brāhmaṇas as the comparison, so that the kṣatriyas here are kings in name only and sit at sacrifices as brāhmaṇas do, and the meanings follow him.

--- verse 30 ---
deva:
अहं प्रजा वां भगवन् रक्षिष्ये दीनयोरिह।

iast:
ahaṁ prajā vāṁ bhagavan rakṣiṣye dīnayor-iha|
anistīrṇa-pratijño'gniṁ pravekṣye hata-kalmaṣaḥ||
en: O lord, I myself will protect the child of you two, who are helpless here. If I do not fulfil my promise, I will enter the fire and so wash away my fault.

--- verse 31 ---
deva:
ब्राह्मण उवाच
संकर्षणो वासुदेवः प्रद्युम्नो धन्विनां वरः।

iast:
brāhmaṇa uvāca
saṁkarṣaṇo vāsudevaḥ pradyumno dhanvināṁ varaḥ|
aniruddho'pratiratho na trātuṁ śaknuvanti yat||

--- verse 32 ---
deva:
तत् कथं नु भवान् कर्म दुष्करं जगदीश्वरैः।
चिकीर्षसि त्वं बालिश्यात् तन्न श्रद्दध्महे वयम्॥
iast:
tat kathaṁ nu bhavān karma duṣkaraṁ jagad-īśvaraiḥ|
cikīrṣasi tvaṁ bāliśyāt tanna śraddadhmahe vayam||
en: Then how do you wish to do this work, which is hard even for the lords of the world? You speak so out of childishness. We do not believe it.
tel: జగదీశ్వరులకే కష్టమైన ఈ కార్యమును నీవు ఎట్లు చేయగోరుచున్నావు? బాల్యబుద్ధిచే నీవు ఇట్లు పలుకుచున్నావు. దానిని మేము నమ్మజాలము.

--- verse 33 ---
deva:
अर्जुन उवाच

iast:
arjuna uvāca
nāhaṁ saṁkarṣaṇo brahman na kṛṣṇaḥ kārṣṇir-eva ca|
ahaṁ vā arjuno nāma gāṇḍīvaṁ yasya vai dhanuḥ||
en: Arjuna said: O brāhmaṇa, I am not Saṁkarṣaṇa, nor Kṛṣṇa, nor the son of Kṛṣṇa. I am the one named Arjuna, whose bow is the Gāṇḍīva.

--- verse 34 ---
deva:
मावमंस्था मम ब्रह्मन् वीर्यं त्र्यम्बक-तोषणम्।

--- verse 35 ---

--- verse 36 ---

--- verse 38 ---

न्यरुणत् सूतिकागारं शरैर्नानास्त्र-योजितैः।

--- verse 39 ---

--- verse 40 ---

iast:

--- verse 41 ---

--- verse 42 ---
