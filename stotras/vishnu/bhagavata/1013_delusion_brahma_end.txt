Title: Śrīmad Bhāgavata 10.13 — The delusion of Brahmā and its end
Devanāgarī: श्रीमद्भागवतम् – दशमस्कन्धः – त्रयोदशोऽध्यायः

Author: Vedavyāsa, by tradition. The Bhāgavata is spoken by the sage Śuka to King Parīkṣit on the bank of the Gaṅgā, and retold by Sūta to the sages gathered at Naimiṣāraṇya.

The differences between the witnesses listed below were read as the verses were glossed; except where this note says otherwise, each is a spelling, a sandhi or a word order of the two editions, none of them changes the sense, and the print's reading therefore stands unbracketed. At verse 19 justification had left a space inside कराङ्घ्र्यादिकं, at verse 49 inside आङ्घ्रिमस्तकम्, and at verse 62 inside अङ्घ्रियुग्मं; each space is closed here. The last word of verse 64, ऐलतेलया, is of uncertain sense; it is taken here with गद्गदया as the faltering speech with which Brahmā begins his praise, and the stuti itself follows in the next adhyāya.

en: Śrīśuka said: Well have you asked, greatly blessed one, best among the devotees of the Lord. You make the story of the Lord new again, though you hear it again and again.

tel: అంతట వయస్సు మీరిన ఆ గోపాలురు తమ బిడ్డల కౌగిలితో మిక్కిలి తృప్తి చెంది, వారిని తలచుకొనుచు కన్నీటితో మెల్లగా, కష్టముతో అచ్చటి నుండి వెళ్ళిరి.

--- verse 35 ---
deva:
व्रजस्य रामः प्रेमर्धेर्वीक्ष्यौत्कण्ठ्यमनुक्षणम्।

muktastaneṣvapatyeṣvapyahetuvidacintayat||

--- verse 36 ---
deva:
किमेतदद्भुतमिव वासुदेवेऽखिलात्मनि।
व्रजस्य सात्मनस्तोकेष्वपूर्वं प्रेम वर्धते॥

kimetadadbhutamiva vāsudeve'khilātmani|
vrajasya sātmanastokeṣvapūrvaṁ prema vardhate||
en: What is this, which seems a wonder? A love such as is felt for Vāsudeva, the Self of all, is growing in Vraja, and in me as well, towards these children as it never did before.

hi: यह आश्चर्य जैसा क्या है? सर्वात्मा वासुदेव में जैसा प्रेम होता है, वैसा प्रेम इन बालकों में व्रजवासियों को और मुझे भी पहले कभी न हुआ, ऐसा बढ़ रहा है।

--- verse 37 ---
deva:
केयं वा कुत आयाता दैवी वा नार्युतासुरी।
प्रायो मायास्तु मे भर्तुर्नान्या मेऽपि विमोहिनी॥
iast:
keyaṁ vā kuta āyātā daivī vā nāryutāsurī|
prāyo māyāstu me bharturnānyā me'pi vimohinī||
en: Who is she, and from where has she come? Is she a goddess, or a woman, or a demoness? Most likely she is the māyā of my Lord. There is no other who could bewilder even me.

--- verse 38 ---
deva:
इति संचिन्त्य दाशार्हो वत्सान् सवयसानपि।

iti saṁcintya dāśārho vatsān savayasānapi|
sarvānācaṣṭa vaikuṇṭhaṁ cakṣuṣā vayunena saḥ||
en: Thinking this, Dāśārha looked upon all the calves and the companions with the eye of knowledge, and he saw them all to be Vaikuṇṭha himself.

--- verse 39 ---

त्वमेव भासीश भिदाश्रयेऽपि।

naite sureśā ṛṣayo na caite

tyuktena vṛttaṁ prabhuṇā balo'vait||

--- verse 40 ---

--- verse 41 ---

--- verse 43 ---

--- verse 44 ---

svayaiva māyayājo'pi svayameva vimohitaḥ||

--- verse 45 ---

--- verse 46 ---

--- verse 48 ---
