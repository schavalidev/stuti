Title: Śrīmad Bhāgavata 12.6 — The final state of Parīkṣit, the serpent sacrifice of Janamejaya, and the branches of the Vedas
Devanāgarī: श्रीमद्भागवतम् – द्वादशस्कन्धः – षष्ठोऽध्यायः
Telugu: శ్రీమద్భాగవతమ్ – ద్వాదశస్కంధః – షష్ఠోఽధ్యాయః

Author: Vedavyāsa, by tradition. The Bhāgavata is spoken by the sage Śuka to King Parīkṣit on the bank of the Gaṅgā, and retold by Sūta to the sages gathered at Naimiṣāraṇya.

The differences between the witnesses listed below were read as the verses were glossed; except where this note says otherwise, each is a spelling, a sandhi or a word order of the two editions, none of them changes the sense, and the print's reading therefore stands unbracketed. The print is followed throughout. The print numbers 80 verses and GRETIL numbers 80. Of the 80 verses GRETIL has under the same number, 48 agree with the print letter for letter once spacing and the spelling of nasals are set aside; the rest differ: 4, 5, 12, 16, 17, 19, 21, 25, 26, 31, 36, 39, 41, 43, 45, 48, 50, 52, 55, 56, 58, 59, 64, 66, 70, 71, 72, 73, 75, 76, 77, 79. Two broken words were put right as the verses were read. At verse 26 justification had left a space inside भुङ्क्त, and it is closed here. At verse 72 the space between काम and उपसरामि was lost, and it is restored; a nominative cannot be the first member of a compound, and GRETIL reads कामउपसरामि as two words. GRETIL agrees with both corrected readings. At verse 39 the print reads यत्तल्लिङ्ग where GRETIL has यत्तल्लिङ्गं. That is a wrong letter and not a wrong space, so the line stands as printed and the meaning follows the only possible reading, that the Oṁkāra is the mark of Brahman. मनइन्द्रिय at verse 69 and ब्रह्महत्यांहःक्षपणं at verse 61 are compounds that GRETIL merely spaces, and they stand as printed. At verse 74 दशपंच शतैः was read with the print as fifteen branches made with hundreds of Yajus verses; the other possible reading, fifteen hundred verses, was considered and not adopted.

--- verse 1 ---
deva:
सूत उवाच
एतन्निशम्य मुनिनाभिहितं परीक्षिद्
व्यासात्मजेन निखिलात्मदृशा समेन।
तत्पादमूलमुपसृत्य नतेन मूर्ध्ना
बद्धांजलिस्तमिदमाह स विष्णुरातः॥
iast:
sūta uvāca
etanniśamya muninābhihitaṁ parīkṣid
vyāsātmajena nikhilātmadṛśā samena|
tatpādamūlamupasṛtya natena mūrdhnā
baddhāṁjalistamidamāha sa viṣṇurātaḥ||
en: Sūta said: Parīkṣit, that protégé of Viṣṇu, heard this which was spoken to him by the sage, the son of Vyāsa, who sees the Self in all and is even-minded. He came to the feet of that sage, bowed his head, joined his palms, and said this to him.
tel: సూతుడు పలికెను: అన్నిటిలో ఆత్మను చూచువాడు, సమదృష్టి కలవాడు అయిన వ్యాసుని కుమారుడు చెప్పిన ఈ మాటను విష్ణురాతుడైన పరీక్షిత్తు వినెను. అతడు ఆ ముని పాదముల చెంతకు చేరి, శిరసు వంచి, దోసిలి కట్టి, ఆయనతో ఇట్లు పలికెను.
hi: सूत ने कहा: सब में आत्मा को देखने वाले और समदृष्टि वाले व्यास के पुत्र ने जो कहा, उसे विष्णुरात परीक्षित् ने सुना। वह उन मुनि के चरणों के पास आया, सिर झुकाया, हाथ जोड़े और उनसे यह कहा।

--- verse 17 ---

--- verse 19 ---

--- verse 24 ---

--- verse 25 ---

--- verse 26 ---

--- verse 27 ---

--- verse 28 ---

--- verse 29 ---
