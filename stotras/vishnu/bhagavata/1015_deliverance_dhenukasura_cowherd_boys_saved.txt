en: Śuka said: Then those two reached the paugaṇḍa years, and in Vraja they became the cowherds whom the herdsmen approved. Grazing the cows along with their friends, by their footsteps they made Vṛndāvana exceedingly holy.

hi: शुक ने कहा: इसके बाद वे दोनों पौगण्ड अवस्था को प्राप्त हुए। व्रज में वे ग्वालों के द्वारा स्वीकृत गोपाल बन गये। अपने सखाओं के साथ गायें चराते हुए उन्होंने अपने चरणों से वृन्दावन को अत्यन्त पवित्र कर दिया।

en: Then Mādhava sounded his flute. He was surrounded by cowherds who sang his glory, and Bala was with him. Driving the animals before him, he entered the forest rich in pasture, wishing to play in that forest full of flowers.

en: That forest was full of sweetly humming bees, of deer and of birds. It had a lake whose water was clear like the mind of the great. The wind there carried the scent of hundred-petalled lotuses. Seeing it, the blessed Lord set his mind on playing there.

--- verse 11 ---

क्वचिच्च कलहंसानामनुकूजति कूजितम्।

kvacicca kalahaṁsānāmanukūjati kūjitam|

--- verse 12 ---
deva:
मेघगम्भीरया वाचा नामभिर्दूरगान् पशून्।

meghagambhīrayā vācā nāmabhirdūragān paśūn|
kvacidāhvayati prītyā gogopālamanojñayā||

--- verse 13 ---

cakorakrauñcacakrāhvabhāradvājāṁśca barhiṇaḥ|

--- verse 14 ---
deva:

iast:

--- verse 15 ---
deva:

--- verse 16 ---

--- verse 25 ---

--- verse 27 ---

एवं सुहृद्वचः श्रुत्वा सुहृत्प्रियचिकीर्षया।

--- verse 28 ---
deva:
बलः प्रविश्य बाहुभ्यां तालान् सम्परिकम्पयन्।

--- verse 29 ---
deva:

--- verse 33 ---

--- verse 34 ---

--- verse 37 ---

--- verse 38 ---
