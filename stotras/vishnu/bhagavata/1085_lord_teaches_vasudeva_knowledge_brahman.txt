Title: Śrīmad Bhāgavata 10.85 — The Lord teaches Vasudeva the knowledge of Brahman, and brings back the six sons of Devakī
Devanāgarī: श्रीमद्भागवतम् – दशमस्कन्धः – पञ्चाशीतितमोऽध्यायः
