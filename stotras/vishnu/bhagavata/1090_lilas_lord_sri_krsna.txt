Title: Śrīmad Bhāgavata 10.90 — The līlās of Lord Śrī Kṛṣṇa
Devanāgarī: श्रीमद्भागवतम् – दशमस्कन्धः – नवतितमोऽध्यायः

At verse 15 the words स्वपिति जगति रात्र्याम् are taken with Śrīdhara as a locative absolute, so that the world sleeps and the Lord keeps his own awareness hidden; the line can also be read as saying that the Lord himself sleeps. At verse 47 तीर्थं चक्रे नृपोनं is taken to mean that he made the earth holy by freeing it of the wicked kings, which agrees with the removal of the earth's burden in the last line; it is also read as a statement about the heavenly river alone.

en: Śuka said: The husband of Śrī dwelt happily in his own city of Dvārakā, rich in wealth of every kind and graced by the foremost of the Vṛṣṇis.
tel: శుకుడు పలికెను: శ్రీదేవి పతి తన నగరమైన ద్వారకలో సుఖముగా నివసించెను. ఆ నగరము అన్ని సంపదలతో నిండియుండెను. వృష్ణివంశ శ్రేష్ఠులు దానిని అలంకరించియుండిరి.
