--- verse 45 ---

--- verse 47 ---

--- verse 48 ---

--- verse 49 ---

--- verse 50 ---

--- verse 52 ---

iast:
devarakṣitayā labdhā nava cātra gadādayaḥ|

--- verse 53 ---

--- verse 55 ---

अष्टमस्तु तयोरासीत् स्वयमेव हरिः किल।

--- verse 56 ---

--- verse 57 ---
