Title: Śrīmad Bhāgavata 11.19 — Devotion, knowledge, and the disciplines of yama, niyama and others
Devanāgarī: श्रीमद्भागवतम् – एकादशस्कन्धः – एकोनविंशोऽध्यायः

Author: Vedavyāsa, by tradition. The Bhāgavata is spoken by the sage Śuka to King Parīkṣit on the bank of the Gaṅgā, and retold by Sūta to the sages gathered at Naimiṣāraṇya.

Recension note: Meanings written 26 Oct 2026 (session S78). The differences between the witnesses listed below were read as the verses were glossed; except where this note says otherwise, each is a spelling, a sandhi or a word order of the two editions, none of them changes the sense, and the print's reading therefore stands unbracketed. The print is followed throughout. The print numbers 45 verses and GRETIL numbers 45. Of the 45 verses GRETIL has under the same number, 24 agree with the print letter for letter once spacing and the spelling of nasals are set aside; the rest differ: 1, 2, 5, 7, 10, 13, 14, 17, 18, 19, 20, 25, 27, 28, 29, 30, 31, 32, 34, 36, 38. Spacing is the print's, as its text layer gives it. The speaker lines (श्रीशुक उवाच and the others) are printed in italic above the verse they introduce, and are given as its first line. One word was put right as the verses were read. At verse 9 justification had left a space inside तवाङ्घ्रि, and it is closed here; GRETIL reads tavāṅghri- whole. Three constructions admit more than one reading, and Śrīdhara is followed in each: at verse 7 the threefold change is taken as māyā that stands only in the middle, so that what is absent at the beginning and the end is unreal in the middle too; at verse 15 vijñāna is the seeing that these things of the three guṇas are not of one nature with the one Self; at verse 16 the created thing is followed at all three times by the subtler created thing that causes it, and what survives dissolution alone is real. The vocative at verse 28 is वारिकर्शन in the print against GRETIL's vārikarṣaṇa; the print is followed, and Śrīdhara takes the word as an address to Kṛṣṇa whose compound sense is not settled, so the meanings keep it as a name. The line-break hyphens at verses 7, 8 and 10 are the print's own and are left where it puts them.

en: The blessed Lord said: He who is full of knowledge and of learning in the scriptures, who is established in the self and does not rest on mere inference, should know this world to be māyā alone, and should give up even his knowledge into Me.

--- verse 28 ---

iast:
śrīr-guṇā nairapekṣyādyāḥ sukhaṁ duḥkha-sukhātyayaḥ|
duḥkhaṁ kāma-sukhāpekṣā paṇḍito bandha-mokṣa-vit||
en: Śrī is the qualities beginning with freedom from want. Happiness is the passing beyond sorrow and pleasure. Sorrow is the longing for the pleasure of desire. The learned man is he who knows bondage and liberation.
tel: నైరపేక్ష్యము మొదలైన గుణములు శ్రీ. దుఃఖమును సుఖమును దాటుట సుఖము. కామసుఖమును ఆశించుట దుఃఖము. బంధమోక్షములను తెలిసినవాడు పండితుడు.
hi: निरपेक्षता आदि गुण श्री हैं। दुख और सुख से पार हो जाना सुख है। काम के सुख की अपेक्षा दुख है। बन्ध और मोक्ष को जानने वाला पण्डित है।

--- verse 42 ---
deva:
मूर्खो देहाद्यहं-बुद्धिः पन्था मन्निगमः स्मृतः।

iast:
mūrkho dehādy-ahaṁ-buddhiḥ panthā man-nigamaḥ smṛtaḥ|

en: The fool is he who takes the body and the rest to be himself. The path is remembered to be that which leads to Me. The wrong path is the scattering of the mind. Heaven is the rising of the sattva guṇa.
tel: దేహము మొదలైనవి తానని భావించువాడు మూర్ఖుడు. నన్ను పొందించునది పంథ అని స్మరించబడినది. చిత్తవిక్షేపము ఉత్పథము. సత్త్వగుణము ఉదయించుట స్వర్గము.
hi: देह आदि को अपना स्वरूप मानने वाला मूर्ख है। जो मुझ तक पहुँचाता है, वह मार्ग कहा गया है। चित्त का विक्षेप उत्पथ है। सत्त्वगुण का उदय स्वर्ग है।

--- verse 43 ---

iast:
narakas-tama-unnāho bandhur-gurur-ahaṁ sakhe|

en: Hell is the rising of tamas. Friend, the kinsman is the teacher, and he is Myself. The home is the human body. He who is rich in good qualities is called rich.
tel: తమస్సు హెచ్చుట నరకము. సఖా, బంధువు గురువు, ఆ గురువు నేనే. మానుష్యమైన శరీరమే గృహము. గుణములచేత ఆఢ్యుడైనవాడే ఆఢ్యుడని చెప్పబడును.
hi: तमोगुण की वृद्धि नरक है। सखे, बन्धु गुरु है और वह गुरु मैं ही हूँ। मनुष्य का शरीर ही घर है। जो गुणों से आढ्य है, वही आढ्य कहा जाता है।

tel: సంతుష్టి లేనివాడు దరిద్రుడు. ఇంద్రియములను జయించనివాడు కృపణుడు. గుణములయందు ఆసక్తి లేని బుద్ధి కలవాడు ఈశ్వరుడు. గుణములయందు సంగము దీనికి విపరీతము.

iast:
eta uddhava te praśnāḥ sarve sādhu nirūpitāḥ|

hi: उद्धव, तुम्हारे ये सब प्रश्न भली भाँति निरूपित हो गए। गुण और दोष के लक्षण का बहुत वर्णन करने से क्या? गुण और दोष को देखना ही दोष है। दोनों से रहित होना ही गुण है।
