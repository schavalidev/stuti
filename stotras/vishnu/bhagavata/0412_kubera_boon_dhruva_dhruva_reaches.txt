Title: Śrīmad Bhāgavata 4.12 — Kubera's boon to Dhruva, and Dhruva reaches the world of Viṣṇu
Devanāgarī: श्रीमद्भागवतम् – चतुर्थस्कन्धः – द्वादशोऽध्यायः

Author: Vedavyāsa, by tradition. The Bhāgavata is spoken by the sage Śuka to King Parīkṣit on the bank of the Gaṅgā, and retold by Sūta to the sages gathered at Naimiṣāraṇya.

The differences between the witnesses listed below were read as the verses were glossed; except where this note says otherwise, each is a spelling, a sandhi or a word order of the two editions, none of them changes the sense, and the print's reading therefore stands unbracketed. The print is followed throughout. The print numbers 52 verses and GRETIL numbers 52. Of the 52 verses GRETIL has under the same number, 40 agree with the print letter for letter once spacing and the spelling of nasals are set aside; the rest differ: 1, 2, 11, 12, 13, 16, 18, 20, 40, 41, 46, 51. Spacing is the print's, as its text layer gives it. The speaker lines (श्रीशुक उवाच and the others) are printed in italic above the verse they introduce, and are given as its first line. Five words were put right as the verses were read. At verse 18 the print's text layer gave भिंक्त, where the word is भक्तिं: the anusvāra had been transposed by the decoder, and GRETIL confirms the reading भक्तिं हरौ भगवति. This is a defect of our own decoding and not a difference between the editions; the same fault has been found and corrected four times elsewhere in this corpus. At verses 30, 42 and 47 the space between two words had been lost, giving तदोत्तानपदःपुत्रो, तद्भक्तगुणैःपराजितम् and महत्त्वमिच्छतांतीर्थं, none of which can be read as it stands; the space is restored in each, and GRETIL has both words separate in each place. The print itself writes उत्तानपदः पुत्रो with the space at verse 38. At verse 36 the same thing had happened to यन्नाव्रजंजन्तुषु, and the space is restored there too; the print writes the assimilated final न् of नाव्रजन् as an anusvāra, which is this edition's habit throughout, and that spelling is kept. At verses 6, 17 and 50 the print writes गुणमय्याऽऽत्ममायया, बद्ध्वाऽऽसनं with मनसाऽऽहृताक्षः, and आत्मनाऽऽत्मानं with a double avagraha where GRETIL has the plain long vowel; that is the edition's own way of writing the sandhi and it is left as printed. At verse 1 the print reads अभ्यवदत् where GRETIL reads nyavadat, and at verse 2 यस्त्वं where GRETIL reads yat tvaṁ; both are readings of the two editions and the print stands.

en: Maitreya said: Knowing that Dhruva had turned back from the slaughter and that his anger had gone, the blessed lord of wealth came there, and, praised by the Cāraṇas, the Yakṣas and the Kinnaras, he spoke to him as he stood with joined palms.

hi: मैत्रेय ने कहा: ध्रुव को संहार से लौटा हुआ और क्रोध से रहित जानकर, धनेश्वर भगवान् वहाँ आए और चारणों, यक्षों तथा किन्नरों से स्तुति किए जाते हुए, हाथ जोड़े खड़े उनसे बोले।
