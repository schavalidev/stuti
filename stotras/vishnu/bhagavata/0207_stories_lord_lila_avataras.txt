en: Brahmā said: When the endless One rose to lift up the earth and took the body of a boar, which is made of all sacrifice, he tore with his tusk the first demon who came at him within the great ocean, as the wielder of the thunderbolt splits a mountain.

hi: ब्रह्माजी ने कहा: पृथ्वी को उठाने के लिये उद्यत अनन्त ने, सकल-यज्ञमयी वराह-तनु धारण करके, महासागर के भीतर आये आदि दैत्य को, वज्रधारी के पर्वत विदीर्ण करने के समान, दाँत से चीर डाला।

en: Brāhmaṇa, he was born in the house of Kardama, of Devahūti, with nine women; and he told his own mother the way to himself, by which she shook off in this life the mud of attachment to the qualities, which is the self's impurity, and reached the state of Kapila.
