en: We meditate on the supreme Truth, from whom this world arises, who is present within it and also apart from it, who knows all things directly and depends on nothing; who taught the Veda through the heart to Brahmā, the first seer, and about whom even learned men are bewildered; in whom this threefold creation appears to be real, as fire, water and earth appear in one another; and who by his own light is for ever free of illusion.

tel: ఇది శ్రీమద్భాగవత మహాపురాణమునందు, పరమహంసల సంహితయందు, ప్రథమస్కంధమునందు నైమిషీయోపాఖ్యానమనే ప్రథమాధ్యాయము.
hi: इस प्रकार श्रीमद्भागवत महापुराण की परमहंसों की संहिता के प्रथम स्कन्ध में नैमिषीय उपाख्यान नामक पहला अध्याय समाप्त हुआ।
