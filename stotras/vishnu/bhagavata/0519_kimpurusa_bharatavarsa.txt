en: Śrī Śuka said: In Kimpuruṣavarṣa, Hanumān, a supreme devotee who delights in the nearness of the Lord's feet, worships the blessed Lord, the primal Person, the elder brother of Lakṣmaṇa, Rāma who is lovely with Sītā, with unbroken devotion, together with the Kimpuruṣas.
