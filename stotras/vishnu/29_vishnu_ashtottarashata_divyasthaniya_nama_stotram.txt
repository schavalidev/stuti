Title: Śrī Viṣṇu Aṣṭottaraśata Divyasthānīya Nāma Stotram
Devanāgarī: श्रीविष्णु-अष्टोत्तरशतदिव्यस्थानीयनामस्तोत्रम्
Telugu: శ్రీ విష్ణు అష్టోత్తరశత దివ్యస్థానీయ నామ స్తోత్రం

Author: No source is named. Gītā Press's contents list the text as `सङ्कलित` (compiled), and its colophon gives only the title. In v. 16 the speaker addresses a sage (`मुने`); in vv. 35–36 Viṣṇu speaks in his own voice ("these are my eight self-manifest forms").

Recension note: **Gītā Press followed; the sanskritdocuments witnesses (SD) are recorded, not adopted.** **Name count 112, not 108 — both witnesses.** The title and v. 33 say "a hundred and eight", but the list runs to 112 in Gītā Press and in SD alike, in the same order. **Stotras.** No difference of reading was found. SD prints `śvetadvīpetu` as one word (v. 3) where Gītā Press has `श्वेतद्वीपे तु`. **Nāmāvalīs** (Gītā Press → SD): no. 4 `अनिरुद्धाय` → `airuddhāya`; no. 16 `अवन्त्याम् अवनीपतये` → `amavantyāṁ avanīpataye`; no. 33 `गदिनाय` → `gadināṁ` (all three SD slips); no. 30 `कूर्माचलाय` → `kūrmamacalāya`; no. 61 `श्रीगृध्रसरस्तीरे` → `śrīgṛdhrasarasastīre`; no. 90 `कृष्णकोट्यां` → `kṛṣṇākoṭyāṁ`. **Where Gītā Press's own stotra and nāmāvalī differ:** v. 9 `कूर्ममचलं` ("the unmoving Tortoise", two words) → no. 30 `कूर्माचलाय`; v. 17 `श्रीगृध्रसरसस्तीरे` → no. 61 `श्रीगृध्रसरस्तीरे`. Both are kept as printed, each in its own place. In the nāmāvalī the Kāñcī shrines become compounds with their place: v. 13 `वरदं … कमललोचनम्` → no. 43 `काञ्च्यां वारणगिरौ कमललोचनवरदाय`, and nos. 44–60 all carry `काञ्च्यां`. **The Kāñcī eighteen.** V. 17 says that eighteen of these stand in Kāñcī (`काञ्च्यामष्टादशस्थितम्`), nos. 43–60. **The eight svayaṁvyakta kṣetras.** Vv. 35–36 name eight self-manifest shrines: Śrīraṅga, Śrīmuṣṇa, Veṅkaṭādri, Śālagrāma, Naimiṣa, Totādri, Puṣkara and the Nara–Nārāyaṇa āśrama (Badarī). **Place names.** The list is the Śrīvaiṣṇava 108 divyadeśas in Sanskrit guise, beginning with the four vyūha heavens and the Ocean of Milk. Many can be matched to known shrines (Śrīraṅga, Kumbhakoṇa's Cakrapāṇi, Tirumalai as Veṅkaṭādri, Ahobila, Siṁhācala, Śrīkūrma). They are transliterated as printed, not identified. **Prefix.** Gītā Press prints `ॐ` before each name; this corpus prints `श्री` for every nāmāvalī without exception (user, 2026-09-08). **Cross-reference:** `shiva/33_shiva_ashtottarashata_divyasthaniya_nama_stotram.txt` and `devi/main/44_shakti_ashtottarashata_divyasthaniya_nama_stotram.txt` are the companion lists for Śiva and Śakti in the same volume.

Blurb: Viṣṇu's 108 holy places, the divyadeśas, as a single litany. It begins in the four heavens of the vyūhas and the Ocean of Milk and comes down to Badarī, Ayodhyā and Mathurā. It then crosses the south, from Ahobila and Tirumalai to Śrīraṅga, with eighteen shrines in Kāñcī alone. To recite the list, the phalaśruti says, is to have studied every Veda, bathed in every tīrtha and known Hari himself.
