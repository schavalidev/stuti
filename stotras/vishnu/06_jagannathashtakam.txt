Title: Jagannāthāṣṭakam
Devanāgarī: जगन्नाथाष्टकम्
Telugu: శ్రీ జగన్నాథాష్టకం

Author: Traditionally attributed to Śrī Ādi Śaṅkarācārya

Source / recension: https://stotranidhi.com/en/jagannatha-ashtakam-in-english/ (IAST), https://stotranidhi.com/hi/jagannatha-ashtakam-in-sanskrit/ (Devanāgarī), https://stotranidhi.com/jagannathashtakam-in-telugu/ (Telugu) — all fetched 2026-09-02. The Sanskrit text is consistent across all three script pages; the Telugu page additionally carries a prose meaning attributed to "Brahmaśrī Maṇḍā Kṛṣṇaśrīkānt Śarma" (copyrighted to Stotranidhi) — that prose meaning is not reproduced here; the Telugu meaning below is an independent rendering.

Verse count: 8

Blurb: This is a hymn of Śaṅkarācārya to Jagannātha of Puri. Eight times it returns to the refrain 'jagannāthaḥ svāmī nayanapathagāmī bhavatu me', meaning 'may Lord Jagannātha ever walk the path of my eyes'. Each verse paints a vivid scene of the deity: flute in hand on the banks of the Yamunā; enthroned beside Balabhadra and Subhadrā on the Nīlācala; garlanded by Rādhā's embrace. It blends the imagery of Kṛṣṇa's līlā with the temple icon's own form, which is characteristic of the way the Jagannātha cult joins the two.

--- verse 2 ---
deva:

Holding a flute in his left arm, a peacock feather on his head, fine silk about his waist, casting sidelong glances at his companions, ever renowned for his sporting life in glorious Vṛndāvana — may Lord Jagannātha ever walk the path of my eyes.

--- verse 3 ---
deva:

वसन् प्रासादान्तः सहजबलभद्रेण बलिना ।

Dwelling within the golden-hued temple on the Blue Mountain (Nīlācala) by the shore of the great ocean, together with his mighty brother Balabhadra, seated with Subhadrā between them, granting all the gods the chance to serve him — may Lord Jagannātha ever walk the path of my eyes.

--- verse 4 ---
deva:
कृपापारावारः सजलजलदश्रेणिरुचिरो

सुरेन्द्रैराराध्यः श्रुतिगणशिखागीतचरितो

--- verse 5 ---
deva:
रथारूढो गच्छन् पथि मिलितभूदेवपटलैः
स्तुतिप्रादुर्भावं प्रतिपदमुपाकर्ण्य सदयः ।
दयासिन्धुर्बन्धुः सकलजगतां सिन्धुसुतया

rathārūḍho gacchan pathi militabhūdevapaṭalaiḥ

dayāsindhurbandhuḥ sakalajagatāṁ sindhusutayā
jagannāthaḥ svāmī nayanapathagāmī bhavatu me ||
en:
Mounted on his chariot, moving along the way, graciously listening at every step to the hymns of praise that arise from the gathered brāhmaṇas, an ocean of compassion, the kinsman of all the worlds together with the ocean's daughter (Lakṣmī) — may Lord Jagannātha ever walk the path of my eyes.

--- verse 6 ---
deva:

The crowning glory of the Supreme Brahman, his eyes wide as blooming blue-lotus petals, dwelling on the Blue Mountain, his feet resting on the head of Ananta (the serpent), blissful in divine rapture, delighting in the embrace of Rādhā's graceful form — may Lord Jagannātha ever walk the path of my eyes.

--- verse 7 ---
deva:

I do not pray for a kingdom, nor for gold, nor for the splendour of worldly pleasures; I do not beg for a lovely bride, desired by all men. His story is ever sung, from age to age, by the lord of the Pramathas (Śiva) — may Lord Jagannātha ever walk the path of my eyes.

--- verse 8 ---
deva:

हर त्वं पापानां विततिमपरां यादवपते ।
अहो दीनानाथं निहितमचलं पातुमनिशं

en:
O Lord of the gods, swiftly take away this worthless cycle of worldly existence; O Lord of the Yādavas, take away the vast spread of my sins. Ah, ever steadfast in protecting the helpless and destitute — may Lord Jagannātha ever walk the path of my eyes.
