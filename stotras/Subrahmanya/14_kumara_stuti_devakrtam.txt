Title: Śrī Kumāra Stutiḥ (Deva-kṛtam)
Devanāgarī: श्री कुमार स्तुतिः (देव कृतम्)
Telugu: శ్రీ కుమార స్తుతిః (దేవ కృతం)

Author: no human author or ṛṣi is named; this is a purāṇic narrative episode, and its speakers are "the devas" as a body of characters within the story itself (deva ūcuḥ, "the gods said"). Unlike the kavaca-type texts in this collection, the source gives no viniyoga, ṛṣi, chandas or devatā metadata for it — it is a stuti embedded in a purāṇa's narrative, not an independently ritualized mantra-stotra.

Source / recension: Base text is sanskritdocuments.org's doc_subrahmanya catalog entry devaiHkRRitaMkArtikeyastotram.itx (https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_subrahmanya/devaiHkRRitaMkArtikeyastotram.itx — its raw ITRANS content had to be pulled by a direct HTTP request rather than the browser tools, which is what worked here), cross-checked word for word in Devanāgarī against stotranidhi.com's own Devanāgarī and English (IAST) editions of the identical text (stotranidhi.com/hi/sri-kumara-stuti-deva-krutam-in-sanskrit/ and stotranidhi.com/en/sri-kumara-stuti-deva-krutam-in-english/ — a Telugu-tradition-facing publisher that also carries a native-script Telugu edition of the same page). stotranidhi.com's automated-fetch block (403 on both WebFetch and the browser-navigation tools) was worked around by requesting the page directly with a browser user-agent string, which succeeded. The two sources agree exactly on the Devanāgarī of all five verses and the opening "devā ūcuḥ" tag; they diverge only in the closing colophon's stated adhyāya number. sanskritdocuments' own file is internally inconsistent on this point — its metadata comment line reads "adhyAya 12/2-6" but its embedded colophon sentence reads "ShaShThAdhyAyAntargataM" ("occurring within the sixth adhyāya"); stotranidhi's colophon consistently reads "dvādaśo'dhyāye" (twelfth adhyāya), agreeing with sanskritdocuments' own metadata line rather than with its own colophon text. This file follows the twelfth-adhyāya reading (matching stotranidhi and sanskritdocuments' metadata line) and treats "ShaShThAdhyAyAntargataM" in the sanskritdocuments colophon as a transcription slip, most likely copied over from the neighbouring vipra-kṛta document on the same site, which genuinely does belong to the sixth adhyāya. vignanam.org was searched directly and does not carry this text at all, so cross-checking here rests on sanskritdocuments.org and stotranidhi.com rather than the corpus's usual vignanam-plus-sanskritdocuments pairing.

Important correction to this item's working assumption: the brief that generated this assignment assumed sanskritdocuments.org's single catalog entry "kumArastutiH (shivapurANAntargatA)" was one continuous document in which the speaker changes partway through from the devas to a vipra, marked by an internal attribution line, and asked that this item (14) be cut from its start down to that internal marker. That assumption does not hold. Direct retrieval of kumArastutiH.itx (https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_subrahmanya/kumArastutiH.itx) shows the document opens with "vipra uvAcha" as its very first line and remains entirely the vipra's speech for all 19 of its verses (a brāhmaṇa whose yajña has been disrupted, pleading with Kumāra to restore it) — it contains no deva-spoken portion whatsoever, and is item 15's text in full, not a joint document to be split in two. The deva-kṛta portion assigned to this file is a separate catalog entry instead: "kArtikeyastotram 3 (devaiH kRRitaM shivapurANAntargatam)", filename devaiHkRRitaMkArtikeyastotram.itx, which opens with its own unambiguous "devA UchuH" and is, start to finish, the devas' five-verse praise offered after the demon Tāraka's death — a separate episode from a different adhyāya of the same Kumāra Khaṇḍa (the twelfth, versus the vipra text's sixth), not a continuation of, or an excerpt carved out of, the vipra text. stotranidhi.com's own cataloging corroborates this independently: it lists "Sri Kumara Stuti (Deva Krutam)" and "Sri Kumara Stuti (Vipra Krutam)" as two separate pages under Śrī Subrahmaṇya Stotrāṇi, not one page with a mid-document speaker change. This file therefore reproduces that entire separate deva-kṛta document in full — it is not a fragment split out of a longer text, and no split point had to be located or guessed at.

Verse count: 5 verses, numbered 2–6 in the source (both sanskritdocuments.org and stotranidhi's Devanāgarī/English editions preserve this numbering). The source's own verse 1 is not part of the excerpt as digitized by either site — it evidently belongs to the surrounding narrative prose (introducing the scene and the "devā ūcuḥ" tag) rather than to the stotra proper, and is reproduced by neither site — so this file begins at verse 2 rather than renumbering to 1, per the brief's instruction not to silently fix source numbering. Note also that stotranidhi's Telugu-language edition of this same page renumbers these same five verses 1–5 instead of 2–6; that is a presentational choice specific to that one language variant and not a textual variant, so it is not followed here.

Sections:
స్తుతిః — Stutiḥ (the devas' five-verse praise of Kumāra, opening with their own speech-tag "devā ūcuḥ")
సమాప్తి వాక్యం — Colophon (the closing sentence naming the Śiva Mahāpurāṇa chapter in which the stuti occurs)

Blurb: A victory-thanksgiving stuti embedded in the Śiva Purāṇa's account of Tāraka's death — the assembled devas, just freed from the danger Tāraka posed, praise Kumāra in his own right as creator-sustainer-destroyer of the worlds and as slayer of Bāṇāsura and Pralamba, addressing him throughout as Śaṅkara's son and their protector; it is a separate episode from, and not a continuation of, the companion vipra-kṛta stuti catalogued alongside it (item 15), which belongs to an unrelated narrative about a brāhmaṇa's disrupted yajña.

--- verse 2 | section: Stutiḥ ---
deva:
देवा ऊचुः ।
नमः कल्याणरूपाय नमस्ते विश्वमङ्गल ।
विश्वबन्धो नमस्तेऽस्तु नमस्ते विश्वभावन ॥
iast:
devā ūcuḥ |
namaḥ kalyāṇarūpāya namaste viśvamaṅgala |
viśvabandho namaste'stu namaste viśvabhāvana ||
en: The devas said: Salutations to you of auspicious form (kalyāṇarūpa); salutations to you, O Viśvamaṅgala, the well-being of the universe. Salutations be to you, O Viśvabandhu, kinsman of the universe; salutations to you, O Viśvabhāvana, who brings the universe into being and nurtures it.
tel: దేవతలు ఇలా పలికారు: మంగళకరమైన రూపము కలవాడవైన నీకు నమస్కారము. విశ్వానికి శుభాన్ని కలిగించేవాడవైన ఓ విశ్వమంగళా, నీకు నమస్కారము. విశ్వానికే బంధువైనవాడా ఓ విశ్వబంధో, నీకు నమస్కారమగుగాక; విశ్వాన్ని పుట్టించి పోషించేవాడా ఓ విశ్వభావనా, నీకు నమస్కారము.
hi: देवताओं ने कहा: मंगलमय रूप वाले, तुम्हें नमस्कार है। हे विश्वमंगल, विश्व के कल्याणकारी, तुम्हें नमस्कार है। हे विश्वबंधु, विश्व के बंधु, तुम्हें नमस्कार हो; हे विश्वभावन, विश्व को उत्पन्न कर उसका पोषण करने वाले, तुम्हें नमस्कार है।

--- verse 3 | section: Stutiḥ ---
deva:
नमोऽस्तु ते दानववर्यहन्त्रे
बाणासुरप्राणहराय देव ।
प्रलम्बनाशाय पवित्ररूपिणे
नमो नमः शङ्करतात तुभ्यम् ॥
iast:
namo'stu te dānavavaryahantre
bāṇāsuraprāṇaharāya deva |
pralambanāśāya pavitrarūpiṇe
namo namaḥ śaṅkaratāta tubhyam ||
en: Salutations to you, slayer of the foremost of the dānavas; O god who took the life of Bāṇāsura; destroyer of Pralamba, of utterly pure form — salutation upon salutation to you, O son of Śaṅkara.
tel: రాక్షసశ్రేష్ఠుని సంహరించినవాడా, బాణాసురుని ప్రాణాలను హరించిన దేవా, నీకు నమస్కారమగుగాక; ప్రలంబాసురుని నాశనం చేసినవాడా, పరమ పవిత్రమైన రూపము కలవాడా, శంకరుని తనయుడా, నీకు నమో నమః.
hi: दानवों में श्रेष्ठ के संहारक, बाणासुर के प्राण हरने वाले देव, तुम्हें नमस्कार हो; प्रलंब के नाशक, अत्यंत पवित्र रूप वाले, हे शंकर-तनय, तुम्हें बार-बार नमस्कार है।

--- verse 4 | section: Stutiḥ ---
deva:
त्वमेव कर्ता जगतां च भर्ता
त्वमेव हर्ता शुचिज प्रसीद ।
प्रपञ्चभूतस्तव लोकबिम्बः
प्रसीद शम्भ्वात्मज दीनबन्धो ॥
iast:
tvameva kartā jagatāṁ ca bhartā
tvameva hartā śucija prasīda |
prapañcabhūtastava lokabimbaḥ
prasīda śambhvātmaja dīnabandho ||
en: You alone are the creator of the worlds, and their sustainer; you alone are their destroyer too, O Śucija, born of the pure fire — be gracious. This whole manifest world is but your reflection; be gracious, O son of Śambhu, O friend of the humble.
tel: ఈ లోకాలన్నింటికీ సృష్టికర్తవు, పోషకుడవు నీవే; సంహారకుడవు కూడా నీవే, ఓ శుచిజా, పవిత్రాగ్నిలో జన్మించినవాడా — ప్రసన్నుడవు కమ్ము. ఈ ప్రపంచమంతా నీ ప్రతిబింబమే; శంభుపుత్రా, దీనులకు బంధువైనవాడా, ప్రసన్నుడవు కమ్ము.
hi: तुम ही समस्त जगतों के कर्ता हो और भर्ता भी; तुम ही उनके हर्ता (संहारक) भी हो, हे शुचिज, पवित्र अग्नि से उत्पन्न — प्रसन्न होओ। यह समूचा प्रपंच तुम्हारा ही प्रतिबिंब है; हे शंभु-पुत्र, हे दीनबंधु, प्रसन्न होओ।

--- verse 5 | section: Stutiḥ ---
deva:
देवरक्षाकर स्वामिन् रक्ष नः सर्वदा प्रभो ।
देवप्राणावनकर प्रसीद करुणाकर ॥
iast:
devarakṣākara svāmin rakṣa naḥ sarvadā prabho |
devaprāṇāvanakara prasīda karuṇākara ||
en: O master who is the protector of the gods, protect us always, O lord; O guardian who preserves the very life-breath of the gods, be gracious, O fount of compassion.
tel: దేవతలను రక్షించే స్వామీ, మమ్మల్ని ఎల్లప్పుడూ రక్షించు, ప్రభూ; దేవతల ప్రాణాలను కాపాడేవాడా, కరుణాకరా, ప్రసన్నుడవు కమ్ము.
hi: हे देवरक्षक स्वामी, हमारी सदा रक्षा करें, हे प्रभु; हे देवताओं के प्राणों की रक्षा करने वाले, हे करुणाकर, प्रसन्न होइए।

--- verse 6 | section: Stutiḥ ---
deva:
हत्वा ते तारकं दैत्यं परिवारयुतं विभो ।
मोचिताः सकला देवा विपद्भ्यः परमेश्वर ॥
iast:
hatvā te tārakaṁ daityaṁ parivārayutaṁ vibho |
mocitāḥ sakalā devā vipadbhyaḥ parameśvara ||
en: Having slain, O all-pervading lord, the demon Tāraka along with his retinue, you have freed all the gods from calamity, O supreme lord. This closes the devas' praise.
tel: ఓ విభూ, తారకాసురుడిని అతని పరివారంతో సహా సంహరించి, దేవతలందరినీ ఆపదల నుండి విముక్తులను చేశావు, పరమేశ్వరా. దేవతల స్తుతి ఇక్కడ ముగుస్తుంది.
hi: हे विभु, तारक दैत्य को उसके परिवार सहित मारकर, तुमने समस्त देवताओं को विपत्ति से मुक्त किया, हे परमेश्वर। यहाँ देवताओं की स्तुति पूर्ण होती है।

--- verse none | section: Colophon ---
deva:
इति श्रीशिवमहापुराणे रुद्रसंहितायां कुमारखण्डे द्वादशोऽध्याये तारकवधानन्तरं देवैः कृत कुमार स्तुतिः ।
iast:
iti śrīśivamahāpurāṇe rudrasaṁhitāyāṁ kumārakhaṇḍe dvādaśo'dhyāye tārakavadhānantaraṁ devaiḥ kṛta kumāra stutiḥ |
en: Thus, in the Śrī Śiva Mahāpurāṇa, in the Rudra Saṁhitā, in the Kumāra Khaṇḍa, in the twelfth chapter, after the killing of Tāraka, the Kumāra-stuti made by the devas is complete.
tel: ఈ విధంగా శ్రీ శివమహాపురాణంలో రుద్రసంహితలో కుమారఖండంలో పన్నెండవ అధ్యాయంలో, తారకవధ అనంతరం, దేవతలు చేసిన కుమార స్తుతి ముగుస్తుంది.
hi: इस प्रकार श्री शिवमहापुराण में रुद्रसंहिता के कुमारखंड में बारहवें अध्याय में, तारकवध के पश्चात्, देवताओं द्वारा की गई कुमार-स्तुति यहाँ पूर्ण होती है।
