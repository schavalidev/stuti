Title: Ṣaṇmukha Stotram
Devanāgarī: श्री षण्मुख स्तोत्रम्
Telugu: శ్రీ షణ్ముఖ స్తోత్రం

Author: Traditionally attributed to Ādi Śaṅkara, said to have composed it at the Tiruccentūr shrine. The sanskritdocuments.org recension states this attribution in an opening line ("ādiśaṅkara proktam" — "spoken by Ādi Śaṅkara"), while the stotranidhi.com/greatindian.net recension states it instead in the closing colophon ("iti śrīmacchaṅkarabhagavataḥ kṛtau ... stotram" — "thus [ends] the work of the venerable Śaṅkara").

Source / recension: Base text is stotranidhi.com's Devanāgarī edition (cross-checked word for word against its own Telugu-script edition, which also supplies the Telugu prose meaning drawn on for the tel field below), independently checked against greatindian.net's IAST reproduction of the same text — identical verses, identical colophon, confirming this is one stable recension in circulation among Telugu-tradition sources — and against sanskritdocuments.org's ITX/HTML edition (doc_subrahmanya catalog, filename ShaNmukhastotramtiruchendUr, transliterated by Sivakumar Thyagarajan). All three sources agree on the 8 verses, their order, and their content; two minor spelling-level variants were found between the sanskritdocuments recension and the stotranidhi/greatindian recension — v.1/v.2 "vṛnda" (sanskritdocuments) vs "bṛnda" (stotranidhi/greatindian), and v.2 "śītaḷāṅka" vs "śītalāṅka" — both instances of the va/ba and ḷa/la interchange common to Telugu- and Tamil-region transmission. The stotranidhi/greatindian reading is used as the base text here; the sanskritdocuments variants are noted rather than silently dropped. This text could not be located on vignanam.org under any slug tried (english/devanagari/telugu variants of "sri-shanmukha-stotram," "shanmukhashtakam," "shanmukha-ashtakam," "tiruchendur-shanmukha-stotram" all 403/absent); vignanam.org instead hosts a different, unrelated "Ṣaṇmukha Ṣaṭkam" by Anantarāma Dīkṣita (matching sanskritdocuments' separate "svāmināthaṣaṭkam" entry), which is not this text. Cross-checking therefore rests on the three sources named above rather than on vignanam.org.

Verse count: 8 numbered verses (aṣṭakam), plus an opening Gaṇeśa-invocation-and-attribution line (sanskritdocuments recension only, not verse-numbered) and a closing colophon sentence (worded differently across recensions, also not verse-numbered).

Sections:
మంగళాచరణం — Maṅgalācaraṇam (opening invocation to Gaṇeśa and attribution to Ādi Śaṅkara; present only in the sanskritdocuments.org recension)
షణ్ముఖాష్టకం — Ṣaṇmukhāṣṭakam (the eight descriptive verses proper, each closing on the same refrain, bhāvayāmi sindhutīravāsinaṁ ṣaḍānanam)
సమాప్తి వాక్యం — Colophon (closing sentence naming the text and its author)

Blurb: This is an aṣṭakam of meditation upon Ṣaḍānana, who is also called Ṣaṇmukha, as he is enthroned at his shrine on the sea shore at Tiruccentūr. Each of the eight verses joins its own set of epithets. They name his peacock mount, his six crowns, his slaying of Tārakāsura and Śūrapadma, his granting of Śacī's wish, and his giving to Agastya the essence of dharma. All eight verses close with the same refrain, 'bhāvayāmi sindhutīravāsinaṁ ṣaḍānanam', meaning 'I meditate on the six-faced one who dwells on the shore of the ocean'. The hymn therefore builds one sustained image of him, rather than telling a continuous story.

--- verse 1 | section: Ṣaṇmukhāṣṭakam ---
deva:
नारदादिदेवयोगिबृन्दहृन्निकेतनं
बर्हिवर्यवाहमिन्दुशेखरेष्टनन्दनम् ।
भक्तलोकरोगदुःखपापसङ्घभञ्जनं
भावयामि सिन्धुतीरवासिनं षडाननम् ॥
iast:
nāradādidevayogibṛndahṛnniketanaṁ
barhivaryavāhaminduśekhareṣṭanandanam |
bhaktalokarogaduḥkhapāpasaṅghabhañjanaṁ
bhāvayāmi sindhutīravāsinaṁ ṣaḍānanam ||
en: I meditate (bhāvayāmi) on the six-faced one (Ṣaḍānana) who dwells on the ocean's shore — whose abode is the hearts of Nārada and the host of divine sages and yogis, who rides the finest peacock, the beloved son of the moon-crested one (Induśekhara, Śiva), who shatters the host of diseases, sorrows, and sins besetting the world of devotees.
tel: నారదుడు మొదలైన దేవయోగుల సమూహపు హృదయాలే నివాసంగా కలవాడు, శ్రేష్ఠమైన నెమలిని వాహనంగా కలవాడు, చంద్రశేఖరుడైన శివునికి ప్రియమైన పుత్రుడు, భక్తుల రోగాలను దుఃఖాలను పాపసమూహాలను నశింపజేసేవాడు అయిన, సముద్రతీరవాసియైన ఆ షడాననుని ధ్యానిస్తున్నాను.
hi: जिनका निवास नारद आदि देवयोगियों के हृदय हैं, जो श्रेष्ठ मयूर को वाहन बनाते हैं, चंद्रशेखर शिव के प्रिय पुत्र हैं, जो भक्तों के रोग, दुःख और पाप-समूह का नाश करते हैं — सिंधुतीरवासी उस षडानन का मैं ध्यान करता हूँ।

deva:

en: I meditate on the six-faced one who dwells on the ocean's shore — the slayer of the demon Tāraka, honored by the host of gods headed by Indra, whose embrace is cooler than the moon and sandal-paste, ever absorbed in his own true nature (ātman), foremost worshipped by yakṣas, siddhas, kinnaras and other divine beings.
tel: తారకాసురుని సంహరించినవాడు, ఇంద్రుడు మొదలైన దేవతాసమూహంచే నమస్కరించబడేవాడు, చంద్రుడు చందనం కంటే చల్లని ఒడి కలవాడు, ఆత్మానందంలో మునిగినవాడు, యక్షులు సిద్ధులు కిన్నెరులు మొదలైన దివ్యులచే పూజించబడేవాడు అయిన సముద్రతీరవాసి షడాననుని ధ్యానిస్తున్నాను.
hi: तारकासुर के शत्रु (संहारक), इंद्र आदि देवसमूह द्वारा वंदित, चंद्र-चंदन से भी शीतल अंक (गोद) वाले, अपने आत्मस्वरूप में सदा रमण करने वाले, यक्ष-सिद्ध-किन्नर आदि दिव्य जनों द्वारा पूजित — सिंधुतीरवासी उस षडानन का मैं ध्यान करता हूँ।

--- verse 7 | section: Ṣaṇmukhāṣṭakam ---
deva:
पूर्णचन्द्रबिम्बकोटितुल्यवक्त्रपङ्कजं
वर्णनीयसच्चरित्रमिष्टसिद्धिदायकम् ।
स्वर्णवर्णगात्रमुग्रसिद्धलोकशिक्षकं
भावयामि सिन्धुतीरवासिनं षडाननम् ॥
iast:
pūrṇacandrabimbakoṭitulyavaktrapaṅkajaṁ
varṇanīyasaccaritramiṣṭasiddhidāyakam |
svarṇavarṇagātramugrasiddhalokaśikṣakaṁ
bhāvayāmi sindhutīravāsinaṁ ṣaḍānanam ||
en: I meditate on the six-faced one who dwells on the ocean's shore — his lotus face equal to a crore of full moons, his noble conduct fit to be praised, granter of every cherished accomplishment, his body the hue of gold, disciplinarian even of the fierce world of siddhas.
tel: కోట్లకొద్దీ పూర్ణచంద్రబింబాలతో సమానమైన ముఖపద్మం కలవాడు, కీర్తించదగిన సచ్చరిత్రుడు, కోరిన సిద్ధులను ఇచ్చేవాడు, బంగారు వర్ణపు శరీరం కలవాడు, ఉగ్రసిద్ధలోకాన్ని కూడా శాసించగలవాడు అయిన సముద్రతీరవాసి షడాననుని ధ్యానిస్తున్నాను.
hi: करोड़ों पूर्णचंद्र-बिंबों के समान मुखकमल वाले, वर्णन योग्य सच्चरित्र वाले, इष्ट सिद्धियाँ देने वाले, स्वर्णवर्ण शरीर वाले, उग्र सिद्धलोक को भी अनुशासित करने वाले — सिंधुतीरवासी उस षडानन का मैं ध्यान करता हूँ।

--- verse 8 | section: Ṣaṇmukhāṣṭakam ---
deva:
पूर्वजन्मसञ्चिताघसङ्घभङ्गतत्परं
सर्वधर्मदानकर्मपूर्वपुण्यसिद्धिदम् ।
सर्वशत्रुसङ्घभङ्गदक्षमिन्द्रजापतिं
भावयामि सिन्धुतीरवासिनं षडाननम् ॥
iast:
pūrvajanmasañcitāghasaṅghabhaṅgatatparaṁ
sarvadharmadānakarmapūrvapuṇyasiddhidam |
sarvaśatrusaṅghabhaṅgadakṣamindrajāpatiṁ
bhāvayāmi sindhutīravāsinaṁ ṣaḍānanam ||
en: I meditate on the six-faced one who dwells on the ocean's shore — intent on destroying the host of sins accumulated across past births, granter of the fruit of merit earned through every dharma, gift, and good deed, skilled in shattering the host of every enemy, husband of Indrajā (Devasenā, Indra's daughter).
tel: పూర్వజన్మలలో పోగుపడిన పాపసమూహాన్ని నశింపజేయడంలో ఆసక్తి కలవాడు, సర్వధర్మదానకర్మల పూర్వపుణ్యఫలాన్ని ఇచ్చేవాడు, సర్వశత్రుసమూహాన్ని అణచడంలో దక్షుడు, ఇంద్రజ (దేవసేన) పతి అయిన సముద్రతీరవాసి షడాననుని ధ్యానిస్తున్నాను.
hi: पूर्वजन्मों में संचित पाप-समूह के नाश में तत्पर, सर्वधर्म-दान-कर्मों के पूर्वपुण्य का फल देने वाले, समस्त शत्रुसमूह के नाश में दक्ष, इंद्रजा (देवसेना) के पति — सिंधुतीरवासी उस षडानन का मैं ध्यान करता हूँ।

--- verse none | section: Colophon ---
deva:
इति श्रीमच्छङ्करभगवतः कृतौ तिरुचेन्दूर् श्री षण्मुख स्तोत्रम् ।
iast:
iti śrīmacchaṅkarabhagavataḥ kṛtau tirucendūr śrī ṣaṇmukha stotram |
en: Thus concludes the Śrī Ṣaṇmukha Stotram of Tiruccentūr, the work of the venerable Śaṅkara (śrīmacchaṅkarabhagavat) — so reads the stotranidhi.com/greatindian.net colophon used here. The sanskritdocuments.org recension closes instead with a plainer line, "iti tiruchendūr ṣaṇmukhastotraṁ athavā ṣaṇmukhāṣṭakaṁ sampūrṇam" ("thus the Tiruccentūr Ṣaṇmukha Stotra, also called the Ṣaṇmukhāṣṭaka, is complete"), without repeating the author's name there, since that recension had already named him in its own opening line above.
tel: శ్రీమచ్ఛంకరభగవానులు రచించిన తిరుచెందూర్ శ్రీ షణ్ముఖ స్తోత్రం ఇక్కడ ముగుస్తుంది — ఇది stotranidhi.com/greatindian.net పాఠంలోని ముగింపు వాక్యం, ఇక్కడ వాడబడింది. sanskritdocuments.org పాఠం దీనికి బదులుగా సరళమైన వాక్యంతో ముగుస్తుంది: "ఇతి తిరుచెందూర్ షణ్ముఖస్తోత్రం అథవా షణ్ముఖాష్టకం సంపూర్ణమ్" (తిరుచెందూర్ షణ్ముఖ స్తోత్రం, షణ్ముఖాష్టకం అని కూడా పిలువబడేది, ఇక్కడ సంపూర్ణమైనది) — ఆ పాఠం ప్రారంభంలోనే శంకరుల పేరు పేర్కొన్నది కాబట్టి ముగింపులో దాన్ని మళ్ళీ చెప్పలేదు.
hi: श्रीमच्छंकर भगवान द्वारा रचित तिरुचेन्दूर् श्री षण्मुख स्तोत्र यहाँ समाप्त होता है — यह stotranidhi.com/greatindian.net के पाठ की समापन पंक्ति है, जो यहाँ प्रयुक्त है। sanskritdocuments.org का पाठ इसके स्थान पर एक सरल पंक्ति से समाप्त होता है: "इति तिरुचेन्दूर् षण्मुखस्तोत्रं अथवा षण्मुखाष्टकं सम्पूर्णम्" (तिरुचेन्दूर् षण्मुखस्तोत्र, जिसे षण्मुखाष्टक भी कहा जाता है, यहाँ पूर्ण होता है) — क्योंकि उस पाठ ने आरंभ में ही शंकर का नाम दे दिया था, अतः अंत में उसे दोहराया नहीं गया।

Recension note: This item does not appear on vignanam.org under any of the slugs tried (english/devanagari/telugu variants of "sri-shanmukha-stotram," "shanmukhashtakam," "shanmukha-ashtakam," "sri-shanmukha-ashtakam," "tiruchendur-shanmukha-stotram" — all returned 403/not-found); vignanam.org's actual "Sri Shanmukha Shatkam" page is a different, unrelated seven-stanza work by Anantarāma Dīkṣita (matching sanskritdocuments.org's separate "svāmināthaṣaṭkam" catalog entry), not this aṣṭakam, and was not used here. In its place, three independent Telugu-tradition-adjacent sources were collated: stotranidhi.com (Devanāgarī page and Telugu-meaning page, used as the base text and as the source for the tel field), greatindian.net (an independent IAST reproduction of the same stotranidhi text, word-for-word identical including the colophon), and sanskritdocuments.org (doc_subrahmanya catalog entry "ShaNmukhastotram athavA ShaNmukhAShTakam tiruchendUrsthita," filename ShaNmukhastotramtiruchendUr.itx, transliterated by Sivakumar Thyagarajan). All three agree on the 8 verses, their sequence, and their content. Two minor spelling-level variants were found between the sanskritdocuments recension and the stotranidhi/greatindian recension: v.1/v.2 "devayogivṛnda"/"devabṛndavanditam" (sanskritdocuments) vs "devayogibṛnda"/"devabṛndavanditam" (stotranidhi/greatindian) — i.e. vṛnda vs bṛnda — and v.2 "śītaḷāṅkam" (sanskritdocuments, retroflex ḷa) vs "śītalāṅkam" (stotranidhi/greatindian, dental la). Both are instances of the va/ba and ḷa/la interchange characteristic of Telugu- and Tamil-region Sanskrit transmission; the stotranidhi/greatindian reading was taken as the base text throughout, consistent with this corpus's Telugu-tradition orientation, with the sanskritdocuments variants recorded here rather than silently dropped. The two recensions also differ in framing rather than content: sanskritdocuments opens with a Gaṇeśa-invocation-and-attribution line ("oṁ śrīgaṇeśāya namaḥ | ādiśaṅkara proktam") not found in stotranidhi/greatindian, while stotranidhi/greatindian instead names the author in its closing colophon, which sanskritdocuments's own (shorter) colophon omits. Both framings are reproduced here, in their respective blocks, so no information from either recension is lost. No verse-numbering irregularities were found; all three sources number and order the eight verses identically, and none contains a phalaśruti verse — the text is a pure descriptive dhyāna-aṣṭakam throughout.
