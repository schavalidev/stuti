Title: Śrī Kārtikeya Karāvalamba Stotram
Devanāgarī: श्रीकार्तिकेयकरावलम्बस्तोत्रम्
Telugu: శ్రీ కార్తికేయ కరావలంబ స్తోత్రం

Author: no author is named anywhere in the source; there is no viniyoga naming a ṛṣi/chandas/devatā (unlike the kavacam in file 07), and the closing colophon is a bare completion-marker ("iti śrīkārtikeyakarāvalambastotraṁ sampūrṇam") without a poet's signature. sanskritdocuments.org labels the metre as vasantatilakā.

Source / recension: Base text is sanskritdocuments.org's proofread Devanāgarī edition (doc_subrahmanya catalog, "kArtikeyakarAvalambastotram," proofread by Rajani Arjun Shankar), which is a genuinely separate, shorter text from the eight-verse "Subrahmaṇya Aṣṭakam / Subrahmaṇya Karāvalamba Stotram" beginning "he svāminātha karuṇākara dīnabandho" that sanskritdocuments' own catalog lists as a second, unrelated entry — the two were checked against each other and confirmed distinct (this text has 5 verses in vasantatilakā, the other has 8 in a different metre and opening line). Cross-checked against vignanam.org's English/IAST page ("Sri Kartikeya Karavalamba Stotram"), which matches this text's shape but has two divergences worth flagging: it silently adopts the bracketed alternate reading "siṅgāra vela" in v.1 and "mahāvanaghne" in v.5 (sanskritdocuments' base text lists these only as parenthetical alternates to its primary readings "śṛṅgāraveṣa" and "mahāvanāgne"), and more significantly it appears to drop the entire opening pāda of v.1 ("oṅkārarūpa śaraṇāśraya śarvasūno"), printing only three pādas for that verse. A stotranidhi.com search-result snippet for v.1 was used to adjudicate: it confirms the full four-pāda reading including "oṅkārarūpa śaraṇāśraya śarvasūno" (so vignanam's omission is treated as an error, not a genuine variant), while itself also preferring "siṅgāra vela" over "śṛṅgāraveṣa" for the second pāda — so that particular word-choice is a real, attested variant, kept here as sanskritdocuments' bracketed alternate rather than adopted into the base text. hariome.com's Telugu-script page ("శ్రీ కార్తికేయ కరావలంబ స్తోత్రం") was used as a Telugu-tradition check and confirms the same title, the same five-verse count, the same refrain, and the same colophon wording, but a verse-by-verse Telugu collation was not separately performed against it. Overall this rests on one full Devanāgarī source plus one full IAST cross-check (with one flagged omission) and two partial/snippet-level confirmations — solid two-source verbatim agreement, short of a three-way full collation.

Verse count: 5 numbered verses in vasantatilakā metre, each ending in the identical refrain "śrīkārtikeya mama dehi karāvalambam," followed by a one-line prose colophon marking the stotra's completion. There is no viniyoga, nyāsa, dhyāna, or phalaśruti section attached to this text.

Sections:
స్తోత్రం — Stotram (the five vasantatilakā verses, each stacking a cluster of Kārtikeya's names and epithets before the closing plea)
పుష్పిక — Puṣpikā (the one-line closing colophon marking the text as complete)

Blurb: A five-verse karāvalamba stotra built on a dhruvapada (fixed-refrain) structure: each vasantatilakā verse strings together a fresh cluster of Kārtikeya's names and epithets — son of Śarva, Guha, husband of Vallī, six-faced Ṣaḍānana, foe of Tāraka — and then repeats, verbatim, the same closing line "śrīkārtikeya mama dehi karāvalambam" ("Śrī Kārtikeya, extend to me the support of your hand"), the begging refrain that gives the karāvalamba genre its name.

--- verse 1 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
ओङ्काररूप शरणाश्रय शर्वसूनो
शृङ्गारवेष सकलेश्वर दीनबन्धो ।
सन्तापनाशन सनातन शक्तिहस्त
श्रीकार्तिकेय मम देहि करावलम्बम् ॥
iast:
oṅkārarūpa śaraṇāśraya śarvasūno
śṛṅgāraveṣa sakaleśvara dīnabandho |
santāpanāśana sanātana śaktihasta
śrīkārtikeya mama dehi karāvalambam ||
en: O you whose very form is the syllable Oṁ, refuge of those who take shelter in you, son of Śarva (Śiva); of splendid, beautifully-adorned bearing, lord of all, friend of the humble; destroyer of anguish, eternal one, holding the śakti-spear in hand — O Śrī Kārtikeya, grant me the support of your hand.
tel: ఓంకార స్వరూపుడవు, శరణాగతులకు ఆశ్రయమైనవాడవు, శర్వుని కుమారుడవు; సుందర శృంగార వేషధారివి, సకలేశ్వరుడవు, దీనబంధువు; సంతాపనాశకుడవు, సనాతనుడవు, శక్తిని చేతిలో ధరించినవాడవు — ఓ శ్రీ కార్తికేయా, నాకు నీ కరావలంబాన్ని (చేయూతను) ప్రసాదించు.
hi: हे ओंकार-स्वरूप, शरणागतों के आश्रय, शर्व (शिव) के पुत्र; सुंदर शृंगार-वेष धारण करने वाले, सकल के ईश्वर, दीनबंधु; संताप के नाशक, सनातन, हाथ में शक्ति धारण करने वाले — हे श्री कार्तिकेय, मुझे अपना करावलंब (हाथ का सहारा) प्रदान करो।

--- verse 2 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
पञ्चाद्रिवास सहजा सुरसैन्यनाथ
पञ्चामृतप्रिय गुहासकलाधिवास ।
गङ्गेन्दु मौलि तनय मयिल्वाहनस्थ
श्रीकार्तिकेय मम देहि करावलम्बम् ॥
iast:
pañcādrivāsa sahajā surasainyanātha
pañcāmṛtapriya guhāsakalādhivāsa |
gaṅgendu mauli tanaya mayilvāhanastha
śrīkārtikeya mama dehi karāvalambam ||
en: Dweller of the five mountains, twin-born, lord of the army of the gods; fond of the fivefold nectar-offering (pañcāmṛta), O Guha who pervades everything; son of the one crowned with the Gaṅgā and the moon (Śiva), mounted on a peacock — O Śrī Kārtikeya, grant me the support of your hand.
tel: పంచగిరులలో నివసించువాడా, సహజంగా (కవలగా) జన్మించినవాడా, దేవసేనలకు నాథుడా; పంచామృతప్రియుడా, ఓ గుహా — సర్వత్రా వ్యాపించినవాడా; గంగను చంద్రుని శిరస్సున ధరించిన శివుని కుమారుడా, నెమలిని వాహనంగా కలవాడా — ఓ శ్రీ కార్తికేయా, నాకు నీ కరావలంబాన్ని ప్రసాదించు.
hi: हे पंच पर्वतों के वासी, सहज (साथ) जन्मे, देवसेना के स्वामी; पंचामृत-प्रिय, हे गुह — सर्वत्र व्याप्त रहने वाले; गंगा व चंद्र को शिर पर धारण करने वाले शिव के पुत्र, मयूर-वाहन वाले — हे श्री कार्तिकेय, मुझे अपना करावलंब प्रदान करो।

--- verse 3 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
आपद्विनाशक कुमारक चारुमूर्ते
तापत्रयान्तक दयापर तारकारे ।
आर्ताऽभयप्रद गुणत्रय भव्यराशे
श्रीकार्तिकेय मम देहि करावलम्बम् ॥
iast:
āpadvināśaka kumāraka cārumūrte
tāpatrayāntaka dayāpara tārakāre |
ārtā'bhayaprada guṇatraya bhavyarāśe
śrīkārtikeya mama dehi karāvalambam ||
en: Destroyer of calamities, dear young prince, beautiful of form; ender of the threefold affliction, full of compassion, foe of Tāraka; granter of fearlessness to the afflicted, O mass of auspiciousness holding the three guṇas — O Śrī Kārtikeya, grant me the support of your hand.
tel: ఆపదలను నశింపజేయువాడా, ముద్దుల కుమారా, సుందర స్వరూపుడా; త్రితాపాలను అంతం చేయువాడా, దయాపరుడా, తారకాసుర శత్రువా; ఆర్తులకు అభయమిచ్చువాడా, త్రిగుణాత్మకమైన శుభరాశీ — ఓ శ్రీ కార్తికేయా, నాకు నీ కరావలంబాన్ని ప్రసాదించు.
hi: हे आपत्तियों के नाशक, प्रिय कुमार, सुंदर स्वरूप वाले; त्रिविध ताप के अंतक, दयालु, तारकासुर के शत्रु; पीड़ितों को अभय देने वाले, त्रिगुणात्मक शुभ-राशि — हे श्री कार्तिकेय, मुझे अपना करावलंब प्रदान करो।

--- verse 4 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
वल्लीपते सुकृतदायक पुण्यमूर्ते
स्वर्लोकनाथपरिसेवित शम्भुसूनो ।
त्रैलोक्यनायक षडानन भूतपाद
श्रीकार्तिकेय मम देहि करावलम्बम् ॥
iast:
vallīpate sukṛtadāyaka puṇyamūrte
svarlokanāthaparisevita śambhusūno |
trailokyanāyaka ṣaḍānana bhūtapāda
śrīkārtikeya mama dehi karāvalambam ||
en: O husband of Vallī, giver of the fruit of good deeds, embodiment of holiness; attended upon by the lords of the heavenly world, O son of Śambhu (Śiva); leader of the three worlds, six-faced one, at whose feet all beings bow — O Śrī Kārtikeya, grant me the support of your hand.
tel: వల్లీదేవి పతీ, సత్కర్మఫలదాతా, పుణ్యస్వరూపుడా; స్వర్గలోకనాథులచే సేవింపబడువాడా, శంభుని కుమారా; ముల్లోకాలకు నాయకుడా, షడాననా, సర్వభూతములు పాదాలచెంత మ్రొక్కు దైవమా — ఓ శ్రీ కార్తికేయా, నాకు నీ కరావలంబాన్ని ప్రసాదించు.
hi: हे वल्ली के पति, सत्कर्म-फलदाता, पुण्य-स्वरूप; स्वर्गलोक के स्वामियों द्वारा सेवित, शंभु-पुत्र; त्रिलोक के नायक, षडानन, जिनके चरणों में समस्त भूत नमन करते हैं — हे श्री कार्तिकेय, मुझे अपना करावलंब प्रदान करो।

--- verse 5 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
ज्ञानस्वरूप सकलात्मक वेदवेद्य
ज्ञानप्रियाऽखिलदुरन्त महावनाग्ने ।
दीनावनप्रिय निरामय दानसिन्धो
श्रीकार्तिकेय मम देहि करावलम्बम् ॥
iast:
jñānasvarūpa sakalātmaka vedavedya
jñānapriyā'khiladuranta mahāvanāgne |
dīnāvanapriya nirāmaya dānasindho
śrīkārtikeya mama dehi karāvalambam ||
en: Whose very form is knowledge, the self of all, knowable through the Vedas; dear to true wisdom, the fire that consumes the boundless great forest [of ignorance]; fond of protecting the humble, free of affliction, O ocean of generosity — O Śrī Kārtikeya, grant me the support of your hand.
tel: జ్ఞానమే స్వరూపముగా కలవాడా, సర్వాత్మకుడా, వేదములచే తెలియదగినవాడా; జ్ఞానప్రియుడా, అంతులేని మహారణ్యాన్ని దహించు అగ్నివంటివాడా; దీనులను రక్షించుటలో ప్రీతి కలవాడా, రోగరహితుడా, దానసముద్రుడా — ఓ శ్రీ కార్తికేయా, నాకు నీ కరావలంబాన్ని ప్రసాదించు.
hi: हे ज्ञान-स्वरूप, सर्वात्मक, वेदों द्वारा जानने योग्य; ज्ञान-प्रिय, अनंत महावन को दहन करने वाली अग्नि के समान; दीनों की रक्षा में रुचि रखने वाले, नीरोग, दान के सागर — हे श्री कार्तिकेय, मुझे अपना करावलंब प्रदान करो।

hi: इस प्रकार श्री कार्तिकेय करावलंब स्तोत्र सम्पूर्ण होता है।

Recension note: Base text is sanskritdocuments.org's proofread Devanāgarī edition of "kArtikeyakarAvalambastotram" (doc_subrahmanya catalog). This is confirmed as a genuinely separate text from the eight-verse "he svāminātha karuṇākara dīnabandho" Subrahmaṇya Aṣṭakam/Karāvalamba Stotram that the same catalog lists as its own entry — the two were checked against each other by opening line, verse count, and metre and found to be unrelated texts sharing only the karāvalamba genre-name. Two variant readings are inherited from the base source's own bracketed alternates and are noted rather than silently resolved: v.1's second pāda is printed here as "śṛṅgāraveṣa sakaleśvara dīnabandho" (sanskritdocuments' primary reading), against the widely-attested alternate "siṅgāra vela sakaleśvara dīnabandho" adopted by both vignanam.org and a stotranidhi.com snippet; and v.5's "mahāvanāgne" (primary) against the alternate "mahāvanaghne" adopted by vignanam.org. Separately, vignanam.org's English/IAST page appears to have accidentally dropped the entire opening pāda of v.1 ("oṅkārarūpa śaraṇāśraya śarvasūno"), printing only three of the verse's four pādas; this is treated as an omission on vignanam's part rather than a genuine variant, since stotranidhi.com's snippet independently confirms the full four-pāda reading. Two hyphenated compound spellings in the base source (गुहा-सकलाधिवास in v.2, स्वर्लोकनाथ-परिसेवित in v.4) were closed up without the hyphen here per this corpus's rule against editorial marks inside deva/iast; this changes only the orthographic display, not the reading. hariome.com's Telugu-script page confirms the same title, five-verse count, refrain, and colophon, giving Telugu-tradition support for the text's overall shape, though a verse-by-verse Telugu collation was not separately performed. No verse-numbering irregularities were found; all sources consulted agree on 5 verses plus the one-line colophon, and this stotra carries no viniyoga, nyāsa, dhyāna, or phalaśruti apparatus (unlike the kavacam in file 07).
