Title: Śrī Ṣaṇmukha Ṣaṭkam
Devanāgarī: श्री षण्मुख षट्कम्
Telugu: శ్రీ షణ్ముఖ షట్కం

Author: Ananta Rāma Dīkṣita (per the closing colophon)

Source / recension: stotranidhi.com — Devanāgarī and English (IAST) editions cross-checked verse-for-verse, with its Telugu edition's per-verse meaning gloss used to verify sense. Confirmed distinct from the already-written Śrī Skanda Ṣaṭkam (Subrahmanya/26) and Śrī Subrahmaṇya Ṣaṭkam (Subrahmanya/27) — a separate composition with its own metre and refrain, attributed to a named author.

Verse count: 7 (6 + phalaśruti)

Sections: స్తుతి — Stuti (vv. 1–6), ఫలశ్రుతి — Phalaśruti (v. 7)

Blurb: This hymn is a garland of epithets that share their opening sounds. In each quarter-verse the words begin with the same sound, such as gi, gā and ga together, or śa repeated, or ka repeated. Every verse then reaches the same refrain: 'jaya jaya he guha ṣaṇmukha sundara, dehi ratiṁ tava pādayuge', meaning 'victory, victory to you, O Guha, O beautiful Ṣaṇmukha; grant me devotion at your two feet'.

--- verse 1 | section: Stuti ---

गुणगणभूषण कोमलभाषण क्रौञ्चविदारण कुन्दतनो ।

en: O son of the mountain's daughter (Pārvatī), risen from the waters of the Gaṅgā, your childlike body fragrant with sweet scent; adorned with a host of virtues, gentle of speech, cleaver of Krauñca, your body white as jasmine; brother of the elephant-faced one (Gaṇeśa), your divine body the destroyer of unconquerable demon-hosts — victory, victory to you, O Guha, beautiful Ṣaṇmukha! Grant me devotion at your two feet.

--- verse 2 | section: Stuti ---
deva:
प्रतिगिरिसंस्थित भक्तहृदिस्थित पुत्रधनप्रद रम्यतनो

iast:
pratigirisaṁsthita bhaktahṛdisthita putradhanaprada ramyatano
bhavabhayamocaka bhāgyavidhāyaka bhūsutavāra supūjyatano |
bahubhujaśobhita bandhavimocaka bodhaphalaprada bodhatano

en: Enthroned on every hill, dwelling in devotees' hearts, granter of sons and wealth, lovely of body; releasing the fear of worldly existence, dispenser of fortune, your body especially worshipped on Tuesday (Bhūsuta's day); resplendent with many arms, releasing bondage, giver of the fruit of awakening, your very body awakening itself — victory, victory to you, O Guha, beautiful Ṣaṇmukha! Grant me devotion at your two feet.

--- verse 3 | section: Stuti ---
deva:
शमधनमानित मौनिहृदालय मोक्षकृदालय मुग्धतनो

iast:
śamadhanamānita maunihṛdālaya mokṣakṛdālaya mugdhatano
śatamakhapālaka śaṅkaratoṣaka śaṅkhasuvādaka śaktitano

en: Honored by silent sages whose wealth is tranquility, the very abode granting liberation, your body enchanting; protector of Śatamakha (Indra), delighter of Śaṅkara, sweet sounder of the conch, your body bearing the spear; beautiful as a thousand Manmathas together, O lord whose ears are adorned with earrings — victory, victory to you, O Guha, beautiful Ṣaṇmukha! Grant me devotion at your two feet.

--- verse 4 | section: Stuti ---
deva:
गुह तरुणारुणचेलपरिष्कृत तारकमारक मारतनो
जलनिधितीरसुशोभिवरालय शङ्करसन्नुत देवगुरो ।
विहितमहाध्वरसामनिमन्त्रित सौम्यहृदन्तर सोमतनो
जय जय हे गुह षण्मुख सुन्दर देहि रतिं तव पादयुगे ॥
iast:
guha taruṇāruṇacelapariṣkṛta tārakamāraka māratano
jalanidhitīrasuśobhivarālaya śaṅkarasannuta devaguro |
vihitamahādhvarasāmanimantrita saumyahṛdantara somatano
jaya jaya he guha ṣaṇmukha sundara dehi ratiṁ tava pādayuge ||
en: O Guha, adorned with a garment red as the young dawn, slayer of Tāraka, your body beautiful as Manmatha's own; whose splendid shrine graces the ocean's shore, O guru of the gods, praised even by Śaṅkara; invited by the Sāma-mantras of great sacrifices duly performed, your body cool as the moon within the hearts of the gentle — victory, victory to you, O Guha, beautiful Ṣaṇmukha! Grant me devotion at your two feet.

--- verse 5 | section: Stuti ---
deva:
लवलिकया सह केलिकलापर देवसुतार्पित माल्यतनो
गुरुपदसंस्थित शङ्करदर्शित तत्त्वमयप्रणवार्थविभो ।
विधिहरिपूजित ब्रह्मसुतार्पित भाग्यसुपूरक योगितनो
जय जय हे गुह षण्मुख सुन्दर देहि रतिं तव पादयुगे ॥
iast:
lavalikayā saha kelikalāpara devasutārpita mālyatano
gurupadasaṁsthita śaṅkaradarśita tattvamayapraṇavārthavibho |
vidhiharipūjita brahmasutārpita bhāgyasupūraka yogitano
jaya jaya he guha ṣaṇmukha sundara dehi ratiṁ tava pādayuge ||
en: Delighting in play together with Lavalikā (Vallī), your body garlanded with flowers offered by celestial maidens; seated in the guru's seat, O lord who revealed to Śaṅkara the truth-laden meaning of the praṇava; worshipped by Brahmā and Hari, honored with offerings by Brahmā's daughter (Sarasvatī), your yogic body filling devotees with good fortune — victory, victory to you, O Guha, beautiful Ṣaṇmukha! Grant me devotion at your two feet.

--- verse 6 | section: Stuti ---
deva:
कलिजनपालन कञ्जसुलोचन कुक्कुटकेतन केलितनो
कृतबलिपालन बर्हिणवाहन फालविलोचनशम्भुतनो ।
शरवणसम्भव शत्रुनिबर्हण चन्द्रसमानन शर्मतनो
जय जय हे गुह षण्मुख सुन्दर देहि रतिं तव पादयुगे ॥
iast:
kalijanapālana kañjasulocana kukkuṭaketana kelitano
kṛtabalipālana barhiṇavāhana phālavilocanaśambhutano |
śaravaṇasambhava śatrunibarhaṇa candrasamānana śarmatano
jaya jaya he guha ṣaṇmukha sundara dehi ratiṁ tava pādayuge ||
