Title: Kārtikeya Stotram
Devanāgarī: कार्तिकेय स्तोत्रम्
Telugu: కార్తికేయ స్తోత్రం

Author: anonymous; embedded within the Śiva Purāṇa's own narrative — the text is presented as words spoken directly by the devas (devā ūcuḥ, "the gods said") to Kārtikeya, with no human composer named.

Source / recension: Base text is sanskritdocuments.org's proofread ITRANS file devaiHkRRitaMkArtikeyastotram.itx (proofread by PSA Easwaran), catalogued in the site's doc_subrahmanya listing as "kArtikeyastotram 3" — one of four differently-sourced Puranic texts that share the same generic catalog name "kArtikeyastotram": KS1 is the Rudrayāmala's prajñāvivardhana hymn (a separate item elsewhere in this corpus, not used here); KS2 opens "bhāsvadvajraprakāśo daśaśatanayanenārcito vajrapāṇiḥ"; KS4 is a Skandapurāṇa text. This item uses KS3 because the catalog's own link list shows it as by far the most reproduced of the four: seven separate manuscript/print scans plus five Hindi translations, one Bengali translation, and a citation in an academic thesis are linked against it, versus a single scan for KS2 (KS4 has nearly as many scans, but no independent translations, so KS3's spread across languages was taken as the stronger sign of wide circulation). Independently, a web search located this same passage under the Śiva Purāṇa's own canonical numbering, 2.4.12.6 (Rudra-saṁhitā = book 2, Kumāra-khaṇḍa = section 4, adhyāya 12, verse 6), at wisdomlib.org — confirming this is a genuine, independently-catalogued Puranic passage and not a website-only compilation — but wisdomlib.org itself blocks automated fetching, so this stands as a title/numbering confirmation only, not a verse-by-verse collation against a second full transcription. vignanam.org, this corpus's designated primary source, carries no page plainly titled "Kartikeya Stotram" in any script; its only two Kārtikeya-titled pages are the Pragna Vivardhana Stotram and the Karavalamba Stotram, both separate items elsewhere in this corpus. stotranidhi.com does carry a page plainly titled "Sri Karthikeya Stotram," but on inspection its text is an entirely different, much longer (17-verse) composition by the Śṛṅgeri Jagadguru Śrī Sacchidānanda Śivābhinava Nṛsiṁha Bhāratī Svāmi, catalogued at sanskritdocuments.org under the distinct name "kArtikeyastotrakadambam" — noted here only to record that it exists and is not the text used for this item, so it is not mistaken for the same work. hariome.com's Kārtikeya-titled pages (Śoḍaśanāma, Pragnyā Vivardhana) likewise do not match this text. Because only the sanskritdocuments.org file itself could be retrieved in full, cross-checking for this item rests on that file's own scan pedigree and the independent Śiva Purāṇa canonical-number confirmation rather than on a second, independently fetched complete transcription — flagged here as reduced cross-checking, consistent with file 08's practice.

Verse count: 5 verses, numbered 2 through 6 exactly as printed in the source — the excerpt carries over the continuous verse-count of its surrounding adhyāya rather than restarting at 1 for the hymn itself, so verse 1 of that count lies outside this passage and is not given here. The hymn is framed by a two-word unnumbered speech-attribution (devā ūcuḥ) and a closing unnumbered prose colophon sentence.

Sections:
ఉవాచ — Uvāca (the two-word narrative tag introducing what follows as spoken by the devas)
స్తోత్రం — Stotram (the five verses proper)
సమాప్తవాక్యం — Colophon (closing sentence naming the Puranic source)

Blurb: A five-verse hymn that the Śiva Purāṇa places in the mouths of the gods, sung to Kārtikeya immediately after he kills the demon Tāraka and his army, praising him in quick succession as the universe's creator-sustainer-destroyer and as the slayer of several other named demons, and asking him to go on protecting the gods always.

--- verse none | section: Uvāca ---
deva:
देवा ऊचुः ।
iast:
devā ūcuḥ |
en: The gods said — the speech-attribution introducing the hymn that follows.
tel: దేవతలు ఇలా పలికారు — తరువాతి స్తోత్రాన్ని పలికే ముందు వాడిన వాక్యం.
hi: देवताओं ने कहा — आगे के स्तोत्र से पहले प्रयुक्त वाक्य।

--- verse 2 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
नमः कल्याणरूपाय नमस्ते विश्वमङ्गल ।
विश्वबन्धो नमस्तेऽस्तु नमस्ते विश्वभावन ॥
iast:
namaḥ kalyāṇarūpāya namaste viśvamaṅgala |
viśvabandho namaste'stu namaste viśvabhāvana ||
en: Salutation to you of auspicious form; salutation to you, O auspiciousness of the universe (viśvamaṅgala); O bond/kinsman of the universe (viśvabandhu), let there be salutation to you; salutation to you, O nurturer of the universe (viśvabhāvana).
tel: మంగళరూపుడా, నీకు నమస్కారం; విశ్వానికి మంగళకరుడవైన వాడా, నీకు నమస్కారం; విశ్వబంధువా, నీకు నమస్కారమగుగాక; విశ్వాన్ని పోషించువాడా, నీకు నమస్కారం.
hi: मंगलरूप वाले, आपको नमस्कार; विश्व के मंगलस्वरूप, आपको नमस्कार; विश्वबन्धु, आपको नमस्कार हो; विश्व का पोषण करने वाले, आपको नमस्कार।

--- verse 3 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
नमोऽस्तु ते दानववर्यहन्त्रे बाणासुरप्राणहराय देव ।
प्रलम्बनाशाय पवित्ररूपिणे नमो नमश्शङ्करतात तुभ्यम् ॥
iast:
namo'stu te dānavavaryahantre bāṇāsuraprāṇaharāya deva |
pralambanāśāya pavitrarūpiṇe namo namaśśaṅkaratāta tubhyam ||
en: Salutation be to you, slayer of the chief of demons, O god who has taken the life-breath of the demon Bāṇāsura; to the destroyer of the demon Pralamba, of pure form — obeisance, obeisance again to you, O dear one born of Śaṅkara.
tel: దానవశ్రేష్ఠుని సంహరించిన నీకు నమస్కారమగుగాక, బాణాసురుని ప్రాణాలను తీసిన దేవా; ప్రలంబాసురుని నశింపజేసిన, పవిత్రరూపుడవైన నీకు మళ్ళీ మళ్ళీ నమస్కారం, శంకరుని ప్రియతనయా.
hi: दानवश्रेष्ठ का संहार करने वाले आपको नमस्कार हो, बाणासुर के प्राण हरने वाले देव; प्रलंबासुर का नाश करने वाले, पवित्ररूप आपको बार-बार नमस्कार, शंकर के प्रिय पुत्र।

--- verse 4 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
त्वमेव कर्त्ता जगतां च भर्त्ता त्वमेव हर्त्ता शुचिज प्रसीद ।
प्रपञ्चभूतस्तव लोकबिम्बः प्रसीद शम्भ्वात्मज दीनबन्धो ॥
iast:
tvameva karttā jagatāṁ ca bharttā tvameva harttā śucija prasīda |
prapañcabhūtastava lokabimbaḥ prasīda śambhvātmaja dīnabandho ||
en: You alone are the creator of the worlds, and you alone their sustainer; you alone are also their destroyer — O pure-born one, be gracious. This visible world is your own reflected image (lokabimba); be gracious, O son of Śambhu, O friend of the humble.
tel: నీవే లోకాలకు సృష్టికర్తవు, నీవే వాటికి పోషకుడవు; నీవే వాటిని లయం చేయువాడవు కూడా — పవిత్రజన్ముడా, ప్రసన్నుడవు కమ్ము. ఈ కనిపించే ప్రపంచం నీ ప్రతిబింబమే; ప్రసన్నుడవు కమ్ము, శంభుపుత్రా, దీనబంధూ.
hi: तुम्हीं जगत् के कर्ता हो, और तुम्हीं उनके भर्ता (पालक) हो; तुम्हीं उनका संहार भी करने वाले हो — पवित्रजन्मा, प्रसन्न हों। यह दिखने वाला संसार तुम्हारा ही प्रतिबिम्ब है; प्रसन्न हों, शम्भुपुत्र, दीनबन्धु।

--- verse 5 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
देवरक्षाकर स्वामिन् रक्ष नस्सर्वदा प्रभो ।
देवप्राणावनकर प्रसीद करुणाकर ॥
iast:
devarakṣākara svāmin rakṣa nassarvadā prabho |
devaprāṇāvanakara prasīda karuṇākara ||
en: O master who is the protector of the gods, protect us always, O lord; O guardian of the very life-breath of the gods, be gracious, O fount of compassion.
tel: దేవతల రక్షకుడవైన స్వామీ, మమ్మల్ని ఎల్లప్పుడూ రక్షించు, ప్రభూ; దేవతల ప్రాణాలను కాపాడువాడా, ప్రసన్నుడవు కమ్ము, కరుణాకరా.
hi: देवताओं के रक्षक स्वामी, हमारी सदा रक्षा करें, प्रभो; देवताओं के प्राणों की रक्षा करने वाले, प्रसन्न हों, करुणाकर।

--- verse 6 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
हत्वा ते तारकं दैत्यं परिवारयुतं विभो ।
मोचितास्सकला देवा विपद्भ्यः परमेश्वर ॥
iast:
hatvā te tārakaṁ daityaṁ parivārayutaṁ vibho |
mocitāssakalā devā vipadbhyaḥ parameśvara ||
en: By your slaying, O mighty one, the demon Tāraka together with his retinue, all the gods have been freed from calamity, O supreme lord.
tel: నీవు తారకాసురుని అతని పరివారంతో సహా సంహరించడం వల్ల, విభూ, దేవతలందరూ ఆపదల నుండి విముక్తులయ్యారు, పరమేశ్వరా.
hi: हे विभो, आपके द्वारा परिवार सहित तारकासुर के वध हो जाने पर, समस्त देवता विपत्तियों से मुक्त हो गए, हे परमेश्वर।

--- verse none | section: Colophon ---
deva:
इति शिवपुराणे रुद्रसंहितायां कुमारखण्डे षष्ठाध्यायान्तर्गतं देवैः कृतं कार्तिकेयस्तोत्रं समाप्तम् ॥
iast:
iti śivapurāṇe rudrasaṁhitāyāṁ kumārakhaṇḍe ṣaṣṭhādhyāyāntargataṁ devaiḥ kṛtaṁ kārtikeyastotraṁ samāptam ||
en: Thus concludes the Kārtikeya-stotra composed by the gods, occurring within the sixth chapter of the Kumāra-khaṇḍa of the Rudra-saṁhitā in the Śiva Purāṇa.
tel: ఈవిధంగా శివపురాణంలోని రుద్రసంహిత, కుమారఖండంలో ఆరవ అధ్యాయంలో ఉన్న దేవతలు రచించిన కార్తికేయ స్తోత్రం సమాప్తమైనది.
hi: इस प्रकार शिवपुराण की रुद्रसंहिता के कुमारखण्ड में छठे अध्याय के अन्तर्गत आने वाला, देवताओं द्वारा रचित कार्तिकेय स्तोत्र समाप्त होता है।

Because only the sanskritdocuments.org file could be retrieved in full, cross-checking rests on that file's own scan pedigree plus the independent canonical-number confirmation rather than a second independently fetched transcription — flagged as reduced cross-checking, per file 08's practice. Two source irregularities are reproduced rather than resolved: (1) the verses are numbered 2-6, not 1-5, carrying over the surrounding adhyāya's continuous verse count rather than restarting for the hymn itself, so verse 1 of that count is not part of this excerpt; (2) the file's metadata comment locates the text at "adhyAya 12" while the colophon transmitted within the text itself calls it "ṣaṣṭhādhyāya" (the sixth chapter) — plausibly two different chapter-counting conventions (an absolute count versus one internal to the Kumāra-khaṇḍa) rather than an error, but left unresolved and reproduced as found. The double consonants in karttā/bharttā/harttā (verse 4) are reproduced exactly as given in the proofread source rather than normalized to the more common kartā/bhartā/hartā spelling.
