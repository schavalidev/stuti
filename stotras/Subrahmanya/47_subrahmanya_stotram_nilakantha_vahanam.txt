Title: Subrahmaṇya Stotram (Nīlakaṇṭha Vāhanam)
Devanāgarī: सुब्रह्मण्यस्तोत्रम् (नीलकण्ठवाहनम्) — सुब्रह्मण्यशैलवासिन् स्तोत्रम्
Telugu: సుబ్రహ్మణ్య స్తోత్రం (నీలకంఠవాహనం) — సుబ్రహ్మణ్యశైలవాసిన్ స్తోత్రం

Author: sanskritdocuments.org's catalogue metadata assigns the file to Ādi Śaṅkarācārya, but nothing in the text or its colophon ("iti śrī subrahmaṇya stotraṁ sampūrṇam") names an author, and the attribution should be treated as the catalogue's own, not the manuscript's. The doubt is sharpened by the composite character of the piece (see the Blurb and the Recension note).

Source / recension: sanskritdocuments.org, doc_subrahmanya/subra_stotra (ITRANS source .itx and the site's generated Devanāgarī page; transliterated and proofread by Raman Anantaraman, with an English translation by A. Narayanaswami printed alongside each verse; last updated 21 December 2019). The site distinguishes this text from the several other compositions bearing the bare title "Subrahmaṇya Stotram" by its own incipit-tags: subrahmaṇyaśailavāsin stotram and (nīlakaṇṭhavāhanam), both preserved in this file's title. Both renderings were compared verse by verse and agree; they are two renderings of one ITRANS file, so that comparison is a transcription check only. A genuinely independent second witness was, however, found on vignanam.org, which carries the same four verses in the same order under the same disambiguating title (https://vignanam.org/devanagari/subrahmanya-stotram-neelakantha-vahanam.html and its /english/ IAST page), confirming that this four-verse sequence circulates as a unit and is not an artefact of one encoder's assembly. The two independent witnesses differ in three places, all recorded in the notes to their verses: at 2c sanskritdocuments has jhallarī-nināda- (झल्लरी, a cymbal) where vignanam has the corrupt jallali-; at 4b and 4d vignanam reads manohāridehaṁ mahaccittageham and mahādevabālaṁ, matching the Subrahmaṇya Bhujaṅgam exactly, where sanskritdocuments splits the words more loosely; and vignanam drops h in several conjuncts (dviṣaḍbujaṁ, prabābhirāma) as a habit of its orthography rather than as variants. One plain encoding corruption in the sanskritdocuments text has been corrected here: verse 2c is printed śaḍakhavādana- in its ITRANS and शड़खवादन in its Devanāgarī, with ड़ standing where ङ् belongs; it is written here as śaṅkhavādana- / शङ्खवादन, which the sense and meter both require and which vignanam's शङ्ख independently confirms. Vocalic r was normalized to the precomposed ṛ.

Verse count: 4. There is no phalaśruti; the piece ends on its fourth verse and a one-line colophon.

Sections:
కుమారశైల శ్లోకాలు — The two Kumāraśaila verses sharing the refrain (vv. 1–2)
ధ్యాన శ్లోకం — Dhyāna verse (v. 3)
భుజంగప్రయాత శ్లోకం — Bhujaṅgaprayāta verse (v. 4)

Blurb: The shortest text in this folder, and openly a compilation rather than a single composition — sanskritdocuments' own editor says so in a note beneath it. The first two verses are the real find: a matched pair in a fifteen-syllable meter of strictly alternating heavy and light syllables, both closing on the same refrain kumāraśailavāsinaṁ bhaje, "I worship him who dwells on Kumāraśaila," and both plainly local to that hill — the god twelve-armed and crowned, his six faces lit by the flash of swinging jewelled earrings, holding spear, śakti, staff, cock and rosary; then radiant beside Vallī and Devayānī, garlanded in jasmine, fond of the sound of cymbals and the blown conch, and red as a new leaf. The third verse is a dhyāna śloka that turns up in many Subrahmaṇya collections, and the fourth is verse 3 of the Subrahmaṇya Bhujaṅgam (file 01 of this folder) with slightly looser word-division. What the compilation is worth keeping for is therefore the opening pair, which are not attested elsewhere in this corpus.

--- verse 1 | section: Kumāraśaila Ślokas ---
deva:
नीलकण्ठवाहनं द्विषड्भुजं किरीटिनं
लोलरत्नकुण्डलप्रभाभिरामषण्मुखम् ।
शूलशक्तिदण्डकुक्कुटाक्षमालिकाधरं
बालमीश्वरं कुमारशैलवासिनं भजे ॥
iast:
nīlakaṇṭhavāhanaṁ dviṣaḍbhujaṁ kirīṭinaṁ
lolaratnakuṇḍalaprabhābhirāmaṣaṇmukham |
śūlaśaktidaṇḍakukkuṭākṣamālikādharaṁ
bālamīśvaraṁ kumāraśailavāsinaṁ bhaje ||
en: I worship the child-lord who dwells on Kumāraśaila — riding the blue-necked one (the peacock), twelve-armed, crowned; his six faces lovely with the light thrown by swinging jewelled earrings; holding trident, śakti-spear, staff, cock and rosary. The word nīlakaṇṭha, "blue-throated," names the peacock here rather than Śiva, and it is by this word that the catalogue distinguishes this stotra from the others of the same bare title.
tel: నీలకంఠుని (నెమలిని) వాహనముగా కలవాడు, పండ్రెండు భుజములవాడు, కిరీటము ధరించినవాడు; కదలుచున్న రత్నకుండలముల కాంతిచే మనోహరమైన ఆరుముఖములు కలవాడు; శూలము, శక్తి, దండము, కుక్కుటము, అక్షమాలికలను ధరించినవాడు — కుమారశైలవాసియైన ఆ బాలేశ్వరుని నేను సేవించుచున్నాను. ఇచ్చట "నీలకంఠ" శబ్దము శివుని కాక నెమలిని సూచించును; ఈ పదముచేతనే ఈ స్తోత్రము ఇదే పేరుగల ఇతర స్తోత్రముల నుండి వేరుపరచబడును.
hi: नीलकंठ (मयूर) को वाहन बनाने वाले, बारह भुजाओं वाले, किरीटधारी; हिलते रत्न-कुंडलों की प्रभा से मनोहर छह मुखों वाले; शूल, शक्ति, दंड, कुक्कुट और अक्षमाला धारण किए हुए — कुमारशैल पर निवास करने वाले उस बालेश्वर की मैं उपासना करता हूँ। यहाँ "नीलकंठ" शब्द शिव नहीं, मयूर का वाचक है; इसी शब्द से यह स्तोत्र इसी नाम के अन्य स्तोत्रों से अलग पहचाना जाता है।

--- verse 2 | section: Kumāraśaila Ślokas ---
deva:
वल्लीदेवयानिकासमुल्लसन्तमीश्वरं
मल्लिकादिदिव्यपुष्पमालिकाविराजितम् ।
झल्लरीनिनादशङ्खवादनप्रियं सदा
पल्लवारुणं कुमारशैलवासिनं भजे ॥
iast:
vallīdevayānikāsamullasantamīśvaraṁ
mallikādidivyapuṣpamālikāvirājitam |
jhallarīninādaśaṅkhavādanapriyaṁ sadā
pallavāruṇaṁ kumāraśailavāsinaṁ bhaje ||
en: I worship the lord who dwells on Kumāraśaila — shining out in the company of Vallī and Devayānī (Devasenā), resplendent with garlands of divine flowers, jasmine and the rest; ever fond of the ringing of cymbals and the blowing of the conch; red as a young leaf. The rhyme on -llī / -llikā / -llarī / -llavā that opens each of the four quarters is deliberate. The source prints śaḍakha- for śaṅkha- here through an encoding fault (ड़ for ङ्), corrected in this file.
tel: వల్లీ దేవయానులతో కలిసి ప్రకాశించు ఈశ్వరుడు; మల్లిక మొదలైన దివ్యపుష్పమాలికలచే విరాజిల్లువాడు; ఝల్లరీనాదమును, శంఖవాదనమును ఎల్లప్పుడూ ప్రియముగా కలవాడు; చిగురువలె ఎర్రనైనవాడు — కుమారశైలవాసియైన ఆ ఈశ్వరుని నేను సేవించుచున్నాను. నాలుగు పాదముల ఆరంభమునందు వల్లీ, మల్లికా, ఝల్లరీ, పల్లవ అను ప్రాస ఉద్దేశపూర్వకమైనది. మూలములో "శంఖ" స్థానే లిపిదోషముచే "శడఖ" ముద్రితమైనది; ఇచ్చట సరిదిద్దబడినది.
hi: वल्ली और देवयानी के साथ उल्लसित ईश्वर; मल्लिका आदि दिव्य पुष्पमालाओं से सुशोभित; झल्लरी के नाद और शंख-वादन के सदा प्रिय; नवपल्लव-सा अरुण — कुमारशैल पर निवास करने वाले उनकी मैं उपासना करता हूँ। चारों चरणों के आरंभ में वल्ली, मल्लिका, झल्लरी, पल्लव का अनुप्रास जानबूझकर है। मूल में लिपि-दोष से "शंख" के स्थान पर "शडख" छपा है, जिसे यहाँ सुधार दिया गया है।

--- verse 3 | section: Dhyāna Śloka ---
deva:
षडाननं कुङ्कुमरक्तवर्णं
महामतिं दिव्यमयूरवाहनम् ।
रुद्रस्य सूनुं सुरसैन्यनाथं
गुहं सदा शरणमहं भजे ॥
iast:
ṣaḍānanaṁ kuṅkumaraktavarṇaṁ
mahāmatiṁ divyamayūravāhanam |
rudrasya sūnuṁ surasainyanāthaṁ
guhaṁ sadā śaraṇamahaṁ bhaje ||
en: Six-faced, red as vermilion, of great understanding, riding the divine peacock, son of Rudra, commander of the army of the gods — Guha, my refuge, I worship always. The meter shifts here to the eleven-syllable upajāti. This is a floating dhyāna verse that appears in a number of Subrahmaṇya collections rather than a composition proper to this text; sanskritdocuments' own editorial note says as much.
tel: ఆరుముఖములవాడు, కుంకుమవలె ఎర్రని వర్ణము కలవాడు, మహామతి, దివ్యమయూరవాహనుడు, రుద్రుని కుమారుడు, సురసైన్యనాథుడు — శరణ్యుడైన ఆ గుహుని నేను ఎల్లప్పుడూ సేవించుచున్నాను. ఇచ్చట ఛందస్సు పదునొకండు అక్షరముల ఉపజాతికి మారును. ఇది అనేక సుబ్రహ్మణ్య సంపుటములలో కనబడు ధ్యానశ్లోకము; ఈ గ్రంథమునకే ప్రత్యేకమైనది కాదని మూలసంపాదకుడే పేర్కొనెను.
hi: छह मुखों वाले, कुंकुम-सी लाल कांति वाले, महामति, दिव्य मयूर पर आरूढ़, रुद्र के पुत्र, सुर-सेना के नाथ — शरण्य गुह की मैं सदा उपासना करता हूँ। यहाँ छंद ग्यारह अक्षरों वाली उपजाति में बदल जाता है। यह अनेक सुब्रह्मण्य संग्रहों में मिलने वाला ध्यान-श्लोक है, इस पाठ की अपनी रचना नहीं — यह मूल संपादक ने स्वयं लिखा है।

--- verse 4 | section: Bhujaṅgaprayāta Śloka ---
deva:
मयूराधिरूढं महावाक्यगूढं
मनोहारी देहं महाचित्तगेहम् ।
महीदेवदेवं महावेदभावं
महादेव बालं भजे लोकपालम् ॥
iast:
mayūrādhirūḍhaṁ mahāvākyagūḍhaṁ
manohārī dehaṁ mahācittageham |
mahīdevadevaṁ mahāvedabhāvaṁ
mahādeva bālaṁ bhaje lokapālam ||
en: I worship the protector of the worlds, the child of Mahādeva — mounted on the peacock, hidden within the great Upaniṣadic sayings, of captivating form, dwelling in the hearts of the great, god of the gods of the earth (the brāhmaṇas), whose very being is the great Veda. This is verse 3 of the Subrahmaṇya Bhujaṅgam, already in this folder as file 01, in bhujaṅgaprayāta; the readings differ only in word-division and sandhi (there manohāridehaṁ mahaccittageham and mahādevabālaṁ, here manohārī dehaṁ mahācittageham and mahādeva bālaṁ).
tel: నెమలిపై అధిష్ఠించినవాడు, మహావాక్యములలో గూఢముగా దాగినవాడు, మనోహరమైన దేహము కలవాడు, గొప్పవారి చిత్తమునందు నివసించువాడు, భూదేవతలకు (బ్రాహ్మణులకు) దేవుడు, మహావేదస్వరూపుడు — మహాదేవుని బాలుడైన ఆ లోకపాలకుని నేను సేవించుచున్నాను. ఇది సుబ్రహ్మణ్య భుజంగము (ఈ సంపుటిలోని మొదటి ఫైలు) లోని మూడవ శ్లోకమే; పాఠభేదము పదవిభాగము, సంధి విషయములలో మాత్రమే.
hi: मयूर पर आरूढ़, महावाक्यों में गूढ़, मनोहर देह वाले, महापुरुषों के चित्त में निवास करने वाले, भूदेवों (ब्राह्मणों) के देव, महावेदस्वरूप — महादेव के उस बालक, लोकपालक की मैं उपासना करता हूँ। यह सुब्रह्मण्य भुजंगम् (इस संग्रह की पहली फाइल) का तीसरा श्लोक ही है; अंतर केवल पद-विभाजन और संधि का है।

Recension note: The compilation has two independent witnesses — the sanskritdocuments.org file transcribed by Raman Anantaraman, and vignanam.org's parallel Devanāgarī and IAST pages — which agree on all four verses and their order, so the four-verse sequence is a real transmitted unit rather than one encoder's assembly. sanskritdocuments prints an editorial note by the encoder that is worth recording in full substance: the first two stanzas appear to belong directly to Kumāraśaila; the third is a dhyāna śloka seen in other Subrahmaṇya stotras; and the fourth is from the Subrahmaṇya Bhujaṅgam. That assessment is confirmed here for verse 4, which is Bhujaṅgam verse 3 as printed in this folder's file 01, differing only in word-division (manohārī dehaṁ / manohāridehaṁ, mahācitta- / mahaccitta-, mahādeva bālaṁ / mahādevabālaṁ). Verses 1 and 2, by contrast, form a genuine matched pair — same fifteen-syllable alternating meter, same closing refrain, the same -ll- rhyme running down the openings of verse 2's four quarters — and they are the part of this text not otherwise represented in this corpus. The file is therefore kept for that pair, with the composite character stated plainly rather than presented as a unified four-verse composition. Kumāraśaila, the hill named in the refrain, is one of the recognised Skanda hills; the text gives no further geographical detail by which to identify it more precisely, and no attempt is made here to do so. The catalogue's attribution to Śaṅkarācārya is metadata only and sits awkwardly with the compiled character of the text.
