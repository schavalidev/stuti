Devanāgarī: कन्दर् षष्ठि कवचम्
Telugu: కంద షష్టి కవచం (తమిళ్)

Author: Dēvarāya Svāmigaḷ (18th–19th c. Tamil Nadu; he names himself in the phalaśruti, v.201, as "bālan Dēvarāyan," "the child Dēvarāyaṉ")

Source / recension: This is a Tamil text, not Sanskrit. (2) Tamil ற is rendered ṟ (Devanāgarī ऱ), kept distinct from ர (r). (3) Unlike the Sanskrit items in this collection, the iast field marks Tamil's short/long e and o distinctly — e/o for short எ/ஒ, ē/ō for long ஏ/ஓ — because that contrast is phonemically load-bearing in Tamil and the source's Telugu vowel signs (ె/ేand ొ/ో) preserve it; this departs from the "e and o only" convention used for the Sanskrit texts in this collection and is noted here rather than silently applied. Sections:
కాప్పు — Kāppu, the four-line protective invocation that opens the work and names it
కుఱళ్ వెంబా — Kuṟaḷ Veṇbā, a two-line couplet in the kuṟaḷ veṇbā metre, fixing the heart on Kumāra's feet
నూల్ — Nūl, the kavacam text proper: invocation of Śaravaṇabhava and his forms (ll.1–45), a long dhyāna of his ornamented six-faced body interleaved with jāti/drum syllables imitating his dance (ll.46–60), a body-part-by-body-part litany asking the vēl (spear) to guard every limb from crown to foot (ll.61–105), an exorcism section naming and repelling classes of black magic, ghosts and disease by name (ll.106–160), a nāmāvali of Skanda's names and shrines (ll.161–180), the poet's own colophon and phalaśruti (ll.181–215), and a closing hymn of praise and the śaraṇam (refuge) refrain (ll.216–238)

Blurb: This is a kavaca, that is, an armour hymn. It goes down the reciter's body from the forehead to the feet. Each part is placed under the guard of Murugan's vēl, his spear, or of one of his named forms. Twice the hymn breaks into jāti syllables, which cannot be translated. These syllables imitate the sound of war drums and of his dance. The hymn then names, in order to drive them away, the particular objects of black magic, the classes of spirits and the diseases that devotees feared in nineteenth-century Tamil Nadu. It closes with the signature of the poet Dēvarāya Svāmigaḷ, and with a statement of what thirty-six daily recitations will achieve.

hi: अनुपम कौशल व दिव्य देह वाले को नमन! इडुंबन नामक राक्षस को उसी के अस्त्र से पराजित करने वाले इडुंबा, नमन! कडंबा (कडंब-पुष्प-मालाधारी), नमन; कंदा, नमन! विजय-चिह्न वेट्चि-पुष्प-माला धारण करने वाले स्वामी, नमन! पळनि पर्वत पर स्वर्ण-सभा के एकमात्र राजा, नमन।

deva:
मयिल्नडमिडुवॊय् मलरडि शरणं
शरणं शरणं शरवण भव ओं
शरणं शरणं षण्मुगा शरणम् ॥
iast:
mayilnaḍamiḍuvoy malaraḍi śaraṇaṁ
śaraṇaṁ śaraṇaṁ śaravaṇa bhava ōṁ
śaraṇaṁ śaraṇaṁ ṣaṇmugā śaraṇam ||
en: O you who dance upon the peacock, your flower-feet are my refuge; refuge, refuge, O Śaravaṇabhava, oṁ; refuge, refuge, O Ṣaṇmukha, refuge! This closing śaraṇam (refuge) refrain ends the kavacam. The sanskritdocuments.org ITRANS recension repeats the final "śaraṇaṁ śaraṇaṁ ṣaṇmukha śaraṇam" line a second time (four refuge-lines instead of vignanam's three); this looks like a copying duplication rather than an intended fourth line, since it would break the base recension's own printed count of 238, so it is noted here rather than adopted.
tel: నెమలిపై నాట్యమాడే ఓ దేవా, నీ పుష్పసమానమైన పాదాలే నాకు శరణం; శరణం శరణం శరవణభవా ఓం; శరణం శరణం షణ్ముఖా శరణం! ఈ ముగింపు శరణాగతి పల్లవితో కవచం ముగుస్తుంది. sanskritdocuments.org లోని ITRANS పాఠంలో చివరి "శరణం శరణం షణ్ముఖా శరణం" పంక్తి రెండోసారి పునరావృతమవుతుంది (మూలపాఠం మూడు పంక్తులకు బదులు నాలుగు); ఇది మూలపాఠం (vignanam) యొక్క 238 పంక్తుల లెక్కను భంగపరుస్తుంది కాబట్టి, ఉద్దేశపూర్వక నాలుగో పంక్తి కాక ప్రతిలేఖన పునరావృతిగా భావించి ఇక్కడ గమనికగా మాత్రమే నమోదు చేయబడింది.
hi: मयूर पर नृत्य करने वाले हे देव, तुम्हारे पुष्प-सम चरण ही मेरी शरण हैं; शरणं शरणं शरवणभव ओं; शरणं शरणं षण्मुखा शरणम्! इस समापन शरणागति-पल्लव के साथ कवच पूर्ण होता है। sanskritdocuments.org के ITRANS पाठ में अंतिम "शरणं शरणं षण्मुख शरणम्" पंक्ति दूसरी बार दोहराई गई है (मूल तीन पंक्तियों के स्थान पर चार); यह मूल पाठ (vignanam) की 238 पंक्तियों की गणना को भंग करती प्रतीत होती है, अतः इसे जानबूझकर जोड़ी गई चौथी पंक्ति के बजाय प्रतिलेखन-पुनरावृत्ति मानकर, यहाँ केवल टिप्पणी के रूप में दर्ज किया गया है।
