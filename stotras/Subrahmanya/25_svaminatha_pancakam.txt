Title: Śrī Svāminātha Pañcakam
Devanāgarī: श्री स्वामिनाथ पञ्चकम्
Telugu: శ్రీ స్వామినాథ పంచకం

Author: the colophon and phala-śruti (v.6) both name Anantarāma Dīkṣita as composer — v.6 itself says "stotraṁ kṛtaṁ citracitraṁ dīkṣitānantarāmeṇa sarvārthasiddhyai" ("this wondrous hymn was composed by Dīkṣita Anantarāma for the accomplishment of every aim"), and the closing line reads "iti śrī anantarāma dīkṣitar kṛtaṁ śrī svāminātha pañcakam" ("thus ends the Śrī Svāminātha Pañcakam composed by Śrī Anantarāma Dīkṣitar"). No further biographical detail is given in the text itself.

Source / recension: Base text is vignanam.org's parallel Devanāgarī, Telugu, and English (IAST) editions of "Sri Swaminatha Panchakam," which agree with each other word for word. This was independently cross-checked against two further sources that also agree word for word: stotranidhi.com's English (IAST) page, and sanskritdocuments.org's doc_subrahmanya catalog, which carries the identical text under the title "svAminAthaShaTkam" (Svāminātha Ṣaṭkam, "Six Verses on Svāminātha," itx file svAminAthaShaTkam.itx, also attributed to Anantarāma Dīkṣita). This three-way agreement is a full cross-check, not a reduced one. The sanskritdocuments.org edition does differ from vignanam/stotranidhi in one respect — verse *numbering*, not content: vignanam/stotranidhi leave the opening two-line invocation ("he svāmināthārtabandho...") unnumbered and then number the five core verses 1–5 and the closing phala-śruti verse 6, whereas sanskritdocuments.org numbers the opening couplet itself as "1," shifting the rest to 2–6 and leaving the phala-śruti unnumbered — six numbered units either way, hence the alternate title "Ṣaṭkam" for what is otherwise the same "Pañcakam." This file follows vignanam.org/stotranidhi.com's own numbering (matching the "Pañcakam" title used in the assignment), and the variant numbering is noted here rather than silently reconciled.

Verse count: 6 numbered items in the recension followed here (an unnumbered 2-line opening invocation, verses 1–5, and a numbered closing phala-śruti verse 6) — see the recension note above on the alternate "Ṣaṭkam" numbering found at sanskritdocuments.org.

Sections:
మంగళాచరణం — Maṅgalācaraṇam (the opening, unnumbered two-line invocation addressing Svāminātha directly as the ash-smeared, compassionate son of the Gaṅgā)
పంచకం — Pañcakam (the five core verses, praising Svāminātha's iconography and residence on the Svāmi hill, and — twice — the specific legend of the child Skanda instructing his own father Śiva/Purāri in the meaning of the praṇava Auṁkāra)
ఫలశ్రుతి — Phalaśruti (the closing numbered verse stating the fruit of recitation and naming the composer, Anantarāma Dīkṣita)

Blurb: A five-verse hymn to Svāminātha — Subrahmaṇya as he is worshipped at Swamimalai — that leans on one specific episode from his mythology rather than a general litany of names: twice (v.1, calling him "guru even to Purāri," and v.5, "declaring the truth of Auṁkāra, laughing in Śambhu's ear") it recalls the story of the child Skanda teaching his own father Śiva the meaning of the syllable Om, framed by an invocatory couplet and closed by a phala-śruti that names its author, Anantarāma Dīkṣita.

--- verse none | section: Maṅgalācaraṇam ---
deva:
हे स्वामिनाथार्तबन्धो ।
भस्मलिप्ताङ्ग गाङ्गेय कारुण्यसिन्धो ॥
iast:
he svāmināthārtabandho |
bhasmaliptāṅga gāṅgeya kāruṇyasindho ||
en: O Svāminātha, kinsman of the afflicted, your limbs smeared with sacred ash, Gāṅgeya (son of the Gaṅgā), ocean of compassion!
tel: ఓ స్వామినాథా, ఆర్తులకు బంధువా, భస్మలేపిత శరీరుడా, గాంగేయా (గంగాపుత్రుడా), కారుణ్యసముద్రుడా!
hi: हे स्वामिनाथ, आर्तों के बंधु, भस्म से लिप्त अंगों वाले, गाङ्गेय (गंगापुत्र), करुणा के सागर!

--- verse 1 | section: Pañcakam ---
deva:
रुद्राक्षधारिन्नमस्ते
रौद्ररोगं हर त्वं पुरारेर्गुरोर्मे ।
राकेन्दुवक्त्रं भवन्तं
माररूपं कुमारं भजे कामपूरम् ॥
iast:
rudrākṣadhārinnamaste
raudrarogaṁ hara tvaṁ purārergurorme |
rākenduvaktraṁ bhavantaṁ
mārarūpaṁ kumāraṁ bhaje kāmapūram ||
en: Homage to you, wearer of rudrākṣa beads, guru even to Purāri (Śiva) himself — destroy my terrible affliction. I worship Kumāra, moon-faced, handsome as Māra (the god of love), the fulfiller of desire (kāmapūra).
tel: రుద్రాక్షధారీ, నీకు నమస్కారం; పురారి (శివుడు)కి కూడా గురువైన నీవు నా భయంకర రోగాన్ని పోగొట్టుము. చంద్రబింబం వంటి ముఖము గల, మారుని వలె సుందరుడైన, కోరికలను తీర్చే కుమారుని (కామపూరుని) నేను భజిస్తున్నాను.
hi: हे रुद्राक्षधारी, तुम्हें नमस्कार; पुरारि (शिव) के भी गुरु तुम मेरे भयंकर रोग को दूर करो। चंद्रमुख, मार (कामदेव) के समान सुंदर, कामनाओं को पूर्ण करने वाले (कामपूर) कुमार का मैं भजन करता हूँ।

--- verse 2 | section: Pañcakam ---
deva:
मां पाहि रोगादघोरात्
मङ्गलापाङ्गपातेन भङ्गात्स्वराणाम् ।
कालाच्च दुष्पाककूलात्
कालकालस्यसूनुं भजे क्रान्तसानुम् ॥
iast:
māṁ pāhi rogādaghorāt
maṅgalāpāṅgapātena bhaṅgātsvarāṇām |
kālācca duṣpākakūlāt
kālakālasyasūnuṁ bhaje krāntasānum ||
en: Protect me from terrible disease by the fall of your auspicious sidelong glance, from loss of voice, and from death and the brink of evil result (duṣpāka, misfortune). I worship the son of Kālakāla (Śiva, "death of Death"), who is throned upon the mountain ridge (krāntasānu).
tel: శుభకరమైన నీ కటాక్షవీక్షణం వల్ల నన్ను భయంకర రోగం నుండి, స్వరభంగం నుండి, మృత్యువు నుండి, దుష్పాకం (దుష్ఫలితం) అనే గట్టు నుండి రక్షించుము. కొండ శిఖరాన్ని అధిరోహించిన (క్రాంతసానుడైన) కాలకాలుని (శివుని) కుమారుని నేను భజిస్తున్నాను.
hi: अपने मंगलमय कटाक्ष से मुझे भयंकर रोग से, स्वर-भंग से, मृत्यु से और दुष्पाक (अशुभ फल) के तट से रक्षा करो। पर्वत-शिखर पर आरूढ़ (क्रांतसानु) कालकाल (शिव) के पुत्र का मैं भजन करता हूँ।

--- verse 3 | section: Pañcakam ---
deva:
ब्रह्मादयो यस्य शिष्याः
ब्रह्मपुत्रा गिरौ यस्य सोपानभूताः ।
सैन्यं सुराश्चापि सर्वे
सामवेदादिगेयं भजे कार्तिकेयम् ॥
iast:
brahmādayo yasya śiṣyāḥ
brahmaputrā girau yasya sopānabhūtāḥ |
sainyaṁ surāścāpi sarve
sāmavedādigeyaṁ bhaje kārtikeyam ||
en: Brahmā and the other gods are his disciples; the sons of Brahmā (the sages) have themselves become the steps of his hill; all the gods together are his army. I worship Kārtikeya, sung of by the Sāmaveda and the other Vedas.
tel: బ్రహ్మాదులు ఎవని శిష్యులో, ఎవని కొండకు బ్రహ్మపుత్రులు (ఋషులు) మెట్లుగా మారారో, దేవతలందరూ ఎవని సైన్యమో, సామవేదాది వేదాలచే స్తుతింపబడే ఆ కార్తికేయుని నేను భజిస్తున్నాను.
hi: ब्रह्मादि जिसके शिष्य हैं, जिसके पर्वत की सीढ़ियाँ स्वयं ब्रह्मपुत्र (ऋषिगण) बन गए हैं, समस्त देवता जिसकी सेना हैं, सामवेद आदि वेदों द्वारा गाए जाने वाले उस कार्तिकेय का मैं भजन करता हूँ।

--- verse 4 | section: Pañcakam ---
deva:
काषाय संवीत गात्रं
कामरोगादि संहारि भिक्षान्न पात्रम् ।
कारुण्य सम्पूर्ण नेत्रं
शक्तिहस्तं पवित्रं भजे शम्भुपुत्रम् ॥
iast:
kāṣāya saṁvīta gātraṁ
kāmarogādi saṁhāri bhikṣānna pātram |
kāruṇya sampūrṇa netraṁ
śaktihastaṁ pavitraṁ bhaje śambhuputram ||
en: I worship the son of Śambhu (Śiva) — his body wrapped in the ochre robe (kāṣāya) of a renunciate, destroyer of desire, disease and the like, holding the mendicant's alms-bowl, his eyes full of compassion, the śakti-spear in hand, the pure one.
tel: కాషాయ వస్త్రధారియై, కామరోగాదులను నశింపజేసేవాడై, భిక్షాపాత్రను ధరించినవాడై, కారుణ్యంతో నిండిన కన్నులు గలవాడై, శక్త్యాయుధాన్ని చేత ధరించిన పవిత్రుడైన శంభుపుత్రుని నేను భజిస్తున్నాను.
hi: काषाय वस्त्र धारण किए, काम-रोग आदि का नाश करने वाले, भिक्षा-पात्र लिए, करुणा से परिपूर्ण नेत्रों वाले, हाथ में शक्ति-आयुध धारण किए पवित्र शंभुपुत्र का मैं भजन करता हूँ।

--- verse 5 | section: Pañcakam ---
deva:
श्रीस्वामि शैले वसन्तं
साधुसङ्घस्य रोगान् सदा संहरन्तम् ।
ओङ्कारतत्त्वं वदन्तं
शम्भुकर्णे हसन्तं भजेऽहं शिशुं तम् ॥
iast:
śrīsvāmi śaile vasantaṁ
sādhusaṅghasya rogān sadā saṁharantam |
oṅkāratattvaṁ vadantaṁ
śambhukarṇe hasantaṁ bhaje'haṁ śiśuṁ tam ||
en: I worship that child who dwells on the holy Svāmi hill, ever destroying the diseases of the community of holy ones, declaring the truth of Auṁkāra (the syllable Om), laughing as he does so in Śambhu's (Śiva's) ear.
tel: పవిత్రమైన స్వామిమలై కొండపై నివసిస్తూ, సాధుసంఘం రోగాలను ఎల్లప్పుడూ పోగొడుతూ, ఓంకార తత్త్వాన్ని బోధిస్తూ, శంభుని (శివుని) చెవిలో నవ్వుతూ చెప్పే ఆ శిశువును నేను భజిస్తున్నాను.
hi: पवित्र स्वामिमलई पर्वत पर निवास करते हुए, साधुसंघ के रोगों को सदा नष्ट करते हुए, ओंकार के तत्त्व को कहते हुए और शंभु (शिव) के कान में हँसते हुए बताने वाले उस शिशु का मैं भजन करता हूँ।

--- verse 6 | section: Phalaśruti ---
deva:
स्तोत्रं कृतं चित्रचित्रं
दीक्षितानन्तरामेण सर्वार्थसिद्ध्यै ।
भक्त्या पठेद्यः प्रभाते
देवदेवप्रसादात् लभेताष्टसिद्धिम् ॥
iast:
stotraṁ kṛtaṁ citracitraṁ
dīkṣitānantarāmeṇa sarvārthasiddhyai |
bhaktyā paṭhedyaḥ prabhāte
devadevaprasādāt labhetāṣṭasiddhim ||
en: This wondrous hymn (citracitra) was composed by Dīkṣita Anantarāma for the accomplishment of every aim; whoever recites it with devotion at dawn shall obtain the eight siddhis (supernatural powers) by the grace of the god of gods.
tel: ఈ అద్భుతమైన స్తోత్రాన్ని దీక్షితుడైన అనంతరాముడు సర్వార్థసిద్ధి కోసం రచించాడు; దీనిని ప్రభాతకాలంలో భక్తితో పఠించేవారు దేవదేవుని అనుగ్రహం వలన అష్టసిద్ధులను పొందుతారు.
hi: यह अद्भुत स्तोत्र दीक्षित अनंतराम द्वारा सर्वार्थसिद्धि के लिए रचा गया; जो प्रातःकाल भक्तिपूर्वक इसका पाठ करता है, वह देवदेव की कृपा से अष्ट सिद्धियाँ प्राप्त करता है।

Recension note: This item was not found in the earlier-scanned portion of sanskritdocuments.org's doc_subrahmanya catalog (which covered entries through "subrahmaṇyāṣṭakaṁ 2"), but it does appear further down that same catalog page — under the alternate title "svAminAthaShaTkam" (Svāminātha Ṣaṭkam) rather than "Pañcakam." Its text (svAminAthaShaTkam.itx, attributed to Anantarāma Dīkṣita, transliterated/proofread by Malathi Ravi Kuduvaa, last updated 23 Jan 2021) is word-for-word identical to vignanam.org's and stotranidhi.com's "Svāminātha Pañcakam," so all three sources fully agree and this is a full, not reduced, cross-check. The only divergence across the three is how the six numbered units are counted: vignanam.org and stotranidhi.com (followed here) leave the opening couplet unnumbered and number the five core verses 1–5 plus a closing verse 6, while sanskritdocuments.org numbers the opening couplet itself as "1" and shifts the remainder to 2–6, leaving the phala-śruti unnumbered — hence "Pañcakam" (five) versus "Ṣaṭkam" (six) as titles for what is, textually, one and the same six-unit composition. No other variant readings were found among the three sources; hariome.com's Telugu-language page for this title (consulted only as a title check, not fetched for full text since the three sources above already gave complete agreement) confirms the same title is current in Telugu-tradition usage.
