candidate titles on sanskritdocuments.org turn out to be **modern compositions carrying an
explicit copyright notice on the source page** (encountered this round mostly on the Devī/Lakṣmī
side: Jyōtirlakṣmī Stōtram, Sītā-Lakṣmī Pañcakam and Aṣṭādaśa Mahālakṣmī Stōtram by Pushpa

Treat this as a third standing skip-rule alongside the existing two (nāmāvalī/sahasranāma
name-list type → separate reference-list treatment; Śataka-length and comparably long kāvya works
→ deferred). Check the source page's footer for a copyright line before writing any title that
looks like a modern composition.
