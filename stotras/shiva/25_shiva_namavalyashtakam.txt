Blurb: Despite its name, this is not a nāmāvalī of the ritual kind. It carries no list of 'oṁ … namaḥ' formulas. It is a proper aṣṭaka in the vasantatilakā metre, in which each verse is built almost entirely of words of direct address. Śaṅkara strings the names of Śiva one after another with almost no connecting syntax: Candracūḍa, Sthāṇu, Giriśa, Dhūrjaṭi, Vṛṣākapi, Śitikaṇṭha, Maṇikarṇikeśa. Then, after three lines of pure naming, each verse breaks into a single plea that never changes: 'saṁsāraduḥkhagahanājjagadīśa rakṣa', meaning 'O lord of the universe, save me out of this dense thicket of worldly sorrow'. Only the first verse admits the reason for the plea, in two words: mām anātham, meaning 'me, who have no protector'. The ninth verse steps out of direct address into the dative, and closes with namaḥ śivāya.

saṁsāraduḥkhagahanājjagadīśa rakṣa ||
en: O you who wear the moon on your crest, ender of Madana, trident in hand; O Sthāṇu, Giriśa, lord of Girijā, Maheśa, Śambhu; O lord of all beings, destroyer of the fear of the frightened — I have no protector: O lord of the universe, save me out of this dense thicket of worldly sorrow.

--- verse 9 ---
deva:
गौरीविलासभवनाय महेश्वराय
पञ्चाननाय शरणागतकल्पकाय ।
शर्वाय सर्वजगतामधिपाय तस्मै
दारिद्र्यदुःखदहनाय नमः शिवाय ॥
इति श्रीमत्परमहंसपरिव्राजकाचार्यस्य श्रीगोविन्दभगवत्पूज्यपादशिष्यस्य श्रीमच्छङ्करभगवतः कृतौ श्रीशिवनामावल्यष्टकं सम्पूर्णम् ॥
iast:
gaurīvilāsabhavanāya maheśvarāya
pañcānanāya śaraṇāgatakalpakāya |
śarvāya sarvajagatāmadhipāya tasmai
dāridryaduḥkhadahanāya namaḥ śivāya ||
iti śrīmatparamahaṁsaparivrājakācāryasya śrīgovindabhagavatpūjyapādaśiṣyasya śrīmacchaṅkarabhagavataḥ kṛtau śrīśivanāmāvalyaṣṭakaṁ sampūrṇam ||
en: To him who is the pleasure-house of Gaurī, to Maheśvara; to the five-faced one, the wish-granting tree for all who come seeking refuge; to Śarva, sovereign of all the worlds; to him who burns away the misery of poverty — salutations, O Śiva. Thus is completed the Śrī Śivanāmāvalyaṣṭakam among the works of the blessed Śaṅkara, the paramahaṁsa parivrājakācārya, disciple of the revered Govinda Bhagavatpāda.
tel: గౌరీ విలాసమునకు భవనమైనవానికి, మహేశ్వరునికి; పంచాననునికి, శరణాగతులకు కల్పవృక్షమైనవానికి; శర్వునికి, సర్వజగత్తులకు అధిపతికి; దారిద్ర్యదుఃఖమును దహించువానికి — నమః శివాయ. ఇట్లు గోవింద భగవత్పూజ్యపాదుల శిష్యులైన శ్రీమచ్ఛంకర భగవత్పాదుల కృతులలో శ్రీ శివనామావల్యష్టకము సంపూర్ణము.
hi: गौरी के विलास-भवन को, महेश्वर को; पंचानन को, शरणागतों के लिए कल्पवृक्ष स्वरूप को; शर्व को, समस्त जगत के अधिपति को; दारिद्र्य-दुःख को दग्ध करने वाले को — नमः शिवाय। इस प्रकार गोविंद भगवत्पूज्यपाद के शिष्य श्रीमच्छंकर भगवान् की कृतियों में श्री शिवनामावल्यष्टकम् सम्पूर्ण होता है।

Recension note: The stotranidhi IAST and Devanāgarī editions were compared verse-by-verse and are identical throughout, including the full Śaṅkara colophon. The closing verse (v. 9) is not unique to this hymn: the same stanza, word for word, appears in the Dāridryadahana Śiva Stotram already in this collection (see 18_daridryadahana_shiva_stotram.txt), where it is one of the verses carrying the refrain "dāridryaduḥkhadahanāya namaḥ śivāya." Its presence here as the ninth and final verse — breaking out of the vocative case used throughout verses 1–8 into the dative of formal salutation — is how both source pages print it, and the shared stanza is a known feature of this group of Śaiva hymns rather than a copying error in either text.
