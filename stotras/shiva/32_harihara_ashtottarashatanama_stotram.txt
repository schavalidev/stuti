Title: Śrī Harihara Aṣṭottaraśatanāma Stotram
Devanāgarī: श्रीहरिहर-अष्टोत्तरशतनामस्तोत्रम्
Telugu: శ్రీ హరిహర అష్టోత్తరశతనామ స్తోత్రం

Author: The **Skanda Mahāpurāṇa, Kāśīkhaṇḍa (pūrvārdha)**. In the frame, **Yama (Dharmarāja)** gives these names to his servants as a list of the people they must not touch: anyone who keeps repeating the names of Hari and Hara is to be left alone. **Agastya** speaks the closing phalaśruti. The Gītā Press colophon: `यमप्रोक्तं`.

Source / recension: Primary text: **Gītā Press, Gorakhpur — Śatanāma Stotra Saṅgraha**, code 1850, pp. 100–104 (stotra pp. 100–102, nāmāvalī pp. 103–104; PDF pp. 101–105), transcribed from the page images of the printed edition. Entry 18 of the volume's forty. **Collated against sanskritdocuments.org `hariharAShTottarashatanAmastotram.itx`** (proofread by PSA Easwaran). Its stated source is the *Bṛhatstotraratnākara* (Nirṇayasāgara, ed. Nārāyaṇa Rām Ācārya, stotra no. 225), a printed edition from a different editorial lineage, so this is a genuinely independent witness. Stotra and nāmāvalī are paired in one file as the publisher prints them, and were checked against each other name by name.

Recension note: **Gītā Press followed; the sanskritdocuments / Nirṇayasāgara witness (SD) is recorded, not adopted.** **Frame.** SD opens `धर्मराज उवाच`; Gītā Press prints no speaker before v. 1. **SD has two verses that Gītā Press lacks:** a verse spoken by the gaṇas (SD 12, `गणावूचतुः | इत्थं द्विजेन्द्र निजभृत्यगणान्सदैव…`) between the stotra's v. 11 and Agastya's verse, and a narrative verse (SD 14, `इति शृण्वन् कथां रम्यां शिवशर्मा…`) after it. So SD counts 14 verses where Gītā Press counts 12. **Readings (Gītā Press → SD):** v. 8 `रजनीशकलावतंस` (one compound; the nāmāvalī agrees, no. 81) → `rajanIsha kalAvataMsa` (two vocatives); v. 11 `सन्नामकां` → `sannAyakAM` ("with a fine central gem") and `द्विजकण्ठगां` → `nijakaNThagatAM` ("placed at one's own throat"); v. 12 `जपेत् स्तनरसं स पिबेन्न मातुः` → `japetstanarasaM na pibetsa mAtuH` (the same sense, in a different word order); colophon `यमप्रोक्तं … काशीखण्डपूर्वार्धे` → `dharmarAjaproktaM … kAshIkhaNDe`. SD's `nArAyANA` in v. 3 is a slip in that file. **The nāmāvalī has 108 numbered names, but only 106 are distinct:** `मुरारये` (nos. 5 and 78) and `भूतेशाय` (nos. 23 and 73) each appear twice, because the stotra itself repeats `मुरारे` and `भूतेश`. **No. 108 is printed `त्रिपथगार्द्रजटाकलापायै`, a feminine dative**, where the masculine `…कलापाय` would be expected. It was confirmed from a 400-dpi render and is kept as printed. **Prefix.** Gītā Press prints `ॐ` before each name; this corpus prints `श्री` for every nāmāvalī without exception (user, 2026-09-08). **Cross-reference:** no other Harihara text exists in the corpus.

Blurb: Yama's instructions to his messengers, set as a hymn: here are the people you must not touch. Each verse strings together a dozen names, Viṣṇu's and Śiva's in alternation — Govinda, then Śambhu, then Dāmodara; lord of Lakṣmī, then the sky-clad one, then the lotus-navelled — and each ends on the same line, that whoever keeps repeating these is to be left alone. The hymn never separates the two gods. It calls the names a single garland, and says that whoever wears it will not see Yama and will never again drink at a mother's breast.

--- verse 1 | section: Dhyānam ---
deva:
माधवोमाधवावीशौ सर्वसिद्धिविधायिनौ ।
वन्दे परस्परात्मानौ परस्परनतिप्रियौ ॥
iast:
mādhavomādhavāvīśau sarvasiddhividhāyinau |
vande parasparātmānau parasparanatipriyau ||
en: I bow to the two lords, Mādhava and Umā's lord, who bring about every accomplishment, each the self of the other, each delighting in bowing to the other.
tel: సర్వసిద్ధులను ప్రసాదించువారు, పరస్పరాత్ములు, పరస్పరనమస్కారప్రియులు అయిన ఈశ్వరులగు మాధవునకు ఉమాధవునకు నమస్కరించుచున్నాను.
hi: एक-दूसरेको प्रणाम करनेके प्रेमी, परस्पर एकात्मरूप, सब प्रकारकी सिद्धियोंको देनेवाले, साक्षात् ईश्वरस्वरूप लक्ष्मीपति और उमापतिको मैं नमस्कार करता हूँ।

--- verse 2 | section: Stotram ---
deva:

hi: हे गोविन्द, माधव, मुकुन्द, हरि, मुरारि; हे शम्भु, शिव, ईश, शशिशेखर, शूलपाणि; हे दामोदर, अच्युत, जनार्दन, वासुदेव — इस प्रकार जो निरन्तर उच्चारण करते हैं, हे दूतो! वे त्याग देने योग्य हैं।

--- verse 12 | section: Phalaśrutiḥ ---
