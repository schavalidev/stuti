Title: Śrī Śiva Kavacam
Devanāgarī: श्री शिव कवचम्

Sections:
ध्यानम् — Dhyānam, invocation and meditation on Śiva (vv. 1–4)
रक्षाप्रार्थना — Rakṣā-prārthanā, general prayer for protection (vv. 5–8)
पञ्चब्रह्मरक्षा — Pañcabrahma-rakṣā, protection by the five directional faces of Śiva — Tatpuruṣa, Aghora, Sadyojāta, Vāmadeva, Īśāna (vv. 9–13)
अङ्गरक्षा — Aṅga-rakṣā, protection limb by limb, head to foot (vv. 14–18)
कालरक्षा — Kāla-rakṣā, protection through the watches of day and night (vv. 19–21)
अवस्थारक्षा — Avasthā-rakṣā, protection standing, within, and without (vv. 21–22)
मार्गरक्षा — Mārga-rakṣā, protection while traveling and in the wild (vv. 23–24)
शत्रुनाशनम् — Śatrunāśanam, verses invoking destruction of hostile armies and beasts (vv. 25–27)
महामन्त्रः — Mahāmantra, the long unnumbered invocation of Sadāśiva/Bhairava beginning oṁ namo bhagavate sadāśivāya (entered as v. 28)
फलश्रुतिः — Phalaśruti, the fruits of wearing this armor, spoken by Ṛṣabha (vv. 29–34, = source vv. 28–33)
कथा — Kathā, Sūta's narrative of Ṛṣabha arming the young prince Bhadrāyus with sword, conch and sacred ash (vv. 35–44, = source vv. 34–43)

Blurb: Unlike most stotras, this is a kavaca in the strictest sense — a verse-by-verse assignment of a form or name of Śiva to each direction, each watch of the day and night, each limb of the body, and each circumstance of travel or danger, so that the reciter is imagined as fully encased in divine armor. Its narrative frame is unusual too: it is not a hymn a devotee composes in the moment, but a teaching the sage Ṛṣabha hands down to a young prince, Bhadrāyus, together with a sword and conch consecrated by mantra and a gift of sacred ash — turning the kavaca into part of an initiation into both spiritual and worldly (martial, royal) protection. The long unnumbered invocation near its center, addressed to Sadāśiva under his fierce Bhairava aspect and packed with repeated imperative verbs (rakṣarakṣa, jvalajvala, nāśayanāśaya), functions almost as a separate mantra embedded within the kavaca.

--- verse 1 | section: Dhyānam ---
deva:
ऋषभ उवाच ।
नमस्कृत्य महादेवं विश्वव्यापिनमीश्वरम् ।
वक्ष्ये शिवमयं वर्म सर्वरक्षाकरं नृणाम् ॥
iast:
ṛṣabha uvāca |
namaskṛtya mahādevaṁ viśvavyāpinamīśvaram |
vakṣye śivamayaṁ varma sarvarakṣākaraṁ nṛṇām ||
en:
Ṛṣabha said: Having bowed to the great god (Mahādeva), the lord who pervades the universe, I shall now declare the armor made of Śiva, the giver of complete protection to men.
tel:
ఋషభుడు పలికెను: విశ్వమంతటా వ్యాపించిన ఈశ్వరుడైన మహాదేవునికి నమస్కరించి, మనుషులకు సర్వరక్షను కలిగించే శివమయమైన ఈ కవచాన్ని నేను చెప్పెదను.
hi:
ऋषभ ने कहा: विश्वव्यापी ईश्वर महादेव को नमस्कार करके, मनुष्यों को सम्पूर्ण रक्षा देने वाले इस शिवमय कवच को मैं कहता हूँ।

Blaze up, blaze up! Destroy, destroy the fear of great death and untimely death! Drive away, drive away the fear of disease! Pacify, pacify the fear of poisonous serpents! Slay, slay the fear of thieves! Drive far away, drive far away my enemies! Rend, rend with your trident! Cleave, cleave with your axe! Cut, cut with your sword! Pound, pound with your skull-staff! Crush, crush with your pestle! Strike down, strike down with your arrows! Terrify, terrify the demonic hosts! Drive away, drive away the evil spirits! Terrify, terrify the kūṣmāṇḍas, the vetālas, the mārī-hosts and the brahma-rākṣasas! Grant, grant me fearlessness! Reassure, reassure me, terrified as I am! Deliver, deliver me from the fear of hell! Revive, revive me! Nourish, nourish me, afflicted by hunger and thirst! Gladden, gladden me, tormented by sorrow! Cover, cover me with the armor of Śiva! O Tryambaka, O Sadāśiva, salutations to you, salutations to you, salutations to you!
