Title: Śrī Baṭuka Bhairava Aṣṭottaraśatanāma Stotram (Āpaduddhāraṇa)
Devanāgarī: श्रीबटुकभैरव-अष्टोत्तरशतनामस्तोत्रम्
Telugu: శ్రీ బటుకభైరవ అష్టోత్తరశతనామ స్తోత్రం

Author: Anonymous; Rudrayāmala Tantra. A dialogue: Pārvatī questions Śaṅkara on Meru, and Īśvara answers; the names themselves are spoken by Īśvara to the goddess.

Source / recension: Primary text: **Gītā Press, Gorakhpur — Śatanāma Stotra Saṅgraha**, code 1850, pp. 24–35 (prefatory portion and ritual apparatus pp. 24–30, stotra pp. 30–33, nāmāvalī pp. 34–35), transcribed from the page images of the printed edition rather than from OCR. Entry 4 of the volume's forty, and by a wide margin the longest so far — twelve pages against five for each of the first three. The volume prints the second title `[आपदुद्धारणबटुकभैरवस्तोत्रम्]` in brackets beneath the first; the colophon of the stotra resolves the relation, giving the work as `इति श्रीरुद्रयामलतन्त्रे आपदुद्धारणं नाम श्रीबटुकभैरवाष्टोत्तरशतनामस्तोत्रं सम्पूर्णम्` — *Āpaduddhāraṇa* is the name of this aṣṭottara, not a separate hymn. One colophon covers the whole, so the whole is one file. Cross-checked internally: the 108 names of the nāmāvalī correspond in order to the name-sequence of stotra vv. 1–14a, and that internal check is the collation this file rests on. **No second edition was collated**, and no web witness has been sought; the readings rest on Gītā Press alone.

Recension note: **Prefix.** Gītā Press prints `ॐ` before each name; this corpus prints `श्री` for every nāmāvalī without exception (user, 2026-09-08). The divergence is recorded here rather than in the rows. **Case.** Every name is in the caturthī (dative), the form `नमः` governs; thirty-five of the 108 re-inflect irregularly, the largest group being the in-stems. Only the printed dative is recorded. **Two independent verse numberings.** The prefatory portion runs 1–52 and the stotra restarts at 1 and runs to 30. Both printed numbers are preserved inside each block's `deva:`; this file's own block numbers run 1–88 straight through, as the corpus format requires. Read the printed number, not the block number, when citing the text. **Structure.** The prefatory portion is not an ornament but the working apparatus of the rite: vv. 26–41 are a full deha-nyāsa and ṣaḍaṅga-nyāsa in verse, naming the epithet to be placed at each point of the body; vv. 42–52 give **four** dhyānas, labelled सात्त्विक, राजस, तामस and स्वरूप, so the reciter chooses the form to visualise. The prose viniyoga and the two nyāsa formulas printed on p. 30 are given their own blocks; they are prose, not verse, and are the only prose in the file. **Verse 47 is a single hemistich**, numbered `॥ ४७ ॥` on its own, as v. 1 of `Subrahmanya/49` is — the volume counts a half-verse as a verse where the sense closes. **Two names repeat.** `सिद्ध` at nos. 16 and 68, `कलानिधि` at nos. 45 and 102, so the list holds 106 distinct names in 108 slots, as printed. **`शौरिणे` (63)** is inflected as an in-stem in the nāmāvalī but appears as the i-stem `शौरिः` in stotra v. 8 — the same verse/list divergence recorded at `shiva/30` nos. 11 and 24. Both retained as printed. **The mantra.** The extraction of the mantra is given in cipher at vv. 9–10 and the volume supplies the assembled form in a footnote on p. 25; that footnote is not a verse and has not been given a block, but the reading is `ॐ ह्रीं बटुकाय आपदुद्धारणाय कुरु कुरु बटुकाय ह्रीं ॐ`.

Blurb: Baṭuka is Bhairava as a boy — Śiva's terrible aspect in the body of a child, crystal-bright in one meditation and cremation-dark in the next. The hymn is built for emergencies: it names him deliverer from calamity, and its closing verses promise release to the sick, the frightened and the man in irons. Unusually for this corpus, the whole ritual apparatus is printed with it — the placing of each epithet on a part of the body, and four alternative visualisations to choose between.

śaṅkaraṁ paripapraccha pārvatī parameśvaram || 1 ||
en: Pārvatī questioned Śaṅkara, the supreme lord, god of gods and teacher of the world, as he sat at ease on the summit of Meru.
tel: మేరుపృష్ఠమున సుఖాసీనుడై ఉన్న దేవదేవుని, జగద్గురువును, పరమేశ్వరుడైన శంకరుని పార్వతి ప్రశ్నించెను.
hi: मेरुके शिखरपर सुखपूर्वक विराजमान देवदेव जगद्गुरु परमेश्वर शंकरसे पार्वतीने पूछा।

--- verse 31 | section: Nyāsaḥ ---

hi: घुटनोंपर घुर्घुरारावको और पिंडलियोंपर रक्तपाणिकको; टखनोंपर पादुकासिद्धको और पैरोंपर सुरेश्वरको;

tel: సర్వజ్ఞుడైన పరమేశ్వరుడగు భైరవుడు ప్రహృష్టుడయ్యెను; సర్వేశ్వరేశ్వరి పరమ భక్తితో సదా జపించెను.
hi: और सर्वज्ञ परमेश्वर भैरव प्रहृष्ट हुए; तथा सर्वेश्वरेश्वरी परम भक्तिसे सदा जप करती रहीं।

deva:
॥ इति श्रीरुद्रयामलतन्त्रे आपदुद्धारणं नाम श्रीबटुकभैरवाष्टोत्तर-
शतनामस्तोत्रं सम्पूर्णम् ॥
iast:
|| iti śrīrudrayāmalatantre āpaduddhāraṇaṁ nāma śrībaṭukabhairavāṣṭottara-
śatanāmastotraṁ sampūrṇam ||
en: Thus ends the Śrī Baṭuka Bhairava Aṣṭottaraśatanāma Stotram called Āpaduddhāraṇa, in the Śrī Rudrayāmala Tantra.
tel: శ్రీ రుద్రయామల తంత్రమునందు ఆపదుద్ధారణమను శ్రీ బటుకభైరవ అష్టోత్తరశతనామ స్తోత్రము సంపూర్ణము.
hi: श्रीरुद्रयामलतंत्रमें आपदुद्धारण नामक श्रीबटुकभैरव-अष्टोत्तरशतनामस्तोत्र संपूर्ण हुआ।
