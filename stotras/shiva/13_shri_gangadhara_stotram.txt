Verse count: 8

Blurb: This is a hymn of eight verses by Appayya Dīkṣita, the polymath theologian of Advaita Śaivism. Each verse recounts a different episode of Śiva's saving grace. He swallowed the Hālāhala poison to save the gods. He granted Upamanyu a river of milk. He protected Mārkaṇḍeya from Death. He armed Arjuna with the Pāśupatāstra. He blessed the young Skanda even amid the mockery of his own kinsmen. He aided Indra against the Tripura demons. He redeemed a soul who called upon him even once. At the last he received the descending Gaṅgā into his matted hair. Each verse closes with the refrain 'ārtatrāṇaparāyaṇaḥ sa bhagavān gaṅgādharo me gatiḥ', meaning 'that Lord Gaṅgādhara, ever devoted to protecting the afflicted, is my refuge'.

--- verse 1 ---
deva:
क्षीराम्भोनिधिमन्थनोद्भवविषात् सन्दह्यमानान् सुरान्
ब्रह्मादीनवलोक्य यः करुणया हालाहलाख्यं विषम् ।
निःशङ्कं निजलीलया कवलयन्लोकानुरक्षादरा-
-दार्तत्राणपरायणः स भगवान् गङ्गाधरो मे गतिः ॥
iast:
kṣīrāmbhonidhimanthanodbhavaviṣāt sandahyamānān surān
brahmādīnavalokya yaḥ karuṇayā hālāhalākhyaṁ viṣam |
niḥśaṅkaṁ nijalīlayā kavalayanlokānurakṣādarā-
-dārtatrāṇaparāyaṇaḥ sa bhagavān gaṅgādharo me gatiḥ ||
en:
Seeing Brahmā and the other gods being burned up by the poison born of the churning of the milk-ocean, who, out of compassion, fearlessly swallowed that poison called Hālāhala as if in mere play, out of eager care to protect the worlds -- that Lord Gaṅgādhara, ever devoted to protecting the afflicted, is my refuge.

--- verse 2 ---
deva:
क्षीरं स्वादु निपीय मातुलगृहे गत्वा स्वकीयं गृहं
क्षीरालाभवशेन खिन्नमनसे घोरं तपः कुर्वते ।
कारुण्यादुपमन्यवे निरवधिं क्षीराम्बुधिं दत्तवान्
आर्तत्राणपरायणः स भगवान् गङ्गाधरो मे गतिः ॥
iast:

kāruṇyādupamanyave niravadhiṁ kṣīrāmbudhiṁ dattavān
ārtatrāṇaparāyaṇaḥ sa bhagavān gaṅgādharo me gatiḥ ||
en:
Who, out of compassion, gave a boundless ocean of milk to Upamanyu -- who, having once drunk sweet milk at his maternal uncle's house and returned to his own home, undertook fierce austerities out of grief at no longer having it -- that Lord Gaṅgādhara, ever devoted to protecting the afflicted, is my refuge.

--- verse 3 ---
deva:
मृत्युं वक्षसि ताडयन् निजपदध्यानैकभक्तं मुनिं
मार्कण्डेयमपालयत् करुणया लिङ्गाद्विनिर्गत्य यः ।
नेत्राम्भोजसमर्पणेन हरयेऽभीष्टं रथाङ्गं ददौ
आर्तत्राणपरायणः स भगवान् गङ्गाधरो मे गतिः ॥

netrāmbhojasamarpaṇena haraye'bhīṣṭaṁ rathāṅgaṁ dadau
ārtatrāṇaparāyaṇaḥ sa bhagavān gaṅgādharo me gatiḥ ||
en:
Who, emerging from the liṅga out of compassion, struck Death on the chest and thereby protected the sage Mārkaṇḍeya, who was devoted solely to meditation on his feet; who also, pleased by the offering of a lotus-eye, gave Hari (Viṣṇu) his desired discus -- that Lord Gaṅgādhara, ever devoted to protecting the afflicted, is my refuge.

--- verse 4 ---
deva:
व्यूढं द्रोणजयद्रथादिरथिकैः सैन्यं महत् कौरवं
दृष्ट्वा कृष्णसहायवन्तमपि तं भीतं प्रपन्नार्तिहा ।
पार्थं रक्षितवानमोघविषयं दिव्यास्त्रमुद्बोधयन्
आर्तत्राणपरायणः स भगवान् गङ्गाधरो मे गतिः ॥

dṛṣṭvā kṛṣṇasahāyavantamapi taṁ bhītaṁ prapannārtihā |
pārthaṁ rakṣitavānamoghaviṣayaṁ divyāstramudbodhayan
ārtatrāṇaparāyaṇaḥ sa bhagavān gaṅgādharo me gatiḥ ||
en:
Who, remover of the distress of those who take refuge in him, protected Pārtha (Arjuna) -- who, seeing the great Kaurava army arrayed by warriors like Droṇa and Jayadratha, felt afraid even with Kṛṣṇa's help at hand -- by awakening in him an unfailing divine weapon (the Pāśupatāstra) -- that Lord Gaṅgādhara, ever devoted to protecting the afflicted, is my refuge.

--- verse 5 ---
deva:
बालं शैवकुलोद्भवं परिहसत् स्वज्ञातिपक्षाकुलं

--- verse 7 ---
deva:
श्रौतस्मार्तपथे पराङ्मुखमपि प्रोद्यन्महापातकं
विश्वातीतमपि त्वमेव गतिरित्यालापयन्तं सकृत् ।

iast:
śrautasmārtapathe parāṅmukhamapi prodyanmahāpātakaṁ

ārtatrāṇaparāyaṇaḥ sa bhagavān gaṅgādharo me gatiḥ ||
en:
Who, renowned of old as an ocean of compassion, protects even one who has turned away from the paths of śruti and smṛti and is steeped in great sin, the moment he merely says once, "you alone, who are beyond the universe, are my refuge" -- that Lord Gaṅgādhara, ever devoted to protecting the afflicted, is my refuge.

dṛṣṭvā bhaktabhagīrathena vinato rudro jaṭāmaṇḍale |
kāruṇyādavanītale suranadīmāpūrayat pāvanīṁ
ārtatrāṇaparāyaṇaḥ sa bhagavān gaṅgādharo me gatiḥ ||
en:
Who was long ago entreated by the honored gods to bear the force of the descending Gaṅgā, and seeing the devoted Bhagīratha bowing before him, Rudra received her in the mass of his matted locks and, out of compassion, let the purifying river of the gods flood the surface of the earth -- that Lord Gaṅgādhara, ever devoted to protecting the afflicted, is my refuge.
tel:
పూర్వం గంగా వేగాన్ని భరించమని గౌరవనీయ దేవతలచే వేడుకోబడి, భక్తుడైన భగీరథుడు నమస్కరించడం చూసి రుద్రుడు తన జటామండలంలో ఆమెను స్వీకరించి, కరుణతో పావనమైన ఆ దేవనదిని భూమిపై ప్రవహింపజేసిన ఆర్తత్రాణపరాయణుడైన ఆ భగవాన్ గంగాధరుడే నాకు గతి.
hi:
पूर्वकाल में सम्माननीय देवताओं द्वारा गंगा के वेग को सहने की प्रार्थना किए जाने पर, भक्त भगीरथ को झुके हुए देखकर रुद्र ने अपनी जटाओं के मंडल में उसे धारण किया और करुणावश उस पावन देवनदी को पृथ्वीतल पर प्रवाहित किया — वह आर्तत्राणपरायण भगवान् गंगाधर ही मेरी गति हैं।
