Title: Śrī Ardhanārīśvara Aṣṭottaraśatanāmāvaliḥ
Devanāgarī: श्रीअर्धनारीश्वर-अष्टोत्तरशतनामावलिः
Telugu: శ్రీ అర్ధనారీశ్వర అష్టోత్తరశతనామావళిః

Author: No source, speaker or author is named in the print or in the witness.

Source / recension: Primary text: **Gītā Press, Gorakhpur — Śatanāma Stotra Saṅgraha**, code 1850, pp. 215–222 (PDF pp. 216–223), transcribed from the page images of the printed edition. It follows the volume's forty numbered entries, in the appendix. **Collated against sanskritdocuments.org `ardhanArIshvarAShTottarashatanAmAvaliH.itx`** (proofread by Aruna Narayanan), which is the **same text**: the same 109 couplets in the same order, the same opening (`महाकैलासशिखरनिलयाय`) and the same closing colophon. The witness is independent of Gītā Press. All 218 name-lines were compared mechanically, and every divergence was then checked against the page image at 400 dpi.

Recension note: **Gītā Press followed; the sanskritdocuments witness (SD) is recorded, not adopted.** Differences (Gītā Press → SD), each verified against the page image: couplet 2 `अपाकृतमहादेवीपुरःस्थाय` → `aprākṛta…`; couplet 12 `मुकुटायै` → `makuṭāyai`; couplet 47 `कलेवरान्त…` → `kalebarānta…`; couplet 72 `दौर्भाग्यवेदनाय` → `daurbhāgyabhedanāya`; couplet 75 `सुपक्वदाडिमी…` → `supakka…`. SD's couplet 34 reads `śrīkaṇṭhanetna…` for `श्रीकण्ठनेत्र…`, an obvious SD slip. SD also misnumbers two couplets (its 12 is printed "19" and its 76 "73"); Gītā Press numbers 1–109 correctly. **Name count 218, not 108.** The piece is a **stotrātmaka nāmāvalī** — the print says so under the title, `[स्तोत्रात्मकनामावलिः]` — in which every numbered unit is a couplet of two names: the first line a name of Śiva in the dative, the second a name of the Goddess, each closed with `नमो नमः`. There are 109 such couplets, so 218 names in all, under a title that promises 108. Both witnesses agree on the count and the numbering. The names are in the blocks themselves; the volume prints no separate index for this piece, and none is added here. **Where `नमो नमः` is not printed.** In four couplets the first line ends with `नमः` alone, not `नमो नमः`: nos. 49 `…दक्षिणामूर्तये`, 60 `…संहारिणे`, 95 `…सारथये` and 102 `…आकृतये`. Each of these is a consonant-stem dative, and the shorter close keeps the line's length. Both witnesses agree; it is kept as printed. **The running head** in the print reads `श्रीअर्धनारीश्वर-अष्टोत्तरशतनामस्तोत्रम्` (stotra), while the colophon reads `…नामावलिः सम्पूर्णा` (nāmāvalī). The colophon's word is used for the title here, as the piece is a nāmāvalī in form. **Prefix.** This piece carries no `ॐ` or `श्री` prefix before its names in the print — the names are vocative-style datives closed by `नमो नमः` — so the corpus's `श्री` prefix rule for nāmāvalīs does not arise. **Cross-reference:** sanskritdocuments' `ardhanAryaShTottarashatanAmastotram` and `ardhanAryaShTottarashatanAmAvalI` (Skanda Purāṇa, beginning `चामुण्डिकाम्बा श्रीकण्ठः`) are a **different** Ardhanārīśvara 108, pairing nominatives two to a line; they were checked and not used.

Verse count: 109

Name count: 218

Sections:
नामावलिः — Nāmāvaliḥ (blocks 1–109 = printed couplets 1–109; each couplet is one name of Śiva and one of the Goddess)
- Colophon (1 block, unnumbered — excluded from the verse count)

Blurb: Śiva and the Goddess named a couplet at a time, in the same breath and the same grammar: his name, then hers, each closed with "obeisance, obeisance". He wears an elephant hide, she a garment like lightning; his throat holds the sea's poison, hers the sheen of conches; he is the five-syllable mantra, she the fifteen letters of Śrīvidyā. It ends where it must, with the supreme Lord joined to the supreme Power and the great Goddess to the great God.

--- verse 108 | section: Nāmāvaliḥ ---

hi: वसुन्धराके महान् भाग्यके सूचकको नमस्कार; अर्धाद्रिके शिखरपर स्थित अर्धनारीश्वरीको नमस्कार।

hi: पराशक्तिसे समायुक्त परेशको नमस्कार; महादेवसे समायुक्त महादेवीको नमस्कार।
