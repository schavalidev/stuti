Title: Śrī Śiva Aṣṭottaraśata Divyasthānīya Nāma Stotram
Devanāgarī: श्रीशिव-अष्टोत्तरशतदिव्यस्थानीयनामस्तोत्रम्

Recension note: **Gītā Press followed; the sanskritdocuments nāmāvalī (SD) was collated name by name.** **Name count 100, not 108 — both witnesses.** The stotra's opening line promises "a hundred and eight holy places" (`अष्टोत्तरशतं … क्षेत्रं`). Gītā Press's stotra, Gītā Press's nāmāvalī and SD's nāmāvalī all give a hundred, in the same order. The shortfall is in the tradition, not a slip in one print. **One difference with SD**, found by a mechanical comparison of all hundred names: no. 30 `विरिञ्चिपुर्यां मार्गेशाय` → SD `virañcipuryāṁ mārgeśāya`. Gītā Press's v. 9 also reads `विरिञ्चिपुर्यां`, and it is kept. **Form of the names.** Each name is a place in the locative followed by the Śiva who dwells there in the dative, e.g. no. 1 `कैवल्यशैले श्रीकण्ठाय`. The internal space is kept as printed. **Stotra and nāmāvalī agree**, with these forms: v. 4 `श्रीमाञ्श्वेतहस्तिपुरेश्वरः`, `वृषध्वजाभिधः` → no. 12 `श्वेतहस्तिपुरे श्रीमते वृषध्वजाय`; v. 19 `प्रसन्नाख्यपुरे` → no. 60 `प्रसन्नपुरे`; v. 24 `स्तनिताख्ये` → no. 80 `स्तनितायां`; v. 15 `चामराज्याख्यनगरे` → no. 46 `चामराज्यनगरे`. **Repeated deities at different places:** Bhīmeśa (nos. 8, 17), Mallikārjuna (nos. 4, 33), Rāmaliṅgeśa (nos. 40, 54), Kālikeśvara (nos. 18, 85), Saṅgameśvara (nos. 79, 87). **The avatāra-īśas.** Nos. 90–100 run through the avatāras of Viṣṇu, each as a Śiva he established or worshipped: Matsyeśa, Kūrmeśa, Varāheśa, Nṛsiṁha, Vāmaneśa, Paraśurāmeśa, Rāmeśvara, Balarāmeśa, Bauddheśa, Kalkīśa, Kṛṣṇeśa. **Place names.** Many are identifiable (Kāśī, Śrīśaila, Drākṣārāma, Kumbhakoṇa, Lepākṣī, Madurai's Cokkanātha, Dharmasthala's Mañjunātha, Nañjanagūḍu's Nañjuṇḍeśvara, Tañjāvūr's Bṛhadīśvara). Others are not certain. They are transliterated as printed, not identified. **Prefix.** Gītā Press prints `ॐ` before each name; this corpus prints `श्री` for every nāmāvalī without exception (user, 2026-09-08). **Cross-reference:** `shiva/30_shiva_ashtottarashatanama_stotram.txt` is a different work (a list of epithets, not of places).

Blurb: A pilgrim's map of Śiva: a hundred places, each with the name he bears there. It runs from Kedāra in the Himālaya and Viśvanātha in Kāśī through Śrīśaila, Drākṣārāma and the temple towns of the Tamil and Kannada country to Nepāla and Kashmir. It ends with the Śivas that Viṣṇu's avatāras set up, from Matsyeśa in Laṅkā to Kalkīśa at Tattvakṣetra.

deva:
अष्टोत्तरशतं भूमौ स्थितं क्षेत्रं वदाम्यहम् ।
कैवल्यशैले श्रीकण्ठः केदारो हिमवत्यपि ॥ १ ॥
iast:
aṣṭottaraśataṁ bhūmau sthitaṁ kṣetraṁ vadāmyaham |
kaivalyaśaile śrīkaṇṭhaḥ kedāro himavatyapi || 1 ||
en: I shall name the hundred and eight holy places that stand on the earth. On Kaivalyaśaila he is Śrīkaṇṭha, and in the Himālaya Kedāra.
tel: భూమియందున్న నూట ఎనిమిది క్షేత్రములను చెప్పెదను. కైవల్యశైలమునందు శ్రీకంఠుడు, హిమవంతమునందు కేదారుడు.
hi: मैं पृथ्वीपर स्थित एक सौ आठ क्षेत्रोंको कहता हूँ। कैवल्यशैलपर श्रीकण्ठ और हिमालयमें केदार हैं।

--- verse 29 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
साकेते बलरामेशो बौद्धेशो वारणावते ।
तत्त्वक्षेत्रे च कल्कीशः कृष्णेशः स्यान्महेन्द्रके ॥ २९ ॥
iast:
sākete balarāmeśo bauddheśo vāraṇāvate |
tattvakṣetre ca kalkīśaḥ kṛṣṇeśaḥ syānmahendrake || 29 ||
en: At Sāketa Balarāmeśa, at Vāraṇāvata Bauddheśa; at Tattvakṣetra Kalkīśa, and at Mahendraka he is Kṛṣṇeśa.
tel: సాకేతమునందు బలరామేశుడు, వారణావతమునందు బౌద్ధేశుడు; తత్త్వక్షేత్రమునందు కల్కీశుడు, మహేంద్రకమునందు కృష్ణేశుడు.
hi: साकेतमें बलरामेश, वारणावतमें बौद्धेश; तत्त्वक्षेत्रमें कल्कीश और महेन्द्रकमें कृष्णेश हैं।

--- verse none | section: Colophon ---
deva:
॥ इति ललितागमे ज्ञानपादे शिवलिङ्गप्रादुर्भावपटलान्तर्गते श्रीशिवाष्टोत्तरशतदिव्यस्थानीयनामस्तोत्रं सम्पूर्णम् ॥
iast:
|| iti lalitāgame jñānapāde śivaliṅgaprādurbhāvapaṭalāntargate śrīśivāṣṭottaraśatadivyasthānīyanāmastotraṁ sampūrṇam ||
en: Thus, in the Lalitāgama, Jñānapāda, within the chapter on the manifestation of the Śiva-liṅga, the Śrī Śiva Aṣṭottaraśata Divyasthānīya Nāma Stotram is complete.
tel: ఇట్లు లలితాగమమునందు జ్ఞానపాదమునందు శివలింగప్రాదుర్భావపటలాంతర్గతమగు శ్రీశివాష్టోత్తరశతదివ్యస్థానీయనామస్తోత్రము సంపూర్ణము.
hi: इस प्रकार ललितागमके ज्ञानपादमें शिवलिङ्गप्रादुर्भावपटलके अन्तर्गत श्रीशिव-अष्टोत्तरशतदिव्यस्थानीयनामस्तोत्र सम्पूर्ण हुआ।
