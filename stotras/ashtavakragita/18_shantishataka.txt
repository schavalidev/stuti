Recension note: Four verses carry readings that are genuinely different sentences rather than slips, and in each Gītā Press is followed as the authority, with the other reading recorded here. Verse 23: both prints read आशा वापि न कुत्रचित्, "nor anywhere any hope", which pairs with जिहासा in the first half; the digital lineage reads नाशो वापि, "nor any destruction". Verse 30: Gītā Press reads कर्तृत्वमदवर्जितम्, "free of the pride of doership", where the Jālimsiṁh print and the digital lineage agree on अकर्तृ स्पन्दवर्जितम्, "not an agent, free of stirring"; this is the one place in the whole work where the Jālimsiṁh print sides with the digital lineage against Gītā Press on a substantive reading. Verse 61: Gītā Press reads निवृत्तिफलदायिनी, the engagement of the steady one "gives" the fruit of desisting, against the digital lineage's निवृत्तिफलभागिनी, "partakes of" it. Verse 75: Gītā Press reads कर्तुमाप्नोति तत्क्षणात्, "at that moment gets to doing", against the other witnesses' अतत्क्षणात्. Verse 29 is a fifth such place, where Gītā Press's न किञ्चिद्धि कृतं कृतम् and the digital lineage's न किञ्चिदकृतं कृतम् differ by an aspirate; the authority is followed, and the OCR could in principle be hiding either.

Three readings are settled by the metre rather than by counting witnesses. At verse 24 नावमानिता is written, which Gītā Press has and which the line needs; the other witnesses' नावमानता is a syllable short. At verse 62 the same test goes the other way: विरागता is written with the three other witnesses, Gītā Press's विरागिता being a syllable too long. At verse 92 तत्त्वविनिश्चयः is written from the digital lineage, Gītā Press's OCR having dropped the वि that the line needs, and at verse 52 अकृतिका for the same reason.

Two words that this work's OCR gets wrong in a regular way are corrected here against the other witnesses: तूष्णीम्भूतस्य at verse 9, where both prints' OCR shows तृष्णी-, which is not a word, and which verse 46 shows as तूष्णीं in the same print; and जागर्ति at verse 94, which the metre requires, and which settles the same word at 17.10. Besides these, a dozen or so readings peculiar to sa.wikisource are plain slips inside its own lineage and are not followed: सर्वपरित्याज (v. 2), परमर्थतः and स्वभावनां (v. 4), विकल्पना for विकल्पनाः (v. 9), नाविचार and न जाति (v. 27), नो समाधिं and न लोपं (v. 18), भ्रमभृतम् (v. 70), कर्मं for कर्तुं (v. 77), सुबुद्धिरपि (v. 95) and मुक्ता for मुक्तो (v. 96).

Blurb: A hundred verses, and the longest single stretch of the work. The colophon calls it the Śāntiśataka, the hundred on peace. It opens with the only salutation in the whole Gītā — homage to that whose nature is happiness alone, at whose dawning of knowledge delusion becomes like a dream — and then settles into its real subject, which is the difference between the man who works at liberation and the man who has stopped. A great deal of it is about the first of these, and it is not gentle: fools practise one-pointedness and restraint hard, while the steady see nothing that needs doing and rest in their own place as though asleep; the dull-witted does not reach release by action in the shape of practice. The chapter's most-quoted image is of a man with no dispositions left, no support and no bonds, who moves as a dry leaf moves when the wind of his old formations throws it about. Towards the end the questions pile up — where is light, where is dark, where is loss, where is anything at all — and the hundredth verse closes the whole teaching as quietly as it can be closed: he does not run to the crowd and does not run to the forest, but stands the same, however and wherever he is.

ब्रुवन्नपि न च ब्रूते कोऽन्यो निर्वासनादृते ॥
iast:
jānann-api na jānāti paśyann-api na paśyati |
bruvann-api na ca brūte ko'nyo nirvāsanād-ṛte ||
en: Knowing, he does not know; seeing, he does not see; speaking, he does not speak. Who else is like this but the man without dispositions?
tel: ఎరుగుచున్నను ఎరుగడు, చూచుచున్నను చూడడు, పలుకుచున్నను పలుకడు. వాసనలు లేనివాడు తప్ప ఇతరుడు ఎవడు ఇట్లుండును?
hi: जानते हुए भी नहीं जानता, देखते हुए भी नहीं देखता, और बोलते हुए भी नहीं बोलता — निर्वासन पुरुष के सिवा दूसरा कौन ऐसा है?

--- verse 91 | section: Neither asleep nor awake ---

भावेषु गलिता यस्य शोभनाशोभना मतिः ॥
iast:
bhikṣur-vā bhūpatir-vāpi yo niṣkāmaḥ sa śobhate |
bhāveṣu galitā yasya śobhanāśobhanā matiḥ ||
en: Beggar or king, whoever is without desire shines — he in whom the judgement of things as fair and foul has melted away.
tel: భిక్షువైనను, రాజైనను, కోరిక లేనివాడే ప్రకాశించును — ఎవని బుద్ధియందు వస్తువులను శోభనమని, అశోభనమని ఎంచుట కరిగిపోయినదో.
hi: भिक्षु हो या राजा, जो निष्काम है वही शोभा पाता है — जिसकी भावों में शोभन और अशोभन की मति गल गई है।

--- verse 92 | section: Neither asleep nor awake ---
deva:

निर्व्याजार्जव-भूतस्य चरितार्थस्य योगिनः ॥
iast:
kva svācchandyaṁ kva saṅkocaḥ kva vā tattva-viniścayaḥ |
nirvyājārjava-bhūtasya caritārthasya yoginaḥ ||
en: Where is wilfulness, where is drawing in, and where is the settling of the truth, for the yogī who has become guileless uprightness itself and whose purpose is accomplished?
tel: కపటము లేని ఆర్జవమే అయిన, కృతార్థుడైన యోగికి స్వాచ్ఛంద్యము ఎక్కడ? సంకోచము ఎక్కడ? తత్త్వవినిశ్చయము ఎక్కడ?
hi: निर्व्याज आर्जवरूप और चरितार्थ योगी को स्वाच्छन्द्य कहाँ, संकोच कहाँ और तत्त्व का विनिश्चय कहाँ?

--- verse 93 | section: Neither asleep nor awake ---
deva:
आत्म-विश्रान्ति-तृप्तेन निराशेन गतार्तिना ।
अन्तर्यदनुभूयेत तत्कथं कस्य कथ्यते ॥
iast:
ātma-viśrānti-tṛptena nirāśena gatārtinā |
antaryad-anubhūyeta tat-kathaṁ kasya kathyate ||
en: What is felt within by one who is content with resting in the Self, who expects nothing and whose distress is gone — how, and to whom, is it to be told?
tel: ఆత్మయందు విశ్రమించుటచే తృప్తుడైన, ఆశ లేని, ఆర్తి తొలగిన వానికి లోపల ఏది అనుభూతమగునో, అది ఎట్లు, ఎవరికి చెప్పబడును?
hi: आत्मविश्रान्ति से तृप्त, निराश और आर्तिरहित पुरुष के भीतर जो अनुभव होता है, वह कैसे और किससे कहा जाये?

--- verse 94 | section: Neither asleep nor awake ---
deva:
सुप्तोऽपि न सुषुप्तौ च स्वप्नेऽपि शयितो न च ।
जागरेऽपि न जागर्ति धीरस्तृप्तः पदे पदे ॥
iast:
supto'pi na suṣuptau ca svapne'pi śayito na ca |
jāgare'pi na jāgarti dhīras-tṛptaḥ pade pade ||
en: Asleep, he is not in deep sleep; dreaming, he is not lying asleep; awake, he is not awake. The steady man is content at every step.
tel: నిద్రించినను గాఢనిద్రలో ఉండడు, కలలో ఉన్నను పరుండినవాడు కాడు, మేలుకొన్నను మేలుకొన్నవాడు కాడు. ధీరుడు అడుగడుగున తృప్తుడు.
hi: सोया हुआ होकर भी सुषुप्ति में नहीं है, स्वप्न में होकर भी सोया हुआ नहीं है, और जागते हुए भी जागता नहीं है। धीर पुरुष पद-पद पर तृप्त है।

--- verse 95 | section: Neither asleep nor awake ---
deva:
ज्ञः सचिन्तोऽपि निश्चिन्तः सेन्द्रियोऽपि निरिन्द्रियः ।
सबुद्धिरपि निर्बुद्धिः साहङ्कारोऽनहङ्कृतिः ॥
iast:
jñaḥ sacinto'pi niścintaḥ sendriyo'pi nirindriyaḥ |
sabuddhir-api nirbuddhiḥ sāhaṅkāro'nahaṅkṛtiḥ ||
en: The knower, though with thought, is free of thought; though with senses, is without senses; though with understanding, is without understanding; though with a sense of "I", is without it.
tel: జ్ఞాని చింతతో ఉన్నను నిశ్చింతుడు; ఇంద్రియములతో ఉన్నను ఇంద్రియములు లేనివాడు; బుద్ధితో ఉన్నను బుద్ధి లేనివాడు; అహంకారముతో ఉన్నను అహంకారము లేనివాడు.
hi: ज्ञानी चिन्तायुक्त होकर भी निश्चिन्त है, इन्द्रियों वाला होकर भी निरिन्द्रिय है, बुद्धि वाला होकर भी निर्बुद्धि है, और अहंकार वाला होकर भी अनहंकृत है।

--- verse 96 | section: Neither asleep nor awake ---
deva:
न सुखी न च वा दुःखी न विरक्तो न सङ्गवान् ।
न मुमुक्षुर्न वा मुक्तो न किञ्चिन्न च किञ्चन ॥
iast:
na sukhī na ca vā duḥkhī na virakto na saṅgavān |
na mumukṣur-na vā mukto na kiñcin-na ca kiñcana ||
en: Neither happy nor unhappy, neither detached nor attached, neither seeking release nor released, he is not anything, and not nothing either.
tel: అతడు సుఖియు కాడు, దుఃఖియు కాడు; విరక్తుడును కాడు, సంగము కలవాడును కాడు; ముముక్షువును కాడు, ముక్తుడును కాడు; ఏదియు కాడు, ఏదియు లేనివాడును కాడు.
hi: वह न सुखी है और न दुःखी, न विरक्त है और न संगवान्, न मुमुक्षु है और न मुक्त; वह न कुछ है और न कुछ भी नहीं है।

--- verse 97 | section: Neither asleep nor awake ---

hi: विक्षेप में भी वह विक्षिप्त नहीं है, समाधि में भी समाधिमान् नहीं है; जाड्य में भी जड़ नहीं है, और पाण्डित्य में भी वह धन्य पुरुष पण्डित नहीं है।

--- verse 98 | section: Neither asleep nor awake ---

iast:
mukto yathāsthiti-svasthaḥ kṛta-kartavya-nirvṛtaḥ |

hi: मुक्त पुरुष जैसी स्थिति हो उसी में स्वस्थ है, कृत और कर्तव्य से निर्वृत है, और तृष्णा के अभाव से सर्वत्र सम है; वह किये और न किये हुए का स्मरण नहीं करता।

--- verse 99 | section: Neither asleep nor awake ---

--- verse 100 | section: Neither asleep nor awake ---
