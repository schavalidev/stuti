Title: Aṣṭāvakra Gītā — Prakaraṇa 5: Ācāryokta Layacatuṣṭaya
Devanāgarī: अष्टावक्रगीता – पञ्चमं प्रकरणम् – आचार्योक्तं लयचतुष्टयम्
Telugu: అష్టావక్ర గీత – పంచమ ప్రకరణము – ఆచార్యోక్తం లయచతుష్టయము

Author: Aṣṭāvakra Muni; the verses of this prakaraṇa are spoken by Aṣṭāvakra

Source / recension: Base text Gītā Press, Gorakhpur, Gītā-Saṅgraha, from the Devanāgarī OCR of archive.org item `gita-sangraha-gitapress`. Collated verse by verse against the Aṣṭāvakra-Gītā with bhāṣā-ṭīkā of Rāybahādur Bābū Jālimsiṁh, Tejkumār Press, Lucknow, fifth printing 1971 (archive.org `ashtavakra-gita-sanskrit-with-hindi-commentary`), an independent print lineage; and against GRETIL's `sa_aSTAvakragItA.xml` with sa.wikisource page id 1446, which are the same John Richards e-text in two scripts and count as one witness. No critical edition of this work exists. Outside the first prakaraṇa Gītā Press prints no उवाच line, so the speaker is taken from its colophon and no speaker line has been supplied. Devanāgarī follows the printed witnesses' sandhi, which the digital lineage breaks at word boundaries. IAST was generated mechanically from the Devanāgarī. English, Telugu and Hindi are original translations made directly from the Sanskrit. Pages 487–488 of the base text.

Recension note: Nothing in these four verses divides the witnesses. sa.wikisource breaks sandhi at ज्ञात्वैकमात्मानं एवमेव (v. 2) and व्यक्तं एवमेव (v. 3), where both prints and GRETIL's IAST run the words together; the printed form is written here.

Verse count: 4

Blurb: Four verses, each ending in the same half-line — evam eva layaṁ vraja, "just so, go to dissolution". The teacher gives four different reasons and the same instruction after each: there is nothing you are attached to, so what is there to renounce; the world rises out of you as a bubble out of water; the world, though it stands in front of the eyes, has no substance in you; and pain and pleasure, hope and disappointment, life and death are all one. The repetition is the method. Nothing new is argued in the second, third or fourth verse — the same door is pointed at four times.

--- verse 1 ---
deva:
न ते सङ्गोऽस्ति केनापि किं शुद्धस्त्यक्तुमिच्छसि ।
सङ्घात-विलयं कुर्वन्नेवमेव लयं व्रज ॥
iast:
na te saṅgo'sti kenāpi kiṁ śuddhas-tyaktum-icchasi |
saṅghāta-vilayaṁ kurvann-evam-eva layaṁ vraja ||
en: You have no attachment to anything. Being pure, what is it that you wish to give up? Dissolving the aggregate that is the body, go thus to dissolution.
tel: నీకు ఏదానితోను సంగము లేదు. శుద్ధుడవైన నీవు ఏమి విడువగోరుచున్నావు? దేహమనే సంఘాతమును లయము చేయుచు, ఇట్లే లయమును పొందుము.
hi: तुम्हारा किसी के साथ संग नहीं है। शुद्ध होकर तुम क्या त्यागना चाहते हो? देहरूपी संघात का विलय करते हुए इसी प्रकार लय को प्राप्त हो जाओ।

--- verse 2 ---
deva:
उदेति भवतो विश्वं वारिधेरिव बुद्बुदः ।
इति ज्ञात्वैकमात्मानमेवमेव लयं व्रज ॥
iast:
udeti bhavato viśvaṁ vāridher-iva budbudaḥ |
iti jñātvaikam-ātmānam-evam-eva layaṁ vraja ||
en: The world rises out of you as a bubble rises out of the sea. Knowing thus that the Self is one, go thus to dissolution.
tel: సముద్రమునుండి బుడగ పుట్టునట్లే నీనుండి విశ్వము పుట్టుచున్నది. ఇట్లు ఆత్మ ఒక్కటేయని ఎరిగి, ఇట్లే లయమును పొందుము.
hi: जैसे समुद्र से बुदबुदा उठता है, वैसे ही तुमसे विश्व उठता है। ऐसा जानकर कि आत्मा एक ही है, इसी प्रकार लय को प्राप्त हो जाओ।

--- verse 3 ---
deva:
प्रत्यक्षमप्यवस्तुत्वाद्विश्वं नास्त्यमले त्वयि ।
रज्जु-सर्प इव व्यक्तमेवमेव लयं व्रज ॥
iast:
pratyakṣam-apy-avastutvād-viśvaṁ nāsty-amale tvayi |
rajju-sarpa iva vyaktam-evam-eva layaṁ vraja ||
en: Though it stands before the eyes, the world, having no substance, does not exist in you who are stainless — it is as plain as a snake in a rope. Go thus to dissolution.
tel: ప్రత్యక్షముగా కనబడుచున్నను, వస్తుత్వము లేనిదగుటచే, నిర్మలుడవైన నీయందు విశ్వము లేదు; త్రాడులో పామువలె ఇది స్పష్టము. ఇట్లే లయమును పొందుము.
hi: प्रत्यक्ष होने पर भी, वस्तुत्व न होने से, निर्मल तुममें विश्व नहीं है; यह रस्सी में सर्प के समान स्पष्ट है। इसी प्रकार लय को प्राप्त हो जाओ।

--- verse 4 ---
deva:
सम-दुःख-सुखः पूर्ण आशा-नैराश्ययोः समः ।
सम-जीवित-मृत्युः सन्नेवमेव लयं व्रज ॥
iast:
sama-duḥkha-sukhaḥ pūrṇa āśā-nairāśyayoḥ samaḥ |
sama-jīvita-mṛtyuḥ sann-evam-eva layaṁ vraja ||
en: Being even in pain and pleasure, full, even in hope and disappointment, even in life and death, go thus to dissolution.
tel: దుఃఖమునందును, సుఖమునందును సమముగా ఉండువాడవై, పరిపూర్ణుడవై, ఆశయందును, నిరాశయందును సముడవై, జీవితమునందును, మృత్యువునందును సముడవై, ఇట్లే లయమును పొందుము.
hi: दुःख और सुख में सम, पूर्ण, आशा और निराशा में सम, तथा जीवन और मृत्यु में सम होकर इसी प्रकार लय को प्राप्त हो जाओ।
