Verse count: 14

Sections:
Where are the elements, where the body (vv. 1–7)
Where is the knower, where the known (vv. 8–14)

Blurb: The last prakaraṇa of the work as the digital lineage has it, and in Gītā Press the last before the index chapter. It is fourteen verses of nothing but the question kva, "where", and the questions are put to the whole apparatus of the teaching itself. Where are the elements, the body, the senses, the mind; where is scripture, where is knowledge of the Self; where is learning and where is ignorance; where is action already set in motion, where is liberation while alive, where is that disembodied absoluteness; where is the knower, the means of knowing, the thing known and the knowing. The thirteenth verse asks where teaching is, and scripture, and pupil, and teacher. Then the fourteenth closes the Gītā in half a line that refuses to add anything at all: what is the use of saying much here — nothing whatever rises in me.

tel: నిర్మలమైన నా స్వరూపమునందు భూతములు ఎక్కడ? దేహము ఎక్కడ? ఇంద్రియములు ఎక్కడ? మనస్సు ఎక్కడ? శూన్యము ఎక్కడ? నైరాశ్యము ఎక్కడ?
