Title: Śrī Lambodara Stotram (Krodhāsura-kṛtam)

Author: Krodhāsura, the demon Anger ("krodhāsura uvāca"), Śrī Mudgala Mahāpurāṇa, Lambodaracarita, chapter 8

Sections:
స్తోత్రం — Stotram, Krodhāsura's praise (vv. 1–10)
వరప్రదానం — Varapradāna, Lambodara's answer and the fruit of recitation (vv. 11–14)

Blurb: The speaker of this hymn is Anger itself. Krodhāsura is the demon whose subduing gives the Lambodara avatāra its whole purpose, so the hymn is an act of surrender performed by the very passion it is named after. Its two most notable verses read Gaṇeśa's body as a map of samādhi. The trunk is asamprajñāta, absorption with no object left in it, while the body beneath is samprajñāta, absorption that still holds one. The great belly is the womb from which both the manifold world and Brahman have come forth. Lambodara's parting boon is fitted exactly to the one who asks it: whoever recites this will never know a fear born of anger.

क्रोधासुर उवाच ।

krodhāsura uvāca |
lambodara namastubhyaṁ śāntiyogasvarūpiṇe |
sarvaśāntipradātre te vighneśāya namo namaḥ ||
en: Krodhāsura said: Salutations to you, Lambodara, whose very form is the yoga of peace; to you, the giver of every peace, to Vighneśa — salutations, salutations.
tel: క్రోధాసురుడు పలికెను: శాంతియోగస్వరూపుడవైన లంబోదరా, నీకు నమస్కారము; సర్వశాంతిని ప్రసాదించువాడవైన విఘ్నేశ్వరునికి నమస్కారము, నమస్కారము.
hi: क्रोधासुर ने कहा: शांति-योग के स्वरूप लम्बोदर, तुम्हें नमस्कार; समस्त शांति के दाता विघ्नेश को नमस्कार, नमस्कार।

--- verse 2 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
असम्प्रज्ञातरूपेयं शुण्डा ते नात्र संशयः ।
सम्प्रज्ञातमयो देहो देहधारिन्नमो नमः ॥
iast:
asamprajñātarūpeyaṁ śuṇḍā te nātra saṁśayaḥ |
samprajñātamayo deho dehadhārinnamo namaḥ ||
en: Your trunk is the very form of asamprajñāta samādhi, the absorption in which no object remains — of this there is no doubt; and your body is made of samprajñāta, the absorption that still holds an object. O wearer of that body, salutations, salutations.
tel: నీ తొండము అసంప్రజ్ఞాత సమాధి స్వరూపము — ఇందులో సందేహము లేదు; నీ దేహము సంప్రజ్ఞాత సమాధిమయము. ఓ దేహధారీ, నమస్కారము, నమస్కారము.
hi: तुम्हारी सूँड़ असंप्रज्ञात समाधि का ही स्वरूप है — इसमें संदेह नहीं; और तुम्हारा देह संप्रज्ञातमय है। हे देहधारी, नमस्कार, नमस्कार।

तेन स्वानन्दवासी त्वं नमः सम्योगधारिणे ॥

en: In Svānanda, the state of his own bliss, the yogins behold you unceasingly, lord of Brahman; and that is why you are called the dweller in Svānanda. Salutations to you, who hold that union.
tel: స్వానందమునందు యోగులు నిన్ను నిత్యము దర్శించుచున్నారు, బ్రహ్మనాయకా; అందుచేతనే నీవు స్వానందవాసివి. ఆ సంయోగమును ధరించిన నీకు నమస్కారము.
hi: स्वानंद में योगी तुम्हें नित्य देखते हैं, हे ब्रह्मनायक; इसीलिए तुम स्वानंदवासी कहलाते हो। उस संयोग के धारक तुम्हें नमस्कार।

--- verse 4 | section: Stotram ---
deva:

ब्रह्म तद्वन्न सन्देहो लम्बोदर नमोऽस्तु ते ॥

samutpannaṁ tvadudarājjagannānāvidhaṁ prabho |
brahma tadvanna sandeho lambodara namo'stu te ||
en: O lord, the world in all its variety has come forth from your belly, and Brahman likewise — of this there is no doubt. O Lambodara, salutations to you.
tel: ఓ ప్రభూ, నానావిధమైన ఈ జగత్తు నీ ఉదరమునుండియే ఉద్భవించినది, బ్రహ్మము కూడ అట్లే — సందేహము లేదు. ఓ లంబోదరా, నీకు నమస్కారము.
hi: हे प्रभो, नाना प्रकार का यह जगत तुम्हारे उदर से ही उत्पन्न हुआ है, और ब्रह्म भी वैसे ही — इसमें संदेह नहीं। हे लम्बोदर, तुम्हें नमस्कार।

--- verse 5 | section: Stotram ---

--- verse 9 | section: Stotram ---
deva:

लम्बोदराय नित्यं नमो नमस्ते महात्मने ॥

en: Therefore be gracious to me, Vighneśa — I am your servant, Gajānana. To Lambodara, salutations without end; salutations to you, great soul.

hi: और जो इसे सुनेगा, उसके लिए भी वैसा ही होगा — इसमें संदेह नहीं। इस स्तोत्र के पाठ से जो जो कोई चाहेगा, वह वह मैं दूँगा।

Recension note: The English (IAST) and Devanāgarī pages at stotranidhi.com were compared line by line and agree exactly across all fourteen verses, including the "krodhāsura uvāca" and "lambodara uvāca" speaker markers and the Mudgala Purāṇa colophon. Verse numbers present in the source have been removed from the deva/iast text per this corpus's convention; the source's ē/ō vowel marks are rendered as plain e/o and its decomposed vocalic r (r plus combining ring below) as precomposed ṛ. Verse 10 is compressed even by the standards of this text; the rendering above takes svataḥ-utthāna / parataḥ-utthāna as the two modes in which manifestation arises, both of them sustained by Gaṇeśa, and reads the concluding question as addressed to him rather than to Brahman.
