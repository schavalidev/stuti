Title: Śrī Heramba Stutiḥ (Nara-Nārāyaṇa-kṛtā)
Devanāgarī: श्री हेरम्ब स्तुतिः (नरनारायणकृता)
Telugu: శ్రీ హేరంబ స్తుతిః (నరనారాయణకృతా)

Author: The twin sages Nara and Nārāyaṇa ("naranārāyaṇāvūcatuḥ"), Śrī Mudgala Mahāpurāṇa, third khaṇḍa

Source / recension: stotranidhi.com — https://stotranidhi.com/en/sri-heramba-stuthi-in-english/ (IAST), https://stotranidhi.com/hi/sri-heramba-stuthi-in-sanskrit/ (Devanāgarī). Both pages were compared verse by verse and carry identical content with no variant readings. Neither page supplies a rendered prose meaning, so the en/tel/hi renderings below are this corpus's own translation of the fetched Sanskrit. The source closes with the colophon "iti śrīmanmudgale mahāpurāṇe tṛtīyekhaṇḍe naranārāyaṇakṛtā śrī heramba stutiḥ," which is not reproduced as a verse block below. Distinct from the "Heramba Stotram" of the same Purāṇa, which is Gaurī's plea during the demon's assault.

Verse count: 14

Sections:
స్తుతి — Stuti, the praise of Nara and Nārāyaṇa (vv. 1–10)
వరప్రదానం — Varapradāna, Heramba's answer and the fruit of recitation (vv. 11–14)

Blurb: Nara and Nārāyaṇa — the twin ascetics of Badarikāśrama, taken in this Purāṇa as the primal teachers of the yoga path — praise Gaṇeśa in his four-armed, lion-riding Heramba form. The hymn's method is to set each attribute against its own opposite: he spreads out every distinction and is himself without distinction, he is with qualities and without them, creator and sustainer and destroyer in one breath. Verse 9 offers the stuti's own gloss on the name: Heramba is lord precisely of those whose pride has fallen away. The dual-number verbs ("we two praise," "we two bow") mark this throughout as the speech of a pair, and Gaṇeśa answers them in a voice like thunder.

--- verse 1 | section: Stuti ---
deva:
नरनारायणावूचतुः ।
नमस्ते गणनाथाय भक्तसंरक्षकाय ते ।
भक्तेभ्यो भक्तिदात्रे वै हेरम्बाय नमो नमः ॥
iast:
naranārāyaṇāvūcatuḥ |
namaste gaṇanāthāya bhaktasaṁrakṣakāya te |
bhaktebhyo bhaktidātre vai herambāya namo namaḥ ||
en: Nara and Nārāyaṇa said: Salutations to you, lord of the gaṇas, guardian of those devoted to you; to him who gives his devotees devotion itself, to Heramba — salutations, salutations.
tel: నరనారాయణులు పలికిరి: గణనాథా, భక్తులను రక్షించువాడా, నీకు నమస్కారము; భక్తులకు భక్తినే ప్రసాదించువాడైన హేరంబునికి నమస్కారము, నమస్కారము.
hi: नर और नारायण ने कहा: गणनाथ, भक्तों के संरक्षक, तुम्हें नमस्कार; भक्तों को भक्ति ही देने वाले हेरम्ब को नमस्कार, नमस्कार।

--- verse 4 | section: Stuti ---

en: To you, lord of Siddhi and Buddhi, who are their very form; to Yoga itself and to the lord of yoga; to the one whose ears are broad as winnowing baskets — salutations to you.
tel: సిద్ధిబుద్ధులకు పతివైన నీకు, సిద్ధిబుద్ధిస్వరూపునికి, యోగస్వరూపునికి, యోగనాథునికి, శూర్పకర్ణునికి — నీకు నమస్కారము.
hi: सिद्धि-बुद्धि के पति, उन्हीं के स्वरूप, योगस्वरूप, योगनाथ, शूर्पकर्ण — तुम्हें नमस्कार।

--- verse 5 | section: Stuti ---

tel: సగుణుడవైన నీకు నమస్కారము, నిర్గుణుడవైన పరమాత్మకు నమస్కారము; సర్వులచే పూజ్యుడవైన, సర్వస్వరూపుడవైన దేవదేవునికి నీకు నమస్కారము.
hi: सगुण तुम्हें नमस्कार, निर्गुण परमात्मा को नमस्कार; सबके पूज्य, सर्वस्वरूप, देवदेव — तुम्हें नमस्कार।

--- verse 6 | section: Stuti ---

सुखशान्तिधरायैव नाभिशेषाय ते नमः ॥

sukhaśāntidharāyaiva nābhiśeṣāya te namaḥ ||
en: To you, the Brahman of all who know Brahman, ever the giver of peace; to the holder of happiness and peace, to him who wears the serpent Śeṣa about his waist — salutations to you.

--- verse 9 | section: Stuti ---
deva:
गताभिमानिनां नाथस्त्वमेवात्र न संशयः ।
तेन हेरम्बनामाऽसि विनायक नमोऽस्तु ते ॥
iast:
gatābhimānināṁ nāthastvamevātra na saṁśayaḥ |
tena herambanāmā'si vināyaka namo'stu te ||
en: Here there is no doubt: you alone are the lord of those whose pride has fallen away — and it is for this that your name is Heramba. O Vināyaka, salutations to you.
tel: అభిమానము (అహంకారము) నశించినవారికి నీవే నాథుడవు — ఇందులో సందేహము లేదు; అందుచేతనే నీకు హేరంబుడను నామము. ఓ వినాయకా, నీకు నమస్కారము.
hi: जिनका अभिमान मिट गया है, उनके नाथ तुम ही हो — इसमें संदेह नहीं; इसी कारण तुम्हारा नाम हेरम्ब है। हे विनायक, तुम्हें नमस्कार।

--- verse 10 | section: Stuti ---
deva:

अतस्त्वां प्रणमावो वै तेन तुष्टो भव प्रभो ॥

en: How shall we two praise you, O lord of the gaṇas — you who are the supreme, made of that non-difference which is yoga? So we only bow to you; be pleased with that, O lord.
tel: ఓ గణాధీశా, యోగమను అభేదస్వరూపుడవైన పరమాత్మవగు నిన్ను మేమిద్దరము ఏమని స్తుతింపగలము? అందుచేత నిన్ను నమస్కరించుచున్నాము మాత్రమే; ఓ ప్రభూ, దానితోనే సంతుష్టుడవు కమ్ము.
hi: हे गणाधीश, योगरूपी अभेद से बने हुए परम तुम्हारी हम दोनों क्या स्तुति करें? इसलिए हम केवल तुम्हें प्रणाम करते हैं; हे प्रभो, उसी से संतुष्ट हो जाओ।

en: Heramba said: Held fast by your devotion, I shall give the boon your hearts desire — speak it, you two greatly blessed primal sages, revealers of the path of yoga.
tel: హేరంబుడు పలికెను: మీ భక్తికి కట్టుబడినవాడనై మీ మనసులోని కోరికను తీర్చెదను — యోగమార్గమును ప్రకాశింపజేయు ఆదిమునులారా, మహాభాగులారా, కోరుకొనుడు.

Recension note: The English (IAST) and Devanāgarī pages at stotranidhi.com were compared line by line and agree exactly across all fourteen verses, including the "naranārāyaṇāvūcatuḥ" and "heramba uvāca" speaker markers and the Mudgala Purāṇa colophon. Verse numbers present in the source have been removed from the deva/iast text per this corpus's convention; the source's ē/ō vowel marks are rendered as plain e/o and its decomposed vocalic r (r plus combining ring below) as precomposed ṛ. In verse 6, nābhiśeṣa is read as "having Śeṣa at the navel/waist," Gaṇeśa's serpent-girdle; the epithet recurs across the Mudgala Purāṇa hymns.
