Title: Gaṇeśa Bhujaṅgam
Devanāgarī: श्री गणेश भुजङ्गम्
Telugu: శ్రీ గణేశ భుజంగం

Author: Ādi Śaṅkarācārya (traditional attribution)

Source / recension: stotranidhi.com Devanāgarī, Telugu, and English (IAST) pages — https://stotranidhi.com/hi/sri-ganesha-bhujangam-in-sanskrit/ ; https://stotranidhi.com/ganesha-bhujangam-in-telugu/ ; https://stotranidhi.com/en/sri-ganesha-bhujangam-in-english/

Verse count: 9

Sections:
Bhujaṅgam (vv. 1–8)
Phalaśruti — the fruit of recitation (v. 9)

Blurb: Composed in the same bhujaṅgaprayāta ("serpent-gliding") meter as Śaṅkara's Subrahmaṇya Bhujaṅgam, this hymn to Gaṇeśa spends its first six verses on vivid darśana — the dancing form, the tinkling anklets, the fragrance-drunk bees, the jewelled crown — before pivoting in verse 7 to identify that same elephant-faced god with the syllable Oṁ itself: formless, beyond the guṇas, the womb of the Veda. The eighth verse folds this into a direct address as creator and dissolver of the universe, and the closing phalaśruti promises that nothing remains out of reach for one who pleases him.

--- verse 9 | section: Phalaśruti ---
deva:
इमं सुस्तवं प्रातरुत्थाय भक्त्या
पठेद्यस्तु मर्त्यो लभेत्सर्वकामान् ।
गणेशप्रसादेन सिध्यन्ति वाचो
गणेशे विभौ दुर्लभं किं प्रसन्ने
iast:
imaṁ sustavaṁ prātarutthāya bhaktyā
paṭhedyastu martyo labhetsarvakāmān |
gaṇeśaprasādena sidhyanti vāco
gaṇeśe vibhau durlabhaṁ kiṁ prasanne
en:
Whatever mortal rises at dawn and recites this excellent hymn with devotion obtains all his desires; by Gaṇeśa's grace his speech itself gains power — and when mighty Lord Gaṇeśa is pleased, what indeed remains hard to attain in this world? This closing verse states the promised fruit of recitation.
tel:
ప్రాతఃకాలంలో లేచి భక్తితో ఈ ఉత్తమస్తవాన్ని పఠించే మనిషి తన కోరికలన్నీ పొందుతాడు; గణేశుని అనుగ్రహం వల్ల వాక్శక్తి సిద్ధిస్తుంది — ప్రభువైన గణేశుడు ప్రసన్నుడైతే ఈ లోకంలో దుర్లభమైనదంటూ ఏముంటుంది?
hi:
जो मनुष्य प्रातःकाल उठकर भक्तिपूर्वक इस उत्तम स्तोत्र का पाठ करता है, वह समस्त कामनाएँ प्राप्त करता है; गणेश की कृपा से वाणी सिद्ध होती है — प्रभु गणेश प्रसन्न हों तो इस संसार में क्या दुर्लभ है?
