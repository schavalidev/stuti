Blurb: This is a late Upaniṣad, but a highly influential one. It identifies Gaṇapati directly with Brahman, in the words 'tvameva pratyakṣaṁ tattvamasi', meaning 'you alone are the visible reality of that'. It folds him into the formulas of Vedānta, naming saccidānanda, the three guṇas and the four states. It then gives the famous Gaṇeśa Gāyatrī, beginning 'ekadantāya vidmahe'. It closes with a dhyāna verse that fixed the standard image of four-armed, elephant-faced Gaṇapati, which is still used in temple worship today.
