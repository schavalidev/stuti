Title: Hanumān Cālīsā
Devanāgarī: हनुमान चालीसा
Telugu: హనుమాన్ చాలీసా
Set: hanuman-calisa

Author: Traditionally attributed to Gosvāmī Tulasīdāsa

Source / recension: stotranidhi.com — Devanāgarī recension at https://stotranidhi.com/hi/hanuman-chalisa-in-sanskrit/ (cross-checked against https://stotranidhi.com/hanuman-chalisa-in-telugu/).

Note: Composed in Hindi/Awadhi, not Sanskrit. The deva: fields below transcribe the actual Awadhi/Hindi text in Devanāgarī exactly as it is traditionally sung; the iast: fields are a plain transliteration of that same Hindi/Awadhi text (not Sanskrit). Because the original is already Hindi, the hi: field for each verse gives a plain modern-Hindi paraphrase (Awadhi differs somewhat from modern Hindi) rather than a further "translation."

Verse count: 43 (2 opening dohās + 40 numbered caupāīs + 1 closing dohā)

Sections:
दोहा — opening dohās (2)
चौपाई — the 40 numbered caupāīs
दोहा — closing dohā (1)

Blurb: The single most widely recited Hanumān text in North India, composed as a chain of 40 rhymed Awadhi couplets bracketed by three dohās. It moves from Hanumān's parentage and physical form, through the Rāmāyaṇa deeds recalled as proof of his power, into a direct, almost conversational plea for strength, wisdom, and freedom from fear — which is precisely why it is chanted as much for protection and courage in daily life as for formal worship.

--- verse 1 | section: दोहा ---
deva:
श्री गुरु चरण सरोज रज
निजमन मुकुर सुधारि
वरणौ रघुवर विमल यश
जो दायक फलचारि ॥
iast:
śrī guru caraṇa saroja raja
nijamana mukura sudhāri
varaṇau raghuvara vimala yaśa
jo dāyaka phalacāri
en: Cleansing the mirror of my mind with the dust of my Guru's lotus feet, I describe the pure fame of Raghuvara (Śrī Rāma), which bestows the four fruits of life (dharma, artha, kāma, mokṣa).
tel: నా గురువుగారి పాదపద్మాల ధూళితో నా మనసు అనే అద్దాన్ని శుభ్రపరచుకుని, నాలుగు పురుషార్థాలను ప్రసాదించే రఘువరుని (శ్రీరాముని) నిర్మలమైన కీర్తిని వర్ణిస్తున్నాను.
hi: अपने गुरु के चरण-कमलों की धूल से मन के दर्पण को स्वच्छ करके, मैं रघुवर (श्रीराम) के उस निर्मल यश का वर्णन करता हूँ, जो चारों फल (धर्म, अर्थ, काम, मोक्ष) देने वाला है।

--- verse 2 | section: दोहा ---
deva:
बुद्धिहीन तनु जानिके
सुमिरौ पवनकुमार
बल बुद्धि विद्या देहु मोहि
हरहु कलेश विकार ॥
iast:
buddhihīna tanu jānike
sumirau pavanakumāra
bala buddhi vidyā dehu mohi
harahu kaleśa vikāra
en: Knowing my body to be devoid of wisdom, I remember Pavanakumāra (Hanumān, son of the Wind); grant me strength, intellect, and knowledge, O Lord, and remove my afflictions and faults.
tel: నా శరీరం బుద్ధిహీనమని తెలుసుకుని, పవనకుమారుడైన హనుమంతుని స్మరిస్తున్నాను; నాకు బలం, బుద్ధి, విద్య ప్రసాదించి, నా బాధలను దోషాలను తొలగించుము.
hi: अपने शरीर को बुद्धिहीन जानकर मैं पवनकुमार हनुमान का स्मरण करता हूँ; मुझे बल, बुद्धि और विद्या दो, तथा मेरे कष्ट और विकार दूर करो।

--- verse 3 | section: चौपाई ---
deva:

जय कपीश तिहु लोक उजागर ॥
iast:
jaya hanumāna jñānaguṇasāgara |

en: Victory to Hanumān, ocean of wisdom and virtue; victory to the lord of monkeys, who illumines all three worlds.
tel: జ్ఞానగుణాలకు సాగరమైన హనుమంతునికి జయము; మూడు లోకాలను ప్రకాశింపజేసే వానరరాజుకు జయము.
hi: ज्ञान और गुणों के सागर हनुमान की जय हो; तीनों लोकों को प्रकाशित करने वाले कपीश की जय हो।

Variant at verse 34 (added 2026-09-12): the second half-line is recited both as सदा रहो रघुपति के दासा and as सादर हो रघुपति के दासा. The two divide the same syllables differently. सदा रहो, "may you ever remain the servant of Raghupati", is the reading printed in this file's base text and in the Gītā Press recension held at hanuman/32_hanuman_chalisa_gitapress.txt, and it is kept as the base here. सादर हो, "be the servant of Raghupati with reverence", is given in brackets. The witness for the bracketed reading is oral, not printed: it was supplied by the maintainer of this corpus from the recitation he was raised with (user, 2026-09-12). It has not been checked against a printed edition. That is recorded plainly here so that a later reader can weigh it for what it is, and can confirm it against a printed witness if one is wanted.

Language: Hindi. The text is Hindi, in the Sanskritised (tatsama) spelling of the Hanumān Cālīsā, which is the form most modern printings use and the form many reciters learn. It is not Sanskrit. Tulsīdās composed the poem in Awadhi, and the Awadhi spelling of the same poem is held in this corpus at `hanuman/32_hanuman_chalisa_gitapress.txt`, from the Gītā Press edition. The two files hold the same poem in two spellings, and neither supersedes the other. A reader recites whichever one they learned. The difference is regular and runs right through the text: य becomes ज (यश → जस, यम → जम, युग → जुग), व becomes ब (विमल → बिमल, वचन → बचन, महावीर → महाबीर), श and ष become स (कलेश → कलेस, शंकर → संकर, कपीश → कपीस), ण becomes न (गुणी → गुनी), and conjuncts open out (प्रताप → परताप, जन्म → जनम, रक्षक → रच्छक). The `deva:` field carries the printed text exactly as it stands. The `iast:` field is a mechanical transliteration of it to corpus convention. The `hi:` field gives a plain modern-Hindi rendering of the sense, and `en:` and `tel:` do the same in English and Telugu. In transliteration ड़ and ढ़ are written ṛ and ṛh. The anusvāra before a stop is written as the homorganic nasal in this file (अंजनिपुत्र → añjaniputra), which is this file's own practice and not a corpus rule.
