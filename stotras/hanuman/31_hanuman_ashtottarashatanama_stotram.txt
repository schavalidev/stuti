Title: Śrī Hanumad Aṣṭottaraśatanāma Stotram
Devanāgarī: श्रीहनुमत्-अष्टोत्तरशतनामस्तोत्रम्
Telugu: శ్రీ హనుమత్ అష్టోత్తరశతనామ స్తోత్రం

Author: Anonymous, and **no scriptural attribution is claimed**. The colophon names no Purāṇa, Tantra or kalpa — the only entry of the eleven so far with none — and carries instead an asterisked editorial footnote: `प्राचीन नामावलीके आधारपर संयोजित`, "compiled on the basis of an ancient nāmāvalī".

Source / recension: Primary text: **Gītā Press, Gorakhpur — Śatanāma Stotra Saṅgraha**, code 1850, pp. 66–70 (stotra pp. 66–68, nāmāvalī pp. 69–70), transcribed from the page images of the printed edition rather than from OCR. Entry 11 of the volume's forty. Stotra and nāmāvalī are paired in one file as the publisher prints them, and agree in order and content at all 108 positions. `hanuman/` was checked and holds 30 files, none of them an aṣṭottaraśatanāma; note that `hanuman/03_panchamukha_hanumat_kavacham.txt` is a different work despite `पञ्चवक्त्र` appearing here as no. 58. **No second edition was collated**, and no web witness has been sought — which matters more than usual here, because the volume itself says the stotra is a modern arrangement (below), so a witness to the *nāmāvalī* it was arranged from would be the thing worth finding.

Recension note: **Prefix.** Gītā Press prints `ॐ` before each name; this corpus prints `श्री` for every nāmāvalī without exception (user, 2026-09-08). **THE VOLUME SAYS THE STOTRA IS ITS OWN COMPILATION, AND THAT REVERSES THE USUAL DEPENDENCE.** An asterisk on the stotra's colophon leads to the footnote `प्राचीन नामावलीके आधारपर संयोजित` — "compiled on the basis of an ancient nāmāvalī". In every earlier entry the stotra is the received text and the nāmāvalī the derived list; here the editors state the opposite. That is a fact about this edition, printed by the editors themselves, and it is recorded rather than argued with; but it does mean the internal stotra-against-nāmāvalī check, which has been the strongest evidence available at every earlier entry, **is worth much less here** — the two lists agreeing tells us the compiler copied accurately, not that two independent transmissions concur. **The count is exactly 108, and all 108 are distinct** — the third entry of the eleven with no repetition at all, after Veṅkaṭeśa (109 names) and Hayagrīva's list (which repeats three). **A separate twelve-name stotra is printed inside this entry, boxed, on p. 68, and is NOT included in this file.** It is the Hanumān Dvādaśanāma Stotra (`हनुमानञ्जनीसूनुर्वायुपुत्रो महाबलः …`), attributed in the print to the **Ānanda Rāmāyaṇa** and given with its own phalaśruti and Hindi gloss. It is a complete work in its own right, not part of the aṣṭottara, and belongs in its own file; it is logged as outstanding in `ASHTOTTARA_AUDIT_2026-09-08.md` rather than folded in here. **Sandhi printed loose in two places** and kept as printed: v. 1 `महावीरः हनूमान्` and v. 9 `प्राज्ञः रामदूतः`, both with visarga before a following consonant where the ordinary sandhi would give `-ो`; likewise v. 13 `गन्धमादनशैलस्थः लङ्कापुरविदाहकः`. **`हनूमान्` in v. 1, `हनुमते` at no. 3** — the verse lengthens the second syllable for the metre, the list does not. Both kept. **Three names carry the Mahābhārata rather than the Rāmāyaṇa**: no. 16 `भीमसेनसहायकृते`, no. 103 `पार्थध्वजाग्रसंवासिने` and no. 104 `शरपञ्जरभेदकाय` place Hanumān on Arjuna's banner in the Kurukṣetra war. **No. 31 `भविष्यच्चतुराननाय`** — "who is to be the four-faced one" — refers to the tradition that Hanumān will be the Brahmā of a coming age; rendered literally rather than expanded.

Blurb: The hundred and eight of the Rāmāyaṇa's own witness, and the busiest narrative list in the volume: the ring carried to Sītā, the Aśoka grove torn up, Siṁhikā and Laṅkinī broken, the sun swallowed, the herb-mountain lifted, Lakṣmaṇa brought back. Three names step outside the story altogether and put him on Arjuna's banner at Kurukṣetra. The last name is the one that holds the rest — the bearer of the whole burden of service at Rāma's feet.

--- verse 1 | section: Dhyānam ---
deva:
वन्दे विद्युज्ज्वलनविलसद्ब्रह्मसूत्रैकनिष्ठं कर्णद्वन्द्वे कनकरचिते कुण्डले धारयन्तम् ।
सत्कौपीनं कपिचरवृतं कामरूपं कपीन्द्रं पुत्रं वायोरिनसुतसुखदं वज्रदेहं वरेण्यम् ॥
iast:
vande vidyujjvalanavilasadbrahmasūtraikaniṣṭhaṁ karṇadvandve kanakaracite kuṇḍale dhārayantam |
satkaupīnaṁ kapicaravṛtaṁ kāmarūpaṁ kapīndraṁ putraṁ vāyorinasutasukhadaṁ vajradehaṁ vareṇyam ||
en: I salute the son of Vāyu: shining like lightning-fire, single-minded in the way of brahman, wearing golden rings in both ears; in a fair loincloth, surrounded always by the monkeys, taking what form he wills; lord of the monkeys, giver of joy to the son of the sun, his body hard as adamant, worthy of all worship.
tel: విద్యుత్కాంతివలె ప్రకాశించువాడు, బ్రహ్మసూత్రమునందు ఏకనిష్ఠుడు, రెండు చెవులయందు సువర్ణకుండలములు ధరించినవాడు, సత్కౌపీనధారి, కపులచే సదా చుట్టబడినవాడు, కామరూపుడు, కపీంద్రుడు, సుగ్రీవునకు సుఖమిచ్చువాడు, వజ్రదేహుడు, వరేణ్యుడు అగు వాయుపుత్రుని వందించుచున్నాను.
hi: विद्युत्-कान्तिके सदृश वर्णवाले, ज्ञानमार्गमें एकनिष्ठ, कर्णयुगलमें सुवर्णनिर्मित कुण्डल धारण करनेवाले, सुन्दर कौपीन धारण करनेवाले, कपियोंसे सदा घिरे रहनेवाले, इच्छानुसार रूप धारण करनेवाले, वानरोंके स्वामी, सुग्रीवको सुख देनेवाले, वज्रके समान देहवाले सर्वपूज्य वायुपुत्रकी मैं वन्दना करता हूँ।

चिरजीवी रामभक्तो दैत्यकार्यविघातकः ।
अक्षहन्ता काञ्चनाभः पञ्चवक्त्रो महातपाः ॥ १२ ॥
iast:
cirajīvī rāmabhakto daityakāryavighātakaḥ |
akṣahantā kāñcanābhaḥ pañcavaktro mahātapāḥ || 12 ||
en: The long-lived; devoted to Rāma; who thwarts the work of the daityas; slayer of Akṣa; golden-hued; five-faced; of great austerity.
tel: చిరజీవి, రామభక్తుడు, దైత్యకార్యవిఘాతకుడు, అక్షహంత, కాంచనాభుడు, పంచవక్త్రుడు, మహాతపుడు.
hi: चिरञ्जीवी, रामभक्त, दैत्योंके कार्यमें विघ्न डालनेवाले, अक्षका वध करनेवाले, स्वर्णके समान कान्तिवाले, पञ्चमुख तथा महातपस्वी।

--- verse 14 | section: Stotram ---

iast:
laṅkinībhañjanaḥ śrīmān siṁhikāprāṇabhañjanaḥ |

en: Who broke Laṅkinī; possessed of splendour; who broke the life of Siṁhikā; who dwells on the Gandhamādana hill; who burned the city of Laṅkā.

hi: लङ्किनीका भञ्जन करनेवाले, श्रीमान्, सिंहिकाके प्राणोंका भञ्जन करनेवाले, गन्धमादन पर्वतपर स्थित तथा लङ्कापुरीको जलानेवाले।

--- verse 16 | section: Stotram ---
