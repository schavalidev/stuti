Title: Śrī Āpaduddhāraka Hanumat Stotram
Devanāgarī: श्री आपदुद्धारक हनुमत् स्तोत्रम्
Telugu: శ్రీ ఆపదుద్ధారక హనుమత్ స్తోత్రం

Author: Attributed to Vibhīṣaṇa (per the closing colophon: iti vibhīṣaṇakṛtaṁ sarvāpaduddhāraka śrīhanumat stotram), with a formal ṛṣi-viniyoga naming Vibhīṣaṇa as ṛṣi and Hanumān as devatā

Source / recension: stotranidhi.com — IAST recension at https://stotranidhi.com/en/sri-apaduddharaka-hanumath-stotram-in-english/ (fetched via Wayback Machine, http://web.archive.org/web/20240419143442/https://stotranidhi.com/en/sri-apaduddharaka-hanumath-stotram-in-english/, since the live site returned HTTP 403/522 to direct fetches) and Devanāgarī recension at https://stotranidhi.com/hi/sri-apaduddharaka-hanumath-stotram-in-sanskrit/ (via http://web.archive.org/web/20250918060048/https://stotranidhi.com/hi/sri-apaduddharaka-hanumath-stotram-in-sanskrit/). The two recensions agree verse for verse. This is a distinct composition from the corpus's Vibhīṣaṇa-kṛta Hanumat Stotram (file 11), though both are attributed to Vibhīṣaṇa. Stotranidhi publishes only the Sanskrit text (no prose meaning); the English, Telugu, and Hindi meanings below are original translations prepared for this corpus.

Verse count: 15 (a viniyoga preamble, two dhyāna verses, eleven verses of praise and phalaśruti, and a closing one-verse mantra), presented here in 15 entries plus the unnumbered opening viniyoga

Blurb: This is a hymn in the manner of a kavaca, addressed to Hanumān as āpaduddhāraka, meaning 'deliverer from calamity'. It is attributed to Vibhīṣaṇa. It opens with a formal viniyoga and two dhyāna verses, which picture Añjaneya with a mountain in one hand and a chain-hook in the other. The body of the stotra invokes Hanumān against sudden calamity, against the threefold sorrow, and against disease and affliction by the planets. It promises freedom from all danger to whoever remembers him: in prison, on a journey, in battle, on water, on land, in the sky, among vehicles, at a crossroads, or in a forest full of elephants, lions, tigers and thieves. It closes with a short mantra addressed to Hanumān as lord of monkeys.

संध्या में हो या प्रभात में, जो अंजनासुत का स्मरण करते हैं, वे निश्चय ही अर्थसिद्धि, विजय और कीर्ति प्राप्त करते हैं, इसमें संदेह नहीं।

--- verse 13 | section: phalaśruti ---
deva:
जप्त्वा स्तोत्रमिदं मन्त्रं प्रतिवारं पठेन्नरः ।
राजस्थाने सभास्थाने प्राप्ते वादे लभेज्जयम् ॥
iast:
japtvā stotramidaṁ mantraṁ prativāraṁ paṭhennaraḥ |
rājasthāne sabhāsthāne prāpte vāde labhejjayam ||
en:
A man who repeats this stotra as a mantra and recites it every time obtains victory in the king's court, in an assembly, or in any debate he undertakes.
tel:
ఈ స్తోత్రాన్ని మంత్రంగా జపించి ప్రతిసారీ పఠించే నరుడు రాజసభలో, సభాస్థానంలో, వాదంలో విజయం పొందుతాడు.
hi:
इस स्तोत्र को मंत्र की तरह जपकर प्रतिबार पढ़ने वाला पुरुष राजसभा में, सभा में, वाद में विजय प्राप्त करता है।

--- verse 14 | section: phalaśruti ---
deva:
विभीषणकृतं स्तोत्रम् यः पठेत् प्रयतो नरः ।
सर्वापद्भ्यो विमुच्येत नाऽत्र कार्या विचारणा ॥
iast:
vibhīṣaṇakṛtaṁ stotram yaḥ paṭhet prayato naraḥ |
sarvāpadbhyo vimucyeta nā'tra kāryā vicāraṇā ||
en:
Whoever, purified and controlled, recites this stotra composed by Vibhīṣaṇa is freed from all calamities — there is no need to doubt this.
tel:
విభీషణుడు రచించిన ఈ స్తోత్రాన్ని పవిత్రుడై పఠించే నరుడు సర్వాపదల నుండి విముక్తుడవుతాడు; ఇందులో సందేహం అక్కర్లేదు.
hi:
विभीषण द्वारा रचित इस स्तोत्र को पवित्र होकर पढ़ने वाला पुरुष समस्त आपत्तियों से मुक्त हो जाता है; इसमें कोई संदेह नहीं करना चाहिए।

--- verse 15 | section: मन्त्रः ---
deva:
मर्कटेश महोत्साह सर्वशोकनिवारक ।
शत्रून् संहर मां रक्ष श्रियं दापय भो हरे ॥
iast:
markaṭeśa mahotsāha sarvaśokanivāraka |
śatrūn saṁhara māṁ rakṣa śriyaṁ dāpaya bho hare ||
en:
O lord of monkeys, of great vigor, remover of every sorrow — destroy my enemies, protect me, and grant me prosperity, O Hari.
tel:
మర్కటేశా, మహోత్సాహా, సర్వశోకనివారకా — శత్రువులను సంహరించు, నన్ను రక్షించు, సంపదను ప్రసాదించు, హరీ.
hi:
हे मर्कटेश, महोत्साही, समस्त शोक को दूर करने वाले — शत्रुओं का संहार करो, मेरी रक्षा करो, हे हरे, मुझे श्री (संपत्ति) प्रदान करो।

Colophon (unnumbered): iti vibhīṣaṇakṛtaṁ sarvāpaduddhāraka śrīhanumat stotram — "Thus ends the Śrī Hanumat Stotram, deliverer from every calamity, composed by Vibhīṣaṇa."
