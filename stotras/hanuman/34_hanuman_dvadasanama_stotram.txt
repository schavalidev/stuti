Title: Śrī Hanumad Dvādaśanāma Stotram
Devanāgarī: श्रीहनुमद्-द्वादशनामस्तोत्रम्
Telugu: శ్రీ హనుమద్ ద్వాదశనామ స్తోత్రం

Author: Traditional. The print gives **no Sanskrit colophon and no title**; the box simply ends, and the Hindi note below it closes with the bracketed source `[आनन्दरामायण]`. The work was located in that source and the attribution holds: it is **Ānanda Rāmāyaṇa, Manoharakāṇḍa, sarga 13, vv. 8–11**, where Śiva speaks it to Pārvatī.

Source / recension: Primary text: **Gītā Press, Gorakhpur — Śatanāma Stotra Saṅgraha**, code 1850, p. 68 (PDF p. 69), transcribed from the page images of the printed edition. It is **printed in a ruled box inside entry 11**, immediately after the colophon of the Hanumad Aṣṭottaraśatanāma Stotra, and is a separate work — it is not part of that stotra and is not in `hanuman/31_hanuman_ashtottarashatanama_stotram.txt`. **Two independent witnesses were collated.** First, the source itself: the **Ānanda Rāmāyaṇa with Rāmateja Pāṇḍeya's `Jyotsnā` Hindi commentary** (archive.org `srimad-ananda-ramayana`, OCR text), Manoharakāṇḍa sarga 13, vv. 8–11. It agrees with Gītā Press word for word in all four verses; where the OCR is broken the reading is still legible, and every word Gītā Press prints is present. Second, **sanskritdocuments.org `doc_hanumaana/Anjaneya12.itx`** (`Śrī Āñjaneya Dvādaśanāma Stotram`), a South Indian line of transmission, whose paired nāmāvalī `AnjaneyadvAdashanAmAvaliH.itx` is credited to the *Hanumatstutimañjari*, a Mahāperiyavāḷ publication. That witness differs in five places, listed below. These are genuinely separate lineages: a Gorakhpur reprint of a North Indian Purāṇic text, the Varanasi commentarial edition of the same Purāṇa, and a South Indian stuti collection. **GRETIL does not carry the Ānanda Rāmāyaṇa** (only the Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa), and a search of sa.wikisource returned nothing for this text.

Recension note: **Gītā Press followed, and the Ānanda Rāmāyaṇa confirms it at every disputed point.** Differences from the sanskritdocuments witness (Gītā Press → that witness): v. 1 `हनुमानञ्जनीसूनुः` → `हनुमान् अञ्जनासूनुः`, the mother's name as Añjanā rather than Añjanī and the compound left unjoined; v. 1 `फाल्गुनसखः` → `फल्गुनसखः`, a short first syllable; v. 2 `सीताशोकविनाशनः` → `सीताशोकविनाशकः`, and its nāmāvalī agrees with itself at `सीताशोकविनाशकाय`; v. 3 `एवं द्वादश नामानि` → `द्वादशैतानि नामानि`; v. 3 `स्वापकाले प्रबोधे च यात्राकाले च यः पठेत्` → `स्वापकाले पठेन्नित्यं यात्राकाले विशेषतः`, "let him recite it daily at the time of sleep, and above all when travelling"; v. 4 `तस्य सर्वभयं नास्ति रणे च विजयी भवेत्` → `तस्य मृत्युभयं नास्ति सर्वत्र विजयी भवेत्`, fear of death rather than fear of every kind. That witness also **stops there** and does not have the fourth verse's second line, `राजद्वारे गह्वरे च भयं नास्ति कदाचन`; its phalaśruti is three lines where Gītā Press has four. The Ānanda Rāmāyaṇa reads with Gītā Press in every one of these places, including the line the other witness lacks. **No verse numbers in the print.** The box gives the four verses unnumbered, with daṇḍas only; they are numbered 1–4 here, and are vv. 8–11 of the Purāṇa chapter. **The twelve names**, as the Hindi note itself counts them: हनुमान्, अञ्जनीसूनु, वायुपुत्र, महाबल, रामेष्ट, फाल्गुनसख, पिङ्गाक्ष, अमितविक्रम, उदधिक्रमण, सीताशोकविनाशन, लक्ष्मणप्राणदाता, दशग्रीवदर्पहा. Gītā Press prints **no nāmāvalī** for this text, so this file carries no name index; the sanskritdocuments nāmāvalī was used as a witness only. **The Hindi field comes from the print's own gloss.** Gītā Press sets a Hindi prose note below the box which names all twelve and explains several — `रामेष्ट` as "dear to Rāma", `फाल्गुनसख` as Arjuna's helper who dwells on his banner, `पिङ्गाक्ष` as "yellow-eyed", `लक्ष्मणप्राणदाता` as the one who brought the sañjīvanī herb and revived Lakṣmaṇa, and the last name as `रावणदर्पहारी`. Those glosses are followed here. **Cross-references.** `hanuman/31_hanuman_ashtottarashatanama_stotram.txt` is the entry this box sits inside, and is a different work. `hanuman/26_hanumat_kavacam_ananda_ramayane.txt` is the Hanumat Kavaca that **follows these verses in the same chapter** of the Ānanda Rāmāyaṇa; its viniyoga draws its bīja, śakti, kīlaka and mantra from this very list — `मारुतात्मजेति बीजं`, `अञ्जनीसूनुरिति शक्तिः`, `लक्ष्मणप्राणदातेति कीलकं`, `पिङ्गाक्षोऽमितविक्रम इति मन्त्रः`. The two works do not overlap in text, and neither contains the other.

Verse count: 4

Sections:
स्तोत्रम् — Stotram (blocks 1–2, the twelve names in the nominative)
फलश्रुतिः — Phalaśrutiḥ (blocks 3–4)

Blurb: Twelve names of Hanumān in two verses, and a promise in two more. The names hold his whole story in order — his mother, his father, his strength, Rāma's love for him, his place on Arjuna's banner, the leap across the ocean, the ending of Sītā's grief, the life given back to Lakṣmaṇa, the pride of Rāvaṇa broken. Whoever recites them on lying down, on waking and on setting out is promised freedom from all fear, victory in battle, and safety at the king's gate and in the deep forest alike.

--- verse 1 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
हनुमानञ्जनीसूनुर्वायुपुत्रो महाबलः ।
रामेष्टः फाल्गुनसखः पिङ्गाक्षोऽमितविक्रमः ॥
iast:
hanumānañjanīsūnurvāyuputro mahābalaḥ |
rāmeṣṭaḥ phālgunasakhaḥ piṅgākṣo'mitavikramaḥ ||
en: Hanumān, son of Añjanī, son of the wind, great in strength; beloved of Rāma, friend of Phālguna (Arjuna), tawny-eyed, of measureless valour.
tel: హనుమంతుడు, అంజనీసూనుడు, వాయుపుత్రుడు, మహాబలుడు; రామేష్టుడు, ఫాల్గునసఖుడు, పింగాక్షుడు, అమితవిక్రముడు.
hi: हनुमान्, अञ्जनीसूनु, वायुपुत्र, महाबल (महाबलवान्); रामेष्ट (रामके प्यारे), फाल्गुन (अर्जुन) के सखा, पिंगाक्ष (पीली आँखोंवाले), अमितविक्रम (अनन्त पराक्रमशाली)।

--- verse 2 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
उदधिक्रमणश्चैव सीताशोकविनाशनः ।
लक्ष्मणप्राणदाता च दशग्रीवस्य दर्पहा ॥
iast:
udadhikramaṇaścaiva sītāśokavināśanaḥ |
lakṣmaṇaprāṇadātā ca daśagrīvasya darpahā ||
en: He who leapt across the ocean, destroyer of Sītā's grief; giver of life to Lakṣmaṇa, and breaker of the pride of the ten-necked Rāvaṇa.
tel: ఉదధిక్రమణుడు, సీతాశోకవినాశనుడు; లక్ష్మణప్రాణదాత, దశగ్రీవుని దర్పమును హరించినవాడు.
hi: उदधिक्रमण (समुद्रको लाँघ जानेवाले), सीताशोकविनाशन (सीताके शोकका नाश करनेवाले); लक्ष्मणप्राणदाता (लक्ष्मणको संजीवनी बूटी लाकर जिलानेवाले) तथा दशग्रीव (रावण) के दर्पको हरनेवाले।

--- verse 3 | section: Phalaśrutiḥ ---
deva:
एवं द्वादश नामानि कपीन्द्रस्य महात्मनः ।
स्वापकाले प्रबोधे च यात्राकाले च यः पठेत् ॥
iast:
evaṁ dvādaśa nāmāni kapīndrasya mahātmanaḥ |
svāpakāle prabodhe ca yātrākāle ca yaḥ paṭhet ||
en: These are the twelve names of the great-souled lord of the monkeys. Whoever recites them at the time of sleep, on waking, and at the time of a journey —
tel: ఇవి మహాత్ముడైన కపీంద్రుని పండ్రెండు నామములు. నిద్రించు సమయమున, మేల్కొను సమయమున, ప్రయాణ సమయమున వీటిని పఠించువాడు —
hi: महात्मा कपीन्द्रके ये बारह नाम हैं। जो मनुष्य सोते समय, जागते समय और यात्रा करते समय इनका पाठ करता है —

--- verse 4 | section: Phalaśrutiḥ ---
deva:
तस्य सर्वभयं नास्ति रणे च विजयी भवेत् ।
राजद्वारे गह्वरे च भयं नास्ति कदाचन ॥
iast:
tasya sarvabhayaṁ nāsti raṇe ca vijayī bhavet |
rājadvāre gahvare ca bhayaṁ nāsti kadācana ||
en: — for him there is no fear of any kind, and he becomes victorious in battle. At the king's gate and in the deep forest he never has fear of any kind.
tel: — అతనికి ఏ విధమైన భయమూ ఉండదు, యుద్ధమునందు విజయము పొందును. రాజద్వారమునందును, గహనారణ్యమునందును అతనికి ఎప్పుడును ఏ విధమైన భయమూ ఉండదు.
hi: — उसे किसी प्रकारका भी भय नहीं होता और वह संग्राममें विजयी होता है। राजद्वार एवं गहन वनमें उसे कभी किसी प्रकारका भय नहीं रहता।
