Title: Śrī Ekamukha Hanumat Kavacam
Devanāgarī: श्री एकमुख हनुमत्कवचम्
Telugu: శ్రీ ఏకముఖ హనుమత్ కవచం

Author: Traditional; framed as a dialogue between Śiva (Īśvara) and Pārvatī, embedded in the Brahmāṇḍa Purāṇa (iti śrī brahmāṇḍapurāṇe śrī ekamukha hanumat kavacam), and said within the text to be the very kavaca that Rāma gave to Vibhīṣaṇa

Source / recension: stotranidhi.com — Devanāgarī recension at https://stotranidhi.com/hi/sri-ekamukha-hanumath-kavacham-in-sanskrit/, cross-checked against the IAST recension at https://stotranidhi.com/en/sri-ekamukha-hanumath-kavacham-in-english/ and the Telugu-script recension at https://stotranidhi.com/sri-ekamukha-hanumath-kavacham-in-telugu/. This is the single-faced (ekamukha) kavaca, distinct from stotranidhi's separate Pañcamukha, Saptamukha, and Ekādaśamukha Hanumat Kavacas. Stotranidhi publishes only the Sanskrit text (in five scripts) for this stotra without a prose meaning; the English, Telugu, and Hindi meanings below are original translations prepared for this corpus. To keep the file to a manageable number of entries, the sixteen dhyāna verses and the twenty-four-verse closing rakṣā-stotra (which protects the body limb by limb, in the same manner as the corpus's existing Pañcamukha Hanumat Kavacam) have each been grouped four verses to an entry rather than given one entry per verse; no verse numbers are altered or omitted in doing so, only combined for length.

Verse count: 23 entries. The source marks 24 units: 5 unmarked preliminary verses of frame-narrative + viniyoga + karanyāsa + aṅganyāsa + digbandha + 16 dhyāna verses + nyāsa + 7 mantras + 24-verse rakṣāstotram with its own internal numbering restarting at the mantra section). Presented here in 23 entries.

Sections:
भूमिका — the frame narrative in which Pārvatī asks Śiva for protection against fear and sorrow, and Śiva promises to teach her the very kavaca Rāma once gave to Vibhīṣaṇa (entry 1)
विनियोगः, करन्यासः, अङ्गन्यासः, दिग्बन्धः — the preliminary ṛṣyādi-viniyoga and ritual hand/body purification (entries 2–5)
ध्यानम् — sixteen verses visualizing Hanumān in his many epithets (entries 6–9)
न्यासः, मन्त्राः — a second nyāsa and seven tantric protection-mantras invoking Hanumān against bhūtas, grahas, and disease (entries 10–17)
रक्षास्तोत्रम् — the closing twenty-four-verse rakṣā portion, spoken by Śrī Rāmacandra, assigning a guardian epithet of Hanumān to each direction and each part of the body, closing with the phalaśruti on how to use the kavaca (entries 18–23)

Blurb: A Brahmāṇḍa Purāṇa kavaca cast as the very protective armor Rāma is said to have given Vibhīṣaṇa, built around the single-faced (as opposed to five-, seven-, or eleven-faced) form of Hanumān. After ritual hand and body purification it moves through sixteen richly pictorial dhyāna verses, then a set of fierce tantric mantras invoking Hanumān against bhūtas, piśācas, grahas, and fevers, before closing with a long rakṣā-stotra, spoken by Rāma himself, that stations a guardian form of Hanumān at every direction and every limb of the reciter's body.

--- verse 1 | section: भूमिका ---
deva:
एकदा सुखमासीनं शङ्करं लोकशङ्करम् ।
पप्रच्छ गिरिजाकान्तं कर्पूरधवलं शिवम् ॥
पार्वत्युवाच ।
भगवन् देवदेवेश लोकनाथ जगद्गुरो ।
शोकाकुलानां लोकानां केन रक्षा भवेद्ध्रुवम् ॥
सङ्ग्रामे सङ्कटे घोरे भूतप्रेतादिके भये ।
दुःखदावाग्निसन्तप्तचेतसां दुःखभागिनाम् ॥
ईश्वर उवाच ।
शृणु देवि प्रवक्ष्यामि लोकानां हितकाम्यया ।
विभीषणाय रामेण प्रेम्णा दत्तं च यत्पुरा ॥
कवचं कपिनाथस्य वायुपुत्रस्य धीमतः ।
गुह्यं ते सम्प्रवक्ष्यामि विशेषाच्छृणु सुन्दरि ॥

guhyaṁ te sampravakṣyāmi viśeṣācchṛṇu sundari ||
en: Once, as Śaṅkara — benefactor of all worlds, beloved of the mountain's daughter, camphor-white Śiva — sat at ease, Pārvatī asked him: "O Lord, God of gods, master of the worlds, guru of the universe, by what means can there be sure protection for beings afflicted with sorrow, in battle, in dreadful peril, in fear of bhūtas and pretas, for those whose minds are scorched by the forest-fire of grief and share in suffering?" Īśvara replied: "Listen, O Devī, I shall speak for the welfare of all beings — the kavaca of the wise lord of monkeys, son of the Wind, that was once lovingly given by Rāma to Vibhīṣaṇa. I shall tell you this secret specially — listen, O lovely one."

--- verse 2 | section: विनियोगः ---
deva:
अस्य श्रीहनुमत् कवचस्तोत्रमन्त्रस्य श्रीरामचन्द्र ऋषिः अनुष्टुप् छन्दः श्रीमहावीरो हनुमान् देवता, मारुतात्मज इति बीजं, ओं अञ्जनासूनुरिति शक्तिः, ओं ह्रैं ह्रां ह्रौं इति कवचं स्वाहा इति कीलकं लक्ष्मणप्राणदाता इति बीजं मम सकलकार्यसिद्ध्यर्थे जपे विनियोगः ॥

asya śrīhanumat kavacastotramantrasya śrīrāmacandra ṛṣiḥ anuṣṭup chandaḥ śrīmahāvīro hanumān devatā, mārutātmaja iti bījaṁ, oṁ añjanāsūnuriti śaktiḥ, oṁ hraiṁ hrāṁ hrauṁ iti kavacaṁ svāhā iti kīlakaṁ lakṣmaṇaprāṇadātā iti bījaṁ mama sakalakāryasiddhyarthe jape viniyogaḥ ||
en: For this hymn-mantra of the Śrī Hanumat Kavaca, the sage is Śrī Rāmacandra; the metre is anuṣṭubh; the deity is the great hero Śrī Hanumān; the seed (bīja) is "mārutātmaja"; the power (śakti) is "oṁ añjanāsūnave"; the armor (kavaca) is "oṁ hraiṁ hrāṁ hrauṁ"; the linchpin (kīlaka) is "svāhā"; the seed is "lakṣmaṇaprāṇadātā"; its application (viniyoga) in japa is for the accomplishment of all my undertakings.

--- verse 4 | section: अङ्गन्यासः ---
deva:
ओं अञ्जनासूनवे हृदयाय नमः ।
ओं रुद्रमूर्तये शिरसे स्वाहा ।
ओं वायुसुतात्मने शिखायै वषट् ।
ओं वज्रदेहाय कवचाय हुम् ।
ओं रामदूताय नेत्रत्रयाय वौषट् ।
ओं ब्रह्मास्त्रनिवारणाय अस्त्राय फट् ।
दिग्बन्धः –
ओं रामदूताय विद्महे कपिराजाय धीमहि । तन्नो हनुमान् प्रचोदयात् ॥
ओं हुं फट् स्वाहा । इति दिग्बन्धः ॥

oṁ vāyusutātmane śikhāyai vaṣaṭ |

en: This is the body-purification (aṅganyāsa): "Oṁ, to Añjanā's son, at the heart, salutation. Oṁ, to the form of Rudra, at the head, svāhā. Oṁ, to the self born of the Wind, at the crown-lock, vaṣaṭ. Oṁ, to the thunderbolt-bodied one, at the armor, hum. Oṁ, to Rāma's messenger, at the three eyes, vauṣaṭ. Oṁ, to the queller of the brahmāstra, at the weapon, phaṭ." Then the directional-binding (digbandha): "Oṁ, we know him as Rāma's messenger, we meditate on the king of monkeys; may that Hanumān inspire us." "Oṁ huṁ phaṭ svāhā" — thus the digbandha.

"ఓం హుం ఫట్ స్వాహా" — ఇది దిగ్బంధం.
hi: यह अंगन्यास है: "ॐ, अंजनासूनु को हृदय पर नमस्कार। ॐ, रुद्रमूर्ति को सिर पर स्वाहा। ॐ, वायुसुतात्मा को शिखा पर वषट्। ॐ, वज्रदेह को कवचस्थान पर हुं। ॐ, रामदूत को तीनों नेत्रों पर वौषट्। ॐ, ब्रह्मास्त्रनिवारक को अस्त्रस्थान पर फट्।" दिग्बंध: "ॐ, हम रामदूत को जानते हैं, कपिराज का ध्यान करते हैं; वह हनुमान हमें प्रेरित करें।" "ॐ हुं फट् स्वाहा" — यह दिग्बंध है।

en: In bondage by chains, in confinement in prison, in bodily paralysis, in burns by fire, and in terrible bodily disease; in grief, in great battle, and for the destruction of the graha caused by killing a brāhmaṇa — reciting it constantly and daily one attains victory, without doubt. A man may write it on birch-bark, on red cloth, on linen, or on a palm-leaf, using the three fragrant substances (sandalwood, saffron, camphor) or with ink, and wear it. Or, this auspicious kavaca, sealed in five-, seven-, or three-metal amulets and worn at the neck, waist, upper arm, throat, or head, grants all one's desires — this is the truth spoken by Śrī Rāma himself.
