Title: Hanumān Cālīsā (Gītā Press recension)
Devanāgarī: श्रीहनुमानचालीसा
Telugu: శ్రీహనుమాన్ చాలీసా

Author: Traditionally attributed to Gosvāmī Tulasīdāsa

Source / recension: **Gītā Press, Gorakhpur — Sundarkāṇḍa (Gītā Press code 1349), printed pages 119-123**, read from the volume's own embedded text layer (the typesetter's own characters, not OCR). That print is the sole witness for every deva: field below; no other edition was consulted for the text.

**This file is one of a pair, and neither supersedes the other.** `hanuman/02_hanuman_chalisa.txt` holds the same work in the Sanskritised recension that circulates widely on the web, transcribed from stotranidhi.com. The two are kept as separate files by the corpus's standing rule (see CLAUDE.md, "Never rewrite an existing file from a new witness"): they are a genuine recension difference, not one text and one set of errors, and collapsing either into the other would destroy the evidence. Read them side by side; the divergences are listed verse by verse in the Recension note below.

Note: Composed in Awadhi, not Sanskrit. The deva: fields transcribe the Gītā Press print character-for-character (spaces normalised, closing daṇḍas kept); the iast: fields are a mechanical transliteration of that same Awadhi text, not of a Sanskrit equivalent. Because the original is already a form of Hindi, the hi: field gives a modern-Hindi paraphrase rather than a further translation.

Verse count: 43 (2 opening dohās + 40 caupāī couplets + 1 closing dohā). The Gītā Press print carries **no verse numerals at all** — not on the dohās and not on the caupāīs — so the numbering 1-43 here is this corpus's own sequential numbering of the units the print sets off, not the print's. The "forty" of the title counts the caupāī couplets (verses 3-42), which is exactly 40; the three dohās are the frame and are not counted in it.

Sections:
Dohā — opening dohās (2)
Caupāī — the 40 caupāī couplets
Dohā — closing dohā (1)

Blurb: The single most widely recited Hanumān text in North India, composed as a chain of 40 rhymed Awadhi couplets bracketed by three dohās. It moves from Hanumān's parentage and physical form, through the Rāmāyaṇa deeds recalled as proof of his power, into a direct, almost conversational plea for strength, wisdom, and freedom from fear — which is precisely why it is chanted as much for protection and courage in daily life as for formal worship. The text as Gītā Press prints it is thoroughly Avadhi in its orthography (चरन, बरनउँ, जसु, लषन, बिद्या), and reading it in that form rather than in the Sanskritised spellings common on the web is a large part of hearing what Tulasīdāsa actually wrote.

--- verse 1 | section: Dohā ---
deva:
श्रीगुरु चरन सरोज रज निज मनु मुकुरु सुधारि।
बरनउँ रघुबर बिमल जसु जो दायकु फल चारि॥
iast:
śrīguru carana saroja raja nija manu mukuru sudhāri|
baranaum̐ raghubara bimala jasu jo dāyaku phala cāri||
en: Cleansing the mirror of my own mind with the dust of my Guru's lotus feet, I recount the spotless fame of Raghubara (Śrī Rāma), which is the bestower of the four fruits of human life — dharma, artha, kāma and mokṣa.
tel: నా గురువుగారి పాదపద్మాల ధూళితో నా మనసు అనే అద్దాన్ని శుభ్రపరచుకుని, ధర్మ అర్థ కామ మోక్షాలనే నాలుగు ఫలాలను ప్రసాదించే రఘువరుని (శ్రీరాముని) నిర్మలమైన కీర్తిని వర్ణిస్తున్నాను.
hi: अपने गुरु के चरण-कमलों की धूल से अपने मन के दर्पण को स्वच्छ करके, मैं रघुबर (श्रीराम) के उस निर्मल यश का वर्णन करता हूँ, जो धर्म, अर्थ, काम और मोक्ष — चारों फल देने वाला है।

--- verse 2 | section: Dohā ---
deva:
बुद्धिहीन तनु जानिके, सुमिरौं पवन-कुमार।
बल बुधि बिद्या देहु मोहिं, हरहु कलेस बिकार॥
iast:
buddhihīna tanu jānike, sumirauṁ pavana-kumāra|
bala budhi bidyā dehu mohiṁ, harahu kalesa bikāra||
en: Knowing this body of mine to be devoid of wisdom, I call to mind Pavana-kumāra (Hanumān, son of the Wind); grant me strength, intelligence and learning, and carry away my afflictions and my faults.
tel: నా శరీరం బుద్ధిహీనమని తెలుసుకుని, పవనకుమారుడైన హనుమంతుని స్మరిస్తున్నాను; నాకు బలం, బుద్ధి, విద్య ప్రసాదించి, నా క్లేశాలను వికారాలను హరించుము.
hi: अपने शरीर को बुद्धिहीन जानकर मैं पवन-कुमार हनुमान का स्मरण करता हूँ; मुझे बल, बुद्धि और विद्या दीजिए, और मेरे क्लेश तथा विकार हर लीजिए।

--- verse 3 | section: Caupāī ---
deva:
जय हनुमान ज्ञान गुन सागर।
जय कपीस तिहुँ लोक उजागर॥
iast:
jaya hanumāna jñāna guna sāgara|
jaya kapīsa tihum̐ loka ujāgara||
en: Victory to Hanumān, ocean of knowledge and of every virtue; victory to the lord of the monkeys, whose light shines out through all three worlds.
tel: జ్ఞానానికీ సద్గుణాలకూ సాగరమైన హనుమంతునికి జయము; మూడు లోకాలను ప్రకాశింపజేసే వానరప్రభువుకు జయము.
hi: ज्ञान और गुणों के सागर हनुमान की जय हो; तीनों लोकों को उजागर करने वाले कपीश की जय हो।

--- verse 4 | section: Caupāī ---
deva:
राम दूत अतुलित बल धामा।
अंजनि-पुत्र पवनसुत नामा॥
iast:
rāma dūta atulita bala dhāmā|
aṁjani-putra pavanasuta nāmā||
en: Messenger of Rāma, abode of strength that has no equal, known by the names Añjani-putra (son of Añjanī) and Pavanasuta (son of the Wind).
tel: రామదూతా, సాటిలేని బలానికి నిలయమా, అంజనీపుత్రుడు, పవనసుతుడు అనే పేర్లు కలవాడా.
hi: राम के दूत, अतुलित बल के धाम, अंजनि-पुत्र और पवनसुत नाम वाले।

--- verse 5 | section: Caupāī ---
deva:
महाबीर बिक्रम बजरंगी।
कुमति निवार सुमति के संगी॥
iast:
mahābīra bikrama bajaraṁgī|
kumati nivāra sumati ke saṁgī||
en: Great hero, valiant one, whose limbs are hard as the thunderbolt — you drive away crooked thinking and stand as the companion of right understanding.
tel: మహావీరుడా, పరాక్రమవంతుడా, వజ్రాంగుడా — దుర్బుద్ధిని తొలగించి సద్బుద్ధికి తోడుగా నిలిచేవాడా.
hi: महावीर, पराक्रमी, वज्र के समान अंगों वाले — कुबुद्धि को दूर करने वाले और सुबुद्धि के संगी।

--- verse 6 | section: Caupāī ---
deva:
कंचन बरन बिराज सुबेसा।
कानन कुंडल कुंचित केसा॥
iast:
kaṁcana barana birāja subesā|
kānana kuṁḍala kuṁcita kesā||
en: Golden of hue, resplendent in beautiful attire, with rings in your ears and hair falling in curls.
tel: బంగారు వర్ణంతో, అందమైన వేషంతో ప్రకాశిస్తూ, చెవులలో కుండలాలతో, ఉంగరాల జుట్టుతో ఉన్నవాడా.
hi: सुवर्ण के समान वर्ण वाले, सुंदर वेश में सुशोभित, कानों में कुंडल और घुंघराले केशों वाले।

--- verse 7 | section: Caupāī ---
deva:
हाथ बज्र औ ध्वजा बिराजै।
काँधे मूँज जनेऊ साजै॥
iast:
hātha bajra au dhvajā birājai|
kām̐dhe mūm̐ja janeū sājai||
en: In your hands the thunderbolt-mace and the banner shine out; across your shoulder the sacred thread of muñja grass is worn as an ornament.
tel: నీ చేతులలో వజ్రం (గద), ధ్వజం ప్రకాశిస్తున్నాయి; భుజంపై ముంజ గడ్డి యజ్ఞోపవీతం అలంకారంగా ఒప్పుతోంది.
hi: हाथ में बज्र (गदा) और ध्वजा शोभित हैं; कंधे पर मूँज का जनेऊ सुशोभित है।

--- verse 8 | section: Caupāī ---
deva:
संकर सुवन केसरीनंदन।
तेज प्रताप महा जग बंदन॥
iast:
saṁkara suvana kesarīnaṁdana|
teja pratāpa mahā jaga baṁdana||
en: Son of Śaṅkara, delight of Kesarī — your radiance and might are immense, and the whole world bows to you.
tel: శంకరుని కుమారుడా, కేసరీనందనుడా — నీ తేజస్సు, ప్రతాపము మహత్తరమైనవి, జగమంతా నిన్ను వందిస్తుంది.
hi: शंकर के पुत्र, केसरीनंदन — तुम्हारा तेज और प्रताप महान है, सारा जगत तुम्हारी वंदना करता है।

--- verse 9 | section: Caupāī ---
deva:
बिद्यावान गुनी अति चातुर।
राम काज करिबे को आतुर॥
iast:
bidyāvāna gunī ati cātura|
rāma kāja karibe ko ātura||
en: Deeply learned, full of virtue, exceedingly shrewd — and always impatient to be doing Rāma's work.
tel: విద్యావంతుడా, గుణవంతుడా, అత్యంత చతురుడా — రామకార్యాన్ని నెరవేర్చడానికి ఎప్పుడూ ఆతురత కలవాడా.
hi: विद्यावान, गुणी, अत्यंत चतुर — और राम का कार्य करने के लिए सदा आतुर।

--- verse 10 | section: Caupāī ---
deva:
प्रभु चरित्र सुनिबे को रसिया।
राम लषन सीता मन बसिया॥
iast:
prabhu caritra sunibe ko rasiyā|
rāma laṣana sītā mana basiyā||
en: One who takes delight in hearing the Lord's deeds recounted, with Rāma, Lakṣmaṇa and Sītā dwelling within your mind.
tel: ప్రభువు చరిత్రను వినడంలో రసికుడా — రామ, లక్ష్మణ, సీతలు నీ మనసులో నివసిస్తున్నారు.
hi: प्रभु का चरित्र सुनने में रसिक — राम, लक्ष्मण और सीता तुम्हारे मन में बसे हैं।

--- verse 11 | section: Caupāī ---
deva:
सूक्ष्म रूप धरि सियहिं दिखावा।
बिकट रूप धरि लंक जरावा॥
iast:
sūkṣma rūpa dhari siyahiṁ dikhāvā|
bikaṭa rūpa dhari laṁka jarāvā||
en: Taking on a minute form you showed yourself to Sītā; taking on a terrible form you burned Laṅkā down.
tel: సూక్ష్మరూపం ధరించి సీతకు దర్శనమిచ్చావు; వికట రూపం ధరించి లంకను దహించావు.
hi: सूक्ष्म रूप धारण कर सीता को दर्शन दिया; विकट रूप धारण कर लंका को जला दिया।

--- verse 12 | section: Caupāī ---
deva:
भीम रूप धरि असुर सँहारे।
रामचंद्र के काज सँवारे॥
iast:
bhīma rūpa dhari asura sam̐hāre|
rāmacaṁdra ke kāja sam̐vāre||
en: Taking on a fearsome form you destroyed the demons, and so set in order all the work of Rāmacandra.
tel: భీమరూపం ధరించి రాక్షసులను సంహరించి, రామచంద్రుని కార్యాలను చక్కబెట్టావు.
hi: भीम रूप धारण कर असुरों का संहार किया और रामचंद्र के कार्य सँवारे।

--- verse 13 | section: Caupāī ---

iast:
lāya sajīvana lakhana jiyāye|
śrīraghubīra haraṣi ura lāye||

Every deva: field is that print, character-for-character, with runs of justification spaces collapsed to one. It stands alongside `hanuman/02_hanuman_chalisa.txt`, which holds the Sanskritised recension from stotranidhi.com; neither file supersedes the other, and the differences between them are listed below. Variant at verse 34 (added 2026-09-12): the Gītā Press edition prints सदा रहो रघुपति के दासा, and that reading is kept as the base text here. The same half-line is also recited as सादर हो रघुपति के दासा, which divides the same syllables differently, and it is given in brackets. The witness for the bracketed reading is oral, not printed: it was supplied by the maintainer of this corpus from the recitation he was raised with (user, 2026-09-12). It is not a Gītā Press reading and has not been checked against any printed edition. That is recorded plainly here so that a later reader can weigh it for what it is. The same bracket stands at verse 34 of hanuman/02_hanuman_chalisa.txt.


**The divergence is systematic, not a scatter of typos.** stotranidhi prints a *Sanskritised* orthography of what is an Avadhi poem — restoring Sanskrit ṇ for Avadhi n, Sanskrit v for Avadhi b, Sanskrit ś for Avadhi s, Sanskrit kh for the print's ṣ, and Sanskrit lexis (यश, वरणौं) for Avadhi (जसु, बरनउँ). Gītā Press prints the Avadhi. Neither is a corruption of the other; they are two orthographic traditions for the same text, and the Gītā Press form is the one Tulasīdāsa's language actually supports. Per the corpus's sourcing rule Gītā Press is the authority, so its readings stand in the deva:/iast: fields throughout.

The recurring classes, with the stotranidhi reading given second: चरन / चरण; बरनउँ / वरणौं; जसु / यश (v1); बिद्या, बिमल, बिकट, बिक्रम, बिभीषन, कबि, कोबिद, बचन, बीरा, बलबीरा / विद्या, विमल, विकट, विक्रम, विभीषण, कवि, कोविद, वचन, वीरा, बलवीरा; कपीस, केसा, सुबेसा, संकर, मुनीसा, सारद, अहीसा, लंकेस्वर, पिसाच, सरना, गौरीसा / कपीश, केशा, सुवेशा, शंकर, मुनीशा, शारद, अहीशा, लंकेश्वर, पिशाच, शरणा, गौरीसा; गुन / गुण; बदन, जस / वदन, यश (v15); जम / यम; जुग, जोजन, परताप / युग, योजन, प्रताप; रच्छक / रक्षक; सर्ब / सर्वसुख; जनम / जन्म (v35); महाबीर / महावीर.

Individual places where the wording, not just the spelling, differs — Gītā Press first:
- v1: `निज मनु मुकुरु` / `निजमन मुकुर`; `दायकु` / `दायक`; `फल चारि` / `फलचारि`.
- v7: `औ` / `औरु`; `जनेऊ` / `जनेवू`.
- v10: `राम लषन सीता मन बसिया` / `राम लखन सीता मन बसिया`. The print's लषन (laṣana) for लखन is the notable one — it is the Avadhi/manuscript form and is kept here exactly as printed. It recurs at v43.
- v13: `सजीवन` / `संजीवन`; `उर लाये` / `वुर लाये` (the stotranidhi वुर looks like a straightforward slip for उर, but it is recorded, not corrected — this file simply follows the print).
- v14: `भरतहि सम भाई` / `भरत सम भायी`.
- v15: `गावैं` … `लगावैं` (plural/honorific) / `गावै` … `लगावै`.
- v19: `लंकेस्वर भए` ("became lord of Laṅkā") / `लंकेश्वर भय`. The stotranidhi भय is unmetrical and unmeaning here; the print's भए is plainly right, and the en/tel/hi were already reading it as "became", so the sense line is unchanged.
- v24: `डर ना` / `डरना`.
- v25: `सम्हारो` / `संहारो`. The print's सम्हारो means "you hold in check, you manage"; the stotranidhi संहारो would read as "destroy". This changes the sense, and the en/tel/hi have been rewritten to follow the print.
- v28: `संकट तें` / `संकटसे`.
- v30: `जो कोइ` / `जो कोयी`; **`सोइ अमित जीवन फल पावै` / `तासु अमित जीवन फल पावै`** — "that very one obtains" against "his boundless fruit"; en/tel/hi adjusted.
- v33: `नौ निधि` / `नव निधि`; **`अस बर दीन` / `असवर दीन्ह`** — the print separates अस बर ("such a boon"), which is what the line means; the stotranidhi असवर is a run-together of the same words.
- v36: **`अंत काल रघुबर पुर जाई` / `अंतकाल रघुपति पुर जायी`** — Raghubara for Raghupati; en/tel/hi adjusted. Also `जन्म` / `जन्मि`.
- v37: `धरई` … `सर्ब सुख करई` / `धरयी` … `सर्वसुखकरयी`.
- v38: **`संकट कटै` ("distress is cut away") / `संकट हरै` ("distress is removed")**; en/tel/hi adjusted to the print's verb.
- v39: `गोसाइं` … `नाइं` / `गोसायी` … `नायी`.
- v40: **`जो सत बार पाठ कर कोई` / `यह शतवार पाठ कर जोयी`** — same sense (a hundred recitations), different Avadhi wording; `होई` / `होयी`.
- v41: **the print reads `हनुमान चलीसा`, with चलीसा, not चालीसा**, though its own title on p. 119 is श्रीहनुमानचालीसा. This is kept exactly as printed and is not emended: चलीसा is a well-attested Avadhi shortening and may well be metrical rather than a defect, but this file does not have a second Gītā Press impression to check it against and so leaves the question open. `पढ़ै` / `पढै`.
- v42: `महँ` / `मह`.
- v43: `हरन` / `हरण`; `मंगल` / `मंगळ` (the stotranidhi ळ is a Dravidian-script convention, not Devanāgarī usage); the print sets the dohā as two lines with a caesura comma, where stotranidhi breaks each into two half-lines.

**Layout kept from the print.** The two opening dohās and the closing dohā are set here as two lines each, as Gītā Press sets them, rather than as the four half-lines of the previous version; the internal caesura is the print's own comma. The daṇḍas are the print's.

**Numbering.** The Gītā Press print carries no verse numerals anywhere in this text — not on the dohās, not on the caupāīs. The 1-43 numbering here is therefore this corpus's sequential numbering of the units the print sets off, retained unchanged from the earlier version of the file so that references to it do not break. It is not the print's numbering and should not be cited as such. The 40 of the title is the count of caupāī couplets (verses 3-42) and that count is exact; the three dohās are the frame.

**Not reproduced as units.** The print opens on p. 119 with the invocation `॥ श्रीहनूमते नमः॥` (note हनूमते with long ū) above the title, and closes on p. 123 with `॥ इति॥`. Both are rubric rather than text of the poem, and both are recorded here rather than given unit numbers, so that the file's unit count matches the earlier version.

**What was not done.** Only two witnesses were compared, and only one of them — the Gītā Press print — is authoritative. No manuscript, no critical edition, and no third printed edition was consulted, so the one open question above (चलीसा at v41) could not be settled. This is a two-witness collation, not a critical text.

Language: Awadhi. The text is Awadhi, the language in which Tulsīdās composed the poem, printed here in the Awadhi (tadbhava) spelling that the Gītā Press edition preserves. It is not Sanskrit, and it is not modern Hindi. The Sanskritised Hindi spelling of the same poem is held in this corpus at `hanuman/02_hanuman_chalisa.txt`. The two files hold the same poem in two spellings, and neither supersedes the other. A reader recites whichever one they learned. The difference is regular and runs right through the text: य becomes ज (यश → जस, यम → जम, युग → जुग), व becomes ब (विमल → बिमल, वचन → बचन, महावीर → महाबीर), श and ष become स (कलेश → कलेस, शंकर → संकर, कपीश → कपीस), ण becomes न (गुणी → गुनी), and conjuncts open out (प्रताप → परताप, जन्म → जनम, रक्षक → रच्छक). The `deva:` field carries the printed text exactly as it stands. The `iast:` field is a mechanical transliteration of it to corpus convention. The `hi:` field gives a plain modern-Hindi rendering of the sense, and `en:` and `tel:` do the same in English and Telugu. In transliteration ड़ and ढ़ are written ṛ and ṛh. The anusvāra before a stop is written ṁ (संकट → saṁkaṭa), following this file's own practice.
