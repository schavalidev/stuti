Title: Śrī Saṅkaṣṭamocana Hanumat Stotram (Śaṅkarācārya-kṛtam)
Devanāgarī: श्री सङ्कष्टमोचन हनुमत् स्तोत्रम् (शङ्कराचार्य कृतम्)

Author: Per the closing colophon, Śrī Maheśvarānanda Sarasvatī, Jagadguru Śaṅkarācārya Svāmi of the Kāśī Pīṭham ("kāśīpīṭhādhīśvara jagadguruśaṅkarācāryasvāmi śrīmaheśvarānandasarasvatī"). Blurb: This is an ornate hymn, dense with compounds, addressed to Hanumān as Saṅkaṣṭamocana, the remover of dire distress. It moves through his feats in the Rāmāyaṇa: the leap across the ocean, the burning of Laṅkā, the subduing of Kālanemi, and the comforting of Sītā. One notable verse recalls his presence upon Arjuna's chariot-banner in the Mahābhārata war. It then describes his blazing form, wielding the mace. It closes with a phalaśruti, which promises steady reciters relief from disease, from enemies, from poverty and from discord in marriage. A signature verse names its author.

en:
Radiant as a flood of vermilion, an ocean of strength and valor, a treasury of the power of intellect, of wondrous majestic glory; a wildfire consuming the pain of the wretched and afflicted, a granter of boons, most excellent — may the lord Saṅkaṣṭamocana, remover of dire distress, extend auspiciousness to us.

उदय होते सहस्र सूर्यों-सा शरीर, पीताम्बर से अलंकृत, प्रज्वलित अग्नि-सी नेत्रों वाले, राक्षस-समूह को कुचलने वाले; प्रलयकालीन मेघों-सी गर्जना वाले, गदा को ऊँचा घुमाने वाले — वे श्रीमान् मारुतनन्दन, विपत्तिभंजक, प्रतिदिन ध्यान करने योग्य हैं।

For men, praise of the lord Saṅkaṣṭamocana destroys the fear of demons and evil spirits, removes disease and violent fevers, slays their enemies, brings about wealth and children, and grants victory.
