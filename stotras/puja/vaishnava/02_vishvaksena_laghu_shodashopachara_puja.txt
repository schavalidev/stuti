Recension / paddhati: **Vaiṣṇava (Śrīvaiṣṇava), South Indian.** This is the Viṣvaksena pūjā to which `01_purvanga_vidhanam.txt` hands on at its close (`श्रीविष्वक्सेन पूजा`), and it is to be used after that file. The smārta rites are kept at `../smarta/` and **the two must never be merged, harmonised or collated against each other.** A user who follows the smārta rite should not use this file.

What marks it as Vaiṣṇava: Viṣvaksena is worshipped **with his consort Sūtravatī**, called into a **kūrca** set upon rice; the attendants of the elephant face are named as his; there is a service of the **ūrdhvapuṇḍra**, the upright mark; and the rite closes by asking the assembly that **no obstacle come to the good rite that follows**. It is a *laghu*, a short, pūjā: most services carry the nāma-mantra only.

Blurb: This is the short worship of Śrī Viṣvaksena, the commander of the Lord's hosts, in the Śrīvaiṣṇava tradition. It is done after the pūrvāṅga, before the worship of the Lord himself, so that no obstacle comes to that worship. In this tradition Viṣvaksena stands where Gaṇapati stands in the smārta rite. He is called, together with his consort Sūtravatī, into a bundle of darbha grass set upon rice, and the life-breath is placed in it. He is meditated upon as dark as a rain cloud, in yellow garments, holding the conch, the discus and the staff. The sixteen services are offered, most of them with his name alone. Among them is the upright mark worn by Vaiṣṇavas. He is worshipped with twenty-three names. At the close the assembly is asked to say that there may be no obstacle in the good rite that follows, and he is sent back to his place, to come again.

--- unit 29 | section: समर्पणम् ---
vidhi:
The dedication, and the asking of the assembly for a blessing on the rite that follows. This worship is done before another rite, and it asks that no obstacle come to that rite.
deva:
अनया श्री-विष्वक्सेन पूजया च भगवान् सर्वात्मकः श्री-विष्वक्सेनः सुप्रीतः सुप्रसन्नः वरदो भवन्तु ॥
उत्तरे शुभ-कर्मण्यविघ्नमस्तु इति भवन्तो ब्रुवन्तु ।
उत्तरे शुभ-कर्मणि अविघ्नमस्तु ॥
iast:
anayā śrī-viṣvaksena pūjayā ca bhagavān sarvātmakaḥ śrī-viṣvaksenaḥ suprītaḥ suprasannaḥ varado bhavantu ||
uttare śubha-karmaṇy-avighnam-astu iti bhavanto bruvantu |
uttare śubha-karmaṇi avighnam-astu ||
en: By this worship of Śrī Viṣvaksena may the Lord Śrī Viṣvaksena, who is the self of all, be well pleased, gracious, and a giver of boons. Please say, 'May there be no obstacle in the good rite that follows.' May there be no obstacle in the good rite that follows.
tel: ఈ శ్రీ విష్వక్సేన పూజచేత సర్వాత్మకుడైన భగవంతుడు శ్రీ విష్వక్సేనుడు సుప్రీతుడు, సుప్రసన్నుడు, వరదుడు అగుగాక. 'తరువాత చేయు శుభకర్మమునకు విఘ్నము లేకుండుగాక' అని మీరు పలుకుడు. తరువాత చేయు శుభకర్మమునకు విఘ్నము లేకుండుగాక.
