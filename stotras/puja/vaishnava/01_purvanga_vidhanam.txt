Devanāgarī: पूजाविधानम् — पूर्वाङ्गम् (वैष्णव पद्धतिः)
Telugu: పూజావిధానం — పూర్వాంగం (వైష్ణవ పద్ధతిః)
Shelves: none

Author: Anonymous (received Śrīvaiṣṇava household rite). No compiler is named. The frame is Śrīvaiṣṇava: the worshipper is located at Śrīraṅga, the work is declared to be `भगवत्कैङ्कर्यरूपम्` — service to the Lord — and the closing line hands on to the worship of Viṣvaksena.

Type: vidhi (ritual manual) — not a stotra. Uses the one-extra-field vidhi format documented in `../../vidhi/madhyandina/01_sandhyopasana_vidhi.txt`: a `vidhi:` field carrying the ritual instruction, then deva / iast / en / tel / hi.

Recension / paddhati: **Vaiṣṇava (Śrīvaiṣṇava), South Indian.** This is important and is not a formality. Pūjā-vidhāna divides by *paddhati* the way sandhyāvandana divides by śākhā, and the divisions are the observances of different communities rather than variant readings of one text. The smārta form is kept separately at `../smarta/` and **the two must never be merged, harmonised or collated against each other.** A user who follows the smārta rite should not use this file.

The differences are substantive, not cosmetic, and they are the reason for the separation:

- **Viṣvaksena, not Gaṇapati, removes obstacles.** The prārthanā names him — `विघ्नं निघ्नन्ति सततं विष्वक्सेनं तमाश्रये` — and the closing saṅkalpa hands on to `श्रीविष्वक्सेन पूजा`. The smārta file worships Mahāgaṇapati at the same point.
- **The ācamana opens differently.** Here it is Acyuta–Ananta–Govinda, then a run of twelve names ending at Dāmodara. The smārta form runs twenty-four names beginning at Keśava.
- **There is a four-syllable āsana** — `अं अनन्तासनाय`, `रं कूर्मासनाय`, `विं विमलासनाय`, `पं पद्मासनाय` — with its own viniyoga naming Śrīkūrma as the deity. The smārta form has no counterpart to this.
- **There is a `पवित्र धारणम्`** (`इदं ब्रह्म पुनीमहे`) and a `नमस्कारम्` built on the accented `नमः सदसे` passage, neither of which stands in the smārta pūrvāṅga.
- **The saṅkalpa is Śrīraṅga-centred** (`श्रीरङ्गस्य … दिक्प्रदेशे`) where the smārta is Śrīśaila-centred (`श्रीशैलस्य`), and it opens `श्रीगोविन्द गोविन्द गोविन्द` rather than with the smārta's `श्रीमहाविष्णोराज्ञया` alone. It also asks for release from the three afflictions — ādhyātmika, ādhidaivika, ādhibhautika — which the smārta form does not.
- Each mantra here carries an explicit **viniyoga** (ṛṣi, chandas, devatā, application) before it, in the manner of the Āśvalāyana rite recorded at `../../vidhi/rigveda/01_rigveda_sandhyavandanam.txt`. The smārta pūrvāṅga gives none.

Accent: **present, but only on the two Vedic passages** — the `नमः सदसे` namaskāra and the prāṇāyāma vyāhṛtis and Gāyatrī. The paurāṇika verses are printed unaccented, which is normal and is not a defect. Udātta `॑` (U+0951), anudātta `॒` (U+0952), dīrgha svarita `᳚` (U+1CDA). **No accent was inferred, derived or reconstructed** — every mark stands where the source put it, and the unaccented verses have been left unaccented rather than filled in from elsewhere.

Corrections applied to the base text, with reasons, because stotranidhi is not a critical edition:

1. **`धृवा` → `ध्रुवा`** in unit 8. The verse is Bhagavad Gītā 18.78, whose universally transmitted reading is `तत्र श्रीर्विजयो भूतिर्ध्रुवा नीतिर्मतिर्मम`. `धृवा` is not a Sanskrit form; the metathesis of `ru`/`ṛ` is a keying slip. The word-space in `विजयोभूतिर्` was also restored to `विजयो भूतिर्`.
2. **`पवित्रोवा` → `पवित्रो वा`** in unit 2, a missing word-space; the smārta page of the same site prints the space.

One normalisation, recorded because it is an editorial change and not a transcription: the source writes the praṇava-before-spirant as `ओग्‍ं`, with a zero-width joiner between `ग्` and the anusvāra. The ZWJ is a rendering hint and carries no phonetic distinction in Devanāgarī, and the corpus runs a zero-ZWJ cleanliness check, so it has been **stripped** and the form is written `ओग्ं` — which is what vignanam prints for the same sound and what `../smarta/01_nitya_puja_vidhanam.txt` carries. Note this is the opposite decision from `../../vidhi/taittiriya/01_krishna_yajurveda_sandhyavandanam.txt`, where the ZWJ is *preserved* — but there it sits in the `tel:` field, where Telugu script needs it to keep `ఓగ్‍ం` distinct from `ఓం`. In Devanāgarī the `ग्` is already explicit and the joiner does no work. **Note for the corpus-wide zero-ZWJ check: the two ZWJ this file contains are both in the paragraph above**, where the defective form is quoted so a future reader can recognise it; the rite's own text — every `deva:` field — carries none.

Not collated: the paurāṇika verses — `अपवित्रः पवित्रो वा`, `शुक्लाम्बरधरं विष्णुं`, `तदेव लग्नं`, `स्मृते सकलकल्याण`, `सर्वदा सर्वकार्येषु`, `आपदामपहर्तारं`. These are common to household manuals across traditions and no critical edition governs them.

Unit count: 19 units. Not a verse count in the stotra sense — the work is a prose order of service punctuated by mantras, and the numbering is this corpus's, not the source's. The text is a **pūrvāṅga**: it is the opening portion only, performed before the pūjā proper of whichever deity is to be worshipped, and it ends by handing on to the Viṣvaksena pūjā.

Blurb: This is the pūrvāṅga, that is, the opening portion, of the Śrīvaiṣṇava pūjā, in nineteen units. It is a genuinely different rite from the smārta pūrvāṅga kept at `../smarta/03_purvanga_vidhanam.txt`, and the two must not be mixed. Here Viṣvaksena, and not Gaṇapati, is the remover of obstacles. The ācamana opens upon a different triad of names. There is an āsana of four syllables, अं अनन्तासनाय, रं कूर्मासनाय, विं विमलासनाय and पं पद्मासनाय, which has no smārta counterpart. The saṅkalpa places the worshipper at Śrīraṅga, where the smārta text says Śrīśaila. The order of service runs from the maṅgalācaraṇa and purification, through the ācamana, the prayers and the salutation, to the putting on of the pavitra, the taking of the seat, prāṇāyāma and the saṅkalpa.
