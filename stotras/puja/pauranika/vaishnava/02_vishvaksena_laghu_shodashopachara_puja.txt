Blurb: The Vedic mantras are recited only by one for whom the upanayana saṁskāra has been performed. This form of the rite leaves them out, and where the rite needs a verse in their place, the Paurāṇika verse is given. The worship so performed is complete. The form with the Vedic mantras is kept separately, for those who have had the upanayana. This is the short worship of Śrī Viṣvaksena, the commander of the Lord's hosts, in the Śrīvaiṣṇava tradition. It is done after the pūrvāṅga, before the worship of the Lord himself, so that no obstacle comes to that worship. In this tradition Viṣvaksena stands where Gaṇapati stands in the smārta rite. He is called, together with his consort Sūtravatī, into a bundle of darbha grass set upon rice, and the life-breath is placed in it. He is meditated upon as dark as a rain cloud, in yellow garments, holding the conch, the discus and the staff. The sixteen services are offered, most of them with his name alone. Among them is the upright mark worn by Vaiṣṇavas. He is worshipped with twenty-three names. At the close the assembly is asked to say that there may be no obstacle in the good rite that follows, and he is sent back to his place, to come again.

--- unit 22 | section: उद्वासनम् ---
vidhi:
The Lord is sent back to his place, to return again.
deva:
श्रीमते विष्वक्सेनाय नमः ।
यथास्थानं उद्वासयामि ।
शोभनार्थे क्षेमाय पुनरागमनाय च ॥
iast:
śrīmate viṣvaksenāya namaḥ |
yathāsthānaṁ udvāsayāmi |
śobhanārthe kṣemāya punarāgamanāya ca ||
en: Salutation to the glorious Viṣvaksena. I send him back to his own place, for good, for well-being, and to come again.
tel: శ్రీమంతుడైన విష్వక్సేనునికి నమస్కారము. శుభము కొఱకును క్షేమము కొఱకును మరల వచ్చుట కొఱకును యథాస్థానమునకు ఉద్వాసన చేయుచున్నాను.
hi: श्रीमान् विष्वक्सेन को नमस्कार। शुभ के लिए, क्षेम के लिए और पुनः आगमन के लिए यथास्थान उद्वासन करता हूँ।
