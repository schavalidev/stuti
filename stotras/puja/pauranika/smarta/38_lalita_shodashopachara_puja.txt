Blurb: The Vedic mantras are recited only by one for whom the upanayana saṁskāra has been performed. This form of the rite leaves them out, and where the rite needs a verse in their place, the Paurāṇika verse is given. The worship so performed is complete. The form with the Vedic mantras is kept separately, for those who have had the upanayana. This is the worship of Śrī Lalitā, the supreme Goddess, in sixteen services. The worshipper first performs the pūrvāṅga and the Haridrā Gaṇapati pūjā. The life-breath is placed in the image, and Lalitā is meditated upon in three verses: rosy in colour, with eyes full of compassion, holding the noose, the goad, the arrows of flowers and the bow. Those verses describe each offering richly: a jewelled pavilion, water with dūrvā and flowers, garments of the colour of the rising sun, anklets and a crest-jewel, and a thousand lamps. The rite closes with the circumambulation, an offering of flowers in the joined hands, the royal services, a verse of meditation, the asking of pardon, and the receiving of the water from her feet.

tīrthaprasāda grahaṇam –
akālamṛtyuharaṇaṁ sarvavyādhinivāraṇam |
samastapāpakṣayakaraṁ śrī lalitā devī pādodakaṁ pāvanaṁ śubham ||
oṁ śrī lalitā devyai namaḥ prasādaṁ śirasā gṛhṇāmi |
itara śrī lalitā stotrāṇi paśyatu |
en: Receiving the water and the prasāda — It removes untimely death and wards off every disease. It destroys all sin. The water from the feet of Śrī Lalitā Devī is purifying and auspicious. Oṁ, salutation to Śrī Lalitā Devī. I receive the prasāda upon my head. See the other stotras of Śrī Lalitā.
tel: తీర్థప్రసాద గ్రహణము — అకాలమృత్యువును హరించునదియు, సర్వ వ్యాధులను నివారించునదియు, సమస్త పాపములను క్షయింపజేయునదియు, పవిత్రమును శుభమును అగు శ్రీ లలితా దేవి పాదోదకము. ఓం శ్రీ లలితా దేవికి నమస్కారము. ప్రసాదమును శిరస్సున గ్రహించుచున్నాను. ఇతర శ్రీ లలితా స్తోత్రములను చూడుము.
hi: तीर्थप्रसाद ग्रहण — अकालमृत्यु को हरने वाला, सब व्याधियों का निवारण करने वाला, समस्त पापों का क्षय करने वाला, पावन और शुभ श्री ललिता देवी का पादोदक। ओम् श्री ललिता देवी को नमस्कार। प्रसाद सिर पर ग्रहण करता हूँ। अन्य श्री ललिता स्तोत्र देखिए।
