Blurb: The Vedic mantras are recited only by one for whom the upanayana saṁskāra has been performed. This form of the rite leaves them out, and where the rite needs a verse in their place, the Paurāṇika verse is given. The worship so performed is complete. The form with the Vedic mantras is kept separately, for those who have had the upanayana. This is the worship of Śrī Vārāhī Devī, the boar-faced Mother-goddess, in sixteen services. The worshipper first performs the opening portion and the pūjā of Gaṇapati. In the second resolution he asks that all trouble from the inner enemies be quieted (the six inner enemies: desire, anger, greed, delusion, pride and envy), that every harm be turned away, and that every undertaking succeed. The life-breath is placed in the image, and the Goddess is meditated upon in two verses: dark as a rain-cloud, three-eyed, riding a horse, holding the sword, the shield, the noose and the goad. Among them are the offerings a married woman wears — saffron, collyrium, the thread of good fortune and turmeric. Her limbs are worshipped with fourteen names, and she is worshipped together with the sixteen powers who attend her. The rite closes with her own mantra at the mantra-flower, prayer, the royal services, the asking of pardon, the receiving of the water from her feet, and the leave-taking.

--- unit 39 | section: क्षमाप्रार्थना ---
deva:
अनया मया कृतेन ध्यान आवाहनादि षोडशोपचार पूजनेन भगवती सर्वात्मिका श्री वाराही देवी सुप्रीता सुप्रसन्ना वरदा भवन्तु ॥
iast:
anayā mayā kṛtena dhyāna āvāhanādi ṣoḍaśopacāra pūjanena bhagavatī sarvātmikā śrī vārāhī devī suprītā suprasannā varadā bhavantu ||
en: By this worship in sixteen services done by me, beginning with meditation and invocation, may Bhagavatī, the Self of all, Śrī Vārāhī Devī, be well pleased, gracious, and the giver of boons.
tel: నాచే చేయబడిన, ఈ ధ్యాన ఆవాహనాది షోడశోపచార పూజనముచే సర్వాత్మిక అయిన భగవతి శ్రీ వారాహీ దేవి సుప్రీతయు, సుప్రసన్నయు, వరదయు అగుగాక.
hi: मेरे द्वारा किए गए, इस ध्यान और आवाहन आदि षोडशोपचार पूजन से सर्वात्मिका भगवती श्री वाराही देवी सुप्रीता, सुप्रसन्ना और वरदा हों।

--- unit 40 | section: तीर्थप्रसाद ग्रहणम् ---
vidhi:
Receiving the water from the Goddess's feet and the food offered to her.
deva:
तीर्थप्रसाद ग्रहणम् –
अकालमृत्युहरणं सर्वव्याधिनिवारणम् ।
समस्तपापक्षयकरं मातृपादोदकं शुभम् ॥
ओं श्रीवाराही देव्यै नमः प्रसादं शिरसा गृह्णामि ।
iast:
tīrthaprasāda grahaṇam –
akālamṛtyuharaṇaṁ sarvavyādhinivāraṇam |
samastapāpakṣayakaraṁ mātṛpādodakaṁ śubham ||
oṁ śrīvārāhī devyai namaḥ prasādaṁ śirasā gṛhṇāmi |
en: Receiving the water and the prasāda — It removes untimely death and wards off every disease. It destroys all sin. The water from the Mother's feet is auspicious. Oṁ, salutation to Śrī Vārāhī Devī. I receive the prasāda upon my head.
tel: తీర్థప్రసాద గ్రహణము — అకాలమృత్యువును హరించునదియు, సర్వ వ్యాధులను నివారించునదియు, సమస్త పాపములను క్షయింపజేయునదియు అగు మాతృ పాదోదకము శుభమైనది. ఓం శ్రీ వారాహీ దేవికి నమస్కారము. ప్రసాదమును శిరస్సుతో గ్రహించుచున్నాను.
hi: तीर्थप्रसाद ग्रहण — अकालमृत्यु को हरने वाला, सब व्याधियों का निवारण करने वाला और समस्त पापों का क्षय करने वाला माता का पादोदक शुभ है। ओम् श्री वाराही देवी को नमस्कार। प्रसाद सिर से ग्रहण करता हूँ।

--- unit 41 | section: विसर्जनम् ---
vidhi:
The leave-taking. The Goddess is asked to return to her own place.
deva:
विसर्जनम् –
इदं पूजा मया देवि यथाशक्त्युपपादिताम् ।
रक्षार्थं त्वं समदाय व्रजस्थानमनुत्तमम् ॥
ओं श्रीवाराही देव्यै नमः यथास्थानमुद्वासयामि ।
iast:
visarjanam –
idaṁ pūjā mayā devi yathāśaktyupapāditām |
rakṣārthaṁ tvaṁ samadāya vrajasthānamanuttamam ||
oṁ śrīvārāhī devyai namaḥ yathāsthānamudvāsayāmi |
en: Leave-taking — This worship, O goddess, has been done by me as far as I was able. Taking it, go for our protection to your own supreme abode. Oṁ, salutation to Śrī Vārāhī Devī. I send her back to her own place.
tel: విసర్జనము — ఓ దేవీ, ఈ పూజ నాచే యథాశక్తిగా చేయబడినది. దీనిని స్వీకరించి, మా రక్షణ కొఱకు నీ ఉత్తమమైన స్థానమునకు వెళ్ళుము. ఓం శ్రీ వారాహీ దేవికి నమస్కారము. యథాస్థానమునకు ఉద్వాసన చేయుచున్నాను.
hi: विसर्जन — हे देवि, यह पूजा मेरे द्वारा यथाशक्ति की गई है। इसे लेकर हमारी रक्षा के लिए अपने उत्तम स्थान को जाइए। ओम् श्री वाराही देवी को नमस्कार। यथास्थान उद्वासन करता हूँ।
