Blurb: The Vedic mantras are recited only by one for whom the upanayana saṁskāra has been performed. This form of the rite leaves them out, and where the rite needs a verse in their place, the Paurāṇika verse is given. The worship so performed is complete. The form with the Vedic mantras is kept separately, for those who have had the upanayana. This is the worship of Śrī Gāyatrī Devī in sixteen services. It has no opening portion of its own. The worshipper first performs the opening portion and the worship of Gaṇapati, and then makes the resolution again before the image. The goddess is meditated upon in four verses: as Brahmāṇī at dawn, as Rudrāṇī at midday, as Sarasvatī at evening, and with five faces. She is asked to remain in the image. Each service is offered with a verse of praise to the Goddess. The rite closes with circumambulation, the handful of flowers, the royal services, salutation, the asking of pardon, and the receiving of the water from her feet.

--- unit 1 | section: पुनः सङ्कल्पः ---
vidhi:
The pūrvāṅga and the Gaṇapati pūjā are done first. Then the worshipper makes the resolution again, before the image.
deva:
पुनः सङ्कल्पम् –
पूर्वोक्त एवं गुण विशेषण विशिष्टायां शुभ तिथौ श्री गायत्री देवतामुद्दिश्य श्री गायत्री देवता प्रीत्यर्थं सम्भवद्भिः द्रव्यैः सम्भवद्भिः उपचारैश्च सम्भविता नियमेन सम्भविता प्रकारेण यावच्छक्ति ध्यानावाहनादि षोडशोपचार पूजां करिष्ये ॥
iast:
punaḥ saṅkalpam –
pūrvokta evaṁ guṇa viśeṣaṇa viśiṣṭāyāṁ śubha tithau śrī gāyatrī devatāmuddiśya śrī gāyatrī devatā prītyarthaṁ sambhavadbhiḥ dravyaiḥ sambhavadbhiḥ upacāraiśca sambhavitā niyamena sambhavitā prakāreṇa yāvacchakti dhyānāvāhanādi ṣoḍaśopacāra pūjāṁ kariṣye ||
en: The second resolution — On this auspicious day, marked by the qualities already named, with Śrī Gāyatrī Devī as the one to whom it is offered, and for the pleasing of the deity Śrī Gāyatrī, with such substances as are to be had, with such services as are to be had, with such observance and in such manner as is possible, I shall perform to the best of my power the worship in sixteen services, beginning with meditation and invocation.
tel: పునః సంకల్పము — ఇంతకు ముందు చెప్పిన గుణవిశేషణములతో కూడిన ఈ శుభ తిథియందు, శ్రీ గాయత్రీ దేవతను ఉద్దేశించి, శ్రీ గాయత్రీ దేవత ప్రీతి కొఱకు, లభించిన ద్రవ్యములతోను, లభించిన ఉపచారములతోను, సాధ్యమైన నియమముతోను, సాధ్యమైన రీతిలోను, యథాశక్తిగా ధ్యానము ఆవాహనము మొదలైన షోడశోపచార పూజను చేయుచున్నాను.
hi: पुनः सङ्कल्प — पूर्वोक्त गुण विशेषणों से युक्त इस शुभ तिथि में, श्री गायत्री देवता के उद्देश्य से, श्री गायत्री देवता की प्रीति के लिए, जो द्रव्य उपलब्ध हों उनसे, जो उपचार उपलब्ध हों उनसे, सम्भव नियम से और सम्भव प्रकार से, यथाशक्ति ध्यान और आवाहन आदि षोडशोपचार पूजा करूँगा।

vidhi:
The first of four meditations: Brahmāṇī, the goddess of the morning.
deva:
ध्यानम् –
ब्रह्माणी चतुराननाक्षवलयं कुम्भं करैः स्रुक्स्रवौ
बिभ्राणा त्वरुणेन्दुकान्तिवदना ऋग्रूपिणी बालिका ।

en: Meditation — May Gāyatrī, meditated upon as Brahmāṇī, be always for our wealth and increase: she who has four faces; who holds in her hands the rosary, the water-pot, and the two sacrificial ladles; whose face shines like the red dawn and the moon; whose form is the Ṛgveda; a young girl; adorned, and honoured by the sound of the jewels that ring as she rides the swan in play.

khaṭvāṅgatriśikhākṣasūtravalayā'bhītiḥ śriyai cāstu naḥ |

en: Sāvitrī is to be meditated upon as Rudrāṇī: in fresh youth, three-eyed, clothed in the skin of a tiger, holding the skull-staff, the trident and the rosary, with bracelets and the gesture of fearlessness — may she be for our good fortune. vidhi:
The fourth meditation: the goddess with five faces.
deva:
मुक्ताविद्रुमहेमनीलधवलच्छायैर्मुखैस्त्रीक्षणैः
युक्तामिन्दुनिबद्धरत्नमकुटां तत्त्वार्थवर्णात्मिकाम् ।
गायत्रीं वरदाभयाङ्कुशकशाः शुभ्रं कपालं गदां
शङ्खं चक्रमथारविन्दयुगलं हस्तैर्वहन्तीं भजे ॥
ओं श्री गायत्री देव्यै नमः ध्यायामि ।
iast:
muktāvidrumahemanīladhavalacchāyairmukhaistrīkṣaṇaiḥ
yuktāmindunibaddharatnamakuṭāṁ tattvārthavarṇātmikām |
gāyatrīṁ varadābhayāṅkuśakaśāḥ śubhraṁ kapālaṁ gadāṁ
śaṅkhaṁ cakramathāravindayugalaṁ hastairvahantīṁ bhaje ||
oṁ śrī gāyatrī devyai namaḥ dhyāyāmi |

en: Asking pardon — Thousands of offences are done by me day and night. Thinking of me as your servant, forgive me, O supreme goddess. I do not know how to invoke you, and I do not know how to take leave of you. I do not know the rule of worship. Forgive me, O supreme goddess. Lacking in mantra, lacking in rite, lacking in devotion, O great goddess — whatever has been worshipped by me, O goddess, let it be complete. vidhi:
Receiving the water from the goddess's feet and the food offered to her.
deva:
तीर्थप्रसाद ग्रहणम् –
अकालमृत्युहरणं सर्वव्याधिनिवारणम् ।
समस्तपापक्षयकरं श्री गायत्री देवी पादोदकं पावनं शुभम् ॥
ओं श्री गायत्री देव्यै नमः प्रसादं शिरसा गृह्णामि ।
iast:

oṁ śrī gāyatrī devyai namaḥ prasādaṁ śirasā gṛhṇāmi |
en: Receiving the water and the prasāda — It takes away untimely death and wards off every disease. It destroys all sin. The water from the feet of Śrī Gāyatrī Devī is purifying and auspicious. Oṁ, salutation to Śrī Gāyatrī Devī. I receive the prasāda upon my head.
