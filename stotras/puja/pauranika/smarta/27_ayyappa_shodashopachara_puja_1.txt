Blurb: The Vedic mantras are recited only by one for whom the upanayana saṁskāra has been performed. This form of the rite leaves them out, and where the rite needs a verse in their place, the Paurāṇika verse is given. The worship so performed is complete. The form with the Vedic mantras is kept separately, for those who have had the upanayana. This is the worship of Śrī Ayyappa, Hariharaputra, the son of Hari and Hara, together with his consorts Pūrṇā and Puṣkalā, in sixteen services. It is the longer of two forms of his worship kept in this collection. The life-breath is placed in the image, and the Lord is meditated upon in two verses, dark and tender with a red lotus in his hand, and seated upon an elephant with the flower-arrow and the sugar-cane bow. His limbs are worshipped under twenty-nine names. After the salutations come the verses of the Śāstā Pañcaratnam, each followed by the call for refuge, and the calls of the Śabarimala pilgrims, called and answered, in Malayalam and Tamil. The rite closes with a song of auspiciousness, a prayer in Tamil for pardon, the asking of pardon, and the Lord's return to his place.

hi: क्षमाप्रार्थना — जिनके स्मरण और नामोच्चारण से तप, पूजा, क्रिया आदि में जो कमी है वह तत्काल पूर्ण हो जाती है — उन अच्युत की मैं वन्दना करता हूँ। हे हरात्मज, मन्त्रहीन, क्रियाहीन और भक्तिहीन होते हुए भी, हे देव, मेरे द्वारा की गई पूजा आपके लिए परिपूर्ण हो। इस ध्यान-आवाहन आदि षोडशोपचार पूजा से सर्वात्मा भगवान्, हरिहरपुत्र श्री अय्यप्प स्वामी सुप्रीत, सुप्रसन्न और वरद हों। श्री अय्यप्प स्वामी का प्रसाद सिर से ग्रहण करता हूँ।

deva:
उद्वासनम् –
ओं श्री-हरिहर-पुत्र अय्यप्प स्वामिनं यथास्थानं प्रवेशयामि ।
iast:
udvāsanam –
oṁ śrī-harihara-putra ayyappa svāminaṁ yathāsthānaṁ praveśayāmi |
en: Returning the Lord to his place — Oṁ, I return Śrī Hariharaputra Ayyappa Svāmī to his own place.
tel: ఉద్వాసనము — ఓం శ్రీ హరిహరపుత్ర అయ్యప్ప స్వామిని యథాస్థానమునకు ప్రవేశింపజేయుచున్నాను.
hi: उद्वासन — ओम्, श्री हरिहरपुत्र अय्यप्प स्वामी को यथास्थान प्रवेश कराता हूँ।

--- unit 37 | section: उद्वासनम् ---
vidhi:
The rite closes with the dedication.
deva:
हरिवरासनम् – (रात्रि पूजा अनन्तरं)
हरिवरासनं पश्यतु ॥
सर्वं श्री अय्यप्प-स्वामि पादार्पणमस्तु ।
iast:
harivarāsanam – (rātri pūjā anantaraṁ)
harivarāsanaṁ paśyatu ||
sarvaṁ śrī ayyappa-svāmi pādārpaṇam-astu |
en: Harivarāsanam — (after the night worship). See the Harivarāsanam. Let all this be an offering at the feet of Śrī Ayyappa Svāmī.
tel: హరివరాసనము — (రాత్రి పూజ తరువాత). హరివరాసనమును చూడుము. సర్వము శ్రీ అయ్యప్ప స్వామి పాదములకు అర్పణమగుగాక.
hi: हरिवरासनम् — (रात्रि पूजा के पश्चात्)। हरिवरासनम् देखिए। यह सब श्री अय्यप्प स्वामी के चरणों में अर्पित हो।
