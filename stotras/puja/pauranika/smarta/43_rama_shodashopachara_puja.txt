Blurb: The Vedic mantras are recited only by one for whom the upanayana saṁskāra has been performed. This form of the rite leaves them out, and where the rite needs a verse in their place, the Paurāṇika verse is given. The worship so performed is complete. The form with the Vedic mantras is kept separately, for those who have had the upanayana. This is the worship of Śrī Rāma, together with Sītā, in sixteen services. The worshipper first performs the opening portion and the worship of Gaṇapati in turmeric, to which the rite sends him. He then makes a second resolution for the fulfilment of his wishes. Rāma is invited to come with Jānakī and to remain present while the worship lasts, and he is meditated upon in three verses: seated in the hero's posture with the gesture of knowledge and Sītā at his side; seated in the jewelled pavilion beneath the wish-granting tree, while Hanumān reads before him and he teaches the sages; and dark, two-armed, clothed in yellow, with eyes like red lotus petals. Each of the services is offered with a verse naming Rāma. His limbs are worshipped from the feet to the head, and the hundred and eight names of Rāma and of Sītā are recited. The rite closes with the royal services, the prayer to take refuge at the feet of Rāma, the asking of pardon, and the receiving of the water from his feet.

hi: ध्यान — श्याम मेघ की कान्ति से सुन्दर, सदा वीरासन में स्थित, एक हाथ में ज्ञानमुद्रा धारण किए और दूसरे करकमल को घुटने पर रखे हुए, पास में स्थित, हाथ में कमल लिए, बिजली के समान सीता को देखते हुए, मुकुट, अंगद आदि अनेक आभूषणों से उज्ज्वल अंगों वाले राघव का मैं भजन करता हूँ। (1) कल्पवृक्ष के नीचे स्वर्ण के महामण्डप में, मध्य में मणिमय पुष्पक आसन पर वैदेही के साथ वीरासन में सुस्थित, सामने पवनपुत्र के पढ़ते हुए मुनियों को परम तत्त्व की व्याख्या करते हुए, भरत आदि से घिरे श्यामवर्ण राम का मैं भजन करता हूँ। (2) लाल कमल की पंखुड़ियों के समान सुन्दर नेत्रों वाले, पीताम्बर से अलंकृत, श्याम अंगों वाले, दो भुजाओं वाले, प्रसन्न मुख वाले, श्री सीता से शोभित, करुणा रूपी अमृत के सागर, प्रिय जनों और भाइयों से सेवित, विष्णु, शिव आदि से सदा सेवनीय, और भक्तों की इष्टसिद्धि देने वाले राम की मैं वन्दना करता हूँ। (3) ओम् रां राम को नमस्कार। ध्यान करता हूँ। ध्यान समर्पित करता हूँ।

अनया ध्यान आवाहनादि षोडशोपचार पूजया भगवान् सर्वात्मकः श्री जानकी सहित श्री रामचन्द्र स्वामि सुप्रीता सुप्रसन्ना वरदा भवन्तु ॥

aparādha sahasrāṇi kriyante'harniśaṁ mayā |

anayā dhyāna āvāhanādi ṣoḍaśopacāra pūjayā bhagavān sarvātmakaḥ śrī jānakī sahita śrī rāmacandra svāmi suprītā suprasannā varadā bhavantu ||
en: Asking pardon — Thousands of faults are committed by me day and night; thinking of me as your servant, forgive me, O Puruṣottama. I do not know how to invoke, I do not know how to send away, I do not know the rule of worship; forgive me, O Puruṣottama. O Janārdana, O god, whatever I have worshipped, lacking in mantra, lacking in rite, lacking in devotion, let it be complete for you. By this worship of sixteen services, beginning with meditation and invocation, may the Lord, the Self of all, Śrī Rāmacandra, together with Śrī Jānakī, be well pleased, gracious, and the giver of boons.

vidhi:
The water from the Lord's feet is received and sipped, and the prasāda is placed on the head.
deva:
तीर्थप्रसाद ग्रहणम् –
अकालमृत्युहरणं सर्वव्याधिनिवारणम् ।
समस्तपापक्षयकरं श्री रामचन्द्र पादोदकं पावनं शुभम् ॥
ओं रां रामाय नमः प्रसादं शिरसा गृह्णामि ।
iast:
tīrthaprasāda grahaṇam –
akālamṛtyuharaṇaṁ sarvavyādhinivāraṇam |
samastapāpakṣayakaraṁ śrī rāmacandra pādodakaṁ pāvanaṁ śubham ||
oṁ rāṁ rāmāya namaḥ prasādaṁ śirasā gṛhṇāmi |
en: Receiving the holy water and the prasāda — The water from the feet of Śrī Rāmacandra takes away untimely death, removes every illness, and destroys all sins; it is purifying and auspicious. Oṁ rāṁ, salutation to Rāma. I receive the prasāda on my head.
tel: తీర్థప్రసాద గ్రహణము — శ్రీ రామచంద్రుని పాదోదకము అకాలమృత్యువును హరించునది, సర్వవ్యాధులను నివారించునది, సమస్త పాపములను క్షయింపజేయునది, పావనమైనది, శుభమైనది. ఓం రాం రామునికి నమస్కారము. ప్రసాదమును శిరస్సుతో స్వీకరించుచున్నాను.
