Blurb: The Vedic mantras are recited only by one for whom the upanayana saṁskāra has been performed. This form of the rite leaves them out, and where the rite needs a verse in their place, the Paurāṇika verse is given. The worship so performed is complete. The form with the Vedic mantras is kept separately, for those who have had the upanayana. This is the worship of Śrī Śyāmalā Devī, the Goddess Mātaṅgī, in sixteen services. The worshipper first performs the opening portion and the pūjā of Gaṇapati. In the second resolution he asks that faults such as the stopping of speech be removed and that his power of intellect grow. The life-breath is then placed in the image, and the Goddess is meditated upon as she sits on a jewelled seat, dark, playing the vīṇā and listening to her parrot.

tel: క్షమా ప్రార్థన — రాత్రింబవళ్ళు నాచే వేలకొలది అపరాధములు చేయబడుచున్నవి. ఇతడు దాసుడు అని నన్ను భావించి క్షమింపుము, ఓ పరమేశ్వరీ. ఆవాహనము ఎఱుగను, విసర్జనము ఎఱుగను. పూజా విధిని ఎఱుగను. క్షమింపుము, ఓ పరమేశ్వరీ. మంత్రహీనమును, క్రియాహీనమును, భక్తిహీనమును, ఓ మహేశ్వరీ — నాచే పూజింపబడినది, ఓ దేవీ, నీకు పరిపూర్ణమగుగాక.

deva:

--- unit 31 | section: तीर्थप्रसाद ग्रहणम् ---
vidhi:
Receiving the water from the Goddess's feet and the food offered to her.
deva:
तीर्थप्रसाद ग्रहणम् –
अकालमृत्युहरणं सर्वव्याधिनिवारणम् ।
समस्तपापक्षयकरं मातृपादोदकं शुभम् ॥

iast:
tīrthaprasāda grahaṇam –
akālamṛtyuharaṇaṁ sarvavyādhinivāraṇam |

en: Receiving the water and the prasāda — It removes untimely death and wards off every disease. It destroys all sin. The water from the Mother's feet is auspicious. Oṁ, salutation to Śrī Śyāmalā Devī. I receive the prasāda upon my head.

hi: तीर्थप्रसाद ग्रहण — अकालमृत्यु को हरने वाला, सब व्याधियों का निवारण करने वाला और समस्त पापों का क्षय करने वाला माता का पादोदक शुभ है। ओम् श्री श्यामला देवी को नमस्कार। प्रसाद सिर से ग्रहण करता हूँ।

--- unit 32 | section: विसर्जनम् ---
vidhi:

deva:

en: Leave-taking — This worship, O goddess, has been done by me as far as I was able. Taking it, go for our protection to your own supreme abode. Oṁ, salutation to Śrī Śyāmalā Devī. I send her back to her own place.
