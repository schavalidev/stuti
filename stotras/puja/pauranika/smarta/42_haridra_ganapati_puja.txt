Blurb: The Vedic mantras are recited only by one for whom the upanayana saṁskāra has been performed. This form of the rite leaves them out, and where the rite needs a verse in their place, the Paurāṇika verse is given. The worship so performed is complete. The form with the Vedic mantras is kept separately, for those who have had the upanayana. Before a larger worship or a vrata is begun, Gaṇapati is first worshipped in a small image shaped from turmeric paste, so that what follows may be free of obstacles. This is that short worship. The reciter first performs the opening portion. The Lord is then invoked into the turmeric, the life-breath is placed in it, and he is meditated upon as golden like turmeric, four-armed, holding the noose, the goad, a sweet and his tusk. The sixteen services follow, most of them brief. At the flowers he is worshipped by his sixteen names, and at the mantra-flower the same sixteen names are recited as a verse, with the promise that no obstacle arises for one who reads or hears them at the beginning of study, at a marriage, on entering or leaving a place, or in any undertaking. The rite closes with circumambulation, the asking of pardon, the receiving of the water from his feet, and the farewell.

hi: उद्वासन — ओम् श्री महागणपति को नमस्कार; उन्हें उनके स्थान को विदा करता हूँ। शुभ के लिए, क्षेम के लिए और पुनः आगमन के लिए।
