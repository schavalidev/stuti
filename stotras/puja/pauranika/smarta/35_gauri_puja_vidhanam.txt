Blurb: The Vedic mantras are recited only by one for whom the upanayana saṁskāra has been performed. This form of the rite leaves them out, and where the rite needs a verse in their place, the Paurāṇika verse is given. The worship so performed is complete. The form with the Vedic mantras is kept separately, for those who have had the upanayana. This is the worship of Śrī Mahāgaurī in sixteen services. It has no opening portion of its own. The worshipper first performs the opening portion and the worship of Gaṇapati, and then makes the resolution again, for the fulfilment of the wishes of the mind. Gaurī is invoked into the image with her household, and meditated upon together with the Mothers. Among the services are those a married woman wears: the thread of good fortune, turmeric, saffron, vermilion and collyrium. At the flowers, Gaurī and the Mothers are worshipped by twenty-four names. The rite closes with the mantra-flower, circumambulation, salutation, the asking of pardon, an offering of water to the gracious goddess, prayer, the royal services, and leave-taking.

--- unit 1 | section: पुनः सङ्कल्पः ---
vidhi:
The pūrvāṅga and the Gaṇapati pūjā are done first. Then the worshipper makes the resolution again, before the image.

पुनः सङ्कल्पम् –
पूर्वोक्त एवं गुण विशेषण विशिष्टायां शुभतिथौ मम मनोवाञ्छाफल सिद्ध्यर्थं श्री गौरी देवतामुद्दिश्य श्री गौरी देवता प्रीत्यर्थं यावच्छक्ति ध्यानावाहनादि षोडशोपचार पूजां करिष्ये ।

en: The second resolution — On this auspicious day, marked by the qualities already named, for the fulfilment of the wishes of my mind, with the deity Śrī Gaurī as the one to whom it is offered, and for the pleasing of the deity Śrī Gaurī, I shall perform to the best of my power the worship in sixteen services, beginning with meditation and invocation.

sthiro bhava varado bhava suprasanno bhava sthirāsanaṁ kuru |
en: Placing of the life-breath — Śrī Mahāgaurī, with her limbs, with her weapons, with her mount, with her power, together with her husband, her children and her household — the deity Śrī Mahāgaurī I invoke, I establish, I worship. Be steady, be the giver of boons, be gracious, take a firm seat.

समर्पणम् –
मन्त्रहीनं क्रियाहीनं भक्तिहीनं सुरेश्वरि ।
यत्पूजितं मया देवि परिपूर्णं तदस्तु मे ॥
अनया ध्यानावहनादि षोडशोपचार पूजया भगवती सर्वदेवात्मिका श्रीमहागौरी सुप्रीता सुप्रसन्ना वरदा भवतु ॥
iast:
samarpaṇam –
mantrahīnaṁ kriyāhīnaṁ bhaktihīnaṁ sureśvari |
yatpūjitaṁ mayā devi paripūrṇaṁ tadastu me ||
anayā dhyānāvahanādi ṣoḍaśopacāra pūjayā bhagavatī sarvadevātmikā śrīmahāgaurī suprītā suprasannā varadā bhavatu ||
en: Offering — Lacking in mantra, lacking in rite, lacking in devotion, O queen of the gods — whatever has been worshipped by me, O goddess, let it be complete for me. By this worship in sixteen services, beginning with meditation and invocation, may Bhagavatī Śrī Mahāgaurī, the Self of all the gods, be well pleased, gracious, and the giver of boons.
tel: సమర్పణము — మంత్రహీనమైనను, క్రియాహీనమైనను, భక్తిహీనమైనను, ఓ సురేశ్వరీ, నాచే పూజింపబడినది, ఓ దేవీ, నాకు పరిపూర్ణమగుగాక. ఈ ధ్యాన ఆవాహనాది షోడశోపచార పూజచే సర్వదేవాత్మిక అయిన భగవతి శ్రీ మహాగౌరి సుప్రీతయు సుప్రసన్నయు వరదయు అగుగాక.
hi: समर्पण — मन्त्रहीन, क्रियाहीन, भक्तिहीन, हे सुरेश्वरि — जो कुछ मेरे द्वारा पूजित हुआ, हे देवि, वह मेरे लिए परिपूर्ण हो। इस ध्यान और आवाहन आदि षोडशोपचार पूजा से सब देवताओं की आत्मा भगवती श्री महागौरी सुप्रीता, सुप्रसन्ना और वरदा हों।

vidhi:
The goddess is given leave to return to her place, and all is offered at her feet.
deva:
उद्वासनम् –
यान्तुदेवगणाः सर्वे पूजामादाय मामकीम् ।
इष्टकामसमृद्ध्यर्थं पुनरागमनाय च ॥
श्रीमहागौरीं यथास्थानमुद्वासयामि ।
शोभनार्थं पुनरागमनाय च ॥
सर्वं श्रीमहागौरी देवता चरणारविन्दार्पणमस्तु ।
iast:
udvāsanam –
yāntudevagaṇāḥ sarve pūjāmādāya māmakīm |
iṣṭakāmasamṛddhyarthaṁ punarāgamanāya ca ||
śrīmahāgaurīṁ yathāsthānamudvāsayāmi |
śobhanārthaṁ punarāgamanāya ca ||
sarvaṁ śrīmahāgaurī devatā caraṇāravindārpaṇamastu |
en: Leave-taking — May all the hosts of the gods depart, taking my worship with them, for the fulfilment of what is desired and to come again. I return Śrī Mahāgaurī to her own place, for good and to come again. Let all be an offering at the lotus feet of the deity Śrī Mahāgaurī.
tel: ఉద్వాసనము — దేవగణములు అన్నియు నా పూజను తీసుకొని, కోరిన కోరికల సమృద్ధి కొఱకు, మరల వచ్చుట కొఱకు వెళ్ళుదురుగాక. శుభము కొఱకు, మరల వచ్చుట కొఱకు శ్రీ మహాగౌరిని యథాస్థానమునకు ఉద్వాసన చేయుచున్నాను. సర్వము శ్రీ మహాగౌరీ దేవత పాదపద్మములకు అర్పణమగుగాక.
hi: उद्वासन — सब देवगण मेरी पूजा लेकर, इच्छित कामनाओं की समृद्धि के लिए और फिर आने के लिए प्रस्थान करें। शुभ के लिए और फिर आने के लिए श्री महागौरी को उनके स्थान पर विदा करता हूँ। सब कुछ श्री महागौरी देवता के चरणकमलों में अर्पित हो।
