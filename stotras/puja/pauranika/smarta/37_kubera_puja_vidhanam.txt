Blurb: The Vedic mantras are recited only by one for whom the upanayana saṁskāra has been performed. This form of the rite leaves them out, and where the rite needs a verse in their place, the Paurāṇika verse is given. The worship so performed is complete. The form with the Vedic mantras is kept separately, for those who have had the upanayana. This is the worship of Śrī Kubera, the lord of wealth and king of the yakṣas, in sixteen services. The worshipper first performs the pūrvāṅga and a short worship of Mahāgaṇapati. In the second resolution he asks for the welfare of his whole family: long life and health, victory and freedom from fear, the growth of children and grandchildren, and wealth, gold, vehicles and cattle. Kubera is called into the image with his limbs, his weapons, his mount and his attendants. He is meditated upon as seated on a man-drawn chariot, adorned with a crown, the friend of Śiva, holding the mace and giving boons. The mantra-flower carries Kubera's own mantras. The rite closes with prayer, the royal services, the asking of pardon, and the receiving of the water from the feet of Lakṣmī and Kubera.

anayā dhyāna āvāhanādi ṣoḍaśopacāra pūjanena bhagavān sarvātmakaḥ śrī kubera svāmi suprīto suprasanno varado bhavantu ||
en: By this worship in sixteen services, beginning with meditation and invocation, may the Lord, the Self of all, Śrī Kubera, be well pleased, gracious, and the giver of boons.
tel: ఈ ధ్యాన ఆవాహనాది షోడశోపచార పూజనముచే సర్వాత్మకుడైన భగవంతుడు శ్రీ కుబేర స్వామి సుప్రీతుడును సుప్రసన్నుడును వరదుడును అగుగాక.
hi: इस ध्यान और आवाहन आदि षोडशोपचार पूजन से सर्वात्मक भगवान् श्री कुबेर स्वामी सुप्रीत, सुप्रसन्न और वरद हों।

--- unit 31 | section: तीर्थप्रसाद ग्रहणम् ---
vidhi:
Receiving the water from the feet and the food offered.
deva:
तीर्थप्रसाद ग्रहणम् –
अकालमृत्युहरणं सर्वव्याधिनिवारणम् ।
समस्तपापक्षयकरं श्री लक्ष्मी कुबेर पादोदकं पावनं शुभम् ॥
श्री कुबेर स्वामि प्रसादं शिरसा गृह्णामि ।
iast:
tīrthaprasāda grahaṇam –
akālamṛtyuharaṇaṁ sarvavyādhinivāraṇam |
samastapāpakṣayakaraṁ śrī lakṣmī kubera pādodakaṁ pāvanaṁ śubham ||
śrī kubera svāmi prasādaṁ śirasā gṛhṇāmi |
en: Receiving the water and the prasāda — It removes untimely death and wards off every disease. It destroys all sin. The water from the feet of Śrī Lakṣmī and Kubera is purifying and auspicious. I receive upon my head the prasāda of Śrī Kubera, the lord.
tel: తీర్థప్రసాద గ్రహణము — అకాలమృత్యువును హరించునదియు, సర్వ వ్యాధులను నివారించునదియు, సమస్త పాపములను క్షయింపజేయునదియు, పవిత్రమును శుభమును అగు శ్రీ లక్ష్మీ కుబేరుల పాదోదకము. శ్రీ కుబేర స్వామి ప్రసాదమును శిరస్సున గ్రహించుచున్నాను.
hi: तीर्थप्रसाद ग्रहण — अकालमृत्यु को हरने वाला, सब व्याधियों का निवारण करने वाला, समस्त पापों का क्षय करने वाला, पावन और शुभ श्री लक्ष्मी कुबेर का पादोदक। श्री कुबेर स्वामी का प्रसाद सिर पर ग्रहण करता हूँ।
