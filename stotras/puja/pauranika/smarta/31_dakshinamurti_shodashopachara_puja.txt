Blurb: The Vedic mantras are recited only by one for whom the upanayana saṁskāra has been performed. This form of the rite leaves them out, and where the rite needs a verse in their place, the Paurāṇika verse is given. The worship so performed is complete. The form with the Vedic mantras is kept separately, for those who have had the upanayana. This is the worship of Śrī Dakṣiṇāmūrti, the Lord Śiva as the teacher who faces south, in sixteen services. The worshipper asks for the growth of intelligence and wisdom, and through them for the knowledge of Brahman. The worshipper first performs the pūrvāṅga and the short Mahāgaṇapati pūjā. Dakṣiṇāmūrti is meditated upon seated on the seat of teaching, pale with ashes, holding the gesture of knowledge, the rosary, the vīṇā and the book, served by the sages. Each service has its own verse addressed to the south-facing one. The rite closes with the handful of flowers, the circumambulation, a prayer, the asking of pardon, and the receiving of the water from his feet.

samastapāpakṣayakaraṁ śrīdakṣiṇāmūrti pādodakaṁ pāvanaṁ śubham ||
en: The holy water — It removes untimely death and wards off every disease. It destroys all sin. The water from the feet of Śrī Dakṣiṇāmūrti is purifying and auspicious.
tel: తీర్థము — అకాలమృత్యువును హరించునదియు, సర్వ వ్యాధులను నివారించునదియు, సమస్త పాపములను క్షయింపజేయునదియు, పవిత్రమును శుభమును అగు శ్రీ దక్షిణామూర్తి పాదోదకము.
hi: तीर्थ — अकालमृत्यु को हरने वाला, सब व्याधियों का निवारण करने वाला, समस्त पापों का क्षय करने वाला, पावन और शुभ श्री दक्षिणामूर्ति का पादोदक।
