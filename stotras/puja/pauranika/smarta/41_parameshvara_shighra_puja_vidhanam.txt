Blurb: The Vedic mantras are recited only by one for whom the upanayana saṁskāra has been performed. This form of the rite leaves them out, and where the rite needs a verse in their place, the Paurāṇika verse is given. The worship so performed is complete. The form with the Vedic mantras is kept separately, for those who have had the upanayana. This is a short worship of Śiva, the supreme lord, for a day when there is little time. It carries its own opening portion. The worshipper purifies himself, prays to the remover of obstacles and to the teacher, sips water with the names of Viṣṇu, lights the lamp, restrains the breath and makes a short resolution. He prays over the water-vessel and to Gaṇapati. Śiva is meditated upon in two verses, as the five-faced lord and as the lord of Umā, and invoked into the image. Six services follow, each offered with the five-syllable mantra: the bath, sandal paste, a flower, incense, the lamp and food. The rite closes with salutation and the offering of its fruit to the lord.

oṁ namaḥ śivāya mantrapuṣpa sahita namaskāraṁ samarpayāmi |
en: Salutation — Oṁ, salutation to Śiva. I offer salutation together with the mantra-flower.
tel: నమస్కారము — ఓం నమః శివాయ. మంత్రపుష్ప సహిత నమస్కారమును సమర్పించుచున్నాను.
hi: नमस्कार — ओम् नमः शिवाय। मन्त्रपुष्प सहित नमस्कार समर्पित करता हूँ।

--- unit 19 | section: समर्पणम् ---
vidhi:
The dedication: pardon is asked for what was lacking, and the fruit of the worship is offered to Parameśvara.
deva:
समर्पणम् –
मन्त्रहीनं क्रियाहीनं भक्तिहीनं महेश्वर ।
यत्पूजितं मयादेव परिपूर्णं तदस्तुते ॥
एतत्फलं श्री परमेश्वरार्पणमस्तु ।
स्वस्ति ॥
iast:
samarpaṇam –
mantrahīnaṁ kriyāhīnaṁ bhaktihīnaṁ maheśvara |
yatpūjitaṁ mayādeva paripūrṇaṁ tadastute ||
etatphalaṁ śrī parameśvarārpaṇamastu |
svasti ||
en: Dedication — Lacking in mantra, lacking in rite, lacking in devotion, O Maheśvara: whatever I have worshipped, O god, let it be made complete for you. Let this fruit be offered to Śrī Parameśvara. Well-being.
tel: సమర్పణము — ఓ మహేశ్వరా, ఓ దేవా, మంత్రహీనముగా, క్రియాహీనముగా, భక్తిహీనముగా నేను ఏది పూజించితినో అది నీకు పరిపూర్ణమగుగాక. ఈ ఫలము శ్రీ పరమేశ్వరునకు అర్పితమగుగాక. స్వస్తి.
hi: समर्पण — हे महेश्वर, हे देव, मन्त्रहीन, क्रियाहीन, भक्तिहीन जो कुछ मैंने पूजा है, वह आपके लिए परिपूर्ण हो। यह फल श्री परमेश्वर को अर्पित हो। स्वस्ति।
