Blurb: The Vedic mantras are recited only by one for whom the upanayana saṁskāra has been performed. This form of the rite leaves them out, and where the rite needs a verse in their place, the Paurāṇika verse is given. The worship so performed is complete. The form with the Vedic mantras is kept separately, for those who have had the upanayana. This is the worship of Śrī Sūryanārāyaṇa in sixteen services, offered to him together with Uṣā, Padminī and Chāyā. It is a rite resolved upon for health: the second resolution names the illnesses to be removed one by one, and the salutation near the close asks the sun to take away the disease of the heart. The life-breath is placed in the image, and the Lord is meditated upon in three verses — within the disc of the sun as Nārāyaṇa with conch and discus, then as red Aruṇa upon the red lotus, then simply as Ravi drawn by seven horses held with seven reins. His limbs are worshipped under twenty names, and he is then worshipped under the twelve names of the twelve months. At the close, water is offered twelve times over, once to each of those twelve names, in a golden vessel and reddened with saffron.

en: The second resolution — On this auspicious day, marked by the qualities already named: for the removal of all the illnesses now present in my body and of those that may yet come, arising from wind, bile and phlegm and produced by many causes — fever, wasting, jaundice, skin disease, colic, dysentery, the wasting of the tissues, sores, urinary disease, fistula and the rest; for the removal of all sin, including the killing of a living being and the killing of a brāhmaṇa; for the swift attainment of health in the body; and for the gaining of the favour of the supreme Brahman, Śrī Sūryanārāyaṇa Svāmī together with Śrī Uṣā, Padminī and Chāyā — he whose self is Hari, Hara and Brahmā, the lord of the twelve names beginning with Mitra, the lord of the twelve months beginning with Aruṇa, attended by twelve encirclings, whose form is the threefold Veda — addressing the deity Śrī Sūryanārāyaṇa Svāmī, with such substances as are to be had, with such observance as is possible and in such manner as is possible, I shall perform to the best of my power the worship in sixteen services, beginning with meditation and invocation, according to the rule of the Puruṣa Sūkta. vidhi:
Receiving the water from the Lord's feet and the food offered to him, and the closing peace.
deva:
तीर्थप्रसाद ग्रहणम् –
अकालमृत्युहरणम् सर्वव्याधिनिवारणम् ।
समस्तपापक्षयकरं श्री सूर्यनारायण पादोदकं पावनं शुभम् ॥
ओं श्री सूर्यनारायणाय नमः प्रसादं शिरसा गृह्णामि ।
iast:
tīrthaprasāda grahaṇam –
akālamṛtyuharaṇam sarvavyādhinivāraṇam |
samastapāpakṣayakaraṁ śrī sūryanārāyaṇa pādodakaṁ pāvanaṁ śubham ||
oṁ śrī sūryanārāyaṇāya namaḥ prasādaṁ śirasā gṛhṇāmi |
en: Receiving the water and the prasāda — It removes untimely death and wards off every disease. It destroys all sin. The water from the feet of Śrī Sūryanārāyaṇa is purifying and auspicious. Oṁ, salutation to Śrī Sūryanārāyaṇa. I receive the prasāda upon my head.
tel: తీర్థప్రసాద గ్రహణము — అకాలమృత్యువును హరించునదియు, సర్వ వ్యాధులను నివారించునదియు, సమస్త పాపములను క్షయింపజేయునదియు, పవిత్రమును శుభమును అగు శ్రీ సూర్యనారాయణ పాదోదకము. ఓం శ్రీ సూర్యనారాయణునికి నమస్కారము. ప్రసాదమును శిరస్సున గ్రహించుచున్నాను.
