Blurb: The Vedic mantras are recited only by one for whom the upanayana saṁskāra has been performed. This form of the rite leaves them out, and where the rite needs a verse in their place, the Paurāṇika verse is given. The worship so performed is complete. The form with the Vedic mantras is kept separately, for those who have had the upanayana. This is the daily pūjā of Sarasvatī in the smārta form. After the materials and the opening acts, the worshipper honours the water-vessel, the conch and the bell, and worships Gaṇapati first so that obstacles may be removed. The sixteen services follow, among them the worship of the goddess limb by limb under thirteen names, and a sacred thread offered with a verse that calls her the very self of the sound-Brahman. It closes with the asking of pardon, the dismissal, and the receiving of the prasāda.

--- unit 27 | section: सरस्वती देवी ध्यानम् ---

oṁ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ || oṁ tatsat brahmārpaṇamastu ||
en: The water from the deity's feet is auspicious: it takes away untimely death, wards off every disease, and destroys all sin. I receive the prasāda of the goddess Sarasvatī upon my head. Oṁ, peace, peace, peace. Oṁ, that is the truth: let this be an offering to Brahman.
