Devanāgarī: पुरुषसूक्त सहित श्रीसूक्त विधान पूजा — पौराणिक पद्धतिः

Telugu: పురుషసూక్త సహిత శ్రీసూక్త విధాన పూజ — పౌరాణిక పద్ధతి

Author: Anonymous (received smārta household rite). No compiler is named and none should be inferred.

Type: vidhi (ritual manual) — not a stotra. The one-extra-field vidhi format: a `vidhi:` field carrying the ritual instruction, then deva / iast / en / tel / hi.

Recension / paddhati: **Smārta, South Indian.** The Vaiṣṇava form of the opening is kept separately at `../../vaishnava/01_purvanga_vidhanam.txt` and **the two must never be merged, harmonised or collated against each other.**

Note: **This is the Vedic-removed form, for a reciter for whom the upanayana saṁskāra has not been performed. The Vedic form is `../../smarta/48_purushasukta_sahita_srisukta_vidhana_puja.txt`, kept unchanged for the initiated. Neither file supersedes the other, and neither is ever to be deleted in favour of the other** (user, 2026-09-17). Built on 2026-09-17 from the same declared unit plan as the Vedic form, not by `../../bin/pauranika_puja.py`; not one Sanskrit word was composed. Every Vedic mantra was removed with its translation: both ṛcs at each of the sixteen services, the seven abhiṣeka mantras, the Gāyatrī over the food, the sprinkling `सत्यं त्वा ऋतेन`, `अमृतमस्तु`, `अमृतोपस्तरणमसि` and `अमृतापिधानमसि`, the prāṇāhuti (removed without substitute, on the user's decision of 2026-09-17), and the closing `ओं शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः`. In the closing dedication the words `पुरुषसूक्त सहित श्रीसूक्त विधान पूर्वक` were removed, because the rite so performed does not follow that rule; nothing was put in their place.

**Unlike `18`, `19` and `22`, this page gives no Paurāṇika verse beside its ṛcs**, so removing them left every service with its name-offering alone. 21 such services were given the verse Gītā Press prints for the same service (see below); the 2 others — the water for the hands, which Gītā Press gives only with the name Śambhu, and the water of fruits, for which it gives none — are offered with the name alone, as Gītā Press directs.

**The gender of the verses is a decision for review.** Gītā Press gives one masculine set of these verses, worked as a Śiva-pūjā, and says a goddess takes the feminine. This page is written for no single deity: its offerings leave the name blank, and its dedication names both a Lord and a goddess. The masculine set was used, because it is the form Gītā Press gives for any deity not a goddess and because this page's own verses of pardon address the deity as `परमेश्वर`, `महेश्वर` and `देव`. The vocatives affected are `देव` (units 2, 8), `परमेश्वर` (units 6, 15, 18), `सुरश्रेष्ठ` (unit 16), `देवेश` (unit 20) and `वरदो` (unit 24). A worshipper of a goddess would say `देवि`, `परमेश्वरि`, `सुरश्रेष्ठे`, `देवेशि` and `वरदा`, as Gītā Press's own Durgā-pūjā does. The Śiva set's arghya verse names Śambhu (`गृहाण भगवन् शम्भो`) and was not used.

Source / recension: the Vedic form named above, for every retained word of the page. Substitutes: Gītā Press, Gorakhpur, Nitya Karma Pūjā Prakāśa (code 592), section `सर्वसामान्य देवी-देव-पूजाका विधान`, its `शिव-पूजा` (PDF pp. 141–145 of archive.org `gp0592-nitya-karma-puja-prakasha`): the verses for `आवाहन`, `आसन`, `पाद्य`, `आचमन`, `दुग्धस्नान`, `दधिस्नान`, `घृतस्नान`, `मधुस्नान`, `शर्करास्नान`, `शुद्धोदकस्नान`, `वस्त्र`, `यज्ञोपवीत`, `चन्दन`, `आभूषण`, `पुष्पमाला`, `धूप`, `दीप`, `नैवेद्य`, `ताम्बूल`, `आरती` and `मन्त्रपुष्पाञ्जलि`. Each was lifted from the archive's OCR of that volume and checked against the page image; the OCR matched the page in 17 of the 21 verses, and was corrected from the image in four: the incense verse, whose first line the OCR garbled and whose `सर्वदेवानां` it dropped; the pure-water verse, where it ran the heading into the text and read `गृह्याताम्`; `तोयमाचमनीयार्थं`, read without its anusvāra; and `भक्त्या` in the mantra-flower verse, read as `भवत्या`. The only changes to Gītā Press's text are the dropping of its exclamation marks after `भगवन्`, `देव` and `देवेश`, and `॥` for its `।।`. The service for the milk bath uses `दुग्धस्नान`, for ghee `घृतस्नान`, and for the bath in pure water `शुद्धोदकस्नान`. Offering with the name alone where no verse is given is the rule of the same section: `केवल नाममन्त्रसे … 'नैवेद्य' आदि चढ़ाना चाहिये`.

Blurb: The Vedic mantras are recited only by one for whom the upanayana saṁskāra has been performed. This form of the rite leaves them out, and where the rite needs a verse in their place, the Paurāṇika verse is given. The worship so performed is complete. The form with the Vedic mantras is kept separately, for those who have had the upanayana. This is a worship in sixteen services. The rite is not written for one deity. The name of the deity is said at each offering, and the closing prayer names both the Lord and the goddess. The bath is given in full: milk, curd, ghee, honey and sugar each with its own verse, then the water of fruits, then pure water. The worship ends with the mantra-flower, circumambulation, the royal services, a prayer for pardon, and the receiving of the water and the prasāda. The opening portion is not included, and is performed first.
