Blurb: The Vedic mantras are recited only by one for whom the upanayana saṁskāra has been performed. This form of the rite leaves them out, and where the rite needs a verse in their place, the Paurāṇika verse is given. The worship so performed is complete. The form with the Vedic mantras is kept separately, for those who have had the upanayana. This is the worship of Śrī Bālā Tripurasundarī, the Goddess in the form of a young girl, in sixteen services. The worshipper first performs the pūrvāṅga and the turmeric Gaṇapati pūjā. Bālā is invoked into the image and meditated upon as a flame held within her seed-syllables, dressed in gold, holding a book, a noose, a goad and a rosary. Among the services are offerings a married woman makes to the Goddess: turmeric and kuṅkuma, a bodice, and the thread of good fortune. The mantra-flower is offered with Bālā's own mantra. The rite closes with a prayer that Bālā may dwell in the heart, the asking of pardon, and the receiving of the water from her feet and of the food offered to her.

dhyānam –

vande pustakapāśasāṅkuśajapasragbhāsurodyatkarāṁ
tāṁ bālāṁ tripurāṁ parātparakalāṁ ṣaṭcakrasañcāriṇīm ||
oṁ śrībālātripurasundaryai namaḥ dhyāyāmi |
en: Meditation — I salute her, Bālā Tripurā, the power beyond the highest, who moves through the six centres: a flame of fire held within the seat of the syllable aiṁ, bearing the syllables sauḥ and klīṁ; wearing a golden garment; bright in limbs washed with the best nectar; her raised hands shining with a book, a noose, a goad and a rosary. tel: ధ్యానము — ఐంకారమను ఆసనమునందు గర్భితమైన అగ్నిశిఖయు, సౌః క్లీం అను కళలను ధరించునదియు, బంగారు వస్త్రమును ధరించునదియు, శ్రేష్ఠమైన అమృతముచే కడుగబడిన అంగములతో ప్రకాశించునదియు, పుస్తకము పాశము అంకుశము జపమాలలతో వెలుగొందు ఎత్తిన హస్తములు కలదియు, పరాత్పర కళయు, షట్చక్రములయందు సంచరించునదియు అగు ఆ బాలా త్రిపురకు నమస్కరించుచున్నాను. ఓం శ్రీ బాలా త్రిపురసుందరికి నమస్కారము. ధ్యానించుచున్నాను.
