Title: Veda Mantra Vikalpa — Alternatives to the Vedic Mantras in the Pūjā Vidhānas
Devanāgarī: वेद मन्त्र विकल्पः — पूजाविधानेषु वैदिकमन्त्राणां स्थाने पाठ्यानि
Telugu: వేద మంత్ర వికల్పం — పూజా విధానాలలో వైదిక మంత్రాల స్థానంలో చదువుకోవలసినవి

Author: Traditional. No composer is named for this arrangement, and none should be inferred. The verses gathered here are the received Paurāṇika ślokas of the pūjā manuals.

Type: vidhi (ritual manual) — not a stotra. Uses the one-extra-field vidhi format of `../smarta/01_nitya_puja_vidhanam.txt`: a `vidhi:` field carrying the ritual instruction, then deva / iast / en / tel / hi.

Blurb: The orders of worship in this collection carry Vedic mantras at several points — the prāṇāyāma, the invocation of Gaṇapati, the bath, the offering of flowers, and the sūktas. By long-standing rule these Vedic mantras are recited only by one for whom the upanayana saṁskāra has been performed. This file gives, for each such place, the Paurāṇika verse that the pūjā manuals put in its stead, so that one who has not received upanayana may perform the whole worship without omission, and without reciting what is not theirs to recite. The worship is complete either way.

Recension / paddhati: **Paddhati-neutral.** The verses here are Paurāṇika, not Vedic, and they are used in the same form on both sides of the smārta / Vaiṣṇava division. This file therefore sits beside `../smarta/` and `../vaishnava/` rather than inside either, and it is not a third paddhati.

Applies to: the twenty-five files in `../smarta/` and `../vaishnava/` that carry accented Vedic mantras — `smarta/01`–`22`, `smarta/24`, `smarta/26`, and `vaishnava/01`.

**This file adds; it replaces nothing.** Not one character of those twenty-five files is changed by it, and none of them is superseded. A reciter for whom the upanayana saṁskāra has been performed uses those files as they stand. A reciter for whom it has not been performed uses them together with this one.

Source / recension: the substitutions are taken from a **Telugu simplified-pūjā recension** — eight handouts of the *Nanduri Srivani – Puja Demos* channel (`039` Vināyaka Caviti, `049` Saṅkaṣṭahara, `035` Varalakṣmī, `070` Lakṣmī-Kubera, `083` Ṛṣi Pañcamī in Telugu and English, `101` Sūrya, `082` Kṛṣṇa ṣoḍaśopacāra), supplied 2026-09-13. `082` titles itself `తేలికగా చేసుకొనే విధానం`, 'the method for performing it easily'. **All eight carry zero accent marks and not one Vedic mantra**, and they substitute consistently at exactly the places catalogued here. Two of them print an adhikāra rubric in the body of the text (see unit 5).

**These are handouts of a video channel, not a printed edition, and they rank below stotranidhi in this corpus's order of witnesses.** They are cited here as evidence that the substitution is an actual living practice and as to *which* verse stands where — not as an authority for the wording of any verse. Wherever the corpus already holds the substitute verse in clean Devanāgarī, that text was used and the handout was not transliterated.

Independent collation: the **contrast** witness is `bhaktinidhi.com` (`/en/pooja-vidhanam-in-english-poorvangam/`, fetched 2026-09-13), an independent Telugu pūrvāṅgam which prints the **Vedic** prāṇāyāma in full (`oṃ bhūḥ … tatsaviturvareṇyaṃ … omāpo jyotī …`) and carries no adhikāra note at all. It confirms that the Vedic form is the mainstream and that the handouts depart from it deliberately, rather than by defect.

A second and stronger kind of witness is **this corpus itself**, and it is the one most of the substitutions rest on:

- `../smarta/02_sankshipta_puja_vidhanam.txt` is a short form of the same rite, from the same lineage as `smarta/01`, which **has no prāṇāyāma section at all** and which reduces snāna and mantrapuṣpa to the bare nāma-mantra — `ॐ श्री इष्ट देवतायै नमः । शुद्धोदक स्नानं समर्पयामि ॥`, `ॐ श्री इष्ट देवतायै नमः । मन्त्रपुष्पं समर्पयामि ॥`. Units 7 and 8 below are that file's own readings, not new composition.
- In `../smarta/01`, unit 29 is the Vedic `गणानां त्वा` and **unit 30 is already the Paurāṇika `सुमुखश्चैकदन्तश्च`**. The same pair stands in eleven files. The substitute is the adjacent unit, already present.
- The Paurāṇika kalaśa verse `कलशस्य मुखे विष्णुः` already stands beside the Vedic `आ कलशेषु धावति` in the nitya-pūjās.

Not independently collated: **`पूरकं कुम्भकं चैव रेचकं तदनन्तरम्`** (unit 3). No printed edition or independent digital corpus carrying this śloka was found — searches against Devanāgarī, IAST and Telugu forms all failed, and neither bhaktinidhi nor samskaaram.com prints it. It is attested here **only** by seven of the eight handouts, and its text was read off the page image of `082` page 3 rather than from the damaged text layer. It is given because the substitution it makes is the most needed one in the whole file; the reader should know it rests on a single lineage.

Note on the source's text layer: all eight PDFs carry an extractable text layer, and **it is corrupt** — the page prints `విష్ణుం`, `వదనం`, `సర్వ` where extraction yields `విష్ణాం`, `వదన్ాం`, `సరవ`. Vowel signs and virāmas are scrambled and visarga is written with the ASCII colon. Every reading taken from these PDFs was therefore verified against the page image. Nothing here was transliterated from the extracted text.

Disambiguation for the reviewer: the handouts' adhikāra rubric says `స్త్రీలైతే` — 'if a woman' — not 'if not initiated'. The two do not mean the same thing, and the rubric is reported in unit 5 as it stands rather than restated in terms of upanayana. The general rule stated in unit 1 is the classical one about upanayana and is not drawn from that rubric.

What it does not have: no substitute is offered for a rite whose whole structure is Vedic. `../smarta/17` heads every one of its sixteen services with a mantra of the Mahānārāyaṇa Upaniṣad, and `../smarta/18`, `19` and `22` are built the same way on the Śrī Sūkta and the Puruṣa Sūkta. Removing the Vedic mantras from those four leaves no rite behind. Unit 9 says so plainly and directs the reciter to the deity's nāma-stotra instead; it does not pretend a verse-for-verse exchange is possible there.

Unit count: 11 units. Not a verse count — the work is a table of substitutions with its instructions.

deva:

iast:
sūcanā — vaidikamantrāḥ kṛtopanayanasaṁskāreṇaiva paṭhanīyāḥ | akṛtopanayanena teṣāṁ sthāne atroktāḥ paurāṇikaślokāḥ paṭhanīyāḥ | evaṁ kṛte pūjā sampūrṇā bhavati ||
en: Note — the Vedic mantras are to be recited only by one for whom the upanayana saṁskāra has been performed. One for whom it has not been performed should recite, in their place, the Paurāṇika verses given here. The worship so performed is complete.
tel: సూచన — వైదిక మంత్రాలను ఉపనయన సంస్కారం జరిగినవారు మాత్రమే చదవాలి. ఉపనయనం జరగనివారు వాటి స్థానంలో ఇక్కడ ఇచ్చిన పౌరాణిక శ్లోకాలను చదువుకోవాలి. అలా చేసినప్పుడు పూజ సంపూర్ణమవుతుంది.
hi: सूचना — वैदिक मन्त्रों का पाठ केवल वे ही करें जिनका उपनयन संस्कार हो चुका है। जिनका उपनयन नहीं हुआ है, वे उनके स्थान पर यहाँ दिये गये पौराणिक श्लोकों का पाठ करें। इस प्रकार की गयी पूजा सम्पूर्ण होती है।

--- unit 3 | section: सामान्य विकल्पाः ---
vidhi:
**In place of the prāṇāyāma.** The pūjā files give prāṇāyāma as the seven vyāhṛtis, the Gāyatrī, and the śiras `ओमापो ज्योती रसोऽमृतम्`. All three are Vedic. This verse is recited instead, and the breathing is done as it describes. This is the substitution most often needed — it falls in eleven of the twenty-five files.
deva:
पूरकं कुम्भकं चैव रेचकं तदनन्तरम् ।
प्राणायाममिदं प्रोक्तं सर्वदेवनमस्कृतम् ॥
iast:
pūrakaṁ kumbhakaṁ caiva recakaṁ tadanantaram |
prāṇāyāmamidaṁ proktaṁ sarvadevanamaskṛtam ||
en: Drawing the breath in, holding it, and letting it out after that — this is called prāṇāyāma, and it is saluted by all the gods.
tel: శ్వాసను లోనికి తీసుకోవడం, నిలిపి ఉంచడం, ఆ తరువాత విడిచిపెట్టడం — దీనినే ప్రాణాయామం అంటారు. దీనికి దేవతలందరూ నమస్కరిస్తారు.
hi: श्वास को भीतर लेना, रोकना, और उसके पश्चात् छोड़ना — इसी को प्राणायाम कहा गया है, और समस्त देवता इसे नमस्कार करते हैं।

--- unit 5 | section: सामान्य विकल्पाः ---
vidhi:
**In the ācamana.** The first three names in the ācamana are recited with `स्वाहा` — `ॐ केशवाय स्वाहा`, `ॐ नारायणाय स्वाहा`, `ॐ माधवाय स्वाहा`. The source files `082` and `070` print a rubric beside the ācamana directing that these three be ended with `नमः` instead. Their rubric reads `స్త్రీలైతే మొదటి 3 నామాలకీ చివర నమః అని చదువుకోండి` — 'if a woman, read *namaḥ* at the end of the first three names'. It is given here in the words the source uses. The remaining twenty-one names already end in `नमः` and are unchanged.
deva:
1. ॐ केशवाय नमः ।
2. ॐ नारायणाय नमः ।
3. ॐ माधवाय नमः ॥
iast:
1. oṁ keśavāya namaḥ |
2. oṁ nārāyaṇāya namaḥ |
3. oṁ mādhavāya namaḥ ||
en: Salutation to Keśava. Salutation to Nārāyaṇa. Salutation to Mādhava.
tel: ఓం కేశవాయ నమః. ఓం నారాయణాయ నమః. ఓం మాధవాయ నమః.

--- unit 9 | section: सामान्य विकल्पाः ---
vidhi:
**In place of the sūktas.** The Puruṣa Sūkta, the Śrī Sūkta, the Durgā Sūkta and the Rudra mantras are Vedic throughout, and no verse-for-verse exchange is possible for them. Where a pūjā recites a sūkta, recite instead the aṣṭottaraśatanāma stotra of that same deity, which this collection holds for every deity worshipped in these files. Four rites — `../smarta/17`, `18`, `19` and `22` — are built on a sūkta from beginning to end, a Vedic mantra heading each of their sixteen services. Those four cannot be adapted. Use the deity's nitya-pūjā instead: `../smarta/05` for Śiva, `07` for Lakṣmī, `08` for Durgā, `11` for Sūrya, each with the substitutions of this file.
deva:
सूचना — यत्र सूक्तं पठ्यते तत्र तस्या एव देवतायाः अष्टोत्तरशतनामस्तोत्रं पठनीयम् ॥
iast:
sūcanā — yatra sūktaṁ paṭhyate tatra tasyā eva devatāyāḥ aṣṭottaraśatanāmastotraṁ paṭhanīyam ||
en: Note — where a sūkta is recited, recite instead the hundred-and-eight names of that same deity.
tel: సూచన — సూక్తం చదివే చోట, ఆ దేవత యొక్క అష్టోత్తరశతనామ స్తోత్రాన్ని చదువుకోవాలి.
hi: सूचना — जहाँ सूक्त का पाठ होता है, वहाँ उसी देवता के अष्टोत्तरशतनाम स्तोत्र का पाठ करें।

--- unit 10 | section: सामान्य विकल्पाः ---
vidhi:
**In place of a deity gāyatrī.** Verses of the form `... विद्महे ... धीमहि ... प्रचोदयात्` are gāyatrīs and are Vedic — `एकदन्ताय विद्महे` for Gaṇapati, `तत्पुरुषाय विद्महे` for Śiva, `नारायणाय विद्महे` for Viṣṇu, `कात्यायनाय विद्महे` for Durgā, `महादेव्यै च विद्महे` for Lakṣmī. In every case the pūjā file gives a dhyāna-śloka for the same deity a few lines away. Recite that dhyāna-śloka a second time in place of the gāyatrī.
deva:
सूचना — गायत्रीस्थाने तस्या एव देवतायाः ध्यानश्लोकः पुनः पठनीयः ॥
iast:
sūcanā — gāyatrīsthāne tasyā eva devatāyāḥ dhyānaślokaḥ punaḥ paṭhanīyaḥ ||
en: Note — in place of a gāyatrī, recite again the dhyāna verse of that same deity.
tel: సూచన — గాయత్రి స్థానంలో ఆ దేవత యొక్క ధ్యాన శ్లోకాన్నే మరల చదువుకోవాలి.
hi: सूचना — गायत्री के स्थान पर उसी देवता के ध्यान श्लोक का पुनः पाठ करें।

--- unit 11 | section: सामान्य विधिः ---
vidhi:
The general rule, for a Vedic mantra not named above. The source files themselves use this device: they close the saṅkalpa with `ध्यान आवाहनादि षोडशोपचार पूजां करिष्ये` and offer the services `मनसा` — mentally. Where no substitute verse is given, the offering is made with the deity's name and the mantra is passed over in silence. Nothing is lost by this. An offering made with the mind is an offering.
deva:
सूचना — यस्य वैदिकमन्त्रस्य विकल्पो नोक्तः, तत्र देवतानाम्ना उपचारं समर्पयेत् मनसा वा । मानसोऽपि उपचारः उपचार एव ॥
iast:
sūcanā — yasya vaidikamantrasya vikalpo noktaḥ, tatra devatānāmnā upacāraṁ samarpayet manasā vā | mānaso'pi upacāraḥ upacāra eva ||
en: Note — where no alternative has been given for a Vedic mantra, make that offering with the deity's name, or make it mentally. An offering made with the mind is an offering all the same.
tel: సూచన — ఏ వైదిక మంత్రానికి వికల్పం ఇవ్వబడలేదో, అక్కడ ఆ దేవత నామంతో ఉపచారాన్ని సమర్పించాలి, లేదా మనసుతో సమర్పించాలి. మనసుతో చేసిన ఉపచారం కూడా ఉపచారమే.
hi: सूचना — जिस वैदिक मन्त्र का विकल्प यहाँ नहीं दिया गया, वहाँ उस देवता के नाम से उपचार समर्पित करें, अथवा मन से समर्पित करें। मन से किया गया उपचार भी उपचार ही है।
