Type: vidhi (ritual manual) — not a stotra. Uses the one-extra-field vidhi format documented in `../../vidhi/madhyandina/01_sandhyopasana_vidhi.txt`: a `vidhi:` field carrying the ritual instruction, then deva / iast / en / tel / hi.

Recension / paddhati: **Smārta, South Indian.** The Vaiṣṇava form of the opening is kept separately at `../vaishnava/01_purvanga_vidhanam.txt` and **the two must never be merged, harmonised or collated against each other.**

**This is a second recension of the Lakṣmī pūjā, beside `07_lakshmi_nitya_puja.txt`.** Both are smārta sixteen-service rites for the same goddess, from two different publishers, and **neither file is to be corrected from the other.** Of the 283 lines of this page, **229 do not stand in `07` in any form** — 54 do.

**This rite is built on the Śrī Sūkta, and `07` is not.** The page's own second saṅkalpa says the worship is performed `श्री सूक्त विधानेन`, by the rule of the Śrī Sūkta, and that claim was checked and it holds: the sixteen services are headed by the ṛcs of the hymn taken in the hymn's own order, one to each service. Invocation takes ṛc 1, the seat ṛc 2, the water for the feet ṛc 3, the water for the hands ṛc 4, the water for sipping ṛc 5, the bath ṛc 6, the garment ṛc 7, the fan ṛc 8 — the ṛc that drives out Alakṣmī — sandal-paste ṛc 9, ornaments ṛc 10, the garland ṛc 11 (to the seer Kardama), incense ṛc 12 (to the seer Ciklīta), the lamp ṛc 13, the food-offering ṛc 14, and betel ṛc 15, which is the last ṛc of the core hymn.

**Four more things this rite has that `07` does not:** the worship of the **eight attainments** in the eight directions; the worship of the **eight Lakṣmīs**; the gift of gold; and, at the end, a whole **Dīpāvalī portion** — the worship of the lamp, the worship of the row of lamps, and an eleven-verse prayer for the night of Dīpāvalī, in which the night is addressed by its older name Sukharātri, the night of ease. That portion is why the page calls itself a `विशेष` pūjā, a special one.

What it does not have: **no pūrvāṅga of its own.** The page opens with two directions, sending the worshipper to the pūrvāṅga and to the turmeric-Gaṇapati pūjā. Use `03_purvanga_vidhanam.txt` before this file.

Source / recension: base text `https://stotranidhi.com/hi/sri-maha-lakshmi-visesha-shodasopachara-puja-in-sanskrit/` (fetched 2026-09-12), a Devanāgarī page carrying accent marks on its Vedic material.

**Gītā Press, Gorakhpur, is a witness for this rite's two unusual services.** `07_lakshmi_nitya_puja.txt` records that the *Nitya Karma Pūjā Prakāśa* carries a `श्रीमहालक्ष्मी-पूजन` at pp. 251–268 **together with an `अष्टसिद्धि-पूजन` and an `अष्टलक्ष्मी-पूजन`** — the very two services this page has and `07` does not. That is recorded here as `07` reports it; the volume was not re-opened for this file, and nothing has been imported from it.

**A correction to this folder's own record.** `07_lakshmi_nitya_puja.txt` says that stotranidhi's rite adds a `प्राणप्रतिष्ठा`, a `पुनः सङ्कल्प`, `मधुपर्क` and `पञ्चामृत स्नान`. Three of those four are right. **There is no `प्राणप्रतिष्ठा`** — neither the Devanāgarī page nor the Telugu page has one. That line came from a survey note written across several deity pages at once and was never checked against this page. The same kind of error was found and corrected while writing `16_anjaneya_shodashopachara_puja.txt`. `07` is another session's file and has not been edited; the finding is recorded here and in `../README.md`.

Independent collation: against **`../../devi/lakshmi/06_sri_suktam.txt`**, this corpus's own Śrī Sūktam, whose base text is vignanam.org's accented śuddha-devanāgarī edition, itself collated there against stotranidhi, two sanskritdocuments.org editions and greenmesg.org. That is a different editorial lineage from this page. Every accented ṛc on this page was matched against it.

**What that collation showed.** Most apparent differences are not differences of text at all: the Śrī Sūktam file prints the **saṁhitā-pāṭha**, which joins words across pāda boundaries (`ता-म्म॒ आव॑ह`, `च॒न्द्रां हि॒रण्म॑यीं-लँ॒क्ष्मी-ञ्जात॑वेदो`), while this page prints the words separated and visarga-final (`तां म॒ आ व॑ह`, `ल॒क्ष्मीं जात॑वेदो`). That is a **pāṭha difference inside one tradition**, not a variant reading, and it is not a reason to prefer one over the other. Three real differences were found, and none was corrected:

1. **`श्रीर्मा देवी जुषताम्` here; `श्रीर्मा देवीर्जुषताम्` in the Śrī Sūktam file** (ṛc 3, at the water for the feet). Both readings are printed in editions of the hymn.
2. **`ज्येष्ठामलक्ष्मीर्नाशयामि` here; `ज्येष्ठामलक्षीर्नाशयामि` in the Śrī Sūktam file** (ṛc 8, at the fan). `अलक्षी` is the older form of the word.
3. **The ṛc at the waving of the light, `सम्राजं च विराजं च`, is not in the Śrī Sūktam file at all.** That file follows vignanam's scope, which stops earlier; its own header records that sanskritdocuments.org prints a fuller text. This page carries the verse, and it is kept.

A fourth place is worth naming for the reader rather than as a variant: the dhyāna verse here reads `शुभ्र वस्त्रोत्तरीया`, and the Śrī Sūktam file reads `शुभ्र वस्तोत्तरीया`. The word is `वस्त्र`, a garment. **That file is another session's work and is not ours to correct** — the difference is recorded here and reported, not repaired there.

Defects in the base text, corrected with reasons, because stotranidhi is a convenience source and not a critical edition:

1. **`महालक्ष्मै` → `महालक्ष्म्यै`, and the same in the eight names of Lakṣmī — fifty places in all.** `लक्ष्मी` is feminine and its dative is `लक्ष्म्यै`. `लक्ष्मै` is not a Sanskrit form. This is a slip and not a habit of the edition, because **the page itself writes `श्रीमहालक्ष्म्यै` correctly** in the direction about the lamp, and its worship of the limbs uses the correct datives `चपलायै`, `कात्यायन्यै`, `श्रियै` throughout. `07_lakshmi_nitya_puja.txt`, from the other publisher, writes `महालक्ष्म्यै` in all twenty-three of its occurrences.
2. **`सर्विसिद्धिप्रदायिनि` → `सर्वसिद्धिप्रदायिनि`** (unit 43). `सर्वि-` is not a word; the compound is `सर्व-सिद्धि`.
3. **`अकालमृत्यहरणं` → `अकालमृत्युहरणम्`** (unit 45). A dropped `u`. The corrected form stands in eleven other files of this corpus.
4. **`क्षमा प्रार्थन` → `क्षमाप्रार्थना`** in the section heading (unit 44), where the final `ā` is dropped.

Observed and deliberately **not** emended:

- **`महेश्वरी` and `देवी` for the vocatives `महेश्वरि` and `देवि`** in the verse asking pardon (unit 44). This is how the verse is commonly printed, and the same lengthening appears in other files of this folder.
- **`सुप्रीता सुप्रसन्ना वरदा भवन्तु`** (unit 44), where the verb is plural although `देवता` is singular. Recorded, not changed.
- **`सम्युतम्`, `सम्युतः`** for `संयुतम्`, `संयुतः`. This publisher writes anusvāra before a semivowel as `म्` throughout. It is an orthographic habit of the edition, not a defect.

Accent: **present on the Vedic material** — the ṛcs of the Śrī Sūkta, the Gāyatrī and the offerings to the five breaths — and also on the first two dhyāna verses, which are not Vedic. The accent on those two is printed by the source and is kept exactly as printed; **none was added, inferred or reconstructed.** Udātta `॑` (U+0951), anudātta `॒` (U+0952), dīrgha svarita `᳚` (U+1CDA). The remaining paurāṇika verses are unaccented, which is normal and not a defect.

**A defect of the source's Veda pages, corrected throughout.** stotranidhi writes final visarga in its accented mantras as the ASCII colon `:` rather than as `ः` (U+0903) — `चक्षु॒:` for `चक्षु॒ः`. It looks right on the page and is wrong in the file: it breaks search, breaks collation against any real edition, and transliterates as a colon. Every such colon inside a `deva:` field here has been mapped to `ः` and the affected `iast:` fields regenerated.

**Never copy stotranidhi's IAST.** Its pages mark anusvāra `ṃ` and avagraha `’` where this corpus uses `ṁ` and `'`. Every `iast:` field here was generated from the Devanāgarī by `../../bin/dev2iast.py`.

Blurb: This is the worship of Śrī Mahālakṣmī in sixteen services. It is a second and different form of the rite kept at `07_lakshmi_nitya_puja.txt`, published by a different house, and the two should not be mixed. This page has no opening portion of its own. The worshipper first performs the pūrvāṅga and the turmeric-Gaṇapati pūjā, to which the page sends him. What makes this rite different is that every one of the sixteen services is headed by a verse of the Śrī Sūkta, taken in the order of that hymn, so that the whole hymn is recited across the worship. The goddess is meditated upon as she who is seated on the lotus and bathed by the elephants of heaven from golden vessels. Besides the usual services she is offered red sandal-paste, vermilion, saffron and scented oil. The eight attainments are then worshipped in the eight directions, and after them the eight Lakṣmīs. The rite ends with a portion for Dīpāvalī: the lamp is itself worshipped as a form of Brahman, the row of lamps is offered, and the worshipper prays through eleven verses for wealth, grain, long life and children, and asks the night of ease to carry his misfortune away.

vidhi:
