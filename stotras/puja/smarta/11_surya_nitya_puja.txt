Title: Sūryanārāyaṇa Nitya Pūjā Vidhānam (Smārta Paddhati)
Devanāgarī: सूर्यनारायण नित्य पूजा विधानम् (स्मार्त पद्धतिः)
Telugu: సూర్యనారాయణ నిత్య పూజా విధానం (స్మార్త పద్ధతిః)

Author: Anonymous (received smārta household rite). No compiler is named and none should be inferred.

Type: vidhi (ritual manual) — not a stotra. Uses the one-extra-field vidhi format documented in `../../vidhi/madhyandina/01_sandhyopasana_vidhi.txt`: a `vidhi:` field carrying the ritual instruction, then deva / iast / en / tel / hi.

Recension / paddhati: **Smārta, South Indian.** Pūjā-vidhāna divides by *paddhati* the way sandhyāvandana divides by śākhā. The Śrīvaiṣṇava form of the opening is kept separately at `../vaishnava/01_purvanga_vidhanam.txt` and **the two must never be merged, harmonised or collated against each other.**

**Relation to `01_nitya_puja_vidhanam.txt`.** `01` is the generic template, printing `श्री इष्ट देवता` as a slot. This is the same rite with the slot filled in for Sūrya by the source itself, published as a complete self-contained order of service and transcribed so. **Units 1–29 are the shared pūrvāṅga, word-for-word the template's**; units 30–60 are the Sūrya ṣoḍaśopacāra proper. Measured against the template, 210 of this page's 349 lines are shared and 139 are new — **60% reuse, the lowest of the ten deity files, and this is the longest of them**. Its siblings are `04_ganapati_nitya_puja.txt` through `10_subrahmanya_nitya_puja.txt`.

**What makes this rite the largest of the ten.** Three things the others do not have, or not at this length:

1. **The Puruṣasūkta is threaded through the services**, as the Śrīsūkta is through the Lakṣmī rite: its opening verse `स॒हस्र॑शीर्​षा॒ पुरु॑षः` at the invocation (unit 32), `पुरु॑ष ए॒वेदग्ं सर्वम्᳚` at the seat (unit 33), `वेदा॒हमे॒त-म्पुरु॑ष-म्म॒हान्तम्᳚` at the waving of lights (unit 50), and `धा॒ता पु॒रस्ता॒द्यमु॑दाज॒हार॑` at the mantra-flower (unit 51) — the last carrying the sūkta's great claim, that there is no other road to go by.
2. **A twenty-name aṅga-pūjā** (unit 42), the longest in the folder, running the twelve solar names and eight more from the feet to the whole body.
3. **A `द्वादशार्घ्याणि`** (unit 54) — twelve separate arghya-offerings, one to each of the twelve solar names. **No other rite in this folder has anything like it**, and it is the structural signature of solar worship.

It also keeps the `उद्यन्नद्य विवस्वान्` verse at the salutation (unit 53), which asks Sūrya to destroy heart-disease and jaundice — a healing prayer, and a reminder that solar worship carried a medical purpose.

**Accent, with an exception:** the Vedic material is accented throughout — the pūrvāṅga's mantras and all four Puruṣasūkta verses. But the **Sūrya Gāyatrī** at unit 51, `ॐ भास्कराय विद्महे महाद्युतिकराय धीमहि । तन्नो आदित्यः प्रचोदयात् ॥`, is printed **without svara marks**, as the Subrahmaṇya Gāyatrī is on its own page (see `10_subrahmanya_nitya_puja.txt`). **It has been left unaccented, exactly as the source has it** — per `CLAUDE.md` accent is transcribed or absent, never inferred.

Udātta `॑` (U+0951), anudātta `॒` (U+0952), dīrgha svarita `᳚` (U+1CDA); the praṇava before a spirant takes the guttural nasal, written `ओग्ं` (`ogṁ`). The sandhi-joined orthography that hyphenates across word boundaries is kept exactly as printed and carried into the IAST.

Source / recension: base text `https://vignanam.org/devanagari/surya-nitya-puja-vidhanam.html` (fetched 2026-09-12), a **native accented Devanāgarī edition** declaring itself "शुद्ध देवनागरी with the right anusvaras marked"; its Telugu page carries an identical accent count, so **nothing here was transliterated by us.**

Independent collation: against **stotranidhi's Sūryanārāyaṇa ṣoḍaśopacāra pūjā** (`https://stotranidhi.com/sri-surya-shodasopachara-puja-in-telugu/`, fetched 2026-09-12; 319 accent marks) and against **Gītā Press, Gorakhpur, *Nitya Karma Pūjā Prakāśa***, whose `पञ्चदेव-पूजा` includes **Sūrya as one of the five**, with its own dhyāna. Three separate editorial lineages.

Differences recorded and **not reconciled**:

- Gītā Press's Sūrya is one of the five pañcāyatana deities and receives a dhyāna within that sequence rather than a rite of his own; it has no dvādaśārghya and no threaded Puruṣasūkta. It also gives a list of flowers **forbidden** to Sūrya (`सूर्यके लिये निषिद्ध फूल`, p. 376) which this text does not.
- As in its other rites, stotranidhi adds a `प्राणप्रतिष्ठा`, a `पुनः सङ्कल्प`, `मधुपर्क` and `पञ्चामृत स्नान`. A second recension, queued as a file of its own.

  (Cross-reference added 2026-09-12, changing not one character of the text above or below it: that second recension has now been **written as a file of its own**, `22_surya_shodashopachara_puja.txt`, where it is transcribed in full and collated against the Puruṣa Sūkta itself. **Neither file is to be corrected from the other.** One detail of the line above was found not to hold when the page was opened: **it has no `मधुपर्क`.** That is recorded in the new file's header and in `../README.md` rather than changed here.)
- This text's pradakṣiṇa verse reads `तानि तानि विनश्यन्ति` where every sibling file and the generic template read `तानि तानि प्रणश्यन्ति`. Both mean 'they perish'; the difference is recorded and **not** normalised to the commoner reading.

Defect in the base text, corrected with reason: **`उपमश्रवस्तवम्` → `उपमश्रवस्तमम्`** at unit 27. The mantra is Ṛgveda 2.23.1, whose transmitted reading is `upamaśravastamam`, a superlative in *-tama-*; `-stavam` is not construable. The same slip stands on every one of this site's deity pages and is corrected in each; the site's own generic template prints it correctly, so the defect is confined to the deity pages. As there, this page also splits `क॒वि-ङ्क॑वी॒नाम्` as `क॒वि-ङ्क॑वी॒नां उप॒-`; a word-division difference in the printed sandhi, kept as printed.

Not collated: the paurāṇika verses shared with the template, and the Sūrya upacāra and dhyāna verses, for which no critical edition exists. `ध्येयः सदा सवितृमण्डलमध्यवर्ती` is the most widely printed of all Sūrya dhyāna verses and is given as this source prints it.

Unit count: 60 units. Not a verse count in the stotra sense — the work is a prose order of service punctuated by mantras, and the numbering is this corpus's, not the source's.

Blurb: This is the daily pūjā of Sūryanārāyaṇa in the smārta form, complete in sixty units, and the longest of the deity pūjās in this folder. After the materials and the opening acts, the worshipper honours the water-vessel, the conch and the bell, and worships Gaṇapati first so that obstacles may be removed. Sūrya is then meditated upon in two units as the one who dwells in the middle of the solar orb. Three things set this rite apart. The Puruṣasūkta is threaded through the services, a verse at the invocation, another at the seat, another at the waving of the lamp and another at the offering of flowers with mantra. The worship of the god limb by limb runs to twenty names, the longest in the folder. And after the salutations comes a section found in no other rite here: twelve separate guest-offerings, one to each of the twelve names of the sun. The salutation itself keeps an old healing verse, which asks the rising sun to destroy heart-disease and jaundice. The rite closes with four verses of pardon, the dismissal and the prasāda.

--- unit 1 | section: आवश्यकानि वस्तूनि ---
vidhi:
The things to be gathered before beginning. The conch is marked optional.
deva:
आवश्यकानि वस्तूनि — आसनं । दीपः । गन्धः । कुङ्कुमं । अक्षताः । पुष्पाणि । धूपः । नैवेद्यं । ताम्बूलं । आचमनीयं । पूजा पात्रं । कलशः । शङ्खः (वैकल्पिकः) । सूर्यनारायण स्वामि प्रतिमा वा चित्रपटः वा ।
iast:
āvaśyakāni vastūni — āsanaṁ | dīpaḥ | gandhaḥ | kuṅkumaṁ | akṣatāḥ | puṣpāṇi | dhūpaḥ | naivedyaṁ | tāmbūlaṁ | ācamanīyaṁ | pūjā pātraṁ | kalaśaḥ | śaṅkhaḥ (vaikalpikaḥ) | sūryanārāyaṇa svāmi pratimā vā citrapaṭaḥ vā |
en: Things required — a seat; a lamp; sandal-paste; kuṅkuma; unbroken rice; flowers; incense; food-offering; betel; water for ācamana; the pūjā vessel; a water-pot; a conch (optional); and an image or picture of Lord Sūryanārāyaṇa.
tel: కావలసిన వస్తువులు — ఆసనం; దీపం; గంధం; కుంకుమ; అక్షతలు; పుష్పాలు; ధూపం; నైవేద్యం; తాంబూలం; ఆచమనీయం; పూజా పాత్ర; కలశం; శంఖం (ఐచ్ఛికం); సూర్యనారాయణ స్వామి ప్రతిమ లేదా చిత్రపటం.
hi: आवश्यक वस्तुएँ — आसन; दीप; गन्ध; कुङ्कुम; अक्षत; पुष्प; धूप; नैवेद्य; ताम्बूल; आचमनीय जल; पूजापात्र; कलश; शङ्ख (वैकल्पिक); तथा सूर्यनारायण स्वामी की प्रतिमा अथवा चित्रपट।

--- unit 3 | section: पूर्वाङ्ग पूजा ---
vidhi:
Purification. Recited to make oneself fit, inwardly and outwardly, for what follows.
deva:
अपवित्रः पवित्रो वा सर्वावस्थां गतोऽपि वा ।
यः स्मरेत् पुण्डरीकाक्षं स बाह्याभ्यन्तरः शुचिः ॥
पुण्डरीकाक्ष पुण्डरीकाक्ष पुण्डरीकाक्षाय नमः ॥
iast:
apavitraḥ pavitro vā sarvāvasthāṁ gato'pi vā |
yaḥ smaret puṇḍarīkākṣaṁ sa bāhyābhyantaraḥ śuciḥ ||
puṇḍarīkākṣa puṇḍarīkākṣa puṇḍarīkākṣāya namaḥ ||
en: Impure or pure, or gone into whatever condition — whoever calls to mind the lotus-eyed one is clean, without and within. Lotus-eyed one, lotus-eyed one: salutation to the lotus-eyed one.
tel: అపవిత్రుడైనా, పవిత్రుడైనా, ఏ అవస్థలో ఉన్నా — పుండరీకాక్షుని ఎవడు స్మరిస్తాడో అతడు బయటా లోపలా శుద్ధుడు. పుండరీకాక్షా, పుండరీకాక్షా, పుండరీకాక్షునికి నమస్కారం.
hi: अपवित्र हो या पवित्र, अथवा किसी भी अवस्था में क्यों न हो — जो पुण्डरीकाक्ष का स्मरण करता है वह बाहर-भीतर से शुद्ध हो जाता है। पुण्डरीकाक्ष, पुण्डरीकाक्ष, पुण्डरीकाक्ष को नमस्कार।

--- unit 4 | section: पूर्वाङ्ग पूजा ---
vidhi:
Prārthanā. The first of the invocatory verses — to Viṣṇu, for the quieting of all obstacles. Note that this deity's own name, Sūryanārāyaṇa, joins the sun to Nārāyaṇa.
deva:
शुक्लाम्बरधरं विष्णुं शशिवर्णं चतुर्भुजम् ।
प्रसन्नवदनं ध्यायेत् सर्वविघ्नोपशान्तये ॥
iast:
śuklāmbaradharaṁ viṣṇuṁ śaśivarṇaṁ caturbhujam |
prasannavadanaṁ dhyāyet sarvavighnopaśāntaye ||
en: For the quieting of every obstacle one should meditate on Viṣṇu — robed in white, moon-coloured, four-armed, his face serene.
tel: సమస్త విఘ్నాలూ శాంతించడానికి — తెల్లని వస్త్రం ధరించినవాడు, చంద్రుని వన్నెవాడు, నాలుగు చేతులవాడు, ప్రసన్నమైన ముఖం కలవాడు అయిన విష్ణువును ధ్యానించాలి.
hi: समस्त विघ्नों की शान्ति के लिये श्वेत वस्त्र धारण किये हुए, चन्द्रमा के समान वर्णवाले, चतुर्भुज, प्रसन्नमुख विष्णु का ध्यान करना चाहिये।

To Gaṇeśa.
deva:
अगजानन पद्मार्कं गजाननमहर्निशम् ।
अनेकदं तं भक्तानां एकदन्तमुपास्महे ॥
iast:

en: We worship day and night that elephant-faced one, a sun to the lotus that is Pārvatī's face, who gives his devotees much though he is the one-tusked.
tel: పార్వతి ముఖపద్మానికి సూర్యుని వంటివాడు, గజముఖుడు, ఒకే దంతం కలవాడైనా భక్తులకు అనేకమైన వరాలిచ్చువాడు — ఆ ఏకదంతుని రేయింబవళ్లు ఉపాసిస్తాము.
hi: पार्वती के मुखकमल के लिये सूर्य के समान, गजानन, एकदन्त होते हुए भी भक्तों को अनेक वर देनेवाले उन गणेश की हम दिन-रात उपासना करते हैं।

To the teacher.

tel: ఓ దివాకరా, నీకు నమస్కారం; ఓ భాస్కరా, పాపమును నశింపజేయుము. త్రయీమయునికి, విశ్వాత్మకు — అర్ఘ్యమును స్వీకరించు; నీకు నమస్కారం. మిత్రునికి — ఈ అర్ఘ్యమును సమర్పిస్తున్నాను; రవికి; సూర్యునికి; భానువుకు; ఖగునికి; పూషునికి; హిరణ్యగర్భునికి; మరీచికి; ఆదిత్యునికి; సవితకు; అర్కునికి; భాస్కరునికి — ఈ అర్ఘ్యమును సమర్పిస్తున్నాను.
hi: हे दिवाकर, आपको नमस्कार; हे भास्कर, पाप का नाश कीजिये। त्रयीमय, विश्वात्मन् — अर्घ्य ग्रहण कीजिये; आपको नमस्कार हो। मित्र को — यह अर्घ्य समर्पित करता हूँ; रवि को; सूर्य को; भानु को; खग को; पूषा को; हिरण्यगर्भ को; मरीचि को; आदित्य को; सविता को; अर्क को; भास्कर को — यह अर्घ्य समर्पित करता हूँ।

--- unit 55 | section: क्षमाप्रार्थना ---
vidhi:
The remaining royal and divine services, offered together by naming them.
deva:

en: Salutation to Sūryanārāyaṇa. I offer the parasol; the fly-whisk; I cause song to be heard; I show dance; I offer all the royal services and all the divine services.

--- unit 56 | section: क्षमाप्रार्थना ---
vidhi:
Asking pardon. The vocative is `जनार्दन`, as in the Viṣṇu rite — fitting, since this deity is Sūryanārāyaṇa.
deva:
मन्त्रहीनं क्रियाहीनं भक्तिहीनं जनार्दन ।
यत्पूजितं मया देव परिपूर्णं तदस्तु ते ॥
iast:
mantrahīnaṁ kriyāhīnaṁ bhaktihīnaṁ janārdana |
yatpūjitaṁ mayā deva paripūrṇaṁ tadastu te ||
en: Lacking in mantra, lacking in rite, lacking in devotion though it was, O Janārdana — let what I have worshipped be counted complete for you, O god.
tel: ఓ జనార్దనా! మంత్రహీనమైనా, క్రియాహీనమైనా, భక్తిహీనమైనా — ఓ దేవా, నేను చేసిన ఈ పూజ నీకు పరిపూర్ణమగుగాక.
hi: हे जनार्दन! मन्त्रहीन, क्रियाहीन और भक्तिहीन होते हुए भी — हे देव, मेरे द्वारा की गयी यह पूजा आपके लिये परिपूर्ण हो।

--- unit 57 | section: क्षमाप्रार्थना ---
vidhi:
A second verse of pardon, which only this rite and the Navagraha rite carry — for what was done out of ignorance, or heedlessness, or because the means were wanting.
deva:
अज्ञानाद्वा प्रमादाद्वा वैकल्यात्साधनस्य वा ।
यन्न्यूनमतिरिक्तं च तत्सर्वं क्षन्तुमर्हसि ॥
iast:
ajñānādvā pramādādvā vaikalyātsādhanasya vā |
yannyūnamatiriktaṁ ca tatsarvaṁ kṣantumarhasi ||
en: Whether from ignorance, or from heedlessness, or from the insufficiency of the means — whatever has been left short or done in excess, all of that you should forgive.
tel: అజ్ఞానముచేత, ప్రమాదముచేత, లేక సాధనముల లోపముచేత — ఏది తక్కువగా లేక ఎక్కువగా జరిగినదో, అదంతా క్షమించుటకు నీవు అర్హుడవు.
hi: अज्ञान से, प्रमाद से, अथवा साधन की विकलता से — जो न्यून अथवा अतिरिक्त हुआ, वह सब क्षमा करने योग्य हैं आप।

--- unit 58 | section: क्षमाप्रार्थना ---
vidhi:
Surrendering the act itself, and the closing declaration of the fruit of the worship.
deva:
कायेन वाचा मनसेन्द्रियैर्वा
बुद्ध्यात्मना वा प्रकृतेः स्वभावात् ।
करोमि यद्यत् सकलं परस्मै
नारायणायेति समर्पयामि ॥
अनया ध्यान-आवाहनादि-षोडशोपचार पूजया श्रीसूर्यनारायण स्वामी सुप्रीतः सुप्रसन्नः वरदो भवतु ॥
iast:
kāyena vācā manasendriyairvā
buddhyātmanā vā prakṛteḥ svabhāvāt |
karomi yadyat sakalaṁ parasmai

anayā dhyāna-āvāhanādi-ṣoḍaśopacāra pūjayā śrīsūryanārāyaṇa svāmī suprītaḥ suprasannaḥ varado bhavatu ||
en: Whatever I do with body, speech, mind or senses, with intellect or self or by the nature of my own disposition, all of it I offer to the supreme Nārāyaṇa. By this sixteen-fold worship beginning with meditation and invocation, may Lord Sūryanārāyaṇa be well pleased, gracious, and a giver of boons.
tel: శరీరంతో, వాక్కుతో, మనస్సుతో, ఇంద్రియాలతో, బుద్ధితో, ఆత్మతో, ప్రకృతి స్వభావంతో నేను ఏది చేసినా అదంతా పరమాత్ముడైన నారాయణునికి సమర్పిస్తున్నాను. ఈ ధ్యాన-ఆవాహనాది షోడశోపచార పూజచేత శ్రీసూర్యనారాయణ స్వామి సుప్రీతుడు, సుప్రసన్నుడు, వరదుడు అగుగాక.

--- unit 60 | section: उद्वासनम् ---
