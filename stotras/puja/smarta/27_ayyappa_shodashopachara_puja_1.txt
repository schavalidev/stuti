**This rite is built on two Vedic texts at once, and says so.** Its second resolution says the worship is performed `पुरुषसूक्त सहित रुद्रसूक्त विधानेन`, and the claim holds: each of the fifteen services from the invocation to the betel is headed first by a ṛc of the **Puruṣa Sūkta** in the hymn's own order, and then by a ṛc of the **first anuvāka of the Śrī Rudram** (Taittirīya Saṁhitā 4.5.1), also in its own order — all fifteen of that anuvāka's ṛcs, one to each service. At the waving of the light the sixteenth ṛc of the Puruṣa Sūkta is joined by the salutation `नमस्ते अस्तु भगवन् विश्वेश्वराय`, which is recited before the second anuvāka; at the mantra-flower the verse of Dhātṛ is joined by `यो रुद्रो अग्नौ` (Taittirīya Saṁhitā 5.5.9); and the closing verse of the Puruṣa Sūkta returns the Lord to his place. This pairs the two sides of the Lord's birth, Hari and Hara. `17_shiva_shodashopachara_puja.txt` uses the Mahānārāyaṇa Upaniṣad in this way and `22_surya_shodashopachara_puja.txt` the Puruṣa Sūkta alone; this is the first rite in the folder to join two.

**What else it has:** a **prāṇapratiṣṭhā**; a **meditation in two verses**; worship of the limbs under **twenty-nine names**, most of them names of Śāstā; the Śāstā Gāyatrī and the root mantra of Mahāśāstā at the mantra-flower; the verses of the **Śāstā Pañcaratnam** within the prayer for refuge, with eight further verses; the **pilgrims' calls** in Malayalam and Tamil; a song of auspiciousness; and a **prayer in Tamil** asking pardon for sins done knowingly and unknowingly.

What it does not have: **no directions at the top**; the page begins at the second resolution. Perform `03_purvanga_vidhanam.txt` and the Gaṇapati pūjā before it. The hundred and eight names and the full śaraṇa-ghoṣa are named but not printed. The Harivarāsanam is named; it is kept at `../../ayyappa/01_harivarasanam.txt`.

Defects in the base text, corrected with reasons, because stotranidhi is a convenience source and not a critical edition:

1. **`दध्येन` → `दध्ना`** (unit 10). `दधि` makes its instrumental `दध्ना`; `दध्येन` is not a form. The page's own second Ayyappa rite prints `दध्ना` in the same line.
2. **`जानुनीं` → `जानुनी`** and **`ऊरूं` → `ऊरू`** (unit 18). The duals of `जानु` and `ऊरु` are `जानुनी` and `ऊरू`; a final anusvāra makes neither a form. `22_surya_shodashopachara_puja.txt` prints both correctly.
3. **`स्वर्णवस्त्रेणसम्युतम्` → `स्वर्णवस्त्रेण सम्युतम्`** (unit 12). Two words run together with no space.
4. **`अन्यधा` → `अन्यथा`** (unit 27). `ध` for `थ`; the word is `अन्यथा`, as the Bagalāmukhī page of the same site prints it.
5. **`प्राणप्रतिष्ठ` → `प्राणप्रतिष्ठा`** (unit 2), **`प्रार्थन` → `प्रार्थना`** (unit 34), **`क्षमाप्रार्थन` → `क्षमाप्रार्थना`** (unit 35) and **`पूज` → `पूजा`** (unit 37), where the final vowel is dropped. The headings `शरणु घोष` and `शरणु प्रार्थन` are left as printed, because `शरणु` is not a Sanskrit word and the heading is not in Sanskrit.

Blurb: This is the worship of Śrī Ayyappa, Hariharaputra, the son of Hari and Hara, together with his consorts Pūrṇā and Puṣkalā, in sixteen services. It is the longer of two forms of his worship kept in this collection. The life-breath is placed in the image, and the Lord is meditated upon in two verses, dark and tender with a red lotus in his hand, and seated upon an elephant with the flower-arrow and the sugar-cane bow. Each of the services that follow is headed by two Vedic verses, one from the Puruṣa Sūkta and one from the Śrī Rudram, joining the praise of Hari and of Hara, and then by a verse of the rite. His limbs are worshipped under twenty-nine names. After the salutations come the verses of the Śāstā Pañcaratnam, each followed by the call for refuge, and the calls of the Śabarimala pilgrims, called and answered, in Malayalam and Tamil. The rite closes with a song of auspiciousness, a prayer in Tamil for pardon, the asking of pardon, and the Lord's return to his place.

hi: प्रार्थना — जाने और अनजाने, समझकर और बिना समझे, मेरे द्वारा किए गए सब पापों को क्षमा करके रक्षा कीजिए — हे सत्यस्वरूप, हे स्वर्ण के समान, हे अठारहवीं सीढ़ी पर निवास करने वाले, हे धनुर्धारी वीर, हे वीर मणिकण्ठ, हे काशी, रामेश्वरम्, पाण्ड्य देश और मलयाळम् के स्वामी। ओम्, हे श्री हरिहरसुत, हे आनन्दचित्त, हे अय्यनय्यप्पन्। हे स्वामी, आप ही शरण हैं, अय्यप्प।

--- unit 35 | section: क्षमाप्रार्थना ---
vidhi:
The asking of pardon, the dedication of the worship, and the receiving of the prasāda.
deva:
क्षमाप्रार्थना –
यस्य स्मृत्या च नामोक्त्या तपः पूजा क्रियादिषु ।
न्यूनं सम्पूर्णतां याति सद्यो वन्दे तमच्युतम् ॥
मन्त्रहीनं क्रियाहीनं भक्तिहीनं हरात्मज ।
यत्पूजितं मया देव परिपूर्णं तदस्तु ते ॥
अनया ध्यान आवाहनादि षोडशोपचार पूजया भगवान् सर्वात्मकः हरिहरपुत्र श्री अय्यप्प स्वामि सुप्रीतो सुप्रसन्नो वरदो भवतु ॥
श्री अय्यप्प स्वामि प्रसादं शिरसा गृह्णामि ॥
iast:
kṣamāprārthanā –
yasya smṛtyā ca nāmoktyā tapaḥ pūjā kriyādiṣu |
nyūnaṁ sampūrṇatāṁ yāti sadyo vande tamacyutam ||
mantrahīnaṁ kriyāhīnaṁ bhaktihīnaṁ harātmaja |
yatpūjitaṁ mayā deva paripūrṇaṁ tadastu te ||
anayā dhyāna āvāhanādi ṣoḍaśopacāra pūjayā bhagavān sarvātmakaḥ hariharaputra śrī ayyappa svāmi suprīto suprasanno varado bhavatu ||
śrī ayyappa svāmi prasādaṁ śirasā gṛhṇāmi ||
en: Asking pardon — By whose remembrance and by the uttering of whose name, whatever is lacking in austerity, worship, rite and the rest at once becomes complete — him, Acyuta, I praise. Lacking in mantra, lacking in rite, lacking in devotion though it was, O son of Hara — let what I have worshipped, O god, be complete for you. By this sixteen-fold worship beginning with meditation and invocation, may the Lord who is the self of all, Śrī Ayyappa Svāmī, the son of Hari and Hara, be well pleased, gracious and a giver of boons. I receive the prasāda of Śrī Ayyappa Svāmī upon my head.
tel: క్షమాప్రార్థన — ఎవరి స్మరణచేతను ఎవరి నామోచ్చారణచేతను తపస్సు, పూజ, క్రియ మొదలగువానియందలి లోపము వెంటనే సంపూర్ణతను పొందునో ఆ అచ్యుతుని వందించుచున్నాను. ఓ హరాత్మజా, మంత్రహీనమైనను క్రియాహీనమైనను భక్తిహీనమైనను, ఓ దేవా, నేను చేసిన పూజ నీకు పరిపూర్ణమగుగాక. ఈ ధ్యాన ఆవాహనాది షోడశోపచార పూజచే సర్వాత్మకుడైన భగవంతుడు, హరిహరపుత్రుడు, శ్రీ అయ్యప్ప స్వామి సుప్రీతుడును సుప్రసన్నుడును వరదుడును అగుగాక. శ్రీ అయ్యప్ప స్వామి ప్రసాదమును శిరస్సుతో గ్రహించుచున్నాను.
hi: क्षमाप्रार्थना — जिनके स्मरण और नामोच्चारण से तप, पूजा, क्रिया आदि में जो कमी है वह तत्काल पूर्ण हो जाती है — उन अच्युत की मैं वन्दना करता हूँ। हे हरात्मज, मन्त्रहीन, क्रियाहीन और भक्तिहीन होते हुए भी, हे देव, मेरे द्वारा की गई पूजा आपके लिए परिपूर्ण हो। इस ध्यान-आवाहन आदि षोडशोपचार पूजा से सर्वात्मा भगवान्, हरिहरपुत्र श्री अय्यप्प स्वामी सुप्रीत, सुप्रसन्न और वरद हों। श्री अय्यप्प स्वामी का प्रसाद सिर से ग्रहण करता हूँ।
