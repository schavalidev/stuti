Recension / paddhati: **Smārta, South Indian.** The Vaiṣṇava form of the opening is kept separately at `../vaishnava/01_purvanga_vidhanam.txt` and **the two must never be merged, harmonised or collated against each other.** This is the only pūjā of Vārāhī in this folder.

**This rite is built on the Śrī Sūkta**, like `18_mahalakshmi_visesha_shodashopachara_puja.txt`, `19_durga_shodashopachara_puja.txt` and `45_shyamala_devi_puja_vidhanam.txt`. Its second saṅkalpa and its closing dedication both name `श्रीसूक्त विधानेन`, and the fifteen ṛcs head fifteen services in the hymn's own order. **Every accented line of this page is identical to the accented lines of the Śyāmalā page `45`**, save one space before a daṇḍa; the two pages are one template. What is Vārāhī's own is everything else: a saṅkalpa for the quieting of trouble from the inner enemies (desire, anger, greed, delusion, pride and envy), a two-verse meditation on the boar-faced Goddess, the offerings a married woman wears (saffron, collyrium, the thread of good fortune, turmeric), a fourteen-name worship of the limbs, **the worship of her sixteen attendant powers** (Uccāṭanī, Śoṣaṇī, Māraṇī and the rest, each with her mistress), the fruit of the season, and at the mantra-flower **the mūla-mantra of Vārāhī**.

**The mūla-mantra at unit 34 is Tantric, not Vedic.** It is made of seed-syllables (`ऐं ग्लौं`, `ठः`, `हुं फट्`) and asks for the binding of the speech, mind, sight and movement of the wicked, understood as the six inner enemies (desire, anger, greed, delusion, pride and envy). Mantras of this kind are traditionally received from a teacher. It is kept here as the page prints it; whether a reader's edition should carry it is a decision for the user, and it is named here so that it is not missed.

What it does not have: **no pūrvāṅga of its own.** Use `03_purvanga_vidhanam.txt` before this file, and then the Gaṇapati pūjā. It does not print the hundred and eight names; it sends the worshipper to the list of names.

Blurb: This is the worship of Śrī Vārāhī Devī, the boar-faced Mother-goddess, in sixteen services. The worshipper first performs the opening portion and the pūjā of Gaṇapati. In the second resolution he asks that all trouble from the inner enemies be quieted (the six inner enemies: desire, anger, greed, delusion, pride and envy), that every harm be turned away, and that every undertaking succeed. The life-breath is placed in the image, and the Goddess is meditated upon in two verses: dark as a rain-cloud, three-eyed, riding a horse, holding the sword, the shield, the noose and the goad. Every one of the services is headed by a verse of the Śrī Sūkta, in the order of that hymn, with a verse of praise after it. Among them are the offerings a married woman wears — saffron, collyrium, the thread of good fortune and turmeric. Her limbs are worshipped with fourteen names, and she is worshipped together with the sixteen powers who attend her. The rite closes with her own mantra at the mantra-flower, prayer, the royal services, the asking of pardon, the receiving of the water from her feet, and the leave-taking.

--- unit 2 | section: पुनः सङ्कल्पः ---
vidhi:
The second resolution. It asks for the quieting of all trouble from the inner enemies (desire, anger, greed, delusion, pride and envy), the turning away of every harm and success in every undertaking, and it names the rule of the Śrī Sūkta.
deva:
पुनः सङ्कल्पम् –
पूर्वोक्त एवं गुण विशेषण विशिष्टायां शुभ तिथौ श्री वाराही मातृका देवता अनुग्रह प्रसाद सिद्धिद्वारा सर्वशत्रुबाधा शान्त्यर्थं, मम सर्वारिष्ट निवृत्त्यर्थं, सर्वकार्य सिद्ध्यर्थं, श्री वाराही देवता प्रीत्यर्थं श्रीसूक्त विधानेन ध्यान आवाहनादि षोडशोपचार पूजां करिष्ये ॥
iast:
punaḥ saṅkalpam –
pūrvokta evaṁ guṇa viśeṣaṇa viśiṣṭāyāṁ śubha tithau śrī vārāhī mātṛkā devatā anugraha prasāda siddhidvārā sarvaśatrubādhā śāntyarthaṁ, mama sarvāriṣṭa nivṛttyarthaṁ, sarvakārya siddhyarthaṁ, śrī vārāhī devatā prītyarthaṁ śrīsūkta vidhānena dhyāna āvāhanādi ṣoḍaśopacāra pūjāṁ kariṣye ||
en: The second resolution — On this auspicious day, marked by the qualities already named: through the grace and favour of Śrī Vārāhī, the Mother-goddess, for the quieting of all trouble from enemies (the six inner enemies: desire, anger, greed, delusion, pride and envy), for the turning away of every harm from me, for success in every undertaking, and for the pleasure of the deity Śrī Vārāhī, I shall perform, according to the rule of the Śrī Sūkta, the worship in sixteen services beginning with meditation and invocation.
tel: పునః సంకల్పము — పూర్వమునందు చెప్పబడిన గుణ విశేషణములతో కూడిన ఈ శుభ తిథియందు — శ్రీ వారాహీ మాతృకా దేవత యొక్క అనుగ్రహ ప్రసాద సిద్ధి ద్వారా సర్వ శత్రుబాధల (అరిషడ్వర్గము: కామ క్రోధ లోభ మోహ మద మాత్సర్యములు) శాంతి కొఱకును, నాకు సర్వ అరిష్టముల నివృత్తి కొఱకును, సర్వ కార్యసిద్ధి కొఱకును, శ్రీ వారాహీ దేవత ప్రీతి కొఱకును, శ్రీసూక్త విధానముచే ధ్యాన ఆవాహనాది షోడశోపచార పూజను చేసెదను.
hi: पुनः सङ्कल्प — पूर्वोक्त गुण विशेषणों से युक्त इस शुभ तिथि में — श्री वाराही मातृका देवता के अनुग्रह और प्रसाद की सिद्धि द्वारा सब शत्रुबाधाओं (छह अन्तःशत्रु: काम, क्रोध, लोभ, मोह, मद, मत्सर) की शान्ति के लिए, मेरे सब अरिष्टों की निवृत्ति के लिए, सब कार्यों की सिद्धि के लिए, और श्री वाराही देवता की प्रीति के लिए, श्रीसूक्त विधान से ध्यान और आवाहन आदि षोडशोपचार पूजा करूँगा।
