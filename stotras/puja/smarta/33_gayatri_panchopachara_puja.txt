**This rite is a japa, not a sixteen-service pūjā.** Its saṅkalpa resolves on `श्री गायत्री महामन्त्र जपं`, and the five services are offered around the japa: invocation of the Gāyatrī, the placing of her pādas on hands and body, meditation, the five services as the five elements (`लं हं यं रं वं`), then the japa, the same placings again, meditation again, the offering of the japa, and leave-taking. Its Gāyatrī material is the Taittirīya sandhyāvandana's own, and it stands in `../../vidhi/taittiriya/01_krishna_yajurveda_sandhyavandanam.txt` units 24–33 and the dismissal there. **The page does not print the Gāyatrī mantra itself at the japa**: it has the heading `॥ गायत्री मन्त्र जपम् ॥` and nothing under it. Nothing was supplied (unit 11).

Blurb: This is the worship of Śrī Gāyatrī Devī with five services, performed together with the japa of her mantra. It has no opening portion of its own. The worshipper first performs the opening portion and the worship of Gaṇapati, and then makes the resolution again. The Gāyatrī is invoked, and her limbs are named. The parts of her mantra are placed on the hands and on the body. She is meditated upon with five faces and ten hands. The five services are then offered to her as the five elements: sandal-paste as earth, flowers as space, incense as air, the lamp as fire, and food as nectar. After the japa the placings and the meditation are repeated. The japa is offered to her, she is given leave to depart, and pardon is asked for whatever was lacking.

--- unit 1 | section: पुनः सङ्कल्पः ---
vidhi:
The pūrvāṅga and the Gaṇapati pūjā are done first. Then the worshipper makes the resolution again. This rite is the japa of the Gāyatrī, and the five services are offered with it.
deva:
पुनः सङ्कल्पम् –
पूर्वोक्त एवं गुण विशेषण विशिष्टायां शुभ तिथौ श्री गायत्री देवता प्रीत्यर्थं पञ्चोपचार सहित श्री गायत्री महामन्त्र जपं करिष्ये ॥
iast:
punaḥ saṅkalpam –
pūrvokta evaṁ guṇa viśeṣaṇa viśiṣṭāyāṁ śubha tithau śrī gāyatrī devatā prītyarthaṁ pañcopacāra sahita śrī gāyatrī mahāmantra japaṁ kariṣye ||
en: The second resolution — On this auspicious day, marked by the qualities already named, for the pleasing of the deity Śrī Gāyatrī, I shall perform the japa of the great mantra of Śrī Gāyatrī, together with the five services.
tel: పునః సంకల్పము — ఇంతకు ముందు చెప్పిన గుణవిశేషణములతో కూడిన ఈ శుభ తిథియందు, శ్రీ గాయత్రీ దేవత ప్రీతి కొఱకు, పంచోపచారములతో కూడిన శ్రీ గాయత్రీ మహామంత్ర జపమును చేయుచున్నాను.
hi: पुनः सङ्कल्प — पूर्वोक्त गुण विशेषणों से युक्त इस शुभ तिथि में, श्री गायत्री देवता की प्रीति के लिए, पञ्चोपचार सहित श्री गायत्री महामन्त्र का जप करूँगा।

en: The invocation of the Gāyatrī — Oṁ, the one syllable, is Brahman. Agni is the deity. Brahman is the seer. The metre is gāyatrī. Its form is the Supreme Self. Its use is union with it.
tel: గాయత్రీ ఆవాహనము — ఓం అను ఏకాక్షరమే బ్రహ్మము. అగ్ని దేవత. బ్రహ్మ ఋషి. ఛందస్సు గాయత్రము. స్వరూపము పరమాత్మ. వినియోగము సాయుజ్యము.

--- unit 4 | section: गायत्री आवाहनम् ---
vidhi:
The goddess is asked to come. Then she is praised as the one by whom the sin of the day and of the night is taken away.
deva:
आया॑तु॒ वर॑दा दे॒वी॒ अ॒क्षरं॑ ब्रह्म॒ सम्मि॑तम् ।
गा॒य॒त्रीं᳚ छन्द॑सां मा॒तेदं ब्र॑ह्म जु॒षस्व॑ मे ॥
यदह्ना᳚त्कुरु॑ते पा॒पं॒ तदह्ना᳚त्प्रति॒ मुच्य॑ते ।
यद्रात्रिया᳚त्कुरु॑ते पा॒पं॒ तद्रात्रिया᳚त्प्रति॒ मुच्य॑ते ।
सर्व॑व॒र्णे म॑हादे॒वि॒ स॒न्ध्या वि॑द्ये स॒रस्व॑ति ।
iast:
āyātu varadā devī akṣaraṁ brahma sammitam |
gāyatrīṁ chandasāṁ mātedaṁ brahma juṣasva me ||
yadahnātkurute pāpaṁ tadahnātprati mucyate |
yadrātriyātkurute pāpaṁ tadrātriyātprati mucyate |
sarvavarṇe mahādevi sandhyā vidye sarasvati |
en: Let the goddess who gives boons come, she who is equal to the imperishable Brahman. O Gāyatrī, mother of the metres, accept this prayer of mine. Whatever sin one does by day, from that one is freed by day. Whatever sin one does by night, from that one is freed by night. O goddess of every colour, great goddess, knowledge of the twilight worship, Sarasvatī.
tel: వరములను ఇచ్చు దేవి, అక్షరమైన బ్రహ్మముతో సమానమైనది, వచ్చుగాక. ఓ గాయత్రీ, ఛందస్సులకు తల్లీ, నా ఈ ప్రార్థనను స్వీకరింపుము. పగలు చేసిన పాపమునుండి పగలే విముక్తి కలుగును. రాత్రి చేసిన పాపమునుండి రాత్రియే విముక్తి కలుగును. ఓ సర్వవర్ణా, మహాదేవీ, సంధ్యావిద్యా, సరస్వతీ.

--- unit 6 | section: गायत्री आवाहनम् ---
vidhi:
The formal invocation of the goddess in her five names. Then the limbs of the Gāyatrī are named: her seer, her metre, her deity, her mouth, head, heart and crest, her colour and her gotra.
deva:
गायत्रीमावा॑हया॒मि॒ । सावित्रीमावा॑हया॒मि॒ । सरस्वतीमावा॑हया॒मि॒ । छन्दर्षीनावा॑हया॒मि॒ । श्रियमावा॑हया॒मि॒ ॥
गायत्रिया गायत्री छन्दो विश्वामित्र ऋषिः सविता देवता अग्निर्मुखं ब्रह्मशिरो विष्णुर् हृदयग्ं रुद्रश्शिखा पृथिवी योनिः प्राणापानव्यानोदान समाना स प्राणा श्वेतवर्णा साङ्ख्यायन सगोत्रा गायत्री चतुर्विग्ं शत्यक्षरा त्रिपदा॑ षट्कु॒क्षि॒ः पञ्चशीर्षोपनयने वि॑नियो॒ग॒ः ॥
iast:
gāyatrīmāvāhayāmi | sāvitrīmāvāhayāmi | sarasvatīmāvāhayāmi | chandarṣīnāvāhayāmi | śriyamāvāhayāmi ||
gāyatriyā gāyatrī chando viśvāmitra ṛṣiḥ savitā devatā agnirmukhaṁ brahmaśiro viṣṇur hṛdayagṁ rudraśśikhā pṛthivī yoniḥ prāṇāpānavyānodāna samānā sa prāṇā śvetavarṇā sāṅkhyāyana sagotrā gāyatrī caturvigṁ śatyakṣarā tripadā ṣaṭkukṣiḥ pañcaśīrṣopanayane viniyogaḥ ||
en: I invoke Gāyatrī. I invoke Sāvitrī. I invoke Sarasvatī. I invoke the metres and the seers. I invoke Śrī. Of the Gāyatrī: the metre is gāyatrī, the seer is Viśvāmitra, the deity is Savitṛ. hi: मन्त्रहीन, क्रियाहीन, भक्तिहीन, हे महेश्वरि — जो कुछ मेरे द्वारा पूजित हुआ, हे देवि, वह आपके लिए परिपूर्ण हो। इस पञ्चोपचार पूजा से सर्वात्मिका भगवती श्री गायत्री देवी सुप्रीता, सुप्रसन्ना और वरदा हों।

en: Oṁ, peace, peace, peace. See the other stotras of Śrī Gāyatrī.
tel: ఓం శాంతిః శాంతిః శాంతిః. ఇతర శ్రీ గాయత్రీ స్తోత్రములను చూడుము.
hi: ओम् शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः। अन्य श्री गायत्री स्तोत्र देखिए।
