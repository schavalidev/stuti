Author: Anonymous (received smārta household rite). No compiler is named and none should be inferred.

Type: vidhi (ritual manual) — not a stotra. Uses the one-extra-field vidhi format documented in `../../vidhi/madhyandina/01_sandhyopasana_vidhi.txt`: a `vidhi:` field carrying the ritual instruction, then deva / iast / en / tel / hi.

Recension / paddhati: **Smārta, South Indian.** The Vaiṣṇava form of the opening is kept separately at `../vaishnava/01_purvanga_vidhanam.txt` and **the two must never be merged, harmonised or collated against each other.**

**This is a second recension of the Durgā pūjā, beside `08_durga_nitya_puja.txt`.** Both are smārta sixteen-service rites for the same Goddess, from two different publishers, and **neither file is to be corrected from the other.** Of the 249 lines of this page, **205 do not stand in `08` in any form** — 44 do.

**This rite is built on the Śrī Sūkta, and `08` is not.** The page's second saṅkalpa says the worship follows `श्रीसूक्त विधानेन`, its closing dedication says so again, and the claim holds: the sixteen services are headed by the ṛcs of the Śrī Sūkta in the hymn's own order, one to each service. Invocation takes ṛc 1, the seat ṛc 2, and so on to betel, which takes ṛc 15. This is the same plan as `18_mahalakshmi_visesha_shodashopachara_puja.txt` uses for Lakṣmī — **the Śrī Sūkta is used here for a rite of Durgā**, and the worshipper who wants the hymn as a hymn will find it at `../../devi/lakshmi/06_sri_suktam.txt`.

**Two places differ from that plan and are worth naming.** The eighth ṛc, which drives out Alakṣmī, heads the **thread of good fortune** here, where `18` puts it at the fan. And the garment carries a **second** Vedic verse beside its ṛc — Ṛgveda 9.97.50, from the Soma Pavamāna hymn, which asks for fair garments and is used here for that reason.

**Four more things this rite has that `08` does not:** a **prāṇapratiṣṭhā**, the placing of the life-breath in the image; the **four meditations of the Durgā Saptaśatī**, on Mahākālī, Mahālakṣmī, Mahāsarasvatī and Durgā; the offerings a married woman wears — the **thread of good fortune**, turmeric powder, saffron, vermilion and collyrium; and the gift of gold. Its second saṅkalpa is also the longest in this folder, naming illness, affliction from the planets and trouble from spirits among the things to be turned away.

What it does not have: **no pūrvāṅga of its own.** Use `03_purvanga_vidhanam.txt` before this file, and then the Gaṇapati pūjā.

Source / recension: base text `https://stotranidhi.com/hi/sri-durga-devi-shodashopachara-puja-in-sanskrit/` (fetched 2026-09-12), a Devanāgarī page carrying accent marks on its Vedic material.

Independent collation, in two parts.

**The Vedic material** was collated against **`../../devi/lakshmi/06_sri_suktam.txt`**, this corpus's Śrī Sūktam, whose base text is vignanam.org's accented edition — a different editorial lineage from this page. Every accented ṛc matched. As with `18`, **most apparent differences are saṁhitā-pāṭha against split, visarga-final printing**, which is a pāṭha difference inside one tradition and not a variant. Two readings differ in the same two places `18` differs (`श्रीर्मा देवी जुषताम्`, `ज्येष्ठामलक्ष्मीर्नाशयामि`), and as there the ṛc at the waving of the light, `सम्राजं च विराजं च`, is **not printed in that file at all**. Nothing was corrected in either direction.

**The four meditations** were collated against the Durgā Saptaśatī files of this corpus, which were written from the Gītā Press edition:

- The **Mahākālī** dhyāna (unit 5) stands **word for word** in `../../devi/durga_saptashati/01_madhukaitabha_vadha.txt` and in `../../devi/durga/40_navarna_vidhi.txt`.
- The **Mahāsarasvatī** dhyāna (unit 7) stands **word for word** in `../../devi/durga_saptashati/05_devi_duta_samvada.txt`.
- The **Mahālakṣmī** dhyāna (unit 6) differs in two places from `../../devi/durga/40_navarna_vidhi.txt`: this page reads `अक्षस्रक्परशूगदेषुकुलिशं` and `धनुः कुण्डिकां` where that file reads `अक्षस्रक्परशुं गदेषुकुलिशं` and `धनुष्कुण्डिकां`, and this page reads **`हस्तैः प्रवालप्रभां`** where that file reads **`हस्तैः प्रसन्नाननां`**. The last is a real difference of reading. Both are recorded and **neither is corrected from the other.**
- The **Durgā** dhyāna (unit 8), `सिंहस्था शशिशेखरा`, is **not in the Saptaśatī**. It stands in `08_durga_nitya_puja.txt`, this rite's sibling from the other publisher, and in `../../devi/durga/42_rgvedokta_devi_suktam.txt`.

The three verses of the asking of pardon (unit 41) stand word for word in `../../devi/durga/44_kshama_prarthana.txt` and `../../devi/durga/27_aparadha_kshamapana_stotram.txt`. The prayer verse `या देवी मधुकैटभप्रमथिनी` (unit 40) is **not** the verse of the same shape in `../../devi/main/13_amba_pancaratna_stotram.txt`, which reads `या देवी मधुकैटभासुररिपुर्या माहिषध्वंसिनी`. They are two different verses on one pattern, not two readings of one verse, and they are not to be reconciled. See `../../DEDUP_AUDIT.md`.

Defects in the base text, corrected with reasons, because stotranidhi is a convenience source and not a critical edition:

1. **`जानूनी` → `जानुनी`** (unit 26). `जानु` is neuter and its dual is `जानुनी`, with a short `u`. The Lakṣmī page of this same site prints `जानुनी` correctly, and so does `17_shiva_shodashopachara_puja.txt`.
2. **`चक्षुभ्यां` → `चक्षुर्भ्याम्`** (unit 23). `चक्षुस्` is an `s`-stem, and its dual instrumental is `चक्षुर्भ्याम्`. `चक्षुभ्यां` is not a form. The syllable count of the pāda is unchanged.
3. **`अकालमृत्यहरणं` → `अकालमृत्युहरणम्`** (unit 43). A dropped `u`. The corrected form stands in eleven other files of this corpus.

Observed and deliberately **not** emended:

- **`कर्पूरज्योतिमुत्पन्नं`** in the verse for the collyrium (unit 23). The expected form is `कर्पूरज्योतिरुत्पन्नं`. **No second witness prints this verse** — `08` does not have this service at all — so there is nothing to correct it from, and it stands as printed.
- **`देवी` for the vocative `देवि`** in the circumambulation verse (unit 37), and **`देवभूपार्हभोग्यं`** in the royal services (unit 39), where `18` reads `देवभूषाढ्यभोग्यं`. Both recorded, neither changed.
- **`सुप्रीता सुप्रसन्ना वरदा भवन्तु`** (unit 42), where the verb is plural although the subject is singular.
- **`सम्युक्तं`, `सम्युतम्`** for `संयुक्तं`, `संयुतम्`. This publisher writes anusvāra before a semivowel as `म्` throughout. It is an orthographic habit of the edition, not a defect.
- The word `[बध्नामि]` in square brackets at the thread of good fortune (unit 18). Those brackets are the source's own: either verb may be said.

Accent: **present on the Vedic material** — the ṛcs of the Śrī Sūkta, the prāṇapratiṣṭhā mantra, the Pavamāna verse at the garment, the Gāyatrī, the offerings to the five breaths, the Kātyāyanī Gāyatrī and the closing śānti. Udātta `॑` (U+0951), anudātta `॒` (U+0952), dīrgha svarita `᳚` (U+1CDA). The paurāṇika verses, including the four meditations, are printed unaccented, which is normal and not a defect. **No accent was inferred, derived or reconstructed.**

**A defect of the source's Veda pages, corrected throughout.** stotranidhi writes final visarga in its accented mantras as the ASCII colon `:` rather than as `ः` (U+0903) — `चक्षु॒:` for `चक्षु॒ः`. It looks right on the page and is wrong in the file: it breaks search, breaks collation against any real edition, and transliterates as a colon. Every such colon inside a `deva:` field here has been mapped to `ः` and the affected `iast:` fields regenerated.

**Never copy stotranidhi's IAST.** Its pages mark anusvāra `ṃ` and avagraha `’` where this corpus uses `ṁ` and `'`. Every `iast:` field here was generated from the Devanāgarī by `../../bin/dev2iast.py`.

Unit count: 43 units. Not a verse count in the stotra sense — the work is a prose order of service punctuated by mantras, and the numbering is this corpus's, not the source's.

Blurb: This is the worship of Śrī Durgā, the supreme Goddess, in sixteen services. It is a second and different form of the rite kept at `08_durga_nitya_puja.txt`, published by a different house, and the two should not be mixed. This page has no opening portion of its own. The worshipper first performs the pūrvāṅga and the Gaṇapati pūjā, to which the page sends him. In the second resolution he names plainly what he asks to have turned away: illness, affliction from the planets, trouble from spirits, and every misfortune. The life-breath is then placed in the image, and the Goddess is invoked with her weapons, her mount and her whole household. She is meditated upon in four verses, as Mahākālī, as Mahālakṣmī, as Mahāsarasvatī and as Durgā herself. Every one of the sixteen services that follow is headed by a verse of the Śrī Sūkta, taken in the order of that hymn. Among the services are those a married woman wears: the thread of good fortune at the neck, turmeric, saffron, vermilion and collyrium for the eyes. The rite closes with prayer, the asking of pardon, and the receiving of the water from her feet and of the food offered to her.

deva:
अनया श्रीसूक्त विधानेन ध्यानावाहनादि षोडशोपचार पूजनेन भगवती सर्वात्मिका श्रीमहाकाली श्रीमहालक्ष्मी श्रीमहासरस्वती स्वरूपिणी श्रीदुर्गा परादेवी सुप्रीता सुप्रसन्ना वरदा भवन्तु ॥

vidhi:
