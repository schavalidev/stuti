Recension / paddhati: **Smārta, South Indian.** The Vaiṣṇava form of the opening is kept separately at `../vaishnava/01_purvanga_vidhanam.txt` and **the two must never be merged, harmonised or collated against each other.**

**This is the worship of a Guru, not of a deity, and its services are shaped by that.** Śaṅkara Bhagavatpāda is worshipped as a saṁnyāsin and as the teacher of the world. Where a deity pūjā offers a sacred thread and ornaments, this rite offers a **full water-pot** (`पूर्णकुम्भ`) as to an ascetic received at the door, a **rosary of rudrākṣa** as his ornament, **sacred ash** with the sandal, and his **staff** (`दण्ड`). There is **no bīja, no nyāsa and no Vedic mantra** anywhere in it: every service is one anuṣṭubh verse of praise followed by the nāma-mantra, and the closing prayer is made of Guru verses — `गुरुर्ब्रह्मा`, `अखण्डमण्डलाकारं` and two verses on Śaṅkara as the one tree whose four branches are his four seats, the second of them naming Kāñcī. The rite ends with the sipping of the water from the Guru's feet and the dedication `कायेन वाचा`.

What it does not have: **no pūrvāṅga of its own.** Use `03_purvanga_vidhanam.txt` first, and then `42_haridra_ganapati_puja.txt`, as the page directs. It does not number its services.

Blurb: This is the worship of Śrī Śaṅkara Bhagavatpāda, the teacher of the world, who established the path of the Veda. The worshipper first performs the opening portion and the worship of Gaṇapati in turmeric. He then resolves to worship the Ācārya, and meditates on him as the home of Śruti, Smṛti and the Purāṇas, and a home of compassion. Each service is offered with a verse of praise. Because he is worshipped as a renunciant and a teacher, he is offered a full water-pot, a rosary of rudrākṣa, sacred ash with the sandal, and his staff, and his hundred and eight names are recited. The prayer that closes the worship is made of verses to the Guru, and of two verses that speak of Śaṅkara as a single tree whose four branches give shade to the world. The water from the Guru's feet is sipped, and all that has been done is offered to Nārāyaṇa.

--- unit 24 | section: प्रार्थना ---
vidhi:
The prayer, in seven verses: the common verses to the Guru, and two verses of Śaṅkara as a tree with four branches — his four seats — the second naming Kāñcī.
deva:
प्रार्थना –
गुरुर्ब्रह्मा गुरुर्विष्णुर्गुरुर्देवो महेश्वरः ।
गुरुरेव परं ब्रह्म तस्मै श्रीगुरवे नमः ॥
अखण्डमण्डलाकारं व्याप्तं येन चराचरम् ।
तत्पदं दर्शितं येन तस्मै श्रीगुरवे नमः ॥
अनेकजन्म सम्प्राप्त कर्मबन्ध विदाहिने ।
आत्मज्ञान प्रदानेन तस्मै श्रीगुरवे नमः ॥
विशुद्ध विज्ञानघनं शुचिहार्दं तमोनुदम् ।
दयासिन्धुं लोकबन्धुं शङ्करं नौमि सद्गुरुम् ॥
देहबुद्ध्या तु दासोऽस्मि जीवबुद्ध्या त्वदंशकः ।
आत्मबुद्ध्या त्वमेवाहमिति मे निश्चिता मतिः ॥
एकः शाखी शङ्कराख्यश्चतुर्धा
स्थानं भेजे तापशान्त्यै जनानाम् ।
शिष्यस्कन्धैः शिष्य शाखैर्महद्भिः
ज्ञानं पुष्पं यत्र मोक्षः प्रसूतिः ॥
गामाक्रम्य पदेऽधिकाञ्चि निबिडं स्कन्धैश्चतुर्भिस्तथा
व्यावृण्वन् भुवनान्तरं परिहरंस्तापं समोहज्वरम् ।
यः शाखी द्विजसंस्तुतः फलति तत् स्वाद्यं रसाख्यं फलं
तस्मै शङ्करपादाय महते तन्मः त्रिसन्ध्यं नमः ॥
iast:
prārthanā –
gururbrahmā gururviṣṇurgururdevo maheśvaraḥ |
gurureva paraṁ brahma tasmai śrīgurave namaḥ ||
akhaṇḍamaṇḍalākāraṁ vyāptaṁ yena carācaram |
tatpadaṁ darśitaṁ yena tasmai śrīgurave namaḥ ||
anekajanma samprāpta karmabandha vidāhine |
ātmajñāna pradānena tasmai śrīgurave namaḥ ||
viśuddha vijñānaghanaṁ śucihārdaṁ tamonudam |
dayāsindhuṁ lokabandhuṁ śaṅkaraṁ naumi sadgurum ||
dehabuddhyā tu dāso'smi jīvabuddhyā tvadaṁśakaḥ |
ātmabuddhyā tvamevāhamiti me niścitā matiḥ ||
ekaḥ śākhī śaṅkarākhyaścaturdhā
sthānaṁ bheje tāpaśāntyai janānām |
śiṣyaskandhaiḥ śiṣya śākhairmahadbhiḥ
jñānaṁ puṣpaṁ yatra mokṣaḥ prasūtiḥ ||
gāmākramya pade'dhikāñci nibiḍaṁ skandhaiścaturbhistathā
vyāvṛṇvan bhuvanāntaraṁ pariharaṁstāpaṁ samohajvaram |
yaḥ śākhī dvijasaṁstutaḥ phalati tat svādyaṁ rasākhyaṁ phalaṁ
tasmai śaṅkarapādāya mahate tanmaḥ trisandhyaṁ namaḥ ||
en: Prayer — The Guru is Brahmā, the Guru is Viṣṇu, the Guru is the god Maheśvara; the Guru is truly the supreme Brahman; salutation to that revered Guru. Salutation to that revered Guru, who showed that state by which the whole, in the form of an unbroken circle, pervades all that moves and does not move. Salutation to that revered Guru, who by the gift of the knowledge of the Self burns away the bonds of karma gathered through many births. I praise Śaṅkara, the true Guru, dense with pure knowledge, pure of heart, the dispeller of darkness, an ocean of compassion, a kinsman to the world. As a body I am your servant; as a living being I am a part of you; as the Self I am you yourself: this is my settled understanding. One tree, called Śaṅkara, took its place in four ways to bring relief from the heat to the people, with great branches that are his disciples and their disciples, where the flower is knowledge and the fruit is liberation. Planted in the earth, with its foot at Kāñci, spreading its four branches, covering the world and taking away the heat and the fever of delusion, the tree praised by the twice-born bears a sweet fruit called rasa; to that great Śaṅkarapāda, salutation at the three junctions of the day.

vidhi:
The water from the Guru's feet is sipped.
deva:
गुरुपादोदकप्राशनम् –
अविद्यामूलनाशाय जन्मकर्मनिवृत्तये ।
ज्ञानवैराग्यसिद्ध्यर्थं गुरुपादोदकं शुभम् ॥
गुरुपादोदकं प्राशयामि ।
iast:
gurupādodakaprāśanam –
avidyāmūlanāśāya janmakarmanivṛttaye |
jñānavairāgyasiddhyarthaṁ gurupādodakaṁ śubham ||
gurupādodakaṁ prāśayāmi |
en: Sipping the water from the Guru's feet — For the destruction of ignorance at its root, for the ending of birth and karma, and for the attainment of knowledge and dispassion, the water from the Guru's feet is auspicious. I sip the water from the Guru's feet.

--- unit 26 | section: समर्पणम् ---
vidhi:
The dedication of all that has been done.
deva:

कायेन वाचा मनसेन्द्रियैर्वा
बुद्ध्यात्मना वा प्रकृतेः स्वभावात् ।
करोमि यद्यत् सकलं परस्मै
नारायणायेति समर्पयामि ॥
अनया पूजया सर्वदेवात्मकः भगवान् श्रीजगद्गुरुः प्रीयताम् ॥
ओं तत् सत् ॥
iast:
samarpaṇam –
kāyena vācā manasendriyairvā
buddhyātmanā vā prakṛteḥ svabhāvāt |
karomi yadyat sakalaṁ parasmai
nārāyaṇāyeti samarpayāmi ||
anayā pūjayā sarvadevātmakaḥ bhagavān śrījagadguruḥ prīyatām ||
oṁ tat sat ||
en: Dedication — Whatever I do, with the body, with speech, with the mind or the senses, with the intellect or the self, or from the bent of my nature, all of it I offer to Nārāyaṇa, the Supreme.
