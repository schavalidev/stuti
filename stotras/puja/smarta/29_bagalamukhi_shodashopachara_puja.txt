Recension / paddhati: **Smārta, South Indian.** The Vaiṣṇava form of the opening is kept separately at `../vaishnava/01_purvanga_vidhanam.txt` and **the two must never be merged, harmonised or collated against each other.** No Bagalāmukhī pūjā stands among `04`–`13`, so there is no sibling from the other publisher.

**This is a household rite, not a Tāntric one.** Bagalāmukhī is one of the ten Mahāvidyās, and her worship is often a rite of dīkṣā with its own mantra. This page has none of that: no bīja, no nyāsa, no mūla-mantra. It is the same publisher's ordinary sixteen services, with paurāṇika verses and the Śrī Sūkta, addressed to Bagalāmukhī. The question of adhikāra that `23_mahaganapati_chaturavrutti_tarpanam.txt` raises does not arise here.

**This rite is built on the Śrī Sūkta.** The page's second resolution says the worship follows `श्रीसूक्त विधानेन`, its dedication says so again, and the claim holds: the services from the invocation to the waving of the light are headed by the fifteen ṛcs of the Śrī Sūkta in the hymn's own order, and the waving of the light by the sixteenth verse, `यः शुचिः प्रयतो भूत्वा`, which follows the hymn and speaks of its recitation. This is the same plan as `18_mahalakshmi_visesha_shodashopachara_puja.txt` and `19_durga_shodashopachara_puja.txt`, from the same publisher, and the three should be read together. Here, as in `19`, **the Śrī Sūkta is used for a goddess other than Lakṣmī.** It differs from `19` in two places of the plan: the eighth ṛc heads the sacred thread, not a thread of good fortune; and the waving of the light takes the sixteenth verse, where `19` takes `सम्राजं च विराजं च`.

**Its second resolution names what the worship is for**: victory for one's own side in a court of law, the calming of affliction worked through the planets, and the quick return of health by the removal of grievous illness. It is a rite resolved upon in trouble.

**What else it has:** a **meditation in three verses** on the Goddess who holds the enemy's tongue; the **bath in the five nectars given one substance at a time**, each with its own verse; a separate **bath in fragrant water**; an **eight-limbed prostration** and a **prayer verse** of its own; and a **verse of taking leave**.

What it does not have: **no directions at the top**; the page begins at the second resolution. Perform `03_purvanga_vidhanam.txt` and the Gaṇapati pūjā before it. No prāṇapratiṣṭhā, no worship of the limbs, no hundred and eight names, no offering to the five breaths at the food-offering, and no mantra-flower beyond the name.

Blurb: This is the worship of Śrī Bagalāmukhī, the goddess clad in yellow who holds the three worlds still, in sixteen services. It is a household rite, made with verses and with the Śrī Sūkta, and it asks for no initiation. In the second resolution the worshipper names plainly what he seeks: victory for his side in a court of law, relief from affliction worked through the planets, and a quick return to health. The Goddess is meditated upon in three verses: seated on a golden throne in the midst of the ocean of nectar, holding the mace, and holding fast the tongue of the enemy. Each of the services that follow is headed by a verse of the Śrī Sūkta, taken in the order of that hymn. She is bathed in each of the five nectars in turn, and in fragrant water. The rite closes with circumambulation, the eight-limbed prostration, a prayer that calls her Bagalā, the asking of pardon, and the receiving of the water from her feet.

en: Taking leave — This worship, O goddess, has been performed by me as far as my strength allows. Taking it, go for our protection to your own supreme abode. Oṁ, salutation to the deity Śrī Pratyaṅgirā. I send her to her own place. Oṁ, peace, peace, peace.
tel: విసర్జనము — ఓ దేవీ, ఈ పూజ నాచే యథాశక్తి చేయబడినది. దీనిని స్వీకరించి మా రక్షణ కొఱకు నీ ఉత్తమ స్థానమునకు వెళ్ళుము. ఓం శ్రీ ప్రత్యంగిరా దేవతకు నమస్కారము. యథాస్థానమునకు ఉద్వాసనము చేయుచున్నాను. ఓం శాంతిః శాంతిః శాంతిః.
hi: विसर्जन — हे देवि, यह पूजा मेरे द्वारा यथाशक्ति सम्पन्न की गई है। इसे ग्रहण करके हमारी रक्षा के लिए अपने उत्तम स्थान को जाइए। ओम् श्री प्रत्यङ्गिरा देवता को नमस्कार। यथास्थान उद्वासन करता हूँ। ओम् शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः।
