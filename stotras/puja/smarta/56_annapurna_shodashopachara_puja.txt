Blurb: This is a worship of Annapūrṇā in sixteen services, built on the Śrī Sūkta: each service is headed by one verse of that Vedic hymn, and followed by a verse of praise and the offering under her name. The goddess is placed in the image with the placing of the life-breath, meditated upon, and worshipped with the hundred and eight names of Annapūrṇā printed in full. Then come incense, the lamp, the food-offering, betel, the waving of the light, the mantra-flower, circumambulation, the royal services of a queen, a prayer, the asking of pardon and the receiving of the water and the prasāda. The opening portion and the Gaṇapati worship are not included, and are performed first.

en: The second resolution — On this auspicious day, marked by the qualities already named, with Śrī Annapūrṇā Devī as the deity addressed, and for the pleasing of Śrī Annapūrṇā Devī, with such substances as are to be had, with such services as are to be had, with such observance as is possible and in such manner as is possible, in the manner of the Śrī Sūkta, I shall perform to the best of my power the worship in sixteen services, beginning with meditation and invocation.
