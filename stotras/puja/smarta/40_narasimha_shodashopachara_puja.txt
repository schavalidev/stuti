Blurb: This is the worship of Śrī Lakṣmī-Nṛsiṁha in sixteen services, by the rule of the Puruṣa Sūkta. The worshipper first performs the opening portion of the pūjā. The rite begins with a second resolution before the image. The breath is established in the image, and the lord is invoked with his weapons, his mount and his retinue. He is meditated upon seated on the lion-throne, with Lakṣmī at his left side. Each of the sixteen services is headed by a verse of the Puruṣa Sūkta and followed by a verse of praise. Among them are the honey-mixture, the bath of five nectars, and the worship of his limbs with twenty of his names. The rite closes with circumambulation, prostration, the royal services, the asking of pardon, and the receiving of the water from his feet.

--- unit 1 | section: पुनः सङ्कल्पः ---
vidhi:
The opening portion of the pūjā is already done. Before the image, the worshipper makes the resolution a second time. He names the rule the rite will follow: the Puruṣa Sūkta, one verse of which heads each service.

पुनः सङ्कल्पम् –

iast:

en: The second resolution — On this auspicious day, marked by the qualities already named: for the attainment of the grace and favour of the lord Nṛsiṁha together with Lakṣmī, and for the pleasing of the lord Śrī Lakṣmī-Nṛsiṁha, I shall perform, by the rule of the Puruṣa Sūkta, the worship in sixteen services, beginning with meditation and invocation.

--- unit 4 | section: ध्यानम् ---

Meditation on Lakṣmī-Nṛsiṁha, seated on the lion-throne with Lakṣmī at his left side.
deva:

सव्ये चक्रधरं च निर्भयकरं वामेन चापं वरम् ।
सर्वाधीशकृतान्तपत्रममलं श्रीवत्सवक्षःस्थलं
वन्दे देवमुनीन्द्रवन्दितपदं लक्ष्मीनृसिंहं विभुम् ॥
ओं श्री लक्ष्मीनृसिंहाय नमः ध्यायामि ध्यानम् समर्पयामि ।
iast:
dhyānam –
lakṣmīśobhitavāmabhāgamamalaṁ siṁhāsane sundaraṁ
savye cakradharaṁ ca nirbhayakaraṁ vāmena cāpaṁ varam |
sarvādhīśakṛtāntapatramamalaṁ śrīvatsavakṣaḥsthalaṁ
vande devamunīndravanditapadaṁ lakṣmīnṛsiṁhaṁ vibhum ||

--- unit 30 | section: क्षमाप्रार्थना ---
vidhi:
Asking pardon, in three verses.
deva:
क्षमा प्रार्थन –
अपराध सहस्राणि क्रियन्तेऽहर्निशं मया ।
दासोऽयमिति मां मत्वा क्षमस्व पुरुषोत्तमा ॥
आवाहनं न जानामि न जानामि विसर्जनम् ।
पूजाविधिं न जानामि क्षमस्व कमलापते ॥
मन्त्रहीनं क्रियाहीनं भक्तिहीनं जनार्दन ।
यत्पूजितं मया देव परिपूर्णं तदस्तुते ॥
iast:
kṣamā prārthana –
aparādha sahasrāṇi kriyante'harniśaṁ mayā |
dāso'yamiti māṁ matvā kṣamasva puruṣottamā ||
āvāhanaṁ na jānāmi na jānāmi visarjanam |
pūjāvidhiṁ na jānāmi kṣamasva kamalāpate ||
mantrahīnaṁ kriyāhīnaṁ bhaktihīnaṁ janārdana |
yatpūjitaṁ mayā deva paripūrṇaṁ tadastute ||
en: Asking pardon — Thousands of offences are committed by me day and night. Thinking of me as your servant, forgive me, O Supreme Person. I do not know how to invoke you, and I do not know how to take leave of you. I do not know the rule of worship. Forgive me, O lord of Kamalā. Whatever I have worshipped, O Janārdana, O god, lacking in mantra, lacking in rite, lacking in devotion — let it be made complete for you.
tel: క్షమా ప్రార్థన — రాత్రింబవళ్ళు నాచే వేలకొలది అపరాధములు చేయబడుచున్నవి. ఇతడు దాసుడు అని నన్ను భావించి, ఓ పురుషోత్తమా, క్షమింపుము. ఆవాహనము ఎఱుగను, విసర్జనము ఎఱుగను. పూజా విధిని ఎఱుగను. ఓ కమలాపతీ, క్షమింపుము. ఓ జనార్దనా, ఓ దేవా, మంత్రహీనముగా, క్రియాహీనముగా, భక్తిహీనముగా నేను ఏది పూజించితినో అది నీకు పరిపూర్ణమగుగాక.
hi: क्षमा प्रार्थना — दिन-रात मेरे द्वारा सहस्रों अपराध किए जाते हैं। यह दास है, ऐसा मानकर, हे पुरुषोत्तम, मुझे क्षमा कीजिए। मैं आवाहन नहीं जानता, विसर्जन नहीं जानता। पूजा की विधि नहीं जानता। हे कमलापति, क्षमा कीजिए। हे जनार्दन, हे देव, मन्त्रहीन, क्रियाहीन, भक्तिहीन जो कुछ मैंने पूजा है, वह आपके लिए परिपूर्ण हो।

--- unit 31 | section: क्षमाप्रार्थना ---
vidhi:
The closing dedication, which names the rule of the Puruṣa Sūkta a second time.
deva:
अनया पुरुषसूक्त विधान पूर्वक ध्यान आवाहनादि षोडशोपचार पूजया भगवान् सर्वात्मकः श्री लक्ष्मीनृसिंह स्वामी सुप्रीता सुप्रसन्ना वरदा भवन्तु ॥
iast:
anayā puruṣasūkta vidhāna pūrvaka dhyāna āvāhanādi ṣoḍaśopacāra pūjayā bhagavān sarvātmakaḥ śrī lakṣmīnṛsiṁha svāmī suprītā suprasannā varadā bhavantu ||
en: By this worship in sixteen services, beginning with meditation and invocation, preceded by the rule of the Puruṣa Sūkta, may the Bhagavān, the Self of all, the lord Śrī Lakṣmī-Nṛsiṁha, be well pleased, gracious, and the giver of boons.

--- unit 32 | section: तीर्थप्रसाद ग्रहणम् ---
vidhi:
Receiving the water from the lord's feet and the food offered to him, and the closing peace.
deva:
तीर्थप्रसाद ग्रहणम् –

समस्तपापक्षयकरं श्री लक्ष्मीनृसिंह पादोदकं पावनं शुभम् ॥
श्री लक्ष्मीनृसिंहाय नमः प्रसादं शिरसा गृह्णामि ।

iast:
tīrthaprasāda grahaṇam –
akālamṛtyuharaṇaṁ sarvavyādhinivāraṇam |
samastapāpakṣayakaraṁ śrī lakṣmīnṛsiṁha pādodakaṁ pāvanaṁ śubham ||
śrī lakṣmīnṛsiṁhāya namaḥ prasādaṁ śirasā gṛhṇāmi |

en: Receiving the water and the prasāda — The water from the feet of Śrī Lakṣmī-Nṛsiṁha is purifying and auspicious. It removes untimely death, wards off every disease, and destroys all sin. Salutation to Śrī Lakṣmī-Nṛsiṁha. I receive the prasāda upon my head. Oṁ, peace, peace, peace.
