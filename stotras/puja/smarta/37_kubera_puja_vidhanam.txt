Type: vidhi (ritual manual) — not a stotra. Uses the one-extra-field vidhi format documented in `../../vidhi/madhyandina/01_sandhyopasana_vidhi.txt`: a `vidhi:` field carrying the ritual instruction, then deva / iast / en / tel / hi.

Recension / paddhati: **Smārta, South Indian.** The Vaiṣṇava form of the opening is kept separately at `../vaishnava/01_purvanga_vidhanam.txt` and **the two must never be merged, harmonised or collated against each other.**

Note: **This is the only pūjā of Kubera in the corpus.** It is a sixteen-service worship of Kubera, the lord of wealth and king of the yakṣas, for the increase of wealth, grain and prosperity in the household.

**This rite is built on the Śrī Sūkta, a hymn to Lakṣmī, used here for Kubera.** The page's second resolution says the worship follows `श्री सूक्त विधानेन`, its closing dedication says so again, and the claim holds: the services from invocation to betel are headed by ṛcs 1 to 15 of the Śrī Sūkta in the hymn's own order. The waving of the light takes the further verse `सम्राजं च विराजं च`, as `18_mahalakshmi_visesha_shodashopachara_puja.txt` and `19_durga_shodashopachara_puja.txt` also do. Kubera and Lakṣmī are worshipped together in South Indian households, and the water from the feet at the close is named the water of `श्री लक्ष्मी कुबेर`.

**The mantra-flower is Kubera's own.** It is the Vedic mantra `राजाधिराजाय प्रसह्यसाहिने`, which also closes the mantra-puṣpa at `../../veda/taittiriya/07_mantra_pushpam.txt`; then a Kubera Gāyatrī, printed without accent; then two short mantras with the seed-syllables `श्रीं ह्रीं क्लीं`; and then the Kubera mantra that asks for wealth and grain. **The seed-syllable mantras and the wealth mantra are tantric, not Vedic,** although the last ends in `स्वाहा`.

What it does not have: **no pūrvāṅga of its own, no prāṇapratiṣṭhā** — only a single line calling Kubera into the image — and **no aṣṭottara printed**. The page sends the worshipper to the pūrvāṅga and to a short sixteen-service Mahāgaṇapati pūjā first; use `03_purvanga_vidhanam.txt` and `15_mahaganapati_shodashopachara_puja.txt`. **No Kubera aṣṭottaraśatanāmāvalī exists in this corpus yet;** the only Kubera text is `../../kubera/01_sri_kubera_stotram.txt`.

Blurb: This is the worship of Śrī Kubera, the lord of wealth and king of the yakṣas, in sixteen services. The page has no opening portion of its own. The worshipper first performs the pūrvāṅga and a short worship of Mahāgaṇapati. In the second resolution he asks for the welfare of his whole family: long life and health, victory and freedom from fear, the growth of children and grandchildren, and wealth, gold, vehicles and cattle. Kubera is called into the image with his limbs, his weapons, his mount and his attendants. He is meditated upon as seated on a man-drawn chariot, adorned with a crown, the friend of Śiva, holding the mace and giving boons. Every service from the invocation to the betel is headed by a verse of the Śrī Sūkta, and each is followed by a verse addressed to Kubera. The mantra-flower carries Kubera's own mantras. The rite closes with prayer, the royal services, the asking of pardon, and the receiving of the water from the feet of Lakṣmī and Kubera.

अनया श्रीसूक्त विधान पूर्वक ध्यान आवाहनादि षोडशोपचार पूजनेन भगवान् सर्वात्मकः श्री कुबेर स्वामि सुप्रीतो सुप्रसन्नो वरदो भवन्तु ॥
iast:
anayā śrīsūkta vidhāna pūrvaka dhyāna āvāhanādi ṣoḍaśopacāra pūjanena bhagavān sarvātmakaḥ śrī kubera svāmi suprīto suprasanno varado bhavantu ||
en: By this worship in sixteen services, beginning with meditation and invocation, performed by the rule of the Śrī Sūkta, may the Lord, the Self of all, Śrī Kubera, be well pleased, gracious, and the giver of boons.
tel: శ్రీసూక్త విధానపూర్వకముగా చేసిన ఈ ధ్యాన ఆవాహనాది షోడశోపచార పూజనముచే సర్వాత్మకుడైన భగవంతుడు శ్రీ కుబేర స్వామి సుప్రీతుడును సుప్రసన్నుడును వరదుడును అగుగాక.
hi: श्रीसूक्त विधानपूर्वक किये गये इस ध्यान और आवाहन आदि षोडशोपचार पूजन से सर्वात्मक भगवान् श्री कुबेर स्वामी सुप्रीत, सुप्रसन्न और वरद हों।

--- unit 31 | section: तीर्थप्रसाद ग्रहणम् ---
vidhi:
Receiving the water from the feet and the food offered. The Vedic closing peace is said at the end.
deva:
तीर्थप्रसाद ग्रहणम् –
अकालमृत्युहरणं सर्वव्याधिनिवारणम् ।
समस्तपापक्षयकरं श्री लक्ष्मी कुबेर पादोदकं पावनं शुभम् ॥
श्री कुबेर स्वामि प्रसादं शिरसा गृह्णामि ।
ओं शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः ।
iast:
tīrthaprasāda grahaṇam –
akālamṛtyuharaṇaṁ sarvavyādhinivāraṇam |
samastapāpakṣayakaraṁ śrī lakṣmī kubera pādodakaṁ pāvanaṁ śubham ||
śrī kubera svāmi prasādaṁ śirasā gṛhṇāmi |
oṁ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ |
en: Receiving the water and the prasāda — It removes untimely death and wards off every disease. It destroys all sin. The water from the feet of Śrī Lakṣmī and Kubera is purifying and auspicious. I receive upon my head the prasāda of Śrī Kubera, the lord. Oṁ, peace, peace, peace.
tel: తీర్థప్రసాద గ్రహణము — అకాలమృత్యువును హరించునదియు, సర్వ వ్యాధులను నివారించునదియు, సమస్త పాపములను క్షయింపజేయునదియు, పవిత్రమును శుభమును అగు శ్రీ లక్ష్మీ కుబేరుల పాదోదకము. శ్రీ కుబేర స్వామి ప్రసాదమును శిరస్సున గ్రహించుచున్నాను. ఓం శాంతిః శాంతిః శాంతిః.
hi: तीर्थप्रसाद ग्रहण — अकालमृत्यु को हरने वाला, सब व्याधियों का निवारण करने वाला, समस्त पापों का क्षय करने वाला, पावन और शुभ श्री लक्ष्मी कुबेर का पादोदक। श्री कुबेर स्वामी का प्रसाद सिर पर ग्रहण करता हूँ। ओम् शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः।
