Blurb: This is a worship of Mahiṣāsuramardinī, the goddess who slew the buffalo demon, in sixteen services built on the Śrī Sūkta: each service is headed by one verse of that Vedic hymn, and followed by a verse of praise and the offering under her name. The goddess is placed in the image with the placing of the life-breath, meditated upon, and worshipped with a hundred and eight names, each beginning with "great". Then come incense, the lamp, the food-offering, betel, the waving of the light, the mantra-flower, circumambulation, the royal services of a queen, a prayer, the asking of pardon and the receiving of the water and the prasāda. The opening portion and the Gaṇapati worship are not included, and are performed first.
