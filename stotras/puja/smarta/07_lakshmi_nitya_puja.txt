Recension / paddhati: **Smārta, South Indian.** Pūjā-vidhāna divides by *paddhati* the way sandhyāvandana divides by śākhā. The Śrīvaiṣṇava form of the opening is kept separately at `../vaishnava/01_purvanga_vidhanam.txt` and **the two must never be merged, harmonised or collated against each other.** A Śrīvaiṣṇava should not use this file — the point matters here, since Lakṣmī is the central deity of that tradition too, and its rite to her is a different rite.

**Relation to `01_nitya_puja_vidhanam.txt`.** `01` is the generic template, printing `श्री इष्ट देवता` as a slot. This is the same rite with the slot filled in for Lakṣmī by the source itself, published as a complete self-contained order of service and transcribed so. **Units 1–29 are the shared pūrvāṅga, word-for-word the template's**; units 30–60 are the Lakṣmī ṣoḍaśopacāra proper. Measured against the template, 216 of this page's 319 lines are shared and 103 are new — 68% reuse, the lowest of the deity files so far, and the reason is given below. Its siblings are `04_ganapati_nitya_puja.txt`, `05_shiva_nitya_puja.txt` and `06_vishnu_nitya_puja.txt`.

**The distinctive thing about this rite: the Śrīsūkta is threaded verse by verse through the services.** Where the Gaṇapati and Śiva rites give one or two Vedic mantras at fixed points, this one sets a **successive verse of the Śrīsūkta** (Ṛgveda Khilāni 5.87) before the paurāṇika verse at seven separate upacāras, so the sūkta unfolds across the whole worship:

| unit | upacāra | Śrīsūkta verse |
|---|---|---|
| 32 | dhyāna | `हिर॑ण्यवर्णां॒ हरि॑णीं` |
| 33 | āvāhana | `ता-म्म॒ आव॑ह॒ जात॑वेदो` |
| 35 | pādya | `अश्वपूर्वां रथमध्यां` |
| 39 | vastra | `उपै॑तु॒ मा-न्दे॑वस॒खः` |
| 41 | gandha | `ग॒न्ध॒द्वा॒रा-न्दु॑राध॒र्​षा᳚म्` |
| 45 | dhūpa | `आपः॑ सृ॒जन्तु॑ स्नि॒ग्धा॒नि॒` |
| 46 | dīpa | `आ॒र्द्रा-म्पु॒ष्करि॑णी-म्पु॒ष्टिम्` |

That accounts for most of the extra 103 lines. The **Lakṣmī Gāyatrī** stands at the mantrapuṣpa (unit 52): `ओ-म्म॒हा॒दे॒व्यै च॑ वि॒द्महे॑ विष्णुप॒त्नी च॑ धीमहि । तन्नो॑ लक्ष्मीः प्रचो॒दया᳚त् ॥`.

Accent: **present throughout.** Udātta `॑` (U+0951), anudātta `॒` (U+0952), dīrgha svarita `᳚` (U+1CDA); the praṇava before a spirant takes the guttural nasal, written `ओग्ं` (`ogṁ`). The source prints its accented mantras in a sandhi-joined orthography that hyphenates across word boundaries, kept exactly as printed and carried into the IAST. **No accent was inferred, derived or reconstructed.**

Source / recension: base text `https://vignanam.org/devanagari/lakshmi-nitya-puja-vidhanam.html` (fetched 2026-09-12), a **native accented Devanāgarī edition** declaring itself "शुद्ध देवनागरी with the right anusvaras marked"; its Telugu page carries an identical accent count, so **nothing here was transliterated by us.** This page carries 199 accent marks, the most of any of the ten deity pages — consistent with its threading of the Śrīsūkta.

Defect in the base text, corrected with reason: **`उपमश्रवस्तवम्` → `उपमश्रवस्तमम्`** at unit 27. The mantra is Ṛgveda 2.23.1, whose transmitted reading is `upamaśravastamam`, a superlative in *-tama-*; `-stavam` is not construable. The same slip stands on this site's Gaṇapati, Śiva and Viṣṇu pages and is corrected in all of them; the site's own generic template prints it correctly, so the defect is confined to the deity pages. As there, this page also splits `क॒वि-ङ्क॑वी॒नाम्` as `क॒वि-ङ्क॑वी॒नां उप॒-`; a word-division difference in the printed sandhi, kept as printed.

Not collated: the paurāṇika verses shared with the template, and the Lakṣmī upacāra and dhyāna verses, for which no critical edition exists. `या सा पद्मासनस्था` and `लक्ष्मीं क्षीरसमुद्रराजतनयाम्` are among the most widely printed Lakṣmī dhyāna verses and are given as this source prints them. The Śrīsūkta verses, being Vedic, were checked against the sūkta's own received order and stand in it.

Blurb: This is the daily pūjā of Lakṣmī in the smārta form, complete in sixty units. It follows the same order as the other deity pūjās in this folder, with Lakṣmī as the chosen deity. After the materials and the opening acts, the worshipper honours the water-vessel, the conch and the bell, and worships Gaṇapati first so that obstacles may be removed. Lakṣmī is then meditated upon in three units. What sets this rite apart is that the Śrīsūkta is threaded through it verse by verse: a successive verse of that sūkta is said before the ordinary verse at the meditation, the invocation, the water for the feet, the garment, the sandal paste, the incense and the lamp, so that the whole sūkta unfolds across the worship. The Lakṣmī Gāyatrī is said at the offering of flowers with mantra. The rite closes with the asking of pardon, the dismissal, and the receiving of the prasāda. Every mantra carries its accent marks.

vidhi:
Asking pardon — a thousand offences are committed day and night; the worshipper asks to be counted a servant.
deva:
अपराधसहस्राणि क्रियन्तेऽहर्निशं मया ।

aparādhasahasrāṇi kriyante'harniśaṁ mayā |
dāso'yamiti māṁ matvā kṣamasva parameśvari ||
en: A thousand offences are committed by me day and night: thinking 'this one is my servant', forgive me, supreme Goddess.
tel: రేయింబవళ్లు నాచేత వేలాది అపరాధాలు చేయబడుతున్నాయి; 'ఇతడు నా దాసుడు' అని తలచి, ఓ పరమేశ్వరీ, నన్ను క్షమించు.

--- unit 58 | section: क्षमाप्रार्थना ---
vidhi:
Surrendering the act itself, and the closing declaration of the fruit of the worship. Note the feminine `सुप्रीता सुप्रसन्ना वरदा`.
deva:
कायेन वाचा मनसेन्द्रियैर्वा
बुद्ध्यात्मना वा प्रकृतेः स्वभावात् ।
करोमि यद्यत् सकलं परस्मै
नारायणायेति समर्पयामि ॥
अनया ध्यान-आवाहनादि-षोडशोपचार पूजया श्रीमहालक्ष्मी देवी सुप्रीता सुप्रसन्ना वरदा भवतु ॥
iast:
kāyena vācā manasendriyairvā
buddhyātmanā vā prakṛteḥ svabhāvāt |
karomi yadyat sakalaṁ parasmai
nārāyaṇāyeti samarpayāmi ||
anayā dhyāna-āvāhanādi-ṣoḍaśopacāra pūjayā śrīmahālakṣmī devī suprītā suprasannā varadā bhavatu ||
en: Whatever I do with body, speech, mind or senses, with intellect or self or by the nature of my own disposition, all of it I offer to the supreme Nārāyaṇa. By this sixteen-fold worship beginning with meditation and invocation, may the goddess Mahālakṣmī be well pleased, gracious, and a giver of boons.
tel: శరీరంతో, వాక్కుతో, మనస్సుతో, ఇంద్రియాలతో, బుద్ధితో, ఆత్మతో, ప్రకృతి స్వభావంతో నేను ఏది చేసినా అదంతా పరమాత్ముడైన నారాయణునికి సమర్పిస్తున్నాను. ఈ ధ్యాన-ఆవాహనాది షోడశోపచార పూజచేత శ్రీమహాలక్ష్మీదేవి సుప్రీత, సుప్రసన్న, వరద అగుగాక.

--- unit 60 | section: उद्वासनम् ---
vidhi:
The taking of the prasāda, and the close.

समस्तपापक्षयकरं देवता पादोदकं शुभम् ॥

akālamṛtyuharaṇaṁ sarvavyādhinivāraṇam |
