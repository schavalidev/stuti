Blurb: This is the worship of Śrī Ayyappa, Hariharaputra, the son of Hari and Hara, together with his consorts Pūrṇā and Puṣkalā, in sixteen services. It is the shorter of two forms of his worship kept in this collection. Before it the worshipper performs the opening portion, the Gaṇapati pūjā and the worship of Śrī Subrahmaṇya, the Lord's brother. The Lord is meditated upon in two verses. The first services are made with his name alone, from the invocation to the fragrant substances, among which sacred ash comes first. His limbs are worshipped under twenty-eight names, and his root mantra is recited. The salutation is made with the verses of the Śāstā Pañcaratnam, each followed by the call for refuge. After the asking of pardon come the calls of the Śabarimala pilgrims, called and answered, in Malayalam and Tamil, and the rite closes with the Lord's return to his place.
