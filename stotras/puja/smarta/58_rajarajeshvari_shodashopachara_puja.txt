Blurb: This is a worship of Rājarājeśvarī in sixteen services, built on the Śrī Sūkta: each service is headed by one verse of that Vedic hymn, and followed by a verse of praise and the offering under her name. The goddess is placed in the image with the placing of the life-breath, meditated upon with her seed-syllables, and worshipped with a hundred and eight names printed in full. Then come incense, the lamp, the food-offering, betel, the waving of the light, the mantra-flower, circumambulation, the royal services of a queen, a prayer, the asking of pardon and the receiving of the water and the prasāda. The opening portion and the Gaṇapati worship are not included, and are performed first.

--- unit 1 | section: पुनः सङ्कल्पः ---
vidhi:
The second resolution. It names the rule the rite follows, the manner of the Śrī Sūkta, which the sixteen services below bear out.

iast:

en: The second resolution — On this auspicious day, marked by the qualities already named, with Śrī Rājarājeśvarī as the deity addressed, and for the pleasing of Śrī Rājarājeśvarī, with such substances as are to be had, with such services as are to be had, with such observance as is possible and in such manner as is possible, in the manner of the Śrī Sūkta, I shall perform to the best of my power the worship in sixteen services, beginning with meditation and invocation.

--- unit 5 | section: षोडशोपचार पूजा ---
vidhi:
The invocation. From here each service is headed by a ṛc of the Śrī Sūkta, taken in the hymn's own order. This is its first ṛc.
deva:
आवाहनं –
हिर॑ण्यवर्णा॒ं हरि॑णीं सु॒वर्ण॑रज॒तस्र॑जाम् ।
च॒न्द्रां हि॒रण्म॑यीं ल॒क्ष्मीं जात॑वेदो म॒ आव॑ह ॥
सर्वमंगळमांगळ्ये भक्ताभीष्टप्रदायिनि ।

--- unit 6 | section: षोडशोपचार पूजा ---
vidhi:
The seat. The ṛc is the second of the Śrī Sūkta.
deva:
आसनं –
तां म॒ आव॑ह॒ जात॑वेदो ल॒क्ष्मीमन॑पगा॒मिनी᳚म् ।

tel: ఆసనము — ఓ జాతవేదా, తొలగిపోని ఆ లక్ష్మిని నాకు తెమ్ము; ఎవరివలన నేను బంగారమును, గోవులను, అశ్వములను, పురుషులను పొందుదునో ఆమెను. ఓ శ్రీ రాజరాజేశ్వరి, మహాదేవీ, పూర్ణచంద్రుని వంటి ముఖము కలదానా, దేవతలచే నమస్కరింపబడు దేవీ, ఈ సింహాసనమును గ్రహింపుము. ఓం శ్రీ రాజరాజేశ్వరికి నమస్కారము. రత్నసింహాసనమును సమర్పించుచున్నాను.

--- unit 8 | section: षोडशोपचार पूजा ---
