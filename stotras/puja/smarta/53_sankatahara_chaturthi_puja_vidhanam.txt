Title: Saṅkaṭahara Caturthī Pūjā Vidhānam

Devanāgarī: सङ्कटहर चतुर्थी पूजा विधानम्

Telugu: సంకటహర చతుర్థీ పూజా విధానము

Author: Anonymous (received smārta household rite). No compiler is named and none should be inferred.

Type: vidhi (ritual manual) — not a stotra. Uses the one-extra-field vidhi format documented in ../../vidhi/madhyandina/01_sandhyopasana_vidhi.txt: a vidhi: field carrying the ritual instruction, then deva / iast / en / tel / hi.

Recension / paddhati: **Smārta, South Indian.** The Vaiṣṇava form of the opening is kept separately at `../vaishnava/01_purvanga_vidhanam.txt` and **the two must never be merged, harmonised or collated against each other.** The rite is kept on the fourth day of the dark fortnight of every month, Saṅkaṭahara Caturthī, and the fast is broken after the moon is seen.

**No other file in this corpus is a Saṅkaṭahara Caturthī rite.** `14_vinayaka_chaviti_vrata.txt` is the Bhādrapada festival worship and `15_mahaganapati_shodashopachara_puja.txt` the general sixteen-service worship of Gaṇeśa; this is a third, monthly rite with verses of its own.

What this rite has: a renewed resolution naming the day; a meditation in two verses; sixteen services, **each with a verse of its own that ends in the same prayer, `सङ्कटं मे निवारय`**, and each offered under a different name of Gaṇeśa; a worship with flowers under sixteen names; a worship with twenty-one pairs of dūrvā grass; three verses of prayer in the upajāti metre; **three arghyas, each given seven times — to the goddess of the fourth tithi, to Gaṇeśa as remover of trouble, and to the moon with Rohiṇī**, which is what marks the day; asking of pardon; dedication; the receiving of the water and the prasāda; and the sending away. It has **no pūrvāṅga of its own**: the page sends the worshipper to the pūrvāṅga first, so use `03_purvanga_vidhanam.txt`. It has no prāṇapratiṣṭhā, no aṅga-pūjā, no aṣṭottara and no mantras over the food.

Source / recension: base text `https://stotranidhi.com/hi/sankata-hara-chaturthi-puja-vidhanam-in-sanskrit/` (fetched 2026-10-17, 165 lines), a **native Devanāgarī page**, so nothing here was transliterated by us. Every `deva:` line was taken from the fetched page by line number with `../../bin/fetch_source.py`, not retyped, and the build checked that every line of the page is used once. The page's last line, `इतर श्री गणेश स्तोत्राणि पश्यतु ।`, is the site's link to its other Gaṇeśa texts and not part of the rite; it is left out. The `iast:` fields were generated from the Devanāgarī by `../../bin/dev2iast.py`; the page's own IAST was not used.

Independent collation: **partial, and the file rests mainly on one editorial lineage.** The sixteen names of the flower-worship (unit 15) are the Nārada Purāṇa list and match, name for name and in order, the first sixteen of the seventeen in `15_mahaganapati_shodashopachara_puja.txt` and the list in `14_vinayaka_chaviti_vrata.txt`, whose base text is vignanam.org — a separate lineage. **The twenty-one dūrvā names (unit 16) are not the list `14` carries**: eleven names are shared, and the other ten here (`अघनाशन`, `विघ्नविध्वंसकर्तृ`, `विश्ववन्द्य`, `अमरेश`, `गजकर्णक`, `नागयज्ञोपवीतिन्`, `फालचन्द्र`, `परशुधारिन्`, `विघ्नाधिप`, `विद्याप्रद`) stand only on this page; the two lists are two traditions and neither corrects the other. The verses `यस्य स्मृत्या च नामोक्त्या` and `मन्त्रहीनं क्रियाहीनं` (unit 28) are common household verses found throughout this folder. **For the sixteen service verses, the three arghya verses, the three prayer verses and the sending-away verse no second witness was found.** The publisher's Telugu page of the same rite (`https://stotranidhi.com/sankatahara-chaturthi-puja-vidhanam-in-telugu/`) was read beside the Devanāgarī; it is the same witness in another script and catches transcription slips only, and it did catch three (below). Gītā Press *Nitya Karma Pūjā Prakāśa* (code 592) was not searched in this session, and its text layer is not held locally; the question is open and recorded here.

Defects in the base text, corrected with reasons, because stotranidhi is a convenience source and not a critical edition:
1. **`पुजां` → `पूजां`** (unit 1). A short `u` for the long `ū` of `पूजा`; the page prints `पूजा` and `पूजया` everywhere else.
2. **`सम्युतम्` → `संयुतम्`** (unit 8), **`सम्युक्ताः` → `संयुक्ताः`** (unit 13) and **`सम्युक्तं` → `संयुक्तं`** (unit 22). `म्` before `य` is written with anusvāra in Sanskrit, `संयुत`; the publisher's own Telugu page prints `సంయుతమ్`, `సంయుక్తాః` and `సంయుక్తం` in all three places.

Observed and deliberately **not** emended:
- **`भगवान् सर्वात्मिकः श्री गणेशः सुप्रीतो सुप्रसन्नो वरदो भवन्तु`** (unit 29): `सर्वात्मिकः` where the masculine is `सर्वात्मकः`, and a plural verb with a singular subject. The same formula was left as printed in `15` and `49`.
- **`मूढाः विहितार्थसिद्धिम्`** (unit 24), where the visarga before `व` would be dropped in sandhi. Verse printings commonly keep it at the pāda boundary.
- **`गच्छ सत्त्वमुमापुत्र`** (unit 31). The reading is unusual, and it is kept and translated as printed, `in your true being`; no second witness was found to compare it with.
- **`कवेरजासिन्धुगङ्गा`** (unit 9), the Kāverī named as `कवेरजा`, daughter of Kavera. A correct form, noted so that it is not mistaken for a slip.
- **`(इति सप्तवारं)`** after each arghya (units 25–27), the page's own instruction, kept inside the text as `49` keeps `(इति ...)` instructions.

Accent: **none. This rite contains no Vedic mantra.** Every verse is paurāṇika, and the page prints no svara mark; 0 accent characters were counted in the units. Since there is no Vedic mantra, **no Paurāṇika twin was written** in `../pauranika/smarta/`: this file can be used as it stands by a reciter for whom the upanayana saṁskāra has not been performed. The closing `ओं शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः` is the common closing words of peace, not a mantra of the Veda.

Unit count: 31 units. Not a verse count in the stotra sense — the work is a prose order of service punctuated by verses, and the numbering is this corpus's own. The two name-lists (units 15 and 16) are numbered one item to a line in deva: and iast:, by the rule for lists.

Blurb: This is the worship of Gaṇeśa for Saṅkaṭahara Caturthī, the fourth day of the dark fortnight of each month, kept for the removal of trouble. It is a worship in sixteen services. Every service has its own verse, and every verse ends with the same prayer, that Gaṇeśa remove the worshipper's trouble. Each offering is made under a different name of his. After the services come a worship with flowers under sixteen names, a worship with twenty-one pairs of dūrvā grass, and three verses of prayer. Then three arghyas are given, each seven times: to the goddess of the fourth tithi, to Gaṇeśa as the remover of trouble, and to the moon together with Rohiṇī. The worship ends with a prayer for pardon, the dedication, the receiving of the water and the prasāda, and the sending away. The opening portion is not included, and is performed first.

--- unit 2 | section: ध्यानम् ---
vidhi:
Meditation. Two verses on the form of Gaṇeśa.
deva:
ध्यानम् –
एकदन्तं महाकायं तप्तकाञ्चनसन्निभम् ।
लम्बोदरं विशालाक्षं वन्देऽहं गणनायकम् ॥
आखुपृष्ठसमासीनं चामरैर्वीजितं गणैः ।
शेषयज्ञोपवीतं च चिन्तयामि गजाननम् ॥
ओं श्रीविनायकाय नमः ध्यायामि ।
iast:
dhyānam –
ekadantaṁ mahākāyaṁ taptakāñcanasannibham |
lambodaraṁ viśālākṣaṁ vande'haṁ gaṇanāyakam ||
ākhupṛṣṭhasamāsīnaṁ cāmarairvījitaṁ gaṇaiḥ |
śeṣayajñopavītaṁ ca cintayāmi gajānanam ||
oṁ śrīvināyakāya namaḥ dhyāyāmi |
en: Meditation — I bow to the leader of the gaṇas, who has one tusk and a great body, who shines like molten gold, who has an ample belly and large eyes. I think upon the elephant-faced one, seated upon the back of the mouse, fanned with whisks by the gaṇas, who wears the serpent Śeṣa as his sacred thread. Oṁ, salutation to Śrī Vināyaka. I meditate.
tel: ధ్యానము — ఏకదంతుడును, మహాకాయుడును, కరిగిన బంగారమువలె ప్రకాశించువాడును, లంబోదరుడును, విశాలమైన కన్నులు కలవాడును అగు గణనాయకునికి నమస్కరించుచున్నాను. ఎలుక వీపుపై కూర్చున్నవాడును, గణములచే చామరములతో వీవబడువాడును, శేషుని యజ్ఞోపవీతముగా ధరించినవాడును అగు గజాననుని చింతించుచున్నాను. ఓం శ్రీ వినాయకునికి నమస్కారము. ధ్యానించుచున్నాను.
hi: ध्यान — एकदन्त, महाकाय, तपाये हुए सोने के समान कान्ति वाले, लम्बोदर, विशाल नेत्रों वाले गणनायक को मैं प्रणाम करता हूँ। मूषक की पीठ पर बैठे, गणों द्वारा चँवर से सेवित, शेषनाग को यज्ञोपवीत रूप में धारण करने वाले गजानन का मैं चिन्तन करता हूँ। ओम् श्री विनायक को नमस्कार। ध्यान करता हूँ।

vidhi:
The invocation. From here each service has a verse of its own, and each verse ends with the same prayer, that the trouble be removed. Each offering is made under a different name of Gaṇeśa.
deva:
आवाहनम् –
आगच्छ देव देवेश सङ्कटं मे निवारय ।
यावत्पूजा समाप्येत तावत्त्वं सन्निधौ भव ॥
ओं गजास्याय नमः आवाहयामि ।
iast:
āvāhanam –
āgaccha deva deveśa saṅkaṭaṁ me nivāraya |
yāvatpūjā samāpyeta tāvattvaṁ sannidhau bhava ||
oṁ gajāsyāya namaḥ āvāhayāmi |
en: Invocation — Come, O god, O lord of the gods, and remove my trouble. Until the worship is completed, remain present here. Oṁ, salutation to the elephant-faced one. I invoke.
tel: ఆవాహనము — ఓ దేవా, దేవేశా, రమ్ము; నా సంకటమును తొలగింపుము. పూజ ముగియునంతవఱకు నీవు సన్నిధియందు ఉండుము. ఓం గజాస్యునికి నమస్కారము. ఆవాహనము చేయుచున్నాను.
hi: आवाहन — हे देव, हे देवेश, आइए और मेरा संकट दूर कीजिए। जब तक पूजा पूर्ण हो, तब तक सन्निधि में रहिए। ओम् गजास्य को नमस्कार। आवाहन करता हूँ।

en: Dedication — By this worship in sixteen services, beginning with meditation and invocation, may the Lord, the Self of all, Śrī Gaṇeśa, be well pleased, gracious and giving boons. Let this Saṅkaṭahara Caturthī worship be an offering to Gaṇeśa.
tel: సమర్పణము — ఈ ధ్యాన ఆవాహనాది షోడశోపచార పూజచే సర్వాత్మకుడైన భగవాన్ శ్రీ గణేశుడు సుప్రీతుడై, సుప్రసన్నుడై, వరదుడగుగాక. ఈ సంకటహర చతుర్థీ పూజ గణేశునికి అర్పణమగుగాక.
hi: समर्पण — इस ध्यान-आवाहन आदि षोडशोपचार पूजा से सर्वात्मक भगवान् श्री गणेश सुप्रीत, सुप्रसन्न और वरदायक हों। यह संकटहर चतुर्थी पूजा गणेश को अर्पित हो।

--- unit 30 | section: तीर्थप्रसाद ग्रहणम् ---
vidhi:
Receiving the water from the feet and the food offered.
deva:
तीर्थप्रसाद स्वीकरण –
अकालमृत्युहरणं सर्वव्याधिनिवारणम् ।
समस्तपापक्षयकरं श्री महागणाधिपति पादोदकं पावनं शुभम् ॥
श्री महागणपति प्रसादं शिरसा गृह्णामि ॥
iast:
tīrthaprasāda svīkaraṇa –
akālamṛtyuharaṇaṁ sarvavyādhinivāraṇam |
samastapāpakṣayakaraṁ śrī mahāgaṇādhipati pādodakaṁ pāvanaṁ śubham ||
śrī mahāgaṇapati prasādaṁ śirasā gṛhṇāmi ||
en: Receiving the water and the prasāda — It removes untimely death and wards off every disease. It destroys all sin. The water from the feet of Śrī Mahāgaṇādhipati is purifying and auspicious. I receive the prasāda of Śrī Mahāgaṇapati upon my head.
tel: తీర్థప్రసాద స్వీకారము — ఇది అకాలమృత్యువును హరించును, సర్వవ్యాధులను నివారించును, సమస్త పాపములను క్షయింపజేయును. శ్రీ మహాగణాధిపతి పాదోదకము పావనము, శుభము. శ్రీ మహాగణపతి ప్రసాదమును శిరస్సుతో స్వీకరించుచున్నాను.
hi: तीर्थ-प्रसाद ग्रहण — यह अकालमृत्यु को हरता है और सब रोगों का निवारण करता है। यह समस्त पापों का नाश करता है। श्री महागणाधिपति का पादोदक पावन और शुभ है। श्री महागणपति का प्रसाद सिर से ग्रहण करता हूँ।

--- unit 31 | section: उद्वासनम् ---
vidhi:
Sending the deity back to his own place, and the closing words of peace.
deva:
उद्वासनम् –
गच्छ सत्त्वमुमापुत्र ममानुग्रहकारणात् ।
पूजितोऽसि मया भक्त्या गच्छ स्वस्थानकं प्रभो ॥
गणपतये नमः यथास्थानं उद्वासयामि ।
शोभनार्थे क्षेमाय पुनरागमनाय च ।
ओं शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः ।
iast:
udvāsanam –
gaccha sattvamumāputra mamānugrahakāraṇāt |
pūjito'si mayā bhaktyā gaccha svasthānakaṁ prabho ||
gaṇapataye namaḥ yathāsthānaṁ udvāsayāmi |
śobhanārthe kṣemāya punarāgamanāya ca |
oṁ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ |
en: Sending away — Go, O son of Umā, in your true being, for the sake of your grace to me. You have been worshipped by me with devotion; go to your own abode, O Lord. Salutation to Gaṇapati. I send you to your own place, for good fortune, for well-being, and for your coming again. Oṁ, peace, peace, peace.
tel: ఉద్వాసనము — ఓ ఉమాపుత్రా, నాపై అనుగ్రహము కొఱకు నీ నిజరూపమున వెళ్ళుము. నాచే భక్తితో పూజింపబడితివి; ఓ ప్రభూ, నీ స్వస్థానమునకు వెళ్ళుము. గణపతికి నమస్కారము. శోభనము కొఱకు, క్షేమము కొఱకు, మరల వచ్చుట కొఱకు, యథాస్థానమునకు ఉద్వాసన చేయుచున్నాను. ఓం శాంతిః శాంతిః శాంతిః.
hi: उद्वासन — हे उमापुत्र, मुझ पर अनुग्रह के लिए अपने सत्य स्वरूप में जाइए। मैंने भक्तिपूर्वक आपकी पूजा की है; हे प्रभु, अपने स्थान को जाइए। गणपति को नमस्कार। शोभन के लिए, क्षेम के लिए और पुनः आगमन के लिए यथास्थान उद्वासन करता हूँ। ओम् शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः।
