Blurb: This is a short worship of Śiva, the supreme lord, for a day when there is little time. It carries its own opening portion. The worshipper purifies himself, prays to the remover of obstacles and to the teacher, sips water with the names of Viṣṇu, lights the lamp, restrains the breath and makes a short resolution. He prays over the water-vessel and to Gaṇapati, and puts on the sacred ash. Śiva is meditated upon in two verses, as the five-faced lord and as the lord of Umā, and invoked into the image. Six services follow, each offered with the five-syllable mantra: the bath, sandal paste, a flower, incense, the lamp and food. The rite closes with salutation and the offering of its fruit to the lord.
