Blurb: This is a worship in sixteen services built on the Puruṣa Sūkta, the Vedic hymn of the cosmic Person. At every service the worshipper first recites one verse of the hymn, so that the whole hymn is recited in order across the worship. The rite is not written for one deity. The name of the deity is said at each offering, and the closing prayer names the Lord. The bath is given in full: milk, curd, ghee, honey and sugar are each poured with their own Vedic mantra, then the water of fruits, then pure water. The worship ends with the mantra-flower, circumambulation, the royal services, a prayer for pardon, and the receiving of the water and the prasāda. The opening portion is not included, and is performed first.
