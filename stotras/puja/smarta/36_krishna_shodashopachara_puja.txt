Type: vidhi (ritual manual) — not a stotra. Uses the one-extra-field vidhi format documented in `../../vidhi/madhyandina/01_sandhyopasana_vidhi.txt`: a `vidhi:` field carrying the ritual instruction, then deva / iast / en / tel / hi.

Recension / paddhati: **Smārta, South Indian.** The Vaiṣṇava form of the opening is kept separately at `../vaishnava/01_purvanga_vidhanam.txt` and **the two must never be merged, harmonised or collated against each other.**

Note: **This rite is built on the Puruṣa Sūkta.** Its closing dedication says the worship follows `पुरुषसूक्त विधान`, and the claim holds: the sixteen services are headed by the ṛcs of the Taittirīya Puruṣa Sūkta in the hymn's own order — invocation ṛc 1, the seat ṛc 2, and so on to the waving of the light, which takes ṛc 16 (`वेदाहमेतं पुरुषं महान्तम्`), and the mantra-flower, which takes `धाता पुरस्ताद्यमुदाजहार` from the uttara-anuvāka. The madhuparka, the bath of five nectars and the akṣata have no ṛc and carry their Paurāṇika verse only. This is the same plan as `22_surya_shodashopachara_puja.txt` follows, and the worshipper who wants the hymn as a hymn will find it at `../../veda/taittiriya/01_purusha_suktam.txt`.

**The Kṛṣṇa worshipped is the child.** The second meditation verse is on the infant Kṛṣṇa at his mother's breast, the invocation calls him `बालरूपधरं`, the incense verse names `बालकृष्ण`, and the water from his feet is the water of `श्री बालकृष्ण`. The seventeen names of the aṅga-pūjā are mostly deeds of his life — the slayer of Pūtanā, the breaker of the cart-demon, the lover of fresh butter, the enemy of Kaṁsa, the giver of wealth to Kucela.

**No second rite for Kṛṣṇa stands in this folder.** `06_vishnu_nitya_puja.txt` is a rite of Viṣṇu from another publisher, and it is not a second recension of this one. The aṣṭottara is not printed here; the page sends the worshipper to it, and it is at `../../krishna/29_krishna_ashtottarashatanama_stotram.txt`.

What it does not have: **no pūrvāṅga of its own.** Use `03_purvanga_vidhanam.txt` before this file, and then the Haridrā Gaṇapati pūjā. No prāṇapratiṣṭhā.

Defects in the base text, corrected with reasons:

1. **`पादयो पाद्यं` → `पादयोः पाद्यं`** (unit 6). A dropped visarga. The locative dual is `पादयोः`, and the Kubera and Lalitā pages of the same site print it so.
2. **`अकालमृत्यहरणं` → `अकालमृत्युहरणं`** (unit 33). A dropped `u`, the same slip corrected in `19_durga_shodashopachara_puja.txt`.

Observed and deliberately **not** emended:

- **`शरणागतवत्सला`** (unit 27), where the vocative for Kṛṣṇa is expected as `शरणागतवत्सल`. The same verse is printed with the same ending on the Kubera page.
- **`इहत्र च`** (unit 22) for the expected `इहात्र च` or `इह च`. No second witness prints this verse.
- **`सुप्रीता सुप्रसन्ना वरदा भवन्तु`** (unit 32). The adjectives are feminine and the verb plural, although the subject, `भगवान् … श्री कृष्ण परमात्मा`, is masculine and singular. This is the formula of the site's goddess pages carried over, and it is left as printed.
- **`महेश्वर`** in the asking of pardon (unit 31), a Śaiva epithet in a rite of Kṛṣṇa. It is part of a fixed verse used across deities.
- **`प्रणामोष्टाङ्गमुच्यते`** (unit 28) for `प्रणामोऽष्टाङ्ग…`. The avagraha is commonly omitted in this verse.
- **`सम्युक्तं`** for `संयुक्तं`. This publisher writes anusvāra before a semivowel as `म्` throughout.

Blurb: This is the worship of Śrī Kṛṣṇa in sixteen services. The page has no opening portion of its own. The worshipper first performs the pūrvāṅga and the Haridrā Gaṇapati pūjā. In the second resolution he asks for the fruit his mind desires and for the knowledge of Brahman. Kṛṣṇa is meditated upon in two verses: as the cowherd adorned with musk, pearls and the flute, and as the infant at his mother's breast. Every one of the sixteen services is headed by a verse of the Puruṣa Sūkta, taken in the order of that hymn, and each is followed by a verse addressed to Kṛṣṇa. In the worship of the limbs, most of the names recall his deeds, such as the slaying of Pūtanā and the breaking of the cart-demon. The rite closes with four verses of prayer, the asking of pardon, and the receiving of the water from his feet.

en: The second resolution — On this auspicious day, marked by the qualities already named, for the gaining of the fruit my mind desires and for the attainment of the knowledge of Brahman, I shall perform the worship of Śrī Kṛṣṇa, the supreme Brahman.

--- unit 3 | section: ध्यानम् ---
vidhi:
Meditation, in two verses. The first sees Kṛṣṇa as the cowherd, adorned and surrounded by the cowherd women. The second sees him as the infant at his mother's breast.
deva:
ध्यानम् –
कस्तूरीतिलकं ललाटफलके वक्षःस्थले कौस्तुभं
नासाग्रे वरमौक्तिकं करतले वेणुं करे कङ्कणम् ।
सर्वाङ्गे हरिचन्दनं च कलयन् कण्ठे च मुक्तावलिं
गोपस्त्रीपरिवेष्टितो विजयते गोपालचूडामणिः ॥
ध्यायामि बालकं कृष्णं मात्रङ्के स्तन्यपायिनम् ।
श्रीवत्सवक्षसं कान्तं नीलोत्पलदलच्छविम् ॥
ओं श्रीकृष्ण परब्रह्मणे नमः ध्यायामि ।
iast:
dhyānam –
kastūrītilakaṁ lalāṭaphalake vakṣaḥsthale kaustubhaṁ
nāsāgre varamauktikaṁ karatale veṇuṁ kare kaṅkaṇam |
sarvāṅge haricandanaṁ ca kalayan kaṇṭhe ca muktāvaliṁ
gopastrīpariveṣṭito vijayate gopālacūḍāmaṇiḥ ||
dhyāyāmi bālakaṁ kṛṣṇaṁ mātraṅke stanyapāyinam |
śrīvatsavakṣasaṁ kāntaṁ nīlotpaladalacchavim ||
oṁ śrīkṛṣṇa parabrahmaṇe namaḥ dhyāyāmi |
en: Meditation — Musk on his forehead, the Kaustubha jewel on his chest, a fine pearl at the tip of his nose, the flute in his palm and a bracelet on his hand, sandal paste over all his limbs and a string of pearls at his throat, surrounded by the cowherd women — the crest-jewel of the cowherds is victorious. I meditate on the child Kṛṣṇa, drinking at his mother's breast, bearing the Śrīvatsa on his chest, lovely, dark as the petal of the blue lotus. Oṁ, salutation to Śrī Kṛṣṇa, the supreme Brahman. I meditate upon him.
tel: ధ్యానము — నుదుట కస్తూరి తిలకము, వక్షస్థలమున కౌస్తుభము, ముక్కు చివర శ్రేష్ఠమైన ముత్యము, అరచేతిలో వేణువు, చేతికి కంకణము, అన్ని అవయవములకు హరిచందనము, కంఠమున ముత్యాల హారము ధరించి, గోపస్త్రీలచే చుట్టబడియున్న గోపాల చూడామణి జయించుచున్నాడు. తల్లి ఒడిలో స్తన్యమును త్రాగుచున్నవాడును, వక్షమున శ్రీవత్సము కలవాడును, మనోహరుడును, నల్లకలువ రేకు వంటి కాంతి కలవాడును అగు బాలకృష్ణుని ధ్యానించుచున్నాను. ఓం పరబ్రహ్మ స్వరూపుడైన శ్రీకృష్ణునికి నమస్కారము. ధ్యానించుచున్నాను.

--- unit 4 | section: षोडशोपचार पूजा ---

The invocation. The first ṛc of the Puruṣa Sūkta, then the verse of invocation to Kṛṣṇa in the form of a child.

अत्य॑तिष्ठद्दशाङ्गु॒लम् ॥
आवाहयामि देवेशं श्रीपतिं श्रीधरं हरिम् ।
बालरूपधरं विष्णुं सच्चिदानन्दविग्रहम् ॥
ओं श्रीकृष्ण परब्रह्मणे नमः आवाहयामि ।
iast:
āvāhanam –
sahasraśīrṣā puruṣaḥ |
sahasrākṣaḥ sahasrapāt |
sa bhūmiṁ viśvato vṛtvā |
atyatiṣṭhaddaśāṅgulam ||
āvāhayāmi deveśaṁ śrīpatiṁ śrīdharaṁ harim |
bālarūpadharaṁ viṣṇuṁ saccidānandavigraham ||

en: Invocation — The Person has a thousand heads, a thousand eyes and a thousand feet; he covers the earth on every side and stands beyond it by ten fingers' breadth. I invoke the lord of the gods, the husband of Śrī, the bearer of Śrī, Hari — Viṣṇu who has taken the form of a child, whose form is being, consciousness and bliss. Oṁ, salutation to Śrī Kṛṣṇa, the supreme Brahman. I invoke him.
tel: ఆవాహనము — పురుషుడు వేయి శిరస్సులు, వేయి కన్నులు, వేయి పాదములు కలవాడు; భూమిని అన్ని వైపుల ఆవరించి, పది అంగుళములు దానిని మించి నిలిచియున్నాడు. దేవేశుడును, శ్రీపతియు, శ్రీధరుడును, హరియు, బాలరూపమును ధరించిన విష్ణువును, సచ్చిదానంద విగ్రహుడును అగు వానిని ఆవాహనము చేయుచున్నాను. ఓం పరబ్రహ్మ స్వరూపుడైన శ్రీకృష్ణునికి నమస్కారము. ఆవాహనము చేయుచున్నాను.

vidhi:
Receiving the water from Kṛṣṇa's feet and the food offered to him, and a direction to the other stotras of Kṛṣṇa. The Vedic closing peace is said before that direction.
deva:
तीर्थप्रसाद ग्रहणम् –
अकालमृत्युहरणं सर्वव्याधिनिवारणम् ।
समस्तपापक्षयकरं श्री बालकृष्ण पादोदकं पावनं शुभम् ॥
ओं श्रीकृष्ण परब्रह्मणे नमः प्रसादं शिरसा गृह्णामि ।
ओं शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः ।
इतर श्री कृष्ण स्तोत्राणि पश्यतु ।

en: Receiving the water and the prasāda — It removes untimely death and wards off every disease. It destroys all sin. The water from the feet of Śrī Bālakṛṣṇa is purifying and auspicious. Oṁ, salutation to Śrī Kṛṣṇa, the supreme Brahman. I receive the prasāda upon my head. Oṁ, peace, peace, peace. See the other stotras of Śrī Kṛṣṇa.
