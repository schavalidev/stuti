Recension / paddhati: **Smārta, South Indian.** Pūjā-vidhāna divides by *paddhati* the way sandhyāvandana divides by śākhā. The Śrīvaiṣṇava form of the opening is kept separately at `../vaishnava/01_purvanga_vidhanam.txt` and **the two must never be merged, harmonised or collated against each other.**

**Relation to `01_nitya_puja_vidhanam.txt`.** `01` is the generic template, printing `श्री इष्ट देवता` as a slot. This is the same rite with the slot filled in for Subrahmaṇya by the source itself, published as a complete self-contained order of service and transcribed so. **Units 1–29 are the shared pūrvāṅga, word-for-word the template's**; units 30–60 are the Subrahmaṇya ṣoḍaśopacāra proper. Measured against the template, 224 of this page's 305 lines are shared and 81 are new — 73% reuse. Its siblings are `04_ganapati_nitya_puja.txt` through `09_saraswati_nitya_puja.txt`.

**Accent, and an exception worth stating plainly: the deity portion of this rite is printed unaccented.** The shared pūrvāṅga carries its accents as in every sibling file — the prāṇāyāma vyāhṛtis, the Gāyatrī, the Ṛgvedic Gaṇapati mantra, the `āpo hiṣṭhā` triad and the Gāyatrī over the food. But the **Subrahmaṇya Gāyatrī** at unit 32 — `ॐ तत्पुरुषाय विद्महे महासेनाय धीमहि । तन्नः षण्मुखः प्रचोदयात् ॥` — is printed **without svara marks**, where the Gaṇapati, Śiva, Viṣṇu, Lakṣmī and Durgā pages all print their deity Gāyatrī accented. **It has been left unaccented, exactly as the source has it.** Per `CLAUDE.md` accent is transcribed or absent, never derived: the accentuation of this verse could be inferred from the Śiva Gāyatrī it is modelled on (`तत्पुरुषाय विद्महे` is identical), but inferring it is precisely what the rule forbids, and it has not been done.

Udātta `॑` (U+0951), anudātta `॒` (U+0952), dīrgha svarita `᳚` (U+1CDA); the praṇava before a spirant takes the guttural nasal, written `ओग्ं` (`ogṁ`). The source prints its accented mantras in a sandhi-joined orthography that hyphenates across word boundaries, kept exactly as printed and carried into the IAST.

**This rite has no `स्तोत्रपठनम्` section** — as the Durgā and Sarasvatī rites have none — and it carries only **one** verse of pardon where the Durgā and Hanumān rites carry three.

Defect in the base text, corrected with reason: **`उपमश्रवस्तवम्` → `उपमश्रवस्तमम्`** at unit 27. The mantra is Ṛgveda 2.23.1, whose transmitted reading is `upamaśravastamam`, a superlative in *-tama-*; `-stavam` is not construable. The same slip stands on every one of this site's deity pages and is corrected in each; the site's own generic template prints it correctly, so the defect is confined to the deity pages. As there, this page also splits `क॒वि-ङ्क॑वी॒नाम्` as `क॒वि-ङ्क॑वी॒नां उप॒-`; a word-division difference in the printed sandhi, kept as printed.

Not collated: the paurāṇika verses shared with the template, and the Subrahmaṇya upacāra and dhyāna verses, for which no critical edition exists. `षड्वक्त्रं शिखिवाहनं` and `षडाननं चन्दनलेपिताङ्गम्` are among the most widely printed Subrahmaṇya dhyāna verses and are given as this source prints them.

Blurb: This is the daily pūjā of Subrahmaṇya in the smārta form, complete in sixty units. It follows the same order as the other deity pūjās in this folder, with Subrahmaṇya as the chosen deity. After the materials and the opening acts, the worshipper honours the water-vessel, the conch and the bell, and worships Gaṇapati first so that obstacles may be removed — here his own elder brother. Subrahmaṇya is then meditated upon in three units, as the six-faced god borne by the peacock, bearing ten weapons, the son of Rudra. The sixteen services follow, among them the worship of the god limb by limb under fourteen names that recall his deeds, and the sacred thread offered as silver thread with a golden upper cloth. Unlike its siblings this rite prescribes no recitation of hymns and carries only one verse of pardon. Note that the Subrahmaṇya Gāyatrī here is printed without accent marks, and has been left so. The rite closes with the dismissal and the receiving of the prasāda.

--- unit 1 | section: आवश्यकानि वस्तूनि ---
vidhi:
The things to be gathered before beginning. The conch is marked optional.
deva:
आवश्यकानि वस्तूनि — आसनं । दीपः । गन्धः । कुङ्कुमं । अक्षताः । पुष्पाणि । धूपः । नैवेद्यं । ताम्बूलं । आचमनीयं । पूजा पात्रं । कलशः । शङ्खः (वैकल्पिकः) । सुब्रह्मण्य स्वामि प्रतिमा वा चित्रपटः वा ।
iast:
āvaśyakāni vastūni — āsanaṁ | dīpaḥ | gandhaḥ | kuṅkumaṁ | akṣatāḥ | puṣpāṇi | dhūpaḥ | naivedyaṁ | tāmbūlaṁ | ācamanīyaṁ | pūjā pātraṁ | kalaśaḥ | śaṅkhaḥ (vaikalpikaḥ) | subrahmaṇya svāmi pratimā vā citrapaṭaḥ vā |
en: Things required — a seat; a lamp; sandal-paste; kuṅkuma; unbroken rice; flowers; incense; food-offering; betel; water for ācamana; the pūjā vessel; a water-pot; a conch (optional); and an image or picture of Lord Subrahmaṇya.
tel: కావలసిన వస్తువులు — ఆసనం; దీపం; గంధం; కుంకుమ; అక్షతలు; పుష్పాలు; ధూపం; నైవేద్యం; తాంబూలం; ఆచమనీయం; పూజా పాత్ర; కలశం; శంఖం (ఐచ్ఛికం); సుబ్రహ్మణ్య స్వామి ప్రతిమ లేదా చిత్రపటం.
hi: आवश्यक वस्तुएँ — आसन; दीप; गन्ध; कुङ्कुम; अक्षत; पुष्प; धूप; नैवेद्य; ताम्बूल; आचमनीय जल; पूजापात्र; कलश; शङ्ख (वैकल्पिक); तथा सुब्रह्मण्य स्वामी की प्रतिमा अथवा चित्रपट।

The opening salutations, before anything else is done. Note that in a rite to Subrahmaṇya the first salutation is to his elder brother.
deva:
श्रीमहागणाधिपतये नमः । श्री गुरुभ्यो नमः । हरिः ओम् ॥
iast:
śrīmahāgaṇādhipataye namaḥ | śrī gurubhyo namaḥ | hariḥ om ||

--- unit 3 | section: पूर्वाङ्ग पूजा ---
vidhi:
Purification. Recited to make oneself fit, inwardly and outwardly, for what follows.
deva:
अपवित्रः पवित्रो वा सर्वावस्थां गतोऽपि वा ।
यः स्मरेत् पुण्डरीकाक्षं स बाह्याभ्यन्तरः शुचिः ॥
पुण्डरीकाक्ष पुण्डरीकाक्ष पुण्डरीकाक्षाय नमः ॥
iast:
apavitraḥ pavitro vā sarvāvasthāṁ gato'pi vā |
yaḥ smaret puṇḍarīkākṣaṁ sa bāhyābhyantaraḥ śuciḥ ||
puṇḍarīkākṣa puṇḍarīkākṣa puṇḍarīkākṣāya namaḥ ||
en: Impure or pure, or gone into whatever condition — whoever calls to mind the lotus-eyed one is clean, without and within. Lotus-eyed one, lotus-eyed one: salutation to the lotus-eyed one.
tel: అపవిత్రుడైనా, పవిత్రుడైనా, ఏ అవస్థలో ఉన్నా — పుండరీకాక్షుని ఎవడు స్మరిస్తాడో అతడు బయటా లోపలా శుద్ధుడు. పుండరీకాక్షా, పుండరీకాక్షా, పుండరీకాక్షునికి నమస్కారం.
hi: अपवित्र हो या पवित्र, अथवा किसी भी अवस्था में क्यों न हो — जो पुण्डरीकाक्ष का स्मरण करता है वह बाहर-भीतर से शुद्ध हो जाता है। पुण्डरीकाक्ष, पुण्डरीकाक्ष, पुण्डरीकाक्ष को नमस्कार।

--- unit 4 | section: पूर्वाङ्ग पूजा ---
vidhi:
Prārthanā. The first of the invocatory verses — to Viṣṇu, for the quieting of all obstacles.
deva:
शुक्लाम्बरधरं विष्णुं शशिवर्णं चतुर्भुजम् ।
प्रसन्नवदनं ध्यायेत् सर्वविघ्नोपशान्तये ॥
iast:
śuklāmbaradharaṁ viṣṇuṁ śaśivarṇaṁ caturbhujam |
prasannavadanaṁ dhyāyet sarvavighnopaśāntaye ||
en: For the quieting of every obstacle one should meditate on Viṣṇu — robed in white, moon-coloured, four-armed, his face serene.
tel: సమస్త విఘ్నాలూ శాంతించడానికి — తెల్లని వస్త్రం ధరించినవాడు, చంద్రుని వన్నెవాడు, నాలుగు చేతులవాడు, ప్రసన్నమైన ముఖం కలవాడు అయిన విష్ణువును ధ్యానించాలి.
hi: समस्त विघ्नों की शान्ति के लिये श्वेत वस्त्र धारण किये हुए, चन्द्रमा के समान वर्णवाले, चतुर्भुज, प्रसन्नमुख विष्णु का ध्यान करना चाहिये।

--- unit 5 | section: पूर्वाङ्ग पूजा ---
vidhi:
To Gaṇeśa — his elder brother, called here the elephant-faced.
deva:
अगजानन पद्मार्कं गजाननमहर्निशम् ।
अनेकदं तं भक्तानां एकदन्तमुपास्महे ॥
iast:
agajānana padmārkaṁ gajānanamaharniśam |
anekadaṁ taṁ bhaktānāṁ ekadantamupāsmahe ||
en: We worship day and night that elephant-faced one, a sun to the lotus that is Pārvatī's face, who gives his devotees much though he is the one-tusked.
tel: పార్వతి ముఖపద్మానికి సూర్యుని వంటివాడు, గజముఖుడు, ఒకే దంతం కలవాడైనా భక్తులకు అనేకమైన వరాలిచ్చువాడు — ఆ ఏకదంతుని రేయింబవళ్లు ఉపాసిస్తాము.
hi: पार्वती के मुखकमल के लिये सूर्य के समान, गजानन, एकदन्त होते हुए भी भक्तों को अनेक वर देनेवाले उन गणेश की हम दिन-रात उपासना करते हैं।

tel: ఓ లక్ష్మీపతీ! నీ పాదద్వయాన్ని స్మరించినప్పుడే అదే శుభలగ్నం, అదే సుదినం, అదే తారాబలం, చంద్రబలం, విద్యాబలం, దైవబలం.

--- unit 7 | section: पूर्वाङ्ग पूजा ---

To the teacher.
deva:

deva:

तस्मात्कारुण्य भावेन रक्ष रक्ष सुरेश्वर ॥

The eight-limbed prostration.

उरसा शिरसा दृष्ट्या मनसा वचसा तथा ।

--- unit 58 | section: क्षमाप्रार्थना ---
