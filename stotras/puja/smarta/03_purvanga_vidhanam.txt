Title: Pūjā Vidhānam — Pūrvāṅgam (Smārta Paddhati)
Devanāgarī: पूजाविधानम् — पूर्वाङ्गम् (स्मार्तपद्धतिः)
Telugu: పూజావిధానం — పూర్వాంగం (స్మార్తపద్ధతిః)

Author: Anonymous (received smārta household rite). No compiler is named and none should be inferred.

Type: vidhi (ritual manual) — not a stotra. Uses the one-extra-field vidhi format documented in `../../vidhi/madhyandina/01_sandhyopasana_vidhi.txt`: a `vidhi:` field carrying the ritual instruction, then deva / iast / en / tel / hi.

Recension / paddhati: **Smārta, South Indian.** Pūjā-vidhāna divides by *paddhati* the way sandhyāvandana divides by śākhā, and the divisions are the observances of different communities rather than variant readings of one text. The Vaiṣṇava form of this same pūrvāṅga is a genuinely different rite and is kept separately at `../vaishnava/01_purvanga_vidhanam.txt`; **the two must never be merged, harmonised or collated against each other.** A user who follows the Vaiṣṇava rite should not use this file.

**Relation to `01_nitya_puja_vidhanam.txt`, and why both files exist.** This text is the *pūrvāṅga* — the opening portion, performed before the pūjā proper of whichever deity is to be worshipped. It is published as a text in its own right and ends by handing on to the Gaṇapati pūjā. `01_nitya_puja_vidhanam.txt` is a different edition of the daily rite which folds a pūrvāṅga of its own into a complete sixteen-service template. The two overlap heavily and **neither supersedes the other**: this one is markedly fuller, and the corpus rule is that one text embedding another is not a reason to retire either. They are cross-referenced in both directions.

Where this file is fuller than `01`, and it is fuller in seven places, all of them real text and not formatting:

1. the accented Ṛgvedic `देवीं वाचमजनयन्त देवाः` (unit 5);
2. `यः शिवो नाम रूपाभ्यां` (unit 6);
3. `लाभस्तेषां जयस्तेषां` (unit 9);
4. `पूजार्थे हरिद्रा कुङ्कुम विलेपनं करिष्ये` (unit 15);
5. the accented `ओं आकलशेषु धावति` and the `आपो वा इदं सर्वं` passage in the kalaśārādhana (unit 22);
6. two further śaṅkha verses, `त्वं पुरा सागरोत्पन्नो` and `गर्भादेवारिनारीणां` (unit 28);
7. a separate `घण्टपूजा` with `ओं जयध्वनि मन्त्रमातः स्वाहा`, standing before the ghaṇṭānāda (unit 30).

Its saṅkalpa (unit 19) is also the long deśa-kāla form, with blanks for place, rivers, year, half-year, season, month, fortnight, lunar day, weekday, asterism, yoga, karaṇa, gotra and name, where `01` prints a short form.

Accent: **present, on the Vedic passages only** — the `देवीं वाचम्` verse, the prāṇāyāma vyāhṛtis and Gāyatrī, and the `आकलशेषु` / `आपो वा इदं सर्वं` passage. The paurāṇika verses are printed unaccented, which is normal for them and is not a defect. Udātta `॑` (U+0951), anudātta `॒` (U+0952), dīrgha svarita `᳚` (U+1CDA). **No accent was inferred, derived or reconstructed** — every mark stands where the source put it, and the unaccented verses have been left unaccented rather than filled in from an accented edition elsewhere.

Independent collation: against **Gītā Press, Gorakhpur, *Nitya Karma Pūjā Prakāśa*** (archive.org `nitya-karma-puja-prakash-gita-press-gorakhpur`, 396 pp.; the deva-pūjā chapters read from the OCR directly) and against **vignanam.org's smārta nitya-pūjā** (`https://vignanam.org/devanagari/nitya-puja-vidhanam-smarta-paddhati.html`), transcribed at `01_nitya_puja_vidhanam.txt`. Three separate editorial lineages.

**The collation's most useful result: Gītā Press agrees with this text against vignanam** on the śaṅkha verse `त्वं पुरा सागरोत्पन्नो विष्णुना विधृतः करे` (unit 28) — GP prints it, this text prints it, vignanam lacks it altogether. That is evidence the verse belongs in the rite and that vignanam's text is the abridged one, not that this one is padded.

Where Gītā Press differs, the differences are recorded and **not reconciled**, because GP is the North Indian smārta tradition and this is the South Indian:

- GP's bhūtotsādana closes `ते नश्यन्तु शिवाज्ञया` — 'let them perish' — against this text's `ते गच्छन्तु शिवाऽज्ञया`, 'let them go'. GP also prefixes an explicit viniyoga (`अपसर्पन्त्विति मन्त्रस्य वामदेव ऋषिः, शिवो देवता, अनुष्टुप् छन्दः`) which this text does not give.
- GP's kalaśa verse reads `कण्ठे रुद्रः समाश्रितः … मध्ये मातृगणाः स्मृताः` with `मूले त्वस्य स्थितो ब्रह्मा`, which agrees; but GP continues into `कुक्षौ तु सागराः सर्वे` with the same four Vedas, and then adds material on the prohibition of stale water that this text omits.
- GP's saṅkalpa is in two forms, niṣkāma and sakāma (`ॐ विष्णुर्विष्णुर्विष्णुः अद्य अहं श्रीपरमेश्वरप्रीत्यर्थं विष्णुशिवगणेशसूर्यदुर्गार्चनं करिष्ये`), naming the five pañcāyatana deities together — a structure with no counterpart here.
- GP prescribes three hand-claps and an explicit āsana-purification (`ॐ पृथ्वि त्वया धृता लोका`) on entering the shrine; neither stands in this text.

None of that was imported.

Corrections and normalisations applied to the base text, with reasons, because stotranidhi is not a critical edition:

1. **ASCII colon → visarga.** The source writes visarga in its accented lines as the ASCII colon `:` — `ओं भुव॑:`, `धियो॒ यो न॑:`, `स॒म्राडापो॑ वि॒राडाप॑:`. A Latin colon cannot occur in Devanāgarī text and every instance sits where visarga belongs, so all were written `ः`. The same defect and the same repair are recorded in `../../vidhi/taittiriya/01_krishna_yajurveda_sandhyavandanam.txt`, where the source's Telugu carried it too.
2. **Zero-width joiner stripped.** The source writes the praṇava-before-spirant as `ओग्‍ं`, with a ZWJ between `ग्` and the anusvāra. The ZWJ is a rendering hint carrying no phonetic distinction in Devanāgarī, the `ग्` is already explicit, and the corpus runs a zero-ZWJ cleanliness check; so it is written `ओग्ं`, which is also what vignanam prints for the same sound. (The opposite decision is correct in the `tel:` field of the Taittirīya sandhyāvandana file, where Telugu script needs the joiner to keep `ఓగ్‍ం` distinct from `ఓం`.)
3. **`मूले त्वस्य स्थितो ब्रह्मा मध्ये मातृगणाः स्मृता ॥`** — the source's `स्मृता` is left as printed; the metre wants `स्मृताः` and vignanam prints `स्मृताः`, but a visarga is not supplied silently. Recorded here instead.
4. **`त्रैलोक्येयानि तीर्थानि वासुदेवस्यदद्रया`** (unit 27) is corrupt in the source: `त्रैलोक्ये यानि` wants its word-space, and `वासुदेवस्यदद्रया` is not construable — vignanam reads `वासुदेवस्य आज्ञया`, 'by Vāsudeva's command', which the sense requires. The word-space is restored; **the corrupt word is left as printed and flagged**, not emended to vignanam's reading, since that would be importing one witness's text into another's file. The English line gives the sense on vignanam's reading and says so.

Not collated: the paurāṇika verses — `अपवित्रः पवित्रो वा`, `शुक्लाम्बरधरं विष्णुं`, `अगजानन पद्मार्कं`, `यः शिवो नाम रूपाभ्यां`, `तदेव लग्नं`, `गुरुर्ब्रह्मा`, `लाभस्तेषां`, `सर्वमङ्गल माङ्गल्ये`, and the dīpārādhana verses. These are common to household manuals everywhere and no critical edition governs them.

Blurb: This is the pūrvāṅga, that is, the opening portion, of the smārta pūjā, given on its own in thirty-one units. It covers everything that comes before the worship of the deity proper. It begins with the maṅgalācaraṇa and with purification. It then gives the prayers and salutations, the ācamana, the lighting of the lamp, the dispelling of the bhūtas, prāṇāyāma and the saṅkalpa. It closes with the worship of the water-vessel, of the conch and of the bell. The pūrvāṅga is the same whichever deity is worshipped afterwards, and it is therefore kept as a separate file, to be used before any of the deity pūjās. Accent marks are present on the Vedic passages only. The Vaiṣṇava pūrvāṅga is a different rite, and it is kept apart at `../vaishnava/01_purvanga_vidhanam.txt`.

--- unit 31 | section: घण्टापूजा ---
vidhi:
Sounding the bell, which closes the pūrvāṅga. The text then hands on to the Gaṇapati pūjā, for which see the corpus's Gaṇapati files and `01_nitya_puja_vidhanam.txt` units 29–31.
deva:
आगमार्थं तु देवानां गमनार्थं तु राक्षसाम् ।
घण्टारवं करोम्यादौ देवताह्वान लाञ्छनम् ॥
इति घण्टानादं कृत्वा ॥
iast:
āgamārthaṁ tu devānāṁ gamanārthaṁ tu rākṣasām |
ghaṇṭāravaṁ karomyādau devatāhvāna lāñchanam ||
iti ghaṇṭānādaṁ kṛtvā ||
en: For the coming of the gods and for the going of the demons, I sound the bell at the outset — the token by which the deity is called. Having thus sounded the bell.
tel: దేవతల రాక కోసం, రాక్షసుల పోక కోసం — దేవతను ఆహ్వానించే గుర్తుగా మొదట ఘంటానాదం చేస్తున్నాను. ఇలా ఘంటానాదం చేసి.
hi: देवताओं के आगमन के लिये और राक्षसों के गमन के लिये — देवता के आह्वान के चिह्नस्वरूप मैं आदि में घण्टानाद करता हूँ। इस प्रकार घण्टानाद करके।
