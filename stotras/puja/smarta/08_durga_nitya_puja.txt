Title: Durgā Nitya Pūjā Vidhānam (Smārta Paddhati)
Devanāgarī: दुर्गा नित्य पूजा विधानम् (स्मार्त पद्धतिः)
Telugu: దుర్గా నిత్య పూజా విధానం (స్మార్త పద్ధతిః)

Author: Anonymous (received smārta household rite). No compiler is named and none should be inferred.

Type: vidhi (ritual manual) — not a stotra. Uses the one-extra-field vidhi format documented in `../../vidhi/madhyandina/01_sandhyopasana_vidhi.txt`: a `vidhi:` field carrying the ritual instruction, then deva / iast / en / tel / hi.

Recension / paddhati: **Smārta, South Indian.** Pūjā-vidhāna divides by *paddhati* the way sandhyāvandana divides by śākhā. The Śrīvaiṣṇava form of the opening is kept separately at `../vaishnava/01_purvanga_vidhanam.txt` and **the two must never be merged, harmonised or collated against each other.**

**This is a smārta daily rite, not a Śākta one.** The distinction matters for this deity above all others: the tantric and Śākta worship of Durgā has its own elaborate apparatus — nyāsa, navārṇa-vidhi, āvaraṇa-pūjā — and none of that is here. What this file carries is the ordinary household ṣoḍaśopacāra with Durgā as the chosen deity. The Saptaśatī's own ritual apparatus is held separately at `../../devi/durga/` and `../../devi/durga_saptashati/`; nothing has been carried between them.

**Relation to `01_nitya_puja_vidhanam.txt`.** `01` is the generic template, printing `श्री इष्ट देवता` as a slot. This is the same rite with the slot filled in for Durgā by the source itself, published as a complete self-contained order of service and transcribed so. **Units 1–29 are the shared pūrvāṅga, word-for-word the template's**; units 30–60 are the Durgā ṣoḍaśopacāra proper. Measured against the template, 229 of this page's 312 lines are shared and 83 are new — 73% reuse. Its siblings are `04_ganapati_nitya_puja.txt` through `07_lakshmi_nitya_puja.txt`.

Accent: **present throughout.** Udātta `॑` (U+0951), anudātta `॒` (U+0952), dīrgha svarita `᳚` (U+1CDA); the praṇava before a spirant takes the guttural nasal, written `ओग्ं` (`ogṁ`). The source prints its accented mantras in a sandhi-joined orthography that hyphenates across word boundaries, kept exactly as printed and carried into the IAST. **No accent was inferred, derived or reconstructed.**

The Vedic material here is the **Durgā Sūkta**, whose opening verse stands at the meditation (unit 30) — `जा॒तवे॑दसे सुनवाम॒ सोम॑`, Ṛgveda 1.99.1, the verse that plays on *durgāṇi*, 'hard passages', and so on the goddess's own name — together with the **Durgā Gāyatrī** (unit 31), `ओ-ङ्का॒त्या॒य॒नाय॑ वि॒द्महे॑ कन्यकु॒मारि॑ धीमहि । तन्नो॑ दुर्गिः प्रचो॒दया᳚त् ॥`, which returns at the mantrapuṣpa (unit 52).

**This rite has no `स्तोत्रपठनम्` section**, where the Gaṇapati, Śiva and Viṣṇu rites each direct the recitation of named hymns at that point. The omission is the source's and has not been filled in; a worshipper who wished to recite the Saptaśatī or a Devī stotra here would be doing what those other rites prescribe, but this page does not prescribe it. Conversely it carries **three** verses of pardon where they carry one or two, including `आवाहनं न जानामि न जानामि विसर्जनम्` — 'I do not know the invocation, nor do I know the dismissal' — which is among the most widely recited of all kṣamā verses and which none of the sibling files has.

- Gītā Press expands the snāna into the full abhiṣeka series and adds `उपवस्त्र` and `दक्षिणा`, as in its other rites; and it says **आरती** where this text says **नीराजन**.

Blurb: This is the daily pūjā of Durgā in the smārta form, complete in sixty units. It is a household rite and not a Śākta one: there is no nyāsa, no navārṇa-vidhi and no āvaraṇa-pūjā, and the Saptaśatī's own apparatus is kept elsewhere in this corpus. After the materials and the opening acts, the worshipper honours the water-vessel, the conch and the bell, and worships Gaṇapati first so that obstacles may be removed. Durgā is then meditated upon in three units, which carry the opening verse of the Durgā Sūkta and the Durgā Gāyatrī. The sixteen services follow, among them the worship of the goddess limb by limb under sixteen names. The Durgā Gāyatrī returns at the offering of flowers with mantra. Unlike its sibling files this rite prescribes no recitation of hymns, and it carries three verses of pardon rather than one, among them the well-known verse that begins 'I do not know the invocation'. The rite closes with the dismissal and the receiving of the prasāda. Every mantra carries its accent marks.

--- unit 1 | section: आवश्यकानि वस्तूनि ---
vidhi:
The things to be gathered before beginning. The conch is marked optional.
deva:
आवश्यकानि वस्तूनि — आसनं । दीपः । गन्धः । कुङ्कुमं । अक्षताः । पुष्पाणि । धूपः । नैवेद्यं । ताम्बूलं । आचमनीयं । पूजा पात्रं । कलशः । शङ्खः (वैकल्पिकः) । दुर्गा देवी प्रतिमा वा चित्रपटः वा ।
iast:
āvaśyakāni vastūni — āsanaṁ | dīpaḥ | gandhaḥ | kuṅkumaṁ | akṣatāḥ | puṣpāṇi | dhūpaḥ | naivedyaṁ | tāmbūlaṁ | ācamanīyaṁ | pūjā pātraṁ | kalaśaḥ | śaṅkhaḥ (vaikalpikaḥ) | durgā devī pratimā vā citrapaṭaḥ vā |
en: Things required — a seat; a lamp; sandal-paste; kuṅkuma; unbroken rice; flowers; incense; food-offering; betel; water for ācamana; the pūjā vessel; a water-pot; a conch (optional); and an image or picture of the goddess Durgā.
tel: కావలసిన వస్తువులు — ఆసనం; దీపం; గంధం; కుంకుమ; అక్షతలు; పుష్పాలు; ధూపం; నైవేద్యం; తాంబూలం; ఆచమనీయం; పూజా పాత్ర; కలశం; శంఖం (ఐచ్ఛికం); దుర్గాదేవి ప్రతిమ లేదా చిత్రపటం.
hi: आवश्यक वस्तुएँ — आसन; दीप; गन्ध; कुङ्कुम; अक्षत; पुष्प; धूप; नैवेद्य; ताम्बूल; आचमनीय जल; पूजापात्र; कलश; शङ्ख (वैकल्पिक); तथा दुर्गादेवी की प्रतिमा अथवा चित्रपट।

--- unit 3 | section: पूर्वाङ्ग पूजा ---
vidhi:
Purification. Recited to make oneself fit, inwardly and outwardly, for what follows.
deva:
अपवित्रः पवित्रो वा सर्वावस्थां गतोऽपि वा ।
यः स्मरेत् पुण्डरीकाक्षं स बाह्याभ्यन्तरः शुचिः ॥
पुण्डरीकाक्ष पुण्डरीकाक्ष पुण्डरीकाक्षाय नमः ॥
iast:
apavitraḥ pavitro vā sarvāvasthāṁ gato'pi vā |
yaḥ smaret puṇḍarīkākṣaṁ sa bāhyābhyantaraḥ śuciḥ ||
puṇḍarīkākṣa puṇḍarīkākṣa puṇḍarīkākṣāya namaḥ ||
en: Impure or pure, or gone into whatever condition — whoever calls to mind the lotus-eyed one is clean, without and within. Lotus-eyed one, lotus-eyed one: salutation to the lotus-eyed one.
tel: అపవిత్రుడైనా, పవిత్రుడైనా, ఏ అవస్థలో ఉన్నా — పుండరీకాక్షుని ఎవడు స్మరిస్తాడో అతడు బయటా లోపలా శుద్ధుడు. పుండరీకాక్షా, పుండరీకాక్షా, పుండరీకాక్షునికి నమస్కారం.
hi: अपवित्र हो या पवित्र, अथवा किसी भी अवस्था में क्यों न हो — जो पुण्डरीकाक्ष का स्मरण करता है वह बाहर-भीतर से शुद्ध हो जाता है। पुण्डरीकाक्ष, पुण्डरीकाक्ष, पुण्डरीकाक्ष को नमस्कार।

--- unit 4 | section: पूर्वाङ्ग पूजा ---

Prārthanā. The first of the invocatory verses — to Viṣṇu, for the quieting of all obstacles.
deva:
शुक्लाम्बरधरं विष्णुं शशिवर्णं चतुर्भुजम् ।
प्रसन्नवदनं ध्यायेत् सर्वविघ्नोपशान्तये ॥
iast:
śuklāmbaradharaṁ viṣṇuṁ śaśivarṇaṁ caturbhujam |
prasannavadanaṁ dhyāyet sarvavighnopaśāntaye ||
en: For the quieting of every obstacle one should meditate on Viṣṇu — robed in white, moon-coloured, four-armed, his face serene.
tel: సమస్త విఘ్నాలూ శాంతించడానికి — తెల్లని వస్త్రం ధరించినవాడు, చంద్రుని వన్నెవాడు, నాలుగు చేతులవాడు, ప్రసన్నమైన ముఖం కలవాడు అయిన విష్ణువును ధ్యానించాలి.
hi: समस्त विघ्नों की शान्ति के लिये श्वेत वस्त्र धारण किये हुए, चन्द्रमा के समान वर्णवाले, चतुर्भुज, प्रसन्नमुख विष्णु का ध्यान करना चाहिये।

--- unit 54 | section: षोडशोपचार पूजा ---

The eight-limbed prostration.

--- unit 55 | section: षोडशोपचार पूजा ---
vidhi:

deva:
1. ॐ श्री दुर्गायै नमः ।

--- unit 56 | section: क्षमाप्रार्थना ---
vidhi:

dāso'yamiti māṁ matvā kṣamasva parameśvari ||

--- unit 57 | section: क्षमाप्रार्थना ---
vidhi:
The second verse of pardon, which **no other file in this folder carries** — the confession of not knowing how to invoke, how to dismiss, or how to worship at all. It is among the most widely recited of the kṣamā verses.

pūjāṁ caiva na jānāmi kṣamyatāṁ parameśvari ||

--- unit 58 | section: क्षमाप्रार्थना ---
vidhi:
The third verse of pardon, with the vocative `सुरेश्वरि`; then the act itself is surrendered and the fruit of the worship declared. Note the feminine `सुप्रीता सुप्रसन्ना वरदा`.

कायेन वाचा मनसेन्द्रियैर्वा
बुद्ध्यात्मना वा प्रकृतेः स्वभावात् ।
करोमि यद्यत् सकलं परस्मै
नारायणायेति समर्पयामि ॥

iast:
mantrahīnaṁ kriyāhīnaṁ bhaktihīnaṁ sureśvari |
yatpūjitaṁ mayā devi paripūrṇaṁ tadastu te ||

buddhyātmanā vā prakṛteḥ svabhāvāt |

nārāyaṇāyeti samarpayāmi ||
