Six differences were found, and none was reconciled by silently choosing one; the Vratarāja's reading stands in the text.

1. **What is done for the ṛṣis.** The Vratarāja reads `उपाकर्मादि चैवोक्तमृषीणां चैव तर्पणम्` — the upākarma and the rest, and the tarpaṇa of the ṛṣis. The Nirṇayasindhu reads `उपाकर्मणि कर्तव्यमृषीणां चैव पूजनम्` — at the upākarma, the worship of the ṛṣis. Tarpaṇa in the one, pūjana in the other.
2. **What the bundle is made with.** The Vratarāja reads `कारयेच्चाक्षतैस्तद्वत्सिद्धार्थैर्हेमैरर्चितैः`, with its own footnote offering `भूषिताम्`. The Nirṇayasindhu reads `कारयेदक्षतैः शस्तैः सिद्धार्थहेमभूषितैः` — with good akṣata, adorned with white mustard and gold. The witness therefore settles that the Vratarāja's footnote variant is a real reading elsewhere.
3. **Where the kalaśa is set.** The Vratarāja reads `उपलिप्ते गृहदेशे`, in a smeared part of the house; the Nirṇayasindhu reads `उपलिप्ते गृहमध्ये`, in the middle of the house.
4. **How the king takes his seat.** The Vratarāja reads `पीठस्योपरि निविशेद्राजामात्यैर्युतश्च सुमुहूर्ते`; the Nirṇayasindhu reads `पीठे तत्रोपविशेद्राजामात्ययुतश्च सुमुहूर्ते`. The same act in different words.
5. **One syllable of the mantra.** The Vratarāja reads `तेन त्वामभिबध्नामि`; the Nirṇayasindhu reads `तेन त्वामपि बध्नामि`. **This is a difference in the mantra itself and is not bracketed as a variant**; the base text's reading stands, and the witness's is recorded here.
6. **What verse 26 enjoins.** The Vratarāja reads `कर्तव्यो रक्षिकाबन्धो द्विजान्सम्पूज्य भक्तितः` — the tying of the rakṣā is to be done, the brāhmaṇas having been worshipped with devotion. The Nirṇayasindhu reads `कर्तव्यो रक्षिताचारो द्विजान्संपूज्य शक्तितः` — the observance is to be kept, the brāhmaṇas having been worshipped to one's ability. Bandha against ācāra, and bhaktitaḥ against śaktitaḥ.

Second witness, for the time of day: **the Dharmasindhu of Kāśīnātha Upādhyāya, Nirnaya Sagar Press 1888**, archive.org `dharmasindhubykashinathupadhyaya1888nirnaysagarpress_498_a`, second paricchheda, under the marginal rubric `रक्षाबन्धनादि`, read from its text layer. It prints the mantra in the same words as the Vratarāja and adds a rule the base text does not give: the rakṣā is tied on this same paurṇamāsī, free of bhadrā, when the tithi runs more than three muhūrtas past sunrise, in the aparāhṇa or the pradoṣa; if it runs less than three muhūrtas, it is done on the previous day at the pradoṣa or thereabouts, free of bhadrā; and it is to be done even on the day of an eclipse or a saṅkrānti. That rule is given in unit 13, in the `vidhi:` field and never in the verses, so that the purāṇic text stays as the Purāṇa gives it. The book's name is kept here and out of the reader's view, as this corpus keeps all sourcing.

The Nirṇayasindhu adds three further rules on the same page, and they too are reported in unit 13, likewise without its name: the rakṣābandhana has a fixed time, so like the Holikā it is done even on an eclipse day, bhadrā alone excepted; if bhadrā holds all day it is done at night, at bhadrā's end, on the authority of the Nirṇayāmṛta; and it is not done when the pratipad is joined to the full moon, on the authority of the Madanaratna. The Nirṇayasindhu also settles the day's order against the upākarma: even when the upākarma falls on the next day, the rakṣā is still tied on the aparāhṇa of the first — `तेन परेद्युरुपाकरणेऽपि पूर्वेद्युरपराह्णे तत्करणं सिद्धम्`.

Third witness, and the one that matters least for the text: **Hemādri, Caturvargacintāmaṇi, Vratakhaṇḍa**, archive.org `chaturvarga-chintamani-hemadri-ed.-bharat-chandra-shiromani-ganesa-yantra-calcutta-vrata-khanda`. It was looked for because the Vratarāja's own opening line quotes the passage through Hemādri. It does carry the mantra, under its own rubric `रक्षाबन्धनमन्त्रः`, but **in a different setting** — inside a worship of Viṣṇu with Jayā and Vijayā, where the rakṣā is tied on sons, kinsmen and relatives, and where the mantra is followed by phala verses that the Vratarāja has none of: that no planet afflicts and no fear of death comes to one who ties it with devotion, that no bhūta, vetāla, rākṣasa or piśāca overpowers him, and that whoever enters battle having tied the rakṣā conquers all his foes and is conquered by none. **None of Hemādri's text was transcribed.** The OCR of that Calcutta edition is broken beyond use — its own reading of the mantra comes out as `तेन तामाश वध्रामि र्ते माचल माच` — and its page images were not read. The Vratarāja's footnote that Hemādri's Vratakhaṇḍa has more verses and a different order is therefore reported at second hand and was not verified against Hemādri himself. **This is a real gap, and closing it needs the page images of the Vratakhaṇḍa.**

Blurb: This is the rakṣābandhana of the full moon of Śrāvaṇa, as the Bhaviṣyottara Purāṇa gives it. Kṛṣṇa tells Yudhiṣṭhira of a war between the gods and the asuras that lasted twelve years, in which Indra was beaten. Bṛhaspati told him that the time was not for valour, and while the two were speaking Śacī said that the full moon would come at daybreak and that she would make a rakṣā by which he would be unconquerable. She tied it on his right hand, and he went out on Airāvata and won back the three worlds. The day itself runs in order. At sunrise there is the bath, by the rule of śruti and smṛti. Then the gods and the fathers are satisfied with water, and the upākarma is done with brāhmaṇas, with the Veda in view, and the ṛṣis are satisfied. For a śūdra the bath and the giving are praised without mantras. In the afternoon an auspicious little bundle is made — unbroken grains, white mustard and gold, in cotton or silk cloth, unstained, strung with many-coloured thread — and set upon a vessel. The house is guarded with well-drawn circles of cow dung with dūrvā and colour, to quiet misfortune. A pot is set on a prepared square in a smeared part of the house, and the king takes his seat at a good muhūrta with his ministers. Then, with auspicious sounds and raised banners, the deity and the honoured brāhmaṇas are worshipped first with cloths and with the rakṣās, and only then does the purohita tie the rakṣā with the mantra. It is not for one class alone: brāhmaṇas, kṣatriyas, vaiśyas, śūdras and other people all keep it, having worshipped the brāhmaṇas with devotion. Whoever keeps it by this rule is free of every fault and happy for a year. It is never tied in bhadrā.

The section opens by naming the day and the authority, and Yudhiṣṭhira asks for the rite. The two verses state what it is for before anything is done: that a person becomes untouchable by evil spirits, that it quiets every sickness and destroys every ill, and that once done it guards for a year.

रक्षाबन्धविधानं मे किञ्चित्कथय केशव ॥

सर्वरोगोपशमनं सर्वाशुभविनाशनम् ॥
सकृत्कृतेनाब्दमेकं येन रक्षा कृता भवेत् ॥ २ ॥

--- unit 3 | section: व्रतकथा ---
vidhi:
Indra calls Bṛhaspati and says he can neither stand nor go. Bṛhaspati answers that this is not the hour for valour, because what is done out of its place and time turns the wrong way. While the two are speaking, Śacī addresses Indra.
deva:
बृहस्पतिमुपामन्त्र्य इदं वचनमब्रवीत् ॥
न स्थातुं चैव शक्नोमि न गन्तुं तैरभिद्रुतः ॥ ५ ॥
सर्वथा योद्धुमिच्छामि यद्भाव्यं तद्भविष्यति ॥
श्रुत्वा सुरपतेर्वाक्यं बृहस्पतिरथाब्रवीत् ॥ ६ ॥
बृहस्पतिरुवाच ॥
न कालो विक्रमस्याद्य त्यज कोपं पुरन्दर ॥
देशकालविहीनानि कार्याणि विपरीतवत् ॥ ७ ॥
क्रियमाणानि दुष्यन्ति सोऽनर्थः सुमहान् भवेत् ॥
तयोः संवदतोरेवं शची प्राह सुरेश्वरम् ॥ ८ ॥
iast:
bṛhaspatimupāmantrya idaṁ vacanamabravīt ||
na sthātuṁ caiva śaknomi na gantuṁ tairabhidrutaḥ || 5 ||
sarvathā yoddhumicchāmi yadbhāvyaṁ tadbhaviṣyati ||
śrutvā surapatervākyaṁ bṛhaspatirathābravīt || 6 ||
bṛhaspatiruvāca ||
na kālo vikramasyādya tyaja kopaṁ purandara ||
deśakālavihīnāni kāryāṇi viparītavat || 7 ||
kriyamāṇāni duṣyanti so'narthaḥ sumahān bhavet ||
tayoḥ saṁvadatorevaṁ śacī prāha sureśvaram || 8 ||
en: Calling Bṛhaspati, Indra spoke these words: Hard pressed by them, I can neither stand nor go. In every way I wish to fight; what is to be will be. Hearing the words of the lord of the gods, Bṛhaspati then said: Today is not the hour for valour. Give up your anger, Purandara. Works done without their place and their time go the wrong way, and are spoilt in the doing; the loss from that would be very great. While the two were speaking so, Śacī addressed the lord of the gods.
tel: ఇంద్రుడు బృహస్పతిని పిలిచి ఈ మాట చెప్పాడు — వారి చేత తరమబడి నేను నిలువలేను, పోనూ లేను. ఎలాగైనా యుద్ధం చేయాలనుకుంటున్నాను. జరగవలసినది జరుగుతుంది. దేవేంద్రుని మాట విని బృహస్పతి ఇలా అన్నాడు — పురందరా, ఇప్పుడు పరాక్రమానికి కాలం కాదు. కోపం విడిచిపెట్టు. దేశకాలాలు లేకుండా చేసే పనులు తలక్రిందులవుతాయి, చేస్తుండగానే చెడిపోతాయి, దానివలన చాలా పెద్ద అనర్థం కలుగుతుంది. వారిద్దరూ ఇలా మాట్లాడుతుండగా శచీదేవి దేవేంద్రునితో అన్నది.

--- unit 4 | section: व्रतकथा ---
vidhi:
Śacī names the day and makes the rakṣā. This is the act the whole rite comes from: she ties the little bundle on Indra's right hand on the full moon.
deva:
अद्य भूतदिनं देव प्रातः पर्व भविष्यति ॥
अहं रक्षां विधास्यामि येनाजेयो भविष्यसि ॥ ९ ॥
इत्युक्त्वा पौर्णमास्यां सा पौलोमी कृतमङ्गला ॥
बबन्ध दक्षिणे पाणौ रक्षापोटलिकां ततः ॥ १० ॥
बद्धरक्षस्ततः शक्रः कृतस्वस्त्ययनो द्विजैः ॥
आरुह्यैरावतं नागं निर्जगाम सुरारिहा ॥ ११ ॥
iast:
adya bhūtadinaṁ deva prātaḥ parva bhaviṣyati ||
ahaṁ rakṣāṁ vidhāsyāmi yenājeyo bhaviṣyasi || 9 ||
ityuktvā paurṇamāsyāṁ sā paulomī kṛtamaṅgalā ||
babandha dakṣiṇe pāṇau rakṣāpoṭalikāṁ tataḥ || 10 ||
baddharakṣastataḥ śakraḥ kṛtasvastyayano dvijaiḥ ||
āruhyairāvataṁ nāgaṁ nirjagāma surārihā || 11 ||
en: Today, lord, is the bhūta day; at daybreak the parvan will come. I shall make a rakṣā, by which you will be unconquerable. Having said so, on the full moon, Paulomī performed the auspicious rites and tied the little rakṣā bundle on his right hand. Then Indra, the rakṣā bound on him and the benediction spoken over him by the brāhmaṇas, mounted the elephant Airāvata and went forth, the slayer of the enemies of the gods.
tel: దేవా, ఈ రోజు భూత దినం (చతుర్దశి); తెల్లవారగానే పర్వం వస్తుంది. నేను ఒక రక్షను చేస్తాను, దానివలన నీవు అజేయుడవు అవుతావు. ఇలా చెప్పి పౌలోమి పౌర్ణమి నాడు మంగళ కార్యాలు చేసి, ఆయన కుడి చేతికి రక్షా పొట్లికను కట్టింది. రక్ష కట్టుకొని, బ్రాహ్మణుల చేత స్వస్త్యయనం చేయించుకొని, దేవ శత్రువులను సంహరించే ఇంద్రుడు ఐరావత గజాన్ని ఎక్కి బయలుదేరాడు.
hi: हे देव, आज भूत दिन है; प्रातः पर्व होगा। मैं रक्षा करूँगी, जिससे आप अजेय हो जायेंगे। ऐसा कहकर पूर्णिमा के दिन पौलोमी ने मंगल कार्य करके उनके दाहिने हाथ में रक्षा-पोटली बाँध दी। तब रक्षा बँधवाकर और ब्राह्मणों से स्वस्त्ययन कराकर, देवशत्रुओं का नाश करने वाले इन्द्र ऐरावत हाथी पर चढ़कर निकल पड़े।

--- unit 5 | section: व्रतकथा ---
vidhi:
The end of the story, and the fruit stated in it. The verse of the fruit is the story's own summing up, and it names four things: victory, ease, children and health, and wealth.
deva:
दुद्राव दानवानीकं क्षणात्काल इव प्रजाः ॥
शक्रस्तु विजयीभूत्वा पुनरेव जगत्त्रये ॥ १२ ॥
एष प्रभावो रक्षायाः कथितस्ते युधिष्ठिर ॥
जयदः सुखदश्चैव पुत्रारोग्यधनप्रदः ॥ १३ ॥
iast:
dudrāva dānavānīkaṁ kṣaṇātkāla iva prajāḥ ||
śakrastu vijayībhūtvā punareva jagattraye || 12 ||

--- unit 6 | section: विधिप्रश्नः ---
vidhi:
Yudhiṣṭhira asks the three things the rest of the passage answers: by what rule, on what tithi, and at what hour.
deva:
युधिष्ठिर उवाच ॥
क्रियते केन विधिना रक्षाबन्धः सुरोत्तमः ॥
कस्मिंस्तिथौ कदा देव ह्येतन्मे वक्तुमर्हसि ॥ १४ ॥
यथाहि भगवन्देव विचित्राणि प्रभाषसे ॥
तथा तथा न मे तृप्तिर्बह्वर्थाः शृण्वतः कथाः ॥ १५ ॥
iast:
yudhiṣṭhira uvāca ||

kasmiṁstithau kadā deva hyetanme vaktumarhasi || 14 ||
yathāhi bhagavandeva vicitrāṇi prabhāṣase ||

en: Yudhiṣṭhira said: By what rule is this best of tyings of the rakṣā done? On what tithi, and at what hour, lord — this you should tell me. For the more, blessed lord, you speak of these wonderful things, the less am I satisfied, hearing stories so full of meaning.

--- unit 7 | section: दिनक्रमः ---
vidhi:
The order of the day, in the text's own sequence. At sunrise the bath, by the rule of śruti and smṛti. Then the satisfying of the gods and the fathers with water, then the upākarma, then the satisfying of the ṛṣis. These are done together with brāhmaṇas, with the Veda in view, to the measure of one's ability.
**The upākarma is named here as the text names it, and its own text is not given.** It is a Vedic karma with a rule of its own, different in every śākhā, and it is not in this corpus. For it, follow your own ācārya and your own śākhā. Nothing of it is printed in this file.
For a śūdra the verse itself provides: the bath and the giving, without mantras, are what is praised.
deva:
कृष्ण उवाच ॥
संप्राप्ते श्रावणे मासि पौर्णमास्यां दिनोदये ॥
स्नानं कुर्वीत मतिमाञ्छ्रुतिस्मृतिविधानतः ॥ १६ ॥
ततो देवान्पितॄंश्चैव तर्पयेत्परमाम्भसा ॥
उपाकर्मादि चैवोक्तमृषीणां चैव तर्पणम् ॥ १७ ॥
कुर्वीत ब्राह्मणैः सार्धं वेदानुद्दिश्य शक्तितः ॥
शूद्राणां मन्त्ररहित स्नानं दानं च शस्यते ॥ १८ ॥
iast:
kṛṣṇa uvāca ||
saṁprāpte śrāvaṇe māsi paurṇamāsyāṁ dinodaye ||
snānaṁ kurvīta matimāñchrutismṛtividhānataḥ || 16 ||
tato devānpitṝṁścaiva tarpayetparamāmbhasā ||
upākarmādi caivoktamṛṣīṇāṁ caiva tarpaṇam || 17 ||
kurvīta brāhmaṇaiḥ sārdhaṁ vedānuddiśya śaktitaḥ ||
śūdrāṇāṁ mantrarahita snānaṁ dānaṁ ca śasyate || 18 ||
en: Kṛṣṇa said: When the month of Śrāvaṇa has come, on the full moon, at the rising of the day, let the thoughtful person bathe according to the rule of śruti and smṛti. Then let him satisfy the gods and the fathers with the best of water. The upākarma and the rest, which has been enjoined, and likewise the satisfying of the ṛṣis, he should do together with brāhmaṇas, with the Veda in view, to the measure of his ability. For śūdras the bath and the giving, without mantras, are what is praised.
tel: కృష్ణుడు చెప్పాడు — శ్రావణ మాసం వచ్చినప్పుడు, పౌర్ణమి నాడు, సూర్యోదయ సమయంలో, బుద్ధిమంతుడు శ్రుతి స్మృతుల విధానం ప్రకారం స్నానం చేయాలి. తరువాత మంచి నీటితో దేవతలను పితరులను తర్పణం చేయాలి. చెప్పబడిన ఉపాకర్మ మొదలైనవి, అలాగే ఋషి తర్పణం — వీటిని బ్రాహ్మణులతో కలిసి, వేదాన్ని ఉద్దేశించి, తన శక్తి కొలది చేయాలి. శూద్రులకు మంత్రం లేకుండా చేసే స్నానం, దానం ప్రశస్తమైనవి.
hi: कृष्ण ने कहा — श्रावण मास आने पर, पूर्णिमा को, दिन के उदय होते ही, बुद्धिमान् को श्रुति और स्मृति के विधान से स्नान करना चाहिये। फिर उत्तम जल से देवों और पितरों का तर्पण करे। जो उपाकर्म आदि कहा गया है, तथा ऋषियों का तर्पण — ये ब्राह्मणों के साथ, वेद को उद्देश्य करके, अपनी शक्ति के अनुसार करे। शूद्रों के लिये मन्त्र रहित स्नान और दान प्रशस्त हैं।

--- unit 8 | section: रक्षाबन्धनविधिः ---
vidhi:

कार्पासैः क्षौमवस्त्रैर्वा विचित्रैर्मलवर्जितैः ॥
विचित्रतन्तुग्रथितां स्थापयेद्भाजनोपरि ॥ २० ॥

tato'parāhṇasamaye rakṣāpoṭalikāṁ śubhām ||
kārayeccākṣataistadvatsiddhārthairhemairarcitaiḥ || 19 ||
kārpāsaiḥ kṣaumavastrairvā vicitrairmalavarjitaiḥ ||

--- unit 9 | section: रक्षाबन्धनविधिः ---
vidhi:
The house is guarded, and the pot is set. The circles of cow dung with dūrvā and colour are drawn to quiet misfortune; the kalaśa stands on a prepared square in a smeared part of the house; and the seat is taken at a good muhūrta.
deva:
कार्या गृहस्य रक्षा गोमयरचितैः सुवृत्तमण्डलकैः ॥
दूर्वावर्णकसहितैश्चित्रैर्दुरितोपशमनाय ॥ २१ ॥
उपलिप्ते गृहदेशे दत्तचतुष्के न्यसेत्कुम्भम् ॥

en: The guarding of the house is to be done with well-drawn circles made of cow dung, painted and set with dūrvā and colour, for the quieting of misfortune. tel: దురితం శాంతించడం కోసం, గోమయంతో చేసిన చక్కని గుండ్రని మండలాలతో — దూర్వ, వర్ణకంతో కూడిన చిత్రమైన వాటితో — ఇంటికి రక్ష చేయాలి. అలికిన ఇంటి ప్రదేశంలో, సిద్ధం చేసిన చతురస్రం మీద కుంభాన్ని ఉంచాలి. మంచి ముహూర్తంలో రాజు మంత్రులతో కలిసి పీఠం మీద కూర్చోవాలి.

--- unit 10 | section: रक्षाबन्धनविधिः ---
vidhi:
The tying itself, and its order. The deity and the honoured brāhmaṇas are worshipped first, with cloths and with the rakṣās; only after that does the purohita tie the rakṣā, with the mantra of the next unit.
deva:
वेश्याजनेन सहितो मङ्गलशब्दैः समुच्छ्रितैश्चिह्नैः ॥
रक्षाबन्धः कार्यः प्रशान्तिदः सर्वविघ्नानाम् ॥ २३ ॥
देवद्विजातिशस्तान्वस्त्रैरक्षाभिरर्चयेत्प्रथमम् ॥
तदनु पुरोधा नृपते रक्षां बध्नीत मन्त्रेण ॥ २४ ॥
iast:
veśyājanena sahito maṅgalaśabdaiḥ samucchritaiścihnaiḥ ||
rakṣābandhaḥ kāryaḥ praśāntidaḥ sarvavighnānām || 23 ||
devadvijātiśastānvastrairakṣābhirarcayetprathamam ||
tadanu purodhā nṛpate rakṣāṁ badhnīta mantreṇa || 24 ||
en: Attended by the people of the household, with auspicious sounds and with raised banners, the tying of the rakṣā is to be done, which gives the quieting of all obstacles. Let him first worship, with cloths and with the rakṣās, the deity and the honoured twice-born; and after that let the purohita tie the rakṣā upon the king with the mantra.

The mantra. It is said while the thread is being tied, and it is the one verse of this rite that is in daily use.

--- unit 12 | section: अधिकारः फलं च ---
vidhi:
Who keeps it, and what it gives. The verse is plain that the rite is not for one class: brāhmaṇas, kṣatriyas, vaiśyas, śūdras and other people all keep it, after worshipping the brāhmaṇas with devotion. The fruit is stated as freedom from every fault, and happiness, for one year.

अनेन विधिना यस्तु रक्षाबन्धं समाचरेत् ॥

iast:
brāhmaṇaiḥ kṣatriyaivaiśyaiḥ śūdrairanyaiśca mānavaiḥ ||

--- unit 13 | section: कालनिर्णयः ---
vidhi:
The rule of time. The verses give one rule, and it is absolute: the tying is not done in bhadrā. Two full moons are named as forbidden in bhadrā, the Śrāvaṇī and the Phālgunī, and what each destroys is stated.
The learned works on the reckoning of days settle the rest. Their rules are not in the purāṇic passage, and are set out here so that the day can be fixed.
1. The tying is done on this same full moon, free of bhadrā, when the tithi runs more than three muhūrtas past sunrise. The hour is the aparāhṇa or the pradoṣa.
2. If the tithi runs less than three muhūrtas past sunrise, the tying is done on the previous day, at the pradoṣa or thereabouts, free of bhadrā.
3. The time being fixed, the tying is done even on the day of an eclipse or of a saṅkrānti.
4. If bhadrā holds through the day, the tying is done at night, when bhadrā has ended.
5. The tying is not done when the pratipad is joined to the full moon.
6. Even when the upākarma falls on the next day, the tying is still done on the aparāhṇa of the first day.
deva:
अयं रक्षा बन्धो भद्रायां न कार्यः ॥
भद्रायां द्वे न कर्तव्ये श्रावणी फाल्गुनी तथा ॥
श्रावणी नृपतिं हन्ति ग्रामं दहति फाल्गुनी ॥
इति वचनात् ।
इति भविष्योत्तरपुराणे रक्षाबन्धनपौर्णमासीव्रतम् ॥
iast:
ayaṁ rakṣā bandho bhadrāyāṁ na kāryaḥ ||
bhadrāyāṁ dve na kartavye śrāvaṇī phālgunī tathā ||
śrāvaṇī nṛpatiṁ hanti grāmaṁ dahati phālgunī ||
iti vacanāt |
iti bhaviṣyottarapurāṇe rakṣābandhanapaurṇamāsīvratam ||
en: This tying of the rakṣā is not to be done in bhadrā — on the authority of the saying: In bhadrā two are not to be done, the Śrāvaṇī and likewise the Phālgunī; the Śrāvaṇī kills the king, and the Phālgunī burns the village. Here ends the Rakṣābandhana Paurṇamāsī Vrata in the Bhaviṣyottara Purāṇa.
tel: ఈ రక్షాబంధనం భద్ర ఉన్నప్పుడు చేయకూడదు — ఎందుకంటే ఇలా చెప్పబడింది: భద్రలో రెండు చేయకూడదు, శ్రావణి మరియు ఫాల్గుని. శ్రావణి రాజును చంపుతుంది, ఫాల్గుని గ్రామాన్ని దహిస్తుంది. భవిష్యోత్తర పురాణంలోని రక్షాబంధన పౌర్ణమాసీ వ్రతం ఇక్కడ ముగిసింది.
hi: यह रक्षाबन्धन भद्रा में नहीं करना चाहिये — इस वचन के कारण: भद्रा में दो नहीं करने चाहिये, श्रावणी और फाल्गुनी। श्रावणी राजा को मारती है और फाल्गुनी ग्राम को जलाती है। इस प्रकार भविष्योत्तर पुराण में रक्षाबन्धन पौर्णमासी व्रत समाप्त हुआ।
