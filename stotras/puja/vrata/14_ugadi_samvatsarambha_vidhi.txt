Blurb: This is the rite of the new year, kept on the first day of the bright fortnight of the month of Caitra, the day called Ugādi in the South. The year is held to begin on this day because Brahmā began the creation of the world on it, at sunrise; and for that reason the tithi taken is the one that is running at sunrise. The weekday on which the sun rises that morning is the lord of the whole year. The day opens with the oil bath, which is not optional but obligatory. Then comes the great śānti, which quiets every portent and destroys the sorrow of the age. Brahmā, who was born from the lotus, is worshipped first, with water for the feet, flowers, incense, cloth, ornaments, homa, offerings and the feeding of brāhmaṇas; after him the other gods are worshipped one by one. The mantra for each is made by setting the name in the fourth case, with the praṇava before it and salutation after it. The long prayer that follows is the heart of the day. It salutes time itself, division by division, beginning with the blink of an eye and going on through the truṭi, the lava, the kṣaṇa, the kalā, the muhūrta, the days and nights, the fortnights, the months, the six seasons, the two ayanas and the years; then the four ages, the planets, the twenty-eight nakṣatras, the rāśis and the karaṇas; then the mountains, the rivers, the holy places, the seven seas and the nine lands of the world; then the sages, the gods and the forefathers. It ends with salutation to Viṣṇu of many forms, the supreme self. After the prayer the brāhmaṇas are worshipped with the gods in view, the homa is offered for the satisfaction of all, the hall is decorated, friends and kinsmen are fed, and a great festival is held. The other acts of the day are the raising of a flag at every house, the eating of the nīm leaf, and the hearing of the phala of the new year, which is the hearing of the pañcāṅga. The Vasanta Navarātri begins on this same day.

--- unit 1 | section: तिथिनिर्णयः ---
vidhi:
The book opens the section with the day and with the rule for choosing it. The year begins on the first tithi of the bright fortnight of Caitra. The rule is that the pratipad must be the one running at sunrise, and the book gives two authorities for it. The first is the Brahma Purāṇa, which says that Brahmā began the creation of the world on that day, at sunrise. The second is the Bhaviṣyottara Purāṇa, which says the same thing in other words.
deva:
अथ चैत्रशुक्लप्रतिपदि सवत्सरारम्भविधिः ॥
ब्राह्मे—अत्र प्रतिपत्सूर्योदयव्यापिनी ग्राह्या ॥
चैत्रे मासि जगद्ब्रह्मा ससर्ज प्रथमेऽह्नि ॥
शुक्लपक्षे समग्रे तु तदा सूर्योदये सति ॥ इतिवचनात् ॥
प्रवृत्ते मधुमासे तु प्रतिपद्युदिते रवौ ॥ इति भविष्योत्तराच्च ॥
iast:
atha caitraśuklapratipadi savatsarārambhavidhiḥ ||
brāhme—atra pratipatsūryodayavyāpinī grāhyā ||
caitre māsi jagadbrahmā sasarja prathame'hni ||
śuklapakṣe samagre tu tadā sūryodaye sati || itivacanāt ||
pravṛtte madhumāse tu pratipadyudite ravau || iti bhaviṣyottarācca ||
en: Now the rite for the beginning of the year, on the first day of the bright fortnight of Caitra. In the Brahma Purāṇa: here the pratipad that is running at sunrise is to be taken. In the month of Caitra, on the first day, Brahmā created the world; it was the full bright fortnight, and the sun had risen. That is the authority. And from the Bhaviṣyottara Purāṇa: when the honey month has begun, on the pratipad, the sun having risen.
tel: ఇక చైత్ర శుక్ల పాడ్యమి నాడు సంవత్సరారంభ విధానం. బ్రహ్మ పురాణంలో: ఇక్కడ సూర్యోదయ వ్యాపిని అయిన పాడ్యమినే గ్రహించాలి. చైత్ర మాసంలో మొదటి రోజున బ్రహ్మ జగత్తును సృష్టించాడు; అది శుక్ల పక్షం, సూర్యుడు ఉదయించిన సమయం. ఇదే ప్రమాణం. భవిష్యోత్తర పురాణంలో కూడా: మధుమాసం ప్రవృత్తమైనప్పుడు, సూర్యుడు ఉదయించిన పాడ్యమి నాడు.
hi: अब चैत्र शुक्ल प्रतिपदा को संवत्सर के आरम्भ की विधि। ब्रह्म पुराण में — यहाँ सूर्योदयव्यापिनी प्रतिपदा ही लेनी चाहिये। चैत्र मास में पहले दिन ब्रह्मा ने जगत् की रचना की; वह शुक्ल पक्ष था और सूर्य उदय हो चुका था। यही वचन प्रमाण है। भविष्योत्तर पुराण से भी — मधुमास के प्रवृत्त होने पर, उदय हुए सूर्य वाली प्रतिपदा को।

--- unit 2 | section: तिथिनिर्णयः ---
vidhi:
What is to be done when the tithi runs at sunrise on both days, or on neither. The rule given is that the earlier day is taken, and the authority is a verse of Vṛddhavasiṣṭha. The verse names three occasions together: the beginning of the year, the beginning of spring, and the pratipad of Bali's kingdom. The base text prints बलिराज्य; the Nirṇayasindhu prints बलिराज्ये, and that reading is given in the bracket.
deva:
दिनद्वये व्याप्ताववयाप्तौ वा पूर्वैव ॥
वत्सरादौ वसन्तादौ बलिराज्य(बलिराज्ये) तथैव च ॥
पूर्वविद्धैव कर्तव्या प्रतिपत्सर्वदा बुधैः ॥
इति वृद्धवसिष्ठवचानादिति बहवः ॥
iast:
dinadvaye vyāptāvavayāptau vā pūrvaiva ||
vatsarādau vasantādau balirājya(balirājye) tathaiva ca ||
pūrvaviddhaiva kartavyā pratipatsarvadā budhaiḥ ||
iti vṛddhavasiṣṭhavacānāditi bahavaḥ ||
en: If the tithi covers sunrise on both days, or on neither, the earlier day is taken. At the beginning of the year, at the beginning of spring, and likewise in the kingdom of Bali, the wise should always keep the pratipad joined to the day before. So many say, on the authority of the words of Vṛddhavasiṣṭha.
tel: రెండు రోజులూ ఆ తిథి సూర్యోదయాన్ని పొందినా, లేదా రెండు రోజులూ పొందకపోయినా, మొదటి రోజునే గ్రహించాలి. సంవత్సరాది నాడు, వసంతాది నాడు, అలాగే బలిరాజ్యంలోనూ పండితులు ఎప్పుడూ పూర్వవిద్ధమైన పాడ్యమినే గ్రహించాలి. వృద్ధవసిష్ఠుని వచనాన్ని బట్టి చాలామంది ఇలా చెబుతారు.
hi: यदि दोनों दिन वह तिथि सूर्योदय में व्याप्त हो, अथवा दोनों दिन न हो, तो पहला दिन ही लेना चाहिये। संवत्सर के आदि में, वसन्त के आदि में और बलिराज्य में भी विद्वानों को सदा पूर्वविद्धा प्रतिपदा ही करनी चाहिये। वृद्धवसिष्ठ के वचन से बहुत लोग ऐसा कहते हैं।

--- unit 3 | section: तिथिनिर्णयः ---
vidhi:
The compiler now gives his own ruling. He says the verse just quoted applies only in the case where the pratipad does not touch sunrise on either day, because then the acts of the new year would otherwise be left undone. Where the pratipad does touch sunrise on both days, the earlier day is already complete in itself and is taken for that reason, not on the strength of this verse.
deva:
युक्तं तु, दिनद्वयेऽप्युदयसम्बन्धाभावे संवत्सरारम्भप्रयुक्तकार्यलोपप्रसक्ताविदं वचनं पूर्वयुताग्राह्यताविधायकम् ।
दिनद्वये तत्सम्बन्धे तु संपूर्णत्वादेव पूर्वाप्राप्तेः ।
कदा कार्यमित्याकांक्षाविरहात्पूर्वयुतत्वविरहाच्च नैतद्वचनात्पूर्वेति ॥
iast:
yuktaṁ tu, dinadvaye'pyudayasambandhābhāve saṁvatsarārambhaprayuktakāryalopaprasaktāvidaṁ vacanaṁ pūrvayutāgrāhyatāvidhāyakam |
dinadvaye tatsambandhe tu saṁpūrṇatvādeva pūrvāprāpteḥ |
kadā kāryamityākāṁkṣāvirahātpūrvayutatvavirahācca naitadvacanātpūrveti ||
en: What is proper is this. When on both days there is no connection with sunrise, the acts enjoined for the beginning of the year would be lost; and it is for that case that this verse lays down that the day joined to the earlier one is to be taken. But when on both days there is that connection, the earlier day is obtained simply because it is complete in itself. There is then no question left as to when the acts are to be done, and there is no joining to an earlier day either. So the earlier day is not taken on the strength of this verse.
tel: సరైన నిర్ణయం ఇది. రెండు రోజులూ సూర్యోదయ సంబంధం లేనప్పుడు సంవత్సరారంభానికి విహితమైన కార్యాలు లోపిస్తాయి; ఆ సందర్భం కోసమే ఈ వచనం పూర్వయుత తిథిని గ్రహించాలని విధిస్తుంది. రెండు రోజులూ ఆ సంబంధం ఉన్నప్పుడు మాత్రం, పూర్వ తిథి సంపూర్ణమైనందునే అది గ్రాహ్యమవుతుంది. అప్పుడు ఎప్పుడు చేయాలనే ఆకాంక్ష ఉండదు, పూర్వయుతత్వమూ ఉండదు. కాబట్టి ఈ వచనం వల్ల పూర్వ తిథి గ్రహించబడటం లేదు.
hi: उचित यह है। जब दोनों दिन सूर्योदय का सम्बन्ध न हो, तब संवत्सर के आरम्भ में विहित कार्यों का लोप होगा; उसी अवस्था के लिये यह वचन पूर्वयुता तिथि के ग्रहण का विधान करता है। परन्तु जब दोनों दिन वह सम्बन्ध हो, तब पूर्वा तिथि सम्पूर्ण होने से ही प्राप्त हो जाती है। तब कब करना चाहिये यह आकांक्षा नहीं रहती और पूर्वयुतपना भी नहीं रहता। इसलिये इस वचन से पूर्वा का ग्रहण नहीं होता।

--- unit 4 | section: तिथिनिर्णयः ---
vidhi:
A second book, the Nirṇayasindhu, settles the same day and carries two verses the base text does not print. The first, from the Jyotirnibandha, says that the weekday on which the sun rises on this pratipad is the lord of the whole year, and repeats that the earlier day is taken. The second, from the Viṣṇudharma quoted through Hemādri, prescribes the worship of Brahmā on this day with a fast, and states the fruit.
deva:
चैत्रे सितप्रतिपदि यो वारोऽर्कोदये स वर्षेशः ।
उदयद्वितये पूर्वो नोदययुगलेपि पूर्वः स्यात् ॥
यस्माच्चैत्रसितादेरुदयाद्भानोः प्रवृत्तिरब्दादेः ॥
चैत्रमासस्य या शुक्लप्रथमा प्रतिपद्भवेत् ।
तदह्नि ब्रह्मणः कृत्वा सोपवासस्तु पूजनम् ॥
संवत्सरमवाप्नोति सौख्यानि भृगुनन्दन ॥
iast:
caitre sitapratipadi yo vāro'rkodaye sa varṣeśaḥ |
udayadvitaye pūrvo nodayayugalepi pūrvaḥ syāt ||
yasmāccaitrasitāderudayādbhānoḥ pravṛttirabdādeḥ ||
caitramāsasya yā śuklaprathamā pratipadbhavet |
tadahni brahmaṇaḥ kṛtvā sopavāsastu pūjanam ||
saṁvatsaramavāpnoti saukhyāni bhṛgunandana ||
en: On the bright pratipad of Caitra, the weekday on which the sun rises is the lord of the year. If the sun rises on it on two days, the earlier is taken; and if on neither of the two days, the earlier is taken as well. This is because the beginning of the year proceeds from the sunrise of the bright first day of Caitra. Whatever is the first day of the bright fortnight of the month of Caitra, on that day one should perform the worship of Brahmā, keeping a fast. He obtains the whole year with its comforts, O son of Bhṛgu.
tel: చైత్ర శుక్ల పాడ్యమి నాడు సూర్యోదయ సమయంలో ఏ వారం ఉంటుందో అదే ఆ సంవత్సరానికి అధిపతి. రెండు రోజులూ ఉదయం ఉంటే పూర్వ తిథిని, రెండు రోజులూ లేకపోయినా పూర్వ తిథినే గ్రహించాలి. ఎందుకంటే చైత్ర శుక్లాది నాటి సూర్యోదయం నుండే సంవత్సరాది ప్రవర్తిస్తుంది. చైత్ర మాసంలో శుక్ల పక్షపు మొదటి పాడ్యమి ఏదో, ఆ రోజున ఉపవాసంతో బ్రహ్మ పూజ చేయాలి. ఓ భృగునందనా, అతడు సంవత్సరమంతటి సౌఖ్యాలను పొందుతాడు.
hi: चैत्र शुक्ल प्रतिपदा को सूर्योदय के समय जो वार होता है वही उस वर्ष का स्वामी है। दो दिन उदय हो तो पूर्वा, और दोनों दिन न हो तो भी पूर्वा ही लेनी चाहिये। क्योंकि चैत्र शुक्लादि के सूर्योदय से ही वर्ष का आरम्भ प्रवृत्त होता है। चैत्र मास के शुक्ल पक्ष की जो पहली प्रतिपदा हो, उस दिन उपवास के साथ ब्रह्मा का पूजन करना चाहिये। हे भृगुनन्दन, वह सम्पूर्ण वर्ष के सुखों को प्राप्त करता है।

--- unit 5 | section: तिथिनिर्णयः ---
vidhi:
The oil bath. The same second book makes the oil bath obligatory on this day and quotes Vasiṣṭha for it, in a verse built on the same three occasions as the verse in unit 2. The Dharmasindhu states the same rule in plain words, and adds that failing to do it brings harm. Neither book prints a mantra for the oil bath.
deva:
तत्र तैलाभ्यङ्गो नित्यः ।
वत्सरादौ वसन्तादौ बलिराज्ये तथैव च ।
तैलाभ्यङ्गमकुर्वाणो नरकं प्रतिपद्यते ॥ इति वसिष्ठोक्तेः ।
अस्यां तैलाभ्यङ्गो नित्यः । अकरणे प्रत्यवायोक्तेः ॥
iast:
tatra tailābhyaṅgo nityaḥ |
vatsarādau vasantādau balirājye tathaiva ca |
tailābhyaṅgamakurvāṇo narakaṁ pratipadyate || iti vasiṣṭhokteḥ |
asyāṁ tailābhyaṅgo nityaḥ | akaraṇe pratyavāyokteḥ ||
en: On this day the oil bath is obligatory. At the beginning of the year, at the beginning of spring, and likewise in the kingdom of Bali, one who does not take the oil bath goes to hell. So Vasiṣṭha has said. On this tithi the oil bath is obligatory, because harm is declared for one who does not do it.
tel: ఈ రోజున తైలాభ్యంగం నిత్యం. సంవత్సరాది నాడు, వసంతాది నాడు, అలాగే బలిరాజ్యంలోనూ తైలాభ్యంగం చేయనివాడు నరకాన్ని పొందుతాడు. ఇది వసిష్ఠుని వచనం. ఈ తిథి నాడు తైలాభ్యంగం నిత్యం; చేయకపోతే ప్రత్యవాయం చెప్పబడింది కాబట్టి.
hi: इस दिन तैलाभ्यङ्ग नित्य है। संवत्सर के आदि में, वसन्त के आदि में और बलिराज्य में भी जो तैलाभ्यङ्ग नहीं करता वह नरक को प्राप्त होता है। ऐसा वसिष्ठ ने कहा है। इस तिथि में तैलाभ्यङ्ग नित्य है, क्योंकि न करने पर प्रत्यवाय कहा गया है।

--- unit 6 | section: महाशान्तिः ---
vidhi:
The Brahma Purāṇa is quoted again, for what was begun on this day. Brahmā set the reckoning of time going, and gave the planets, the years, the seasons, the months and the fortnights to the lords of the saṁvatsaras. The gods and the sages bowed to him, praised him and served him in his assembly, and then went to the Himālaya and took up each his own work. What was kept as dharma from that day onward is still kept, and is to be kept with care.
deva:
ब्राह्मे—प्रवर्तयामास तथा कालस्य गणनामपि ॥
ग्रहानब्दानृतून्मासान्पक्षान् संवत्सराधिपान् ॥
ददौ स भगवान् ब्रह्मा सर्वदेवसमागमे ।
ब्राह्मयां सभायां ब्रह्माणमनिर्देश्यतनुं ततः ॥
यथाक्तास्ते नमस्यन्तः स्तुवन्तश्चाप्युपासते ॥
तपस्ते कृतशुश्रूषा गत्वा चैव हिमालयम् ॥
स्वानि स्वान्यथ कर्माणि तेन युक्ताश्च चक्रिरे ॥
ब्राह्मी सभा कामरूपा विशेषेण तदानघ ॥
धारयन्त्यमलं रूपमनिर्देश्यं मनोहरम् ॥
ततःप्रभृति यो धर्मः पूर्वैः पूर्वतरैः कृतः ॥
अद्यापि रूढः सुतरां स कर्तव्यः प्रयत्नतः ।
iast:
brāhme—pravartayāmāsa tathā kālasya gaṇanāmapi ||
grahānabdānṛtūnmāsānpakṣān saṁvatsarādhipān ||
dadau sa bhagavān brahmā sarvadevasamāgame |
brāhmayāṁ sabhāyāṁ brahmāṇamanirdeśyatanuṁ tataḥ ||
yathāktāste namasyantaḥ stuvantaścāpyupāsate ||
tapaste kṛtaśuśrūṣā gatvā caiva himālayam ||
svāni svānyatha karmāṇi tena yuktāśca cakrire ||
brāhmī sabhā kāmarūpā viśeṣeṇa tadānagha ||
dhārayantyamalaṁ rūpamanirdeśyaṁ manoharam ||
tataḥprabhṛti yo dharmaḥ pūrvaiḥ pūrvataraiḥ kṛtaḥ ||
adyāpi rūḍhaḥ sutarāṁ sa kartavyaḥ prayatnataḥ |
en: In the Brahma Purāṇa: he also set going the reckoning of time. In the assembly of all the gods, the lord Brahmā gave the planets, the years, the seasons, the months and the fortnights to the lords of the saṁvatsaras. Then, in the assembly of Brahmā, they worshipped Brahmā whose body cannot be described, bowing to him and praising him as they had been told. Having done him service, they went to the Himālaya and there, joined to him, performed each his own work. O sinless one, the assembly of Brahmā took at that time whatever form it wished, and it bore especially a spotless form, beyond description and delightful. The dharma that was kept from that day onward by those before us, and by those before them, is firmly established even now, and is to be kept with effort.
tel: బ్రహ్మ పురాణంలో: ఆయనే కాలగణనను కూడా ప్రవర్తింపజేశాడు. సర్వదేవతల సమావేశంలో భగవంతుడైన బ్రహ్మ గ్రహాలను, సంవత్సరాలను, ఋతువులను, మాసాలను, పక్షాలను సంవత్సరాధిపతులకు ఇచ్చాడు. తరువాత బ్రాహ్మీ సభలో, నిర్దేశింపరాని శరీరం కల బ్రహ్మను వారు చెప్పినట్లుగా నమస్కరిస్తూ, స్తుతిస్తూ ఉపాసించారు. ఆయనకు శుశ్రూష చేసి హిమాలయానికి వెళ్ళి, ఆయనతో కూడి తమ తమ కర్మలను చేశారు. ఓ అనఘా, ఆ సమయంలో బ్రాహ్మీ సభ కోరిన రూపాన్ని ధరించేది; విశేషంగా అది నిర్మలమైన, నిర్దేశింపరాని, మనోహరమైన రూపాన్ని ధరించేది. ఆ రోజు నుండి పూర్వులు, వారికంటే పూర్వులు ఆచరించిన ధర్మం ఇప్పటికీ స్థిరంగా నిలిచి ఉంది; దానిని ప్రయత్నపూర్వకంగా ఆచరించాలి.
hi: ब्रह्म पुराण में — उन्होंने काल की गणना भी प्रवृत्त की। सब देवताओं के समागम में भगवान् ब्रह्मा ने ग्रहों, वर्षों, ऋतुओं, मासों और पक्षों को संवत्सर के अधिपतियों को दिया। फिर ब्राह्मी सभा में, अनिर्देश्य शरीर वाले ब्रह्मा को उन्होंने जैसा कहा गया था वैसे नमस्कार और स्तुति करते हुए उपासा। उनकी शुश्रूषा करके वे हिमालय गये और वहाँ उनसे युक्त होकर अपने अपने कर्म किये। हे अनघ, उस समय ब्राह्मी सभा इच्छानुसार रूप धारण करती थी, और विशेष रूप से निर्मल, अनिर्देश्य और मनोहर रूप धारण करती थी। उस दिन से जो धर्म पूर्वजों ने और उनसे भी पूर्वजों ने किया, वह आज भी दृढ़ रूप से प्रचलित है; उसे प्रयत्न से करना चाहिये।

--- unit 7 | section: महाशान्तिः ---
vidhi:
The great śānti is the first act of the day. The verses say what it does. Nothing is printed here about how it is performed; the text names it and states its fruit, and goes straight on to the worship of Brahmā.
deva:
तत्र कार्या महाशान्तिः सर्वकल्मषनाशिनी ॥
सर्वोत्पातप्रशमनी कलिदुःखप्रणाशिनी ॥
आयुःप्रदा वृद्धिकरी धनसौभाग्यवर्धनी ॥
मङ्गल्या च पवित्रा च लोकद्वयसुखावहा ॥
iast:
tatra kāryā mahāśāntiḥ sarvakalmaṣanāśinī ||
sarvotpātapraśamanī kaliduḥkhapraṇāśinī ||
āyuḥpradā vṛddhikarī dhanasaubhāgyavardhanī ||
maṅgalyā ca pavitrā ca lokadvayasukhāvahā ||
en: On that day the great śānti is to be performed. It destroys every stain. It quiets every portent and destroys the sorrow of the Kali age. It gives long life, it brings increase, and it makes wealth and good fortune grow. It is auspicious and it is pure, and it brings happiness in both worlds.
tel: ఆ రోజున మహాశాంతిని చేయాలి. అది సర్వ కల్మషాలను నాశనం చేస్తుంది. అన్ని ఉత్పాతాలను శమింపజేస్తుంది, కలి దుఃఖాన్ని నశింపజేస్తుంది. ఆయువును ఇస్తుంది, వృద్ధిని కలిగిస్తుంది, ధన సౌభాగ్యాలను పెంచుతుంది. అది మంగళకరమైనది, పవిత్రమైనది, రెండు లోకాలలోనూ సుఖాన్ని కలిగించేది.
hi: उस दिन महाशान्ति करनी चाहिये। वह सब पापों का नाश करने वाली है। सब उत्पातों को शान्त करने वाली और कलि के दुःख को नष्ट करने वाली है। वह आयु देने वाली, वृद्धि करने वाली और धन तथा सौभाग्य को बढ़ाने वाली है। वह मङ्गलमयी और पवित्र है, और दोनों लोकों में सुख देने वाली है।

--- unit 8 | section: ब्रह्मपूजा ---
vidhi:
The worship itself. Brahmā, who was born from the lotus, is worshipped first, and the services are named. After him the other gods are worshipped in order, each one separately.
deva:
तस्यामादौ तु संपूज्यो ब्रह्मा कमलसंभवः ॥
पाद्यार्घ्यं पुष्पधूपैश्च वस्त्रालंकारभूषणैः ॥
होमैर्बल्युपहारैश्च तथा ब्राह्मणभोजनैः ॥
ततः क्रमेण देवेभ्यः पूजा कार्या पृथक्पृथक् ॥
iast:
tasyāmādau tu saṁpūjyo brahmā kamalasaṁbhavaḥ ||
pādyārghyaṁ puṣpadhūpaiśca vastrālaṁkārabhūṣaṇaiḥ ||
homairbalyupahāraiśca tathā brāhmaṇabhojanaiḥ ||
tataḥ krameṇa devebhyaḥ pūjā kāryā pṛthakpṛthak ||
en: On that day Brahmā, born from the lotus, is to be worshipped first — with water for the feet and the arghya, with flowers and incense, with cloth and ornaments and jewels, with homa, with the bali offering, with gifts, and with the feeding of brāhmaṇas. After that the worship of the gods is to be done in order, each one separately.

--- unit 9 | section: ब्रह्मपूजा ---
vidhi:
How the gods are worshipped, and how their mantras are made. Each service is offered with kuśa water, sesame and unbroken rice, with flower, incense and lamp, and with food. The mantra is made by putting the praṇava at the beginning and the salutation at the end. The book then explains its own words: मन्त्रम् is a singular used for the whole class, and बहुरूपं means that the many mantras are to be taken up, that is, every name in the prayer that follows, from ॐ नमो ब्रह्मणे to ॐ विष्णवे परमात्मने नमः, is set in the fourth case with ॐ before it and नमः after it, and is used as the mantra of that deity.

मंत्रमिति जातावेकवचनम् ॥
बहुरूपं मंत्र नानारूपान्मंत्रान्परिस्पृशेत्परिगृह्णीयादित्यर्थः ॥

--- unit 10 | section: कालप्रार्थनामन्त्राः ---
vidhi:
The prayer begins. It salutes Brahmā first, and then the divisions of time one after another, from the smallest to the largest — the blink of an eye, the truṭi, the lava, the kṣaṇa, the kāṣṭhā, the kalā, the nāḍikā and the muhūrta, then the nights and the days, the two fortnights, the months, the six seasons, the two ayanas and the five years of the yuga.

One who knows the Veda should always offer the two portions of ghee at the place of the principal offering. यविष्ठाय means to the best. वेदवित् means one who knows the rule stated in the Veda. In the Madanaratna the reading is वेदवत्, and it is explained as by the rule stated in the Veda. चक्षुषी means the two portions of ghee. प्राधानिके स्थाने means at the beginning of the principal homa.

--- unit 20 | section: ब्राह्मणपूजा होमो महोत्सवश्च ---
vidhi:
The end of the rite. After the homa the auspicious beginning is made, and the book records that the Madanaratna reads instead that the hall is decorated first and the auspicious beginning made after. Then the brāhmaṇas, friends, relatives and kinsmen are fed, the worshipper eats, and a great festival is held. The section closes with the statement of what this rite is.
deva:
होमारम्भे ततः कुर्यान्मङ्गलारम्भणं नरः ॥
मदनरत्ने—शालाशोभां ततः कुर्यान्मङ्गलालम्भनं ततः ॥ इति पाठः ॥
भोजयित्वा द्विजान्सर्वान्सुहृत्सम्बन्धिबान्धवान् ॥
विशेषेण च भोक्तव्यं कार्यश्चापि महोत्सवः ॥
वनसंवत्सरारम्भः सर्वसिद्धिप्रवर्तकः ॥
इति संवत्सरारम्भविधिः ॥
iast:
homārambhe tataḥ kuryānmaṅgalārambhaṇaṁ naraḥ ||
madanaratne—śālāśobhāṁ tataḥ kuryānmaṅgalālambhanaṁ tataḥ || iti pāṭhaḥ ||
bhojayitvā dvijānsarvānsuhṛtsambandhibāndhavān ||
viśeṣeṇa ca bhoktavyaṁ kāryaścāpi mahotsavaḥ ||
vanasaṁvatsarārambhaḥ sarvasiddhipravartakaḥ ||
iti saṁvatsarārambhavidhiḥ ||
en: At the beginning of the homa a man should make the auspicious beginning. In the Madanaratna the reading is: he should then decorate the hall, and after that make the auspicious beginning. Having fed all the brāhmaṇas, and his friends, relatives and kinsmen, he should himself eat with care, and a great festival should be held. This beginning of the new year sets every accomplishment going. Thus the rite for the beginning of the year.
tel: హోమారంభంలో మనిషి మంగళారంభం చేయాలి. మదనరత్నంలో పాఠం ఇలా ఉంది: ముందు శాలను అలంకరించి, ఆ తరువాత మంగళారంభం చేయాలి. బ్రాహ్మణులందరికీ, మిత్రులకు, సంబంధీకులకు, బాంధవులకు భోజనం పెట్టి, తాను విశేషంగా భుజించాలి; మహోత్సవం కూడా చేయాలి. ఈ నూతన సంవత్సరారంభం సర్వ సిద్ధులను ప్రవర్తింపజేసేది. ఇది సంవత్సరారంభ విధి.

--- unit 21 | section: दिनस्य अन्यानि कृत्यानि ---
vidhi:
What else belongs to the day. The Dharmasindhu names the acts in one sentence. The oil bath, and the saṅkalpa in which the name of the new saṁvatsara is spoken, and the beginning of study, are done on the pratipad of the mala-māsa itself when Caitra is intercalary. At every house a flag is raised. The nīm leaf is eaten. The phala of the new year is heard, which is the hearing of the pañcāṅga. The Navarātri begins on the same day. No mantra is printed for the raising of the flag, for the eating of the nīm leaf, or for the hearing of the pañcāṅga.
deva:
चैत्रस्य मलमासतये वत्सरारम्भनिमित्तकतैलाभ्यङ्गसंकल्पादौ नूतनवत्सरनामकीर्तनाध्यारम्भश्च मलमासप्रतिपद्येव कुर्यात् ।
प्रतिगृहं ध्वजारोपणं निम्बपत्राशनं वत्सरादिफलश्रवणं नवरात्रारम्भो नवरात्रोत्सवादिनिमित्ताभ्यङ्गादिश्च शुद्धमासप्रतिपदि कार्यः ।
अस्यां तैलाभ्यङ्गो नित्यः । अकरणे प्रत्यवायोक्तेः ।
अस्यामेव प्रतिपदि देवीनवरात्रारम्भः ॥
iast:
caitrasya malamāsataye vatsarārambhanimittakatailābhyaṅgasaṁkalpādau nūtanavatsaranāmakīrtanādhyārambhaśca malamāsapratipadyeva kuryāt |
pratigṛhaṁ dhvajāropaṇaṁ nimbapatrāśanaṁ vatsarādiphalaśravaṇaṁ navarātrārambho navarātrotsavādinimittābhyaṅgādiśca śuddhamāsapratipadi kāryaḥ |
asyāṁ tailābhyaṅgo nityaḥ | akaraṇe pratyavāyokteḥ |
asyāmeva pratipadi devīnavarātrārambhaḥ ||
en: When Caitra is an intercalary month, the oil bath that is done for the sake of the beginning of the year, the saṅkalpa and the rest, the speaking of the name of the new saṁvatsara, and the beginning of study are to be done on the pratipad of the intercalary month itself. The raising of the flag at every house, the eating of the nīm leaf, the hearing of the phala of the new year, the beginning of the Navarātri, and the oil bath and the rest done for the sake of the Navarātri festival are to be done on the pratipad of the pure month. On this tithi the oil bath is obligatory, because harm is declared for one who does not do it. On this same pratipad the Devī Navarātri begins.
