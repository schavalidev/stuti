# `puja/vrata/` — the vrata kalpas

A vrata kalpa is not a nitya-pūjā. It is kept on a named day or on a named recurrence, it
almost always carries a story, and very often it carries a toram — a thread whose knots are
worshipped one by one and which is then bound on the wrist. That apparatus has no counterpart
in the daily worship, which is why these files sit in their own folder rather than among the
ṣoḍaśopacāra pūjās in `../smarta/`.

The split is by **genre**, not by paddhati. Every file here states its own paddhati in its
withheld `Recension / paddhati:` field, exactly as the files in `../smarta/` do. **Śākhā does
not apply to this genre**, so no file here carries a `Recension / śākhā:` field; what divides
vrata texts is paddhati and region.

Each of these rites begins by pointing the worshipper at the shared opening, which stays where
it is in `../smarta/03_purvanga_vidhanam.txt`. The Śrīvaiṣṇava form of that opening is at
`../vaishnava/01_purvanga_vidhanam.txt`, and **the two must never be merged, harmonised or
collated against each other.**

## Vrata files that are not in this folder

The folder was made on 2026-09-14, after several vrata kalpas had already been written into
`../smarta/`. **Those files belong to other sessions and were not moved, renamed or altered.**
They are:

- `../smarta/14_vinayaka_chaviti_vrata.txt` — the Varasiddhi Vināyaka vrata
- `../smarta/24_anaghashtami_vrata_kalpam.txt` — the Anaghāṣṭamī vrata
- `../smarta/26_sravana_mangalagauri_vrata_kalpam.txt` — the Śrāvaṇa Maṅgaḷagaurī vrata
- `../smarta/49_naga_panchami_puja_vidhi.txt` — the Nāga Pañcamī rite

Moving them is a change to another session's work and needs the maintainer's word first. Until
then a reader looking for a vrata must look in both folders, and this list is how they know to.

## One rite is written twice, on purpose

`03_mangala_gauri_vrata_kalpam.txt` here and `../smarta/26_sravana_mangalagauri_vrata_kalpam.txt`
are **the same rite from the same base text**, written by two hands that did not see each other's
work. They were collated against **different printed editions** — Machilipatnam 1958 for the
older file, Rajahmundry 1998 for this one — and they differ in unit count, in the story they
tell, and in whether an udyāpana is given. Both files say so in their own headers, and neither
supersedes the other. **Whether to retire one of them is the maintainer's decision and has not
been made.**
