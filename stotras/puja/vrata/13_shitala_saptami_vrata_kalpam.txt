Note on whether this rite is Vedic: **it is not.** Every service in this rite is offered either by the name-mantra `ॐ शीतलायै नमः` or by a Paurāṇika śloka. **No Vedic mantra occurs anywhere in the section**, and the passage was searched for one. A Paurāṇika twin of this file is therefore neither needed nor made; there is nothing in it that an uninitiated worshipper would have to be given in another form.

Accent: none, and none is expected, for the reason just given.

Note on the print's orthography, applied uniformly and counted once rather than reported as forty separate repairs. The Khemraj Devanāgarī of 1901 writes **ब for व** in `व्रत` and its compounds throughout (`शीतलाब्रतम्`, `ब्रतविधिः`, `पतिब्रता`), and writes **ए for ऐ** in `वैधव्य` throughout (`अवेधव्य`). Both are the printing house's own spelling conventions and not readings; both are set here in the modern spelling so that the file matches the rest of the corpus, and the print's spelling is recorded here instead of in forty footnotes.

Note on the count of services. The passage calls the worship `षोडशोपचार` by implication — it is the sixteen-service pūjā of the household rite — but the sentence actually names **twenty-two** acts, from the invitation to the bowing. They are set out below one to a line, numbered as the source orders them, and the count is twenty-two because that is what the book prints, not sixteen because that is what the genre is called.

Blurb: This is the vrata of Śītalā, kept by women on the seventh day of a dark fortnight. Śītalā is the goddess worshipped against fever and against the diseases that break out on the skin, and her name means the cool one. The fruit stated for the vrata is that the woman who keeps it shall never be widowed and shall never be parted from her husband, and after that children, grandchildren, wealth and grain. It is a rite of one day and of one afternoon. A pot without a crack is set on a seat marked with an eight-petalled lotus, a golden image of Śītalā is placed above it, and she is meditated upon as the first verse describes her — riding an ass, wearing no clothes, holding a broom and a water pot, with a winnowing fan upon her head. Every service from the invitation to the bowing is offered with the one name-mantra, salutation to Śītalā. The food offered is curd and rice with five kinds of cooked grain and ghee, and nothing in the rite is heated or new. Seven girls, called the seven gaurīs, are worshipped and fed. A brāhmaṇa is given the vāyana, which is curd, rice, fruit and a fee. The Śītalāṣṭakam, the prayer of eight to Śītalā, is read on this day; its first verse is the meditation used here. The story is told last. A king's daughter named Śubhakārī is sent to keep this vrata before she leaves for her husband's house. In the forest she cannot find the tank, and an old woman shows it to her. She keeps the vrata, and with the merit she has just earned she brings back to life first a brāhmaṇa killed by a snake, then her own husband. The old woman is Śītalā herself, and before she disappears she grants that no woman who keeps this vrata shall be widowed, made poor, or parted from her husband.

--- unit 1 | section: तिथिनिर्णयः ---
vidhi:
The book opens the section with the day and the rule for choosing it. `शुक्लादि` means the month counted from the bright fortnight, which is the reckoning of the South; on that reckoning the day falls in the dark fortnight of Śrāvaṇa. The rule is that the tithi must be the one running at midday, and the book gives its authority for it: Hārīta, quoted through the Mādhavīya.
deva:
शीतलासप्तमी ॥ अथ शुक्लादिश्रावणकृष्णसप्तम्यां शीतलाव्रतम् ॥
तच्च मध्याह्नव्यापिन्यां कार्यम् ॥
तथा च माधवीये हारीतः — पूजाव्रतेषु सर्वेषु मध्याह्नव्यापिनी तिथिः ॥ इति ॥
iast:
śītalāsaptamī || atha śuklādiśrāvaṇakṛṣṇasaptamyāṁ śītalāvratam ||
tacca madhyāhnavyāpinyāṁ kāryam ||
tathā ca mādhavīye hārītaḥ — pūjāvrateṣu sarveṣu madhyāhnavyāpinī tithiḥ || iti ||
en: Śītalā Saptamī. Now, on the seventh day of the dark fortnight of Śrāvaṇa, the month being counted from the bright fortnight, comes the Śītalā vrata. It is to be done on the day on which that tithi is running at midday. So Hārīta, quoted in the Mādhavīya: in all vratas of worship, the tithi is the one that runs at midday.
tel: శీతలా సప్తమి. శుక్ల పక్షం నుండి మాసం లెక్కించే పద్ధతిలో శ్రావణ కృష్ణ సప్తమి నాడు శీతలా వ్రతం. ఆ తిథి మధ్యాహ్నం ఉన్న రోజునే దీనిని చేయాలి. మాధవీయంలో ఉదాహరించిన హారీతుని మాట: పూజా ప్రధానమైన వ్రతాలన్నిటిలో మధ్యాహ్న వ్యాపిని తిథియే గ్రాహ్యం.
hi: शीतला सप्तमी। शुक्ल पक्ष से मास गिनने की रीति के अनुसार श्रावण कृष्ण सप्तमी को शीतला व्रत होता है। वह उसी दिन करना चाहिये जिस दिन वह तिथि मध्याह्न में व्याप्त हो। माधवीय में उद्धृत हारीत का वचन — सब पूजाप्रधान व्रतों में मध्याह्नव्यापिनी तिथि ही ग्राह्य है।

--- unit 2 | section: तिथिनिर्णयः ---
vidhi:
The day is not settled the same way everywhere, and this file records the disagreement rather than choosing. The base text has Śrāvaṇa kṛṣṇa saptamī, as unit 1 prints it. The Nirṇayasindhu, on the authority of Hemādri, puts the vrata a month later, in the dark fortnight of Bhādrapada, and adds that when the tithi is divided the earlier day is taken; its line is printed below. Gujarat keeps the same day as the base text under its Gujarati name, Śrāvaṇa vad sātam. North India keeps a Śītalā day in the dark fortnight of Caitra, called Śītalāṣṭamī or Basoḍā. **The app's calendar shows Śrāvaṇa śukla saptamī, the bright fortnight, which no printed book read here supports.** All of these are set down and none is corrected; which day a household keeps is decided by its own custom and its own almanac, not by this file.
deva:
भाद्रकृष्णसप्तम्यां शीतलाव्रतम् । तत्र पूर्वा ग्राह्येति हेमाद्रौ ॥
iast:
bhādrakṛṣṇasaptamyāṁ śītalāvratam | tatra pūrvā grāhyeti hemādrau ||
en: On the seventh day of the dark fortnight of Bhādrapada is the Śītalā vrata; and there the earlier day is to be taken — so in Hemādri.
tel: భాద్రపద కృష్ణ సప్తమి నాడు శీతలా వ్రతం; అక్కడ పూర్వ తిథినే గ్రహించాలి — అని హేమాద్రిలో ఉన్నది.
hi: भाद्रपद कृष्ण सप्तमी को शीतला व्रत होता है; वहाँ पूर्वा तिथि ग्रहण करनी चाहिये — ऐसा हेमाद्रि में कहा है।

--- unit 3 | section: स्कान्दोक्तो व्रतविधिः ---
vidhi:
The rite itself, quoted from the Skānda Purāṇa. It opens with the meditation, which is also the first verse of the Śītalāṣṭakam, and with the direction that the goddess is to be established on the pot and worshipped by her name-mantra. Everything she is described as holding is a thing used to sweep, to cool or to clean.
deva:
अथ व्रतविधिः । स्कान्दे —
वन्देऽहं शीतलां देवीं रासभस्थां दिगम्बराम् ।
मार्जनीकलशोपेतां शूर्पालंकृतमस्तकाम् ॥
कुम्भे संस्थापयेद्देवीं पूजयेन्नाममन्त्रतः ॥
iast:
atha vratavidhiḥ | skānde —
vande'haṁ śītalāṁ devīṁ rāsabhasthāṁ digambarām |
mārjanīkalaśopetāṁ śūrpālaṁkṛtamastakām ||

en: Now the rite of the vrata. In the Skānda Purāṇa: I bow to the goddess Śītalā, who is seated upon an ass and clothed in the directions alone; who holds a broom and a water pot, and whose head is adorned with a winnowing fan. One should establish the goddess upon the pot and worship her with the name-mantra.

hi: अब व्रत की विधि। स्कन्दपुराण में — गर्दभ पर बैठी हुई, दिगम्बरा शीतला देवी को मैं प्रणाम करता हूँ; उनके हाथों में मार्जनी और कलश हैं और मस्तक शूर्प से अलंकृत है। उस देवी को कुम्भ पर स्थापित करके नाममन्त्र से पूजा करे।

--- unit 4 | section: स्कान्दोक्तो व्रतविधिः ---
vidhi:
The verse said while the food is offered. The food named is curd and rice together with five kinds of cooked grain, mixed with ghee. It is the offering of a cooling rite, and nothing in it is hot.
deva:
शीतले पञ्चपक्वान्नदध्योदनयुतं शुभम् ।
नैवेद्यं गृह्यतां देवि घृतमिश्रं च सुन्दरि ॥
iast:
śītale pañcapakvānnadadhyodanayutaṁ śubham |
naivedyaṁ gṛhyatāṁ devi ghṛtamiśraṁ ca sundari ||
en: Śītalā, let this auspicious offering be taken — five kinds of cooked grain together with curd and rice, mixed with ghee, O goddess, O beautiful one.
tel: ఓ శీతలా, ఐదు రకాల పక్వాన్నాలతో, పెరుగన్నంతో కూడిన, నేయి కలిపిన ఈ శుభమైన నైవేద్యాన్ని స్వీకరించు, ఓ దేవీ, ఓ సుందరీ.
hi: हे शीतले, पाँच प्रकार के पक्वान्न तथा दही-भात से युक्त और घृत से मिला हुआ यह शुभ नैवेद्य ग्रहण कीजिये, हे देवि, हे सुन्दरि।

en: The brāhmaṇī, that devoted wife, rejoicing, bowed down to the old woman and said: Grant me this, Mother — salutation to you — that I be never a widow and never parted. And whatever other woman shall keep the vrata of Śītalā, let her too have freedom from widowhood, freedom from poverty, and no parting from her husband. So be it, said the goddess Śītalā, who takes what form she wishes, and she vanished. Having received Śītalā's boon, the king's daughter went to her own house. She who, by worshipping her who is worshipped by all the world and dwells where the lotuses are, had gained every good thing of the world, and who had received the grace of Śītalā — that daughter of the king became like Pārvatī. Thus in the Bhaviṣya Purāṇa the Śītalā vrata is complete.
