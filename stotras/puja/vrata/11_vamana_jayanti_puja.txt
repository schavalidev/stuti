Title: Śrī Vāmana Jayantī Pūjā — the worship of Vāmana on the twelfth tithi of the bright fortnight of Bhādrapada, joined with Śravaṇa
Devanāgarī: श्री वामन जयन्ती पूजा — भाद्रपद शुक्ल श्रवणयुक्त द्वादशी व्रत विधानम्
Telugu: శ్రీ వామన జయంతి పూజ — భాద్రపద శుక్ల శ్రవణ ద్వాదశి నాటి వ్రత విధానం

Author: Traditional. The rite is the Vratarāja's, compiled by Viśvanātha; the verses of the vidhāna are the Bhaviṣyottara's, spoken by Śrī Kṛṣṇa to Yudhiṣṭhira and quoted by Hemādri. No author is named for the sixteen services and none should be inferred.

Type: vidhi (ritual manual) — not a stotra. It uses the one-extra-field vidhi format of this folder: a `vidhi:` field carrying the instruction, then deva / iast / en / tel / hi.

Recension / paddhati: **Nibandha, all-India.** The base is a Sanskrit nibandha, not a regional paddhati print, and it states no region. Śākhā does not apply to this genre; what divides vrata texts is paddhati and region. The rite points at no pūrvāṅga of its own: the base prints the saṅkalpa and goes straight to the services. A worshipper who wants the shared opening has it at `../smarta/03_purvanga_vidhanam.txt`, and a Śrīvaiṣṇava worshipper at `../vaishnava/01_purvanga_vidhanam.txt`; **the two must never be merged, harmonised or collated against each other.** The month is Bhādrapada in both reckonings, so no bracketed alternative is written.

Source / recension: base text the **Vratarāja of Viśvanātha, with the Hindī ṭīkā of Mādhavācārya, Khemrāj Śrīkṛṣṇadās edition**, archive.org `vrataraj-of-vishvanath-sharma-with-bhasha-tika-by-madhavacharya-khemraj`, **printed pp. 629–632** (scan leaves n652–n655). The `iast:` was made from that Devanāgarī mechanically with `bin/dev2iast.py`; no IAST was typed by hand.

Independent collation: two witnesses, neither of them the base.

1. **The Dharmasindhu of Kāśīnātha Upādhyāya, with Marāṭhī translation**, archive.org `in.ernet.dli.2015.484720`, **printed p. 130** (scan leaf n149), section `अथ वामनजयन्ती`. Its Sanskrit mūla and the Marāṭhī rendering below it were both read off the page image. It gives the day differently: at **midday**, bathe at the confluence of rivers, worship a golden Vāmana and give arghya in a golden vessel; the image with its retinue is given to a brāhmaṇa the **next** day.
2. **Vratha Ratnākaramu, volume 2**, the Telugu print, at its వామనజయంతి / శ్రవణద్వాదశీ vratam. Read from its OCR only, which is poor.

**The Telugu print is a full rite in its own right, and it is not a Telugu rendering of the base.** It has its own saṅkalpa, a midday bath at the saṅgama with a saṅgama-prārthanā, a karaka-pūraṇa, a śikya-sthāpana with its dadhi-bhakta, and a **ṣoḍaśopacāra whose every service is carried by a ṛk of the Puruṣa Sūkta**. Its verses are those of a second Vratarāja recension — the eGangotri scan of the Vratarāja prints the same rite, verse for verse, in Sanskrit — so the Telugu print and that recension are one witness, not two. **The Vratarāja of the Khemrāj edition is kept as the base**, for three reasons, and the choice is stated rather than made silently: the base is legible and was read from its page images, the Telugu print's OCR is not and could not be lifted; the base's rite carries no Vedic mantra and is therefore usable by an uninitiated reciter as it stands, while the Telugu rite is Vedic throughout; and the base is complete from saṅkalpa to colophon. **The Telugu rite is a real gap in this folder and closing it needs the page images of that print.** Nothing of it was written into this file.

**Three prints, three different arghyas — they are not variants of one verse and were not collated into one.** The base's arghya stands in the units. What the other two prints have is recorded here:

- **Dharmasindhu**, two arghya mantras, one after the other, read off the page image:
  `नमस्ते पद्मनाभाय नमस्ते जलशायिने । तुभ्यमर्घ्यं प्रयच्छामि बालवामनरूपिणे ॥ १ ॥`
  `नमः शार्ङ्गधनुर्बाणपाणये वामनाय च । यज्ञभुक्फलदात्रे च वामनाय नमो नमः ॥ २ ॥`
  Its pūjā mantra, given before them, is `देवेश्वराय देवाय देवसंभूतिकारिणे । प्रभवे सर्वदेवानां वामनाय नमो नमः ॥`, and its dāna mantra `वामनः प्रतिगृह्णाति वामनोऽहं ददामि ते । वामनं सर्वतोभद्रं द्विजाय प्रतिपादये ॥`.
- **Vratha Ratnākaramu 2 (and the second Vratarāja recension)**: the arghya is the ṛk `त्रिपादूर्ध्व...` of the Puruṣa Sūkta followed by the verse `गन्धपुष्पफलैर्युक्तं हेमपात्रे निवेदितं । स्वर्गङ्गासलिलं पुण्यमर्घ्यं स्वीकुरु केशव ॥`. The earlier note in `../../VRATA_DAY_OBSERVANCES.md` that this print's arghya is "त्रिपादूर्ध्व" named the Vedic pratīka only; the verse that follows it is the one given here.

Two further places where the witnesses differ, recorded and not reconciled. The base's dāna mantra reads `वामनो वै ददाति च` where the Dharmasindhu reads `वामनोऽहं ददामि ते` — the base has Vāmana giving, the Dharmasindhu has the giver giving; the second half-verses differ likewise. And the base's vastra verse reads `महाहवरिपुस्कन्धधृतचक्राय` where the Telugu print reads `ఘృష్టచక్రాయ`, a held disc against a whetted one.

Note on defects repaired in the base, each counted. **Two, and they are the same word twice.** The Khemrāj compositor sets the ligature `गृह्ण` for `गृहाण` in the pādya verse and again in the puṣpa verse; the metre of both lines wants four syllables and the same page spells the word out in full as `गृहाण` in the arghya verse three lines below. Both were repaired to `गृहाण`. **No other character of the base was altered.** Three suspected defects were **not** repaired, for want of a witness: `जलजास्याय शंसिने` in the dhyāna verse, where the snāna verse on the same page reads `जलजास्याय शङ्खिने` and `शंसिने` is probably a misprint for it, but `शंसिने` is a word and the metre does not decide; `वामनस्तारकोभाभ्यां` in the dāna verse, which reads as corrupt but which the second Vratarāja recension prints identically; and `मार्गेण चर्मणा नद्धं` in verse 32. Nothing was reconstructed and nothing is marked `[?]`: every line of the base was legible on the page image.

Accent: **none, and none is expected.** Every mantra of this rite is a Paurāṇika verse. **There is no Vedic mantra anywhere in this file, so no Paurāṇika twin in `../pauranika/vrata/` was made and none is needed.** The base does prescribe a homa with the Vedic `इदं विष्णुर्विचक्रमे`, but that homa belongs to the udyāpana kept once in the twelfth year, not to the annual worship; it is named in the vidhi of the closing units by its pratīka, as the base names it, and the mantra itself is not printed here. A reciter who is not initiated performs the whole of this rite as it stands and does not perform that homa.

Unit count: 26 units. The rite falls into the printed vidhāna of the vrata, the resolve, the sixteen services with the worship of the limbs, the prayer, the giving of the śikya, and the homa and gifts of the twelfth year.

Blurb: This is the worship of Vāmana on his birth tithi, the twelfth of the bright fortnight of Bhādrapada joined with the Śravaṇa asterism. The day is kept with a fast, and the worship is done at night. A vessel is made of gold, silver, wood or bamboo, and in it is set a golden Vāmana made according to one's means, with the tuft, the water-pot, the umbrella and the sacred thread. The vessel is covered with new cloth, fruit is set on it, and it is filled with an āḍhaka of sesame, or a prastha, or a kuḍava; failing sesame, with barley, wheat or white sesame. The Lord is then worshipped with the sixteen services, each with its own verse, and his limbs are worshipped from the feet to the head by the ten descents — Matsya at the feet, Kūrma at the knees, Varāha at the private parts, Nṛsiṁha at the navel, Vāmana at the breast, Rāma at the arms, Paraśurāma at the ears, Kṛṣṇa at the face, Buddha at the eyes and Kalki at the head. The prayer that closes the worship bows to him who lies on the water, to the dwarf form, to Trivikrama and to the binder of Bali. The gift proper to the day is the śikya, the swing-basket: curd-rice, a water-pot, an umbrella and sandals are put in it and given to a brāhmaṇa. The vrata is kept for twelve years, and in the twelfth year it is concluded with a homa, with the gift of the image and a cow to the ācārya, and with the feeding of brāhmaṇas. The fruit stated for it is the wiping out of faults done in this birth and in other births, and the increase of sons and grandsons.

--- unit 1 | section: व्रतविधानम् ---
vidhi:
The day and its rule, from the Bhaviṣyottara kathā that the base prints before the worship. The tithi is the twelfth of the bright fortnight of Bhādrapada joined with Śravaṇa. The base's own footnote on this page argues from the Bhāgavata, against the Nirṇayasindhu, that the twelfth and not the eleventh is the primary day; the verses themselves are spoken of the eleventh, because the kathā tells of Vijayā ekādaśī, and the vrata is taken up on the tithi that carries Śravaṇa.
The fast is kept, and the worship is done at night.
deva:
एकादश्यां सोपवासो रात्रौ संपूजयेद्धरिम् ॥
कुर्यात्पात्रं तु सौवर्णं रौप्यं वा दारुवंशजम् ॥ ३० ॥
iast:
ekādaśyāṁ sopavāso rātrau saṁpūjayeddharim ||
kuryātpātraṁ tu sauvarṇaṁ raupyaṁ vā dāruvaṁśajam || 30 ||
en: Fasting on the eleventh, let him worship Hari well at night. Let him make a vessel of gold, or of silver, or of wood or bamboo.
tel: ఏకాదశి నాడు ఉపవాసం ఉండి, రాత్రి హరిని చక్కగా పూజించాలి. బంగారంతో గాని, వెండితో గాని, కర్రతో గాని, వెదురుతో గాని ఒక పాత్రను చేయాలి.

The mantra it uses is the Vedic `इदं विष्णुर्विचक्रमे`, which the base names by its opening words and does not print. **This homa is the one Vedic limb of the whole vrata, and it belongs to the concluding rite of the twelfth year, not to the annual worship.** It is done by an initiated worshipper with the ācārya; a reciter who is not initiated performs everything else in this file as it stands and leaves this to him.
deva:
ततः प्रभातसमये स्नात्वाचार्यादिभिः सह ॥
वामनं पूजयेत्प्राग्वद्धोमं कुर्याद्विधानतः ॥ ४२ ॥
मन्त्रेणेदं विष्णुरिति समिदाज्यतिलौदनैः ॥
प्रतिद्रव्यं सहस्रं वा शतमष्टोत्तरं हुनेत् ॥ ४३ ॥
iast:
tataḥ prabhātasamaye snātvācāryādibhiḥ saha ||
vāmanaṁ pūjayetprāgvaddhomaṁ kuryādvidhānataḥ || 42 ||
mantreṇedaṁ viṣṇuriti samidājyatilaudanaiḥ ||
pratidravyaṁ sahasraṁ vā śatamaṣṭottaraṁ hunet || 43 ||
en: Then, at daybreak, having bathed along with the ācārya and the rest, let him worship Vāmana as before and perform the homa as it is laid down — with the mantra beginning "Viṣṇu strode over this", with kindling sticks, clarified butter, sesame and rice, offering a thousand oblations, or a hundred and eight, of each substance.
tel: తరువాత ప్రాతఃకాలంలో ఆచార్యుని మొదలైనవారితో కలిసి స్నానం చేసి, వామనుని ముందటి వలె పూజించి, విధి ప్రకారం హోమం చేయాలి. "ఇదం విష్ణుర్విచక్రమే" అనే మంత్రంతో, సమిధలతో, ఆజ్యంతో, నువ్వులతో, అన్నంతో — ఒక్కొక్క ద్రవ్యంతో వేయి ఆహుతులు గాని, నూట ఎనిమిది ఆహుతులు గాని ఇవ్వాలి.
hi: तदनन्तर प्रातःकाल में आचार्य आदि के साथ स्नान करके, वामन की पूर्ववत् पूजा करे और विधि के अनुसार होम करे। "इदं विष्णुर्विचक्रमे" इस मन्त्र से समिधा, घी, तिल और अन्न के द्वारा — प्रत्येक द्रव्य से सहस्र अथवा एक सौ आठ आहुतियाँ दे।

--- unit 25 | section: होमदानविधिः ---
vidhi:
The gifts. The image and a cow are given to the ācārya, and black cows are given — twelve, or eight, or six, or four, or at the least one that gives milk.
deva:
ब्राह्मणान् भोजयित्वा तु द्वादशाष्टव्रती नृप ॥
प्रतिमां च तथा धेनुमाचार्याय निवेदयेत् ॥ ४४ ॥
एवं कृते तु राजेन्द्र गाः कृष्णा द्वादशाष्ट वा ॥
षट् चतस्रोऽथवा देया एकावापि पयस्विनी ॥ ४५ ॥
iast:
brāhmaṇān bhojayitvā tu dvādaśāṣṭavratī nṛpa ||
pratimāṁ ca tathā dhenumācāryāya nivedayet || 44 ||
evaṁ kṛte tu rājendra gāḥ kṛṣṇā dvādaśāṣṭa vā ||
ṣaṭ catasro'thavā deyā ekāvāpi payasvinī || 45 ||
en: Having fed brāhmaṇas, O king, the keeper of the vrata should offer the image and a cow to the ācārya. When this has been done, best of kings, black cows are to be given — twelve, or eight, or six, or four, or even one that gives milk.
tel: రాజా, బ్రాహ్మణులకు భోజనం పెట్టి, వ్రతం చేసినవాడు ప్రతిమను, ఆవును ఆచార్యునికి సమర్పించాలి. అలా చేసిన తరువాత, రాజేంద్రా, నల్ల ఆవులను ఇవ్వాలి — పన్నెండు గాని, ఎనిమిది గాని, ఆరు గాని, నాలుగు గాని, కనీసం పాలిచ్చే ఒక్క ఆవునైనా ఇవ్వాలి.
hi: हे राजन्, ब्राह्मणों को भोजन कराकर व्रती प्रतिमा और धेनु आचार्य को समर्पित करे। ऐसा करने पर, हे राजेन्द्र, काली गायें दी जायँ — बारह, अथवा आठ, अथवा छह, अथवा चार, अथवा एक ही दूध देने वाली।

--- unit 26 | section: होमदानविधिः ---
vidhi:
The mantra of the gift, and the worship of the brāhmaṇas that closes the rite. The Dharmasindhu gives a differently worded form of this same mantra; the difference is recorded in this file's source note and is not written in here.
deva:
वामनः प्रतिगृह्णाति वामनो वै ददाति च ॥
वामनस्तारकोभाभ्यां वामनाय नमो नमः ॥ ४६ ॥
प्रत्येकं ब्राह्मणान्कुम्भैर्दक्षिणावस्त्रचन्दनैः ॥
शक्त्या सम्पूजयेद्राजन्सर्वत्रैष विधिः स्मृतः ॥ ४७ ॥
iast:
vāmanaḥ pratigṛhṇāti vāmano vai dadāti ca ||
vāmanastārakobhābhyāṁ vāmanāya namo namaḥ || 46 ||
pratyekaṁ brāhmaṇānkumbhairdakṣiṇāvastracandanaiḥ ||
śaktyā sampūjayedrājansarvatraiṣa vidhiḥ smṛtaḥ || 47 ||
en: Vāmana receives, and it is Vāmana who gives; Vāmana is the deliverer of both. Salutation, salutation to Vāmana.
Let him worship the brāhmaṇas one by one, according to his means, with pots, with gifts, with garments and with sandal, O king; this rule is held to be the same everywhere.
tel: వామనుడే స్వీకరిస్తాడు, వామనుడే ఇస్తాడు. వామనుడే ఇరువురికి తారకుడు. వామనునికి నమస్కారం, నమస్కారం.
రాజా, బ్రాహ్మణులను ఒక్కొక్కరిని శక్తి కొలది కుంభాలతో, దక్షిణతో, వస్త్రాలతో, చందనంతో పూజించాలి. ఈ విధి అంతటా ఒకటే అని చెప్పబడింది.
hi: वामन ही ग्रहण करते हैं और वामन ही देते हैं; वामन ही दोनों के तारक हैं। वामन को नमस्कार, नमस्कार।
हे राजन्, ब्राह्मणों को एक-एक करके अपनी शक्ति के अनुसार कुम्भों से, दक्षिणा से, वस्त्रों से और चन्दन से पूजे; यह विधि सर्वत्र यही कही गयी है।
