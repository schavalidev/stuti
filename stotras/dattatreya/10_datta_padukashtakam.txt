Title: Śrī Datta Pādukāṣṭakam (Nṛsiṁhavāḍī Kṣetre)
Devanāgarī: श्री दत्त पादुकाष्टकम् (नृसिंहवाडी क्षेत्रे)
Telugu: శ్రీ దత్త పాదుకాష్టకం (నృసింహవాడీ క్షేత్రే)

Author: Śrī Vāsudevānanda Sarasvatī (per the text's own colophon)

Blurb: This hymn was composed by Vāsudevānanda Sarasvatī in honour of the sacred pādukās of Dattātreya, that is, the sandals bearing his footprints, enshrined at Nṛsiṁhavāḍī, where the rivers Kṛṣṇā and Pañcagaṅgā meet. It moves through several metres. It begins with the pādukās themselves, which Indra and the ancient gods have sung. It then describes the temple's three doorways and its many lamps. It praises the sacred Kṛṣṇā, which it calls the eightfold tīrtha. It recalls the lineage that was born at Karañjapura. It closes with a wish: that whoever comes to Nṛsiṁhavāḍī in fear of worldly existence may never wish to leave it, but may attain the most blessed state, honoured throughout the world.

en: Present at the confluence of the Kṛṣṇā and the Pañcagaṅgā, Śrīpāda, Śrīvallabha, dwelling in the hearts of devotees — I bow to that Dattātreya in the very form of his sandals, who proclaims splendid wisdom.
tel: కృష్ణా-పంచగంగా సంగమస్థానమునందు నెలకొన్నవాడు, శ్రీపాదుడు, శ్రీవల్లభుడు, భక్తుల హృదయములలో నివసించువాడు — పాదుకారూపమున ఉన్న ఆ దత్తాత్రేయుని, శోభాయమానమైన విద్యను ప్రసాదించువానిని వందనము చేయుచున్నాను.
hi: कृष्णा-पंचगंगा के संगम-स्थान पर विराजमान, श्रीपाद, श्रीवल्लभ, भक्तों के हृदय में निवास करने वाले — पादुका-रूप में उस दत्तात्रेय को, जो शोभायमान विद्या प्रदान करते हैं, मैं वंदन करता हूँ।
