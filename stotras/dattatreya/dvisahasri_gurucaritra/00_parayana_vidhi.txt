Blurb: This is the order of the seven-day recitation, and it is the book's own. A reader who intends a saptāha of the Śrī Gurucaritam follows it rather than the schedule used for the Marathi Guru Caritra, which is a longer book differently divided. The division is given by end-point and not in equal portions: the reader stops each day at the close of a named adhyāya, and the portions are of quite unequal length because the stopping places follow the sense of the story. On the first day the saṅkalpa is made and the book itself is worshipped, and the reading then begins. Every day opens the same way, with the worship of the book and then the Śrīgurustuti, and only after that are the day's adhyāyas read. Every day closes the same way, with the concluding worship and then the stotra of apology for faults, which begins rasajñā vaśā. On the seventh day the reading ends with the twenty-third adhyāya and is followed by the two rahasyas, the Yogarahasya and the Bodharahasya, which stand after the adhyāyas in the book. Throughout the seven days the ordinary rules of a pārāyaṇa are kept — purity, and restraint in food and in conduct.

en: On the first day one should read to the end of the fourth adhyāya, on the second to the end of the ninth, on the third to the end of the fourteenth, on the fifth to the end of the nineteenth, on the sixth to the end of the twenty-first, and on the seventh to the end of the twenty-third; and having read so far, one should then read the two rahasyas. — This is the direction word for word as the volume prints it. It names six days and not seven: the fourth day is absent from it. The editor's Marathi explanation beneath the line supplies the fourth day, and places it at the end of the seventeenth adhyāya. The next unit sets out all seven.
