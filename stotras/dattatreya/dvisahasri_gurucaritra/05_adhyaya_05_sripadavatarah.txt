Blurb: Nāmadhāraka asks Siddha to tell the stories of the avatāras, and Siddha praises the question and begins. He says that the Lord takes birth in age after age to protect dharma, to save the good and to destroy the wicked, and that He comes for no other reason; seeing men weak and poor in understanding, He appears among them out of love, to lift them up. In Pīṭhāpura in the eastern country there lived a brāhmaṇa named Rājā and his wife Sumatī, and both of them worshipped Śrī Datta every day. One day, on a śrāddha day, before the brāhmaṇas had eaten, a beggar came to their house and asked for food. Sumatī gave it to him. Datta was pleased with her mind, and showed her His own form, bearing the garland, the water pot, the ḍamaru, the trident, the conch and the discus, clothed in a tiger skin, three faced, with matted hair and smeared with ash. He said that she had given the food in the belief that He receives what is offered to the gods and the fathers, and asked her to name a boon. She praised Him and asked that He Himself be born as her son. He granted it, said that a son like Himself would be born to her, and disappeared. She told her husband everything, and both of them rejoiced. She feared she had done wrong in giving the food before the rite, but her husband said that she had done well, that their family was purified, and that a boon hard to obtain had been won. He completed the worldly śrāddha with the food that remained. In time she conceived and gave birth to a son. Those who read the signs said that this was Dattātreya Himself, born there for the sake of devotion. Because his feet bore auspicious marks he was called Śrīpāda, and he grew as the moon grows with the rays of the sun. He was invested with the sacred thread and himself taught the three Vedas. When his father made ready to have him married, he refused, saying that as one who renounces he would wed only the fortune of yoga, and that it was already his, for he is Śrīvallabha. Seeing him about to leave, his parents wept and asked how they would live without him. He placed his hand upon the heads of his two brothers, and at once they became whole in body and learned. His mother, seeing that form she had never seen before, remembered what Datta had said, and spoke with a faltering voice: she asked that His māyā not cover her, and said she could not think of Him as her son when the worlds lie in His womb. He told her to keep that form always in her heart, and said she would cross the ocean of māyā quickly and come to union with Him. He said her two sons would live a hundred years, would serve their parents, and would be rich in learning, wealth, sons and grandsons. Though praised, he told his brothers that he was going forth now, and that their parents must be served, for to a householder good sons are his deity. He went round his parents three times, bowed to them, and with their leave went to Kāśī and to the forest of Badarī, and then, wishing to raise up the good, to Gokarṇa, where Gaṇeśa had established the Śiva liṅga.

पङ्ग्वन्धार्भेक्षणान्नित्यं भवपाशविमोचनी । स्मृतिः प्रवयसोर्नौ ते हरे दूरतरा भवेत्

en: Our other two children are lame and blind, and we must look at them daily. In our old age, Hari, the remembrance of you, which frees from the bonds of worldly life, will become very far from us. — They name their real difficulty. Two other sons remain, but they are helpless, and caring for them will take up the parents' whole attention. The loss they fear is not comfort but the memory of the Lord, and they say so.

--- verse 27 ---
deva:
इति वाक्यं सकारुण्यं श्रुत्वारं भ्रातृमस्तके । सुखाकरं निजकरं तद्वात्सल्याद्दधौ हरिः

en: Hearing this compassionate speech, Hari at once placed his own hand, which is the source of happiness, upon the heads of his brothers, out of affection for them. — The answer is not in words but in an act. He does not touch the parents, who asked, but the two brothers, who are the reason for their asking. The result of that touch is told in the next verse.
tel: ఈ కరుణాభరితమైన మాటను విని, హరి వాత్సల్యముతో సుఖమునకు నెలవైన తన హస్తమును వెంటనే సోదరుల శిరస్సులపై ఉంచెను. — సమాధానము మాటలలో కాక చేతలో ఉన్నది. అడిగిన తల్లిదండ్రులను కాక, వారి అడుగుటకు కారణమైన ఇద్దరు సోదరులను అతడు తాకుచున్నాడు. ఆ స్పర్శ ఫలితము తరువాతి శ్లోకమున చెప్పబడినది.

--- verse 28 ---
deva:

en: At that very moment the two became sound in body and knowing in mind. There is nothing to wonder at in one who moves in play and is the Lord. — The cure is immediate and complete: the lame walks, the blind sees, and both become wise. The closing words say that no marvel should be made of it, since this is only play to him. In the fourth line the print gives līlāvihāriṇīdanno; the last syllables are clear but the word division is not, and it is left as printed.
tel: అదే క్షణమున ఆ ఇరువురు చక్కని అవయవములు కలవారును, జ్ఞానులును అయిరి. లీలగా సంచరించువాడును ఈశ్వరుడును అయినవానియందు ఆశ్చర్యపడవలసినది ఏదియు లేదు. — ఆ స్వస్థత తక్షణము, పూర్తి; కుంటివాడు నడిచెను, గ్రుడ్డివాడు చూచెను, ఇరువురు జ్ఞానులైరి. ఇది ఆయనకు లీల మాత్రమే కావున ఆశ్చర్యపడనక్కరలేదని చివరి మాట చెప్పుచున్నది. నాల్గవ పాదమున లీలావిహారిణీదన్నో అని ముద్రితమై ఉన్నది; చివరి అక్షరములు స్పష్టముగా ఉన్నను పదచ్ఛేదము స్పష్టము కాదు, ముద్రితరూపమే ఉంచబడినది.

ado rūpaṁ tvayā dṛṣṭaṁ nityaṁ hṛdyavadhāraya | māyāsindhuṁ drutaṁ tīrtvā matsāyujyaṁ gamiṣyasi
en: The form you have seen, hold it always firmly in your heart. You will quickly cross the ocean of māyā and come to union with me. — The son answers the prayer she has just made. He does not remove māyā by argument but gives her a fixed object for the mind. Sāyujya is the union with the Lord in which there is no further return.
tel: నీవు చూచిన ఆ రూపమును ఎల్లప్పుడు హృదయమున దృఢముగా నిలుపుకొనుము. మాయాసముద్రమును త్వరగా దాటి నా సాయుజ్యమును పొందుదువు. — ఇప్పుడే ఆమె చేసిన ప్రార్థనకు కుమారుడు సమాధానమిచ్చుచున్నాడు. వాదముతో మాయను తొలగింపక, మనస్సునకు ఒక స్థిరమైన ఆధారమును ఆయన ఇచ్చుచున్నాడు. సాయుజ్యము అనగా భగవంతునితో కలియుట; దాని నుండి మరల తిరుగుట లేదు.
hi: जो रूप आपने देखा है, उसे सदा हृदय में दृढ़ता से धारण कीजिए। आप शीघ्र ही माया-सागर को पार कर मेरे सायुज्य को प्राप्त होंगी। — अभी की गई प्रार्थना का उत्तर पुत्र देता है। वह तर्क से माया नहीं हटाता, मन को एक स्थिर आधार देता है। सायुज्य अर्थात् भगवान् से एक होना, जहाँ से फिर लौटना नहीं।

--- verse 33 ---
deva:
शतायुषौ सुतौ तेऽपि पितृशुश्रूषणोत्सुकौ । विद्याश्रीपुत्रपौत्राढ्यौ मातर्मक्षु भविष्यतः
iast:
śatāyuṣau sutau te'pi pitṛśuśrūṣaṇotsukau | vidyāśrīputrapautrāḍhyau mātarmakṣu bhaviṣyataḥ
en: Your other two sons will live a hundred years and will be eager to serve their parents. Mother, they will soon be rich in learning, in wealth, and in sons and grandsons. — The practical fear of the parents is answered plainly. What is promised first is not their prosperity but their willingness to serve. The word mother here is spoken by the son who is about to leave.
tel: నీ మిగిలిన ఇద్దరు కుమారులు నూరేండ్లు జీవింతురు; తల్లిదండ్రుల సేవయందు ఆసక్తి కలవారగుదురు. అమ్మా, వారు త్వరలోనే విద్య, సంపద, పుత్రపౌత్రములతో కూడినవారగుదురు. — తల్లిదండ్రుల ఆచరణాత్మక భయమునకు స్పష్టమైన సమాధానము. మొదట వాగ్దానము చేయబడినది వారి సంపద కాదు, వారి సేవాసక్తి. ఇచటి అమ్మా అను పిలుపు, వెళ్లబోవుచున్న కుమారునిదే.
hi: आपके शेष दो पुत्र सौ वर्ष जीवित रहेंगे और माता-पिता की सेवा में तत्पर होंगे। माता, वे शीघ्र ही विद्या, सम्पत्ति तथा पुत्र-पौत्र से युक्त होंगे। — माता-पिता के व्यावहारिक भय का सीधा उत्तर है। पहले जिसका वचन दिया गया वह उनकी समृद्धि नहीं, सेवा की तत्परता है। यहाँ माता का सम्बोधन उसी पुत्र का है जो जाने को है।

--- verse 34 ---
deva:
स्तुतोऽपि भ्रातरौ प्राह प्रव्रजाम्यधुना सदा । संसेव्यौ पितरौ तौ हि गृहिसत्पुत्रदैवतम्
iast:
stuto'pi bhrātarau prāha pravrajāmyadhunā sadā | saṁsevyau pitarau tau hi gṛhisatputradaivatam
en: Though praised by them, he said to his brothers, I am going forth now. Both the parents are always to be served, for to a householder good sons are his deity. — He turns the duty over to the brothers he has just made whole. The rule he gives them is stated in general terms and holds for every householder. He does not stay, but he makes sure the parents are not left unserved.
tel: వారు స్తుతించినను, అతడు సోదరులతో ఇట్లనెను: నేను ఇప్పుడు వెళ్లుచున్నాను. తల్లిదండ్రులిరువురను ఎల్లప్పుడు సేవింపవలెను; గృహస్థునకు సత్పుత్రులే దైవము. — ఇప్పుడే తాను స్వస్థపరచిన సోదరులకే అతడు ఆ బాధ్యతను అప్పగించుచున్నాడు. అతడు చెప్పు నియమము సామాన్యముగా చెప్పబడినది; ప్రతి గృహస్థునకును వర్తించును. అతడు ఉండలేదు, కాని తల్లిదండ్రులు సేవలేక ఉండకుండునట్లు చూచుచున్నాడు.

--- verse 35 ---
deva:
इत्युक्त्वा त्रिः परिक्रम्य प्रणम्य पितरौ स तु । जगाम तदनुज्ञातः काशीं च बदरीवनम्
iast:
ityuktvā triḥ parikramya praṇamya pitarau sa tu | jagāma tadanujñātaḥ kāśīṁ ca badarīvanam
en: Saying this, he went round his parents three times and bowed to them, and with their permission he went to Kāśī and to the forest of Badarī. — He leaves in the proper way, with circumambulation, prostration and leave taken. The permission matters, because Datta had told the mother not to disregard the son's word. Kāśī and Badarī are the two places to which a renunciate first goes.
tel: ఇట్లు చెప్పి అతడు తల్లిదండ్రులకు మూడుమారులు ప్రదక్షిణము చేసి నమస్కరించి, వారి అనుజ్ఞతో కాశికిని బదరీవనమునకును వెళ్లెను. — అతడు తగిన విధముగానే బయలుదేరుచున్నాడు; ప్రదక్షిణము, నమస్కారము, అనుజ్ఞ. ఆ అనుజ్ఞ ముఖ్యమైనది; కుమారుని మాటను తిరస్కరింపవద్దని దత్తుడు తల్లికి చెప్పియుండెను. కాశి, బదరి అనునవి సన్యాసి మొదట చేరు రెండు స్థలములు.

--- verse 36 ---
