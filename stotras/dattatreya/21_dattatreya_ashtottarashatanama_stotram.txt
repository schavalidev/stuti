Title: Śrī Dattātreya Aṣṭottaraśatanāma Stotram
Devanāgarī: श्रीदत्तात्रेय-अष्टोत्तरशतनामस्तोत्रम्
Telugu: శ్రీ దత్తాత్రేయ అష్టోత్తరశతనామ స్తోత్రం

Author: **Śrī Vāsudevānanda Sarasvatī** — named in the colophon of both stotra and nāmāvalī (`इति श्रीवासुदेवानन्दसरस्वतीविरचितं`). This is the Ṭembe Svāmī of the Dattātreya tradition (d. 1914), and the first entry of this workstream with a **named, historically recent author** rather than a Purāṇa or Tantra attribution.

Source / recension: Primary text: **Gītā Press, Gorakhpur — Śatanāma Stotra Saṅgraha**, code 1850, pp. 57–61 (stotra pp. 57–59, nāmāvalī pp. 60–61), transcribed from the page images of the printed edition rather than from OCR. Entry 9 of the volume's forty. Stotra and nāmāvalī are paired in one file as the publisher prints them, and were checked against each other name by name: **the nāmāvalī is what settles the name divisions**, which matters more here than in any earlier entry, because the stotra's compounds run together across name boundaries and several would not be divisible from the verse alone. The two agree in order and content at all 108 positions. Several readings were re-rendered at 400–900 dpi; three remain unresolved and are listed in the recension note. `dattatreya/` was checked and holds 20 files, none of them an aṣṭottaraśatanāma. **No second edition was collated**, and no web witness has been sought — which for this text is a real limitation rather than a formality, given the unresolved glyphs.

Recension note: **Prefix.** Gītā Press prints `ॐ` before each name; this corpus prints `श्री` for every nāmāvalī without exception (user, 2026-09-08). **The count is exactly 108** — the first four entries of the volume, plus this one, hit the canonical number, against 109 at entry 6 and 114 at entry 8. **107 distinct names in 108 slots:** `यतये` stands at both no. 93 and no. 107, and the repetition is in the stotra too (vv. 21 and 23), so it is the author's and is kept. **The stotra is already in the dative** — like entry 6 (Veṅkaṭeśa) and unlike the rest — so the nāmāvalī is close to a straight re-setting of it, and `नमः` appears inside the verses. **THIS IS A MANTRA-GARBHA COMPOSITION, AND THAT IS WHY THE COMPOUNDS ARE ARTIFICIAL.** The closing verse says so outright: `मस्करीशोमनुस्यूतः` — "strung through with the *manu* (mantra) of the lord of ascetics". The names are therefore shaped to carry syllables, not only to mean; many are compounds no ordinary stotra would form, and a few (nos. 78 `मश्रिये`, 91 `मस्थाय`, 92 `मसुबन्धवे`) are barely more than syllable-carriers with a thin sense fitted over them. **No attempt has been made here to extract the embedded mantra** — that is a separate piece of work and would need a commentary. The English column renders each compound literally and does not smooth over what is opaque. **THREE READINGS ARE UNRESOLVED AND ARE RECORDED AS PRINTED, NOT EMENDED.** (a) No. 4 / v. 1, printed `ऐन्द्र्यद्ध्र्या ओजसे` as two words. The natural sense wants `ऐन्द्र्या ऋद्ध्या` (→ `ऐन्द्र्यर्ध्या`), "by Indra's ṛddhi"; the page at 700 dpi shows a `द्ध्र्` cluster, not a repha. Transcribed as printed. (b) No. 22 / v. 6, printed `वदद्ब्रेण्य-`. Sense wants `वदद्वरेण्य-`, "excellent in speaking"; at 900 dpi the conjunct is unambiguously `द्ब्र`, and the stotra and the nāmāvalī print the same shape on different pages, so it is not a single slipped sort. Transcribed as printed. (c) No. 26 / v. 7, printed `अद्व्वरसद्म-`; the expected form is `अध्वरसद्म-`, "the house of sacrifice", and the gloss follows that sense while the transcription follows the page. **All three are cases where one witness cannot settle the question** — a second printed edition would, and none was available. Stated as hypotheses about the readings, not as findings against the edition. **Two names are printed as two words** and are kept that way: no. 62 `धीमतां धीकराय` and no. 73 `यतीन्द्रचर्यां दधते`. **No. 34 `भूत्यै` is feminine** (`भूति`, majesty) — the only feminine dative in the list, and it corresponds to `भूत्या` in v. 9, an instrumental. Both are kept as printed. **Scope note.** The author died in 1914 and the text is long out of copyright; it is included on the same footing as the anonymous Purāṇic entries, and the exclusion this workstream applies to living authors' compositions on sanskritdocuments.org does not touch it.

Blurb: This is a set of one hundred and eight names, composed to carry a mantra as much as to carry a meaning. The closing verse says as much, calling the hymn one that is strung through with the *manu* of the lord of ascetics. The result is unlike the other hymns in this folder. It forms compounds that an ordinary stotra would not form. It names seed-syllables directly as names, among them hrīṁ, klīṁ and drāṁ. Beside these stands the plain figure of the ascetic smeared with ash and with shaven head, the son of Atri and Anasūyā, who put an end to Alarka's grief and who walks without feet.

deva:
दिगम्बरं भस्मविलेपिताङ्गं बोधात्मकं मुक्तिकरं प्रसन्नम् ।
निर्मानसं श्यामतनुं भजेऽहं दत्तात्रेयं ब्रह्मसमाधियुक्तम् ॥
iast:
digambaraṁ bhasmavilepitāṅgaṁ bodhātmakaṁ muktikaraṁ prasannam |
nirmānasaṁ śyāmatanuṁ bhaje'haṁ dattātreyaṁ brahmasamādhiyuktam ||
en: I worship Dattātreya, clad in the directions, his limbs smeared with ash, knowledge itself, the giver of release, serene; free of mind's movement, dark of body, established in the samādhi of brahman.
tel: దిగంబరుడు, భస్మలేపిత శరీరుడు, బోధస్వరూపుడు, ముక్తిప్రదుడు, ప్రసన్నుడు, మనోవృత్తులు లేనివాడు, శ్యామవర్ణ శరీరుడు, బ్రహ్మసమాధియుక్తుడు అగు దత్తాత్రేయుని నేను భజించుచున్నాను.
hi: जो दिगम्बर वेषमें रहनेवाले हैं, अपने समस्त अङ्गोंमें भस्म लगाये हुए हैं, ज्ञानस्वरूप हैं, मुक्ति प्रदान करनेवाले हैं, प्रसन्न रहनेवाले हैं तथा जिनका शरीर कृष्णवर्णका है और जिनका मन वृत्तियोंसे रहित है—ऐसे ब्रह्मसमाधिमें प्रतिष्ठित रहनेवाले भगवान् दत्तात्रेयका मैं ध्यान करता हूँ।
