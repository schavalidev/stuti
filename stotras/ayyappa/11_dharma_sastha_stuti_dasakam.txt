Blurb: This hymn has ten verses in the ornate Vasantatilakā metre. It dwells at length, in rich compound words, on the beauty of Dharmaśāstā. It names his hair crowned with peacock feathers, his jewelled earrings, his bow and arrow, his sandal paste and his garlands. Throughout it calls him the son of both Hari, who is Viṣṇu, and Īśa, who is Śiva. It calls him also the boat that carries devotees across the ocean of worldly existence. It closes with a personal prayer for unwavering devotion at his feet.

en:
I ever praise him, for the destruction of every calamity — born of Īśāna and Keśava, the one lord of all the worlds; who grants fruit according to the hopes of those who take to his lotus feet, a full moon risen over the boundless ocean of compassion.

ఓ భూతనాథా, భగవన్, నీ సుందర పాదాంబుజములయందు నా భక్తి అచంచలముగా ఉండుగాక. సర్వజగన్నాథుడవు, భజించువారికి భవాబ్ధిపోతమువు, సమస్తసృష్టికి మూలమువు అయిన నీకు నిత్యము నమస్కారము.

हे भूतनाथ भगवन्, आपके सुंदर चरणकमलों में मेरी भक्ति अचंचल बनी रहे। समस्त जगत् के नाथ, भजने वालों के लिए भवसागर की नौका-स्वरूप, समस्त सृष्टि के मूल आपको नित्य नमस्कार।
