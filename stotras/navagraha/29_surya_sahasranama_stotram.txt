Title: Śrī Sūrya Sahasranāma Stotram (Bhaviṣya Purāṇa)
Devanāgarī: श्रीसूर्यसहस्रनामस्तोत्रम्
Telugu: శ్రీసూర్యసహస్రనామస్తోత్రం

Author: attributed in the viniyoga to Bhagavān Vedavyāsa as ṛṣi, with anuṣṭubh metre and Savitṛ as deity. The frame is the Bhaviṣya Purāṇa's familiar pair: King Śatānīka asks to hear the thousand names of Savitṛ, and the sage Sumantu — who says the Sun appeared to him in person when he praised him with these names — replies. The colophon places it in the Bhaviṣya Purāṇa's "seventh kalpa": `॥ इति श्रीभविष्यपुराणे सप्तमकल्पे श्रीभगवत्सूर्यस्य सहस्रनामस्तोत्रं सम्पूर्णम् ॥`.

Source / recension: **Gītā Press, Gorakhpur, "Sahasranāma Stotra Saṅgraha" (code 1594), text 5** — stotram printed pp. 143–155, nāmāvalī pp. 156–173 — transcribed in full from the page images. **No independent edition could be collated, and this is stated plainly rather than implied away.** The only digital text of this recension, sanskritdocuments.org `suuryasahasraBhaviShya.itx`, is the right text — it carries the same Śatānīka–Sumantu frame, where that collection's two other Sūrya sahasranāmas are Rudrayāmala and Skanda texts — but its own footer records that it was "originally encoded from Śāktapramoda" and then "**revised by PSA Easwaran from Gita press to avoid errors**". It has been corrected *toward* the edition transcribed here, so its agreement proves nothing about Gītā Press, and it has not been counted as a witness. It was consulted only to disambiguate glyphs already re-rendered at high resolution, never as a source of readings. In place of an external collation, Gītā Press was checked **against itself**: it prints the text twice, as verse stotram and as dative nāmāvalī, and those two typesettings were transcribed independently. **All 1008 index entries are accounted for in the stotram's name-verses** — 941 match directly, 52 are vowel-initial names absorbed by sandhi into the word before them, and 15 are consonant-stem datives (कालघ्ने, योगिने, पूष्णे, विदुषे, परस्मै and the like) whose form the matching could not reduce. That check catches transcription error, which is the principal risk in reading this volume; it cannot catch Gītā Press's own editorial choices.

Recension note: The typeface of this volume has hazards that were met and resolved during transcription — the `प्त` ligature reads as `स` at ordinary resolution, `य` as `द`, `ण` as `ग`, and `भ` and `घ` are near-identical — and about forty lines were re-rendered at 400 dpi before being recorded (among them प्राप्तयानः, सप्तसप्तिः, सप्ताश्वः, सप्तार्चिः, स्थितिस्थेयः, मेरुमेयः, घृणिः). — The digital text carries a four-verse preamble Gītā Press does not print (Sumantu prescribing the observance of the bright seventh of Māgha, and Śatānīka's first question); Gītā Press begins at `नाम्नां सहस्रं सवितुः श्रोतुमिच्छामि`. It is recorded here and not supplied. — Gītā Press itself reads `नैकाकारो` at v70 where the digital text has `नकारो`; the Gītā Press reading is kept. — Two numbering misprints in the printed index, text correct and numeral wrong, both verified at 400 dpi: entry 112 is printed `१२२`, and entry 309 is printed `३०२`; each of those numerals also occurs correctly elsewhere on the same page. The index below is contiguous. — Readings transcribed as printed that look odd as Sanskrit, and are flagged rather than emended: v112 `खगोऽन्धो`; the index's `कलतां वराय` (358), `उच्चैर्नकाराय` (519), `विष्टुताय` (345), `भूष्याय` (492); and the repetitions of `सुखदः` within v51 and of `शुभप्रदः` within v69.

Verse count: **134**, together with an unnumbered prose viniyoga and an unnumbered four-line dhyāna verse. Verses 1–10 set out why the stotra is recited; the names run from v12 (where the index's first entry falls) to v128; `॥ फलश्रुतिः ॥` covers 129–134. Printed irregularities reproduced as printed: v128 is set as three lines, and v134 is in a longer metre over four.

Name count: **1008, and genuinely so** — the traditional *sāṣṭasahasra*, "a thousand and eight". The index runs contiguously to 1008 with no padding and no footnote; its last eight entries (1001–1008) are distinct names answering to the stotra's closing verses. This differs from the Dakārādi Durgā Sahasranāma in the same volume, whose last eleven entries repeat one name to make up a round thousand. Of the 1008, 907 are distinct; the repeats are common epithets of the Sun — महान्, होता, अनलः, भगवान् — recurring at intervals of up to 437 entries, as is usual in the genre.

Blurb: A sahasranāma of the Sun as the whole of the Veda's cosmos: Savitṛ is Viśvavid and Viśvajit, maker and dissolver of time, the sacrificer (hotṛ) and the sacrifice, the seven-horsed, the seven-flamed, lord of the manvantaras — and, in the dhyāna, Nārāyaṇa himself, seated on a lotus at the centre of the solar disc with conch and discus in hand. It comes framed by the Bhaviṣya Purāṇa's own warrant: the sage who recites it says that when he praised the Sun with these names, the god appeared to him in person.

--- verse 1 | section: Pūrvabhāga ---
deva:
नाम्नां सहस्रं सवितुः श्रोतुमिच्छामि हे द्विज।
येन ते दर्शनं यातः साक्षाद् देवो दिवाकरः॥
iast:
nāmnāṁ sahasraṁ savituḥ śrotumicchāmi he dvija|
yena te darśanaṁ yātaḥ sākṣād devo divākaraḥ||
en: Śatānīka said: O twice-born one, I wish to hear the thousand names of Savitṛ, by which the god Divākara himself came visibly before you.
tel: శతానీకుడు పలికెను: ఓ ద్విజుడా, ఏ నామములచే దివాకరుడైన దేవుడు స్వయముగా నీకు ప్రత్యక్షమయ్యెనో, ఆ సవితృని సహస్రనామములను వినగోరుచున్నాను.
hi: शतानीक ने कहा: हे द्विज, मैं सविता के वे सहस्र नाम सुनना चाहता हूँ, जिनके द्वारा दिवाकर देव स्वयं साक्षात् तुम्हें दर्शन देने आए।

iast:
sarvamaṅgalamaṅgalyaṁ sarvapāpapraṇāśanam|
stotrametanmahāpuṇyaṁ sarvopadravanāśanam||
en: This hymn is the most auspicious of all auspicious things, the destroyer of every sin, supremely meritorious, and the remover of every calamity.
tel: ఈ స్తోత్రము సర్వమంగళములలో మంగళకరమైనది, సర్వపాపములను నశింపజేయునది, మహాపుణ్యప్రదమైనది, సర్వ ఉపద్రవములను తొలగించునది.
hi: यह स्तोत्र सब मंगलों में मंगलमय, सब पापों का नाशक, महापुण्यदायक और सब उपद्रवों को मिटाने वाला है।

deva:

ज्वराद्यैर्मुच्यते राजन् स्तोत्रेऽस्मिन् पठिते नरः॥
iast:
na tadasti bhayaṁ kiñcit yadanena na naśyati|
jvarādyairmucyate rājan stotre'smin paṭhite naraḥ||

tel: దీనిచే నశింపని భయము ఏదియు లేదు; ఓ రాజా, ఈ స్తోత్రము పఠించినచో మనుష్యుడు జ్వరము మొదలగువాని నుండి విముక్తుడగును.
hi: ऐसा कोई भय नहीं जो इससे नष्ट न हो; हे राजन्, इस स्तोत्र के पाठ से मनुष्य ज्वर आदि से मुक्त हो जाता है।

--- verse 6 | section: Pūrvabhāga ---
