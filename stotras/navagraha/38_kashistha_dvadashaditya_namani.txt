Title: Kāśīstha Dvādaśāditya Nāmāni
Devanāgarī: काशीस्थद्वादशादित्यनामानि
Telugu: కాశీస్థ ద్వాదశాదిత్య నామాని

Author: The colophon places it in the **Skanda Purāṇa, Kāśīkhaṇḍa**. The Wikisource witness gives the exact place: Kāśīkhaṇḍa adhyāya 46, vv. 45–47. The speaker addresses **Ghaṭodbhava** ("the pot-born", Agastya).

Source / recension: Primary text: **Gītā Press, Gorakhpur — Śatanāma Stotra Saṅgraha**, code 1850, p. 223 (PDF p. 224), transcribed from the page images of the printed edition. It follows the volume's forty numbered entries, in the appendix; the whole piece — three verses and the twelve-name index — occupies one page. **Collated against sa.wikisource.org, `स्कन्दपुराणम्/खण्डः ४ (काशीखण्डः)/अध्यायः ०४६`**, vv. 45–47, the same passage inside the full chapter. It is independent of Gītā Press. Stotra and nāmāvalī are paired in one file as the publisher prints them, and were checked against each other name by name.

Recension note: **Gītā Press followed; the Wikisource text (WS) is recorded, not adopted.** The two agree word for word. WS differs only in orthography, writing anusvāra where Gītā Press prints the class nasal: `सांबादित्य`, `गंगादित्य`, `रक्षंत्यमी` for `साम्बादित्य`, `गङ्गादित्य`, `रक्षन्त्यमी`. **Verse numbering.** Gītā Press prints the three verses unnumbered; in WS they are the chapter's vv. 45–47, and the blocks here are numbered 1–3. **What comes before.** WS's v. 44 introduces the list — the sun, knowing Kāśī's power, "made himself twelvefold and took his station in the city of Kāśī" — and Gītā Press does not print it. **Stotra and nāmāvalī agree**, with one division: v. 2 `वृद्धकेशवसंज्ञकौ` ("the two named Vṛddha and Keśava") becomes two names, nos. 8 `वृद्धादित्याय` and 9 `केशवादित्याय`. The nāmāvalī also supplies `आदित्य` where the verse abbreviates: v. 2 `खखोल्कः` → no. 6 `खखोल्कादित्याय`. **Each name carries its place**, `काश्यां` ("at Kāśī"), in the nāmāvalī though not in the verses. **Prefix.** Gītā Press prints `ॐ` before each name; this corpus prints `श्री` for every nāmāvalī without exception (user, 2026-09-08). **The nāmāvalī's own colophon** reads `॥ इति श्रीस्कन्दपुराणे काशीखण्डे काशीस्थद्वादशादित्यनामावलिः सम्पूर्णा ॥`; the colophon block here carries the one printed after the verses. **Cross-reference:** `navagraha/13_aditya_dvadashanama_stotram.txt` is the twelve names of the sun (Dhātā, Mitra and the rest) and is a different work; these twelve are shrines in Kāśī.

Blurb: The sun made himself twelvefold and settled in Kāśī. Here are the twelve, from Lolārka at the meeting of the Asi to Yamāditya, who between them guard the holy city for ever against the wicked.

--- verse 1 | section: Nāmāni ---
deva:
लोलार्क उत्तरार्कश्च साम्बादित्यस्तथैव च ।
चतुर्थो द्रुपदादित्यो मयूखादित्य एव च ॥
iast:
lolārka uttarārkaśca sāmbādityastathaiva ca |
caturtho drupadādityo mayūkhāditya eva ca ||
en: Lolārka, Uttarārka and Sāmbāditya; the fourth is Drupadāditya, and Mayūkhāditya as well.
tel: లోలార్కుడు, ఉత్తరార్కుడు, సాంబాదిత్యుడు; నాలుగవవాడు ద్రుపదాదిత్యుడు, మయూఖాదిత్యుడును.
hi: लोलार्क, उत्तरार्क तथा साम्बादित्य; चौथे द्रुपदादित्य और मयूखादित्य भी।

--- verse 2 | section: Nāmāni ---
deva:
खखोल्कश्चारुणादित्यो वृद्धकेशवसंज्ञकौ ।
दशमो विमलादित्यो गङ्गादित्यस्तथैव च ॥
iast:
khakholkaścāruṇādityo vṛddhakeśavasaṁjñakau |
daśamo vimalādityo gaṅgādityastathaiva ca ||
en: Khakholka and Aruṇāditya, and the two named Vṛddha and Keśava; the tenth is Vimalāditya, and Gaṅgāditya as well.
tel: ఖఖోల్కుడు, అరుణాదిత్యుడు, వృద్ధుడు కేశవుడు అను పేరుగలవారిద్దఱు; పదియవవాడు విమలాదిత్యుడు, గంగాదిత్యుడును.
hi: खखोल्क और अरुणादित्य, तथा वृद्ध एवं केशव नामवाले दोनों; दसवें विमलादित्य और गंगादित्य भी।

--- verse 3 | section: Nāmāni ---
deva:
द्वादशश्च यमादित्यः काशिपुर्यां घटोद्भव ।
तमोऽधिकेभ्यो दुष्टेभ्यः क्षेत्रं रक्षन्त्यमी सदा ॥
iast:
dvādaśaśca yamādityaḥ kāśipuryāṁ ghaṭodbhava |
tamo'dhikebhyo duṣṭebhyaḥ kṣetraṁ rakṣantyamī sadā ||
en: And the twelfth is Yamāditya, in the city of Kāśī, O pot-born one. These guard the holy field for ever from the wicked in whom darkness prevails.
tel: పండ్రెండవవాడు యమాదిత్యుడు; ఓ ఘటోద్భవా, కాశీపురమునందు వీరు తమోధికులగు దుష్టులనుండి క్షేత్రమును ఎల్లప్పుడు రక్షింతురు.
hi: और बारहवें यमादित्य हैं; हे घटोद्भव (अगस्त्य)! काशीपुरीमें ये सब तमोगुणसे अधिक दुष्टोंसे क्षेत्रकी सदा रक्षा करते हैं।

--- verse none | section: Colophon ---
deva:
॥ इति श्रीस्कन्दपुराणे काशीखण्डे काशीस्थद्वादशादित्यनामानि ॥
iast:
|| iti śrīskandapurāṇe kāśīkhaṇḍe kāśīsthadvādaśādityanāmāni ||
en: Thus, in the Śrī Skanda Purāṇa, Kāśīkhaṇḍa, the names of the twelve Ādityas that stand in Kāśī.
tel: ఇట్లు శ్రీస్కందపురాణమునందు కాశీఖండమునందు కాశీస్థద్వాదశాదిత్యనామములు.
hi: इस प्रकार श्रीस्कन्दपुराणके काशीखण्डमें काशीस्थ द्वादश आदित्योंके नाम।
