Blurb: This presents Venus as the guru of the daityas and as the planet of pleasure. He is clad in white, bright as crystal, lit like silver, gently smiling, and as fair as jasmine and pearl. He is a descendant of Bhṛgu and a master of policy. He is lord of Libra and Taurus, and he was born under Jyeṣṭhā. He is the friend of the enemies of the gods, and yet he frees Bali from his bonds. The list asks him for rain, for offspring, for prosperity, and at the last for release.
