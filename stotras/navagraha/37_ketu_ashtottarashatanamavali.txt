Title: Śrī Ketu Aṣṭottaraśatanāmāvalī
Devanāgarī: श्री केतु-अष्टोत्तरशतनामावलिः
Telugu: శ్రీ కేతు అష్టోత్తరశతనామావళిః

Author: Traditional; the source names no Purāṇa or composer.

Source / recension: Primary text: **a Telugu-script nāmāvalī collection, Smārtha e-books (smarthamitra.in), "108–1008"** (scanned PDF, page images only, PDF metadata author "kprss", 2016), printed pp. 248–250 (PDF pp. 250–252, the last pages of the volume). **Not a Gītā Press edition; Gītā Press's *Śatanāma Stotra Saṅgraha* (code 1850) does not contain this text.** Transcribed from the Telugu page images into Devanāgarī; doubtful readings were checked on 300-dpi crops. **Collated against sanskritdocuments.org `ketu108.itx`** (source: *Ashtoththara Shathanamavali Shatakam*, ed. R. Muralikrishna; transliterated by S. Kalyanaraman, proofread by Detlef Eichler), an independent printed-anthology witness.

Recension note: **The Smārtha nāmāvalī is followed; the Muralikrishna witness (SD) is recorded, not adopted.** Both lists share one core and one overall order, like the Rāhu list, and include the same house-by-house jyotiṣa names (`चतुर्थे मातृनाशकाय` etc.), which are printed run together here as in the source. But they differ often in detail, and in places in sequence. **Numbering.** The print's tally marks reach 108, and so does this file. **No. 91 is two names under one number**, `शुभाशुभफलप्रदाय सुधापायिने`, printed across two lines with one `నమః`; SD has them as two separate names. **Readings kept as printed, checked at 300 dpi (Smārtha → SD):** no. 23 `पुष्पवद्ग्रहणे` → `puṣpavadgrāhiṇe`; no. 32 `शिखिनेन्धनाय` → SD has `śikhine` at no. 30 and `kuśedhmapriyāya` at no. 71 (the Smārtha form looks like a fusion of the two); no. 75 `सर्पाक्षिकाताय` → `sarpākṣijātāya` (the print clearly has కా, not జా). The English glosses for these are marked "reading as printed". Other differences: no. 5 `नवमग्रहाय` → `navagrahayutāya`; no. 10 `फलधूम्रसङ्काशाय` → `phulladhūmrasaṅkāśāya`; no. 17 `पापकण्टकाय` → SD `krūrakaṇṭhāya` at no. 17; no. 26 `चित्रशुभ्रधराय` → `citravastradharāya`; no. 48 `नरपीठकाय` → `nṛpīḍāya`; no. 66 `द्वितीयेस्फुटवक्त्रदाय` ("giving clear speech") → `dvitīye 'sphuṭavagdātre` ("giving unclear speech"), a reversal that turns on an avagraha the Telugu print does not mark; no. 73 `षष्ठेअनृतवते` → no counterpart in SD; no. 84 `गृहोत्तंसाय` → SD `grahanāyakāya`. SD also has `amarāya`, `pīḍakāya`, `amartyāya`, `viṣṇudṛṣṭāya`, `asureśvarāya`, `vaicitryakapaṭasyandanāya` and `vicitraphaladāyine`, which the Smārtha list does not have in that form. The Smārtha list has instead `करालवदनाय`, `रक्तलोचनाय`, `विदाहकाय`, `कपिलाक्षाय` and `हिमगर्भाय`. **Duplicates:** `पिङ्गलाक्षाय` (nos. 9 and 100) and `कालाग्निसन्निभाय` (nos. 47 and 106). **Prefix.** The source prints `ఓం` (ॐ) before the first name of each column; this corpus prints `श्री` for every nāmāvalī without exception (user, 2026-09-08). **Cross-reference:** `navagraha/23_ketu_kavacham.txt` is a separate Ketu text.

Blurb: Rāhu's other half — the headless body, and yet here called large-headed, head-only, banner-shaped, the smoke-coloured comet with hair standing on end. Son of Siṁhikā and of Death, Śani's companion and Śukra's friend, eater of horse-gram and wearer of cat's-eye, he too is named by his effects house by house. The list closes on something unexpected: a fire that is cold within.
