Title: Śrī Candra Aṣṭottaraśatanāma Stotram
Devanāgarī: श्रीचन्द्र-अष्टोत्तरशतनामस्तोत्रम्
Telugu: శ్రీ చంద్ర అష్టోత్తరశతనామ స్తోత్రం

Author: Anonymous. **No source text is named**: the colophon is bare (`इति श्रीचन्द्राष्टोत्तरशतनामस्तोत्रं सम्पूर्णम्`), and Gītā Press footnotes the title word `स्तोत्रं` with `प्राचीन नामावलीके आधारपर संयोजित` — "composed on the basis of an old nāmāvalī". On the volume's own statement, then, the verse form was **assembled from a name-list**, not transmitted as a stotra.

Source / recension: Primary text: **Gītā Press, Gorakhpur — Śatanāma Stotra Saṅgraha**, code 1850, pp. 91–94 (stotra pp. 91–92, nāmāvalī pp. 93–94; PDF pp. 92–95), transcribed from the page images of the printed edition. Entry 16 of the volume's forty. **Collated against sanskritdocuments.org `chandra108nAmastotra.itx`** (provided by Manda Krishna Shrikanth, proofread by KSR Ramachandran and PSA Easwaran) — an independent digital witness of the same verse text; it is not from Gītā Press and carries its own variant notes. Stotra and nāmāvalī are paired in one file as the publisher prints them, and were checked against each other name by name.

Recension note: **Gītā Press followed; the sanskritdocuments witness (SD) is recorded, not adopted.** Differences (Gītā Press → SD): v. 2 `जयोद्योगो` → `jagadyoniH`; v. 5 `नित्यानुष्ठानदस्तथा` → `nityAnuShThAnadAyakaH`; v. 6 `शुचिः` → `shuchI`; v. 7 `भुक्तिदश्चैव भद्रश्च भक्तदारिद्र्यभञ्जनः` → `bhuktido muktido bhadro bhaktadAridryabha~njakaH` (SD adds `मुक्तिदः` and gives Gītā Press's `भञ्जनः` as its variant); v. 11 `सिताङ्गः सितभूषणः` → `sItAMgo sItabhUShaNaH`, with variants `ShItAMgo`/`pItAMgo` noted in SD; v. 14 `चतुरस्रासनारूढः चतुरो` → `chaturashrAsanArUDhashchaturo`, and `विवस्वन्मण्डलाज्ञेयवासो` → `vivasvanmaNDalAgneyavAso`; v. 15 `दान्तो` → `dAntyo`; vv. 15–16 Gītā Press `ग्रसितार्को द्विजराजः ॥ द्युतिलकश्च द्विभुजः तथैव द्विजपूजितः` → SD `grasitArko grahAdhipaH || dvijarAjo dyutilako dvibhujo dvijapUjitaH` (SD has the extra name `ग्रहाधिपः`); v. 17 `मुनिस्तुतो` → `munistutyo`, `पलाशसमिधप्रियः` → `phalAshasamidhapriyaH`, and **the last line differs entirely**: Gītā Press `चन्द्रमाश्चेति नामानि कीर्तिदानि शुभानि वै`, SD `evaM nakShatranAthasya nAmnAmaShTottaraM shatam`. So in Gītā Press **`चन्द्रमाः` is itself the 108th name**, and the list ends on it (no. 108 `चन्द्रमसे`); in SD the name is absent and the line is a count claim. **Stotra and nāmāvalī diverge once:** v. 14 prints `विवस्वन्मण्डलाज्ञेयवासो`, the nāmāvalī no. 89 `विवस्वन्मण्डलाग्नेयवासाय`; SD agrees with the nāmāvalī (`Agneya`, "south-east", the Moon's quarter in the navagraha maṇḍala). The stotra's `ज्ञे` looks like a misprint for `ग्ने`; **the stotra's print is kept** and translated by the nāmāvalī's sense, which is flagged here rather than silently mended. **V. 17 is a tristich**, three lines under one number. **No phalaśruti.** **Nāmāvalī: 108 names, numbered 1–108, all distinct**, in the stotra's order. **Prefix.** Gītā Press prints `ॐ` before each name; this corpus prints `श्री` for every nāmāvalī without exception (user, 2026-09-08). **Cross-reference:** `navagraha/24_chandra_stotram.txt` and `navagraha/14_chandra_kavacham.txt` are separate Candra texts; no overlap in text was found.

Blurb: A hymn built out of a name-list, as the volume itself admits — and it reads like one, a white catalogue of the Moon: white parasol, white banner, white limbs, white ornaments, white garlands, white sandal paste, a chariot of ten horses, a square seat south-east of the Sun. Between the images come the astronomy and the myth: the digits that wax and wane, the earth's shadow that covers him, the eclipse in which he swallows the Sun, born of Atri's eye and of the ocean, husband of Rohiṇī, father of Budha.

--- verse 1 | section: Dhyānam ---
deva:
कर्पूरस्फटिकावदातमनिशं पूर्णेन्दुबिम्बाननम् ।
मुक्तादामविभूषितेन वपुषा निर्मूलयन्तं तमः ॥
हस्ताभ्यां कुमुदं वरं विदधतं नीलालकोद्भासितम् ।
स्वस्याङ्कस्थमृगोदिताश्रयगुणं सोमं सुधाब्धिं भजे ॥
iast:
karpūrasphaṭikāvadātamaniśaṁ pūrṇendubimbānanam |
muktādāmavibhūṣitena vapuṣā nirmūlayantaṁ tamaḥ ||
hastābhyāṁ kumudaṁ varaṁ vidadhataṁ nīlālakodbhāsitam |
svasyāṅkasthamṛgoditāśrayaguṇaṁ somaṁ sudhābdhiṁ bhaje ||
en: I worship Soma, the ocean of nectar: always white as camphor and crystal, his face the full moon's disc; his body adorned with strings of pearls uprooting the darkness; holding a white water-lily and the boon-gesture in his hands, radiant with dark curls; whose virtue, it is said, is to give shelter to the deer on his lap.
tel: కర్పూరము, స్ఫటికము వలె ఎల్లప్పుడు నిర్మలమైనవాడు, పూర్ణచంద్రబింబమువంటి ముఖము గలవాడు, ముత్యాలహారములచే అలంకరింపబడిన దేహముతో చీకటిని నిర్మూలించువాడు, చేతులయందు కలువను, వరముద్రను ధరించినవాడు, నల్లని ముంగురులచే ప్రకాశించువాడు, తన ఒడిలోని మృగమునకు ఆశ్రయమిచ్చుటయే గుణముగా గలవాడు అయిన సుధాసముద్రరూపుడగు సోముని భజించుచున్నాను.
hi: कर्पूर और स्फटिक मणिके समान जिनका निर्मल वर्ण है, जिनका मुखमण्डल पूर्णेन्दुबिम्बके समान है, मुक्ताकी मालाओंसे अलंकृत शरीरके द्वारा जो अन्धकारका उन्मूलन करनेवाले हैं, जो श्यामल केशराशिके कारण अतिशय सुशोभित हैं, जो अपने हाथोंमें कमलका पुष्प एवं वरमुद्रा धारण किये हुए हैं, मृगलांछनको अपनी गोदमें आश्रय देना जिनका गुण माना गया है, इस प्रकारके सुधासमुद्ररूप चन्द्रमाका मैं सदा ध्यान करता हूँ।
