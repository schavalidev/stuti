Devanāgarī: श्री बृहस्पति-चतुष्षष्ट्युत्तरशतनामावलिः
Telugu: శ్రీ బృహస్పతి చతుష్షష్ట్యుత్తర శతనామావళిః

Author: Traditional; the source names no Purāṇa or composer.

Source / recension: Primary text: **a Telugu-script nāmāvalī collection, Smārtha e-books (smarthamitra.in), "108–1008"**. This is a scanned PDF (page images only, no text layer, PDF metadata author "kprss", 2016); Bṛhaspati is on printed pp. 234–238 (PDF pp. 238–242). **It is not a Gītā Press edition, and Gītā Press's *Śatanāma Stotra Saṅgraha* (code 1850) does not contain this text.** The names were transcribed from the Telugu page images into Devanāgarī, with doubtful readings checked on 300-dpi crops. **Collated against sanskritdocuments.org `guru108.itx`**, whose stated source is *Ashtoththara Shathanamavali Shatakam*, ed. R. Muralikrishna (transliterated by S. Kalyanaraman). That is a separate printed anthology, so it is an independent witness, but **for only part of this list**: it is a 108-name list, and this one runs to 164.

Recension note: **THIS IS A 164-NAME LIST, NOT A 108.** The source titles it `చతుష్షష్ట్యుత్తర శతనామావళిః` ("a hundred and sixty-four names"), and the file follows the source. **Numbering.** The print marks a tally every ten names and reaches 162. The 164 in the title comes out only if two comma-joined pairs are counted as two names each: `ధనదాత్రే, భర్త్రే` (here nos. 138–139) and `కృష్ణాయ, సూక్ష్మాయ` (nos. 161–162). This file splits them, as the title requires, so from no. 139 on the numbers here run one ahead of the printed tally marks, and from no. 162 on, two ahead. **Relation to the 108-name witness (SD).** Smārtha nos. 1–101 correspond to SD's list, almost name for name, and Smārtha nos. 117–128 to SD's closing names. Smārtha nos. 102–116 and 129–164 have no counterpart in SD. So the longer list contains the 108 with additions, and many of the additions are repeats: `बृहस्पतये` appears four times plus `श्रीबृहस्पतये` at the end, `सुराचार्याय` three times, `श्रीमते` three times, and `दयावते`, `शुभलक्षणाय`, `जीवाय`, `वाचस्पतये`, `सदानन्दाय` and `धन्याय` twice each. **Readings within the shared portion (Smārtha → SD).** No. 2 `गुणवराय` → `guṇakarāya` (SD gives `guṇavarāya` as a variant). Nos. 19–20 `अध्वरकृते परस्मै` (two names) → `adhvarakṛtparāya` (one). No. 30 `बृहत्प्रदाय` → `bṛhadrathāya`. No. 36 `सुरकार्यहितङ्कराय` → `surakāryakṛtodyamāya`. No. 57 `आङ्गीरसाब्दसञ्जाताय` → `aṅgirovarṣasañjatāya`. No. 61 `स्वर्णवर्णाय` → `svarṇakāyāya`. No. 67 `…मणिमण्डनमण्डिताय` → `…maṇimaṇḍalamaṇḍitāya`. Nos. 69–72 (`कलिदोषनिवारकाय इन्द्राधिदेवाय देवेशाय देवताभीष्टदायकाय`) → SD one name, `indrādyamarasaṅghapāya`. No. 74 `…विभासुराय` → `…vibhāvasave`. No. 87 `…प्रचारकाय` → `…pracāravate`. No. 94 `वराय` → absent from SD. No. 123 `सर्वपाय` → `sarvagāya`; checked on a 300-dpi crop, and the print clearly reads సర్వపాయ. No. 126 `सर्वहृष्टाय` → `sarvadātuṣṭāya`. No. 127 `सर्वगाय` → `sarvadāya`. **Uncertain reading:** no. 157 `कालरुग्वेत्रे` is transcribed exactly as printed (కాలరుగ్వేత్రే, checked at 300 dpi). No sense is secure; it may be a corruption, perhaps of `kālavid-` or `ṛgvettṛ`. It is not emended and has no witness. **Prefix.** The source prints `ఓం` (ॐ) before the first name of each column; this corpus prints `श्री` for every nāmāvalī without exception (user, 2026-09-08). **Cross-reference:** `navagraha/21_brihaspati_kavacham.txt` is a separate text.

Blurb: The guru of the gods, given more names than the usual hundred and eight: son of Aṅgiras, lord of speech, golden-bodied and yellow-clad, long-bearded, lord of Sagittarius and Pisces and of the asterism Puṣya. He is the counsellor who saves the gods' armies from disaster and the priest of Indra's court, and the list keeps coming back to him by name — Bṛhaspati, Bṛhaspati — like a litany that will not let go.
