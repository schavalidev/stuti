Title: Śrī Aṅgāraka Aṣṭottaraśatanāmāvalī
Devanāgarī: श्री अङ्गारक-अष्टोत्तरशतनामावलिः
Telugu: శ్రీ అంగారక అష్టోత్తరశతనామావళిః

Author: Traditional; the source names no Purāṇa or composer. The list is the name sequence of the Maṅgala / Aṅgāraka Aṣṭottaraśatanāma Stotram (`महीसुतो महाभागो मङ्गलो मङ्गलप्रदः …`) set out as a nāmāvalī.

Source / recension: Primary text: **a Telugu-script nāmāvalī collection, Smārtha e-books (smarthamitra.in), "108–1008"**. This is a scanned PDF (page images only, no text layer, PDF metadata author "kprss", 2016) with 28 nāmāvalīs; Aṅgāraka is on printed pp. 228–230 (PDF pp. 232–234). **It is not a Gītā Press edition, and Gītā Press's *Śatanāma Stotra Saṅgraha* (code 1850) does not contain this text**, so no Gītā Press reading exists to follow. The names were transcribed from the Telugu page images into Devanāgarī. **Collated against sanskritdocuments.org `angAraka108nAmastotra.itx`** (provided by Manda Krishna Srikanth, proofread by KSR Ramachandran). That is the same name sequence in **verse (stotra) form**, 16 ślokas, from a different editorial lineage, so it is an independent witness. Where the two differ, the nāmāvalī is followed and the difference recorded.

Recension note: **The nāmāvalī (Smārtha) is followed; the verse witness (SD) is recorded, not adopted.** The two agree name for name, in the same order, with these exceptions (nāmāvalī → SD). No. 68 `भव्यभूताय` → `bhavyabhūṣaṇaḥ`. No. 75 `अमिताशनाय` → SD `meṣavāho mitāśanaḥ`; the verse's sandhi can be read either way, and the nāmāvalī reads `amita-`. No. 93 `यौवनाय` ("youthful") → `yāvano` (of the Yavana country); the Telugu print clearly reads యౌవనాయ. No. 51 `शरणागतपोषणाय` → `-poṣakaḥ`. No. 65 `दुष्टगर्वविमोचनाय` → `-vimocakaḥ`. No. 98 `शुचिकराय`, no. 99 `शूराय`: same as SD. **The list has 108 numbered names; `सुखप्रदाय` appears twice** (nos. 19 and 105), as `sukhapradaḥ` does twice in SD vv. 3 and 16. **No. 108 is `श्रीमते अङ्गारकाय`** (two words under one number), a closing formula; SD's verse has no counterpart for it, because it ends on `सर्वाभीष्टफलप्रदः`. SD also has an `asṛg aṅgārako` pair (v. 14), which the nāmāvalī gives as two names, nos. 88 `असृजे` and 89 `अङ्गारकाय`. **Prefix.** The source prints `ఓం` (ॐ) before the first name of each column; this corpus prints `श्री` for every nāmāvalī without exception (user, 2026-09-08). **Cross-reference:** `navagraha/20_angaraka_mangala_stotram.txt` and `navagraha/15_runa_vimochana_angaraka_stotram.txt` are separate Aṅgāraka texts, not this list.

Blurb: The son of the Earth, named a hundred and eight ways: red-clad, red-bodied, copper-eyed, riding a ram and carrying mace, spear and trident. The list places him as an astronomer would — south of the Sun, moving retrograde and stationary, lord of Aries and Scorpio, seated in a triangular maṇḍala, ruler of Avanti — and then asks of him what the anxious ask of Mars: freedom from debt, enemies, bad dreams and fear.
