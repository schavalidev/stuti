Title: Āditya Hṛdayam
Devanāgarī: आदित्य हृदयम्
Telugu: ఆదిత్య హృదయం

Author: Traditional — spoken by Sage Agastya to Śrī Rāma, from the Yuddhakāṇḍa of the Vālmīki Rāmāyaṇa (Sarga 107)

Source / recension: stotranidhi.com — https://stotranidhi.com/en/aditya-hridayam-in-english/ (IAST), https://stotranidhi.com/hi/aditya-hridayam-in-sanskrit/ (Devanāgarī), https://stotranidhi.com/aditya-hrudayam-in-telugu/ (Telugu script).

Verse count: 31

Sections:
పీఠిక — Introduction, the setting on the battlefield (vv. 1–3)
స్తోత్రం — Stotram, Agastya's teaching (vv. 4–26)
ఉపసంహారం — Conclusion, Rāma's recitation and the outcome (vv. 27–31)

Blurb: The flagship single-planet Sūrya text, framed as battlefield counsel rather than temple liturgy — Agastya interrupts Rāma's exhausted standoff with Rāvaṇa to teach him this hymn, which works by identifying the Sun with essentially every other god in the pantheon (Brahmā, Viṣṇu, Śiva, Yama, Varuṇa, Kubera) rather than praising him as one deity among many. The frame story matters as much as the verses: Rāma listens, recites it three times, and only then picks up his bow, which is why the text is chanted to this day less for solar worship in the abstract than for courage before a decisive confrontation.

--- verse 30 | section: ఉపసంహారం ---
deva:
रावणं प्रेक्ष्य हृष्टात्मा युद्धाय समुपागमत् ।
सर्वयत्नेन महता वधे तस्य धृतोऽभवत् ॥
iast:
rāvaṇaṁ prekṣya hṛṣṭātmā yuddhāya samupāgamat |
sarvayatnena mahatā vadhe tasya dhṛto'bhavat ||
en:
Beholding Rāvaṇa, with a heart full of joy, he advanced to battle; with the greatest of all effort, he became firmly resolved upon slaying him.
tel:
రావణుని చూసి, హర్షభరిత హృదయముతో యుద్ధమునకు ఉపక్రమించెను; సర్వప్రయత్నముతో అతనిని వధించుటకు దృఢసంకల్పుడయ్యెను.
hi:
रावण को देखकर, हर्षित हृदय से युद्ध के लिए आगे बढ़े; पूरे प्रयास के साथ उसके वध के लिए दृढ़ संकल्पित हो गए।

--- verse 31 | section: ఉపసంహారం ---
deva:
अथ रविरवददनिरीक्ष्य रामं
मुदितमनाः परमं प्रहृष्यमाणः ।
निशिचरपतिसङ्क्षयं विदित्वा
सुरगणमध्यगतो वचस्त्वरेति ॥
iast:
atha ravir avadan nirīkṣya rāmaṁ
muditamanāḥ paramaṁ prahṛṣyamāṇaḥ |
niśicarapatisaṅkṣayaṁ viditvā
suragaṇamadhyagato vacastvareti ||
en:
Then the Sun, gazing upon Rāma with a joyful mind, exceedingly delighted, knowing that the destruction of the lord of the night-wanderers (Rāvaṇa) was now certain, standing amidst the assembly of gods, urgently spoke this word: "Make haste!"

hi:
तब सूर्य ने राम को देखकर, आनंदित मन से, परम प्रसन्न होकर, निशाचरराज के विनाश को निश्चित जानकर, देवगणों के मध्य खड़े होकर शीघ्रता से यह वचन कहा: "शीघ्रता करो।"
