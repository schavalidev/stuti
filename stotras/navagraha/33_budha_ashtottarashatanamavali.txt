Devanāgarī: श्री बुध-अष्टोत्तरशतनामावलिः

Author: Traditional; the source names no Purāṇa or composer.

Source / recension: Primary text: **a Telugu-script nāmāvalī collection, Smārtha e-books (smarthamitra.in), "108–1008"**. This is a scanned PDF (page images only, no text layer, PDF metadata author "kprss", 2016); Budha is on printed pp. 231–233 (PDF pp. 235–237). **It is not a Gītā Press edition, and Gītā Press's *Śatanāma Stotra Saṅgraha* (code 1850) does not contain this text.** The names were transcribed from the Telugu page images into Devanāgarī. **Collated against sanskritdocuments.org `budha108.itx`**, whose stated source is *Ashtoththara Shathanamavali Shatakam*, ed. R. Muralikrishna (transliterated by S. Kalyanaraman, proofread by Detlef Eichler). That is a separate printed nāmāvalī anthology, so it is an independent witness. Where the two differ, the Smārtha nāmāvalī is followed and the difference recorded.

Recension note: **The Smārtha nāmāvalī is followed; the Muralikrishna witness (SD) is recorded, not adopted.** Both run 108 names in the same order. The differences (Smārtha → SD) are as follows. Nos. 19–21: `वेदान्तज्ञानभास्वराय विद्याविचक्षणविभवे विद्वत्प्रीतिकराय` → SD `vedāntajñānabhāskarāya vidyāvicakṣaṇāya viduṣe vidvatprītikarāya`, where SD splits `viduṣe` off as a name of its own. No. 22 `बुधाय` → `ṛjave`; this makes `बुधाय` a duplicate in the Smārtha list, nos. 1 and 22. No. 18 `वेदतत्त्वज्ञाय` → `vedatattvāśāya` (an SD slip). No. 23 `विश्वानुकूलसञ्चारिणे` → `-sañcārāya`. No. 38 `प्रसादवदनाय` → `prasannavadanāya`. No. 50 `वाताङ्गिने` → `vātāṅgāya`. No. 65 `चलितेन्द्रियाय` ("of restless senses") → `jitendriyāya` ("who has conquered the senses"), a reversal of sense; the Telugu print clearly reads చలితేంద్రియాయ, so it is kept as printed. No. 72 `सुखिने` → `mahate`. No. 82 `कलुषहारकाय` → same. No. 85 `विश्वपावनाय` → `viśvapavanāya`. No. 93 `बन्धुमुक्ताय` → `bandhuyuktāya`. No. 94 `भानुमण्डलसंश्रिताय` → `vanamaṇḍalasaṁśritāya`. No. 95 `अर्केशानप्रदेशस्थाय` → `arkeśānanivāsasthāya`. No. 100 `प्रियभाषणाय` → `priyabhūṣaṇāya`. No. 102 `माधवासक्ताय` → `mādhavasaktāya`. **No. 108 `बुधग्रहाय` is absent from SD**, which ends at `ghanaphalāśrayāya`. SD also splits `viduṣe` off at no. 20. SD marks its own tally at every tenth name, but a mechanical count of its `OM … namaH` entries gives 107. **Prefix.** The source prints `ఓం` (ॐ) before the first name of each column; this corpus prints `श्री` for every nāmāvalī without exception (user, 2026-09-08). **Cross-reference:** `navagraha/16_budha_kavacham.txt` and `navagraha/25_budha_stotram.txt` are separate Budha texts, not this list.

Blurb: Mercury as the scholar among the planets: son of Soma, of the Ātreya gotra, knower of the Vedas and of logic, skilled in trade, sparing with fever and death. He is yellow-clad and yellow-bodied, bright as campaka flowers, mounted on a lion with sword and shield, lord of Gemini and fond of Virgo. The list also keeps an honest note of his character — quick, restless, never far from the Sun.
