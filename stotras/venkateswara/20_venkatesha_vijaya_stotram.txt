Title: Śrī Veṅkaṭeśa Vijaya Stotram
Devanāgarī: श्री वेङ्कटेश विजय स्तोत्रम्
Telugu: శ్రీ వేంకటేశ విజయ స్తోత్రం

Author: Named in the phalaśruti only by epithet — the sūri "who strikes dread into disputants" (vādi-sādhvasa-kṛt). This is closely parallel to the title Vādīndra-bhīkṛt claimed by the author of the Vijayāryā Saptavibhakti Stotram (file 16 in this collection), and the two works may well be by the same hand; the recension does not say so outright, so the identification is left as a likelihood rather than a fact.

Source / recension: stotranidhi.com — Devanāgarī: https://stotranidhi.com/hi/sri-venkatesha-vijaya-stotram-in-sanskrit/ ; English/IAST cross-check: https://stotranidhi.com/en/sri-venkatesha-vijaya-stotram-in-english/ . Both recensions agree line-for-line across all 55 lines including the colophon. Verse 6 line 2 is reproduced exactly as transmitted, including the zero-width joiner the Devanāgarī carries inside "संमत" and the hyphen the IAST carries at the same point; the compound "jitāmatasaṁmata" there is obscure and its rendering below is conjectural.

Verse count: 14 (13 verses of invocation, 1 phalaśruti)

Sections:
स्तोत्रम् — Stotram (vv. 1–13)
फलश्रुतिः — Phalaśruti (v. 14)

Blurb: These are thirteen verses in a rapid line of twelve syllables, built almost entirely of words of direct address, so that the hymn reads as one long unbroken address. Each verse lands on the command that gives the work its name: vijayībhava and jayībhava, meaning 'be victorious'. Its shape is a descent through Viṣṇu's history rather than a static portrait. The opening verses hold to the hill itself, naming Añjanaśaila, the Svāmi lake, and the waterfalls sounding through the perfumed groves. Verses 8 and 9 drop into Kṛṣṇa's childhood at Gokula, and the killing of Pūtanā, Dhenuka, Keśin, Kāliya and Kaṁsa. Verse 10 turns to the charioteer of the Pāṇḍavas. Verses 11 and 12 turn to Rāma against Khara, Dūṣaṇa and Rāvaṇa. Verse 13 returns to the mountain and its animals: the monkeys, the elephants and the deer towards which the god is said to be tender.

--- verse 1 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
दैवतदैवत मङ्गलमङ्गल
पावनपावन कारणकारण ।
वेङ्कटभूधरमौलिविभूषण
माधव भूधव देव जयीभव ॥
iast:
daivatadaivata maṅgalamaṅgala
pāvanapāvana kāraṇakāraṇa |
veṅkaṭabhūdharamaulivibhūṣaṇa
mādhava bhūdhava deva jayībhava ||
en: O god of gods, auspiciousness of all that is auspicious, purifier of the pure, cause of causes; O ornament upon the crest of the Veṅkaṭa mountain — Mādhava, husband of the Earth, O god, be victorious.
tel: దైవతములకు దైవతమా, మంగళములకు మంగళమా, పావనులకు పావనుడా, కారణములకు కారణమా; వేంకటాచల శిఖరమునకు విభూషణమా — మాధవా, భూధవా, దేవా, జయశీలుడవగుము.
hi: हे देवताओं के देवता, मंगलों के मंगल, पावनों के पावन, कारणों के कारण; हे वेंकटाचल के मौलि के विभूषण — माधव, भूधव, देव, जयी हों।

en: O you whose body is like a raincloud, worker of compassion; whose lovely eyes are autumn lotuses; whose lotus feet are the crest-jewel of the gods — O Lord of the Veṅkaṭa hill, be victorious.

hi: हे वारिद के समान देह वाले, दयाकर; शरद कमल के समान चारु नेत्रों वाले; जिनके चरणकमल देवों के शिरोमणि हैं — हे वेंकटशैलपति, विजयी हों।

hi: हे अंजनशैल के निवासी, निरंजन, समस्त जनों को रंजित करने वाले, अंजन के समान श्याम — हे जगदीश्वर, कृपामृत से शीतल सीकरों की वर्षा करने वाली अपनी दृष्टि से मुझे अभिषिक्त करें।

--- verse 4 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
वीतसमाधिक सारगुणाकर

vītasamādhika sāraguṇākara

en: O you who have neither equal nor superior, mine of essential excellences; whose body is pure sattva alone, supreme Person; adept at ferrying us across the terrible ocean of birth and death — O Lord of the Veṅkaṭa hill, be victorious.
tel: సమానులును అధికులును లేనివాడా, సారగుణాకరా; కేవలసత్త్వమే తనువుగా కలవాడా, పురుషోత్తమా; భయంకరమైన భవార్ణవమును దాటించుటలో కోవిదుడా — వేంకటశైలపతీ, విజయుడవగుము.
hi: हे समान और अधिक से रहित, सारगुणों की खान; केवल सत्त्वमय शरीर वाले, पुरुषोत्तम; भीषण भवार्णव से पार उतारने में कोविद — हे वेंकटशैलपति, विजयी हों।

--- verse 6 | section: Stotram ---

tel: నందకుమారకా, గోకులపాలకా, గోపవధువుల వరుడా, పరాత్పరుడైన కృష్ణా; శ్రీవసుదేవుని కుమారా, జన్మభయమును తొలగించువాడా — వేంకటశైలపతీ, విజయుడవగుము.
