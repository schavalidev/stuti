Title: Śrī Veṅkaṭeśa Aṣṭottaraśatanāma Stotram
Devanāgarī: श्रीवेङ्कटेश-अष्टोत्तरशतनामस्तोत्रम्
Telugu: శ్రీ వేంకటేశ అష్టోత్తరశతనామ స్తోత్రం

Author: Anonymous; Varāha Purāṇa. A chain of transmission is stated inside the text: Śeṣa told it to Kapila, Sūta heard it from Kapila, and Sūta recites it to the sages who question him.

Source / recension: Primary text: **Gītā Press, Gorakhpur — Śatanāma Stotra Saṅgraha**, code 1850, pp. 40–47 (stotra pp. 40–44, nāmāvalī pp. 45–47), transcribed from the page images of the printed edition rather than from OCR. Entry 6 of the volume's forty. The colophon of both stotra and nāmāvalī reads `इति श्रीवराहपुराणे`. Stotra and nāmāvalī are paired in one file as the publisher prints them. Cross-checked internally, name against name — and this text makes that check unusually strong, because the names stand in the dative *inside the verses themselves* (see the recension note), so the nāmāvalī and the stotra are two printings of the same inflected forms and any divergence between them is visible at once. Four such divergences were found and are recorded below. Checked against the twenty other Veṅkaṭeśa files in `venkateswara/`: no aṣṭottaraśatanāma was present, and this is the folder's first. **No second edition was collated**, and no web witness has been sought.

Recension note: **Prefix.** Gītā Press prints `ॐ` before each name; this corpus prints `श्री` for every nāmāvalī without exception (user, 2026-09-08). **Case — this text is built differently from the first five.** Elsewhere in the volume the stotra lists names in the nominative and the nāmāvalī re-inflects them into the caturthī. Here the stotra is *already* in the caturthī and already carries `नमः`: v. 46 says so explicitly — `अष्टोत्तरशतं नाम्नां चतुर्थ्या नमसान्वितम्`, "the hundred and eight of names, in the fourth case, joined with *namas*". The hymn is therefore a namaskāra, not a name-list, and the nāmāvalī is close to a straight re-setting of it. **THE NĀMĀVALĪ HAS 109 ENTRIES, NOT 108.** It is numbered continuously 1–109 with no gap, its last line is `१०९ ॐ श्रीनिवासाय नमः`, and its colophon still calls it `अष्टोत्तरशतनामावलिः`. Verified at 500 dpi. The stotra's own count claim (v. 46) is 108, and the two closing names of v. 46 are `गोविन्दाय` and `श्रीनिवासाय` — so the surplus is at the very end, where a compositor may have set as a numbered entry what the verse offers as its closing salutation. **The count is left at 109 because that is what the volume prints**; `Name count: 109` is deliberate and is the first departure from 108 in this workstream. Do not silently drop the last row to reach the famous number. **All 109 are distinct** — this is the only file so far with no repeated name, where the first five each repeat two or three. So the surplus is a genuine extra name, not a doubling, which makes an accidental 109th the likeliest explanation rather than a variant count. **Four verse/nāmāvalī divergences, all retained as printed:** (a) no. 21 `अधिकन्यापरिष्वक्तवक्षसे` against v. 19 `अब्धिकन्यापरिष्वक्तवक्षसे` — the verse is right (*abdhi-kanyā*, the ocean's daughter, i.e. Lakṣmī); the nāmāvalī has lost the `ब्`, and this is a genuine misprint in the list, verified at 500 dpi. (b) no. 56 `तीर्थस्वामिसरस्नात-` against v. 30 `तीर्थस्वामिसरःस्नात-`, the visarga dropped at a line break. (c) no. 14 `वायुस्तुताय` drops the `सदा` of v. 16. (d) no. 5 `सदञ्जनगिरीशाय` keeps the `सद`- that v. 15 supplies. **Orthography: `ताक्ष्र्य` for `तार्क्ष्य`.** Nos. 27–28 and v. 21 all print `ताक्ष्र्य` — Garuḍa's name with the `र्` metathesised into the conjunct rather than standing as a repha over it. The volume is consistent in this, in verse and in list; verified at 500 dpi and retained. **Numbering.** vv. 4 and 15 are tristichs, three lines under one number, where the rest are distichs; the volume does not renumber. **Two dhyāna verses**, not one, both unnumbered and both glossed in a Hindi footnote. **Sthala-specific.** Unlike the first five, most of these names are tied to Tirumala and its neighbourhood — the Suvarṇamukharī and Kumāradhārikā, the Svāmi lake, the Añjana, Vṛṣabha, Nārāyaṇa, Siṁha and Varāha hills, the Cola prince, the brahmin Kṛṣṇa made a teacher of Vedānta. The English column identifies these where the reference is secure and renders the compound literally where it is not.

Blurb: The Tirumala hill's own hundred and eight, and the most local list in the corpus: it names the god by the ridges he settled on, the tanks pilgrims bathe in, and the people he answered — Agastya, Śuka, a Cola prince, a brahmin called Kṛṣṇa whom he made a teacher of Vedānta. The hymn is addressed throughout rather than described: every name is already in the dative with *namaḥ* attached, so reciting the stotra and performing the archana are the same act.

en: To the unfallen, homage ever, and to him whose abode is the Nīla hill; homage to the lord of the ocean of milk, to the dweller on the Vaikuṇṭha hill.
tel: అచ్యుతునకు నిత్యము నమస్కారము, నీలాద్రినిలయునకు; క్షీరాబ్ధినాథునకు, వైకుంఠాచలవాసికి నమస్కారము.

--- verse 46 | section: Stotram ---
deva:

--- verse 47 | section: Stotram ---

--- verse 48 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
गोविन्दाय नमो नित्यं श्रीनिवासाय ते नमः ।

hi: गोविंदको सदा नमस्कार, श्रीनिवासको तुम्हें नमस्कार। — चतुर्थी विभक्तिमें 'नमस्' से युक्त यह नामोंका अष्टोत्तर शत:

--- verse 49 | section: Phalaśrutiḥ ---

--- verse 50 | section: Phalaśrutiḥ ---
deva:

hi: अर्चनामें विशेषरूपसे यह अष्टोत्तर शत ग्राह्य है। जो वेंकटेश-अभिधेय नामोंसे वेंकटाद्रिनिवासीकी —

--- verse 51 | section: Phalaśrutiḥ ---
deva:
अर्चयेन्नामभिस्तस्य फलं मुक्तिर्न संशयः ।

--- verse 52 | section: Phalaśrutiḥ ---
