Title: Śrī Kṛṣṇa Stotram (Indra Kṛtam)
Devanāgarī: श्री कृष्ण स्तोत्रम् (इन्द्र कृतम्)
Telugu: శ్రీ కృష్ణ స్తోత్రం (ఇంద్ర కృతం)

Author: Ascribed in the text to Indra. The colophon reads इति श्रीब्रह्मवैवर्ते महापुराणे श्रीकृष्णजन्मखण्डे एकविंशोऽध्याये इन्द्रकृत श्री कृष्ण स्तोत्रम् — Brahmavaivarta Mahāpurāṇa, Kṛṣṇajanma Khaṇḍa, adhyāya 21.

Source / recension: https://stotranidhi.com/hi/sri-krishna-stotram-indra-kritam-in-sanskrit/ (Devanāgarī) and https://stotranidhi.com/en/sri-krishna-stotram-indra-kritam-in-english/ (IAST). All twenty-one verses were compared word by word across the two editions and agree exactly, including the spellings बृन्दारण्ये (for वृन्दारण्ये) in v. 8 and कालीय (for कालिय) in v. 16.

Verse count: 21

Sections:
స్తోత్రం — Stotram (vv. 1–17)
మంత్రపరంపర — Transmission of the hymn and mantra (vv. 18–20)
ఫలశ్రుతి — Phalaśruti, the fruits of recitation (v. 21)

Blurb: This is Indra's hymn after the humiliation at Govardhana, from the Brahmavaivarta Purāṇa. It begins at the highest possible altitude, with the imperishable, the supreme Brahman, beyond the guṇas, formless, made purely of its own will. It passes through the colours that Kṛṣṇa wears in the four ages. Then, from the eighth verse, it drops abstraction entirely and becomes a chain of glimpses, each introduced by the same word, kutracit, meaning 'somewhere'. Somewhere he rests against Rādhā's breast in an empty wood. Somewhere he does up the heavy weight of her braid. Somewhere he paints her feet with red lac. Somewhere he takes the betel she has already chewed. Somewhere he goes off with her, and somewhere he leaves her behind. In its grammar the whole of this is one long string of accusatives, suspended from the single verb vande in verse 5. The last verses turn to the hymn's own lineage: Bṛhaspati taught it to Indra before the fight with Vṛtra, Brahmā to Kumāra at Puṣkara, and Kumāra to Aṅgiras. They end with the promise that its reciter will not see Yama's messengers even in a dream.

kālīyamūrdhni pādābjaṁ dattavantaṁ ca kutracit |
vinodamuralīśabdaṁ kurvantaṁ kutracinmudā ||
en: somewhere setting his lotus foot upon Kāliya's head; somewhere joyfully setting the flute sounding in play;
tel: ఎక్కడో కాళియుని తలపై పాదపద్మాన్ని ఉంచినవాడు; ఎక్కడో సంతోషంగా వినోదంగా మురళీనాదం చేయువాడు;
hi: कहीं कालिय के मस्तक पर चरणकमल रखते हुए; कहीं प्रसन्नता से विनोदपूर्वक मुरली का शब्द करते हुए;

--- verse 17 | section: Stotram ---
deva:
गायन्तं रम्यसङ्गीतं कुत्रचिद्बालकैः सह ।
स्तुत्वा शक्रः स्तवेन्द्रेण प्रणनाम हरिं भिया ॥
iast:
gāyantaṁ ramyasaṅgītaṁ kutracidbālakaiḥ saha |
stutvā śakraḥ stavendreṇa praṇanāma hariṁ bhiyā ||
en: somewhere singing beautiful music with the boys. — Having praised him with this greatest of hymns, Śakra bowed down before Hari in fear.
tel: ఎక్కడో బాలురతో కలిసి రమ్యమైన సంగీతం పాడువాడు. — ఈ స్తవశ్రేష్ఠంతో స్తుతించి, శక్రుడు భయంతో హరికి ప్రణామం చేశాడు.
hi: कहीं बालकों के साथ रम्य संगीत गाते हुए। — इस स्तवश्रेष्ठ से स्तुति कर शक्र ने भय से हरि को प्रणाम किया।

--- verse 18 | section: Transmission ---
deva:
पुरा दत्तेन गुरुणा रणे वृत्रासुरेण च ।
कृष्णेन दत्तं कृपया ब्रह्मणे च तपस्यते ॥
iast:
purā dattena guruṇā raṇe vṛtrāsureṇa ca |
kṛṣṇena dattaṁ kṛpayā brahmaṇe ca tapasyate ||
en: This hymn was given to Indra long ago by his own guru, at the time of the battle with the demon Vṛtra; and it was given in mercy by Kṛṣṇa himself to Brahmā when Brahmā was performing austerities.
tel: పూర్వం వృత్రాసురునితో యుద్ధ సమయంలో ఇంద్రునికి అతని గురువుచేత ఈ స్తోత్రం ఇవ్వబడింది; తపస్సు చేస్తున్న బ్రహ్మకు కృష్ణుడే కృపతో దీనిని ఇచ్చాడు.
hi: पूर्व में वृत्रासुर के साथ युद्ध के समय गुरु द्वारा यह स्तोत्र इन्द्र को दिया गया था; और तपस्या करते हुए ब्रह्मा को स्वयं कृष्ण ने कृपापूर्वक दिया।

--- verse 19 | section: Transmission ---
deva:
एकादशाक्षरो मन्त्रः कवचं सर्वलक्षणम् ।
दत्तमेतत् कुमाराय पुष्करे ब्रह्मणा पुरा ॥
iast:
ekādaśākṣaro mantraḥ kavacaṁ sarvalakṣaṇam |
dattametat kumārāya puṣkare brahmaṇā purā ||
en: The eleven-syllabled mantra, and the kavaca complete in all its marks — these were given long ago by Brahmā to Kumāra at Puṣkara.
tel: ఏకాదశాక్షర మంత్రం, సర్వలక్షణాలతో కూడిన కవచం — వీటిని పూర్వం పుష్కరక్షేత్రంలో బ్రహ్మ కుమారునికి ఇచ్చాడు.
hi: एकादशाक्षर मन्त्र और सर्वलक्षणयुक्त कवच — ये पूर्वकाल में पुष्कर में ब्रह्मा ने कुमार को दिए।

--- verse 20 | section: Transmission ---
deva:
कुमारोऽङ्गिरसे दत्तं गुरवेऽङ्गिरसां मुने ।
इदमिन्द्रकृतं स्तोत्रं नित्यं भक्त्या च यः पठेत् ॥
iast:
kumāro'ṅgirase dattaṁ gurave'ṅgirasāṁ mune |
idamindrakṛtaṁ stotraṁ nityaṁ bhaktyā ca yaḥ paṭhet ||
en: Kumāra, O sage, gave it to Aṅgiras, the preceptor of the Aṅgirases. — And whoever recites this hymn made by Indra daily and with devotion,
tel: ఓ మునీ, కుమారుడు దానిని అంగిరసులకు గురువైన అంగిరసునికి ఇచ్చాడు. — ఇంద్రుడు రచించిన ఈ స్తోత్రాన్ని ఎవరు నిత్యం భక్తితో పఠిస్తారో,
hi: हे मुने, कुमार ने उसे अंगिरसों के गुरु अंगिरा को दिया। — और जो इस इन्द्रकृत स्तोत्र का नित्य भक्तिपूर्वक पाठ करता है,

--- verse 21 | section: Phalaśruti ---
deva:
स हि प्राप्य दृढां भक्तिमन्ते दास्यं लभेद्ध्रुवम् ।
जन्ममृत्युजराव्याधिशोकेभ्यो मुच्यते नरः ।
न हि पश्यति स्वप्नेऽपि यमदूतं यमालयम् ॥
iast:
sa hi prāpya dṛḍhāṁ bhaktimante dāsyaṁ labheddhruvam |
janmamṛtyujarāvyādhiśokebhyo mucyate naraḥ |
na hi paśyati svapne'pi yamadūtaṁ yamālayam ||
en: he obtains unshakeable devotion, and at the end certainly attains the standing of Kṛṣṇa's servant. Such a man is released from birth, death, old age, disease and grief; nor does he see, even in a dream, the messengers of Yama or the house of Yama.
tel: అతడు దృఢమైన భక్తిని పొంది, చివరకు నిశ్చయంగా దాస్యాన్ని పొందుతాడు. అట్టి మనుష్యుడు జన్మ, మృత్యువు, జర, వ్యాధి, శోకాల నుండి విముక్తుడవుతాడు; స్వప్నంలో సైతం యమదూతను గాని యమాలయాన్ని గాని చూడడు.
hi: वह दृढ़ भक्ति प्राप्त कर अन्त में निश्चय ही दास्य प्राप्त करता है। ऐसा मनुष्य जन्म, मृत्यु, जरा, व्याधि और शोक से मुक्त हो जाता है; और स्वप्न में भी यमदूत या यमालय को नहीं देखता।

Recension note: The Devanāgarī and IAST editions on stotranidhi.com agree word for word across all twenty-one verses, including the colophon placing the hymn at Brahmavaivarta Purāṇa, Kṛṣṇajanma Khaṇḍa, adhyāya 21. Two spellings peculiar to this edition have been retained rather than normalised, since both pages carry them: बृन्दारण्ये / bṛndāraṇye for वृन्दारण्ये in v. 8, and कालीय / kālīya for कालिय in v. 16. Verse 4's colour scheme — yellow in the Dvāpara and dark in the Kali age — is the Brahmavaivarta's own arrangement and is not the more familiar ordering; it is translated as printed. The metre throughout is anuṣṭubh; v. 21 is printed as three lines rather than two, as is common for a closing verse. Two section boundaries fall inside a verse rather than between verses: the narrative closing of Indra's praise occupies only the second line of v. 17, and the phalaśruti begins in the second line of v. 20. The verses have been assigned whole to the section in which they begin, and the shift is marked by a dash in the translations.
