Title: Śrī Kṛṣṇa Stutiḥ (Bhīṣma Kṛtam)
Devanāgarī: भगवत् स्तुतिः (भीष्म कृतम्)
Telugu: శ్రీ కృష్ణ స్తుతిః (భీష్మ కృతం)

Author: Ascribed in the text to Bhīṣma. The colophon reads इति श्रीमद्भागवते महापुराणे प्रथमस्कन्धे नवमोऽध्याये भीष्मकृत भगवत् स्तुतिः — Śrīmad Bhāgavata Mahāpurāṇa, first skandha, ninth adhyāya (verses 32–42 of that chapter).

Source / recension: https://stotranidhi.com/hi/bhishma-kruta-bhagavat-stuti-in-sanskrit/ (Devanāgarī) and https://stotranidhi.com/en/bhishma-kruta-bhagavat-stuti-in-english/ (IAST). The two editions were compared verse by verse and agree in every word, including three readings that differ from the ordinary printed Bhāgavata text (see the recension note). The source files this text under its Viṣṇu section and titles it Bhagavat Stutiḥ; it is given a Kṛṣṇa title here to match the rest of this collection, and the source's own title is retained in the Devanāgarī line above.

Verse count: 11

Sections:
భీష్మ స్తుతి — Bhīṣma Stuti (vv. 1–11)

Blurb: This is Bhīṣma's praise, spoken from the bed of arrows, with Yudhiṣṭhira and Kṛṣṇa standing over him. What sets it apart from other hymns of praise is that it is almost entirely a soldier's memory of how Kṛṣṇa looked in the field. He is grey all over with the dust thrown up by the horses. Drops of sweat are shaken loose in his hair. His skin is split open by Bhīṣma's own arrows. His armour glitters. Its central verse is the one in which Kṛṣṇa breaks his own vow not to take up arms. He leaps from the chariot, catches up its wheel, lets his upper cloth fall away, and comes to kill Bhīṣma, for no reason but to make Bhīṣma's boast come true. Only then does the hymn widen out. It turns to the gopīs, who went blind with rapture and took on his very nature, then to the Rājasūya assembly, and at the last to a verse in which the difference between the seer and the seen falls away: one sun, appearing separately to every eye. The whole is in the long puṣpitāgrā metre.

--- verse 1 | section: Bhīṣma Stuti ---
deva:
भीष्म उवाच ।
इति मतिरुपकल्पिता वितृष्णा
भगवति सात्वतपुङ्गवे विभूम्नि ।
स्वसुखमुपगते क्वचिद्विहर्तुं
प्रकृतिमुपेयुषि यद्भवप्रवाहः ॥
iast:
bhīṣma uvāca |
iti matirupakalpitā vitṛṣṇā
bhagavati sātvatapuṅgave vibhūmni |
svasukhamupagate kvacidvihartuṁ
prakṛtimupeyuṣi yadbhavapravāhaḥ ||
en: Bhīṣma said: So now my mind is set, and set free of craving, upon the Lord — the bull of the Sātvatas, the all-pervading — who, though established in his own happiness, has taken on prakṛti now and again in order to play, and from whom the whole flood of becoming pours out.
tel: భీష్ముడు పలికెను — ఈ విధంగా నా బుద్ధి తృష్ణ లేనిదై, సాత్వతశ్రేష్ఠుడు, సర్వవ్యాపి అయిన భగవంతునియందు నిలిచింది; ఆయన తన స్వసుఖంలో నిలిచియుండి కూడా ఒకానొకప్పుడు లీలకోసం ప్రకృతిని స్వీకరిస్తాడు, ఆయన నుండే ఈ సంసారప్రవాహం వెలువడుతుంది.
hi: भीष्म ने कहा — इस प्रकार मेरी बुद्धि तृष्णारहित होकर उस भगवान् में स्थापित हो गई है, जो सात्वतों में श्रेष्ठ और सर्वव्यापी हैं; जो अपने ही सुख में स्थित होते हुए भी कभी लीला के लिए प्रकृति को स्वीकार करते हैं, और जिनसे यह भवप्रवाह निकलता है।

munigaṇanṛpavaryasaṅkule'ntaḥ
sadasi yudhiṣṭhirarājasūya eṣām |
arhaṇamupapeda īkṣaṇīyo
mama dṛśigocara eṣa āvirātmā ||
en: In the assembly at Yudhiṣṭhira's Rājasūya, thronged with hosts of sages and the foremost of kings, he received their homage, the one worth looking at above all others; and here he is — that same Self, made manifest, within range of my own sight.
tel: యుధిష్ఠిరుని రాజసూయ సభలో మునిగణాలతోను, రాజశ్రేష్ఠులతోను నిండిన ఆ సదస్సులో అందరి పూజను అందుకున్నవాడు, దర్శనీయుడు; ఆ ఆత్మయే ఇప్పుడు ప్రత్యక్షమై నా కంటికి కనబడుతున్నాడు.
hi: युधिष्ठिर के राजसूय की उस सभा में, जो मुनिगणों और श्रेष्ठ नरपतियों से भरी थी, जिन्होंने सबका अर्हण ग्रहण किया, जो सबसे अधिक दर्शनीय थे — वही आत्मा प्रकट होकर इस समय मेरी दृष्टि के सम्मुख हैं।

--- verse 11 | section: Bhīṣma Stuti ---
deva:
तमिममहमजं शरीरभाजां
हृदि हृदि धिष्टितमात्मकल्पितानाम् ।
प्रतिदृशमिव नैकधाऽर्कमेकं
समधिगतोऽस्मि विधूतभेदमोहः ॥
iast:
tamimamahamajaṁ śarīrabhājāṁ
hṛdi hṛdi dhiṣṭitamātmakalpitānām |
pratidṛśamiva naikadhā'rkamekaṁ
samadhigato'smi vidhūtabhedamohaḥ ||
en: This same unborn one, seated in the heart of every embodied being, of all whom he himself has fashioned — as the one sun seems many, one to each eye that sees it — him I have now reached, the delusion of difference shaken off.
tel: తానే కల్పించిన శరీరధారులందరి హృదయహృదయంలోనూ నిలిచిన ఈ అజుడైన స్వామిని — ఒక్కడే అయిన సూర్యుడు ప్రతి కంటికీ వేరువేరుగా కనబడినట్లు — భేదమోహాన్ని వదిలించుకుని నేను ఇప్పుడు పొందాను.
hi: शरीरधारियों के — जिन्हें उन्होंने स्वयं रचा है — प्रत्येक हृदय में स्थित इस अजन्मा को, जैसे एक ही सूर्य प्रत्येक दृष्टि के लिए अनेक प्रतीत होता है, वैसे ही भेद के मोह को दूर कर मैंने अब प्राप्त कर लिया है।

Recension note: The Devanāgarī and IAST pages on stotranidhi.com are word-for-word identical. Three readings differ from the ordinary printed text of Bhāgavata 1.9 and have been left as the source prints them rather than emended: पृथना / pṛthanā for पृतना / pṛtanā ("army") in v. 5; हताः गताः / hatāḥ gatāḥ in v. 8, where the visarga is left unsandhied before g; and धिष्टितम् / dhiṣṭitam for धिष्ठितम् / dhiṣṭhitam in v. 11. In v. 5 a word straddles the pāda break (य-/-श्चरणरतिः) and both source pages print it hyphenated; that hyphenation is kept. The metre throughout is puṣpitāgrā, with twelve syllables in the odd pādas and thirteen in the even ones. The eleven verses correspond to Bhāgavata 1.9.32–42.
