Title: Śrī Kṛṣṇa Aṣṭottaraśatanāma Stotram
Devanāgarī: श्रीकृष्ण-अष्टोत्तरशतनामस्तोत्रम्
Telugu: శ్రీ కృష్ణ అష్టోత్తరశతనామ స్తోత్రం

Author: Anonymous; Śrī Padma Purāṇa, Uttara-khaṇḍa, per the colophon of both stotra and nāmāvalī — the same khaṇḍa cited for entry 5 (Viṣṇu, `vishnu/26`) and entry 10 (Rāma, `rama/26`).

Source / recension: Primary text: **Gītā Press, Gorakhpur — Śatanāma Stotra Saṅgraha**, code 1850, pp. 71–75 (stotra pp. 71–73, nāmāvalī pp. 74–75), transcribed from the page images of the printed edition rather than from OCR. Entry 12 of the volume's forty. Stotra and nāmāvalī are paired in one file as the publisher prints them, and were checked against each other name by name: they agree in order and content at all 109 positions, and the count of names inside the verses was taken independently, verse by verse, before the nāmāvalī was read — see the recension note. `krishna/` was checked and holds 28 files, none of them an aṣṭottaraśatanāma. **No second edition was collated**, and no web witness has been sought.

Recension note: **Prefix.** Gītā Press prints `ॐ` before each name; this corpus prints `श्री` for every nāmāvalī without exception (user, 2026-09-08). **THE COUNT IS 109, NOT 108 — THE SECOND SUCH CASE, AND IT BEHAVES LIKE THE FIRST.** The nāmāvalī runs 1–109 with no gap, ends `१०९ ॐ परात्पराय नमः`, and still carries the colophon `अष्टोत्तरशतनामावलिः`. Counting the nominatives in the stotra gives the same 109 — distributed 7-4-5-4-5-6-5-5-7-7-6-5-5-4-6-6-4-4-6-5-3 across vv. 1–21a — so the surplus is attested by both lists. **The surplus sits at the very end**, exactly as at entry 6 (Veṅkaṭeśa, also 109): the last three names, `सर्वतीर्थात्मक`, `सर्वग्रहरूपी` and `परात्पर`, occupy the first line of v. 21, whose second line is the count claim itself — `एवं श्रीकृष्णदेवस्य नाम्नामष्टोत्तरं शतम्`. So the text asserts 108 in the same verse in which it prints the 107th, 108th and 109th names. **Left at 109 as printed.** This is now the pattern to watch: entries 6 and 12 both overrun by one at the closing verse, entry 8 (Hayagrīva) overruns by six spread through the hymn. Three of the twelve are not 108. **All 109 are distinct** — no repetition anywhere, which is the third such list after Veṅkaṭeśa and Hanumān. **Verse 21 is deliberately kept as one block**, mixing the last three names and the count claim as the print does, rather than split to tidy the sections; the `Sections:` field marks it. **No. 43 `परस्मै ज्योतिषे` and no. 31 `योगिनां पतये` are printed as two words** and kept that way, as is no. 62 `स्यमन्तकमणेर्हर्त्रे`, which carries a genitive inside the name straight from v. 12. **No. 56 is `मधुघ्ने` in the list against `मधुहा` in v. 11** — regular `-हन्` stem inflection, not a divergence; compare the same pattern at entry 8. **No. 73 `कृष्णाव्यसनकर्षकाय`** takes `कृष्णा` as Draupadī, whose distress Kṛṣṇa relieved; the English column says so rather than leaving the feminine unexplained. **The Bhāgavata is not named but is everywhere in the list** — Pūtanā, Śakaṭa, Tṛṇāvarta, the yamalārjunas, Dhenuka, Kālīya, Govardhana, the stolen garments, Mucukunda, the Syamantaka, Kubjā — while the Mahābhārata supplies Śiśupāla, Bhīṣma, Yudhiṣṭhira, Vidura, the Gītā and the universal form. The colophon assigns the whole to the Padma Purāṇa.

Blurb: Vraja and Kurukṣetra in one list, and no seam between them: the butter-smeared child who danced for it, the cart-demon and the whirlwind and the twin arjuna trees, the hill held up, the clothes carried off — then Śiśupāla's head, Bhīṣma's release, Yudhiṣṭhira set on his throne, and the ocean of the Gītā's nectar. The hymn declares itself a hundred and eight in the same verse in which it prints its hundred and ninth name.

--- verse 1 | section: Dhyānam ---
deva:
शिखिमुकुटविशेषं नीलपद्माङ्गदेशं विधुमुखकृतकेशं कौस्तुभापीतवेशम् ।
मधुररवकलेशं शं भजे भ्रातृशेषं व्रजजनवनितेशं माधवं राधिकेशम् ॥

en: I worship Mādhava, the lord of Rādhikā: crowned with a peacock's plume, his body dark as the blue lotus, curls upon his moon-like face, his dress golden with the Kaustubha; master of the sweet-sounding art of the flute; well-being itself; whose brother is Śeṣa; the beloved of the women of Vraja.
tel: నెమలిపింఛపు కిరీటము కలవాడు, నీలకమలమువంటి శరీరము కలవాడు, చంద్రునివంటి ముఖముపై కురులు కలవాడు, కౌస్తుభకాంతిచే పీతవర్ణ వేషము కలవాడు, మధురధ్వనిరూప కళకు అధిపతి, కల్యాణస్వరూపుడు, శేషావతారుడైన బలరాముని సోదరుడు, వ్రజవనితలకు వల్లభుడు, రాధికేశుడు అగు మాధవుని భజించుచున్నాను.
